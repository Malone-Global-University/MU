Decision, Risk, and Information

This section develops mathematical tools for making decisions when outcomes are uncertain, information is incomplete, and choices involve competing objectives. The lessons move from decision tables and expected value through loss, uncertainty, sensitivity, information theory, reliability, strategic interaction, allocation, and the responsible communication of risk.
