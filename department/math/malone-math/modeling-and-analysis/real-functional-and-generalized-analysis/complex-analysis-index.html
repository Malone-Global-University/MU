Complex Analysis

Complex analysis studies functions of complex variables and the strong relationships among geometry, differentiation, integration, infinite series, singularities, and conformal mappings.

Learning Path

Begin with the geometry and algebra of complex numbers, then move through functions, limits, differentiation, analyticity, and the Cauchy–Riemann relationships. From there, develop contour integration, Cauchy's theorem, Laurent series, singularities, and residues before finishing with conformal mapping and applications.
