Real, Functional, and Generalized Analysis

This section develops rigorous analysis from the completeness of the real numbers through convergence, metric and normed spaces, measure and integration, function spaces, operators, distributions, and the foundations of functional analysis.

Explore the Lessons

  • Real-Number Completeness — Supremum, infimum, completeness, monotone convergence, Cauchy sequences, and foundational consequences of the real-number system.
  • Sequences and Function Convergence — Numerical sequences, subsequences, Cauchy behavior, pointwise convergence, uniform convergence, and preservation theorems.
  • Metric Spaces — Distance, open and closed sets, convergence, completeness, compactness, and continuity in abstract spaces.
  • Normed and Inner-Product Spaces — Norms, \(p\)-norms, inner products, orthogonality, Banach spaces, Hilbert spaces, and projections.
  • Continuity and Compactness — Continuity in metric spaces, uniform continuity, compactness, sequential compactness, and major compactness theorems.
  • Rigorous Differentiation and Integration — Derivatives from limits, mean-value theorems, Riemann integration, Darboux sums, and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
  • Measure and Measurable Functions — Sigma-algebras, measures, measurable sets, null sets, measurable functions, and almost-everywhere language.
  • Lebesgue Integration — Simple functions, nonnegative and signed integrals, \(L^1\), and the major convergence theorems of measure theory.
  • Function Spaces — \(C(K)\), \(\ell^p\), \(L^p\), \(L^\infty\), norms, completeness, separability, and Hilbert-space structure.
  • Operators and Linear Functionals — Bounded operators, operator norms, kernels, ranges, dual spaces, weak convergence, and foundational operator theorems.
  • Distributions and Generalized Functions — Test functions, regular and singular distributions, the Dirac delta, weak derivatives, tempered distributions, and generalized solutions.
  • Introductory Functional Analysis — Banach and Hilbert spaces, duality, weak convergence, compact operators, major structural theorems, and introductory spectral ideas.

Section Progression

The lessons are arranged to move from rigorous real analysis into increasingly general spaces and operators:

\[ \boxed{ \text{completeness} \to \text{convergence} \to \text{metric structure} \to \text{normed structure} \to \text{measure and integration} \to \text{function spaces} \to \text{operators} \to \text{generalized functions} }. \]
