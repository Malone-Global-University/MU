Explore Proportion

Proportion connects ratios, rates, algebra, geometry, measurement, and many kinds of mathematical modeling. This lesson set develops the idea progressively, beginning with intuitive proportional reasoning and moving toward formal relationships and problem solving.

Begin with the narrative introduction for a gentle entry into the subject, continue through the concept lessons, or go directly to the problem-solving materials.
