Math Explorations Series

Explore mathematics through interconnected concept-driven lessons that emphasize understanding, problem solving, applications, and the relationships between ideas.

Overview

The Math Explorations Series examines important and interesting mathematical concepts outside the constraints of a traditional chapter-by-chapter course. Each exploration may combine narrative explanation, mathematical principles, worked examples, problem sets, visual aids, and links to related concepts. Topics are organized as interconnected learning clusters, allowing readers to follow mathematical ideas across subjects, revisit concepts from different perspectives, and explore them in greater depth.
