Foundational Research Subjects

This index identifies 25 research subjects and their principal concepts for the study of accounting systems, banking automation, computer science, and financial software architecture. It provides an initial foundation for the Bank Stack capstone, connecting historical accounting and banking practices with the theoretical principles underlying the design, development, operation, and verification of computerized financial systems. This list is a starting research agenda, not an approved system specification or exhaustive banking curriculum.
