Capstone Projects

About Capstone Projects

Computer science capstone projects integrate knowledge from multiple areas of computing and, where necessary, other academic and professional disciplines to address substantive engineering problems. Each project connects foundational research with practical investigation, design, implementation, testing, and reflection.

Projects are developed iteratively. Research and implementation inform one another, and conclusions are revised as new evidence and practical experience emerge.

Capstone Project Directory

Banking Software Engineering (Bank Stack)

A multidisciplinary investigation of accounting history, modern banking practices, computer science, and software engineering, directed toward understanding and constructing functional banking software from first principles.

Stage: Foundational research and project formulation.

Project Approach

  1. Define the problem and establish the relevant foundational disciplines.
  2. Investigate historical development, theory, and contemporary practice.
  3. Develop conceptual models and testable design hypotheses.
  4. Construct and evaluate practical implementations.
  5. Document findings, limitations, and lessons for further study.

Project documentation distinguishes established knowledge from proposed designs, experimental results, and unresolved questions.
