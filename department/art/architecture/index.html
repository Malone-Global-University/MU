Welcome to the Architecture Department

Architecture at Malone Global University bridges art, technology, and cultural identity. We study how African and African American architects have shaped the built environment through innovation, sustainability, and expression of community values.

Our Mission

The department aims to inspire a generation of designers who build with cultural wisdom, environmental sensitivity, and moral integrity. From rural planning to digital design, we connect tradition to innovation.
