Malone Doctrine

The governing ethical and strategic system of the Malone Network.

Doctrine Overview

Malone Doctrine is the governing ethical and strategic system of Malone Global University and the wider Malone Network. It establishes the standards by which conduct, leadership, institutional alignment, public representation, political action, and economic activity are evaluated.

The Ethics Department develops explanatory works, research, commentary, policy briefs, and public interpretation. The authoritative normative code is maintained separately in the MGU Library for direct reference, version control, and formal institutional use.
