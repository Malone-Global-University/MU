Primer Overview

The Malone Primer is the explanatory and developmental body of work supporting Malone Doctrine. It uses research, argument, historical analysis, policy development, case studies, and institutional examination to identify problems, evaluate competing interpretations, propose solutions, and clarify how the Doctrine may be understood and applied.

Primer materials do not independently establish or amend normative authority. Their purpose is to support discovery, preserve reasoning, test proposed standards, and develop conclusions that may later be codified within the canonical Malone Doctrine maintained in the MGU Library.

Specifically:

Research → empirical and investigative.
Argument → logical, philosophical, legal, and rhetorical.
Human experience → historical, biographical, sociological, and practical.
Purpose, development, and application → explanatory rather than normative.
