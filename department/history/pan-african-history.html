📜 Pan-African School – History Curriculum Tree

Level 1 – Foundations
  • Introduction to History – what history is, sources, and interpretation
  • World Civilizations I – ancient Mesopotamia, Egypt, Indus Valley, China
  • African History I – early African civilizations, Kingdom of Kush, Nok culture
  • Historical Thinking Skills – chronology, cause & effect, continuity & change
  • Oral Histories & Traditions – storytelling, preservation of heritage
Level 2 – Core History
  • World Civilizations II – Greece, Rome, Islamic Caliphates, Medieval Europe
  • African History II – Ghana, Mali, Songhai Empires
  • Early Modern World – Renaissance, Reformation, Scientific Revolution
  • Colonialism in Africa – European contact, trade, and conquest
  • Historical Research Methods – archives, primary/secondary sources
Level 3 – Applied & Regional Studies
  • Modern African History – independence movements, nation-building
  • 19th Century World – industrialization, imperialism, global trade
  • 20th Century Africa – decolonization, Pan-Africanism, Cold War impacts
  • World Wars I & II – causes, key events, consequences
  • History of the African Diaspora – slavery, emancipation, cultural legacy
  • Comparative History – thematic studies across regions
Level 4 – Advanced / Specializations
  • Historiography – schools of thought, debates in history writing
  • Economic History – trade routes, resource economies, development
  • Political History – governance systems, revolutions, diplomacy
  • Social & Cultural History – gender roles, religion, arts
  • Environmental History – climate impacts, agriculture, sustainability
  • Military History – strategy, campaigns, technology evolution
Practical Training & Projects
  • Archival Research – using national and local archives
  • Oral History Projects – interviewing elders, documenting community heritage
  • Museum & Heritage Studies – curation, conservation, exhibition design
  • Field Trips – historical sites, monuments, archaeological digs
  • Capstone Project – in-depth research on a chosen historical topic
Electives & Cross-Disciplinary Options
  • African-American History – civil rights, cultural contributions
  • History of Science & Technology – innovations, diffusion, impacts
  • Globalization & History – interconnectedness through time
  • Historical Fiction Writing – creative reconstruction of past events
  • History & Film – how cinema shapes and reflects history
