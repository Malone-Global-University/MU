WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Batch creation of Hip-Hop Pioneer biography pages (Old School era expansion)

Date
2026-06-03

Area
Music Department / Hip-Hop Artist Biography Pages (HTML template system)

Problem
The Music Department lacked a consistent set of structured biography pages for foundational hip-hop artists and groups. Existing content coverage was incomplete, and several key old-school pioneers either had missing pages or inconsistent formatting relative to the Afrika Bambaataa template standard.

Intended Goal
Generate a uniform set of HTML biography pages for early hip-hop artists and groups using the established department template, ensuring consistent structure, navigation components, metadata, and historical tone across all entries.

Root Cause
Content expansion had progressed in isolated batches without a fully completed foundational “Old School hip-hop” catalog. Earlier pages existed, but many artists had not yet been standardized into full template-compliant HTML documents, leading to uneven coverage across the archive.

Steps Taken

1. Established batch production approach for grouped artist page generation rather than single-page ad hoc creation.
2. Produced initial batch of five full template-compliant HTML biography pages (Kool DJ Herc, Grandmaster Flash, Grandmaster Caz, Busy Bee, DJ Hollywood).
3. Generated second batch of five full template-compliant HTML pages (Cold Crush Brothers, Kurtis Blow, Lovebug Starski, Spoonie Gee, The Sugarhill Gang).
4. Generated third batch of five full template-compliant HTML pages (Treacherous Three, Grandmaster Flash and the Furious Five, Melle Mel, Doug E. Fresh, Pete Rock & CL Smooth).
5. Generated additional Southern hip-hop and regional expansion pages (Project Pat, Gangsta Boo, La Chat).
6. Added Detroit collective entry (D12) with entity-linked references and full template structure.
7. Maintained consistent HTML structure including hero sections, breadcrumb placeholders, bottom navigation, footer scripts, and metadata descriptions.

Key Change
A standardized repeatable workflow was established for generating large batches of consistent hip-hop biography HTML pages using a unified departmental template.

Outcome
A substantial set of correctly structured, uniform HTML biography pages was produced, covering foundational hip-hop pioneers, Golden Age artists, and key Southern/Detroit contributors. The pages now follow a consistent design system and are ready for integration into the Music Department directory.

Validation
Visual and structural inspection of generated HTML confirms consistent inclusion of required components (head metadata, hero section, main content blocks, navigation placeholders, and footer scripts). Pages maintain coherent formatting and predictable structure suitable for directory indexing.

Rules Going Forward
All future artist pages must strictly follow the established HTML template structure, including consistent metadata formatting, hero section usage, and navigation/footer integration. Batch generation should be preferred for related artist groups to maintain stylistic and structural consistency across the archive.

Notes
This session significantly expanded the Hip-Hop biography catalog from foundational old-school artists into Southern and Midwest rap eras. Future expansion should continue moving chronologically into Golden Age (late 80s–90s mainstream breakouts such as Rakim, Nas, Public Enemy, Ice Cube, etc.) and ensure group/solo distinction is consistently handled.
