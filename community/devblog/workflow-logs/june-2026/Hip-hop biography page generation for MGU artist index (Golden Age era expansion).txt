WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Hip-hop biography page generation for MGU artist index (Golden Age era expansion)

Date
2026-06-03

Area
Music Department → Hip-Hop Biography HTML pages (artist index templates)

Problem
A consistent set of missing or incomplete biography pages existed for key hip-hop artists and groups within the Music Department directory. Several entries either lacked pages entirely or required standardized HTML structure to match the existing MGU biography format. The gap affected continuity across the hip-hop archive, especially for Golden Age and early foundational artists.

Intended Goal
Each requested artist or group should have a fully structured, uniform HTML biography page following the established MGU department template. Pages should maintain consistent metadata, hero section formatting, biography/legacy/cultural impact structure, and shared component integration (header, footer, navigation). Content should remain historically grounded and stylistically consistent across entries.

Root Cause
The archive expansion was incomplete, with earlier entries created individually over time rather than through a unified batch generation process. This led to inconsistent coverage of artists and missing pages for several important groups and MCs. There was no enforced batch completion pass for entire collectives or related artist clusters.

Steps Taken

1. Generated full HTML biography page for Big Daddy Kane using MGU department template structure.
2. Created standardized pages for multiple foundational hip-hop acts including Biz Markie, Boogie Down Productions, De La Soul, DJ Jazzy Jeff, EPMD, Eric B. & Rakim, Heavy D, Kid ’n Play, Juice Crew, LL Cool J, MC Lyte, Monie Love, Queen Latifah, Rakim, Run-DMC, Salt-N-Pepa, Slick Rick, Ultramagnetic MCs, Whodini, and related entries.
3. Maintained consistent structural formatting across all pages including head metadata, hero section, biography blocks, legacy sections, cultural impact sections, and shared component scripts.
4. Ensured uniform integration of `/component/header.html`, `/component/footer.html`, and `/component/bottom-nav.html` across all generated files.
5. Preserved narrative tone appropriate for educational music history documentation while differentiating stylistic identity per artist or group.

Key Change
Established a complete, standardized set of Golden Age hip-hop biography HTML pages using a consistent MGU departmental template structure.

Outcome
The Music Department hip-hop index now has a significantly expanded and structurally consistent set of biography pages covering foundational artists and groups. Pages are uniformly formatted and ready for integration into the broader navigation system.

Validation
Visual inspection of repeated template structure confirms consistent page layout, proper component loading scripts, and uniform section hierarchy across all generated biographies. No structural breaks or missing sections were introduced in the batch.

Rules Going Forward
All future artist biography pages must strictly follow the established MGU department HTML template, including consistent metadata, hero formatting, and section ordering. Batch generation should be preferred for related artist groups to avoid incomplete coverage. Shared component scripts must remain unchanged unless system-level updates are required.

Notes
This session effectively completed a “Golden Age Hip-Hop Core Directory” expansion pass. Future work can extend into later eras (1990s–2000s mainstream expansion, Southern hip-hop, West Coast evolution, and experimental/underground movements) using the same structural framework.
