WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Base44 Intranet Functional Planning and Scope Definition

Date
2026-05-21

Area
Base44 intranet application planning and system architecture

Problem
The intranet concept initially focused too heavily on page structure and visual layout rather than the operational purpose of the system. Without defining what the intranet actually needed to accomplish, there was a risk of building disconnected pages that looked organized but did not improve communication, workflows, or internal operations.

Intended Goal
Define the intranet around functional outcomes and organizational needs so the platform becomes a practical operational tool rather than a static information repository.

Root Cause
Early planning naturally drifted toward navigation structure, page categories, and visual organization before identifying the core operational responsibilities the intranet must support for employees and administrators.

Steps Taken

1. Identified Base44 as the target low-code/no-code application platform for intranet development.
2. Outlined an initial intranet structure including Home, Departments, HR, IT, Projects, Directory, Events, Forms, and Admin areas.
3. Generated a visual intranet concept image to help frame the platform direction and intended user experience.
4. Shifted planning emphasis from page hierarchy to operational functionality and user workflows.
5. Defined the primary functional pillars of the intranet:

   * Communication
   * Knowledge management
   * Collaboration and workflow
   * Employee services and support
   * Engagement and organizational culture
6. Discussed how each functional pillar translates into real intranet usage scenarios and daily operational value.

Key Change
The planning process shifted from “what pages should exist” to “what operational problems the intranet should solve.”

Outcome
The intranet concept became more strategically focused and operationally grounded. The system direction now emphasizes utility, workflows, accessibility, and organizational support rather than static navigation design alone.

Validation
Validation was performed by reviewing whether each proposed section directly supported a business function or employee workflow. The resulting structure now maps cleanly to communication, collaboration, support, and knowledge-sharing objectives.

Rules Going Forward

* Define operational purpose before designing page layouts.
* Every intranet section must support a measurable workflow, communication need, or employee service.
* Avoid creating pages that exist only for organizational appearance.
* Focus on systems employees will actively use daily.
* Prioritize workflows, permissions, and usability over visual complexity during early planning stages.

Notes
Future planning sessions should focus on:

* Role-based permissions
* Workflow automation
* Internal request systems
* Search and knowledge retrieval
* Integration with external tools
* Mobile usability
* Dashboard personalization
* Notification and approval systems
