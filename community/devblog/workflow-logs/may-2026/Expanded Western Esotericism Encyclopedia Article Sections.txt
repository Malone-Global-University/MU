Rules Going Forward
Keep Western esotericism framed as a broad historical and interpretive field, not as one unified hidden organization.
Distinguish influence, resemblance, borrowing, symbolism, secrecy, and institutional control.
Use evidence-based language when discussing Illuminati mythology, secret societies, occult symbols, and claims of hidden power.
Preserve the section pattern: overview, definition, historical sources, major currents, ritual/symbolic interpretation, conspiracy boundary, methods, importance, evidence statement, related topics, and article summary.
Use HTML-ready sections with clear IDs so the expanded table of contents can link cleanly to each part of the article.
