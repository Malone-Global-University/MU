WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Modern Car Systems Reference Outline

Date
2026-05-21

Area
MGU automotive / transportation reference content

Problem
The session needed a clear, organized explanation of the main systems that make up a modern car. The information had to be broad enough to cover conventional gasoline vehicles, hybrids, and electric vehicles without becoming too technical or narrowly focused on one vehicle type.

Intended Goal
Create a practical systems-level outline that can be used as a foundation for automotive lessons, reference pages, infographics, or future “How Things Work” content about modern cars.

Root Cause
Modern cars are not one single machine but an integration of many overlapping mechanical, electrical, electronic, safety, and comfort systems. Without a structured breakdown, the topic can become scattered across engine parts, body components, electronics, and driver-assistance features.

Steps Taken

1. Identified the major system categories found in modern vehicles, including powertrain, energy storage, cooling, steering, suspension, braking, electrical, safety, body/chassis, climate control, lighting, infotainment, and computer control systems.
2. Explained each system in plain language, describing its main purpose and typical components.
3. Included both conventional vehicle examples and electric vehicle equivalents where relevant, such as fuel tanks versus battery packs, alternators versus DC-DC converters, and engine braking versus regenerative braking.
4. Grouped the systems conceptually around what the car must do: move, steer, stop, support, power electronics, protect occupants, control temperature, and manage information.
5. Produced a reusable overview that can be expanded later into individual pages or diagrams.

Key Change
The car was reframed as a coordinated set of major systems rather than a loose list of individual parts.

Outcome
The session produced a clear reference outline of modern car systems that can support future MGU automotive content, including lessons, index pages, diagrams, or infographic planning.

Validation
The outline was checked for coverage across the major vehicle functions: propulsion, energy storage, control, safety, structure, comfort, electronics, and road contact. It also accounted for both internal-combustion vehicles and electric vehicles, preventing the explanation from being limited to only one drivetrain type.

Rules Going Forward
Automotive overview pages should begin with system-level organization before moving into component-level detail. When discussing modern cars, include gasoline, hybrid, and electric vehicle variations where appropriate. Keep “system” categories functional: what the system does, what parts it includes, and how it connects to the rest of the vehicle.

Notes
This outline can be expanded into a full MGU transportation systems page or broken into separate “How a Car Works” topic pages, such as how the powertrain works, how the braking system works, how suspension works, how car computers communicate, and how electric vehicle battery systems work.
