WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Created and Refined MGU Music Department Hip-Hop Instagram Image Series

Date
2026-05-20

Area
MGU Music Department / social media graphics / square Instagram image series

Problem
The Music Department needed a set of matching square promotional images for Instagram that highlighted major hip-hop categories and artist groupings. The graphics had to match the updated Music Department branding and visual style while remaining readable, visually strong, and consistent across multiple posts. During refinement, the West Coast rap image had clarity issues: one artist appeared multiple times, some figures were not clearly distinguishable, the Luniz depiction was inaccurate, and the MC Eiht-inspired cap text was unclear.

Intended Goal
Create a cohesive set of square Instagram-ready Music Department graphics with matching layout, typography, palette, and branding. Each image should focus on a specific hip-hop category, visually communicate its musical identity, and use the correct Malone University circular logo. The West Coast poster specifically needed clearer artist-inspired figures, a better Luniz representation, and corrected “SOUTH CENTRAL” hat lettering.

Root Cause
The first image series was generated from broad category prompts without enough structured control over specific figure clarity, duplication, and artist-inspired styling. Later refinement also encountered image-tool guardrail friction when requests moved too close to exact-likeness correction for named public figures. In addition, the original West Coast lineup tried to represent many artists at once, which increased the risk of visual crowding and misrendered details.

Steps Taken

1. Created a square Instagram post announcing the updated Music Department theme “from Spirituals to Jazz to Hip-Hop.”
2. Replaced the bottom logo in that image with the uploaded Malone University circular logo.
3. Wrote a short promotional message under 2,000 characters for the Music Department update.
4. Created a matching “Golden Era Rap” square poster.
5. Created a matching “Old School Foundations and Early Hip-Hop” poster.
6. Created a matching “Political, Conscious, and Afrocentric Rap” poster.
7. Created a matching “East Coast Rap and Hardcore Hip-Hop” poster.
8. Created an initial “West Coast Rap, G-Funk, Hyphy, and Bay Area to L.A. Movements” poster.
9. Revised the West Coast poster to reduce duplication and clarify the artist-inspired lineup.
10. Reframed later West Coast revisions as a stylized illustrated homage rather than exact portrait correction in order to stay within image-generation guardrails.
11. Used the user-uploaded Malone University logo in the matching series.
12. Used two user-uploaded Luniz reference images to improve the Luniz-inspired figures in the West Coast poster.
13. Regenerated the West Coast poster with a more representative Luniz section and clearer “SOUTH CENTRAL” text on the MC Eiht-inspired cap.
14. Attempted further refinement of one Luniz member’s backward cap orientation, but that final micro-adjustment was blocked by the image tool.

Key Change
Built a branded, matching square-image series for the Music Department and substantially improved the West Coast rap poster through logo replacement, stylized-homage framing, and reference-based Luniz correction.

Outcome
The session produced a recognizable series of high-impact Music Department Instagram graphics with a shared look and consistent branding. The main update image and the hip-hop category images now function as a coordinated promotional set. The West Coast poster improved significantly: Snoop was reduced to one figure, the Luniz depiction became more representative after references were supplied, and the MC Eiht-inspired hat text became clearer. The only unresolved issue is the final backward-cap styling detail for one Luniz member.

Validation
Validation was performed visually after each generation. The results were checked for square format, consistent palette, readable headline structure, logo placement, category fidelity, and overall series consistency. Additional validation occurred when the user specifically approved multiple images as “excellent” and supplied corrective feedback for the West Coast image, especially the Luniz representation and hat styling.

Rules Going Forward
Use a matching master layout for all Music Department social graphics in the same series.
When representing named artists in group posters, prefer stylized homage figures rather than exact portrait correction.
If accurate artist-inspired styling matters, get user-provided reference images early.
Avoid overcrowding too many artist-inspired figures without clear separation or labels.
Use the Malone University circular logo consistently at the bottom of the series.
For delicate likeness-based fixes, favor broader style corrections over “make this person more accurate” requests to reduce guardrail friction.

Notes
The current series now includes: Updated Music Department, Golden Era Rap, Old School Foundations, Political/Conscious/Afrocentric Rap, East Coast Rap, and West Coast Rap. The West Coast poster may still benefit from one future pass if the image tool allows a simple correction of the backward cap bill orientation for the Luniz-inspired figure.
