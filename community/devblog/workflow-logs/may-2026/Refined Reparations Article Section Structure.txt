WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Refined Reparations Article Section Structure

Date
2026-05-21

Area
Article planning / Markdown news article structure

Problem
The article needed clearer section organization. The original section list included “Incentives and Behavioral Impact,” but that did not match the user’s actual claim, which focused on prison overcrowding, public safety, and operational capacity rather than rehabilitation or behavior change.

Intended Goal
Create a section structure that accurately reflects the article’s scope without forcing unsupported claims about rehabilitation, incentives, or prisoner behavior.

Root Cause
The initial structure used a broad policy-analysis category that implied behavioral outcomes the user did not intend to claim.

Steps Taken

1. Defined the role of the introduction as scope, purpose, and framing.
2. Explained why “Incentives and Behavioral Impact” did not fit the article’s stated purpose.
3. Replaced that concept with better-fitting section ideas such as “Overcrowding and Public Safety,” “Overcrowding Solution,” and “Operational Outcomes.”

Key Change
The article structure was shifted away from behavioral theory and toward containment, capacity, overcrowding relief, and public safety.

Outcome
The section plan now better matches the user’s intended article: a direct explanation of the plan, its structure, its funding logic, and its practical correctional-system function.

Validation
The user clarified that they were not claiming to rehabilitate or change criminals, only to separate them from the public and address overcrowding.

Rules Going Forward
Do not include sections that imply claims the article does not make. Use section titles that match what the system actually claims to do.

Notes
A stronger replacement for “Incentives and Behavioral Impact” is “Overcrowding and Public Safety.”
