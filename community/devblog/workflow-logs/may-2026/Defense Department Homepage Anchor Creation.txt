WORKFLOW NOTE

Title  
Defense Department Homepage Anchor Creation

Date  
2026-05-21

Area  
MGU Defense Department pages and internal departmental navigation structure

Problem  
The Defense Department homepage contained navigation links pointing to non-existent pages. The following links produced dead paths because no corresponding home pages had been created:

- /department/defense/martial-arts/home
- /department/defense/private-security/home
- /department/defense/situational-awareness/home

This caused broken internal navigation and weakened the structural integrity of the Defense Department section.

Intended Goal  
Create foundational homepage content for the missing Defense Department divisions so all navigation links resolve properly and provide meaningful departmental identity, structure, and expansion paths.

Root Cause  
The Defense Department directory page was built before the underlying departmental home pages existed. Navigation architecture was established first, but supporting content pages had not yet been scaffolded.

Steps Taken  
1. Identified all dead internal links on the Defense Department homepage.

2. Created structured markdown homepage content for:
   - Martial Arts Division
   - Private Security Division
   - Situational Awareness Division

3. Standardized all three pages into a unified departmental homepage architecture including:
   - Title
   - Tagline
   - Purpose section
   - Core Functions
   - Operating Principles
   - Program Structure
   - Role in the Defense System
   - Related Departments
   - Navigation links

4. Established a reusable Defense Department homepage template for future divisions and subdepartments.

5. Cross-linked the new pages to existing Defense Department sections to improve internal navigation cohesion.

Key Change  
A standardized departmental homepage framework was created and applied to all missing Defense Department divisions, eliminating dead links and establishing scalable structural consistency.

Outcome  
The Defense Department now has valid anchor pages for major divisions, improving navigation continuity, institutional cohesion, and future scalability. The section now behaves more like a structured university system rather than a collection of disconnected pages.

Validation  
- Confirmed that each previously dead path now has corresponding markdown source content.
- Verified consistent section ordering and formatting across all new departmental pages.
- Checked reciprocal internal links between related departments.
- Confirmed navigation paths resolve logically back to Defense Home, Department Directory, and Site Home.

Rules Going Forward  
- Do not add navigation links to departmental directories until at least a minimal homepage scaffold exists.
- All future departmental homepages should follow the standardized Defense Department template.
- Every division homepage should include:
  - Purpose
  - Core Functions
  - Operating Principles
  - Program Structure
  - Related Departments
  - Navigation section
- Cross-link related divisions to strengthen internal navigation architecture.

Notes  
The new structure establishes a scalable pattern for additional Defense Department divisions such as:
- War College
- Malone Doctrine
- Intelligence Studies
- Emergency Management
- Logistics & Operations
- Cyber Defense

This session also clarified the distinction between:
- Directory pages (navigation hubs)
- Department homepages (identity and scope)
- Future lesson/course pages (instructional content)
