WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Science Index Overview Section Standardization

Date
2026-05-21

Area
MGU science index pages and category landing page content structure

Problem
The science-related index pages lacked a consistent introductory overview section above their category listings. Users entering the pages would immediately encounter category links without first receiving a high-level explanation of the discipline, its scope, or its relationship to other scientific fields.

Intended Goal
Create standardized overview sections for major science index pages that introduce each discipline with concise, academically styled summaries placed above the category sections. The overviews should establish conceptual framing, define the field, explain its scope, and maintain a consistent tone across the MGU science taxonomy.

Root Cause
The original index structures focused primarily on navigation and categorization rather than disciplinary orientation. Without overview content, the pages functioned more like raw directories than educational entry points.

Steps Taken

1. Established a standardized overview-writing format for science index pages using semantic HTML section structures with heading and paragraph content.
2. Created a reusable academic writing style emphasizing:

   * definition of the discipline,
   * scope of study,
   * major processes or themes,
   * broader scientific significance.
3. Generated overview sections for:

   * Astronomy
   * Biology
   * Chemistry
   * Earth Science
   * Environmental Science
   * Physics
   * Scientific Foundations
   * Science
   * Computer Science
4. Standardized HTML structure using:

   * `<section>`
   * `aria-labelledby`
   * semantic `<h2>`
   * descriptive paragraph blocks.
5. Aligned wording and tone across all generated overviews to maintain encyclopedia-level consistency throughout the MGU science ecosystem.

Key Change
Science index pages were transformed from simple category directories into structured educational entry points through the addition of standardized overview sections.

Outcome
The science indices now provide conceptual orientation before navigation, making the pages feel more authoritative, educational, and professionally organized. The overviews improve readability, contextual understanding, and continuity across the MGU science taxonomy.

Validation
Visual and structural review confirmed:

* consistent HTML formatting,
* uniform academic tone,
* proper heading hierarchy,
* semantic accessibility structure,
* smooth transition between overview content and category listings.

Rules Going Forward

* Every major discipline index should include a concise overview section above categories.
* Overview sections should define the field before describing subdivisions.
* Tone should remain academic, broad, and encyclopedia-like rather than conversational.
* Overviews should avoid excessive detail or lesson-style explanations.
* Semantic HTML structure should remain consistent across all index pages.

Notes
This overview format can now be expanded consistently into:

* mathematics,
* engineering,
* humanities,
* philosophy,
* history,
* social sciences,
* technology,
* arts,
* library encyclopedia domains,
* department landing pages,
* ebook front matter introductions.
