WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Expansion of Computer Science Encyclopedia Article Library

Date
2026-05-21

Area
MGU Library Encyclopedia — Computer Science taxonomy, article structure, and specialized computing coverage

Problem
The Computer Science encyclopedia taxonomy had been structurally planned, but many index sections either lacked article content entirely or needed standardized encyclopedia-style article pages to establish consistent depth, formatting, navigation structure, and conceptual coverage across the domain.

Intended Goal
Create a scalable and internally consistent encyclopedia article set for the Computer Science section of the MGU Library, using standardized article structures with reusable navigation sections, conceptual explanations, related-topic linking, and domain-specific taxonomy alignment.

Root Cause
The encyclopedia taxonomy had matured faster than the article production layer. Structural categories existed, but many underlying conceptual articles had not yet been written or normalized into a unified encyclopedia format.

Steps Taken

1. Continued systematic article generation across multiple Computer Science encyclopedia categories using a consistent semantic structure.

2. Expanded article coverage for:

   * Computational Foundations
   * Computer Architecture
   * Applications of Computing
   * Data Systems
   * Hardware Systems
   * Human Interaction Systems
   * Networking Systems
   * Security Systems
   * Software Systems
   * Specialized and Emerging Computing

3. Standardized article structure across generated pages:

   * “On This Page” navigation block
   * Multi-section conceptual explanations
   * Related Topics section
   * Consistent semantic heading hierarchy
   * Encyclopedia-oriented explanatory tone

4. Completed Software Systems article sequence:

   * Software Overview
   * System Software
   * Operating Systems
   * Firmware
   * Device Drivers
   * Application Software
   * Middleware
   * Virtualization Software

5. Completed Specialized and Emerging Computing article sequence:

   * Embedded Computing
   * High Performance Computing
   * Artificial Intelligence Systems
   * Quantum Computing
   * Edge Computing
   * Neuromorphic Computing

6. Evaluated taxonomy placement for “Data Center” and determined best-fit placement under Networking Systems due to its role as infrastructure-level network and cloud operational architecture.

7. Refined encyclopedia linkage strategy by continuing cross-domain related-topic references between architecture, networking, AI, infrastructure, security, and software systems.

Key Change
The Computer Science encyclopedia evolved from a partially indexed taxonomy into a substantially populated, internally linked conceptual reference framework with standardized article architecture.

Outcome
The MGU Computer Science encyclopedia now has broad foundational coverage across major computing domains with consistent article formatting, scalable taxonomy alignment, and improved conceptual continuity between sections. The article system now reads as a coherent encyclopedia rather than isolated topic pages.

Validation
Validation was performed through:

* Structural consistency checks between articles
* Cross-category taxonomy continuity review
* Verification of internal related-topic linking
* Confirmation that article tone and depth remained encyclopedia-oriented
* Review of category completion status for Software Systems and Specialized Computing

Rules Going Forward

* Continue encyclopedia article generation using the standardized semantic structure already established.
* Preserve consistent “On This Page” and “Related Topics” sections across all future articles.
* Maintain conceptual rather than tutorial-style writing for encyclopedia entries.
* Keep taxonomy placement aligned to operational/system function rather than only physical implementation.
* Use cross-domain related-topic linking to strengthen encyclopedia navigability and conceptual cohesion.

Notes
“Data Center” was identified as fitting best under Networking Systems because it functions primarily as networked infrastructure architecture connecting internet infrastructure, cloud computing, and distributed computing systems.

The encyclopedia taxonomy is now mature enough that future work can increasingly focus on:

* Depth expansion
* Internal linking refinement
* Anchor/index generation
* Higher-level overview pages
* Infrastructure and platform architecture articles
* Subdomain specialization branches within Computer Science.
