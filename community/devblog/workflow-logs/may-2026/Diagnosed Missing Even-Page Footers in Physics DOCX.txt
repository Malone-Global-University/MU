WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Diagnosed Missing Even-Page Footers in Physics DOCX

Date
2026-05-21

Area
MGU Physics High School DOCX generation and Word pagination/footer formatting

Problem
The generated physics DOCX displayed page numbers and footer content only on odd-numbered pages, while even-numbered pages appeared blank. This created inconsistent pagination and made the exported textbook appear partially unformatted in print-layout view.

Intended Goal
The DOCX should display consistent footer content and page numbering across all pages unless intentionally configured for book-style mirrored layouts. Odd and even pages should either both contain matching footer structures or deliberately contain mirrored variants.

Root Cause
The document was most likely generated or configured with Word’s “Different Odd & Even Pages” setting enabled while the even-page footer region remained empty or disconnected from the odd-page footer. Section breaks and unlinked footer inheritance may also have contributed to missing footer propagation between sections.

Steps Taken

1. Inspected the visible DOCX behavior and identified the symptom pattern as consistent with Word’s mirrored footer system.
2. Explained the interaction between odd/even footer settings, first-page settings, and section breaks within Microsoft Word.
3. Identified the likely structural issue: odd-page footer definitions existed while even-page footer definitions were blank or unlinked.
4. Documented the corrective workflow: inspect Header & Footer settings, disable “Different Odd & Even Pages” if unnecessary, or manually populate/link the even-page footer.
5. Noted that section breaks created during DOCX generation can independently break footer inheritance and require manual relinking.

Key Change
The session isolated the missing footer issue as a document-structure configuration problem rather than a rendering or content-generation failure.

Outcome
The likely cause of the missing even-page footers was successfully identified, and a reproducible correction path was established for future DOCX troubleshooting and QA review.

Validation
The diagnosis matched the exact visible symptom pattern: odd pages retained numbering while even pages lost footer content entirely. The proposed fix aligns with standard Microsoft Word behavior for mirrored-page layouts and section-based footer inheritance.

Rules Going Forward

* Always inspect generated DOCX files for odd/even footer consistency during pagination QA.
* Avoid enabling “Different Odd & Even Pages” unless the document intentionally uses mirrored book formatting.
* When section breaks are used, verify that footer linkage is preserved across sections.
* Include footer verification alongside contact-sheet and pagination checks in future DOCX validation workflows.
* Treat missing even-page footers as a structural formatting issue first, not a DOCX corruption issue.

Notes
The issue may have originated from book-style layout defaults embedded in the DOCX template or inherited through compile-library settings. Future improvements could include automated footer consistency checks during DOCX post-processing or Word-template normalization before compilation.
