Rules Going Forward

Freemasonry should be treated as a related but distinct subject, not as identical to the Illuminati. Modern Illuminati claims should be framed as mythology, conspiracy culture, symbolism, or political imagination unless supported by historical evidence. Related topic links should connect parent categories, historical subpages, source pages, and mythology pages without confusing them.
