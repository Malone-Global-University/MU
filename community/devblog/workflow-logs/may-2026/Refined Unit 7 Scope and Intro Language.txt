Title
Refined Unit 7 Scope and Intro Language

Date
2026-05-21

Area
Wealth and Asset Management Track Structure — Layer 2 Products / Activities

Problem
Unit 7 originally used the title “Asset Classes and Position Administration” with a short description focused primarily on listing asset categories. After renaming the unit to “Financial Instruments and Position Management,” the existing paragraph no longer fully reflected the upgraded operational and lifecycle-management focus shown in the lesson structure.

Intended Goal
Align Unit 7’s short intro paragraph with the revised title and lesson scope while preserving the concise descriptive style used throughout the original curriculum document.

Root Cause
The original paragraph was written around static asset classification concepts rather than operational instrument handling, lifecycle tracking, and portfolio position management workflows emphasized in the revised unit lessons.

Steps Taken

1. Reviewed the existing Layer 2 unit intro conventions to preserve consistent sentence length, structure, and tone.
2. Analyzed the revised Unit 7 lesson sequence to identify the operational themes emphasized across the unit.
3. Rewrote the Unit 7 intro paragraph to shift emphasis from asset categories alone toward position tracking, management, reconciliation, and lifecycle event handling.
4. Ensured the revised wording remained short enough to visually and structurally match neighboring unit descriptions.

Key Change
Unit 7 was reframed from a static asset-type overview into an operational portfolio position management and instrument lifecycle unit.

Outcome
The revised Unit 7 description now better matches the upgraded curriculum direction, aligns with the lesson content, and maintains stylistic consistency with the rest of the track structure document.

Validation
Compared the final Unit 7 paragraph against Units 5, 6, 8, and 9 for tone, sentence density, and formatting consistency. Confirmed the new wording reflected trade activity, lifecycle management, and operational infrastructure concepts introduced in the lessons.

Rules Going Forward
Future unit intro paragraphs should reflect the dominant operational purpose of the lessons rather than only listing categories or concepts. Concise structural consistency across units should remain a priority.

Notes
The revised Unit 7 now bridges Layer 2 product coverage more naturally into Layer 3 operational infrastructure topics such as portfolio accounting, reconciliation, pricing, and books-and-records systems.
