WORKFLOW NOTE

Title
Martial Arts Department Page Structure Expansion

Date
2026-05-21

Area
Defense Department / Martial Arts Division / Aikido, Karate, and Malone Ryu pages

Problem
The martial arts pages needed a clearer structure for presenting each fighting system. The existing pages identified the systems and related departments, but they did not yet provide a consistent middle layer for system-specific reference pages such as history, terminology, and techniques.

Intended Goal
Each martial arts homepage should clearly introduce the system, explain its role in the Defense Department, provide direct links into its own internal learning pages, and then connect outward to related departments.

Root Cause
The original structure treated related departments and system-specific pages too closely together. This made it harder to distinguish between internal study paths for a martial system and external connections to other Defense Department areas.

Steps Taken

1. Created markdown homepage drafts for Aikido, Karate, and Malone Ryu using the shared department template.
2. Added matching paragraph descriptions for the Related Departments section, including Situational Awareness, Private Security, and War College.
3. Built a Martial Arts Division block describing the division’s purpose, training focus, and pressure-tested methodology.
4. Defined a new section to sit between “Role in the Defense System” and “Related Departments” for system-specific links.
5. Added standardized link snippets for History, Terminology, and Techniques pages for Aikido, Karate, and Malone Ryu.

Key Change
A new internal system-pages layer was added so each martial art can link to its own history, terminology, techniques, and future study pages before connecting outward to related departments.

Outcome
The Martial Arts Division now has a cleaner and more scalable page architecture. Aikido, Karate, and Malone Ryu can each function as full learning systems with quick-reference pages while still fitting into the larger Defense Department structure.

Validation
The structure was checked against the existing page flow: identity, purpose, role in the system, internal system links, and related departments. The new snippets match the tone, length, and HTML pattern already used in the Martial Arts Taught section.

Rules Going Forward
Keep system-specific pages separate from related department links. Use the same list-and-paragraph format for all future martial arts. Include History, Terminology, and Techniques as core reference links for every fighting system added later.

Notes
This structure can later extend naturally to boxing, grappling, weapons systems, combatives, and other martial arts without changing the overall template.
