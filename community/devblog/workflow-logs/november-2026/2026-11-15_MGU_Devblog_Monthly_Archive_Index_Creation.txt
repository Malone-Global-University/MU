Title
MGU Devblog Monthly Archive Index Creation and Template Standardization

Date
2026-11-15

Area
Malone Global University (MGU) — Community Devblog

Affected directories:

- /community/devblog/design-notes/
- /community/devblog/technical-notes/
- /community/devblog/content-production/

Problem
Several Devblog monthly archive directories contained text records but lacked corresponding index.html pages. Consequently, records were not organized into accessible monthly listings, limiting navigation, discoverability, and historical documentation.

The initial November 2026 Design Notes index also used an HTML structure that did not match the established MGU Community template.

Additional monthly directories required consistent navigation, shared styling, and direct links to their existing text files.

Intended Goal
Establish a standardized monthly archive system in which every populated Devblog directory has an index.html page that:

- Uses the existing MGU Community design and shared components.
- Lists the month's text records with functional relative hyperlinks.
- Preserves original filenames without modification.
- Provides navigation to other monthly archives and Devblog categories.
- Supports additional records without requiring structural redesign.

Root Cause
The Devblog directory structure had expanded faster than its monthly navigation infrastructure.

Text records were being created and stored in categorized monthly folders, but corresponding HTML index pages were not consistently generated.

The initial November Design Notes page also demonstrated a template inconsistency: it used /component/lib.css rather than the appropriate Community stylesheet, /component/com.css.

A reusable monthly archive structure was therefore necessary to maintain consistency across Devblog categories.

Steps Taken

1. Reviewed the existing Workflow Logs archive template. Examined the November 2026 Workflow Logs HTML to identify the correct page structure, stylesheet, shared components, navigation, and footer implementation.

2. Corrected the November 2026 Design Notes page. Rebuilt the original index using /component/com.css, the shared header and footer, automatic breadcrumbs, bottom navigation, and the established five-section layout. Linked 2026-11-14 reorganizing notes.txt.

3. Established a simplified monthly template. Adopted the revised November Design Notes page supplied by the user as the standard for subsequent monthly indexes. The template supports a growing collection of records within each month.

4. Created the July 2026 Design Notes index. Generated design-notes/july-2026/index.html and linked 2026-07-28 Malone Doctrine Normative Code.txt.

5. Created the May 2026 Design Notes index. Generated design-notes/may-2026/index.html with four records:
   - 2026-05-05 Color Coded Header CSS.txt
   - 2026-05-12 mojibake.txt
   - 2026-05-14 topnav bar.txt
   - 2026-05-17 MGU ROADMAP.txt

6. Created the August 2026 Technical Notes index. Generated technical-notes/august-2026/index.html with:
   - 2026-08-17 Dictionary JSON creation.txt
   - 2026-08-19 Library Clean Up.txt

7. Updated the August Technical Notes index. Added 2026-05-22_Added_News_Department_Indices.txt without removing the existing August records. Because the additional record was dated May, it was identified separately from the August entries.

8. Created four Content Production monthly indexes.
   - April 2026: 1 linked record.
   - May 2026: 5 linked records.
   - June 2026: 4 linked records.
   - July 2026: 3 linked records.

9. Preserved exact filenames and relative link structure. Retained spaces, underscores, capitalization, and original extensions. The July Content Production file 2026-07-22_Malone_Doctrine_Resumes.txt.txt retained its double extension.

10. Packaged Content Production deliverables. Created content-production-indexes.zip, containing the four monthly directories and their generated index.html pages.

11. Reviewed generated HTML structure and references. Checked the page structures and file-link strings against the supplied filenames. Live-server testing and deployment were not performed.

Key Change
Standardized MGU Devblog monthly archive pages around one reusable HTML template, enabling consistent access to categorized development records without altering their original files.

Outcome
Eight distinct monthly index pages were created or corrected across three Devblog categories.

| Category | Monthly indexes | Linked records |
|---|---:|---:|
| Design Notes | 3 | 6 |
| Technical Notes | 1 | 3 |
| Content Production | 4 | 13 |
| Total | 8 | 22 |

The resulting archive structure provides:

- Consistent monthly page presentation.
- Direct access to individual text records.
- Shared MGU Community navigation.
- Standardized page organization across Devblog categories.
- A repeatable workflow for future monthly index generation.

The Content Production pages were additionally distributed as one ZIP package for convenient installation.

Validation
The following checks were incorporated into the page-generation process:

1. Template consistency: Pages retained /component/com.css and the established Community header, breadcrumb, bottom-navigation, and footer components.
2. Filename accuracy: Link targets were checked against the original filenames provided for each directory.
3. Extension preservation: Confirmed that unconventional filenames, including .txt.txt, were preserved.
4. Relative linking: Text-file hyperlinks were constructed for files located alongside their respective monthly index.html pages.
5. Navigation consistency: Monthly archive and Devblog category navigation were included.
6. Archive coverage: Confirmed the requested eight distinct monthly pages and 22 file references.

Validation limitation: These were structural and filename checks of generated files. Successful live-server deployment, browser rendering against production components, and HTTP availability of all 22 text files were not independently verified.

Rules Going Forward

1. Every populated monthly Devblog directory should contain an index.html page.
2. Use the simplified November 2026 Design Notes HTML as the standard monthly archive template.
3. Use /component/com.css for Community Devblog pages. Do not substitute /component/lib.css.
4. Preserve the site's established header, footer, breadcrumb, and bottom-navigation components.
5. Maintain consistent page sections: Purpose, Monthly Files, Archive Navigation, Connection to the Devblog, and Devblog Navigation.
6. Preserve original text filenames exactly, including capitalization, spaces, underscores, and file extensions.
7. Use properly encoded relative hyperlinks for filenames containing spaces.
8. Keep monthly file listings chronological when practical.
9. Add new records to existing monthly indexes rather than generating duplicate monthly pages.
10. Flag records whose filename dates do not match the archive month instead of silently changing their filenames.
11. Keep links between related monthly archives updated as additional pages are created.
12. Validate local file references and site navigation before publication.
13. Keep HTML index creation separate from editing the underlying historical work logs and notes.

Notes
The Devblog monthly archive infrastructure now has a repeatable implementation pattern for Design Notes, Technical Notes, and Content Production.

The process can be extended to Workflow Logs, Site Updates, Experiments, and other Devblog categories without establishing new visual conventions.

Recommended follow-up: Review the parent index.html pages for each Devblog category to ensure every newly created monthly archive is discoverable from its category landing page.

The May-dated technical note included in the August archive should also be evaluated for eventual placement in a May 2026 Technical Notes directory if chronological organization becomes a strict archival requirement.

Future monthly builds should use the directory's actual file inventory as the authoritative source for links, avoiding unnecessary changes to existing text records.
