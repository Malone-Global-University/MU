Sixth idea:

Standard lesson design
Every expanded lesson should use the same instructional architecture:
1. Lesson explanation: Plain-language instruction supported by examples and diagrams.
2. Interactive vocabulary: Hover or keyboard-focus definitions, with hyperlinks to canonical dictionary entries.
3. Encyclopedia connections: Links from key concepts to substantive articles.
4. Practical walkthrough: Numbered instructions, supported by relevant screenshots and tutorials.
5. Practice and assessment: Exercises, common mistakes, review questions, and a completion checklist.
Tooltips should work on mobile by tap and remain accessible by keyboard. Avoid excessive linking of repeated terms.

The governing development sequence should be: Dictionary → Encyclopedia → Lesson → Tutorial → Practice Lab, with the ability to move back and forth as new dependencies arise.

That will allow the division to grow into a coordinated learning system without requiring the entire library to be completed in advance.
