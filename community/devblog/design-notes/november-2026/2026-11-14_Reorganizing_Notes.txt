Site Updates: Major developments every 6-8 weeks

Instagram Posts: was once a week but now less, maybe once a every 3 weeks as I am working on bigger projects and do not feel like posting excessively. local worklogs: Typically when doing something technical or major planning, or running into a problem I need to work out a solution for. Once I solve the problem I document it and post it on devblog//worklogs as a reminder and historical milestone in case the problem happens again.

These are the three modes of documentation I am using and they overlap. I even have two types of worklogs: standardized worklogs given using AI prompts of chat sessions; and hand written notes that detail work done that day written in causal language to explain the why of what is happening, almost like Devblog announcements site updates pre drafts, or notes for creating an eventual site update.




I have three concerns:

1. Remembering to write notes if important.
2. Posting notes to make them public.
3. Posting notes to the correct section.

The last part is the hardest because a single work session usually multitasks many things. I will have to jot nots as working then split them into mini nots to post them in the correct area or else everything might get labeled as "content".

Because this deals with how the website "feels" I would consider this a "design note". When writing short causal notes they will most likely be "content" to explain the "why" of new teaching pages, or "design" to explain how I want to modify features and access.

Writing better notes will help me stay organized in the future. Then this is a design issue.

I will label such notes as "yyyy-mm-dd title.txt" so that they can be organized by date newest to earliest and still display information on what the note pertains to.
