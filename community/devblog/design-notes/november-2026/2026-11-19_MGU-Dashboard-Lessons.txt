MGU's first interactive lesson, "What Is a Computer?", is now live at https://maloneuniversity.org/lessons/?content=what-is-a-computer. The lesson dashboard is deployed and functioning correctly. This marks a major milestone in establishing MGU's centralized instructional delivery system.

I have decided to maintain a two-layer architecture for now. The `/department/` pages will serve as brochure-style academic navigation pages containing course and class information, with the ability to expand later to include metadata, descriptions, syllabi, and learning objectives. These pages will link to the centralized `/lessons/` dashboard, where the actual interactive lessons are delivered.

This is the simplest and most practical arrangement for the current development stage. It separates academic catalog information from instructional content, avoids duplication, and allows both systems to expand independently. Department pages explain what MGU teaches, while the lesson dashboard delivers the instruction.
