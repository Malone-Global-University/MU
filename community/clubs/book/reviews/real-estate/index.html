Real Estate Book Reviews

Critical reviews of books on property investment, analysis, acquisition, and management.

Welcome

The Real Estate section of the MGU Book Club evaluates books on residential and commercial property investing, property markets, acquisitions, development, financing, and operations. It is intended for readers studying the business of real estate, from introductory terminology to professional investment and asset-management practices.

Reviews assess the quality of underwriting methods, assumptions about income and expenses, treatment of financing and risk, and applicability to different property types and markets. They also identify areas in which market conditions, lending practices, or legal requirements may have changed since a book was published.

Subjects

  • Commercial Real Estate: Property types, acquisitions, leasing, institutional practices, and transactions.
  • Market Analysis & Valuation: Demand, comparables, income analysis, cap rates, and property pricing.
  • Financing & Investment Strategy: Capital structures, investor returns, leverage, and risk.
  • Property & Asset Management: Operations, maintenance, tenants, financial oversight, and performance.
