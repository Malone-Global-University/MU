Investing Book Reviews

Reviews of books on investment analysis, capital allocation, risk, and asset management.

Welcome

The Investing section of the MGU Book Club evaluates books dealing with investment decisions and the ownership, financing, and management of assets. Its coverage includes securities analysis, portfolio construction, asset allocation, risk, market behavior, and long-term capital management.

Reviews assess analytical rigor, the assumptions behind proposed strategies, treatment of uncertainty and downside risk, and the extent to which historical performance is presented responsibly. Investment ideas are evaluated as educational arguments rather than treated as guarantees of future results.

Subjects

  • Securities & Valuation: Stocks, bonds, business valuation, financial ratios, and fundamental analysis.
  • Portfolio Construction: Diversification, asset allocation, investment horizons, and rebalancing.
  • Risk & Market Behavior: Volatility, credit risk, behavioral finance, and investment mistakes.
  • Institutional Investing: Funds, investment mandates, asset management, and capital allocation.
