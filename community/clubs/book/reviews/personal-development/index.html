Personal Development Book Reviews

Reviews of books on learning, habits, communication, productivity, and personal growth.

Welcome

The Personal Development section of the MGU Book Club reviews works about learning, self-improvement, communication, goal-setting, productivity, and the development of practical skills. It covers both research-informed treatments of behavior and more experience-based approaches to individual development.

Reviews examine the credibility of a book’s claims, the quality of evidence supporting proposed techniques, and the feasibility of applying its recommendations. They distinguish systematic research from anecdotes and assess whether advice accommodates different circumstances and objectives.

Subjects

  • Learning & Study Skills: Comprehension, memory, self-directed education, and deliberate practice.
  • Habits & Productivity: Planning, time management, routines, and personal effectiveness.
  • Communication & Relationships: Writing, speaking, listening, negotiation, and interpersonal skills.
  • Personal Leadership: Judgment, resilience, goal-setting, and decision-making.
