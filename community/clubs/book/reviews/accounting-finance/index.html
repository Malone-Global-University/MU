Accounting & Finance Book Reviews

Critical evaluations of books on financial reporting, banking, valuation, and financial decision-making.

Welcome

The Accounting & Finance section of the MGU Book Club features critical reviews of books covering accounting principles, corporate finance, commercial banking, financial analysis, and the institutions that manage capital. These works contribute to financial literacy and to understanding how businesses, lenders, and other financial organizations operate.

Reviews assess the accuracy and clarity of explanations, the quality of case studies and supporting evidence, and the usefulness of methods presented for academic study and professional practice. When relevant, assessments distinguish durable principles from regulations, market conditions, and techniques that may have changed since publication.

Subjects

  • Financial Accounting: Financial statements, reporting standards, managerial accounting, and interpretation of financial information.
  • Corporate Finance & Valuation: Capital structure, valuation methods, financial planning, mergers, and acquisitions.
  • Banking & Financial Institutions: Commercial banking, financial intermediaries, fintech, risk management, and institutional conduct.
  • Financial Markets: Credit markets, instruments, liquidity, and financial-system infrastructure.
