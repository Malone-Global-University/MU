Business & Management Book Reviews

Reviews of books on entrepreneurship, organizations, management, and corporate strategy.

Welcome

The Business & Management section of the MGU Book Club examines works concerning the formation, organization, operation, and governance of enterprises. Its coverage includes entrepreneurship, leadership, business strategy, management systems, organizational behavior, and the choices facing business owners and executives.

Reviews evaluate the strength of a book’s arguments, the reliability of its evidence, and the practical constraints that may affect the application of its advice. Attention is given to the difference between general management principles, industry-specific practices, and claims based primarily on individual success stories.

Subjects

  • Entrepreneurship: Business formation, startup planning, business models, and growth.
  • Management & Leadership: Organizational design, decision-making, people management, and leadership.
  • Strategy & Operations: Competitive strategy, execution, process improvement, and performance.
  • Corporate Governance: Boards, executive accountability, organizational controls, and institutional stewardship.
