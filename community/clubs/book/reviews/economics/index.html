Economics Book Reviews

Reviews of books on economic theory, monetary systems, policy, and economic history.

Welcome

The Economics section of the MGU Book Club gathers reviews of books explaining how economies function, how resources are allocated, and how economic institutions and public policies affect households, firms, and societies. Its scope ranges from foundational theory to monetary economics, financial history, and modern policy debates.

Reviews examine analytical assumptions, the use of economic data, historical evidence, and the relationship between theoretical models and observed outcomes. They distinguish descriptive analysis from normative policy recommendations and consider whether a book’s conclusions follow from the evidence presented.

Subjects

  • Microeconomics: Incentives, pricing, firms, consumers, markets, and competition.
  • Macroeconomics: Growth, unemployment, inflation, recessions, and aggregate demand.
  • Money & Monetary Policy: Central banking, interest rates, monetary transmission, and financial stability.
  • Economic History & Policy: Historical change, inequality, institutions, and public-policy choices.
