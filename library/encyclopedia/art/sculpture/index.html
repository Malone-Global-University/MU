Sculpture Overview

Sculpture studies the creation of three-dimensional artistic forms through carving, modeling, casting, construction, and spatial installation. It combines material understanding, physical craftsmanship, structural awareness, and aesthetic design. Across stone, metal, wood, clay, ceramics, and installation work, the discipline examines how artists shape mass, volume, texture, balance, and space into physical artistic presence.

Disciplines

  • Stone Sculpture

    Study carving and shaping stone through chiseling, abrasion, reduction methods, material properties, and structural planning.

  • Metal Sculpture

    Learn sculptural fabrication through welding, casting, forging, assembly, finishing, and structural metal construction.

  • Wood Sculpture

    Examine carving and construction in wood through cutting, shaping, grain control, assembly, and surface finishing.

  • Clay and Ceramics

    Study modeling, firing, glazing, and ceramic form creation through clay-based sculptural and vessel-making techniques.

  • Installation Art

    Explore large-scale spatial works designed to transform environments through immersive arrangement, context, and audience interaction.
