Operations Overview

Operations studies the systems, processes, resources, and controls that organizations use to produce goods and deliver services. It focuses on workflow, capacity, quality, supply chains, logistics, procurement, and continuous improvement. Across process design, supply chain management, logistics, procurement, quality management, and business process optimization, operations connects strategy with the practical execution of organizational work.

Disciplines

  • Process Design

    Study the structure of workflows through inputs, outputs, sequence, capacity, bottlenecks, controls, and operational logic.

  • Supply Chain Management

    Examine coordinated movement of materials, information, suppliers, production, inventory, distribution, and customer fulfillment.

  • Logistics

    Learn transportation, warehousing, routing, distribution, inventory movement, delivery coordination, and physical flow management.

  • Procurement

    Study sourcing, supplier selection, purchasing, contract management, vendor relationships, cost control, and supply reliability.

  • Quality Management

    Explore systems for maintaining standards through inspection, process control, improvement methods, defect reduction, and reliability.

  • Business Process Optimization

    Examine improvement of organizational workflows through measurement, redesign, automation, simplification, efficiency, and performance gains.
