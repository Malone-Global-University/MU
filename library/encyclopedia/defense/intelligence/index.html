Intelligence Overview

Intelligence studies the collection, processing, analysis, and dissemination of information used to support military and national security decision-making. The field examines structured methods of gathering information from human, technical, and geospatial sources while protecting against adversary intelligence efforts. Intelligence disciplines provide situational awareness and strategic insight for defense operations.

Disciplines

  • Counterintelligence

    Study defensive measures against espionage, infiltration, and intelligence compromise.

  • Geospatial Intelligence

    Examine spatial and imagery-based intelligence derived from geographic information.

  • Human Intelligence

    Study intelligence gathered through interpersonal sources and human collection methods.

  • Intelligence Analysis

    Learn structured interpretation and synthesis of collected intelligence information.

  • Intelligence Cycle

    Study the end-to-end process of intelligence planning, collection, analysis, and dissemination.

  • Signals Intelligence

    Examine interception and analysis of electronic communications and signal emissions.
