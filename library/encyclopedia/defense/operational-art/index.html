Operational Art Overview

Operational art studies the planning and conduct of campaigns and major operations that connect strategic objectives to tactical actions. The field examines how forces are organized, positioned, supplied, and directed across theaters to achieve strategic outcomes through coordinated military action. It serves as the bridge between high-level strategy and battlefield tactics.

Disciplines

  • Battlespace Design

    Study spatial and temporal arrangement of forces and effects within operational environments.

  • Campaign Planning

    Examine structured design of military campaigns across multiple operations.

  • Force Employment

    Study allocation and use of military forces for operational objectives.

  • Operational Logistics

    Examine sustainment planning and logistical support at operational scale.

  • Theater Operations

    Study coordination of military activity across broad geographic theaters.
