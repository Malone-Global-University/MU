Navigation and Surveying Overview

Navigation and surveying study the measurement, positioning, mapping, and traversal of geographic space through mathematical, observational, and technological methods. The field examines coordinate frameworks, land measurement, geodesy, navigation systems, and positional reference techniques used for accurate spatial determination.
