Country Overview

Nepal is a landlocked South Asian federal parliamentary republic between India and China, extending from the lowland Terai through middle hills to the high Himalayas. It abolished its monarchy in 2008 and adopted a federal constitution in 2015. The March 2026 general election produced a decisive House of Representatives majority for the Rastriya Swatantra Party, followed by Balendra Shah’s appointment as prime minister. Nepal combines agriculture, tourism, hydropower and remittance-dependent household incomes; extreme topographic differences create both biodiversity and exposure to earthquakes, floods and landslides.
