Country Overview

Kyrgyzstan is a landlocked mountainous republic in Central Asia bordered by Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and China. Its Tian Shan ranges, high-altitude pastures and Lake Issyk-Kul shape settlement, rural livelihoods, biodiversity and tourism. It gained independence from the Soviet Union in 1991 and operates under a presidential constitutional system introduced in 2021. The economy combines services, mining, agriculture, construction, hydropower and remittances, with significant dependence on regional markets and cross-border infrastructure. The November 2025 parliamentary election renewed its 90-seat legislature.
