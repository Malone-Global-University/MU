Country Overview

Türkiye is a transcontinental republic spanning Anatolia and southeastern Europe, with strategic control of the Bosporus and Dardanelles between the Black Sea and Mediterranean basin. Its diversified economy includes manufacturing, services, tourism, agriculture, transportation and construction. The present presidential executive system was introduced following constitutional changes; 2025 population and GDP figures differ slightly across national registration, revised national accounts and international World Bank series.
