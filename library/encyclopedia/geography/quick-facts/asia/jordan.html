Country Overview

Jordan is a constitutional hereditary monarchy in Western Asia, bordering Syria, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Israel and the West Bank. The Hashemite Kingdom has a predominantly arid landscape, a short Red Sea coastline at Aqaba, and important historical sites at Petra, Jerash and Wadi Rum. Its economy combines services, tourism, manufacturing, phosphate and potash production, and external trade. Water scarcity, labor-market pressures and refugee-hosting commitments are major policy issues. King Abdullah II remains head of state and Prime Minister Jafar Hassan leads the cabinet.

Sources and Methodology

Jordan’s 2015 Department of Statistics census is a counted population and should not be substituted for the World Bank 2025 resident estimate. UNHCR end-2025 registrations, UNRWA Palestine-refugee records and resident estimates have different coverage and cannot simply be summed. The World Bank modeled unemployment statistic is not the same indicator as the IMF’s government labor-force statistic. WTO 2024 merchandise trade excludes tourism, remittances and other services.

Editorial cutoff: November 6, 2026 (Pacific Time). Official data and international series have different observation dates, definitions, and publication lags.
