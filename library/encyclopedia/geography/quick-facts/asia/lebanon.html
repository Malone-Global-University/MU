Country Overview

Lebanon is a Mediterranean country of Western Asia whose coastal cities, mountains and Beqaa Valley connect the Levant with wider regional trading networks. The state has a parliamentary republic shaped by a complex religious power-sharing system. Lebanese society is multilingual and maintains extensive international diaspora links, while banking distress, currency depreciation, displacement and armed conflict have complicated economic and institutional recovery since 2019. The national population is estimated rather than measured through a recent census; its current borders and security environment demand careful sourcing.
