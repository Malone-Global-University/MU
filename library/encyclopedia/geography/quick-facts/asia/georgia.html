Country Overview

Georgia is a South Caucasus state on the Black Sea, classified by the United Nations in Western Asia and closely connected to European political institutions. It restored independence in 1991 and operates under a parliamentary constitutional system. A diverse landscape ranges from alpine Greater Caucasus mountains to subtropical coastal wetlands. Economic growth, regional trade and tourism coexist with political confrontation over governance and EU integration. Abkhazia and the Tskhinvali/South Ossetia region remain outside Tbilisi’s effective control, and demographic census statistics exclude these territories. [1] [4] [16]

Sources and Methodology

Entries are a source-anchored reference compilation, not a single statistical extract. Census counts, administrative population figures and international estimates are separate series; nominal GDP is not purchasing-power-parity output. WTO, UNCTAD and national customs trade series can differ by definition, coverage and reporting period. Regional classification follows the United Nations M49 statistical convention rather than asserting exclusive cultural or political geography. UNESCO inscribed, tentative and intangible heritage are distinct categories.

Editorial cutoff: November 6, 2026 (Pacific Time). A dated statistic describes its reference period, not necessarily its publication date. Disputed or internationally contested governance claims are described with their attribution and jurisdiction. The page may require later revision when new releases or events occur.
