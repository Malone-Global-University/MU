Country Overview

Kuwait is a small, highly urbanized Arabian Gulf state occupying the northern end of the Gulf, bordered by Iraq and Saudi Arabia. Its economy is shaped by petroleum reserves, a major refining sector, public financial assets and a large expatriate workforce. The Al Sabah family has ruled since the eighteenth century. Since May 2024, the dissolution of the National Assembly and temporary suspension of specified constitutional provisions have altered the country’s ordinary legislative arrangements. Kuwait is a Gulf Cooperation Council and OPEC member whose climate, water supply, and public revenues are tied to energy and environmental conditions.
