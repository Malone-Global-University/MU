Country Overview

Malaysia is a Southeast Asian federation divided between the Malay Peninsula and northern Borneo. A constitutional monarchy with an elected federal parliament, it combines advanced electronics and export manufacturing with services, agricultural commodities, energy production and tourism. Its multiethnic population, longstanding maritime commercial networks and highly diverse tropical environments shape an economy integrated into ASEAN and global supply chains. Kuala Lumpur is the national capital, while Putrajaya is the federal administrative center.
