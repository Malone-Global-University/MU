Country Overview

The Kingdom of Cambodia is a constitutional monarchy in mainland Southeast Asia centred on the lower Mekong and Tonle Sap basin. Its internationally recognized cultural patrimony includes Angkor, the early Khmer archaeological sites, and memorial locations connected to the country’s traumatic twentieth-century history. An export-focused economy anchored in garments, footwear and other manufacturing faces headwinds from subdued domestic demand, financial conditions and regional border tensions. Prime Minister Hun Manet heads the government, while King Norodom Sihamoni is head of state. [11] [4] [9]

People and Society

Official and majority language
Khmer is the official national language and the principal language of everyday life. [1] [6]
Khmer writing system
The Cambodian language uses the Khmer script, developed over a long literary history. [11]
Other languages
Vietnamese, Chinese varieties and minority languages are spoken by distinct communities. [6]
Religious tradition
Theravada Buddhism is central to the country’s public religious and cultural life. [11]
Monasteries
Buddhist temples and monastic institutions play important educational, cultural and community roles. [11]
Other religions
Muslim Cham, Christian and other religious communities add to national diversity. [6] [10]
Khmer cultural history
Angkor-era architecture, inscriptions, performance and material culture are major heritage foundations. [11]
Angkor Wat
Part of the Angkor World Heritage site, a symbolic monument of Cambodian cultural identity. [11]
Angkor Thom
An Angkorian urban and monumental complex included within the wider Angkor heritage landscape. [11]
Preah Vihear
Temple complex on a mountain escarpment and UNESCO World Heritage property since 2008. [11]
Sambor Prei Kuk
Archaeological site of ancient Ishanapura, inscribed on the World Heritage List in 2017. [11]
Koh Ker
Ancient Lingapura / Chok Gargyar archaeological complex, inscribed in 2023. [11]
