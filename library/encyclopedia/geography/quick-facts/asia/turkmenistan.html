Country Overview

Turkmenistan is a landlocked Central Asian republic with a Caspian Sea coastline, the Karakum Desert and substantial natural-gas reserves. Its economy is strongly influenced by state-owned energy activities and public investment, while cotton, textiles, transport and trade provide additional sectors. The modern state emerged from Soviet independence in 1991 and maintains a declared policy of permanent neutrality. Because official statistics have coverage and transparency limitations, this profile distinguishes national census counts, World Bank estimates and reported export figures.

Data and status note: The December 2022 official census enumerated 7,057,841 residents; the World Bank’s 2025 model estimates 7,618,847. The World Bank reports US$49.83 billion in 2025 nominal GDP and 6.3% growth, while the UN country team reports 2025 total exports of US$11.2 billion and gas production of 76.5 billion cubic meters. The WTO public profile still displays 2000-era goods-trade figures: they are deliberately excluded from present-day trade tables. The UNESCO list contains five inscribed properties (four cultural and one natural).

Sources & Methodology

The official 2022 census and World Bank 2025 population estimates are different series. World Bank economic indicators, UN country-team exports and EBRD assessment are cited separately; the WTO public Turkmenistan trade profile remains exceptionally outdated and is not a valid source for present-day trade values. Political reporting is current to the cited 2026 officeholder list. UNESCO’s five inscribed properties are distinct from nine tentative candidates and separate intangible heritage listings.
