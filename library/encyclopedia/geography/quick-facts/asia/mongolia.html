Country Overview

Mongolia is a landlocked East Asian country between the Russian Federation and China, with a long tradition of pastoral mobility and a mineral-export economy. The country is a multiparty parliamentary democracy with an elected president, a prime minister accountable to the State Great Khural, and significant urban concentration around Ulaanbaatar. Coal, copper and other minerals shape international trade, while herding, dzud winters, desertification and air pollution shape its development challenges. Figures below distinguish the World Bank 2025 population estimate, Mongolia’s own population series and merchandise-trade reporting.
