Country Overview

Israel is a parliamentary state on the eastern Mediterranean littoral, established in 1948 and home to Jewish and Arab communities with significant religious, linguistic and cultural diversity. Its economy includes technology-intensive services, industry, agriculture and international trade. As of November 6, 2026, no coalition had yet been formed after the October 27 parliamentary election, and the preceding Netanyahu government continued in a caretaker capacity. Jerusalem, the occupied Palestinian territories and the occupied Golan Heights require careful distinction between Israeli administrative claims and international legal positions.

Data and status note: Israel’s national statistical agency estimated approximately 10.305 million residents in September 2026, compared with the World Bank’s 10.123 million estimate for 2025; these reflect different dates and statistical series. The national statistical series includes some territories under Israeli administration whose sovereignty is disputed internationally. Israel designates Jerusalem as its capital, but the status of East Jerusalem is disputed and subject to UN resolutions. The West Bank and Gaza must not be treated as sovereign Israeli territory. Netanyahu continued as prime minister in a caretaker role after the inconclusive October 2026 election.

Sources and Methodology

Annual population estimates, national CBS totals and the 2022 census are distinct statistical series. Israel’s official population publications may include East Jerusalem and the occupied Golan Heights in the national resident total; international data may use different territorial coverage. Capital and geographic details distinguish state claims from international legal status. WTO merchandise figures exclude services; Israeli high-technology services contribute separately to external receipts. World Bank GDP is nominal current-US-dollar data, and IMF 2026 values are projections. The UNESCO Israel World Heritage count excludes the Old City of Jerusalem and its Walls, which is recorded separately in UNESCO’s listing following a Jordanian nomination.

Editorial cutoff: November 6, 2026 (Pacific Time). All stated population counts, prices, elections, and legal statuses have specific reference dates or qualifications. This page should be updated following major new statistical releases or changes of government.
