Country Overview

Myanmar, also known as Burma, is a Southeast Asian country spanning the Irrawaddy basin, Shan Plateau, border highlands and the Andaman Sea and Bay of Bengal. Its contemporary institutions cannot be described accurately without the February 2021 military coup, the subsequent armed conflict and the contested December 2025–January 2026 elections. Former military commander Min Aung Hlaing became president in April 2026 under a military-dominated government; opposition parties were excluded or dissolved, and large areas remained outside consistent central control. The country has rich ethnolinguistic traditions and agricultural, energy and manufacturing activity, but conflict, displacement and the March 2025 earthquake have disrupted livelihoods and statistical collection.

Sources and Methodology

Methodological and political-source note: the Union 2024 final census includes directly enumerated and estimated non-enumerated areas; its publisher is a state authority under military-dominated governance. The World Bank supplies a different 2025 population series and calendar GDP. The 2025–26 elections excluded significant territory and opposition groups, and official officeholder titles should not be treated as proof of democratic legitimacy. UNHCR refugee and IDP populations differ and change frequently. WTO exports are for 2024 but imports for 2025; no cross-year implied trade balance. UNESCO has separate heritage registries.
