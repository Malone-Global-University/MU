Country Overview

Timor-Leste is a democratic republic in maritime Southeast Asia occupying eastern Timor, the Oe-Cusse enclave and nearby islands. Independent since 2002 following an Indonesian occupation and a UN-supported political transition, it became the eleventh member of ASEAN on October 26, 2025. Public expenditure, the Petroleum Fund, subsistence agriculture, coffee and emerging services shape an economy seeking to diversify beyond the former Bayu-Undan offshore production.
