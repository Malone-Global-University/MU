Country Overview

Sri Lanka is an island republic south of India in the Indian Ocean with an economy shaped by trade, manufacturing, tourism, plantation agriculture and remittances. Its elected executive president and 225-member parliament operate amid the continuing recovery from the 2022 debt and balance-of-payments crisis. The governing National People’s Power (NPP) alliance won a strong parliamentary majority in November 2024. The 2024 population census has a finalized total published after its preliminary release. This profile separates census enumerations, World Bank estimates, goods exports, services exports, and different historical names and institutional functions of Colombo and Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte.

The World Bank estimates 21,756,000 for 2025, a separate model series. World Bank current-dollar 2025 GDP is US$108.83 billion. Central Bank 2025 provisional goods exports were US$13.5814 billion and imports US$21.4799 billion; adding services exports produces a larger total not comparable with merchandise-only figures. Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte is the legislative capital, while Colombo remains the main commercial centre and hosts numerous state offices.

Sources and Methodology

MGU Quick Facts distinguishes final and provisional census data, annual model estimates, nominal GDP from purchasing-power values, and merchandise from services trade. The 2024 census final total supersedes the preliminary publication; 2025 trade figures are expressly provisional. World Heritage is a register of inscribed sites, not a count of intangible elements or tentative nominations. Current government descriptions are dated to November 2026.

Editorial cutoff: November 6, 2026 (Pacific Time). All stated population counts, prices, elections, and legal statuses have specific reference dates or qualifications. This page should be updated following major new statistical releases or changes of government.
