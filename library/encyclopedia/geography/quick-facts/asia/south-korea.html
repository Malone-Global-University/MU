Country Overview

South Korea, formally the Republic of Korea, occupies the southern part of the Korean Peninsula and is a major advanced industrial and technological economy. Seoul is the national capital and the center of a metropolitan region housing more than half of the population. The country is a constitutional presidential republic led by President Lee Jae-myung, elected in June 2025 after the removal of his predecessor; Han Seong-sook took office as prime minister in July 2026. Its economy is especially prominent in semiconductors, automobiles, shipbuilding and digital services. This reference profile explicitly separates the national 2025 census from World Bank population estimates, identifies merchandise trade by reporting year, and presents the North–South Korea division and heritage inscriptions with precise qualifications.

Sources and Methodology
