Country Overview

Tajikistan is a mountainous Central Asian republic whose economy is closely linked to labor migration, remittances, agriculture, metals, hydropower and regional trade. It borders Afghanistan, China, Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan, and the Pamir Mountains dominate its east. President Emomali Rahmon has led the state for decades, while Prime Minister Qohir Rasulzoda heads the cabinet. The 2025 legislative elections retained a bicameral parliament amid longstanding international concerns about electoral competition and civil liberties. This profile distinguishes World Bank annual estimates from censuses, separates current-year GDP from the older WTO merchandise-trade observation, and reports the five currently inscribed UNESCO World Heritage properties.

Sources and Methodology

MGU Quick Facts compares explicitly dated national political, World Bank annual and WTO historical figures. The 2025 GDP, population and remittance data do not share the same reference year as the WTO detailed 2023 merchandise profile. Regional geography, census methods and government classifications can differ; the UNESCO World Heritage total excludes tentative and intangible items. Some political and rights conditions are contested; institutional and independent reporting are distinguished.
