Country Overview

Laos, officially the Lao People’s Democratic Republic, is a landlocked Southeast Asian state whose mountainous landscape, Mekong River corridor, and transport connections link China, Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia and Myanmar. A Lao People’s Revolutionary Party-led socialist republic established in 1975, the country combines agriculture, hydropower, mining, manufacturing and services. Infrastructure investment and cross-border trade provide opportunities but external debt and climate variability constrain development. The February 2026 National Assembly election was followed by leadership reappointments in March and an important prime-ministerial change in October 2026.

Sources and Methodology

Laos population and GDP are dated World Bank 2025 measures. The WTO merchandise partner/product profile reports 2023, so no 2025 figure is inferred from it. Political data from the February IPU election, March legislative sitting, and October 5 Lao News Agency appointments are reconciled chronologically to avoid outdated names. The United Nations officially lists November 24, 2026 as the scheduled future graduation date from LDC status. UNESCO World Heritage currently lists four properties including the natural transboundary Hin Nam No addition, while intangible heritage registers three separate traditions.
