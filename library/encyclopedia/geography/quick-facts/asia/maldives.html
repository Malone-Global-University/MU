Country Overview

The Maldives is a low-lying Indian Ocean republic composed of coral atolls southwest of India and Sri Lanka. Its people are concentrated in the Greater Malé area and inhabited island communities, while tourism islands, fisheries, foreign employment and imported energy shape the economy. The 2008 constitutional settlement established a presidential system with an elected People’s Majlis. Sea-level rise, coastal erosion, fiscal pressures and dependence on tourism and imported supplies remain major structural policy concerns.

Published official 2022 census summaries differ slightly across revisions (515,122 and 515,132), so this page does not treat those versions as identical. WTO merchandise imports/exports are 2024 and exclude international tourist services, the country’s major external earnings channel. The Maldives has no inscribed UNESCO World Heritage properties; its Coral Stone Mosques remain on the tentative list.
