Country Overview

Viet Nam is a Southeast Asian country extending along the eastern Indochinese Peninsula from the Red River Delta to the Mekong Delta. Its economic transformation since Đổi Mới has combined one-party socialist institutions with a globally integrated export-manufacturing sector. In 2026, Tô Lâm holds both the Communist Party general secretaryship and state presidency, while Lê Minh Hưng leads the government. The country’s 2025 administrative consolidation reshaped its province-level map.

The 2025 reduction to 34 province-level units supersedes older references to 63.
