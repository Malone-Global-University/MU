Country Overview

Uzbekistan is a doubly landlocked Central Asian republic connecting the historic Silk Road cities of Samarkand, Bukhara, and Khiva with the densely populated Fergana Valley and the Aral Sea basin. The country has undertaken market-oriented reforms while managing water scarcity, migration, energy-system changes, and rapid population growth. Its 2026 census-related population figures are presented separately from the World Bank’s 2025 model.

Sources & Methodology

The permanent-population count updated after the 2026 census, the initial 2026 census count and the World Bank 2025 model are not interchangeable. The 2025 national foreign-trade figures are a preliminary mixed goods-and-services measure; comparing them directly with WTO merchandise-only data can mislead. Constitutional seat counts refer to statutory sizes; currently occupied seats can be fewer. UNESCO inscriptions and tentative nominations are separate categories.
