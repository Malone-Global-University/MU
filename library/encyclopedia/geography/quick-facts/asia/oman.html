Country Overview

Oman is an Arabian Peninsula state with coastlines on the Gulf of Oman and Arabian Sea and a strategic position beside the Strait of Hormuz. The Sultanate is governed by Sultan Haitham bin Tarik under its Basic Law, with an appointed State Council and an elected Shura Council. Oil, liquefied natural gas, shipping, tourism and expanding manufacturing shape the economy; Oman Vision 2040 guides diversification. National population counts include a substantial expatriate workforce and differ from international intercensal estimates.

Sources and Methodology

Country references prioritize Oman’s Foreign Ministry and National Centre for Statistics and Information alongside World Bank, WTO/UN Comtrade, IPU, IMF, and UNESCO. September 2026 registered residents are not directly interchangeable with World Bank annual model estimates. GDP is current-dollar nominal output, not PPP; WTO goods statistics represent 2024 rather than the World Bank’s 2025 economic year. Council information is subject to royal decree and membership changes, and the 2023 election’s 90 seats are the best-dated reference for the elected Shura chamber.
