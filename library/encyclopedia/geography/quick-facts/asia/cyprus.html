Country Overview

The Republic of Cyprus is an independent Eastern Mediterranean island state classified by the United Nations in Western Asia and a member of the European Union since 2004. A constitutional presidential republic, it has an internationally recognized government that exercises effective authority principally in the island’s south; the north is administered separately and divided by a UN-monitored buffer zone. Tourism, information services, maritime activities and international business contribute heavily to its economy. Its official economic and population statistics are particularly sensitive to differences between islandwide coverage and government-controlled areas. [3] [15] [6]

Sources and Methodology

Editorial cutoff: November 6, 2026 (Pacific Time). A dated statistic describes its reference period, not necessarily its publication date. Disputed or internationally contested governance claims are described with their attribution and jurisdiction. The page may require later revision when new releases or events occur.
