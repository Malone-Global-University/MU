Country Overview

Singapore is a sovereign city-state at the southern tip of the Malay Peninsula. It combines a global maritime and aviation gateway, advanced manufacturing, financial services and an exceptionally internationalized economy within a compact urban territory. Governance is a parliamentary republic led by Prime Minister Lawrence Wong, with President Tharman Shanmugaratnam serving as head of state. Its population is differentiated into citizens, permanent residents and non-residents; the government reported 6.2085 million total inhabitants at end-June 2026. Singapore has one UNESCO World Heritage property, the Botanic Gardens, and a separate UNESCO intangible-heritage inscription for hawker culture.

Sources and Methodology

Population figures follow the national June 2026 register/administrative estimate, not a single new full census. The 6.2085 million total includes non-residents while 4.2315 million residents comprise citizens and permanent residents. Ethnic and age composition measures are resident-only and cannot be projected onto all non-residents. World Bank nominal GDP (US$603.87 billion) is a 2025 current-dollar estimate; Singapore national goods trade (S$1,398 billion) is calendar 2025 and includes domestic exports and re-exports. The 2025 election produced 97 constituency members, distinct from other legislative categories. One UNESCO World Heritage property is inscribed; hawker culture is intangible, and the Padang is tentative.
