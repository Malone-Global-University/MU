Country Overview

Thailand is a mainland Southeast Asian constitutional monarchy with a diversified economy in manufacturing, tourism, agriculture, trade and services. The country contains the Chao Phraya basin, northern highlands, northeastern Khorat Plateau and two distinct peninsular coastlines. Following the February 2026 House election, Anutin Charnvirakul was appointed prime minister again in March with a Bhumjaithai-led coalition. This country profile uses dated World Bank GDP and population series, identifies the WTO goods-trade profile as calendar 2024, and incorporates UNESCO’s ninth property inscribed in 2026. The monarchy, bicameral legislature and the distinct indirectly selected Senate are described separately.

Data and status note: The World Bank reports 71,619,863 residents and US$577.01 billion in 2025 nominal GDP, with 2.4% real growth. Thailand’s official civil-registration counts and international model estimates can differ in methodology. The February 8, 2026 House election returned a 500-seat assembly, with Bhumjaithai winning 192 seats and the People’s Party 120; Anutin was appointed prime minister on March 19. The Senate was selected indirectly in 2024 and is not a second directly elected 500-seat chamber. The WTO detailed merchandise profile lists exports of US$300.7594 billion and imports of US$301.4667 billion in 2024, while UNESCO now counts nine properties (six cultural, three natural).
