Country Overview

North Korea, formally the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea (DPRK), occupies the northern portion of the Korean Peninsula. It is a highly centralized one-party state led by Kim Jong Un, with nuclear and missile programs subject to United Nations Security Council sanctions. The population, economy, and humanitarian picture are unusually difficult to measure independently: this profile labels internationally modeled demographic figures and South Korean external estimates instead of presenting them as audited DPRK national statistics.

Data and status note: North Korea publishes limited and selectively accessible official statistics. The World Bank/UN 2025 population estimate of 26,571,036 differs from the South Korean statistical authority’s roughly 25.87 million estimate for 2024. The Bank of Korea’s 2025 real GDP growth estimate of 3.5%, GNI and customs-trade totals are independent external reconstructions; UNData currency-converted GDP is methodologically distinct, not a confirmed national account. The 2026 party congress, assembly and leadership appointments supersede older leadership lists. UNESCO lists three inscribed properties, including Mount Kumgang (2025).

Sources and Methodology

Statistics rely on United Nations models, South Korea’s Bank of Korea and Statistics Korea, humanitarian agencies, UNESCO, and clearly dated reporting. DPRK national statistics are often unavailable or not independently verifiable. The Bank of Korea real-growth, GNI and goods-trade values are external estimates; do not mix them with UNData US-dollar GDP without explicitly qualifying conversion and methodology. North Korean official texts and alternative romanizations vary. Human-rights conclusions are attributed to the United Nations and cited monitoring organizations; this is not a claim of direct independent inspection.

Editorial cutoff: November 6, 2026 (Pacific Time). All stated population counts, prices, elections, and legal statuses have specific reference dates or qualifications. This page should be updated following major new statistical releases or changes of government.
