Country Overview

Pakistan is a populous federal parliamentary republic in South Asia, bordered by Afghanistan, Iran, China and India and fronting the Arabian Sea. Its economy combines agriculture, textiles, services, remittances and manufacturing, while the Indus basin and mountain systems are central to its physical geography. The state has four provinces and distinct arrangements in Islamabad, Gilgit-Baltistan and Pakistan-administered Kashmir. This reference separates its enumerated 2023 census count, World Bank 2025 demographic and GDP estimates, 2024 WTO merchandise figures, and IMF fiscal-year projections.

The World Bank’s US$407.31 billion 2025 nominal GDP and 3.7% calendar-year growth are not the same reporting basis as IMF Pakistan fiscal-year estimates. Pakistan’s November 2025 Twenty-Seventh Amendment established a Federal Constitutional Court, changing the judiciary’s organization; pre-2025 descriptions of the Supreme Court’s constitutional jurisdiction may be outdated. The 2024 WTO goods trade figures are distinct from Pakistan fiscal-year customs totals. The status of Kashmir is contested; territorial descriptions identify administrators without deciding sovereignty.

Sources and Methodology

MGU Quick Facts uses the 2023 Pakistan Bureau of Statistics census separately from the World Bank’s 2025 population estimate. World Bank calendar-year GDP/growth/inflation figures and IMF fiscal-year estimates are not interchangeable; WTO/UN Comtrade totals are 2024 merchandise trade, not all goods and services or Pakistan financial-year records. Governance descriptions incorporate the November 13, 2025 Twenty-Seventh Amendment and Federal Constitutional Court, and differentiate elected, reserved and indirect legislative seats. Kashmir is territorially contested; descriptions of Pakistan-administered regions state administration without prejudging sovereignty.
