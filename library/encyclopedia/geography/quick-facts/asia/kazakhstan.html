Country Overview

Kazakhstan is the world’s largest landlocked country, occupying a vast steppe and desert territory in Central Asia, with a smaller portion west of the Ural River commonly described as geographically European. Since independence in 1991 it has developed major oil, gas, uranium, minerals and grain sectors. A new constitution approved in March 2026 took effect July 1, replacing the bicameral parliament with a 145-seat elected Kurultai. President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev remained head of state, and Olzhas Bektenov was reappointed prime minister in August 2026.
