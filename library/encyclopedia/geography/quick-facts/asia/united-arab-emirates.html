Country Overview

The United Arab Emirates is a federation of seven emirates on the southeastern Arabian Peninsula, with coastlines on the Persian/Arabian Gulf and Gulf of Oman. Abu Dhabi is the federal capital, while Dubai is a major international commercial and aviation hub. Hydrocarbons remain important, but trade, logistics, tourism, finance, aviation, advanced manufacturing and services form a diversified economy. National statistics must distinguish the large expatriate-resident population from citizenship, and customs merchandise trade from re-exports and domestic non-oil trade measures.

Sources & Methodology

UAE total population is not a citizen count. The 2025 World Bank population estimate and 2024 FCSC population are separately identified, as is the 2024 country-page GDP compared with a 2025 estimate in the World Bank April 2026 regional note. WTO and UNCTAD 2024 trade series vary because of coverage, compilation and re-exports; the detailed WTO numbers are explicitly dated. The UAE currently has three inscribed World Heritage properties; pending tentative nominations and separately inscribed intangible traditions are excluded from the three-site count.
