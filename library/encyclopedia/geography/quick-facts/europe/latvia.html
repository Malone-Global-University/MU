Country Overview

Latvia is a Baltic state on the eastern shore of the Baltic Sea, classified by the United Nations in Northern Europe. Its modern republic was proclaimed on 18 November 1918. Following Soviet occupation and annexation during the Second World War, Latvia restored its independence in 1990–1991. Riga is the capital, and the country belongs to the European Union, NATO, euro area and Schengen area. [6]

Historical context: Latvian state formation followed centuries under Baltic German, Polish-Lithuanian, Swedish and Russian imperial rule. Independence was declared in 1918 and defended during the Latvian War of Independence. The republic was occupied by the Soviet Union in 1940, by Nazi Germany in 1941–1944/45 and again by the Soviet Union until the restoration of state independence in 1991. Latvia entered the European Union and NATO in 2004 and adopted the euro in 2014. [9]
