Country Overview

Malta is an island republic in the central Mediterranean Sea, south of Sicily. It comprises the inhabited islands of Malta, Gozo and Comino and smaller islets. After long periods of Mediterranean and European rule, Malta gained independence from the United Kingdom in 1964 and became a republic in 1974. The capital, Valletta, is a historic fortified port. Malta is an EU member and uses the euro. [6]

Historical context: The Maltese archipelago preserves prehistoric temple complexes and long maritime histories. The Knights Hospitaller (Order of St John) ruled from 1530 until the French invasion of 1798. After local resistance to French rule, Malta came under British authority. It became independent on 21 September 1964, a republic on 13 December 1974, joined the EU in 2004 and adopted the euro on 1 January 2008. [11]
