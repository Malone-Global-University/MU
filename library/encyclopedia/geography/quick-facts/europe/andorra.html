Country Overview

Andorra is a small landlocked parliamentary co-principality in the eastern Pyrenees between France and Spain. Its constitution gives the French president and the Bishop of Urgell joint head-of-state roles, while elected institutions govern daily affairs. Tourism, finance, commerce and proximity to the European single market underpin its service economy.

Andorra uses the euro under a monetary agreement but is not a member state of the EU or euro area. The 2026 association-agreement process requires separate legal steps before any full implementation.
