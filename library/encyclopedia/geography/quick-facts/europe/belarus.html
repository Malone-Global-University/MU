Country Overview

Belarus is a landlocked Eastern European republic between the European Union, Ukraine and Russia. Its economy combines state-owned industry, agriculture and trade with close ties to Russia. The political system is presidentially dominated and international monitoring reports severe restrictions on political competition and civil liberties; official election results are presented with those qualifications.
