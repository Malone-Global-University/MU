Country Overview

Albania is a southeastern European republic on the Adriatic and Ionian seas. Its mountainous terrain, Mediterranean coast, Albanian-speaking population, emigration patterns, tourism economy, and European Union accession negotiations are central to its contemporary profile. The national statistics office and World Bank population series are shown with their respective reference dates.
