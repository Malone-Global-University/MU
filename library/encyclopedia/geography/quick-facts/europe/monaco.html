Country Overview

Monaco is a sovereign microstate on the Mediterranean coast of Western Europe, surrounded on land by France and adjoining the Ligurian Sea. The House of Grimaldi has ruled for centuries, and the contemporary principality is a hereditary constitutional monarchy headed by Prince Albert II. Extensive cross-border commuting, luxury tourism, financial and professional services, and land reclamation shape its economy. [6]

Historical context: The Grimaldi family established a durable dynastic presence in Monaco beginning in the late thirteenth century. Monaco entered French protection in the seventeenth century and formalized its modern territorial sovereignty by a treaty with France in 1861. A constitution was first granted in 1911, followed by the 1962 Constitution. Prince Albert II succeeded Prince Rainier III in 2005. The sovereign state is closely integrated economically with France but is not a member of the European Union. [6]
