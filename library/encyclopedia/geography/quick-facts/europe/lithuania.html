Country Overview

Lithuania is a Baltic republic on the eastern shore of the Baltic Sea, classified as Northern Europe in the UN M49 system. The historic Grand Duchy was an important medieval and early-modern European polity. The modern state declared independence on 16 February 1918, and Lithuania restored state independence on 11 March 1990 after Soviet occupation. Vilnius is the capital, and Lithuania belongs to the European Union, NATO, euro area and Schengen area. [6]

Historical context: The medieval Grand Duchy of Lithuania expanded widely in eastern Europe, then joined Poland through dynastic union and, in 1569, the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth. Partitions in the late eighteenth century placed most Lithuanian territory within the Russian Empire. Lithuania declared modern independence in 1918 and was occupied and annexed by the Soviet Union in 1940, followed by Nazi German occupation and renewed Soviet rule. The 1990 restoration act preceded broad international recognition in 1991. Lithuania joined NATO and the European Union in 2004 and adopted the euro in 2015. [10]
