Country Overview

Slovakia is a landlocked Central European parliamentary republic with Bratislava as its capital. It was part of Czechoslovakia from 1918, except during the years of wartime occupation and the separate Slovak state, and became an independent state on 1 January 1993. Its mountainous north includes the High Tatras; the southern Danube corridor supports large cities and industry. It joined the European Union and NATO in 2004 and adopted the euro in 2009. [6]

Historical context: Lands now in Slovakia passed through the Great Moravian sphere and then centuries of rule within the Kingdom of Hungary. The Habsburg and Austro-Hungarian periods shaped the region’s languages and social divisions. Czechoslovakia formed after World War I; during World War II, a separate authoritarian Slovak state existed under Nazi German influence. A restored Czechoslovakia entered communist rule in 1948. The peaceful Velvet Revolution in 1989 initiated democratization; the Czech and Slovak republics separated on 1 January 1993. Slovakia subsequently joined NATO and the EU in 2004, Schengen in 2007 and the euro area in 2009. [12]
