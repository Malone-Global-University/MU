Country Overview

Montenegro is a Balkan state on the Adriatic Sea, bordering Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Kosovo and Albania. Its modern independence followed a 2006 referendum ending the State Union of Serbia and Montenegro. Podgorica is the administrative capital, while Cetinje has special historical and constitutional standing. It is a NATO member and EU candidate, and uses the euro without belonging to the euro area. Historical context: Medieval principalities in the region were followed by centuries of Ottoman-Venetian borderland politics and the rise of an independent Montenegrin principality. The Congress of Berlin recognized its independence in 1878, and it became a kingdom in 1910. Following the First World War it joined the Yugoslav state; during the late twentieth century it was part of the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia and later the union with Serbia. A 2006 referendum led to the present independent republic. Montenegro joined NATO in 2017 and has been negotiating EU accession since 2012. [10]
