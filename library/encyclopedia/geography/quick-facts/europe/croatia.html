Country Overview

Croatia is a parliamentary republic at the intersection of central and southeastern Europe, with a long Adriatic coastline and many islands. Its modern independence emerged from the dissolution of Yugoslavia in 1991, following a long history of Croatian state traditions and political unions. Croatia joined the European Union in 2013 and entered both the euro area and Schengen area on 1 January 2023. [6]

Historical context: Croatian medieval statehood, the Croatian–Hungarian union, Habsburg rule, and twentieth-century Yugoslav statehood preceded the modern republic. The 1991–1995 war accompanied the dissolution of Yugoslavia and included population displacement and extensive destruction. Croatia’s subsequent political and legal reforms supported NATO admission in 2009 and European Union accession in 2013. [10]
