Country Overview

Finland is a Nordic parliamentary republic in Northern Europe, bordering Sweden, Norway and Russia and extending from the Baltic Sea to the Arctic region. Its parliament approved a declaration of independence from Russia on 6 December 1917. Finland is a bilingual state with Finnish and Swedish as national languages, and the Sámi have special constitutional protections for language and culture. Finland joined the European Union in 1995, the euro area in 1999, and NATO in 2023. [6]

Historical context: The territory formed the eastern part of the Swedish realm for centuries and became an autonomous grand duchy under Russia in 1809. Finland declared independence in 1917 and fought a civil war in 1918, then consolidated a republican constitutional order in 1919. It retained independence through the wars of 1939–1945. The 2000 constitution restructured the institutional balance between president and parliament in a contemporary parliamentary framework. [9]
