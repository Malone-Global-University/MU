Country Overview

Romania is a republic in southeastern Europe, extending from the Carpathian Mountains to the Danube Delta and Black Sea. Bucharest is its capital. The modern state emerged from the nineteenth-century union of Moldavia and Wallachia and declared independence from Ottoman suzerainty in 1877. Romania ended communist dictatorship in 1989 and joined NATO in 2004 and the European Union in 2007. It remains outside the euro area but became fully integrated into the Schengen area in January 2025. [6]

Historical context: Romania arose from the historical principalities of Moldavia and Wallachia, united through the election of Alexandru Ioan Cuza in 1859. Independence was proclaimed in 1877 and recognized the following year. Transylvania and other territories united with Romania after the First World War, creating a larger interwar state. The country underwent dictatorship, territorial losses and military alliances during the Second World War, followed by Soviet-backed communist rule. The 1989 revolution removed Nicolae Ceaușescu. Political and economic reform preceded entry into NATO in 2004 and the European Union in 2007. [6]
