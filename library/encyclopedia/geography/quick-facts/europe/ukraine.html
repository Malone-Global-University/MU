Country Overview

Ukraine is an Eastern European republic with its capital at Kyiv and a Black Sea coastline. Modern independence was declared in 1991 after the dissolution of the Soviet Union. Russia occupied and purported to annex Crimea in 2014 and launched a full-scale invasion in February 2022; hostilities and occupation continued at the editorial cutoff of November 2026. Ukraine’s internationally recognized borders encompass Crimea and other regions under foreign occupation. War has disrupted statistical collection, population location and economic activity; estimates are explicitly qualified throughout this entry. [8]

Historical context: The region’s history includes Kyivan Rus’, the Grand Duchy of Lithuania, Polish-Lithuanian rule, the Cossack Hetmanate and periods under Russian and Habsburg imperial governments. Revolutionary governments asserted Ukrainian independence in 1917–1921, but much of Ukraine was incorporated into the Soviet Union. Stalin-era collectivization and the Holodomor famine, World War II mass violence, and Soviet rule deeply shaped Ukrainian society. Ukraine declared independence in August 1991 and voters supported it that December. The 2004 Orange Revolution and 2013–2014 Euromaidan protests marked struggles over political direction. Russia occupied Crimea in 2014, supported armed groups in eastern Ukraine and invaded on a much larger scale on 24 February 2022. Ukraine has defended its sovereignty while enduring enormous losses, population displacement and damage to housing, infrastructure and heritage. [12]

Data interpretation: Ukraine’s 603,550 km² surface area describes internationally recognized territory, not the area effectively administered by Kyiv. The World Bank 2025 population is a published modeled/international series, not a contemporary census or count of people physically living in government-controlled areas; refugees abroad, occupation, migration and incomplete war reporting affect comparability. Do not calculate density or a welfare measure from these figures without reconciling coverage. [1]

The official Ukraine export-support portal, citing State Customs Service, reports 2025 goods exports of US$40.37 billion and imports of US$84.74 billion. GDP is not wages: Nominal GDP is aggregate economic output; GDP per capita divides that output by population. Neither measure represents average or median personal earnings. [1]
