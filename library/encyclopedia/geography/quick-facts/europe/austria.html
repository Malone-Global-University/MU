Country Overview

Austria is a federal parliamentary republic in Central Europe, connecting the Alps and the Danube basin. A European Union and euro-area member, it combines high-income manufacturing and services, tourism, and a strong social-market institutional tradition. National and World Bank population series and separate euro-valued and US-dollar trade figures are identified by their reporting conventions.
