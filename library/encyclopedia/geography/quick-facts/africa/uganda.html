Country Overview

Uganda, officially the Republic of Uganda, is a landlocked East African country in the African Great Lakes region. It borders South Sudan, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and its capital is Kampala. Uganda became independent from the United Kingdom on October 9, 1962 and adopted a republican form of government in 1963. Its economy relies on agriculture, services, mineral processing, manufacturing, tourism and trade; gold and coffee are particularly important merchandise exports. President Yoweri Museveni began a new term in May 2026, while Robinah Nabbanja is prime minister. [6] [2] [7] [12] [11]

Obote’s confrontation with the Buganda monarchy changed the balance between central authority and kingdoms.
1971 military coup
Idi Amin seized power, beginning a period of authoritarian rule and widespread violence.
1979 political change
A Tanzanian-backed military campaign and Ugandan exile forces overthrew Amin.
1980s civil conflict
Political and armed struggles preceded the National Resistance Movement’s takeover in 1986.
1986 change of government
Yoweri Museveni and the National Resistance Movement came to national power. [12]
Current constitution
Adopted in 1995 and subsequently amended. [10]
