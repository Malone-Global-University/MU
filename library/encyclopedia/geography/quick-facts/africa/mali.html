Country Overview

Mali, officially the Republic of Mali, is a large landlocked state in Western Africa, spanning the Sahara, Sahel and more fertile southern savanna. Its capital is Bamako. The modern country emerged from the breakup of the short-lived Mali Federation in 1960 and proclaimed the Republic of Mali on September 22 that year. It has a long West African historical heritage encompassing the Mali and Songhai empires, trans-Saharan commerce and Timbuktu's scholarly traditions. The contemporary economy depends heavily on gold mining, cotton and farming. [3] [6] [16] [14]

General Assimi Goïta serves as president of the military-led transition and General Abdoulaye Maïga as transitional prime minister. The July 2025 transitional charter provides a renewable five-year mandate; democratic elections remain postponed. Mali left ECOWAS in January 2025 but remains in the BCEAO monetary zone. Transition status: Mali’s 2023 constitutional text and 2025 transition charter coexist with military-led governance. National Transitional Council members are appointed, and the 2025 political-party dissolution was challenged in court. These facts must not be represented as an ordinary elected civilian government.
