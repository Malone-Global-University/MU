Country Overview

Tuvalu is a sovereign Polynesian island country of nine low-lying atolls and reef islands distributed across the central Pacific Ocean. Funafuti is the capital, with public administration, education and much of the country’s infrastructure concentrated on its narrow Fongafale islet. Tuvaluan communities have maintained land, sea, church, village and family institutions through centuries of oceanic exchange, British rule under the Ellice Islands name and independence on October 1, 1978. Tuvalu is a constitutional monarchy with King Charles III as sovereign and Feleti Teo as prime minister. On September 30, 2026, Temukisa Auina Penehuro Hauma became the country’s first woman governor-general. A 2023 constitutional reform declared the intended continuity of Tuvaluan statehood despite physical climate-change impacts; the Falepili Union treaty with Australia, effective in 2024, also recognizes continued sovereignty and creates a migration pathway. Fisheries access, state services, external assistance, the Tuvalu Trust Fund and digital-domain licensing are materially different economic flows from the country’s very limited merchandise exports. The 2022 population census, World Bank 2025 population estimate, national 2025 GDP release and World Bank national accounts use different measures and are separately labeled.

The Tuvalu Central Statistics Division’s November 2025 IMTS bulletin reports the peak monthly export value in July 2024–June 2025 at approximately A$87,300 (April 2025), with large import dependence; it does not supply a validated annual headline for this profile. UNCTAD’s 2024 estimate of merchandise exports is approximately US$0.033m. The World Bank/WTO 2025 series rounds a very small annual number to US$0m; this must not be interpreted as literally zero trade.

The World Bank gives 1.6% real GDP growth for 2025, while Tuvalu’s Central Statistics Division announced 5.2% in its September 2026 national accounts update, with A$100.0 million real GDP in constant 2023 prices. Neither figure is a current-U.S.-dollar GDP total. Fishing access charges, domain licensing, investment income and grants are not merchandise exports.
