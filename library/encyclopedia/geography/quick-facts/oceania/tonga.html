Country Overview

Tonga is a Polynesian island kingdom in the South Pacific, comprising Tongatapu, ʻEua, Haʻapai, Vavaʻu, the Niuas and numerous smaller islands. Its chiefly hierarchies and the historical Tongan maritime polity developed before sustained European contact, while the modern constitutional monarchy dates to reforms in the nineteenth century. Tonga retained its monarchy through a British protected-state relationship and resumed full responsibility for foreign affairs in 1970. Nukuʻalofa, on Tongatapu, is the capital. King Tupou VI is sovereign; following the November 2025 election, Lord Fakafanua was elected prime-minister-designate and appointed the kingdom's twentieth prime minister on December 18, 2025. Agriculture, fisheries, tourism, public services and remittances support the economy, while exposure to storms, earthquakes and volcanic hazards is unusually pronounced. The January 2022 Hunga Tonga–Hunga Haʻapai eruption and tsunami caused extensive damage. The 2021 census count, World Bank 2025 annual indicators and official calendar-2025 customs goods-trade figures are separately labeled throughout this profile.
