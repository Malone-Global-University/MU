Country Overview

The Republic of Palau is a sovereign Micronesian island state in the western Pacific, comprising volcanic islands, coral limestone islands and low atolls in a rich marine environment. Palauan societies developed clan, chiefly and maritime relationships prior to Spanish, German and Japanese colonial administrations and the United States-administered Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands. Palau adopted a constitution in 1981 and became independent in free association with the United States on October 1, 1994. The national government is based at Ngerulmud in Melekeok State, while Koror remains the largest population and business center. President Surangel S. Whipps Jr began a second term in January 2025 after winning the November 2024 election, with Vice President Raynold Oilouch. Tourism, public services, construction, fisheries and Compact-related financial support shape the economy, while marine conservation—including the UNESCO-listed Rock Islands Southern Lagoon—is central to national identity and international relations. The population and GDP figures below distinguish the 2020 census, the 2025 census program and the World Bank 2025 estimates, whose methods and publication timetables differ.
