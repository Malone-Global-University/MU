Sidney Poitier

Actor, director, and activist for civil rights and Black liberation (1927-2022)

Biography

(1927-2022) - Sidney Poitier was a Bahamian-American actor, director, and activist who broke racial barriers in Hollywood. He leveraged his fame to advocate for civil rights, social justice, and equality, supporting movements led by Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. and other Black leaders.

Achievements

  • First Black actor to win the Academy Award for Best Actor (1964).
  • Starred in groundbreaking films that challenged racial stereotypes.
  • Advocated publicly for civil rights and social justice causes.
  • Served as a cultural ambassador and supported global human rights initiatives.
