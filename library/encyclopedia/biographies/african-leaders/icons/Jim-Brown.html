Jim Brown

Athlete, actor, activist, and ally in the Black liberation movement (1936-2023)

Biography

(1936-2023) - Jim Brown was an American professional football player turned actor and activist. He leveraged his fame to support civil rights causes, co-founding the Black Economic Union and participating in the Black Power movement, while using sports and media as platforms for social change.

Achievements

  • One of the greatest NFL players of all time.
  • Founded the Black Economic Union to promote African American entrepreneurship.
  • Advocated for social justice, education, and civil rights through media and activism.
  • Actively participated in protests and demonstrations supporting Black liberation causes.
