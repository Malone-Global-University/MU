Orval Faubus

Governor of Arkansas (1910-1994) - Opponent of school desegregation

Biography

(1910-1994) - Orval Eugene Faubus was the Governor of Arkansas best known for his opposition to the integration of Little Rock Central High School in 1957. He became a symbol of resistance to federal civil rights enforcement and Black liberation in the American South.

Impact / Legacy
