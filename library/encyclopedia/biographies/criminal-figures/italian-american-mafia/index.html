Italian-American Mafia Biographies

Biographical Directory of American Organized Crime

This index brings together biographical profiles of individuals associated with major Italian-American Mafia organizations in the United States. The collection covers influential leaders, members, associates, and other figures whose activities shaped the historical record of organized crime.

Profiles are grouped by their principal organizational collection. Inclusion does not, by itself, establish formal Mafia membership: some individuals were associates, cooperating witnesses, or other figures connected with these organizations.

Current directory: 125 biographies in six populated collections, including the five New York families and the Chicago Outfit.

Research and Editorial Scope
