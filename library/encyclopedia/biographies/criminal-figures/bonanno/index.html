Bonanno Crime Family Biographies

Leadership, factional struggles, infiltration, and the transformation of the Bonanno organization.

Collection Overview

The Bonanno crime family emerged from the Castellammarese faction led by Salvatore Maranzano and was subsequently associated with Joseph Bonanno, whose long tenure gave the organization its enduring name. Its history includes the 1960s succession conflict known as the Banana War, later narcotics investigations, the Donnie Brasco operation, and the prosecution of Joseph Massino. This directory identifies important subjects for independent biographical study, not an exhaustive membership roll.

This is a curated 20-person publication roster, not a claim that these biographies are already online or that every listed figure was an inducted member. Each proposed biography should be separately researched, sourced, written, and published. The person’s historical classification is a starting point for verification, not a substitute for documentary proof.

Editorial Standards

Publication status: All 20 entries above are planned biography subjects. Plain-text filenames are intentional; links to individual biography pages should be activated only when the articles exist at those paths.

  • Distinguish boss, acting boss, underboss, consigliere, captain, soldier, associate, and cooperating witness. Leadership status may vary by period and source.
  • Do not equate charges or press-release allegations with findings of guilt. Record the procedural outcome and the date of each case.
  • Prefer court opinions, judgments, trial records, federal investigative material, congressional hearings, contemporaneous reporting, and credible historical scholarship. Attribute disputed reconstructions.
  • Identify relevant family transfers and cross-family relationships, but retain one canonical biography for each subject.
  • Explain the human consequences of organized crime without romanticizing coercion or violence.
