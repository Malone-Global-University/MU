Biographical Directory of Irish-American Organized Crime

This MGU Crime Encyclopedia index provides access to profiles of individuals associated with Irish-American criminal networks, neighborhood gangs, and organized-crime syndicates in the United States. The collection brings together figures from different cities, periods, and organizations for historical and comparative study.

Profiles are indexed alphabetically by surname rather than assigned to a single syndicate. Inclusion reflects the encyclopedia’s editorial collection and does not, by itself, establish a person’s ethnicity, formal membership, or association with every group discussed in the collection.

Research and Editorial Scope

This biographical index documents people whose lives appear in the historical record of American organized crime. The profiles may address criminal enterprises, rivalry and conflict, policing, prosecution, and their broader social consequences. Inclusion is for educational documentation, not endorsement or celebration.

This index links to individual biography pages in the present collection; it is not a complete roster of every person associated with Irish-American organized crime.
