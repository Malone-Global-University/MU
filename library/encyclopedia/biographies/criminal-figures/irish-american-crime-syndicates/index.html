Biographical Directory of American Organized Crime

This index brings together the Malone Global University Criminal Figures biographies associated with Irish-led, Irish-American, and historically related criminal organizations in the United States. The directory covers nineteenth-century gangs, Prohibition-era bootlegging operations, waterfront syndicates, neighborhood racketeering networks, and later federal organized-crime investigations.

Biographies are grouped under their principal organizational collection rather than by ancestry. Some groups, including Chicago's North Side Gang, were multiethnic; some people were associates or allies rather than formal members. A directory placement is not an independent legal finding of membership or responsibility for any offense. One canonical biography should be maintained for each person, with cross-references from other relevant organizations.

Current directory: 32 assigned biography filenames in 19 populated collections. Eleven further gang folders from the original directory inventory are listed for future biography expansion. The additional Vannie Higgins Gang is included as a nineteenth collection, giving the complete directory 30 organization names. Profile links reflect the existing MGU directory-index filenames; their live deployment has not been independently confirmed.

Research and Editorial Scope

The Crime Encyclopedia organizes individual lives within a broader history of urban gangs, organized crime, Prohibition, political corruption, law enforcement, prosecutions, and affected communities. Inclusion is for historical and educational documentation, not endorsement or an assertion that ethnicity determines criminal conduct.

Biographical subjects may have been gang leaders, members, associates, rivals, witnesses, or other historically significant participants. Editors should distinguish allegations, recorded affiliations, convictions, acquittals, exonerations, and later retrospective claims. This index uses one principal filing location per person while allowing additional cross-references.

For extended organizational history rather than individual biography, consult the Irish-American Crime Syndicates organization collection. For related but distinct Mafia biographies, visit the Italian-American Mafia biography collection.
