Abolitionists

Biographies of abolitionists, anti-slavery activists, resistance leaders, and political figures who challenged slavery and advanced Black freedom.

Overview

This directory brings together major figures associated with resistance to slavery and the abolitionist movement. The collection includes formerly enslaved abolitionists, political leaders, militant opponents of slavery, and figures whose resistance to enslavement became central to the history of Black liberation in the United States.
