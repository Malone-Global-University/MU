Muhammad Ali

World Heavyweight Champion and activist for civil rights and Black liberation (1942-2016)

Biography

(1942-2016) - Muhammad Ali was an American professional boxer, activist, and cultural icon. Beyond his legendary sports career, Ali was a vocal advocate for civil rights, religious freedom, and global social justice, challenging racial inequality and standing firmly against the Vietnam War.

Achievements

  • Three-time World Heavyweight Champion and one of the greatest boxers of all time.
  • Refused induction into the U.S. Army for the Vietnam War on religious and moral grounds.
  • Supported civil rights movements and global humanitarian causes.
  • Used his fame to draw attention to racial inequality, poverty, and injustice.
