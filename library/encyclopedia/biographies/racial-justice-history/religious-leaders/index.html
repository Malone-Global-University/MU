Religious Leaders

Biographies of religious, spiritual, and faith-centered political figures whose leadership shaped Black communities, African societies, liberation movements, and public life.

Overview

This directory brings together biographies of religious leaders whose influence extended into politics, social organization, cultural identity, anti-colonial struggle, civil rights, or community leadership. The collection reflects a range of religious traditions, historical periods, and geographic regions across Africa and the African diaspora.
