Black Panthers

Biographies of Black Panther Party founders, organizers, members, affiliates, and related figures who shaped the movement's politics, community programs, culture, and legacy.

Overview

This directory brings together biographies of individuals associated with the Black Panther Party and the broader Black Power movement. The collection includes national and local leaders, rank-and-file organizers, political prisoners, artists, educators, and other figures whose lives intersected with the Panthers' community programs, revolutionary politics, self-defense philosophy, internal conflicts, state repression, and long-term historical legacy.
