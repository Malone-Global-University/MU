Militias and Armed Resistance

Biographies and profiles of armed resistance movements, militia organizations, and related leaders whose actions shaped struggles against colonial rule, racial domination, or political repression.

Overview

This directory brings together biographies and movement profiles associated with armed resistance, guerrilla organization, militia activity, and anti-colonial struggle. Subjects in this collection are examined within their historical circumstances, including their political objectives, methods, relationship to civilian populations, opposition forces, controversies, and long-term significance.
