Antagonists

Biographies of political leaders, officials, organizers, ideologues, and perpetrators whose actions materially opposed Black freedom, civil rights, political participation, or equality.

About This Directory

This directory groups historical figures by the principal role through which they became significant to the history of slavery, white supremacy, segregation, racial repression, political disfranchisement, or violence against Black people and civil-rights movements. “Antagonist” is used here as an analytical classification within the MGU biography collection; individual biographies provide the historical context, evidence, controversies, and balanced assessment appropriate to each subject.
