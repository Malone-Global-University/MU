Advocates

Biographies of legal, political, civic, and public figures whose careers intersected with Black representation, civil rights, public service, or racial-justice advocacy.

Overview

This directory collects biographies of public figures whose work involved law, government, political representation, civic leadership, public advocacy, or major questions affecting Black Americans. Inclusion in this category does not imply identical political views or a uniform record on racial issues. Individual biographies assess each subject according to their actions, accomplishments, controversies, and historical significance.
