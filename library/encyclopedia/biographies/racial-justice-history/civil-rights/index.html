Overview

This directory brings together biographies of individuals associated with the modern Civil Rights Movement, Black political organizing, racial-justice advocacy, and related struggles for equality and self-determination. The collection includes figures representing different generations, organizations, political philosophies, strategies, and approaches to Black advancement.
