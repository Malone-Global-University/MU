Economic Leaders

Biographies of entrepreneurs, institution builders, political-economic thinkers, and historical figures whose work shaped Black wealth, economic development, self-determination, and financial power.

Overview

This directory brings together biographies of figures whose historical significance includes entrepreneurship, wealth creation, economic nationalism, state-led development, philanthropy, institutional investment, or broader efforts to expand economic capacity within African and African-descended communities. The subjects represent different eras, regions, political systems, and economic philosophies.
