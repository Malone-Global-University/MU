Political Leaders

Biographies of African, African American, Caribbean, and diaspora political leaders whose careers shaped government, independence, civil rights, state power, diplomacy, and Black political development.

Overview

This directory brings together political leaders from the United States, Africa, the Caribbean, and the wider African diaspora. The collection includes presidents, prime ministers, governors, legislators, revolutionaries, independence leaders, monarchs, military rulers, diplomats, and other political figures whose careers intersected with Black political power, national liberation, governance, civil rights, or the development of African and African-descended communities.
