Overview

Entertainment Figures documents the lives and careers of individuals who contributed to the development of theater, music, dance, motion pictures, animation, and popular entertainment. The collection examines their artistic work, professional development, historical circumstances, and cultural influence.

The initial collection contains ten biographies. Articles are organized by principal area of contribution for thematic browsing and listed alphabetically by article title for direct access. Many of these individuals worked across several disciplines; each biography remains in a single shared article directory.

Vaudeville, Theater, and Nightlife

Performers and entertainment entrepreneurs associated with variety theater, musical revues, popular song, and international nightlife.

Ada “Bricktop” Smith

American singer, dancer, and nightclub proprietor whose career connected African American entertainment with the international nightlife of Paris.

Baby Esther

African American child entertainer known for her vocal performance style and her place in historical accounts of the Helen Kane–Betty Boop litigation.

Helen Kane

American singer and actress associated with Jazz Age popular music and the distinctive vocal style that figured in her lawsuit over Betty Boop.

Josephine Baker

American-born French entertainer, dancer, and singer whose international career extended into resistance work and civil rights activism.

Music and Composition

Vocalists, bandleaders, and composers whose work shaped jazz, concert music, and American musical culture.

Cab Calloway

American jazz singer, bandleader, and entertainer associated with the Cotton Club, scat singing, swing music, and musical film.

William Grant Still

American composer and conductor known for his orchestral music, operas, and contributions to African American concert music.

Motion Pictures and Screen Performance

Actors and dancers whose work helped define screen performance and the Hollywood entertainment industry.

Fred Astaire

American dancer, singer, and actor recognized for his influential stage and screen choreography and Hollywood musical performances.

Marilyn Monroe

American actress, model, and singer whose film career and public image made her a major figure in twentieth-century popular culture.

Animation and Film Production

Creative and technical figures in the development of American animation and the motion-picture industry.

Dave Fleischer

American animation director and co-founder of Fleischer Studios, associated with the production of Betty Boop, Popeye, and Superman cartoons.

Max Fleischer

American animator, inventor, producer, and co-founder of Fleischer Studios, known for the rotoscope and pioneering work in theatrical animation.
