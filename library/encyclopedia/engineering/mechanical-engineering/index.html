Mechanical Engineering Overview

Mechanical engineering studies the design, analysis, manufacture, and operation of mechanical systems involving motion, force, heat, and energy transfer. The field examines machines, mechanics, thermodynamics, fluid systems, and manufacturing processes. It governs engineered physical systems involving moving parts and energy transformation.

Disciplines

  • Fluid Mechanics

    Study behavior of liquids and gases in motion and at rest.

  • Machine Design

    Examine engineering design of machines and mechanical components.

  • Manufacturing Systems

    Study processes and systems used to produce engineered components and products.

  • Statics and Dynamics

    Study forces, equilibrium, and motion in mechanical systems.

  • Thermodynamics

    Explore heat, energy, work, and thermodynamic system behavior.
