Materials Engineering Overview

Materials engineering studies the structure, properties, processing, and performance of engineering materials. The field examines metals, polymers, ceramics, composites, and failure mechanisms to determine how materials behave under operational conditions. It governs selection and engineering of materials for technical applications.

Disciplines

  • Ceramics

    Study engineering of inorganic nonmetallic materials and ceramic systems.

  • Composites

    Examine engineered materials formed from combined constituent materials.

  • Material Failure Analysis

    Study mechanisms and causes of material degradation and structural failure.

  • Metallurgy

    Study extraction, processing, structure, and behavior of metallic materials.

  • Polymers

    Explore engineering of polymeric and plastic material systems.
