Literature and Literary Theory Overview

Literature and literary theory study written artistic works, interpretive frameworks, narrative structures, genre systems, and comparative literary traditions. The field examines how literary texts produce meaning, reflect culture, and function as artistic and intellectual artifacts.

Disciplines

  • Comparative Literature

    Study literary works across cultures, languages, and traditions comparatively.

  • Genre Studies

    Examine literary classification and conventions across genres.

  • Literary Analysis

    Study close reading and interpretive examination of literary texts.

  • Literary Theory

    Explore theoretical frameworks for interpreting literature and textual meaning.

  • Narrative Studies

    Study structures, techniques, and theory of storytelling and narrative form.
