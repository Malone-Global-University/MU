Funktown USA

Overview

Funktown USA was an Oakland drug-trafficking organization active during the mid-1980s and led by Harvey Whisenton. Contemporary federal and press accounts describe it as a major heroin and cocaine organization operating in East Oakland.

Position in Oakland's Drug Market

Funktown USA emerged in an environment already shaped by Felix Mitchell's Mob and Mickey Moore's Family. Retrospective reporting identifies Funktown USA as a third major organization in the violent competition surrounding Oakland's heroin trade.

1985 Federal Enforcement

Federal agents and Oakland police arrested Whisenton in October 1985. Authorities alleged that an East Oakland auto-body shop served as a front for heroin sales and said the organization was involved in both heroin and cocaine distribution.

In May 1986, Whisenton was sentenced to 17 years in federal prison after pleading guilty in connection with sales to undercover agents. Several associates also received federal sentences.

Claim Status

Contemporary law-enforcement officials alleged that Funktown USA contracted with the Black Guerrilla Family prison gang for enforcement. This encyclopedia treats that relationship as a sourced allegation unless a specific judicial finding or documentary record establishes the scope of the relationship.

Historical Significance

Funktown USA represents the next stage of Oakland's narcotics landscape after the initial Mitchell–Moore rivalry. Its rise illustrates the fragmentation and reorganization of the city's drug market during the transition from a predominantly heroin-centered economy toward a market increasingly shaped by cocaine.
