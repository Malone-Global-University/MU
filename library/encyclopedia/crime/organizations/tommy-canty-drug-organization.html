Tommy Canty Drug Organization

Overview

The Tommy Canty Drug Organization is a descriptive encyclopedia label for the multilayered Baltimore narcotics conspiracy organized and managed by Tommy Lee Canty Jr. during the 1980s.

Interstate Supply

The Fourth Circuit described Canty as purchasing cocaine and heroin from suppliers in New York, Miami, and Los Angeles. Those connections place the organization within a national distribution system rather than an isolated local market.

Processing and Distribution

Canty processed and packaged narcotics for resale through an extensive network of participants. The organization was multilayered, with numerous individuals performing different roles in the conspiracy.

Investigation

Local authorities began investigating in 1987. Federal investigators joined the effort in 1988, and the combined inquiry used undercover infiltration and wiretaps. Twenty-six members were arrested.

Federal Prosecution

A late-1989 federal indictment charged members with offenses including conspiracy to distribute cocaine and heroin from January 1986 to May 1989. Appeals by numerous defendants were decided by the Fourth Circuit in 1992.

Historical Significance

The Canty organization illustrates the increasing integration of Baltimore into national cocaine routes while preserving the city's longstanding heroin market. It is also a strong example of federal electronic-surveillance and conspiracy techniques used against a large urban trafficking network.
