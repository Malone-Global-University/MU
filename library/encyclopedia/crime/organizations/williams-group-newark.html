Williams Group — Newark

Overview

The Williams Group is a descriptive label used by New Jersey organized-crime investigators for the cocaine and heroin organization led by Roger “Little Akbar” Williams in Newark.

Origins

State investigators described Williams as a close associate of Wayne Pray who established a separate drug organization after Pray's incarceration.

Prince Street Base

The group operated in the Prince Street area and was described as a multi-member neighborhood distribution ring.

Relationship to Ratchford Network

The Williams group reportedly maintained a territorial relationship with the Ratchford Brothers before a later dispute over the Prince Street area.

Historical Interpretation

The Williams group is best understood as part of the post-Pray fragmentation of Newark's drug market, not as proof that Williams became the formal successor leader of The Family.
