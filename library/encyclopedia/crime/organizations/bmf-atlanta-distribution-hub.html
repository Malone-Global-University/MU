BMF Atlanta Distribution Hub

Overview

The BMF Atlanta Distribution Hub is a descriptive encyclopedia label for the Atlanta operating center of the Black Mafia Family. It is not intended to replace the main Black Mafia Family organization entry. The purpose of this page is to isolate the Atlanta-specific people, facilities, events, and distribution relationships within the national organization.

National Organization, Local Hub

Federal authorities described BMF as a nationwide cocaine-distribution organization with major operations in Atlanta, Detroit, Orlando, and Los Angeles. At its 2003–2004 peak, prosecutors stated that BMF was moving hundreds of kilograms of cocaine into Atlanta, Detroit, and other distribution hubs every month.

Atlanta Leadership and Personnel

Demetrius “Big Meech” Flenory was closely associated with the Atlanta side of the enterprise. Fleming Daniels was identified in appellate records as a high-ranking member who was frequently present at an Atlanta storehouse where cocaine and money were exchanged. Other Atlanta defendants included Vernon Coleman, Barima McKnight, Lamar Fields, Victor Hammonds, Franklin Nash, Bryant Shaw, Darryl Taylor, and others.

Transportation System

Federal prosecutors stated that BMF used vehicles, including limousines, equipped with concealed compartments or “traps.” Cocaine was transported into distribution hubs and drug proceeds were then moved back toward suppliers using the same concealment methods.

Atlanta Storehouses and Stash Locations

The Daniels appellate decision references an Atlanta storehouse where drugs and money were exchanged. In late November 2004, Atlanta police and DEA agents also raided an expensive BMF stash house in northwest Atlanta, recovering firearms, fictitious identifications, BMF material, and marijuana.

Regional Distribution

Atlanta did not function only as a consumption market. Vernon Coleman's case documents BMF cocaine moving from the organization to a buyer in Birmingham, Alabama. This reflects Atlanta's broader role as a Southeastern redistribution center.
