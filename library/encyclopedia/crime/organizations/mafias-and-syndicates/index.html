Crime Organizations: all categories

American syndicates

  • Italian-American Mafia

    The American La Cosa Nostra tradition, including New York’s Five Families and their individual histories.

  • Irish-American Crime Syndicates

    Historical Irish-American organized-crime networks, including Prohibition-era bootlegging syndicates, gambling enterprises, and criminal organizations that played significant roles in the development of organized crime across the United States.

  • Philadelphia Black Mafia

    Historical Philadelphia organized-crime syndicate; distinct from the later Black Mafia Family.

  • Junior Black Mafia

    Philadelphia-area criminal organization historically associated with the city’s organized-crime landscape.

  • The Council

    Nicky Barnes’s Harlem-centered organized crime leadership network, distinct from the Frank Lucas operation.

European and British networks

  • Camorra

    Distinct organized-crime groups originating in Italy’s Campania region.

  • ’Ndrangheta

    Calabrian organized-crime network; distinct from the Italian-American Mafia.

  • Eurasian Organized Crime

    Broad umbrella for post-Soviet criminal networks, including Russian-speaking groups and the Vory tradition.

  • London Underworld

    British historical underworld and its separate syndicates, firms, and criminal networks.

Asian and Israeli networks

  • Chinese Triads

    Umbrella category for distinct historical and contemporary triad societies and related networks.

  • Yakuza

    Japanese organized-crime groups and federations; not one organization with a single leadership.

  • Israeli Organized Crime

    Independent crime families and networks operating in and beyond Israel.

Classification and Documentation

These entries are grouped for encyclopedia navigation; they are not all members of a shared hierarchy. A parent collection, a named criminal enterprise, and an operational branch may appear at different levels. Statements about criminal activity should be supported by the evidence in each individual article, and present-day status should not be inferred from historical labels.
