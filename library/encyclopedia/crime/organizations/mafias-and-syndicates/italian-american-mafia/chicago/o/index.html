Chicago Outfit — Biographical Directory

Twenty profiles tracing the Chicago organization’s origins, Prohibition-era expansion, postwar influence, and later federal prosecutions.

Biographies · Italian-American Mafia · Chicago

People and Historical Context

Browse the twenty figures selected for this Chicago Outfit research collection. Profiles include organizational leaders, senior associates, financial intermediaries, and individuals connected with Chicago-linked operations in other cities. This directory follows broad historical periods rather than a definitive chain of command.

Chicago’s criminal networks changed over time, and sources sometimes disagree on a person’s formal title, degree of authority, or membership. Inclusion in this directory does not by itself establish a criminal conviction or formal Mafia membership.
