The other gangs of Chicago

Independent syndicates, shifting alliances, and rival criminal factions during the Beer Wars. These organizations are classified separately from the Colosimo–Torrio–Capone lineage.

Key distinction. “Irish versus Italian” is an inadequate model: the North Side leadership was multiethnic; the Sicilian Genna organization initially aligned with Torrio; Joe Aiello’s Sicilian faction opposed Capone; and the Irish-associated Druggan–Lake Valley Gang cooperated with Capone.
