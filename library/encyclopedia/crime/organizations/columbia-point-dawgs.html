Columbia Point Dawgs

Overview

The Columbia Point Dawgs (CPD), also known as the Point, are a Boston street organization that originated in the former Columbia Point Housing Development during the 1980s.

Expansion

Federal prosecutors described CPD as Boston's largest and most influential citywide gang at the time of the 2015 federal sweep. Over time, members established drug-trafficking crews throughout Boston and extended trafficking activity into other parts of New England.

Drug Markets

Federal charging documents alleged distribution of kilogram quantities of heroin, cocaine, crack cocaine, and oxycodone. Later individual cases documented specific cocaine and heroin quantities attributed to CPD leaders and associates.

Violence and Rivalries

Federal prosecutors alleged that CPD used violence and intimidation to maintain influence in Boston's gang and drug markets. During the period leading to the 2015 sweep, CPD was engaged in a violent conflict with the Greenwood Street Posse and allied groups.

Operation Rising Tide

A two-year wiretap investigation culminated in June 2015 with six federal indictments charging forty-eight leaders, members, and associates and more than thirty search warrants.

Historical Significance

CPD demonstrates a different Boston organizational model from the New York Boys: a gang rooted in one public-housing development that evolved into multiple citywide trafficking crews over several decades.
