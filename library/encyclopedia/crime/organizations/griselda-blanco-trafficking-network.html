Griselda Blanco Trafficking Network

Overview

The Griselda Blanco Trafficking Network is a descriptive encyclopedia label for the cocaine-importation and distribution organization associated with Griselda Blanco. The label is used to organize documented relationships and should not be treated as a formal name used consistently by participants.

International Structure

The federal case against Blanco described a conspiracy connecting Colombia to the United States. Cocaine entered through Miami and other locations and was distributed onward to major U.S. markets, including New York.

Couriers and Concealment

Federal prosecutors described the organization using couriers and concealed compartments in clothing and other objects to move cocaine. These methods belonged to an earlier period of cocaine importation before multiton cartel logistics became dominant.

South Florida Factions

Later Miami law-enforcement accounts described multiple South Florida factions linked to Blanco's wider organization. Rivalries among traffickers and disputes over cocaine transactions contributed to severe violence.

Dadeland Connection

Investigators later associated participants in the 1979 Dadeland Mall shooting with factions connected to Blanco's trafficking network. The event became one of the most visible symbols of the violent transformation of Miami's cocaine market.
