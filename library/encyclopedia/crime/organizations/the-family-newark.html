The Family — Newark

Overview

The Family was a Newark-based organized-crime network led by Wayne “Akbar” Pray. Federal and state records describe the organization as involved in cocaine and marijuana importation and distribution in New Jersey and beyond during the 1970s and 1980s.

Scale

New Jersey organized-crime investigators estimated The Family at hundreds of members and associates at its height. Its size distinguished it from many later Newark groups that were concentrated in a single neighborhood or housing complex.

Distribution Structure

Investigators described a layered organization in which senior figures could remain insulated from retail narcotics transactions. The network included midlevel distributors and street sellers and used multiple locations and associates to move drugs and money.

Supply

Federal prosecution evidence established a large cocaine enterprise with interstate and international dimensions. Pray was convicted of conspiracy to import more than 98 kilograms of cocaine and of administering a continuing criminal enterprise involving more than 150 kilograms.

Fragmentation

After Pray's incarceration, state investigators described The Family as fragmented. Former lieutenants and associates operated more independently, and smaller neighborhood groups became increasingly visible.
