Hankton Enterprise

Overview

The Hankton Enterprise was a Central City New Orleans drug organization led by Telly Hankton. Federal prosecutors described its activities as dating to approximately 1996, while the Fifth Circuit characterized it as a gang that sold drugs in Central City.

Drug Distribution

The enterprise participated in the distribution of controlled substances in and around New Orleans. Federal RICO charges treated narcotics trafficking as one of the central purposes of the organization.

Territorial Conflict

In 2004, the organization entered a violent conflict with a rival group led by Brian Broussard. The Fifth Circuit described the dispute as a turf war that produced numerous shootings and at least seven killings.

Witness Intimidation and Obstruction

Federal prosecutors alleged and later proved multiple efforts to intimidate witnesses, obstruct state proceedings, create false alibis, and retaliate against individuals who cooperated with law enforcement.

Federal Dismantling

A federal RICO prosecution resulted in guilty pleas from numerous members and associates and trial convictions for Telly Hankton, Walter Porter, Andre Hankton, and Kevin Jackson.
