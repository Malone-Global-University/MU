Domestic Drug Distribution Enterprises

A geographically organized directory of 38 named domestic drug enterprises and related criminal networks, including lesser-known historical groups.

Overview

This directory brings together 38 named organizations, trafficking networks, drug-distribution operations, and related criminal groups from the United States. The collection preserves the depth of MGU’s historical research, including less widely known organizations.

Entries are arranged by the principal place associated with each subject, where identified. Geographic headings provide navigation; they do not imply common leadership, affiliation, or legal classification. Organizations with multistate histories may appear under a founding city or a significant operational hub.

Classification and Documentation

This is an editorial research collection, not a claim that every entry was a self-governing drug organization or part of a shared criminal hierarchy. It includes distinct drug enterprises, street-gang-linked operations, local crews, larger syndicates, and operational branches to preserve navigation across MGU’s existing articles.

A branch or affiliated unit must not be confused with an independent organization. Any connection between named groups should be established in the relevant articles through reliable evidence. Allegations, convictions, historical interpretations, and present-day operational status should also remain clearly distinguished.

For the related top-level collections, see Domestic Street Gangs, Neighborhood Crews, Mafias & Syndicates, and Cartels & Transnational Trafficking.
