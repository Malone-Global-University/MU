Classification and Documentation

These entries are grouped for encyclopedia navigation; they are not all members of a shared hierarchy. A parent collection, a named criminal enterprise, and an operational branch may appear at different levels. Statements about criminal activity should be supported by the evidence in each individual article, and present-day status should not be inferred from historical labels.
