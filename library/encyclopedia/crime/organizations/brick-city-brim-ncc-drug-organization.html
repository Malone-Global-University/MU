Brick City Brim NCC Drug Organization

Overview

The Brick City Brim NCC Drug Organization is a descriptive label for the Newark heroin and crack-cocaine network led by Keith Herd around the New Community Corporation development.

Gang Affiliation

Federal prosecutors described the organization as composed of members of the Brick City Brim set of the Bloods. The NCC organization should be distinguished from other Brick City Brim subsets and Newark Bloods-affiliated drug groups operating in different neighborhoods.

Drug Market

The organization sold heroin and crack cocaine around Hayes Street and 14th Avenue and also supplied customers and other distributors elsewhere.

Internal Cooperation

Members alerted each other to police and rivals, shared narcotics supplies and customers, pooled proceeds, and raised bail money for associates.

Investigation

The FBI-led inquiry used video and physical surveillance, confidential informants, controlled purchases, narcotics seizures, and telephone wiretaps. Multiple waves of arrests occurred in 2018.
