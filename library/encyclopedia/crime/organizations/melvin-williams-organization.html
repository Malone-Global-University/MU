Melvin Williams Organization

Overview

The Melvin Williams Organization is a descriptive encyclopedia label for the Baltimore narcotics network headed by Melvin “Little Melvin” Williams. Federal court records and contemporary reporting describe Williams as the head of an organization involved in heroin and cocaine trafficking over an extended period.

West Baltimore Base

The organization was closely associated with West Baltimore. Contemporary law-enforcement sources described it as a major force in the area's heroin trade. Exact estimates of territory or market share should be treated cautiously because illicit markets were competitive and difficult to measure.

Drug Activities

Federal court records state that investigators concluded Williams had dealt in heroin and cocaine since 1967. By the 1980s, the organization was sufficiently important to attract a coordinated investigation involving the IRS, DEA, ATF, and Baltimore City Police Department.

Financial Structure

The federal investigation examined not only narcotics transactions but also property, corporate relationships, reported income, vehicles, cash, and other assets. Scrapp Investment Company became relevant in later forfeiture and financial proceedings because of its relationship to Williams and his family.

Law-Enforcement Pressure

Searches in December 1984 and Williams' 1985 federal conviction substantially increased pressure on the organization. Investigators nevertheless continued examining Williams' financial and criminal relationships after his imprisonment.
