Jemeker Thompson Trafficking Network

Overview

The Jemeker Thompson Trafficking Network is a descriptive label for the cocaine-distribution enterprise associated with Jemeker Thompson and her partners and distributors. The label is used for organizational indexing and should not be understood as a formal name used by the participants.

Los Angeles Foundation

The network developed from Thompson's involvement in the South Central Los Angeles drug economy. It grew during the same broad period as several other independent cocaine organizations, demonstrating that the city's crack-era market was decentralized and competitive.

Distribution Structure

Accounts of Thompson's career describe a network of trusted associates and out-of-state distributors rather than a conventional hierarchical street gang. This structure allowed cocaine acquired through Southern California channels to move into other U.S. markets.

Money Laundering and Legitimate Businesses

Federal charges against Thompson included money laundering as well as drug conspiracy. Her case therefore provides an important organizational example of the financial layer of drug trafficking: proceeds had to be stored, transferred, disguised, or integrated into apparently legitimate economic activity.

Collapse

The network was weakened by arrests and cooperation by associates. Thompson became a fugitive before being arrested in 1993 and later convicted. The prosecution effectively ended the organization as a major interstate trafficking enterprise.
