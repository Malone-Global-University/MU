Young Melph Mafia

Overview

The Young Melph Mafia (YMM) was a Central City New Orleans street gang whose members grew up in and around the former Melpomene Housing Development. Federal prosecutors charged members with crack-cocaine distribution and firearms offenses in 2014.

Geographic Base

The organization was closely associated with the former Melpomene development and the surrounding Central City area. The name “Melph” reflected that neighborhood connection.

Drug Distribution

Federal prosecutors alleged that YMM members engaged in high-volume street-level drug distribution over several years. The 2014 superseding indictment charged a conspiracy involving 28 grams or more of crack cocaine.

Firearms

The federal indictment also alleged that members routinely carried firearms while distributing narcotics and charged multiple defendants with a firearms conspiracy.

Historical Significance

YMM fits the broader New Orleans pattern documented by federal drug-intelligence agencies: locally rooted crews often organized around a neighborhood or housing development rather than affiliating with a national gang.
