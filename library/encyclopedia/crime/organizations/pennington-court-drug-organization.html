Pennington Court Drug Organization

Overview

The Pennington Court Drug Organization is a descriptive encyclopedia label for the Newark narcotics ring headed by Simmied Tutt and targeted in the 2007 investigation known as Operation King & His Court.

Geographic Base

The organization controlled narcotics sales at the Pennington Court Housing Complex. Its participants included individuals from Newark, Irvington, New York, and Pennsylvania.

Hierarchy

The eight-month investigation identified Tutt as the principal target and Abdul Oliver as a senior subordinate. Investigators sought to separate mid- and upper-level figures from the street-level market.

Violence and Market Control

DEA reporting stated that the investigation focused on those responsible for the violence associated with the drug trade as well as the organization's day-to-day narcotics operations.

Takedown

More than one hundred officers participated in the April 2007 operation, which involved arrest warrants and searches.
