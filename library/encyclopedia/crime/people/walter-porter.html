Walter Porter

Overview

Walter Porter was a member of the Hankton Enterprise and one of four defendants who proceeded to trial in the major federal RICO prosecution arising from the organization's activities in New Orleans.

Role in the Enterprise

Federal prosecutors and the Fifth Circuit associated Porter with violent acts carried out in furtherance of the Hankton Enterprise and with efforts to retaliate against rivals and witnesses.

2016 Convictions

Porter was convicted of RICO conspiracy, obstruction conspiracy, multiple murders in aid of racketeering, firearms offenses, and additional violent-crime counts.

Sentence

Porter received life imprisonment in November 2016.

Appeal

The Fifth Circuit later vacated several of Porter's firearm convictions and remanded those counts because the jury may have relied on a RICO conspiracy as a legally invalid predicate crime of violence. The court otherwise affirmed the principal racketeering and murder convictions.
