Rashannibal “Prince” Drummond

Overview

Rashannibal “Prince” Drummond was a 23-year-old man killed outside the Velvet Room nightclub in Midtown Atlanta during the early morning hours of July 25, 2004. His death later became one of the principal violent-crime events associated with the Atlanta Black Mafia Family investigation.

Velvet Room Incident

Federal prosecutors later described the killing as following a confrontation in the nightclub parking lot. According to the government's public account, Drummond struck the side of a luxury vehicle to alert its driver that it was backing toward him. A fight followed, and Drummond was beaten and shot.

Fleming Daniels Prosecution

Fleming “Ill” Daniels was indicted in Fulton County in connection with Drummond's death. In 2010, Daniels pleaded guilty to voluntary manslaughter and received a twenty-year sentence.

Encyclopedia Treatment

Drummond should be indexed as a victim and event participant rather than as a criminal actor. His page is included because the killing materially affected the documented history of BMF in Atlanta and produced a separate state prosecution.
