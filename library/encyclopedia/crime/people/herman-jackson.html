Herman Jackson

Southeast Asian heroin importer and supplier associated with Leslie Atkinson and the Frank Lucas network.

Geographic Classification

Primary geography: Thailand / United States
New York connection: wholesale supply into the Lucas network

Overview

Herman Jackson was a retired military figure who became involved in a major Thailand-to-United States heroin-smuggling conspiracy. North Carolina appellate records state that Jackson and Leslie Atkinson began smuggling heroin from Thailand in 1968.

Role in the Network

New York court records place Jackson with Atkinson on the wholesale-supply side of Frank Lucas’s enterprise. The same records state that after Lucas cooperated against them, Atkinson and Jackson themselves provided information to investigators about the 1974 killing of Lucas retailer George Ford.

Legal Record

Jackson was convicted and imprisoned in major drug cases. Court records further state that Jackson, Atkinson, and Lucas continued narcotics activity from prison before law-enforcement cooperation fractured their relationships.

Historical Significance

Jackson links the Lucas story to a broader military-connected Southeast Asian heroin-importation network that existed independently of Harlem street distribution.

Evidence Status

A. Importation role and Lucas-network connection are supported by state appellate and New York court records.
