New York–Washington Research Cluster

  • Alberto “Alpo” Martinez — Harlem trafficker who expanded into Washington, D.C. and Northern Virginia; later federal cooperator.
  • Azie Faison — Harlem cocaine figure and later participant source on the era.
  • Richard “Rich” Porter — Harlem trafficker whose 1990 killing became a central event in the historical story later adapted in Paid in Full.
  • William “Donnell” Porter — 12-year-old kidnapping and murder victim; younger brother of Rich Porter.
  • Wayne “Silk” Perry — Washington, D.C. figure associated with Martinez and later convicted in a federal murder case.
  • Kenneth “Supreme” McGriff — founder of the Supreme Team in South Jamaica, Queens.
  • Gerald “Prince” Miller — McGriff’s nephew and second-in-command in the Supreme Team.
  • Domencio Benson — Brooklyn figure killed in Washington, D.C. in 1991.
  • Garrett “Gary” Terrell — lieutenant in Martinez’s organization and later homicide victim.
  • Alveta “Penny” Hopkins — cooperating witness / victim connected to the Perry prosecution.
  • Angelo Parisi — D.C. narcotics officer connected to the Martinez investigation.
  • Dan Reilly — FBI investigator connected to the Perry/Martinez investigation.
