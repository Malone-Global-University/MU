Jasmine “J-Real” Perry

Overview

Jasmine “J-Real” Perry was a member of the New Orleans street organization known as the 39ers and a defendant in the federal racketeering prosecution brought against the group.

Federal Indictment

Perry was one of the defendants charged in the 45-count RICO indictment unsealed in June 2015.

Trial

After a six-week trial, a federal jury convicted Perry of RICO conspiracy. The jury returned special findings on some homicide allegations and rejected others, an important distinction that should be preserved when describing the case.

Sentence

Perry received life imprisonment in July 2017.

Historical Significance

Perry's case helps reconstruct the 39ers through a formal federal verdict rather than through generalized street reputation. The verdict demonstrates why individual allegations within a racketeering case should be tracked separately from the overall conspiracy conviction.
