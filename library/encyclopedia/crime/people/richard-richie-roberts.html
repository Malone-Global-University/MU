Richard “Richie” Roberts

Essex County prosecutor and narcotics investigator associated with the New Jersey investigation and prosecution of Frank Lucas.

Geographic Classification

Primary jurisdiction: Essex County, New Jersey
New York connection: Frank Lucas / Country Boys investigation

Overview

Richard “Richie” Roberts was an assistant prosecutor in Essex County who became closely associated with the investigation and prosecution of Frank Lucas’s New Jersey narcotics activity.

Role in the Network

Later accounts identify Roberts as a major figure in the Special Narcotics Task Force investigation that culminated in the 1975 Teaneck raid and related prosecutions. The 2007 film American Gangster substantially dramatized his role; the Russell Crowe character was not a literal documentary reconstruction of every investigator involved.

Legal Record

Roberts later left prosecution for private practice and represented Lucas in a later drug case. His later personal legal history is outside the scope of this network entry.

Historical Significance

Roberts belongs on the law-enforcement side of the Lucas map and is useful for separating the real multi-agency investigation from the film’s simplified detective narrative.

Evidence Status

B. His connection to Lucas is well established by contemporary/later reporting, while the film representation should not be treated as a literal case history.
