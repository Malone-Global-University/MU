William “Donnell” Porter

Twelve-year-old Harlem kidnapping and murder victim whose case became intertwined with the criminal history surrounding his older brother, Rich Porter.

Overview

William “Donnell” Porter was abducted in Harlem on December 5, 1989 while walking to school. The kidnappers sought ransom from his family because his older brother Richard “Rich” Porter was reputed to have substantial proceeds from drug trafficking.

Kidnapping

Contemporary reporting described a $500,000 ransom demand and reported that part of William’s finger was sent to the family as pressure to pay. Rich Porter was killed while William was still missing.

Discovery of Body

William’s body was found in the Bronx on January 28, 1990. The event should be documented on a dedicated kidnapping-and-murder page so that William is treated as a victim in his own right rather than merely as an element of his brother’s biography.

Historical Significance

The case demonstrates how kidnapping, extortion, drug-market wealth, family vulnerability, and homicide could intersect. It is also a useful test case for the encyclopedia’s evidence standard because later retellings attribute responsibility differently and should be reconciled against the controlling case record.
