Demetrius “Troll” Williams

Overview

Demetrius “Troll” Williams was identified by federal prosecutors as a leader of the Columbia Point Dawgs, or CPD, a Boston gang with roots in the former Columbia Point Housing Development.

Columbia Point Dawgs

Federal records describe CPD as a citywide gang that developed drug-trafficking crews across Boston after emerging from Columbia Point in the 1980s.

Drug Distribution

Williams admitted involvement in trafficking at least 880 grams of crack cocaine, ten kilograms of cocaine, and 700 grams of heroin in Massachusetts with other CPD members and associates.

Family and Crew Structure

Federal prosecutors identified several relatives and associates, including Williams' brothers and father, as participants in the broader CPD trafficking structure.

Conviction and Sentence

Williams pleaded guilty to RICO conspiracy and conspiracy to possess with intent to distribute cocaine, crack cocaine, and heroin. In September 2017, he received a 151-month federal prison sentence.

Historical Significance

Williams' case provides a documented leadership example within CPD and helps distinguish the organization from earlier Orchard Park-based networks and later neighborhood crews.
