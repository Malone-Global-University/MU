Eric “Fats” Wilkins

Principal lieutenant and enforcer in the Chambers Brothers Organization.

Overview

Eric L. “Fats” Wilkins was identified by the Sixth Circuit as the principal lieutenant of the Chambers Brothers Organization. His case provides unusually specific documentation of the internal management structure beneath Larry and Billy Joe Chambers.

Role

Federal evidence showed that Wilkins served as an enforcer and member of the organization’s “wrecking crew,” which used violence and intimidation to maintain discipline. In the Chambers brothers’ absence, he supervised crack-house operations, oversaw drug distribution and collections, paid organization members, delivered drugs to sales locations, and collected proceeds.

Historical Significance

Wilkins demonstrates why the Chambers organization should be studied as more than a family operation. The enterprise possessed delegated management, enforcement, logistics, and retail functions characteristic of a structured criminal organization.

Evidence Status

A. Role and duties are stated directly in the Sixth Circuit record.
