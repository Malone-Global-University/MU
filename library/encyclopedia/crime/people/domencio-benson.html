Domencio Benson

New York drug figure killed in Washington, D.C. in 1991; Wayne Perry later pleaded guilty in a federal case that included Benson’s murder.

Overview

Domencio Benson was a New York drug figure whose killing became one of the homicide counts in the federal case against Wayne “Silk” Perry. Contemporary reporting rendered his first name as Domenico, while the federal government’s death-penalty notice in the Perry case used Domencio. This encyclopedia uses the federal spelling while preserving the contemporary variant.

Killing in Washington

Benson, age 27, was found shot in the 1400 block of Seventh Street in Washington’s Shaw area in July 1991. Contemporary reporting identified him as being from New York and stated that police initially had no suspects.

Federal Perry Case

The government’s federal death-penalty notice against Wayne Perry identified Count Five as the murder of Domencio Benson and alleged that Perry intentionally killed him. Perry later pleaded guilty to five first-degree murders in the broader drug-conspiracy prosecution.

Connection to Alberto Martinez

Later accounts associate the Benson killing with Alberto “Alpo” Martinez and describe it as part of the violent enforcement surrounding Martinez’s Washington operations. The strongest documentary anchor is Perry’s federal prosecution; detailed claims about Martinez’s immediate role should be attributed to indictments, participant accounts, or later reporting when used.

Network Connections

  • Alberto “Alpo” Martinez — later accounts connect Benson’s death to the Martinez organization.

Evidence Status
