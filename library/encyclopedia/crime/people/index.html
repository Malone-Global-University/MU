New York–Washington Research Cluster

  • Alberto “Alpo” Martinez — Harlem trafficker who expanded into Washington, D.C. and Northern Virginia; later federal cooperator.
  • Azie Faison — Harlem cocaine figure and later participant source on the era.
  • Richard “Rich” Porter — Harlem trafficker whose 1990 killing became a central event in the historical story later adapted in Paid in Full.
  • William “Donnell” Porter — 12-year-old kidnapping and murder victim; younger brother of Rich Porter.
  • Richard “Fritz” Simmons — Harlem wholesaler remembered in participant histories as the “Consignment King”; source-limited entry.
  • Kevin Chiles — Harlem/Washington trafficker and later participant source associated with Don Diva.
  • Clarence “Preacher” Heatley — leader of the Preacher Crew; federal records connect him to the Donnell Porter kidnapping and murder.
  • John “Apple” Porter — founding Preacher Crew member and uncle of Donnell Porter; exact kidnapping role requires source distinction.
  • Kenneth “Supreme” McGriff — founder of the Supreme Team in South Jamaica, Queens.
  • Gerald “Prince” Miller — McGriff’s nephew, second-in-command, and later operational leader of the Supreme Team.
  • Lorenzo “Fat Cat” Nichols — leader of a major South Jamaica narcotics organization and documented 1987 supplier ally of the Supreme Team.
  • Howard “Pappy” Mason — leader of the Bebos and federal defendant convicted of ordering the murder of Officer Edward Byrne.
  • Brian Rooney — New York State parole officer murdered in 1985 after enforcing Nichols’s parole.
  • Edward Byrne — NYPD officer murdered in South Jamaica in 1988 while guarding a drug-case witness.
  • Rayful Edmond III — leader of a large late-1980s Washington cocaine organization; later government cooperator.
  • Melvin D. Butler — California-based supply connection and convicted co-conspirator in the Edmond case.
  • Curtis “Curtbone” Chambers — Edmond co-defendant, later documentary collaborator and participant source on D.C. networks.
  • Wayne “Silk” Perry — Washington, D.C. figure associated with Martinez and later convicted in a federal murder case.
  • Shelton “Shorty Pop” Walker / Watkins — 1989 D.C. “most wanted” fugitive and original Junkyard Band vocalist.
  • Matthew N. Blake — December 1989 Washington homicide victim in a case charged to Shelton Walker.
  • Domencio Benson — New York figure killed in Washington, D.C. in 1991; contemporary reporting also spelled his name “Domenico.”
  • Garrett “Gary” Terrell — lieutenant in Martinez’s organization and later homicide victim.
  • Alveta “Penny” Hopkins — cooperating witness / victim connected to the Perry prosecution.
  • Angelo Parisi — D.C. narcotics officer connected to the Martinez investigation.
  • Dan Reilly — FBI investigator connected to the Perry/Martinez investigation.
