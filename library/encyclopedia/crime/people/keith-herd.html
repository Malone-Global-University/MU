Keith Herd

Overview

Keith Herd was the leader of a Newark heroin and crack-cocaine organization operating around Hayes Street and 14th Avenue in the New Community Corporation development. Federal prosecutors identified the organization as composed of members of the Brick City Brim set of the Bloods.

Leadership

Federal investigators described Herd as the main heroin supplier in and around the NCC area and as the leader of the Brick City Brim set operating there.

Organization Practices

Members shared narcotics, customers, and proceeds, alerted one another to police and rivals, and raised bail money for associates following arrests. The organization also supplied customers and distributors outside the immediate NCC area.

2018 Takedowns

Federal investigators charged an initial group of defendants in March 2018 and a larger second group in August. The investigation used physical and video surveillance, confidential sources, controlled narcotics purchases, seizures, and telephone wiretaps.

Continuing Criminal Enterprise Charge

In 2019, a federal grand jury added a continuing-criminal-enterprise charge against Herd based on his alleged leadership role. Herd later pleaded guilty to heroin-conspiracy and distribution charges.

Sentence

In May 2021, Herd received a twenty-year federal prison sentence and five years of supervised release.
