Frankalphonse “Frank” James

Longtime Barnes associate and one of the seven governing members of The Council.

Geographic Classification

Borough: Manhattan
Neighborhood: Harlem / Upper Manhattan network
City: New York City

Overview

Federal appellate records identify James as a longtime, close Barnes associate in the narcotics business and a member of The Council.

Role in the Network

James operated his own narcotics business within the cooperative Council structure. Barnes later became a government witness against former associates, including defendants in the Council prosecution.

Legal Record

James was prosecuted in the early 1980s for narcotics and related racketeering activity; the Second Circuit affirmed convictions arising from the Council case.

Later Significance

James is a core Council member and should be mapped separately from non-member lieutenants prosecuted in Barnes’s 1977 conspiracy case.

Evidence Status

A. Longtime association, Council membership, and prosecution are documented in federal appellate decisions.
