Wallace Rice

One of the seven governing members of The Council in the 1970s–1980s Harlem narcotics network.

Geographic Classification

Borough: Manhattan
Neighborhood: Harlem / Upper Manhattan network
City: New York City

Overview

Rice was a longtime Barnes associate and one of the Council members identified in federal prosecutions.

Role in the Network

The Council case describes Rice as part of the seven-member governing body coordinating narcotics sources, territories, disputes, and the businesses of incarcerated members.

Legal Record

Rice was prosecuted with other Council figures in the early 1980s; the Second Circuit affirmed the Council convictions in 1985.

Later Significance

Rice belongs in the core Council map rather than being treated as a peripheral Barnes lieutenant.

Evidence Status

A. Council membership and prosecution are established by federal court records.
