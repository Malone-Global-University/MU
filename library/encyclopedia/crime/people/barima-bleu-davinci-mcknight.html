Barima “Bleu DaVinci” McKnight

Overview

Barima “Bleu DaVinci” McKnight was an Atlanta BMF associate and recording artist who was charged in the 2007 Atlanta cocaine-distribution case. Federal sentencing records document his involvement in handling cocaine and drug proceeds.

BMF Association

McKnight was one of sixteen defendants charged in the Atlanta BMF indictment. His public identity was closely associated with BMF's music and entertainment branding, but the criminal case should be distinguished from broader claims about artists or industry figures who were never charged.

Operational Role

In later appellate litigation involving co-defendant Vernon Coleman, the Eleventh Circuit contrasted the defendants' roles and described McKnight as loading and unloading cocaine and money and counting money. That description provides a limited but judicially documented account of his operational involvement.

Federal Sentence

In October 2008, McKnight was sentenced to five years and four months in federal prison, followed by five years of supervised release.

Source Caution

Popular accounts of BMF often merge the music-label image, Atlanta nightlife, and the drug conspiracy into one narrative. This encyclopedia separates McKnight's documented criminal conduct from uncharged associations with musicians, promoters, or entertainment figures.
