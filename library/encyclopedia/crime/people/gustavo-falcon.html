Gustavo Falcon

Overview

Gustavo Falcon was a member of the Falcon–Magluta organization and the younger brother of Augusto “Willie” Falcon. He was charged in the 1991 federal cocaine case but remained a fugitive for approximately twenty-six years.

Organization Role

Federal prosecutors linked Gustavo Falcon to large interstate cocaine shipments. His later guilty plea held him accountable for participation in the conspiracy operated by Willie Falcon and Salvador Magluta.

1991 Indictment and Fugitive Period

The April 1991 indictment charged Gustavo Falcon with participating in the cocaine conspiracy. He disappeared before prosecution and remained outside federal custody until April 2017.

2018 Guilty Plea

On February 1, 2018, Falcon pleaded guilty to conspiracy to possess with intent to distribute and to distribute cocaine.

Sentence

In April 2018, a federal judge sentenced Falcon to 135 months in prison. The U.S. Attorney's Office described him as the final charged member of the original case to be brought to justice.

Historical Significance

Falcon's case extends the operational history of the 1991 prosecution into the twenty-first century and demonstrates the long tail of fugitive investigations arising from South Florida's cocaine era.
