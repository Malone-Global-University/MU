Simmied Tutt

Overview

Simmied Tutt was identified by the Drug Enforcement Administration as the head of a Newark narcotics organization targeted in the 2007 multi-agency operation known as Operation King & His Court.

Pennington Court Organization

DEA reporting described Tutt and approximately twenty co-conspirators from Newark, Irvington, New York, and Pennsylvania as controlling the narcotics trade at the Pennington Court Housing Complex.

Hierarchy

The eight-month investigation was designed to establish the organization's hierarchy and identify those responsible for leadership, violence, and day-to-day narcotics operations.

April 2007 Takedown

More than one hundred federal, state, county, and local officers participated in the operation. Authorities executed arrest and search warrants and took Tutt and other identified members into custody.

Historical Significance

Tutt's organization illustrates the persistence of housing-complex-centered narcotics markets in Newark after the decline of the larger citywide organizations documented in the 1970s and 1980s.
