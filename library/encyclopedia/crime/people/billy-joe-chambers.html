Billy Joe Chambers

Co-leader of the Chambers Brothers Organization, a major Detroit crack-cocaine distribution network of the 1980s.

Overview

Billy Joe Chambers and his brother Larry Chambers organized and directed the Chambers Brothers Organization in Detroit. Federal appellate records describe a structured crack-cocaine network operating from approximately 1983 through February 1988.

Operational Role

Court testimony placed Billy Joe Chambers in direct operational roles, including crack-cocaine manufacturing at organization locations. The enterprise used multiple sales houses and a hierarchy of workers, supervisors, and enforcers.

Federal Conviction

Billy Joe Chambers was convicted of conspiracy and operating a continuing criminal enterprise. A later district-court opinion records a sentence of 365 months on the continuing-criminal-enterprise conviction after the overlapping conspiracy count was addressed.

Evidence Status

A. Organizational leadership, operating methods, conviction, and sentence are documented by federal courts.
