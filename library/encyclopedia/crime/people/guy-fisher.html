Guy Thomas Fisher

Senior Council member and Barnes lieutenant who preceded Joseph “Jazz” Hayden in the No. 2 position.

Geographic Classification

Borough: Manhattan
Neighborhood: Harlem / Upper Manhattan network
City: New York City

Overview

Fisher was one of the seven members of The Council and a major figure in the Harlem narcotics network associated with Barnes.

Role in the Network

The 1977 Barnes appellate record describes Guy Fisher as the previous Number 2 man before his incarceration, after which Joseph Hayden was promoted to that position. Fisher later remained a central defendant in the Council prosecution.

Legal Record

In the 1983 Council case, Fisher was convicted of narcotics, continuing-criminal-enterprise, racketeering, and related offenses and received a life sentence.

Later Significance

After nearly four decades in federal custody, Fisher received compassionate release in October 2020. The court emphasized his educational achievements and rehabilitation while also describing the gravity of the Council’s crimes.

Evidence Status

A. Council membership, hierarchy, conviction, sentence, and 2020 release are supported by federal court records.
