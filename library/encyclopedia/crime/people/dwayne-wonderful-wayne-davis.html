Dwayne “Wonderful Wayne” Davis

Early Young Boys Incorporated leader in Detroit’s late-1970s heroin economy.

Overview

Dwayne “Wonderful Wayne” Davis is identified by the University of Michigan’s Detroit policing history project as one of the principal early leaders of Young Boys Incorporated. YBI became nationally notable for its hierarchical organization, use of young street sellers, branded heroin packets, and efforts to obtain narcotics outside older Detroit supply arrangements.

Role in YBI

The historical record describes Davis as part of the leadership group that included Milton “Butch” Jones, Raymond “Baby Ray” Peoples, and Mark “Block” Marshall. For encyclopedia purposes, Davis should be treated as an early organizational leader rather than merged with later YBI leadership phases that federal appellate records center more heavily on Jones.

Source Limitation

Compared with Jones, Davis is less fully described in the appellate cases used for this project. Biographical details beyond his leadership role should therefore be added only as contemporary newspaper, court, or archival material is verified.

Evidence Status

B. Leadership role is supported by an academic historical project drawing on Detroit archival sources; fuller biography remains source-limited.
