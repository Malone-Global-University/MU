Garrett “Gary” Terrell

Lieutenant in Alberto “Alpo” Martinez’s drug organization who was later murdered in a case incorporated into the federal prosecution of Wayne Perry and Tyrone Price.

Overview

Garrett Terrell, often referred to in later accounts as “Gary” or “Big Head Gary,” was identified by contemporary reporting as a onetime lieutenant in Alberto Martinez’s drug organization. His death became part of the federal drug-and-murder case involving Wayne Perry and Tyrone Price.

Role in the Martinez Organization

The clearest contemporary description of Terrell’s position comes from reporting on the 1994 federal case, which called him a former lieutenant in Martinez’s drug organization. Later oral histories and documentary accounts place him within Martinez’s Washington network and connect him to other violent episodes, but those details should be attributed individually.

Murder and Federal Case

The federal death-penalty notice against Wayne Perry identified Count Seventeen as the murder of Garrett Terrell. In 1994, Tyrone LaSalle Price pleaded guilty to murdering Terrell. Contemporary reporting stated that prosecutors alleged Perry and Price killed victims at Martinez’s behest.

Network Connections

  • Alberto “Alpo” Martinez — Terrell was described as a lieutenant in Martinez’s organization.
  • Wayne “Silk” Perry — federal prosecution included Terrell’s murder.
  • Tyrone LaSalle Price — pleaded guilty to murdering Terrell.
  • Alveta “Penny” Hopkins — another victim in the same federal prosecution.

Evidence Status

A/B. Terrell’s organizational role and murder prosecution are supported by contemporary reporting and federal filings. Claims about his participation in unrelated homicides should be sourced separately.
