Jeremiah “Germ Gee” Mines

Overview

Jeremiah “Germ Gee” Mines was identified by federal prosecutors as a member of the Orchard Park Trailblazers in Roxbury.

Drug Sales

Federal prosecutors documented two crack-cocaine sales by Mines to a cooperating witness in November 2015 and March 2016 near the Orchard Gardens K-8 Pilot School.

2017 Federal Case

Mines was one of twelve defendants charged in the federal sweep announced in June 2017 following a nearly two-year investigation around Orchard Gardens.

Guilty Plea

In October 2017, Mines pleaded guilty to two counts of distribution of cocaine base within 1,000 feet of a school.

Historical Significance

Mines' case contributes to the reconstruction of Orchard Park Trailblazers activity through controlled purchases and formal federal proceedings rather than generalized reputation.
