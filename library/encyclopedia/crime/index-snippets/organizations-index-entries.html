Initial Organizations and Networks

  • Country Boys — Frank Lucas Organization — Harlem heroin distribution network led by Frank Lucas.
  • Atkinson–Jackson Heroin Import Network — Southeast Asian wholesale/import pipeline linked by court records to the Lucas organization.
  • The Council — seven-member Harlem-centered governing body led by Nicky Barnes, active 1972–1983.
  • Preacher Crew — Harlem-and-Bronx racketeering enterprise led by Clarence “Preacher” Heatley.
  • Supreme Team — South Jamaica, Queens organization founded by Kenneth “Supreme” McGriff.
  • Lorenzo “Fat Cat” Nichols Organization — major South Jamaica narcotics enterprise; documented supplier ally of the Supreme Team.
  • Bebos — Howard “Pappy” Mason-led South Jamaica crack organization.
  • Martinez Trafficking Organization — Working encyclopedia label for the New York–Washington organization described in federal records; title should follow the terminology used by the controlling case documents.
  • Rayful Edmond Organization — Washington, D.C. trafficking organization of the late 1980s.
  • Cash Money Brothers — Brooklyn racketeering and narcotics organization centered in Lafayette Gardens.
  • McGriff Enterprise — Later Period — post-incarceration wholesale narcotics and racketeering enterprise distinct from the original Supreme Team phase.

Detroit Organizations

  • Young Boys Incorporated — structured Detroit heroin/cocaine organization of the late 1970s and 1980s.
  • Chambers Brothers Organization — 1980s Detroit crack-cocaine network.
  • Dog Pound — 1995–2001 Detroit enterprise led by Milton “Butch” Jones.
  • Black Mafia Family — Detroit-founded multistate cocaine and money-laundering organization.
