Killing of Rashannibal “Prince” Drummond

Overview

During the early morning hours of July 25, 2004, 23-year-old Rashannibal “Prince” Drummond was beaten and shot in the parking lot of the Velvet Room nightclub in Midtown Atlanta.

Confrontation

Federal prosecutors later stated that the incident began after Drummond struck the side of a luxury vehicle to warn its driver not to back over him. A confrontation followed in the parking lot.

Death

Drummond was assaulted and shot during the incident. His death became one of the most prominent violence-related episodes discussed in the Atlanta BMF prosecution.

Fleming Daniels Case

Fleming “Ill” Daniels was charged in Fulton County in connection with the killing. In 2010, Daniels pleaded guilty to voluntary manslaughter and received a twenty-year sentence.

Claim Status

The federal BMF case and the state homicide case were separate proceedings. The broader presence of BMF associates at the scene should not be used to assign criminal responsibility to individuals who were not convicted in connection with Drummond's death.
