Northwest Atlanta BMF Stash-House Raid, 2004

Overview

In late November 2004, Atlanta Police Department officers and DEA agents raided an expensive residence in northwest Atlanta associated with the Black Mafia Family. Federal prosecutors later identified the raid as a turning point in BMF's Atlanta operations.

Search

The house was unoccupied when officers entered. The search recovered three firearms, fictitious identification documents, BMF paraphernalia, and marijuana.

Aftermath

According to the U.S. Attorney's Office, Demetrius “Big Meech” Flenory and much of BMF left Atlanta after the raid. The organization remained the subject of investigations in multiple federal districts.

Historical Significance

The raid illustrates how pressure on a stash or logistics location can affect an organization even when no arrests occur at the scene. It also helps explain the transition from BMF's conspicuous Atlanta phase to the multi-state federal prosecution period.
