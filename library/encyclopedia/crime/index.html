Crime Encyclopedia

Explore people, organizations, events, and stories connected to crime, criminal activity, law enforcement, organized crime, illicit economies, public corruption, and the historical and social contexts in which they developed.

Overview

The Crime Encyclopedia organizes entries by the type of subject being documented. Some entries focus on individuals or organizations, while others examine a specific event, criminal episode, investigation, conflict, prosecution, or larger story that connects multiple people and events.

The purpose of this section is historical and encyclopedic: to document criminal networks, major cases, institutions, significant events, and the broader communities and periods in which they operated.

Categories

  • Stories

    Narrative and thematic entries that connect multiple people, organizations, places, and events into a broader criminal history, case history, or social context.

  • Events

    Specific incidents and developments such as crimes, killings, kidnappings, robberies, investigations, arrests, prosecutions, raids, trials, prison events, and major turning points.

  • Organizations

    Criminal enterprises, gangs, trafficking networks, organized-crime groups, law-enforcement bodies, investigative units, and other institutions central to documented cases.

  • Individuals

    Biographical entries on offenders, alleged offenders, victims, investigators, prosecutors, witnesses, informants, law-enforcement officials, and other significant figures.
