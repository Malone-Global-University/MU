New York Boys Federal Indictments, 1990–1991

Overview

On December 11, 1990, a federal grand jury indicted Darryl Whiting and thirty co-defendants in the investigation of the New York Boys drug organization. A superseding indictment returned on April 11, 1991 expanded the case to fifty defendants.

Charges

The indictments included cocaine conspiracy, substantive cocaine-distribution offenses, firearms charges, money laundering, and a continuing-criminal-enterprise allegation against Whiting.

Scale

The expansion to fifty defendants reflected the breadth of the federal investigation and the number of individuals alleged to have participated in or supported the organization.

Historical Significance

The indictments marked one of the most significant federal crack-era prosecutions in Boston and created the judicial record through which the New York Boys can be reconstructed today.
