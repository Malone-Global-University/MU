Killing of Matthew N. Blake

December 6, 1989 homicide in Southeast Washington that became one of the murder charges connected to fugitive Shelton “Shorty Pop” Walker/Watkins.

Summary

Matthew N. Blake was killed on December 6, 1989 during a shootout in the 3300 block of Camden Street SE, Washington, D.C.

Contemporary Investigation

When Shelton Cortez Walker was arrested later that month, D.C. police identified Walker—who also used the surname Watkins—as wanted on a murder charge in connection with Blake’s death. The same arrest report connected Walker to several other violent cases and described the arrest as a joint D.C.–Montgomery County–FBI effort.

Evidence Limits

The currently identified contemporary report establishes the date, location, and murder charge. It does not by itself establish the motive, the full sequence of the shootout, or a connection between Blake’s killing and the later Martinez–Perry trafficking organization. Those claims should remain outside the event entry until stronger documentation is located.
