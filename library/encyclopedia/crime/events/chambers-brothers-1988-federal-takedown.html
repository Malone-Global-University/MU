1988 Federal Prosecution of the Chambers Brothers Organization

Federal indictment and trial that dismantled a major Detroit crack-cocaine network.

Event

In 1988 a federal grand jury in the Eastern District of Michigan returned a large indictment against members of the Chambers Brothers Organization. A joint trial began in October 1988 against senior members and associates.

Charges

The prosecution included narcotics conspiracy, continuing-criminal-enterprise, firearm, and related charges. Federal appellate opinions describe the underlying organization as a structured crack network led by Larry and Billy Joe Chambers.

Result

Senior leaders and organization members were convicted, and the resulting sentences and appeals effectively ended the organization’s period as a major Detroit crack-distribution enterprise.
