Operation Rising Tide Federal Sweep, 2015

Overview

On June 18, 2015, more than 500 federal, state, and local officers carried out a coordinated sweep targeting the Columbia Point Dawgs after a two-year wiretap investigation known as Operation Rising Tide.

Indictments

Six federal indictments charging forty-eight leaders, members, and associates were unsealed. The cases alleged drug-trafficking and firearms offenses.

Searches and Seizures

Investigators executed more than thirty search warrants. Federal prosecutors reported seizures including firearms, heroin, cocaine, crack cocaine, oxycodone, vehicles, drug paraphernalia, and approximately $1.5 million in cash.

Wiretap Intelligence

Court-authorized cellphone wiretaps allowed investigators to monitor drug-trafficking activity and, on some occasions, intervene when communications indicated that a shooting might occur.

Historical Significance

The sweep demonstrates the scale of modern federal gang enforcement in Boston: multi-agency surveillance, electronic interception, coordinated arrests, asset seizure, and multiple simultaneous indictments.
