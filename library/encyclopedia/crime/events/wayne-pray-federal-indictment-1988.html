Wayne Pray Federal Indictment, 1988

Overview

In 1988, federal prosecutors in New Jersey indicted Wayne “Akbar” Pray and associates in a major narcotics case targeting The Family.

Federal Theory

The government alleged that Pray administered a large cocaine and marijuana enterprise with interstate and international dimensions.

Trial

The case proceeded to a lengthy federal jury trial in Newark in 1989. Prosecutors used testimony concerning narcotics, travel, finances, expenditures, and organizational relationships.

Outcome

Pray was convicted in October 1989 of multiple drug offenses, including being a principal administrator of a continuing criminal enterprise.
