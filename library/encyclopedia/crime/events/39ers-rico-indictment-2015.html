39ers RICO Indictment, 2015

Overview

On June 18, 2015, a federal grand jury returned a 45-count indictment against members of the New Orleans street gang known as the 39ers. The indictment was publicly unsealed the following day.

Racketeering Allegations

Prosecutors charged defendants with RICO violations, drug trafficking, firearms offenses, and multiple homicides allegedly committed in furtherance of the enterprise.

Organizational Theory

The indictment described the 39ers as an alliance between G-Strip and 3NG formed to strengthen drug trafficking and violent-crime capacity.

Trial Outcome

Ten defendants were convicted following a six-week trial in 2017. Verdicts varied across the homicide allegations, and those distinctions are preserved on the case and individual biography pages.
