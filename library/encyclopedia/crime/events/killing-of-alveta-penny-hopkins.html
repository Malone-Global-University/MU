Killing of Alveta “Penny” Hopkins

Witness-retaliation homicide incorporated into the federal drug-and-murder prosecution of Wayne Perry and Tyrone Price.

Overview

Alveta “Penny” Hopkins was murdered after cooperating with federal investigators in the case against Alberto “Alpo” Martinez. Her death became one of the homicide and witness-retaliation components of the federal prosecution surrounding Wayne Perry’s organization.

Federal Case

The government’s death-penalty notice against Perry identified Count Thirteen as the murder of Alveta Hopkins. In 1994, Tyrone LaSalle Price pleaded guilty to murdering Hopkins and to retaliating against a federal witness.

Investigative Significance

The Hopkins killing is important because it demonstrates that the Martinez/Perry investigation involved not only trafficking and disputes among dealers but also violence directed at people believed to be cooperating with authorities.

Evidence Status

A/B. Supported by federal prosecution records and contemporary reporting.
