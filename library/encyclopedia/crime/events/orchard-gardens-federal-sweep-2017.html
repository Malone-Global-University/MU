Orchard Gardens Federal Sweep, 2017

Overview

On June 15, 2017, federal prosecutors announced charges against twelve men accused of selling crack cocaine in and around the Orchard Gardens Housing Development in Roxbury.

Investigation

The charges followed a nearly two-year investigation. Three cooperating witnesses conducted thirty-four controlled drug purchases between October 2015 and May 2017.

Gang Associations

Eleven of the twelve defendants were alleged members or associates of the Orchard Park Trailblazers or Vine/Forest Street, another local gang.

Firearms

An earlier phase of the investigation involved the purchase of twelve firearms and crack cocaine from an identified Orchard Park Trailblazers member.

Historical Significance

The operation shows a later enforcement model focused on controlled purchases and narrowly documented street-level drug transactions around a specific housing development.
