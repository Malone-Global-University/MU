Brick City Brim NCC Takedowns, 2018

Overview

Federal authorities carried out multiple arrest waves in 2018 against a heroin and crack-cocaine organization operating around the New Community Corporation development in Newark.

March 2018 Charges

Keith Herd and an initial group of defendants were charged in March 2018 following an FBI-led investigation into the organization.

August 2018 Expansion

Federal prosecutors announced charges against sixteen additional defendants in August, expanding the scope of the case.

Investigative Methods

The inquiry used surveillance, confidential sources, controlled narcotics purchases, seizures, record analysis, and court-authorized telephone wiretaps.

Historical Significance

The takedowns document a modern Newark drug organization combining local gang affiliation with shared narcotics supply, shared customers, open-air sales, and coordinated warnings about police and rivals.
