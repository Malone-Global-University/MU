Teaneck Raid on Frank Lucas — January 1975

Major law-enforcement raid on Frank Lucas’s New Jersey residence that accelerated the collapse of his narcotics network.

Event

In January 1975, federal and local investigators raided Lucas’s home in Teaneck, New Jersey. Authorities seized more than $500,000 in cash and other evidence. The raid became one of the most visible events in the investigation of the Country Boys organization.

Investigative Significance

The raid was followed by broader arrests, indictments, and cooperation from people connected to Lucas. Contemporary and later accounts differ on some dramatic details, so the encyclopedia should anchor the event to the documented seizure and subsequent prosecutions rather than film scenes.
