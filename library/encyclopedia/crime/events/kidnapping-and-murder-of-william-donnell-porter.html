Kidnapping and Murder of William “Donnell” Porter

Harlem ransom kidnapping beginning December 5, 1989 and ending with the discovery of twelve-year-old William Porter’s body in the Bronx in January 1990.

Summary

William “Donnell” Porter was abducted while traveling to school in Harlem. The kidnappers demanded money from the Porter family because William’s older brother, Richard “Rich” Porter, was reputed to be a successful drug dealer.

Chronology

  • December 5, 1989: William is kidnapped on the way to school.
  • December 1989: ransom communications demand hundreds of thousands of dollars; part of William’s finger is delivered as coercive proof.
  • January 3–4, 1990: Rich Porter is killed while William remains missing.
  • January 28, 1990: William’s body is found in the Bronx.
