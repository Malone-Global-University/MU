Ricky Ross 1995 Sting and Arrest

Overview

In early 1995, federal authorities conducted an undercover operation that reunited Ricky Donnell “Freeway Rick” Ross with his former cocaine supplier Oscar Danilo Blandón, who was then cooperating with the Drug Enforcement Administration. The operation culminated in Ross' arrest in the San Diego area during a planned 100-kilogram cocaine transaction.

Undercover Negotiations

According to the Department of Justice Inspector General's later review, Ross negotiated a 100-kilogram cocaine deal with Blandón in February 1995. The planned transaction involved a substantial cash down payment and additional participants.

Arrest

On March 2, 1995, Ross and associates were arrested during the planned delivery in the San Diego area. Blandón and an undercover DEA officer were central to the operation.

Legal Consequences

The federal prosecution charged Ross and co-defendants with cocaine conspiracy and distribution offenses. Ross was convicted by a federal jury in March 1996 and received a mandatory life sentence later that year under the sentencing framework then applied to his prior convictions.

Controversy

The use of Blandón as a government informant became especially controversial after reporting emphasized his earlier Contra connections and his role as one of Ross' suppliers. Ross raised entrapment and government-misconduct arguments, but the jury rejected the entrapment defense and the trial court upheld the conviction.
