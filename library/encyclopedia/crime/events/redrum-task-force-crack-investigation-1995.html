REDRUM Task Force Crack Investigation, 1995

Overview

In 1995, the New Orleans REDRUM Task Force culminated a sixteen-month Organized Crime Drug Enforcement Task Force investigation into a violent crack-cocaine organization operating in two New Orleans housing projects.

Investigation

The federal Department of Justice described the case as targeting a violent drug-trafficking organization and resulting in the arrests of five subjects.

Violence During the Investigation

According to the Attorney General's annual report, fifteen murders occurred in the two housing projects during the investigative period.

Aftermath

The same federal report stated that homicides declined in the area immediately after the arrests. Because the available summary does not identify every participant or both projects by name, this page avoids assigning the REDRUM case to a specific project-based organization without additional documentation.

Historical Significance

The operation provides an early federal example of the enforcement strategy later used more extensively in New Orleans: combining drug investigations with targeted efforts against violent neighborhood organizations.
