Orchard Park, Roxbury

Overview

The former Orchard Park Housing Project in Roxbury was the primary geographic base of the New York Boys drug-distribution organization during Boston's crack era.

New York Boys

The First Circuit expressly described the New York Boys as a large-scale drug ring operating out of Orchard Park. The organization was led by Darryl Whiting and became the target of an extensive undercover federal investigation.

Federal Case

The resulting indictments ultimately included fifty defendants and charged cocaine conspiracy, substantive distribution offenses, firearms violations, money laundering, and a continuing criminal enterprise.

Later Redevelopment

The area was later redeveloped and became associated with Orchard Gardens. The later Orchard Park Trailblazers operated in the same broad geography, but they should not be treated as the same organization as the New York Boys.
