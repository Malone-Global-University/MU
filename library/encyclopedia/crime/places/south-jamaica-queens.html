South Jamaica, Queens — Crime-History Index

Geographic index for Crime Encyclopedia entries connected to South Jamaica and the Baisley Park area of Queens.

Scope

This page indexes crime-history material associated with South Jamaica, Queens. It is not a general history of the neighborhood and should not be read as characterizing South Jamaica primarily through crime.

Baisley Park

The Baisley Park Houses are a New York City Housing Authority development in South Jamaica. Historical public-housing documentation dates the development to 1961. The federal Supreme Team case repeatedly identifies the vicinity of the Baisley Park Houses as the organization’s original geographic base.

Supreme Team

The principal Crime Encyclopedia organization currently associated with this place is the Supreme Team, founded by Kenneth “Supreme” McGriff. Gerald “Prince” Miller served as second-in-command and later assumed operational leadership.

Relationship to Harlem

South Jamaica and Harlem were separate New York crime geographies. McGriff and Miller should not be inserted into the Rich Porter–Azie Faison–Alpo Martinez organization merely because all became well known in New York street history and hip-hop culture. Connections should be added only when a supplier, partnership, rivalry, family, or case relationship is documented.
