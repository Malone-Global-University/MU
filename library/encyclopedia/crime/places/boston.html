Boston

Overview

Boston, Massachusetts, developed a network of neighborhood- and housing-development-based drug organizations during the crack era and subsequent decades. Roxbury and Dorchester became especially important geographic centers for cocaine, crack, heroin, firearms, and gang investigations.

Crack Era

The New York Boys operated a major cocaine-distribution ring from Orchard Park in Roxbury during the late 1980s and early 1990s. The federal case eventually included fifty defendants.

Citywide Gang Networks

The Columbia Point Dawgs originated in the former Columbia Point Housing Development in the 1980s and later established drug-trafficking crews throughout the city.

Neighborhood Persistence

Later federal cases show continuing drug activity in Roxbury and Dorchester, including the Orchard Park Trailblazers investigation around Orchard Gardens and multi-defendant cocaine investigations in Dorchester.

Enforcement Model

Boston investigations increasingly combined federal, state, and local agencies, cooperating witnesses, controlled purchases, cellphone wiretaps, firearms seizures, RICO charges, and large multi-defendant sweeps.

Historical Interpretation

Boston is best understood through recurring geographic nodes rather than a single citywide succession. The same neighborhoods and housing developments could host different organizations in different periods.
