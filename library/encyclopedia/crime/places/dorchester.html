Dorchester

Overview

Dorchester is another major geographic center in Boston's drug and gang history. Federal cases document cocaine and crack distribution, heroin trafficking, citywide gang activity, and interstate drug connections.

Columbia Point

The Columbia Point Dawgs originated in the former Columbia Point Housing Development, now Harbor Point, before expanding drug-trafficking crews throughout Boston.

2010 Cocaine Ring

In December 2010, federal prosecutors charged twenty defendants in a cocaine and crack conspiracy operating in Dorchester and other Boston neighborhoods.

2011 Roxbury–Dorchester Investigation

A separate 2011 investigation targeted cocaine and heroin dealing around Warren Gardens, Codman Square, and Uphams Corner.

Historical Significance

Dorchester's role is not reducible to one organization. It functioned as a recurring market and residential base for different trafficking crews across several decades.
