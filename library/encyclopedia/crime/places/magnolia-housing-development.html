Magnolia Housing Development

Overview

The Magnolia Housing Development was one of New Orleans' major public housing complexes and became an important geographic reference point in the city's crime history. Numerous unrelated individuals and groups were associated with the development over different periods.

Project-Based Crew Structure

Federal intelligence assessments of New Orleans emphasized that local gangs and crews often took their identity from a housing development or neighborhood. Magnolia therefore functioned as a geographic base for different networks rather than as the name of one continuous criminal organization.

Drug Markets

New Orleans housing developments were important retail locations for crack cocaine and later heroin. Federal assessments described project-based distribution networks as significant suppliers not only within the city but also to surrounding communities.

Violence and Cultural Memory

Magnolia became nationally known through New Orleans music and popular culture as well as through crime reporting. Cultural references should be kept separate from evidence concerning specific criminal organizations or individuals.
