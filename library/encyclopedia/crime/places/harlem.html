Harlem — Crime-History Index

Geographic index for Crime Encyclopedia entries involving Harlem, with an initial focus on organized narcotics markets, extortion crews, homicide, and related investigations.

Scope

This is a crime-history index, not a general history of Harlem. Crime entries should be integrated with the broader Harlem encyclopedia so that narcotics and violence are treated as one dimension of a neighborhood with major political, cultural, religious, commercial, and intellectual histories.

Evidence Structure

The Harlem cluster contains unusually rich oral history but uneven formal documentation. Court records should control legal attribution; biographies, documentaries, participant interviews, and music references should be preserved as cultural or oral-history sources and labeled accordingly.
