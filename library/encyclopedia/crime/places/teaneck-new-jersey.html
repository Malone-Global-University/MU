Teaneck, New Jersey — Frank Lucas Crime-History Index

New Jersey location associated with Frank Lucas’s residence, the 1975 raid, and the New Jersey side of his narcotics prosecution.

Frank Lucas Connection

Lucas lived in Teaneck during the period when his Harlem narcotics network was under investigation. In January 1975, investigators raided his residence and seized more than $500,000 in cash, an event that became central to the prosecution narrative.
