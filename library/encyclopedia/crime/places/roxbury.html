Roxbury

Overview

Roxbury is one of the central geographic nodes in Boston's modern drug and gang history. Federal cases document large-scale crack distribution, neighborhood gangs, controlled purchases, and violent rivalries in and around several Roxbury housing developments.

Orchard Park

During the late 1980s and early 1990s, the New York Boys operated from the Orchard Park Housing Project. The federal prosecution became one of Boston's largest crack-era cases.

Orchard Gardens

Decades later, federal investigators again focused on the same general area, by then known as Orchard Gardens, in a case involving members and associates of the Orchard Park Trailblazers and Vine/Forest Street.

Other Drug Markets

Federal cases in the 2010s also documented cocaine distribution around Warren Gardens and other Roxbury locations, illustrating that the neighborhood contained multiple separate drug markets rather than one continuous organization.
