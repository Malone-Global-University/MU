Pennington Court, Newark

Overview

Pennington Court was a Newark public-housing complex that became the center of a major narcotics investigation in 2006–2007.

Narcotics Market

DEA reporting stated that Simmied Tutt's organization commanded and controlled narcotics sales at the complex.

Regional Participants

The organization included participants from Newark, Irvington, New York, and Pennsylvania, illustrating how a highly localized retail market could depend on a wider regional network.

Operation King & His Court

After an eight-month investigation, more than one hundred officers participated in an April 2007 takedown targeting the organization's hierarchy.
