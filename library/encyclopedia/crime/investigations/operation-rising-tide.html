Operation Rising Tide

Overview

Operation Rising Tide was the two-year federal, state, and local investigation that produced the 2015 Columbia Point Dawgs sweep.

Wiretap Investigation

Investigators used court-authorized cellphone wiretaps to monitor suspected drug transactions, firearms activity, and violent conflicts involving CPD members and associates.

Multi-Agency Structure

The operation involved the FBI, DEA, ATF, U.S. Marshals Service, Boston Police Department, Massachusetts State Police, Suffolk County authorities, correctional agencies, and federal prosecutors.

Drug and Firearms Evidence

The investigation produced evidence involving heroin, cocaine, crack cocaine, oxycodone, marijuana, firearms, ammunition, vehicles, and cash.

June 2015 Sweep

The operation culminated in more than thirty search warrants and federal indictments charging forty-eight leaders, members, and associates.

Historical Significance

Operation Rising Tide represents the mature federal gang-investigation model: electronic interception, coordinated intelligence, asset seizure, and simultaneous prosecutions across multiple crews within one organization.
