Investigation of Frank Lucas and the Country Boys

Multi-jurisdictional narcotics investigation culminating in the 1975 Teaneck raid and major federal and New Jersey prosecutions.

Scope

The Lucas investigation crossed New York and New Jersey and involved federal narcotics agents, local police, prosecutors, informants, and cooperating members of the organization. The Teaneck raid became the most visible enforcement event, but the case developed through surveillance, seizures, witness cooperation, and related conspiracy investigations.

Investigative Breaks

Later accounts emphasize cooperation by a Lucas relative after the Teaneck raid. Court records independently show that George Ford, Charles Morris, and Warren Sims became government cooperators at different stages, and Lucas himself later supplied information against former associates.

Law-Enforcement Figures

Richard “Richie” Roberts became the best-known prosecutor associated with the New Jersey side of the Lucas story. The film American Gangster compresses a larger investigative apparatus into a more individualized narrative, so case pages should avoid treating the film character as a literal reconstruction.
