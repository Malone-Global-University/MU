New York Boys Undercover Investigation

Overview

The New York Boys undercover investigation was an extensive federal operation targeting the large-scale drug ring operating from Orchard Park in Roxbury.

Undercover Strategy

The First Circuit described the prosecution as arising from an extensive undercover law-enforcement operation. Undercover officers and cooperating participants gathered evidence concerning cocaine distribution and the organization's internal structure.

Witnesses

At trial, an undercover agent and several co-conspirators testified against Darryl Whiting and other defendants. Their testimony was used with other evidence to establish the conspiracy.

Expansion of the Case

The investigation initially produced a 1990 indictment against Whiting and thirty co-defendants. The 1991 superseding indictment expanded the prosecution to fifty defendants.

Historical Significance

The investigation was an early Boston example of federal authorities targeting an entire crack-distribution enterprise rather than limiting enforcement to individual street sales.
