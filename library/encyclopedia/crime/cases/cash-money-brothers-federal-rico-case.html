Federal RICO Case Against the Cash Money Brothers

Eastern District of New York racketeering prosecution of the Brooklyn-based Cash Money Brothers, culminating in life sentences for leader Damion Hardy and enforcer Aaron Granton.

Indictment

On July 19, 2005, federal prosecutors announced racketeering and narcotics charges against thirteen CMB leaders, members, and associates. The case included crack distribution, murder, attempted murder of a witness, kidnapping and attempted robbery of a drug dealer, assault, and firearms offenses.

Investigation

The indictment followed an 18-month joint investigation involving U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement, the FBI, the NYPD, and the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Eastern District of New York.

Hardy and Granton Trial

After lengthy proceedings, Damion Hardy and Aaron Granton were convicted in April 2015. Their convictions included racketeering, multiple murders in aid of racketeering, kidnapping, and narcotics offenses. They received multiple life sentences in May 2015.

Appeal

The Second Circuit affirmed the judgments in August 2017, rejecting challenges involving competency, anonymous-jury procedures, evidentiary rulings, and sufficiency of the evidence.
