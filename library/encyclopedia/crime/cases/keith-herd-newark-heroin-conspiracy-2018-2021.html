Keith Herd Newark Heroin Conspiracy (2018–2021)

Overview

The federal case against Keith Herd grew out of the FBI investigation of a heroin and crack-cocaine organization operating around Newark's New Community Corporation development.

Initial Charges

Herd and other defendants were first charged in 2018. A grand jury later returned superseding indictments expanding the drug-conspiracy charges.

Continuing Criminal Enterprise

In April 2019, prosecutors announced that Herd had been charged with engaging in a continuing criminal enterprise based on his alleged leadership role in the NCC organization.

Guilty Plea

In December 2020, Herd pleaded guilty to conspiracy to distribute and possess with intent to distribute one kilogram or more of heroin and to a heroin distribution and possession offense.

Sentence

In May 2021, Herd was sentenced to 240 months in federal prison and five years of supervised release.

Historical Significance

The case documents a modern Newark organization through wiretaps, controlled purchases, surveillance, and federal conspiracy charges and provides a useful comparison with the much larger continuing-criminal-enterprise prosecution of Wayne Pray three decades earlier.
