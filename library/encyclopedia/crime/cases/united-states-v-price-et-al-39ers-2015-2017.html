United States v. Price et al. — 39ers (2015–2017)

Overview

The federal prosecution of the 39ers arose from a 45-count RICO indictment charging members with racketeering conspiracy, narcotics trafficking, firearms offenses, and multiple acts of violence.

2015 Indictment

The indictment charged Leroy Price, Ashton Price, Alonzo Peters, Jasmine Perry, McCoy Walker, Terriouse Owney, Evans Lewis, Curtis Neville, Rico Jackson, Tyrone Knockum, Solomon Doyle, Washington McCaskill, Damian Barnes, and others.

Racketeering Theory

The government alleged that the 39ers were formed from the G-Strip and 3NG alliance and used drug trafficking and violence to advance the enterprise.

2017 Trial

Ten defendants were convicted following a six-week jury trial. All ten were found guilty of RICO conspiracy. Drug-conspiracy and homicide-related findings varied by defendant.

Sentences

Several principal defendants, including Leroy Price, Ashton Price, and Jasmine Perry, received life sentences. Other defendants received lengthy federal terms based on their individual convictions.

Claim-Status Importance

The verdict forms demonstrate why the encyclopedia should distinguish an indictment allegation from a jury finding. Some defendants were found guilty in relation to particular killings and acquitted or not found responsible for others.
