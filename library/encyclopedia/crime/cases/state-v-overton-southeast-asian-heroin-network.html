State v. Overton — Southeast Asian Heroin-Smuggling Network (1982)

North Carolina appellate case documenting the Atkinson–Jackson import conspiracy and Vernon Lucas’s role in supplying Frank Lucas.

Importance to the Crime Encyclopedia

State v. Overton is one of the strongest primary records for reconstructing the international supply side of the Frank Lucas network. The case reviewed convictions arising from a multi-person heroin conspiracy whose history extended back to the late 1960s.

Lucas Connection

The appellate court recorded evidence that Vernon Lucas worked for Leslie “Ike” Atkinson in 1973–1974, sent money to Bangkok, received heroin through the mail, and turned that heroin over to his brother Frank Lucas for distribution in New York.

Broader Import Structure

The opinion describes Atkinson and Herman Jackson as early leaders of a Thailand-to-U.S. heroin-smuggling operation using military personnel, mail systems, aircraft, luggage, and furniture concealment. It therefore provides a documentary alternative to later simplified or sensationalized smuggling stories.
