Columbia Point Dawgs Federal Prosecutions (2015–2022)

Overview

The Columbia Point Dawgs federal prosecutions arose from Operation Rising Tide and the June 2015 sweep against CPD leaders, members, associates, and connected suppliers.

2015 Indictments

Six federal indictments initially charged forty-eight leaders, members, and associates with drug-trafficking and firearms offenses. Later federal releases described more than fifty defendants tied to the broader CPD cases.

RICO and Drug Charges

Some defendants faced RICO conspiracy charges while others were prosecuted for cocaine, crack cocaine, heroin, oxycodone, firearms, or related offenses.

Leadership Conviction

Demetrius “Troll” Williams pleaded guilty to RICO conspiracy and a multi-drug distribution conspiracy and received a 151-month sentence in 2017.

Suppliers and Associated Crews

Federal cases also documented suppliers and associated drug crews operating outside the original Columbia Point base, including cocaine and heroin supply relationships in Randolph, Stoughton, Roxbury, and Dorchester.

Long Tail of Prosecutions

Individual CPD-related cases continued for years. As late as 2022, federal prosecutors were sentencing defendants tied to the 2015 investigation.

Historical Significance

The cases demonstrate how CPD evolved into a dispersed network of citywide crews and suppliers and why one umbrella organization can generate numerous separate federal prosecutions rather than one single trial.
