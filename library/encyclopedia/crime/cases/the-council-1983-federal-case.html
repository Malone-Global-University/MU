Federal Prosecution of The Council, 1983–1985

Federal narcotics and racketeering prosecution built substantially on Leroy Barnes’s cooperation against former associates.

Overview

The later federal case charged figures associated with The Council, including Guy Fisher, Ishmael Muhammed, Frank James, Thomas Forman, and Wallace Rice. Joseph Hayden and Barnes were identified as co-conspirators in the appellate account.

What the Case Establishes

The Second Circuit provides one of the clearest judicial descriptions of The Council: seven governing members, separate member businesses, shared bulk heroin purchases, pooled sources, allocated sales territories, dispute resolution, and management of jailed members’ business interests.

Outcome

The appellate court affirmed convictions arising from the Council prosecution in 1985. Contemporary reporting records lengthy sentences, including life terms for several leading defendants.
