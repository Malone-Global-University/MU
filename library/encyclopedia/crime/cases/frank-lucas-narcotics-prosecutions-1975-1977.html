Frank Lucas Narcotics Prosecutions — 1975–1977

Federal and New Jersey prosecutions that dismantled the principal Frank Lucas narcotics enterprise.

Federal Case

Lucas was indicted on federal narcotics charges in 1975 after the Teaneck investigation and was ultimately sentenced to a 40-year federal term. Contemporary records describe a broad conspiracy involving numerous defendants and large-scale heroin distribution in Harlem and the South Bronx.

New Jersey Case

Lucas was separately convicted in New Jersey narcotics proceedings and received a 25-to-30-year state sentence intended to run consecutively to the federal term, producing a combined sentence exceeding seventy years.

Cooperation

Lucas later became a government cooperator. His federal sentence was reduced to time served in 1981, and New Jersey authorities also reconsidered his state sentence in light of his assistance.

Source Reconciliation

Later summaries sometimes compress the federal and state proceedings into a single “70-year sentence.” This page preserves them as separate prosecutions whose consecutive terms created the commonly cited total.
