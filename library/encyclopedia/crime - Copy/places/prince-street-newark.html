Prince Street, Newark

Overview

The Prince Street area became an important geographic node in Newark's drug economy after the imprisonment of Wayne Pray. New Jersey organized-crime investigators identified multiple organizations operating or competing in the area.

Williams Group

Roger “Little Akbar” Williams' cocaine and heroin organization was based in the Prince Street area.

Territorial Competition

State investigators reported a turf conflict between the Williams group and the Ratchford network near Prince Street after an earlier period of territorial accommodation.

Geographic Interpretation

Prince Street should be treated as a recurring market location rather than the permanent territory of one organization. Leadership and group boundaries changed over time.
