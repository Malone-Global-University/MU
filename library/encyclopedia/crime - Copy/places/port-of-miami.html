Port of Miami

Overview

The Port of Miami became one of several important maritime entry points used by cocaine traffickers moving drugs into South Florida. Its large volume of legitimate international cargo created opportunities to conceal illicit shipments within commercial traffic.

Container Smuggling

Federal drug-intelligence reporting documented cocaine concealed in large shipping containers and mixed with legitimate cargo. Corrupt or cooperating workers could facilitate removal of narcotics before customs inspection.

Stash-House Transfer

After cocaine was removed from containers, traffickers moved shipments to private vehicles and stash houses for further distribution. The port therefore represented only the first stage of a larger logistics chain.

Broader Maritime System

The port operated within a much larger South Florida maritime environment that included private boats, fishing vessels, coastal offloading locations, and Bahamian or Caribbean routes.
