Columbia Point, Boston

Overview

The former Columbia Point Housing Development in Boston was the original geographic base of the Columbia Point Dawgs. The site was later redeveloped as Harbor Point.

Origins of CPD

Federal prosecutors state that the Columbia Point Dawgs began in the development during the 1980s. The organization later expanded far beyond its original geographic base.

Citywide Expansion

Over time, CPD members established drug-trafficking crews throughout Boston and extended distribution activity into other New England markets.

Geographic Significance

Columbia Point is therefore best treated as an organizational origin point rather than as the permanent territorial boundary of CPD.
