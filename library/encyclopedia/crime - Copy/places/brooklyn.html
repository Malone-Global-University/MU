Brooklyn — Crime-History Index

Geographic index for Brooklyn-related Crime Encyclopedia entries, now including the Bedford-Stuyvesant Cash Money Brothers cluster as well as interstate figures such as Domencio Benson.

Scope

This page is an indexing hub rather than a claim that Brooklyn possessed one unified criminal network. Separate neighborhoods, organizations, markets, and eras should be developed independently.

Lafayette Gardens / Bedford-Stuyvesant

The most developed Brooklyn cluster currently centers on the Cash Money Brothers, which federal prosecutors said controlled narcotics trafficking in the Lafayette Gardens Houses for more than a decade. Damion “World” Hardy led the organization and Aaron “E-Bay” Granton became a top enforcer.

The organization’s conflict with Ivery “Peanut” Davis generated a multi-year retaliation cycle. Darryl “Homicide” Baum was among the Davis associates killed during that conflict.

Brooklyn–Queens Crossover

CMB remained a distinct Brooklyn organization, but Granton provides a documented cross-borough connection. Federal prosecutors stated that a separate gang identified as the Supreme Team recruited him in 2001 for the murder of Troy Singleton in Queens.
