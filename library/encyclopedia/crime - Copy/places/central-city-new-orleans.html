Central City, New Orleans

Overview

Central City is a historic New Orleans neighborhood that became an important setting in several documented drug and gang investigations. Federal cases connected both the Hankton Enterprise and Young Melph Mafia to the area.

Hankton Enterprise

The Fifth Circuit expressly described the Hankton Enterprise as a gang led by Telly Hankton that sold drugs in Central City.

Young Melph Mafia

The Young Melph Mafia was associated with the former Melpomene Housing Development in Central City. Federal prosecutors described its members as engaging in high-volume street-level drug distribution.

Neighborhood Geography

Central City should not be treated as the territory of a single organization. Different groups operated there during different periods, and individual blocks or developments could support distinct crews.
