Orchard Gardens, Roxbury

Overview

Orchard Gardens is a Roxbury housing development located in the area formerly associated with Orchard Park. Federal prosecutors described it as the largest publicly funded housing development in Roxbury during the 2017 gang investigation.

2015–2017 Investigation

A nearly two-year investigation targeted drug dealing and violence in and around the development. Three cooperating witnesses made thirty-four controlled drug purchases.

Orchard Park Trailblazers

Federal prosecutors attributed part of the area's crime problem to members and associates of the Orchard Park Trailblazers and their active feuds with rival gangs.

School-Zone Cases

Because the development is near schools, several defendants were charged with distributing crack cocaine within 1,000 feet of a school.

Historical Continuity

The area demonstrates continuity of drug-market geography across decades without proving continuity of organization. The New York Boys and Orchard Park Trailblazers belong to different periods and should remain separate entries.
