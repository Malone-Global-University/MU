Northwest Atlanta

Overview

Northwest Atlanta is significant to the BMF case because Atlanta police and DEA agents raided a BMF-associated stash house there in late November 2004. Federal prosecutors later identified the raid as a major event preceding the departure of Demetrius Flenory and other BMF members from Atlanta.

Stash-House Function

Stash houses are used to conceal drugs, money, weapons, records, or organizational property away from ordinary retail activity. Federal drug-intelligence reports describe stash-house systems as a common feature of Atlanta-area trafficking organizations.

November 2004 Raid

According to the U.S. Attorney's Office, the northwest Atlanta house was unoccupied when agents entered it. Investigators recovered three firearms, fictitious identification documents, BMF paraphernalia, and marijuana.

Operational Consequence

Federal prosecutors stated that Demetrius Flenory and much of BMF left Atlanta after the raid. That makes the event a practical turning point between the organization's highly visible Atlanta phase and the broader federal dismantling that followed.
