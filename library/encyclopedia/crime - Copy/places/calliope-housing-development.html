Calliope Housing Development

Overview

The Calliope Housing Development, later known as B.W. Cooper, was one of New Orleans' major public housing complexes. Like Magnolia and other developments, it became part of the geography through which neighborhood-level drug markets were described and policed.

Housing-Project Drug Markets

Federal drug-intelligence reporting from the late 1990s and early 2000s identified New Orleans public housing developments as important retail markets for crack cocaine and heroin.

Local Crew Structure

New Orleans crews were commonly defined by a project, neighborhood, or street rather than by affiliation with a national gang. This makes place pages essential for reconstructing the city's network history.

Continuity and Change

The criminal organizations associated with the area changed over time. The place should therefore be treated as a recurring market location rather than as a single gang identity.
