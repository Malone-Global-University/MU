39ers

Overview

The 39ers were a New Orleans street gang active approximately from 2007 through 2014. Federal prosecutors described the organization as an alliance between two smaller neighborhood groups: G-Strip and 3NG.

Formation

G-Strip operated on Gallier Street in the Ninth Ward, while 3NG operated on or near Third and Galvez Streets. Prosecutors alleged that the groups joined forces to expand drug trafficking and increase their capacity for violent crime.

Drug Trafficking

The 2015 RICO indictment charged members with narcotics offenses including heroin and crack-cocaine distribution. Several defendants were later convicted of participating in a controlled-substances conspiracy involving large drug quantities.

Violence

The federal indictment alleged numerous killings committed in connection with racketeering activity. Trial verdicts varied by defendant and by individual homicide allegation, so this encyclopedia tracks those findings separately rather than treating every indictment allegation as proven.

Federal Prosecution

A 45-count RICO indictment was unsealed in June 2015. Ten defendants were convicted after a six-week trial in February 2017, and several senior defendants later received life sentences.
