Orchard Park Trailblazers

Overview

The Orchard Park Trailblazers were a Roxbury gang associated by federal prosecutors with drug dealing and violence in and around the Orchard Gardens Housing Development.

Geographic Base

The group's identity is tied to the Orchard Park/Orchard Gardens area of Roxbury. This geography also has earlier significance in Boston drug history because the New York Boys operated from the former Orchard Park Housing Project during the crack era.

2015–2017 Investigation

Federal, state, and local investigators conducted a nearly two-year inquiry into drug dealing and violence around the development. Three cooperating witnesses made thirty-four controlled drug purchases from defendants during the investigation.

Federal Charges

In June 2017, twelve men were charged with crack-cocaine distribution offenses. Eleven were alleged members or associates of the Orchard Park Trailblazers or the rival Vine/Forest Street gang.

Individual Convictions

Identified Orchard Park Trailblazers members including Raymond Gaines, Jeremiah Mines, Tyree Draughn, and others later pleaded guilty or were sentenced for cocaine-base distribution.

Historical Significance

The group illustrates continuity of drug-market geography without organizational continuity. Orchard Park remained a recurring location in Boston drug history, but the Trailblazers were not simply a continuation of Darryl Whiting's New York Boys.
