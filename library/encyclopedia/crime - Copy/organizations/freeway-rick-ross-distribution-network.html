Freeway Rick Ross Distribution Network

Overview

The Freeway Rick Ross Distribution Network is a descriptive label for the cocaine-distribution enterprise operated by Ricky Donnell “Freeway Rick” Ross and his associates. It should not be read as the formal legal name of a corporation, gang, or single consistently named organization.

Operating Model

The network developed from street-level sales into kilogram-scale wholesale distribution. Ross used multiple suppliers and sold into a broad base of local dealers. The availability of inexpensive powder cocaine, the growth of crack retailing, and high demand in South Central Los Angeles provided the economic environment in which the network expanded.

Ross' relationship with Oscar Danilo Blandón was especially important because Blandón provided access to substantial wholesale quantities. Federal investigators, however, found that Blandón was not Ross' only source.

Geographic Expansion

Ross' distribution activity extended beyond Los Angeles. He later became involved in interstate cocaine trafficking, including activity connected to Ohio and other markets. The precise scale of every claimed city connection varies by source, so this encyclopedia distinguishes court-established cases from retrospective estimates of the network's national reach.

Relationship to Street Gangs

Individual members of Los Angeles street gangs participated in cocaine distribution, and Ross sold into a market in which Blood and Crip affiliations were important features of neighborhood life. Available research does not support reducing the entire crack economy to centralized control by either gang. The network is better understood as a commercial distribution system serving numerous independent dealers and neighborhood groups.

Law-Enforcement Response

By 1987, the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department and LAPD had formed a joint task force that focused intensively on Ross and his associates. Misconduct allegations against members of that task force undermined aspects of the local enforcement campaign and later became a major part of the historical record.
