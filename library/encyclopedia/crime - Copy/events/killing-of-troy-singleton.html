Killing of Troy Singleton

October 21, 2001 Queens contract killing that creates a documented personnel link between the late McGriff/Supreme Team network and Brooklyn’s Cash Money Brothers.

Event

Troy Singleton was shot and killed after leaving a nightclub in Queens on October 21, 2001. Federal prosecutors described him as a close associate of Eric “E. Money Bags” Smith.

Motive

The government proved that McGriff’s organization arranged Singleton’s murder to prevent him from retaliating for Smith’s killing.

Cash Money Brothers Connection

In the later CMB prosecution, federal prosecutors identified Aaron “E-Bay” Granton as a participant in the Singleton contract murder. They stated that Granton was recruited by the Supreme Team because his CMB killings had established his reputation as an effective enforcer.

Historical Significance

The Singleton murder is one of the clearest documented examples in this research volume of a violent specialist from one borough-based criminal organization being recruited by another organization for a specific homicide.
