Hankton RICO Indictment, 2014

Overview

On June 19, 2014, a federal grand jury in the Eastern District of Louisiana indicted Telly Hankton, Andre Hankton, Walter Porter, Kevin Jackson, and nine other defendants in a 24-count case targeting the Hankton Enterprise.

Charges

The indictment included alleged violations of RICO, federal controlled-substances law, federal firearms law, and the Violent Crimes in Aid of Racketeering statute.

Enterprise Allegations

The government alleged that the organization used drug trafficking and violence to maintain its position and retaliated against rivals and witnesses.

Later Proceedings

Nine co-defendants entered guilty pleas. Telly Hankton, Andre Hankton, Walter Porter, and Kevin Jackson proceeded to trial in 2016.
