Killing of Richard “Rich” Porter

January 1990 homicide of Harlem drug trafficker Richard Porter during the unresolved kidnapping of his younger brother William “Donnell” Porter.

Summary

Richard “Rich” Porter was killed in early January 1990. His body was found in the Bronx with more than $2,000 in cash, causing investigators at the time to discount simple robbery as the obvious motive.

Immediate Context

Porter’s younger brother William had been kidnapped on December 5, 1989 and remained missing. Contemporary investigators therefore considered whether Rich had been killed while privately attempting to negotiate his brother’s return.

Later Attribution

Later participant accounts and Martinez-related reporting attributed the killing to Alberto “Alpo” Martinez.
