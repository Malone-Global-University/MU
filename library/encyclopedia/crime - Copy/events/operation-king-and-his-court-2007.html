Operation King & His Court, 2007

Overview

On April 25, 2007, more than one hundred federal, state, county, and local officers carried out Operation King & His Court, targeting the narcotics organization headed by Simmied Tutt at Newark's Pennington Court Housing Complex.

Eight-Month Investigation

The operation followed an eight-month inquiry during which investigators mapped the hierarchy and identified individuals responsible for drug-market control, violence, and day-to-day operations.

Arrests and Searches

Authorities executed arrest warrants and search warrants and took Tutt, senior associate Abdul Oliver, out-of-state distributors, and Newark participants into custody.

Historical Significance

The operation is an important midpoint in Newark's chronology, showing the transition from large late-twentieth-century wholesale organizations toward targeted enforcement against housing-complex drug markets.
