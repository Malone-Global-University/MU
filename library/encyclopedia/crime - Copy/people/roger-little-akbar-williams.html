Roger “Little Akbar” Williams

Overview

Roger G. “Little Akbar” Williams was a Newark drug-network leader associated with Wayne “Akbar” Pray. New Jersey organized-crime investigators described Williams as the leader of a cocaine and heroin organization operating in the Prince Street area after Pray's incarceration.

Relationship to Wayne Pray

State investigators described Williams as a close associate of Pray and reported that Williams developed his own organization after Pray was imprisoned. This makes the Williams group an important example of fragmentation rather than formal succession within The Family.

Prince Street Organization

Government intelligence described the Williams group as a multi-member cocaine and heroin distribution ring centered in the Prince Street area of Newark.

Territorial Conflict

State investigators reported a later territorial conflict between the Williams group and the Ratchford family near Prince Street after a period in which the groups had generally respected one another's operating areas.

Source Note

The detailed public record on Williams is substantially thinner than the federal record on Pray. Organizational size, territorial relationships, and leadership descriptions on this page therefore rely heavily on New Jersey State Commission of Investigation reporting and should be distinguished from findings in a published federal trial opinion.
