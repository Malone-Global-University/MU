Leroy “Lee” Price

Overview

Leroy “Lee” Price was a member of the New Orleans street organization known as the 39ers. Federal prosecutors charged the organization with racketeering, drug trafficking, firearms offenses, and multiple killings.

39ers Organization

The 39ers were formed through an alliance between the G-Strip gang, operating on Gallier Street in the Ninth Ward, and the 3NG gang, operating around Third and Galvez Streets. Prosecutors alleged that the alliance increased the groups' drug-trafficking capacity and ability to commit violence.

2015 RICO Indictment

Price was named in the 45-count federal RICO indictment unsealed in June 2015.

2017 Trial

A federal jury convicted Price of RICO conspiracy and drug conspiracy and returned special findings concerning several killings attributed to him in the racketeering case.

Sentence

Price received life imprisonment in July 2017.
