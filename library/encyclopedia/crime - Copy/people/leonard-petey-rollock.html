Leonard “Petey” Rollock

Barnes-organization conspirator and heroin distributor convicted in the 1977 federal case; not one of the seven Council members.

Geographic Classification

Borough: Manhattan
Neighborhood: Harlem network
City: New York City

Overview

Rollock appears in the 1977 Barnes prosecution as a subordinate participant in the narcotics conspiracy.

Role in the Network

The appellate record describes a heroin transaction connected to locations used by the Barnes organization and notes Rollock’s planned meeting with “Nick.” He should therefore be mapped beneath the Council layer, not inside the seven-member governing body.

Legal Record

Rollock was convicted of narcotics conspiracy and a substantive heroin distribution count and received concurrent 15-year sentences plus special parole.

Later Significance

His page provides the first clear subordinate layer beneath the Council members and helps distinguish governance from distribution personnel.

Evidence Status

A. Conviction, sentence, and relationship to the Barnes conspiracy are documented by the Second Circuit.
