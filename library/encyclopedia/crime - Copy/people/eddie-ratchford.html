Eddie Ratchford

Overview

Eddie Ratchford was identified by New Jersey organized-crime investigators as a leading figure in the Ratchford Brothers drug network, one of several smaller Newark organizations operating after the height of Wayne Pray's The Family.

Ratchford Brothers

State investigators described Eddie, Willie, and Marvin Ratchford as controlling drug distribution in parts of Newark, including Clinton Hill and public-housing locations. The organization was reported to be deliberately smaller than The Family.

Relationship to Other Newark Groups

The Ratchford Brothers were reported to have business and territorial relationships with the Brown Family and later with Roger “Little Akbar” Williams' organization.

Historical Significance

The Ratchford network demonstrates the fragmentation of Newark's drug economy into smaller neighborhood organizations after the decline of a dominant citywide wholesaler. It should not be treated as a formal branch of The Family without more specific evidence.
