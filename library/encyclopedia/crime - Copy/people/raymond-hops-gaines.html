Raymond “Hops” Gaines

Overview

Raymond “Hops” Gaines was identified by federal prosecutors as a member of the Orchard Park Trailblazers, a Roxbury gang associated with drug activity around the Orchard Gardens Housing Development.

Federal Investigation

Gaines was one of twelve defendants charged after a nearly two-year federal, state, and local investigation into drug dealing and violence in and around Orchard Gardens.

Controlled Purchase

On June 21, 2016, Gaines sold crack cocaine to a cooperating witness near a school in Roxbury.

Guilty Plea

In November 2017, Gaines pleaded guilty to distributing cocaine base within 1,000 feet of a school.

Historical Significance

Gaines' case provides an individually documented example of the later Orchard Park Trailblazers network operating in the same broad Roxbury geography associated decades earlier with the New York Boys.
