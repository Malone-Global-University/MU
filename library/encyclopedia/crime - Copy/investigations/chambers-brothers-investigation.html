Investigation of the Chambers Brothers Organization

Detroit police and federal investigation that used informants, controlled purchases, surveillance, and extensive search-warrant activity against the Chambers crack network.

Overview

The Chambers Brothers investigation combined local narcotics enforcement with federal DEA work. Court records show controlled purchases, searches of organization properties, cooperating insiders, and extensive intelligence on the enterprise’s internal structure.

Terry Colbert

Former organization member Terry Colbert became a confidential informant and provided detailed information concerning recruitment, advertising, crack-house operations, and other methods. The Sixth Circuit recorded that his information supplied the basis for at least 75 percent of 101 search warrants executed during the investigation.

Outcome

The investigation culminated in the 1988 federal prosecution and conviction of senior leaders and associates.
