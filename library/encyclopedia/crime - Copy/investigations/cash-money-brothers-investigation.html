Investigation of the Cash Money Brothers

Multi-agency federal and local investigation into the Brooklyn-based CMB narcotics and racketeering organization.

Overview

The federal CMB indictment announced in July 2005 followed an 18-month joint investigation involving Immigration and Customs Enforcement, the FBI, the NYPD, and the Eastern District of New York.

Investigative Scope

The investigation combined narcotics evidence with homicide, witness-retaliation, kidnapping, firearms, and racketeering allegations. This made the case broader than a conventional drug-distribution prosecution and allowed investigators to reconstruct a decade-long pattern of territorial control and retaliatory violence.

Long-Term Prosecution

The investigation and prosecution continued for more than a decade. In 2016, the Justice Department recognized members of the prosecution and investigative team for work that culminated in the convictions and life sentences of Damion Hardy and Aaron Granton.
