Hankton Enterprise Federal Investigation

Overview

The Hankton Enterprise federal investigation reconstructed a long-running Central City drug organization through homicide evidence, witness testimony, surveillance, state cases, and federal racketeering investigation.

Drug and Gang Intelligence

Investigators linked the Hankton Enterprise to narcotics distribution in Central City and to a prolonged territorial conflict with rival dealers.

Witness Evidence

Witness cooperation was a central issue because the enterprise used intimidation and retaliation against individuals who provided information to law enforcement. Those acts later became part of the federal racketeering case.

Integration of State and Federal Cases

The RICO prosecution incorporated conduct previously investigated or prosecuted at the state level, including violent crimes and alleged efforts to obstruct state proceedings.

2014 Indictment

The federal grand jury returned the principal indictment in June 2014. The later trial involved more than seventy witnesses and dozens of exhibits.
