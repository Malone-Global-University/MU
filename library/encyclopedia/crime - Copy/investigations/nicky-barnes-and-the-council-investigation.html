Investigation of Nicky Barnes and The Council

Federal narcotics investigation and prosecution sequence that moved from the Barnes organization to the later Council case.

1970s Phase

Federal investigators targeted Barnes’s large Harlem narcotics organization, culminating in the 1977 trial and Barnes’s continuing-criminal-enterprise conviction.

Cooperation Phase

Beginning in the early 1980s, Barnes supplied information to federal authorities about former associates and criminal activity. Archival legal-history material records his first public testimony in 1983 and his identification of Council members later that year.

1983 Council Case

Barnes’s cooperation became an investigative bridge into the federal case against former Council associates. The resulting appellate record remains a central source for reconstructing the organization.
