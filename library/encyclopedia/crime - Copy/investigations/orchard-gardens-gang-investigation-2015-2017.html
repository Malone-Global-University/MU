Orchard Gardens Gang Investigation, 2015–2017

Overview

The Orchard Gardens gang investigation was a nearly two-year federal, state, and local inquiry into drug dealing and violence around the Orchard Gardens Housing Development in Roxbury.

Controlled Purchases

Between October 2015 and May 2017, three cooperating witnesses conducted thirty-four controlled drug purchases from defendants in or around the development.

Gang Focus

The investigation focused in part on members and associates of the Orchard Park Trailblazers and Vine/Forest Street gangs, which federal prosecutors said were involved in active feuds contributing to violence in the area.

Weapons Evidence

During an earlier phase, investigators purchased twelve firearms and more than thirty-five grams of crack cocaine from an identified Orchard Park Trailblazers member in thirteen separate transactions.

2017 Charges

The investigation culminated in charges against twelve men in June 2017. Numerous defendants later entered guilty pleas or received federal sentences for crack-cocaine distribution.
