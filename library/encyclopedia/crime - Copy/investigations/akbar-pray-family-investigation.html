Akbar Pray and The Family Investigation

Overview

The investigation of Wayne “Akbar” Pray and The Family required law enforcement to move beyond ordinary street-level narcotics arrests and reconstruct the leadership, finances, importation system, and distribution hierarchy of a large criminal enterprise.

Insulation from Street Sales

New Jersey investigators described Pray as highly insulated from direct narcotics transactions. The organization's layered selling structure made arrests of low-level participants less likely to expose senior leadership.

Financial Evidence

The federal prosecution relied in part on evidence concerning expenditures, travel, vehicles, businesses, and other indications of wealth that prosecutors argued could not be explained through legitimate income.

International Dimension

The federal case ultimately produced a conviction for conspiracy to import more than 98 kilograms of cocaine, demonstrating that the investigation reached beyond Newark's retail markets.

1988–1990 Outcome

The inquiry culminated in the 1988 indictment, 1989 conviction, and 1990 life sentence imposed on Pray.
