Brick City Brim NCC Investigation

Overview

The Brick City Brim NCC investigation targeted the heroin and crack-cocaine organization led by Keith Herd around Newark's New Community Corporation development.

Violent Crime Initiative

Federal prosecutors identified the Herd organization as one of the original targets of the Newark Violent Crime Initiative, a partnership created in 2017 to focus resources on violent criminal groups.

Surveillance and Controlled Purchases

The FBI-led investigation used physical and video surveillance, confidential informants, cooperating witnesses, dozens of controlled heroin and crack-cocaine purchases, narcotics seizures, and telephone wiretaps.

Organization Findings

Investigators concluded that members shared narcotics, customers, proceeds, intelligence about police and rivals, and financial support after arrests.

Charges and Resolution

Multiple waves of charges began in 2018. A grand jury later added a continuing-criminal-enterprise count against Herd, who ultimately pleaded guilty to heroin-related offenses and received a twenty-year sentence.
