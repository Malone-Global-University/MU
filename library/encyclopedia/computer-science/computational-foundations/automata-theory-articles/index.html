Subject Overview

This collection is the child index for the Automata Theory parent article in the MGU Computer Science Encyclopedia. It maps fifteen designated specialist topics from finite-state recognition through grammars, Turing machines, and formal verification. The parent remains the authoritative introduction to definitions, historical development, examples, and the relationships among these topics.

Publication status: All specialist destinations below are planned. Their hyperlinks identify the intended extensionless article URLs in this directory. A link may lead to a not-yet-published page until the corresponding article is commissioned and deployed. Suggested Study Sequence

Begin with alphabets and formal languages, then develop DFAs, NFAs, regular expressions, and minimization. Study pumping arguments after learning finite-state closure and equivalence techniques. Continue to grammars, pushdown automata, and parsing, where stack memory makes nested structure possible. Finish with Turing machines, recognition versus decision, reductions, and automata-based model checking.

The recommended sequence is conceptual rather than a publication schedule. The specialist topics connect to existing comprehensive sibling articles without duplicating the parent subject.
