Business Computing Sub-Articles

Focused references on the design, control, and operation of enterprise computing.

Subject Unit Overview

Business Computing examines the application of digital systems to organizational work. The Business Computing parent article provides the comprehensive foundation. The entries listed below develop specialized aspects of its subject matter; the bibliography and definitions in the parent establish the wider academic context.

These destinations are designated within the MGU Encyclopedia hierarchy. Specialist entries can be developed progressively without changing the parent subject or adding top-level categories.
