Purpose and Organization

This directory collects the designated specialist subarticles of Industrial Computing. The parent article provides the substantive encyclopedia overview. These narrower titles identify distinct areas warranting sustained technical and historical treatment. Active links are established in advance; the listed child articles are planned and not yet published.

The directory uses the adopted MGU hierarchy: the parent article and its industrial-computing-articles/ folder occupy the same level under Applications of Computing. The independent Industrial Control Systems article remains a separate reference subject rather than being copied into this child collection.

Planned Specialist Articles

All 12 links below designate proposed article destinations within this directory. Their presence here is a production roadmap, not a live-publication claim.

  1. Computer-Integrated Manufacturing — Coordinating engineering, production planning, and manufacturing execution through a common digital environment.
  2. Manufacturing Execution Systems — Work-order dispatch, production state, genealogy, and plant-level manufacturing operations.
  3. Industrial Edge Computing — Local computation, buffering, data processing, and edge-to-enterprise integration at industrial sites.
  4. Industrial Data Interoperability — Protocol translation, semantic identifiers, information models, and cross-vendor industrial data exchange.
  5. Industrial Condition Monitoring — Sensor-driven assessment of asset condition and the reliability of operational measurements.
  6. Predictive Maintenance Systems — Models and workflows for equipment failure forecasting and planned intervention.
  7. Industrial Quality Informatics — Digital inspection, statistical process control, nonconformance evidence, and quality disposition.
  8. Industrial Asset Performance Management — Combining asset registers, operating history, reliability analysis, and maintenance planning.
  9. Digital Threads in Manufacturing — Traceable information linking engineering configurations, production, inspection, and service.
  10. Industrial Digital Twins — Validated digital representations for simulation, monitoring, and industrial decision support.
  11. Production Planning and Scheduling Systems — Finite-capacity planning, sequencing, dispatch, and response to production disruptions.
  12. Industrial Energy Analytics — Measuring and evaluating energy, utilities, and resources with production context.

Relationship to the Parent Article

The Industrial Computing parent covers the complete discipline from equipment and operational data through manufacturing execution, quality, reliability, safety and economic governance. Each child will examine a narrower subject in greater detail without reproducing the parent article's full survey.

Existing cross-disciplinary entries are linked as Related Topics and retain their authoritative locations; the proposed child directory does not replace established articles under other domains.

Publication and Editorial Scope

The directory and links are part of the approved encyclopedia information architecture. The designated titles should be evaluated against the live inventory before individual child production, and each future article should have a clear scope, source basis, internal navigation, and cross-reference relationship to its parent.

Until produced, the child targets are intentional missing destinations rather than accidental routing defects. The actual deployment status of files is determined separately by the site operator.
