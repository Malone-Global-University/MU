Entertainment Computing Sub-Articles

Focused entries on interactive entertainment, media engineering, production operations, accessibility, distribution, and preservation.

Subject Unit Overview

Entertainment Computing is the authoritative parent article for games, media creation and delivery, and interactive digital experiences. This directory organizes deeper reference entries according to the established MGU parent–child structure. It does not create a separate Gaming parent or a new top-level category.

The entries below are planned publication destinations. The links are deliberately active under WA-006 even though the corresponding child articles have not been produced. Readers should consult the comprehensive parent article until individual specialist articles are published.

Academic Relationships

This specialist unit treats game and entertainment technology as an applied computing discipline. Computer Graphics, Real-Time Rendering, Cloud Computing, and Human–Computer Interaction remain related independent encyclopedia articles. Planned children should be written with distinct scopes and should not reproduce those neighboring entries.
