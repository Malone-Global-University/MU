The American Mafia (also known as the Italian-American Mafia or La Cosa Nostra) is a prominent organized crime group composed of Italian-Americans and Sicilian immigrants. [1] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mafia)

Origins and Rise

• Immigration Roots: The Mafia emerged in the late 19th and early 20th centuries in impoverished Italian immigrant neighborhoods in New York City, Chicago, New Orleans, and other northeastern cities. [1] (https://www.history.com/articles/mafia-in-the-united-states), [2] (https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/38662/chapter/335786928), [3] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mafia)
• Prohibition Era: The 1920s ban on alcohol turned bootlegging into a massive revenue stream, elevating mobsters like Al Capone in Chicago and Lucky Luciano in New York to extreme power. [1] (https://www.thetimes.com/us/news-today/article/new-york-mafia-five-families-carlo-gambino-d2t7nngsz), [2] (https://www.facebook.com/investingwithcolleen/videos/the-italian-mob-didnt-start-in-hollywood-movies-it-started-in-the-streets-of-ame/1206764251251292/), [3] (https://www.facebook.com/investingwithcolleen/videos/the-italian-mafia-in-america/2095401830998314/)
• The Commission: Following the 1930–1931 Castellammarese War, Lucky Luciano and Meyer Lansky reorganized New York's criminal factions into the Five Families and established a governing "Commission" to coordinate territory and settle disputes. [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EgXxqcHaiCY), [2] (https://www.thetimes.com/us/news-today/article/new-york-mafia-five-families-carlo-gambino-d2t7nngsz)

The New York Five Families

New York City has historically been anchored by five primary crime families: [1] (https://www.thetimes.com/us/news-today/article/new-york-mafia-five-families-carlo-gambino-d2t7nngsz)
• Bonanno crime family
• Colombo crime family
• Gambino crime family (led famously by figures like Carlo Gambino and John Gotti)
• Genovese crime family
• Lucchese crime family [1] (https://www.thetimes.com/us/news-today/article/new-york-mafia-five-families-carlo-gambino-d2t7nngsz), [2] (https://www.facebook.com/investingwithcolleen/videos/the-italian-mob-didnt-start-in-hollywood-movies-it-started-in-the-streets-of-ame/1206764251251292/)

Hierarchy and Code

• Structure: Typical families are headed by a Boss, aided by an Underboss and Consigliere (advisor), who oversee Capos (captains), Soldiers (made members), and non-member Associates. [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-q0j8il3ZQ&t=1), [2] (https://www.history.com/articles/mafia-in-the-united-states)
• Omertà: The organization relies on a strict code of silence (omertà) and blood-bound loyalty oaths. [1] (https://www.history.com/articles/mafia-in-the-united-states), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-q0j8il3ZQ&t=1)
• Operations: Beyond early bootlegging, the Mob expanded into illegal gambling, loan-sharking, labor union racketeering, extortion, and infiltration of legitimate commercial and construction industries. [1] (https://www.facebook.com/TheItalianAmericanPage/videos/for-decades-the-mafia-operated-in-the-shadowsbuilding-an-empire-of-money-influen/2626522537781745/), [2] (https://www.history.com/articles/mafia-in-the-united-states), [3] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EgXxqcHaiCY)

Decline and Current Status

• RICO and Informants: The passage of the federal RICO (Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organizations) Act in 1970, combined with high-profile turncoats (such as Joseph Valachi and later mob informants) breaking omertà, severely crippled the Mafia's leadership base late in the 20th century. [1] (https://www.history.com/articles/mafia-in-the-united-states), [2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-q0j8il3ZQ&t=1), [3] (https://www.facebook.com/TheItalianAmericanPage/videos/for-decades-the-mafia-operated-in-the-shadowsbuilding-an-empire-of-money-influen/2626522537781745/)
• Today: The Italian-American Mafia never completely vanished, shifting away from flashy, violent street wars toward quieter, lower-profile operations and white-collar corruption. [1] (https://www.thetimes.com/us/news-today/article/new-york-mafia-five-families-carlo-gambino-d2t7nngsz), [2] (https://www.facebook.com/TheItalianAmericanPage/videos/for-decades-the-mafia-operated-in-the-shadowsbuilding-an-empire-of-money-influen/2626522537781745/)
