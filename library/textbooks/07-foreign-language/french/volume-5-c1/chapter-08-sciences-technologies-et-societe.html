Science, Technology, and Society · Speak and Write French: Specialized Mastery and Expert Communication

Chapter Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

  1. Distinguish technical mediation — where a genuine risk, a genuine capability, an open ethical question, and a real precaution must each be named accurately for a non-specialist reader — from Chapter 7’s policy-and-institution precision, while recognizing that a single technical story can involve both.

Communication Scenario

The drainage sensors promised at the end of Chapter 7 have now been installed under the covered market, and the town hall has scheduled a public information session before they go live. The sensors measure water levels in real time and are meant to warn the market office before flooding becomes severe — but M. Ouedraogo has heard a rumor that the sensors also track which stalls stay open latest, and Mme Rigal wants to know whether the system can actually be trusted to work, or whether it is being oversold as a fix for a problem that will keep happening regardless. Sophie and Karim, covering the session for La Revue du quartier, realize that a good technical explainer cannot simply reassure or simply alarm: it has to say exactly what the sensors can and cannot do, name the genuine risk of a false sense of security if people stop watching the water themselves, raise honestly the question of what happens to the data the sensors collect, and state the actual precaution — regular calibration checks — that keeps the system reliable. Chapter 8 gives Sophie, Karim — and you — the tools to mediate a technical innovation for a general audience without overselling it, underselling it, or dodging the ethical question it raises.

  1. State precisely what a technology could go wrong in doing, without exaggeration or false reassurance.
  2. State precisely what a technology can actually do, as distinct from what it is designed or hoped to do.
  3. Raise a genuine ethical question about a technology without asserting an unsupported answer.
  4. State the concrete precaution required for a technology’s safe and responsible use.

Pronunciation Focus

Pronunciation Focus — Delivering a hedge without sounding evasive

No new sound-spelling or vowel feature is introduced in this chapter. The task is delivery: a genuine hedge (il convient de se demander si…) can sound evasive if it is delivered with the same flat, list-like pacing as a stated fact, which risks leaving a listener unsure whether the speaker actually has a position. No single pitch contour is required for any of this chapter’s frames. A brief, level pause before il convient de se demander si can help mark that a genuine open question follows, rather than a rhetorical one; by contrast, être en mesure de and présenter un risque de are typically delivered at a steadier, more declarative pace, since both state something the speaker is prepared to stand behind.

  • Stating a risk: Le système présente un risque de fausse alerte en cas de fortes pluies inhabituelles. — steady, declarative pace, slight emphasis on risque de.
  • Stating a capability: Les capteurs sont en mesure de signaler une montée des eaux environ deux heures à l’avance. — measured pace, slight emphasis on en mesure de to distinguish genuine capability from a vaguer promise.
  • Raising an ethical question: Il convient de se demander si les données collectées par les capteurs seront un jour utilisées à d’autres fins. — a brief pause before the frame, then an even, non-rhetorical delivery through to the end of the question.
  • Stating a precaution: On peut maintenir le système fiable à condition de vérifier son calibrage chaque mois. — measured pacing sets the precaution apart from the capability it qualifies.

Cumulative review: this chapter’s spoken practice also recycles active sound-spelling, liaison, enchaînement, elision, and rhythm systems from earlier volumes. As a short recycling drill, practice être en mesure for enchaînement between the normally pronounced final consonant of être and en, des impacts for liaison between the plural article and the vowel-initial noun (note that impact itself is pronounced with both the c and the final t sounded), l’innovation for elision, and anticiper un risque for nasal-vowel control and phrase rhythm.

Grammar: Nommer un risque, une capacité, une question éthique

Purpose: introduce a newly productive risk-statement frame (présenter un risque de + nom/infinitif), a newly productive technical-capability frame (être en mesure de + infinitif), a newly productive ethical-questioning frame (il convient de se demander si + indicatif), and a newly productive precaution frame (à condition de + infinitif), while recycling the complete Unité I toolkit and Chapters 6–7’s frames. No new tense, mood, subjonctif trigger, or clitic pattern is introduced in this chapter.

Key distinction — technical mediation is an independent dimension

Chapters 6 and 7 each added a precision dimension: contractual obligation and rights (Ch. 6), and institutional responsibility and public interest (Ch. 7). This chapter adds another independent dimension: stating what a technology risks doing wrong, what it can actually do, what ethical question it genuinely raises, and what precaution keeps it safe. Note that il convient de se demander si takes the indicative (si + indicative, as in ordinary yes/no questions embedded in a clause), not the subjunctive; this chapter introduces no new mood. This dimension can combine with any earlier one — a technical risk can be cited as the source of an institutional responsibility (Ch. 7), or explained for a general audience (Ch. 2) — but it is distinct because it specifically concerns technical accuracy: what a system does, not what a person or institution must do.

8.1 Stating a risk precisely: présenter un risque de

Grammar Focus — Naming what could go wrong, without exaggeration

Présenter un risque de + noun/infinitive is newly productive. It states a specific, genuine possibility of technical failure or harm, distinct from a vague warning such as c’est peut-être dangereux. Naming the risk specifically — what, exactly, could go wrong, and under what circumstance — lets a reader judge its seriousness rather than reacting to an undefined fear.

  • Le système présente un risque de fausse alerte en cas de fortes pluies inhabituelles, mais aucun risque connu pour la sécurité des personnes. The system presents a risk of false alarm during unusually heavy rain, but no known risk to personal safety. présente un risque de (newly productive) names a specific, bounded risk rather than a vague danger

8.2 Stating a genuine capability: être en mesure de

Grammar Focus — What a technology can actually do, not merely what it is meant to do

Être en mesure de + infinitive is newly productive. It states that a person, institution, or system has the capacity or means to perform an action, and is more precise than describing only a technology’s purpose or design intent. In this chapter’s technical-mediation tasks, learners should ground such capability claims in available evidence — naming a test, a source, or an observed result — rather than presenting an untested design goal as an established fact.

  • Les capteurs sont en mesure de signaler une montée des eaux environ deux heures à l’avance, sur la base des essais réalisés depuis mars. The sensors are able to signal a rise in water levels about two hours in advance, based on the tests conducted since March. sont en mesure de (newly productive) states a capability; the cited evidence, not the frame itself, grounds the claim

8.3 Raising an ethical question and stating a precaution: il convient de se demander si / à condition de

Building Block — Risk, capability, open question, and precaution together
[capability: être en mesure de…] + [risk: présenter un risque de…] + [open ethical question: il convient de se demander si + indicatif] + [precaution: on peut maintenir le système fiable / sûr à condition de + infinitif]
Il convient de se demander si + indicative is newly productive. It raises a genuine ethical or open question — here, about data collection — without asserting an answer the evidence does not yet support; the indicative after si is the same construction used in ordinary embedded yes/no questions, so no new mood is required. This differs from simply asserting a conclusion (les données seront mal utilisées), which would overstate what is actually known. À condition de + infinitive is newly productive. It states the concrete action that must be taken for a system to remain reliable or safe, distinguishing a real, checkable precaution from a vague reassurance such as tout est sous contrôle. Because the understood subject of the infinitive after à condition de matches the subject of the main clause, the main clause needs a human or institutional subject (on, la mairie, les techniciens) who actually performs the precaution — not the system itself, which cannot check its own calibration.
Les capteurs sont en mesure de signaler une montée des eaux environ deux heures à l’avance. Le système présente néanmoins un risque de fausse alerte en cas de pluies inhabituelles. Par ailleurs, il convient de se demander si les données de fréquentation collectées par les capteurs seront un jour utilisées à d’autres fins que la prévention des inondations — la mairie affirme que non, mais aucun texte ne l’interdit formellement à ce jour. Les techniciens peuvent maintenir le système fiable à condition de vérifier son calibrage chaque mois.
The sensors are able to signal a rise in water levels about two hours in advance. The system nonetheless presents a risk of false alarm during unusual rain. In addition, it is worth asking whether the foot-traffic data collected by the sensors will one day be used for purposes other than flood prevention — the town hall says no, but no text formally prohibits it as of now. Technicians can keep the system reliable provided they check its calibration every month.

Practice — Risk, capability, open question, or precaution?

Decide which newly productive frame best fits each statement.

  1. Le système peut vraiment détecter une fuite avant qu’elle ne s’aggrave, d’après les tests menés jusqu’ici. (capability)
  2. Il existe une possibilité réelle, mais limitée, que le capteur déclenche une alerte à tort. (risk)
  3. On ne sait pas encore avec certitude si ces données seront un jour réutilisées ailleurs, et la question mérite d’être posée. (open question)

Model Dialogue: La séance d’information sur les capteurs

Context: the town hall holds a public information session before the new drainage sensors go live. M. Ouedraogo and Mme Rigal have questions; a municipal technician, Mme Diarra, mediates with Sophie and Karim present. Register: neutre/courant to soutenu, precise and public-facing.

M. Ouedraogo : On me dit que ces capteurs peuvent prévoir les inondations. C’est vrai, ou c’est juste ce qu’on espère ? I'm told these sensors can predict flooding. Is that true, or is that just what's hoped for?
Mme Diarra : C’est vérifié : les capteurs sont en mesure de signaler une montée des eaux environ deux heures à l’avance, sur la base des essais réalisés depuis mars. It's verified: the sensors are able to signal a rise in water levels about two hours in advance, based on the tests conducted since March.
Mme Rigal : Et s’il pleut fort, mais différemment que d’habitude, est-ce que le système peut se tromper ? And if it rains hard, but differently than usual, can the system get it wrong?
Mme Diarra : Oui — le système présente un risque de fausse alerte en cas de pluies inhabituelles, mais aucun risque connu pour la sécurité des personnes. Yes — the system presents a risk of false alarm during unusual rain, but no known risk to personal safety.
M. Ouedraogo : On m’a dit aussi que ces capteurs enregistrent quels commerçants ferment tard. C’est vrai ? I've also heard these sensors record which vendors close late. Is that true?
Karim : Le capteur mesure surtout le niveau de l’eau, mais il effectue aussi un comptage de fréquentation. Il convient donc de se demander si ces données de fréquentation seront un jour utilisées à d’autres fins que la prévention des inondations. The sensor mainly measures water level, but it also logs a foot-traffic count. So it's worth asking whether that foot-traffic data will one day be used for purposes other than flood prevention.
Mme Diarra : La mairie affirme que non, mais je reconnais qu’aucun texte ne l’interdit formellement à ce jour — c’est une question légitime. The town hall says no, but I'll admit no text formally prohibits it as of now — that's a legitimate question.
Sophie : Et pour la fiabilité, qu’est-ce qui garantit que le système reste précis dans le temps ? And for reliability, what guarantees the system stays accurate over time?
Mme Diarra : Nous pouvons maintenir le système fiable à condition de vérifier son calibrage chaque mois ; sans cette vérification, sa précision peut se dégrader progressivement. We can keep the system reliable provided we check its calibration every month; without that check, its accuracy can gradually degrade.

This dialogue models all four newly productive frames — capability, risk, open ethical question, and precaution — while keeping the explanation accessible to non-specialist vendors, recycling Chapter 2’s audience-adaptation habits without naming them.

Speaking Practice

Pratique contrôlée — Capacité ou risque ?

With a partner, one person describes a plain-language technology fact (invented or based on the chapter). The other restates it precisely using être en mesure de if it is a genuine capability, or présenter un risque de if it names something that could go wrong.

Pratique contrôlée — Poser la question, pas trancher

With a partner, one person states a technical fact. The other raises a genuine open question about it using il convient de se demander si, without asserting an answer.

Item 1

les capteurs effectuent un comptage de fréquentation en plus de la mesure du niveau de l’eau (il convient de se demander si… ces données seront un jour croisées avec d’autres fichiers)

Item 2

le système envoie une alerte automatique au service du marché (il convient de se demander si… une alerte erronée pourrait un jour provoquer une fermeture inutile)

Culture and Communication

Culture in Context — Public trust and the precautionary habit in French technical communication

In public-facing technical communication, explicitly naming genuine uncertainties and limits, as this chapter's il convient de se demander si frame does, can support credibility, particularly when explaining technologies that involve data collection. In France, this kind of communication also takes place within a broader European data-protection environment that encourages attention to how personal data are collected, used, and retained.

Regional Variation — Technology governance varies across Francophone contexts

Data-protection frameworks, municipal technology procurement, and public consultation norms around new technologies vary across France, Belgium, Switzerland, Québec, and other Francophone jurisdictions. This chapter's examples reflect common French municipal conventions; the underlying skill — stating a technology's capability, risk, open question, and precaution precisely — transfers across these contexts even where the specific regulatory frameworks differ.

Common Mistakes

Common Mistake — Answering an open question instead of leaving it open

Error: Using il convient de se demander si to introduce a question, then immediately asserting a confident answer the evidence does not support, which defeats the purpose of raising it as genuinely open.

Correction: Let the question stand as a question: il convient de se demander si ces données seront un jour utilisées à d’autres fins can be followed by what is currently known (a stated policy, an absence of formal guarantee) without converting the open question into a settled claim.

Common Mistake — Making an unsupported capability claim

Error: Using être en mesure de to describe something a system is merely designed or hoped to do, while presenting the claim as though it were already backed by evidence, when no test, source, or observed result is actually cited.

Correction: Être en mesure de itself simply states a capacity or means; the task requirement is to ground that claim in cited evidence, as in sur la base des essais réalisés depuis mars. For an untested design goal, it is more honest to say so directly with a frame such as le système est conçu pour…, already active from earlier volumes, rather than dressing up a hope as a demonstrated capability.

Common Mistake — Naming a vague danger instead of a specific risk

Error: Using présenter un risque de followed by a vague or unbounded harm (un risque de problème) rather than naming exactly what could go wrong and under what condition.

Correction: Name the specific failure and its circumstance, as in un risque de fausse alerte en cas de pluies inhabituelles, which lets a reader judge the risk's actual seriousness and likelihood.

Reading Practice

  1. Capability: detecting a rise in water levels about two hours before it becomes critical, based on tests since March. Risk: false alarm during unusual precipitation. They differ because the capability is a demonstrated positive function, while the risk names a specific bounded failure mode.
  2. The open question is whether foot-traffic data could one day be used for other purposes; it is left open because the town hall's exclusion is stated as current policy, not a formally guaranteed limit.
  3. The precaution is verifying the system's calibration every month, done by a qualified technician.
  4. La traçabilité appears to mean the ability to track exactly what data is kept and for how long; un biais appears to mean a systematic measurement distortion, here identified near high-traffic zones.

Writing Workshop — Model text

Modèle — Fiche d’information sur une innovation technique

Chers habitants, une précision sur les nouvelles poubelles connectées installées près du marché. Ce dispositif est en mesure de signaler à la mairie, en temps réel, quand un conteneur est presque plein, sur la base des essais menés depuis janvier. Cette capacité reste toutefois limitée à la détection du remplissage ; elle ne dit rien de la nature des déchets déposés. Le système présente également un risque de fausse alerte lorsque des déchets volumineux faussent temporairement la mesure de remplissage. Il convient par ailleurs de se demander si les données de localisation des conteneurs, collectées en parallèle, seront un jour croisées avec d’autres bases de données municipales ; la mairie l’exclut pour l’instant, sans qu’un texte le garantisse formellement. La collectivité peut maintenir le système fiable à condition de remplacer les capteurs défectueux dans un délai de deux semaines après leur signalement. Un habitant qui constate une anomalie peut la signaler directement au service du marché.

Dear residents, a clarification on the new connected bins installed near the market. This system is able to signal to the town hall, in real time, when a container is nearly full, based on tests conducted since January. This capability, however, remains limited to detecting fill level; it says nothing about the nature of the waste deposited. The system also presents a risk of false alarm when bulky waste temporarily distorts the fill-level measurement. It is also worth asking whether the container location data, collected in parallel, will one day be cross-referenced with other municipal databases; the town hall currently rules this out, without any text formally guaranteeing it. The public authority can keep the system reliable provided faulty sensors are replaced within two weeks of being reported. A resident who notices a problem can report it directly to the market office.
