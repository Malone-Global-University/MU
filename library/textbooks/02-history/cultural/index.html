Cultural History

Cultural History investigates the ways people create, exchange, preserve, and contest meaning through artistic expression, intellectual life, traditions, media, and everyday practices. Its textbooks situate cultural movements within the social, political, economic, and technological conditions that shaped their development.

Overview

This collection focuses on the history of cultural production and reception: how forms of expression originate, spread, change, and acquire significance across generations and communities.

Individual textbook directories contain the available chapters and supporting materials for each cultural history series.

Textbook Collection

The current collection features a comprehensive study of a major modern cultural movement.

  • The Culture: A Complete History of Hip-Hop

    A historical examination of hip-hop as music, dance, visual expression, industry, politics, and global culture. The study traces its African diasporic and Caribbean influences, emergence in the Bronx, recording and regional movements, evolving technologies, and international reach.
