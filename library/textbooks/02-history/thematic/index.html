Thematic History

Thematic History brings together textbooks organized by historical subject rather than by a single geographic region or population. These studies trace the development of institutions, ideas, activities, technologies, and social processes across different places and periods.

Overview

Thematic textbooks use a defined subject as their organizing principle. This approach supports comparison across societies, connecting events and long-term changes that might otherwise be studied only within separate national or regional histories.

The collection will expand as additional subject histories are developed, while individual published textbooks remain accessible through their dedicated directories.

Textbook Collection

The current collection begins with the global history of organized crime and illicit systems.

  • Organized Crime: A Global History

    A comparative global history of organized criminal groups, illicit markets, political corruption, transnational networks, policing, and state responses. The textbook examines how criminal organizations and their relationships with society and government have changed over time.

Future Areas of Study

This category can also accommodate historical studies of mathematics, science, technology, engineering, religion, medicine, law, warfare, economics, education, and other specialized subjects as those textbook series are developed. These are prospective subjects, not links to existing textbooks.
