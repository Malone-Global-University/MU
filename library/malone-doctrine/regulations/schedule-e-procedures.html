Schedule E — Procedures

The authoritative schedule of approved operational methods used to execute Malone Doctrine Rules.

Overview

Schedule E will contain the formally adopted Procedures of Malone Doctrine. Procedures establish the approved step-by-step methods through which particular Rules are carried out, administered, documented, or enforced.

Authority

Procedures possess operational authority. Every Procedure must implement one or more Rules and remain consistent with all superior authority. A Procedure may direct execution but may not alter the substance of the Rule it implements.
