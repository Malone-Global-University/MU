Schedule B — Principles

The authoritative schedule of broad ethical standards derived from the axioms of Malone Doctrine.

Overview

Schedule B will contain the formally adopted Principles of Malone Doctrine. Principles establish broad ethical standards derived from the axioms and provide the normative basis from which Maxims and Rules are developed.

Authority

Principles possess normative authority. Every Principle must derive from and remain consistent with one or more Axioms. No Principle may contradict, narrow, or nullify a governing Axiom.
