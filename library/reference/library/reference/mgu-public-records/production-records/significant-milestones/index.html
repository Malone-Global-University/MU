Significant Milestones

Event-driven records of completed educational packages, inaugural publications, and substantive institutional achievements.

Collection Overview

Significant Milestones is a subordinate collection of Production Records. It documents what a coordinated body of institutional work accomplishes, rather than merely enumerating individual files or daily activities. Entries are filed when a qualifying achievement occurs, independently of weekly and monthly reporting cycles.

Formal milestone reports are maintained as PDF documents when prepared and filed. This HTML index provides dated summaries and links to the associated collections and available documentary records.

Qualifying Milestones

  • Collection establishment: first substantive published collection in a new educational resource category.
  • Series inauguration: publication of the first installment of an identified larger series, without implying completion of the entire series.
  • Package completion: completion of a defined set of related works serving a coherent purpose.
  • Volume completion: completion of a specified textbook, encyclopedia, or other substantial volume.
  • Program completion: completion of a defined institutional development initiative.
  • Capability establishment: introduction of a substantive new institutional or instructional function.
  • Major modernization: completion of a substantial technical, architectural, or editorial improvement.

Filing and Accounting Standards

Each formal milestone record should identify its achievement date, preparation date, responsible institutional function, category, completion status, defined package or series boundary, constituent resources, supporting evidence, and limitations. Distinguish completion, publication, and independent quality approval. Count each distinct completed package once; do not add package counts to individual file totals.

Subsequent expansion does not invalidate the completion of a defined earlier package. Material corrections should be documented through an identified corrected edition or addendum, consistent with the parent Production Records standards.
