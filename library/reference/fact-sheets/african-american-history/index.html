African American History

African American History fact sheets document the people, events, laws, institutions, movements, and historical conditions that have shaped Black life in the United States. The collection provides concise, structured references for chronological study, topic research, and comparison across major historical periods.

Collection Overview

This collection organizes African American history by primary historical period while allowing individual fact sheets to address subjects that extend across more than one era. Each resource is assigned one canonical location and may be connected to related subjects through cross-references, reducing duplication while preserving historical context.

Historical Periods

  • Civil Rights Era

    Fact sheets on the twentieth-century struggle against segregation and racial discrimination, including major organizations, leaders, protests, legislation, court decisions, and federal actions.

  • Jim Crow Era

    Fact sheets on the laws, customs, institutions, violence, political restrictions, economic systems, and resistance movements associated with racial segregation after Reconstruction.

  • Reconstruction Era

    Fact sheets on emancipation, post-Civil War constitutional change, federal reconstruction policy, Black political participation, institutions established for freedpeople, and organized resistance to Reconstruction.
