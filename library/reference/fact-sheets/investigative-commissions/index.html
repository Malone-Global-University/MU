Investigative Commissions

Investigative commissions are temporary bodies established by governments or other authorities to investigate significant public events, institutional failures, civil disturbances, disasters, misconduct, or matters of national concern. Their reports often influence legislation, public policy, institutional reform, and historical understanding.

Collection Overview

This collection serves as the canonical location for fact sheets on official investigative commissions. Each entry explains why the commission was established, its legal authority, membership, investigative methods, principal findings, recommendations, public reception, and long-term historical significance.

Commissions investigate events and institutions but are distinct from the events themselves. Historical events, organizations, legal cases, laws, and government agencies remain in their own collections and are connected through cross-references rather than duplicate entries.

Collection Scope

  • Federal, state, and local investigative commissions
  • Independent review panels and special commissions
  • Civil disorder and riot investigations
  • Police accountability and misconduct investigations
  • Government inquiries into national crises and public safety failures
  • Commission findings, recommendations, and historical impact

Reference Entries

Investigative commission fact sheets are listed alphabetically by the official or commonly recognized name of the commission.

  • Christopher Commission

    An independent commission established by the City of Los Angeles in 1991 to investigate the Los Angeles Police Department following the Rodney King beating.

  • Kerner Commission

    The National Advisory Commission on Civil Disorders, created in 1967 to investigate the causes of urban unrest and recommend policies to address racial inequality.

  • Webster Commission

    An independent review panel led by former FBI Director William H. Webster that examined intelligence, preparedness, and law enforcement actions related to the 2009 Fort Hood shooting.
