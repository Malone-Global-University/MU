Labor History

Labor History fact sheets examine workers, workplaces, unions, labor movements, employment systems, strikes, organizing campaigns, and workplace policy. The collection explains how labor relationships have shaped economic life, law, politics, and community development.

Collection Overview

This collection serves as the canonical location for historical subjects centered on work, workers, employment, labor organization, and the distribution of power in the workplace. Entries may address working conditions, wages, occupational identity, labor law, racial and gender divisions of labor, industrial change, and worker resistance.

Individual laws, organizations, economic systems, and historical events remain in their corresponding categories and may be connected through cross-references.

Collection Scope

  • Workers, occupations, and workplace conditions
  • Unions, organizing, strikes, and collective bargaining
  • Labor law, wages, benefits, and employment policy
  • Forced labor, migration, and racial or gender divisions of labor
  • Industrialization, automation, and changes in work

Reference Entries

Labor history fact sheets are listed chronologically or by labor field as they are added.

Related Collections

  • Economic Systems

    Explanations of economic models and systems that structure production, ownership, and distribution.

  • Social Movements

    Reference summaries of organized efforts seeking social, cultural, legal, or institutional change.

  • Laws and Legislation

    Summaries of statutes and legal provisions governing labor and employment.

  • African American History

    Historical references on Black workers, labor institutions, migration, and community development.
