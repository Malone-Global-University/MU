Laws and Legislation

Laws and Legislation fact sheets summarize statutes, legislative acts, codes, legal provisions, and major policy enactments. Each entry identifies the law’s purpose, provisions, sponsors or governing authority, historical context, implementation, and continuing significance.

Collection Overview

This collection serves as the canonical location for formal legislative enactments and statutory rules. Entries distinguish enacted law from proposals, regulations, court interpretations, political arguments, and administrative practice while explaining how legal provisions operate in context.

Individual constitutional amendments, court cases, government programs, and historical events remain in their corresponding categories and may be connected through cross-references.

Collection Scope

  • Federal and state statutes
  • Legislative acts, codes, and major bills
  • Civil rights, labor, criminal, economic, and public welfare laws
  • Legal authority, provisions, enforcement, and implementation
  • Legislative history, amendment, repeal, and continuing effects

Reference Entries

Law and legislation fact sheets are listed chronologically, alphabetically, or by policy field as they are added.

  • Anti-Miscegenation Laws

    State laws that prohibited marriage or intimate relationships between people of different races, particularly between white and Black Americans.

  • Black Codes (1865–1866)

    State laws enacted after the Civil War that restricted the rights and freedoms of formerly enslaved people and sought to preserve a racial labor system.

  • Civil Rights Act of 1866

    The first federal law to define United States citizenship and affirm equal civil rights regardless of race.

  • Civil Rights Act of 1964

    A landmark federal law prohibiting discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, or national origin in major areas of public life.

  • Emancipation Proclamation

    President Abraham Lincoln's 1863 executive order declaring freedom for enslaved people in territories under Confederate control.

  • Executive Order 14074

    A 2022 presidential order establishing federal initiatives to strengthen accountability, public trust, and standards in policing and criminal justice.

  • George Floyd Justice in Policing Act

    A proposed federal policing reform bill addressing accountability, use of force, qualified immunity, data collection, and law enforcement standards.

  • Grandfather Clauses

    Voting provisions that exempted certain citizens from literacy tests or other requirements based on their ancestors' voting eligibility.

  • Insurrection Act

    A federal law authorizing the President to deploy military forces within the United States under specified circumstances.

  • Jim Crow Laws

    State and local laws that mandated racial segregation and restricted the civil rights of African Americans following Reconstruction.

  • Poll Taxes

    Fees imposed as a prerequisite for voting that were widely used to discourage or prevent electoral participation by poorer citizens.

  • Reconstruction Acts of 1867

    Federal laws establishing military governance in the former Confederate states and setting conditions for their readmission to the Union.

  • Voting Rights Act of 1965

    A landmark federal law protecting voting rights by prohibiting discriminatory voting practices and strengthening federal enforcement.
