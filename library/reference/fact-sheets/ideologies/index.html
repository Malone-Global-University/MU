Ideologies

Ideologies fact sheets explain organized systems of political, social, economic, religious, or philosophical thought. Each entry identifies an ideology’s core assumptions, values, goals, view of power, institutional preferences, historical development, and practical applications.

Collection Overview

This collection serves as the canonical location for systems of thought that interpret society and prescribe how power, institutions, rights, resources, identity, or public life should be organized. Entries distinguish ideology from individual slogans, political parties, social movements, government programs, and personal beliefs.

Related parties, movements, concepts, and economic systems remain in their corresponding categories and may be connected through cross-references.

Collection Scope

  • Political and constitutional ideologies
  • Economic and social ideologies
  • National, racial, cultural, and identity ideologies
  • Religious and philosophical systems of thought
  • Ideological origins, principles, applications, and criticisms

Reference Entries

Ideology fact sheets are listed alphabetically or by major ideological tradition as they are added.
