Organizations

Organizations fact sheets profile groups that coordinate people, resources, authority, or activity around a stated purpose. The collection covers civic, political, religious, cultural, commercial, labor, military, charitable, and community organizations.

Collection Overview

This collection serves as the canonical location for organized groups whose identity, structure, membership, leadership, activities, history, and relationships are central to the subject. Entries distinguish formal organizations from broader institutions, informal movements, brands, coalitions, affiliates, and independent actors.

Institutions, political parties, labor movements, and social movements remain in their corresponding categories when those organizational types are the primary subject.

Collection Scope

  • Associations, clubs, societies, and networks
  • Civic, charitable, cultural, religious, and educational groups
  • Commercial, labor, military, and political organizations
  • Leadership, membership, structure, purpose, and activities
  • Affiliates, chapters, coalitions, disputes, and institutional responsibility

Reference Entries

Organization fact sheets are listed alphabetically or by organizational field as they are added.
