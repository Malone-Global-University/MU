Administrative Procedures

Public institutional procedures documenting repeatable administrative operations, coordination, recordkeeping, and production authorization at Malone Global University.

Collection Overview

Administrative Procedures is the operational-procedures collection within MGU Public Records. It documents recurring institutional activities that require a consistent sequence of responsibilities, approvals, records, and completion criteria. Its purpose is to support administrative continuity as MGU develops additional academic programs, personnel, publishing functions, and shared services.

Substantive documents in this collection are formal PDF procedures. HTML pages are used only as navigation and document indexes; the complete procedural text remains in the PDF, with the professional and executive language of the original record preserved.

Publication in this publicly accessible collection does not itself establish that a draft procedure has been adopted. Each future document should identify its approval status and, where applicable, its effective date.

Scope of Administrative Procedures

Appropriate subjects include formally documented, repeatable administrative processes, such as:

  • Institutional record publication: submission, review, authorization, PDF release, indexing, and maintenance of public institutional documents.
  • Academic production administration: proposal intake, work assignment, production authorization, and handoff between responsible units.
  • Contributor administration: onboarding processes for faculty, subject specialists, editors, and other content contributors, including assignment of administrative responsibilities.
  • Cross-department coordination: shared-resource requests, content ownership escalations, and administrative coordination across departments and the Library.
  • Issue resolution: reporting, escalation, corrective action, responsible-party assignment, and administrative closure of operational issues.

Proposed Document Program

The following are proposed initial procedure subjects, not currently published PDFs. They are listed to define the collection's intended coverage; no document links are assigned before publication.

  1. Institutional Document Preparation, Review, and Publication Procedure — records the process for producing, reviewing, authorizing, and publishing a formal MGU document.
  2. Academic Content Proposal and Production Authorization Procedure — defines intake and authorization of new educational-content production initiatives.
  3. Faculty and Contributor Onboarding Procedure — establishes administrative steps for engaging and preparing instructional contributors.
  4. Cross-Department Resource Coordination Procedure — establishes administrative handoffs and resolution of shared-content ownership questions.
  5. Administrative Issue Escalation and Corrective-Action Procedure — establishes a documented route from issue reporting to verified closure.

Filing Boundaries

Administrative Procedures documents how recurring institutional functions are performed. Strategic decisions about what the university should establish or invest in belong in Institutional Planning; implementation strategies for existing academic departments belong in Department Planning. The general design of coursework belongs in Curriculum Frameworks.

Adopted institutional decisions and directives are recorded in Institutional Decisions and Directives. Publication and scholarly acceptance requirements belong in Editorial and Publication Standards, while document identification, revision status, and supersession controls belong in Document Control and Versioning.

This collection is not MGU's external legal-reference collection. It does not reproduce court decisions, statutes, or other authoritative legal instruments as institution-created procedures. Private personnel records, sensitive credentials, and confidential operational details are likewise excluded from public PDFs.

Document Control and Public Access

Published procedures should identify their title, issuing or responsible unit, date, revision, approval status, and applicable scope. If a procedure is replaced, its index entry should distinguish the current document from historical or superseded editions. One canonical PDF should be maintained for each published version; additional category pages may link to that document without creating duplicate copies.

As part of MGU Public Records, these procedures are viewable public institutional documents. Index pages describe and locate the documents but do not substitute for the official PDF text.
