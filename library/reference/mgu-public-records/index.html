MGU Public Records Overview

MGU Public Records is Malone Global University’s publicly accessible repository for institutional planning reports, whitepapers, administrative procedures, policy documentation, technical standards, production records, and documented audits. These records explain how the university is planned, organized, developed, maintained, and evaluated.

Substantive publications are issued as PDF documents. This page and its category indexes are navigational HTML only; institutional documents are not rewritten as simplified HTML articles. The publication status shown for each document distinguishes proposals and drafts from adopted, superseded, or archived records.

The collection is arranged into three divisions and fourteen primary categories. The Library Catalog Codex is maintained separately under the MGU Reference Library; it is not a Public Records category.

Institutional Document Categories

I. Institutional and Academic Planning

  • Institutional Planning — University-wide development: academic offerings, governance, facilities, workforce, and strategic investments.
  • Administrative Procedures — Documented operating procedures for recurring institutional and cross-department functions.
  • Curriculum Frameworks — University-wide educational architecture, learning objectives, sequencing, assessment, and instructional methodology.
  • Department Planning — Planning reports for the growth and implementation of established academic departments.
  • Institutional Decisions and Directives — Formal records of adopted nonlegal institutional decisions, directives, and changes in policy direction.

II. Technical, Editorial, and Knowledge Infrastructure

  • Website Architecture — Site organization, navigation, digital systems, components, and information architecture.
  • Build, Automation, and Deployment — Production pipelines, build processes, automation, verification, and deployment documentation.
  • Templates and Style Guides — Specifications for approved institutional document templates, page layouts, and presentation standards.
  • Editorial and Publication Standards — Scholarly accuracy, editorial review, citations, attribution, and publication acceptance criteria.
  • Digital Operations and Recovery — Digital preservation, backup, service continuity, restoration, and maintenance policy.
  • Research and Source Management — Source acquisition, provenance, evidence evaluation, and research-register standards.

III. Production Records and Quality Assurance

  • Production Records — Historical production summaries, completed-work registers, and publication milestones.
  • Quality Assurance and Audits — Documented quality reviews, including identified AI-assisted audits, corrective actions, and acceptance findings.
  • Document Control and Versioning — Publication identifiers, revision status, supersession, archival controls, and master records.

Publication and Classification Policy

Each institutional document has one primary category and a canonical PDF location. Other category indexes may reference it without creating duplicate copies. Titles and descriptions in these directories should distinguish existing publications from planned documents.

Historical software-release-notes and weekly-updates folders remain preserved pending classification. They are not counted as permanent categories in the revised directory, and their existing files are not modified by this index update.

Architecture note: The MGU Public Records institutional planning report (PDF) documents the earlier fifteen-category proposal. The Library Catalog Codex has since been moved outside Public Records to /library/reference/library-catalog-codex/, leaving fourteen primary categories in this index.
