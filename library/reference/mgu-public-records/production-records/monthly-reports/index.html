Collection Scope and Filing Standards

Monthly progress reports serve as retrospective institutional production records. They should identify the relevant reporting month and preserve distinctions among newly produced materials, revisions or maintenance of existing files, completed milestones, and work remaining in progress.

Original PDF filenames are retained for historical traceability, and the report PDFs remain the substantive archival documents. This HTML page is an index, not a replacement for those records. Subsequent revised or superseding editions should be clearly identified without obscuring prior reports.

Monthly reports may be cross-referenced with weekly reports and supporting production evidence. A report’s presence in this index does not by itself establish publication approval for every work mentioned in its narrative.
