Computer Science Publication Ledgers

Master and divisional production registers for the MGU Computer Science Encyclopedia, documenting planned scope, work in progress, completed articles, deployment and publication.

Collection Overview

This directory is part of MGU Public Records — Production Records and supports the Computer Science volume of the MGU Encyclopedia. It belongs to the Library Production ledger family.

The publication ledgers establish an item-level inventory of planned and existing content across all twelve divisions. They provide a common basis for commissioning articles, recording production progress, reconciling local files, confirming public release, and controlling changes to the editorial scope.

Collection status: Active production program. Initial baseline: November 23, 2026 (Pacific time). The figures shown on this page describe that baseline, not a live count of published articles.

Production and Publication Status

The register distinguishes the authority for a planned destination from evidence that an article has been authored, installed or released. These are separate operational milestones.

Plan basis
Prescribed, named, proposed, structural or exceptional scope. Approve proposed additions before commissioning them.
Production status
Produced means that evidence of authoring or delivery is recorded. Not recorded as produced means that the baseline lacks such evidence; it does not certify that a file is unwritten.
Local deployment
Present in snapshot or not in snapshot refers to the dated Windows directory inventory. Reconcile a fresh inventory before declaring current installation.
Live publication
Unverified until the intended public address has been checked. Active links and delivered files do not prove live availability.
Completion and publication dates
Record actual milestone dates separately from filesystem modification timestamps.

Ledger Maintenance and Reporting

  1. Select the division and unit, and confirm the exact intended destination and scope authority.
  2. Check current files and prior deliverables before assigning an article to avoid duplicate production.
  3. Record production completion, supporting evidence and the completion date.
  4. Reconcile the local installation and verify the canonical public address separately.
  5. Keep the workbook, consolidated CSV and divisional CSVs synchronized. Preserve dated snapshots for historical reporting and update this index's displayed baseline figures when issuing a revised baseline.

Use the ledgers to support weekly and monthly production reports. Distinguish completed works from file activity, and do not count proposed, deferred or unverified items as confirmed publications.

Completed Production Ledgers

No closed or archived divisional publication ledgers are listed in this edition. All twelve divisional registers remain in the active collection. Individual articles may be complete or published while their parent ledger remains active.

When a defined production scope is closed, preserve a dated final ledger and any applicable completion or publication record here. Later expansion may be managed through a revised or successor ledger.

Upcoming Production Ledgers and Scope Extensions

No separate future ledger has been assigned a filename in this directory. Near-term scope work consists of resolving the undefined unit child lists, reviewing proposed specialist destinations and reconciling inventory and scope exceptions.

New registers and approved scope extensions will be indexed when their filenames and authority are established. This directory is specific to Computer Science; other encyclopedia volumes and institutional ledger families belong in their respective Production Records collections.
