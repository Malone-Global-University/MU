Weekly Reports Overview

This collection provides a chronological index to Malone Global University’s formal weekly production and development reports. The reports document work described for the corresponding reporting periods, including educational content creation, material revisions, institutional website development, and related production activity.

Archive coverage: March 16–November 15, 2026. Indexed reports: 35 PDF files, including two separately retained reports with overlapping August coverage. The PDF files remain in their existing weekly-updates/ archive; this page is a navigational index and does not create duplicate copies.

The periods below are derived from the supplied report filenames. Inclusion in the archive establishes the existence of a listed document, not independent verification of every production claim it contains.

Collection Scope and Filing Standards

The original PDF filenames and reporting date ranges are preserved to maintain historical traceability. Cross-month periods appear under the month in which the reporting week begins. The August 17–23 and August 17–24 documents are both listed because both filenames occur in the supplied inventory; their relationship is not assumed to be a revision or supersession.

The September 28–October 4 report retains the source filename MGU_Weekly_Progress_Report_Septemberr_28-October_4_2026.pdf in its hyperlink, including the additional “r” in Septemberr. The display title uses the correct month spelling for readability.

The source scan august-17-24-modified-files.txt is not a PDF progress report and is intentionally excluded from this publication index. Operational file inventories and supporting scans may be preserved separately as production evidence.

These entries should remain available for historical reference. Any subsequently corrected, revised, or superseding PDF should be clearly identified, rather than silently replacing an earlier edition. Reported file activity must be distinguished from independently confirmed publication or scholarly approval.
