## 5. Facts spot-checked against dated documentary sources
- 2020 Constitutional Court reduction and constitutional amendments: Library of Congress Global Legal Monitor and Russian Supreme Court constitutional text (source entries R01–R02).
- Political-rights assessment: Freedom House, *Freedom in the World 2026*, classifies Russia as Not Free with a 12/100 score. This is a source-defined composite, not a poll of citizens (R03).
- Territorial legality: UN General Assembly resolution ES-11/4 rejects legal validity of Russia’s 2022 attempted annexations of four Ukrainian regions (R14).
- Demography: World Bank population estimate **143,513,328 in 2025**, with territorial-coverage and estimate caveats (R06–R07).
- Poverty: Rosstat’s 2025 SDG publication gives **13.5% in 2015**, **12.2% in 2020**, **7.1% in 2024**, under its national definition (R08).
- Ethnicity: Rosstat’s 2024 yearbook Table 4.8 uses a per-100,000 denominator of census respondents **who specified ethnicity**, with ethnic Russians represented by 80,849 per 100,000. Not a figure for all residents (R09).
- Religious identification: Pew/ISSP reports Orthodox adult identification at 31% (1991), 53% (1998), and 72% (2008); frequent service attendance is a separate category (R12).
- Russia’s 2025 macroeconomic indicators: World Bank reports ~1.0% real GDP growth and ~8.7% annual consumer-price inflation, with 2026 forecasts treated separately (R07, R10–R11).
- OSCE freedom of alliance choice: 1999 Charter for European Security paragraph 8, not inferred solely from the 1990 Charter of Paris (R15, R23).
- Eurasian Economic Union: 2014 treaty; effective 1 January 2015 for founding members, with separate accession dates for Armenia and Kyrgyzstan (R16–R17).
- Human-rights monitoring: UN Human Rights Council Resolution 63/22 issued in October 2026, before the stated November 4 cutoff (R04).

## 6. Source and accuracy limitations
This production pass conducted targeted source verification and coherence review, not exhaustive external archival collation of all narrative claims. The source register contains 24 linked public documentary entry points; **every external link was not checked individually for live availability**. Some documents describe particular earlier years and are not evidence of 2026 conditions. A further independent editorial review should inspect individual contested claims, source attribution, percentages, and any updates required when this chapter is republished.
