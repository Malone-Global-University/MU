Chapter-level assessment and publication review records for Malone Global University's Russian History textbook series.

Collection Overview

This register links only to Russian History audit and review records identified in the supplied institutional directory inventory. Records are arranged by chapter; chapters without recorded audits are omitted. Where multiple audits exist for a chapter, each is listed.

Inventory scope: 9 chapters (Chapters 64–72); 9 existing audit and review records. The links preserve the existing .md files rather than referring to converted PDF editions. A record described as “final” is not, by itself, evidence of independent scholarly peer review.
