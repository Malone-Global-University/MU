## Targeted historical and documentary corrections

1. **2008 war:** Softened the claim that Georgia's army "collapsed as an organized force"; described withdrawals, losses, and continuity of the national armed forces. Recharacterized the EU Tagliavini inquiry as an independent international fact-finding report, not a negotiated political document; distinguish immediate outbreak, background escalation, and legal assessment.
2. **Displacement:** Added the UNHCR breakdown of approximately 192,000 displaced at the conflict's height (September 2008) and the distinction between peak displacement and long-term non-return.
3. **Real GDP:** Identified the retrospective Rosstat/World Bank annual real GDP growth series: 2008 **5.2%**, 2009 **−7.8%**, 2010 **4.5%**, 2011 **4.3%**. Do not silently substitute IMF provisional estimates issued during the recession.
4. **New START:** Clarified the treaty's three categories: 1,550 counted deployed strategic warheads, 700 deployed strategic delivery vehicles, and 800 deployed/non-deployed launchers and heavy bombers, with specified counting rules. Replaced awkward language on the preamble with the treaty's acknowledgment of the relationship between offensive and defensive strategic arms.
5. **Election observation:** Distinguishes OSCE/ODIHR's *5 March 2012 preliminary findings* (available before inauguration) from its *11 May final report* (published after the Chapter 69 political cutoff), and retains the January 12, 2012 Duma final report.
6. **WTO chronology:** Treats the December 2011 accession-package approval as a Chapter 69 development while identifying formal membership on **22 August 2012** as beyond the May 2012 endpoint. Corrected the continuity/comparison feature accordingly.
7. **Time zones:** Corrected the initial reduction from eleven to nine time zones to **March 2010**, while keeping the separate 2011 timekeeping changes.
8. **Chapter boundary:** The March 2012 presidential election and May 6 protest confrontation conclude Chapter 69; the implementation of Putin's next presidential term is assigned to Chapter 70.
