## Consolidated correction record

Specific errors, ambiguities, or unsupported precision in the original condensed paragraphs corrected directly in the final candidate:

1. **Odesa, 2 May 2014:** clarify **48 total deaths**, six in street clashes and 42 associated with the Trade Unions Building fire, rather than attributing roughly 40 undifferentiated deaths to one group.
2. **Minsk II, February 2015:** distinguish the Trilateral Contact Group's adoption of the Package of Measures from the Normandy leaders' political support and subsequent UN Security Council endorsement.
3. **World Bank GDP series:** use a single specified **2022-vintage** growth series in narrative and data tables. For example, 2016 is **+0.2%**, not a decline; 2018 ~2.8% and 2019 ~2.2% are the selected vintage. Earlier literature and subsequent revised statistical series may differ.
4. **Inflation, 2015:** describe ~13% as **year-end** inflation rather than an unspecified average rate.
5. **2018 pension reform:** distinguish proposed 65-year male retirement age by **2028**, proposed 63-year female retirement age by **2034**, and the subsequent final female age of 60 following presidential revision.
6. **Syrian basing arrangements:** Hmeimim aviation-group agreement originated **2015**; later Tartus naval arrangements expanded in **2017**, not both originating in 2017.
7. **European security principle:** identify the **1999 OSCE Istanbul Charter for European Security** as the source of the explicit right to choose alliance arrangements, rather than attributing the phrasing only to the 1990 Charter of Paris.
8. **INF Treaty:** distinguish U.S. suspension and withdrawal announcement **2 February 2019** from completed withdrawal and termination **2 August 2019**.
9. **Russian COVID vaccination:** qualify “roughly half” as about **45.8% fully vaccinated by 31 December 2021**, based on a dated historical series.
10. **Pandemic mortality:** explicitly identify about **1,029,181 excess deaths in 2020–2021** as one **peer-reviewed demographic model estimate**, not as an official COVID death count.
11. **2020 constitutional vote:** state official reported **77.92% approval and approximately 67.97% turnout**, without implying independent verification of these percentages.
12. **Other carried-forward facts:** clarify Russia's Crimea military control versus internationally recognized Ukrainian sovereignty; 2014–2021 Donbas death categories; MH17's technical, criminal and later judicial evidentiary roles; constitutional authority versus contemporaneous political claims; precise chapter cutoff of 31 December 2021.
