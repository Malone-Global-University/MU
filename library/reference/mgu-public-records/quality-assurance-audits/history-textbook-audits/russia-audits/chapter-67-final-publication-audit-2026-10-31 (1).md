## Final surgical changes applied to Claude's revised HTML

1. **§67.2:** Replaced incorrect assertion that the 1990 RSFSR Congress contained seats reserved for the Communist Party and public organizations. Its **1,068 seats** were divided among **900 territorial and 168 national-territorial constituency seats**; the special organizational-seat system belonged to the 1989 USSR Congress. Documentary election information: https://elib.shpl.ru/ru/nodes/11512-vybory-narodnyh-deputatov-rsfsr-1990-goda?view=list
2. **§67.37:** Corrected the 150% refinancing-rate decision from **May 19 to May 27, 1998**, explicitly distinguishing May 19 (raised to 50%) from May 27 (raised to 150%). Official Bank of Russia data: https://www.cbr.ru/eng/statistics/idkp_br/refinancing_rates1/
3. **§67.39:** Replaced loose “a quarter or more” wage-loss claim with an IMF-grounded **approximately 23% fall in average annual real wages in 1999 versus 1998**, allowing that the worst monthly comparisons showed larger falls: https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2004/315/article-A001-en.xml
4. **§67.20:** Narrowed the overbroad claim that the entire Congress categorically refused participation in the 1993 Constitutional Conference to a statement about the **congressional leadership's challenge to its authority** and the competing constitutional draft.
5. **§67.29 and §67.34 Primary Source Windows:** Added precise, externally grounded names/dates and reader links for the Chechnya and OSCE source records while retaining non-verbatim labeling and original interpretive text.
