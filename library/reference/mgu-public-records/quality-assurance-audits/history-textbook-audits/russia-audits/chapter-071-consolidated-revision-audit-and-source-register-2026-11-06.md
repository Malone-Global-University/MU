## Historical and editorial corrections incorporated
1. **Cross-chapter references:** corrected original ten-heading cross-references to the expanded Chapter 69 and Chapter 70 architectures. Euromaidan is now §§70.11–70.13; Crimea §§70.14–70.16; Donbas §§70.17–70.23; sanctions/economy §§70.24–70.29; Syria §§70.30–70.34; 2008 Georgian campaign §§69.5–69.10. No surviving `§70.4`, `§70.5`, `§70.6`, `§70.7`, `§70.8`, or `§69.3` references were detected in displayed text.
2. **Mariupol fatalities:** original narrative replaced a vague/ambiguous statement with the OHCHR verified *minimum* of 1,348 civilian deaths as reported June 16, 2022; separate February 2024 HRW/Truth Hounds/SITU study estimated at least 8,034 *excess deaths* using cemetery evidence. Their categories and dates are not equated.
3. **Kakhovka attribution:** physical mechanism and seismic/infrasound evidence are distinguished from the question of an individual perpetrator or final adjudication. The scientific study is a documentary lead, not a judicial determination.
4. **2026 military chronology:** drafted as of November 4, with careful differentiation of geolocated presence, Russian/Ukrainian military claims, temporary infiltration and established control. No outcome after cutoff is asserted.
5. **Civilian harms and occupation:** UN verification, survivor interviews, ICC warrants, national claims, refugee/IDP stocks and legal categories are deliberately distinguished.
6. **Damage and macroeconomic series:** RDNA5 direct damage ($195.1 billion), broader losses ($666.7 billion) and recovery needs ($587.7 billion) are separate concepts measured through December 31, 2025; Russian GDP 2023–2025 actual observations are separated from a 2026 central-bank survey forecast.
7. **Diplomatic cutoff:** announced future November 10–11, 2026 Abu Dhabi talks are explicitly recorded as *planned* before the November 4 cutoff, never described as completed.
8. **Presentation:** original full-width desktop MGU sidebar and design system retained; an accessible collapsible mobile contents menu added for screens where the sidebar is hidden, plus feature-index links and documentary reference anchors.
