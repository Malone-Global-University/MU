## 2. Historical/editorial corrections applied

1. **Independent candidates:** Corrected the erroneous “more than a tenth of the seats” claim. Official ECI table: **37 of 489 elected seats = 7.57%**, despite **15.90%** of recorded candidate-votes. Official party-return data also used to make the comparison table precise.
2. **Polling arrangements:** Replaced “over 200,000 polling stations” with the contemporary Election Commission report's **132,560 physical polling stations, containing 196,084 polling booths**. Clarified that later ECI summaries call the 196,084 figure “polling stations,” a different counting convention.
3. **Election denominators:** Clarified that **44.99%** Congress, **15.90%** independents, and other party percentages are shares of recorded *candidate-votes*. Multi-member constituencies permitted more than one candidate-vote per voter; these returns cannot be equated with unique voter turnout. The approximately **45.7% turnout** is reported separately as the Commission's retrospective turnout series.
4. **Women's enrollment:** Attributed the approximately **2.8 million removed roll entries** to Sukumar Sen's estimate, as discussed by historian Ornit Shani. Made explicit that these were registration exclusions caused by missing individual names, not a statute prohibiting women from voting.
5. **Article 324:** Identified the election commission's constitutional authority as originating in **Article 324 of the Constitution**, distinct from the Representation of the People Acts of 1950 and 1951.
6. **Documentary research:** Replaced the tentative five-item bibliography with 18 linked research items, including original Assembly opening and Objectives Resolution debates, Ambedkar's November 1949 speech, the Constitution as published in the original 1949 *Gazette*, the First Amendment, the Representation of the People Acts, original ECI reports, Supreme Court history, and 1951 Parliament records. Added documentary research links inside each source window. Current/consolidated statute texts are flagged as potentially amended.
7. **Metadata/counts:** Volume VI retained as **Postcolonial South Asia**, consistent with the supplied manuscript; the correct Part XVI title retained. Chronology has 33 entries; vocabulary preview and glossary each have 30, correcting the draft handoff's count descriptions without padding content.
