## Factual and bibliographic corrections consolidated

1. **Election table:** Congress seats by province now follow the official-return-based classification summarized in Vidya Borooah, *Implementation across National Boundaries*, table 2, p. 117, based on *Returns Showing the Results of Elections in India, 1937* (Cmd. 5589): Madras 159; Bombay **85**; United Provinces **134**; Bihar **91**; Central Provinces and Berar 70; Orissa 36; Bengal 54; Punjab 18; Assam **32**; NWFP 19; Sindh **8**. Total **706 / 1,585**. The table distinguishes election-day party labeling from subsequent affiliations; the Punjab Unionist approximately 95-member grouping is qualified rather than treated as identical to the original ticket total.
2. **Franchise:** removed the erroneous statement that only 10–16% of *adults* had the vote. Distinguished contemporary proposed electorate (~35 million; ~14% total population, ~27% adults) from later actual registration and differing populations used in denominators.
3. **Muslim League electoral data:** distinguished reserved-seat performance, province-specific outcomes, independent Muslim candidates and later provincial alliances; UP League figure harmonized to 26 within the selected return convention.
4. **Independent Labour Party:** compared the Lok Sabha Secretariat's retrospective **15 wins / 17 contested** with contemporary party-label classifications showing **13** in Bombay; specified attribution and documented both rather than asserting a resolved single figure.
5. **Khare affair:** expanded July 1938 Central Provinces cabinet crisis, governor's intervention, Congress Working Committee censure, and Ambedkar's separate interested interpretation, making clear that partisan accusations are not independently demonstrated motivations.
6. **Rajkot:** corrected Sir Maurice Gwyer's title to **Chief Justice of the Federal Court of India**, distinguished arbitration from adjudication by a Supreme Court, treated representation disputes and Gandhi's subsequent repudiation of reliance on the award, and avoided calling the fast's conclusion a durable settlement.
7. **Linlithgow, June 1937:** distinguished 21 June speech reference in the Nehru edition from 22 June broadcast/publication references; chronology now indicates **21–22 June 1937** rather than universalizing one date.
8. **Narrative depth:** expanded provincial constitutional power, elections and geography, ministerial accountability, rural/labor policy, communal accusations as evidence, competing nationalist positions, ILP assembly politics, Bose's Haripura–Tripuri dispute, princely-state representation and differentiated regional coalitions.
9. **Source register:** replaced editorial “verify/confirm” production notes with an eight-item source-document register and a supporting annotated evidence list with traceable URLs, document identities, dates, and limitations. Primary-source boxes continue to be explicitly labeled editorial paraphrases.
