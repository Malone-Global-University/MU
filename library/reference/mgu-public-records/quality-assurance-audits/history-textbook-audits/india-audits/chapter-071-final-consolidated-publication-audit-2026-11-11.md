## 4. Narrative integrity and chapter-boundary checks

All 20 approved section responsibilities remain intact. The expanded material supports the assigned historical thesis: the Raj combined sovereignty exercised from London and Calcutta with provincial and district bureaucracy, indirect rule, military and police power, courts, and restricted consultation, while depending on Indian personnel, intermediaries, and institutions to function. In particular:

1. §§71.1–71.4 explain the India Office, Council of India, Viceroy, and service recruitment, with corrected ICS admission history.
2. §§71.5–71.8 differentiate provincial forms and trace district record-keeping, municipal consultation, and budget responsibilities; an **explicitly illustrative** revenue-appeal pathway is not masquerading as a factual case reconstruction.
3. §§71.9–71.12 analyze indirect rule, Baroda, Mysore, succession, and frontier legal diversity while respecting differences among princely states, provinces, and independent neighboring polities.
4. §§71.13–71.16 explain army, police, legal codes, courts, procedural equality and the Ilbert controversy, without replacing Chapters 70 or 73.
5. §§71.17–71.19 distinguish official from additional legislative members and nominal from effective representation, and more clearly relate historians' arguments to identifiable works.
6. §71.20 remains substantive synthesis rather than replacing formal review apparatus.

The chapter does not substantially retell the military suppression of 1857 (Chapter 70), the economic history reserved for Chapter 72, the social classification assigned to Chapter 73, or the formation and later diversification of nationalism in Chapters 76–77.

## 6. Documentary transparency and limits of approval

- Two individual legal/policy source windows (Ripon's 1882 resolution and the 1861 Councils Act) remain **explicitly editorial paraphrases**, not quotation-collated original-text extracts. The district-gazetteer exercise is an expressly **illustrative source-genre study**, not a facsimile or exact district entry. These are appropriate instructional formats if described accurately.
- The included 20-entry guide identifies relevant texts and scholarship but is **not** a page-by-page verification of every claim, not a claim that each original printed edition or archive item was personally collated, and not a substitute for later facsimile-based source editing.
- The geography feature is a **text-guided atlas exercise**, not an embedded historical map plate.
- Exact byte parity with the separately mentioned Chapter 2 model is not verifiable because the model was not supplied. CSS preservation was checked against the current Claude original, with only the stated mobile fix.
