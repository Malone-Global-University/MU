### 1977 election and Bhutto's trial

Source Window 4 now identifies the Inter-Parliamentary Union's specific historical 7 March 1977 election entry and a contemporary FRUS diplomatic assessment, https://data.ipu.org/parliament/PK/PK-LC01/election/PK-LC01-E19770307/ and https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v19/d236 . Source Window 5 links the reproduced text of Pakistan Supreme Court's 6 March 2024 advisory opinion, https://pakistancaselaw.com/case/193764/reference-by-the-president-of-islamic-republic-of-pakistan-under-article-186-of . The 2024 court held the original proceedings failed fair-trial and due-process requirements but stated it could not set aside the final historical conviction under its advisory jurisdiction. Later judgment must not be mistaken for a reopening of the murder evidence.

### Federal Shariat Court and zakat legislation

Chronology reordered so the **May 1980 Federal Shariat Court creation precedes the 20 June 1980 Zakat and Ushr Ordinance**. Source Window 6 now links specific historical legal texts or an identified legislation index: https://www.pakistani.org/pakistan/constitution/orders/po1_1980.html ; https://pakistani.org/pakistan/legislation/zia_po_1979/ord7_1979.html ; https://pakistancode.gov.pk/english/LGu0xBD?action=primary&page=1&year=1980 . These sites sometimes reproduce *amended* versions; the chapter discloses the distinction rather than projecting later language backwards.

### 1988 general election figures — material source discrepancy

The Inter-Parliamentary Union historical page reports **92 PPP seats in its prose summary but 94 in its seat-distribution table** (https://data.ipu.org/election-summary/HTML/2241_88.htm). The draft's unsupported 'roughly 93 of 205' was replaced by a statement that PPP emerged as the largest party without an absolute majority, the IJI secured approximately 55 seats, and the archival discrepancy is disclosed explicitly. A definitive single PPP count cannot be certified from this internally inconsistent page alone. The historical transition to Benazir Bhutto's premiership on 2 December 1988 is not dependent on that exact count.

### Historical economic comparison

Quantitative Study 1 was replaced with **published decade-average sectoral growth data** rather than unsourced approximate estimates for entire Bhutto and Zia regimes. The revised table reports approximate IMF-published 1970s versus 1980s annual growth rates of GDP **4.8% / 6.5%**, agriculture **2.4% / 5.4%**, industry **6.1% / 7.6%**, manufacturing **5.5% / 8.2%**, and services **6.3% / 6.7%**. These are **decade indicators, not identical to presidential or fiscal-year terms**; the limitations and global economic confounders are explained. Institutional study: https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2002/247/article-A002-en.xml . The underlying series have undergone revisions; the table must not be treated as a new original statistical calculation.

### Afghan refugee totals

Quantitative Study 2 now uses **dated original UNHCR annual-report totals** and does not rely on unverified mid-year figures from a secondary transcription. Approximately 400,000 at the start of 1980; approximately 1.4 million at the turn of 1980–1981; and more than 2.375 million by December 1981 **as reported to UNHCR**. The text distinguishes government registration, government estimate, and actual total displacement. Sources: https://www.unhcr.org/node/1395 ; https://www.unhcr.org/publications/report-united-nations-high-commissioner-refugees-12 ; https://www.unhcr.org/publications/report-united-nations-high-commissioner-refugees-26 . Source Window 7 also includes specific FRUS diplomatic memoranda about Pakistan and aid: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v12/d51 ; https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v19/d405 .

## Disclosures and limits

1. The **two map studies are text-guided**, not illustrated cartography; publication is therefore approved for the *scholarly textual HTML edition*, not an illustrated-map edition.
2. Most source windows have explicitly labeled **documentary paraphrases**, not authenticated verbatim historical quotations. Links to later amended legal instruments are identified as such.
3. The 1988 IPU seat totals have an **unresolved internal contradiction**. The chapter does not pretend a single authoritative total was established by that page.
4. The named source URLs were selected or corroborated through targeted research, but **not all 26 links underwent live HTTP accessibility tests**. Access restrictions and institutional redirects may affect readers, especially for the IMF and PDF resources.
5. No claim is made that every individual clause, archival record, or manuscript sentence has undergone external professional peer review. This is editorial approval for the documented textual edition following a consolidated factual and technical review.
