## IV. Independent factual and source verification

The following sources were independently identified and compared against the chapter's principal claims. Links are included to enable future editors to reproduce checks; those links do not signify that every report was analyzed cover to cover.

### A. Treaties, national legal history and climate commitments

1. **Indus Waters Treaty (1960):** [United Nations Treaty Collection](https://treaties.un.org/Pages/showDetails.aspx?clang=_en&objid=0800000280135336); its framework and dispute procedures are historically correct. The [Permanent Court of Arbitration, August 31, 2026](https://pca.int/en/news/pca-press-release-pca-case-no-2023-01-the-indus-waters-western-rivers-arbitration-islamic-republic-of-pakistan-v-republic-of-india-6/) documents the 2026 award. India's rejection of the tribunal's authority is distinguished from the tribunal's conclusions; the dispute is not presented as resolved.
2. **Ganges Water Sharing Treaty (1996):** official [Government of India treaty text](https://mowr.nic.in/core/WebsiteUpload/2023/2023011877.pdf). Its signing on December 12, 1996 and 30-year term are consistent with a scheduled December 2026 expiry; the chapter does not assert a post-cutoff renewal outcome.
3. **Bangladesh river commission:** [legal analysis of the 2013 Act and September 2014 establishment](https://online.thedailystar.net/law-our-rights/law-watch/news/strengthening-the-national-river-conservation-commission-bangladesh-1813927), [official 2013 legislation](https://www.dpp.gov.bd/upload_file/gazettes/20160_58673.pdf), and the [2019 Turag judgment](https://www.supremecourt.gov.bd/resources/documents/1048627_WP13989of2016.pdf). The distinction between institutional establishment and subsequent guardianship is now correct.
4. **Paris Agreement:** [United Nations Treaty Collection](https://treaties-prod.un.org/pages/ViewDetails.aspx?chapter=27&clang=_en&mtdsg_no=XXVII-7-d&src=TREATY). The chapter distinguishes its procedural obligations and nationally determined targets.
5. **India 2031–2035 NDC:** official [UNFCCC submission](https://unfccc.int/node/656817) and independent [Climate Action Tracker assessment](https://climateactiontracker.org/countries/india/2035-ndc/). The separate conditional and unconditional elements are now stated precisely.
6. **Maldives second NDC (2025):** [International Energy Agency policy summary](https://www.iea.org/policies/29759-second-ndc-of-maldives). A conditional emissions-reduction objective of 1.52 MtCO₂e relative to business as usual by 2035 is attributed to the newer submission.

### B. Scientific and disaster claims

7. **Himalayan cryosphere:** [ICIMOD HI-WISE 2023](https://hkh.icimod.org/hi-wise/hi-wise-report/). The chapter recognizes heterogeneity among mountain regions, melt dynamics and river basins; projections are described as conditional.
8. **Climate scenarios and sea-level ranges:** [IPCC AR6 WGI Summary for Policymakers](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/summary-for-policymakers/) and [IPCC AR6 WGII Asia assessment](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/chapter/chapter-10/). Global sea-level ranges and reference period have been made explicit.
9. **Pakistan floods, 2022:** [World Bank joint assessment release](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2022/10/28/pakistan-flood-damages-and-economic-losses-over-usd-30-billion-and-reconstruction-needs-over-usd-16-billion-new-assessme): more than **US$14.9 billion damages**, about **US$15.2 billion economic losses** and at least **US$16.3 billion resilient reconstruction needs**; the categories are distinct.
10. **April–May 2026 regional heat:** [World Weather Attribution's May 2026 study](https://www.worldweatherattribution.org/climate-change-exposes-hundreds-of-millions-to-longer-and-deadlier-pre-monsoon-heat-in-south-asia/). The comparison of increased likelihood and thermal intensity is reported as an event-attribution result rather than observational proof about each individual day.
11. **Cyclone Amphan (2020):** [GDACS/ECHO event reporting](https://www.gdacs.org/report.aspx?eventid=1000667&eventtype=TC) contains country-specific figures and illustrates the variation among preliminary death tallies. The revised narrative identifies Bangladesh separately from India.
12. **Cyclone Ditwah (2025):** [World Bank GRADE press release, December 22, 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/12/22/damage-from-cyclone-ditwah-in-sri-lanka-estimated-at-4-1-billion) supports the direct-physical-damage estimate.
13. **Adaptation finance:** [UNEP Adaptation Gap Report 2026](https://www.unep.org/index.php/en/publications/report/adaptation-gap-report-2026) supports the reported US$28.2–31.7 billion 2024 international-public flow and its distinction from estimated future needs.
