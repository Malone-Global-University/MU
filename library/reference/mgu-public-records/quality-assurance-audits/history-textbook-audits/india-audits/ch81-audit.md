## Editorial actions completed

1. **Delhi Manifesto (§81.5):** Changed to **1 November 1929** and named signatories supported by the contemporaneous identification; Jinnah's documented presence at the 23 December Irwin meeting remains separate from the uncertain signatory claim.
2. **Qissa Khwani Bazaar (§81.11):** Removed the unsupported 'twenty to thirty' official tally. Explained the colonial **approximately forty** deaths versus Shah's retrospective **approximately 230** deaths and more than 500 injured. Preserved attribution and uncertainty.
3. **Garhwal Rifles (§81.11):** Distinguished nationalist accounts of refusal to fire from the official court-martial charge of refusing orders to proceed from barracks into the city; retained the seventeen sentences in the 1930 parliamentary account and acknowledged differences in dating.
4. **1932 arrests and convictions (§81.16):** Identified the 80,000 cumulative arrests as a contemporaneous estimate attributed to Malaviya through a later account, not an official census. Reported the **39,517 convictions** and **31,114 convicted persons still imprisoned at 31 March** separately from arrests.

6. **Nehru characterization (§81.19):** Dated the 'charter of slavery' characterization to **27 March 1936**, rather than implying it was spoken during the Act's passage.
7. **Bibliography/source register:** Completed Sayed Wiqar Ali Shah's full 2013 journal citation and DOI; supplied source links for the Delhi Manifesto, Hansard evidence, the 1935 Act and Nehru's 1936 statement; identified the provenance and limits of the Malaviya arrest claim.
