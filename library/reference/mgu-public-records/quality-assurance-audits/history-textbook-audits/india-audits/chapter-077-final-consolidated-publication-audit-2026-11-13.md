## Historical corrections and source-based refinements

1. **Bengal 1905 population:** Clarified that ~78 million refers to the earlier Bengal administration, ~31 million to new Eastern Bengal **and Assam**, and ~54 million to residual Bengal. The new unit included Assam, so the figures must not be summed as a division of 78 million. Sources: Banglapedia, [Partition of Bengal, 1905](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php?title=Partition_of_Bengal%2C_1905); Government of India Resolution July 1905.
2. **R. C. Dutt bibliography:** Removed imprecise undifferentiated 1901–1903 span and identified his first economic history (1902) and the Victorian-age companion (1903/1904 edition variation). Bibliography check: [Azim Premji University — Romesh Chunder Dutt](https://azimpremjiuniversity.edu.in/indian-economists/romesh-chunder-dutt).
3. **Sekhar Bandyopadhyay edition:** Corrected *From Plassey to Partition* (2004 original) versus *From Plassey to Partition and After* (2015 revised and enlarged). Check: [author bibliography](https://vuw.academia.edu/SekharBandyopadhyay/CurriculumVitae).
4. **1906 national education chronology:** Foundation of the National Council of Education, Bengal on 11 March 1906, inauguration of Bengal National College on 14 August 1906. Source: [NCE Bengal institutional history](https://ncebengal.com/golden-foundation/).
5. **1907 Surat split:** Added the relationship between proposed presidency, 1906 program, unsuccessful compromises, and the later organization of Congress. Treat partisan retrospective accounts carefully. Source: [Congress documentary compilation](https://library.bjp.org/jspui/bitstream/123456789/337/1/Congress-Evolution%20-%20Congress%20Resolutions%20-%201885-1934-Freedom%20Movement.pdf).
6. **1909 reform:** Differentiated Indian Councils Act from electoral regulations giving operational form to separate Muslim electorates; did not present limited franchise as parliamentary democracy.
7. **1908 Muzaffarpur / Alipore:** Distinguished the intended target Douglas Kingsford from two Kennedy-family civilian victims; distinguished convictions from Aurobindo acquittal. Trial document collation not represented as complete.
8. **1909 Dhingra shooting:** Named Dr Cawas Lalcaca as a separate person killed when intervening, not an anonymous collateral figure. Peer-reviewed study: [British Journal of Criminology](https://academic.oup.com/bjc/article/64/2/417/7240613).
9. **1911–1912 rearrangement:** Differentiated the 1911 announcement from implementation in 1912, explained why territorial settlement was not a return to the exact pre-1905 units. Source: [States Reorganisation Commission report (1955), §§21–24](https://origin1504-mha.nic.in/sites/default/files/2022-08/State%2520Reorganisation%2520Commisison%2520Report%2520of%25201955_270614%5B1%5D.pdf).
10. **League 1913:** Distinguished March 22–23 Lucknow constitutional objective from Jinnah membership entered on October 10, 1913. Source: [NIHCR chronology](https://nihcr.edu.pk/Downloads/Chronology.pdf).
11. **Source register:** Corrected identified scholarly edition and added eight externally traceable documentary/bibliographic locators. Six embedded windows remain **editorial paraphrases and source studies, not collated transcripts**.

Source locators document selective external checks, not exhaustive verification of each narrative sentence.
