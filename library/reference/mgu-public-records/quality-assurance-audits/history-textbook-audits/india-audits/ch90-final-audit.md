## 2. Historical corrections and scholarly expansion

1. **Correct court and legal chronology:** `State v. Dosso`, **27 October 1958**, was decided by the **Supreme Court of Pakistan**, not the Federal Court. The **Federal Court** is properly named for the 1955 *Maulvi Tamizuddin Khan* litigation. The discussion differentiates revolutionary legality from constitutional authorization and the 1955 necessity reference from 1958 reasoning. Sources: https://pja.gov.pk/system/files/CONSTITUTIONAL_HISTORY.pdf and reported judgment (PLD 1958 SC 533), https://pbbarcouncil.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/constcases.pdf.
2. **Bogra Formula arithmetic explained:** proposed lower house 165 East / 135 West, upper house 10 East / 40 West, yielding **175/175** in joint sitting, with cross-wing concurrence conditions. Source: https://norr.numl.edu.pk/attachments/31_1593150320_1737e112b0.pdf.
3. **One Unit clarified:** a corresponding western administrative unit, **not** a province equal to East Bengal in geographical size or population; special/tribal arrangements remained complex. The western regional opposition is developed.
4. **Language movement and 1954 election:** expanded student mobilization, civil-liberties issues, contested casualty counts and memorialization. The **223** United Front seats are properly described as out of **237 Muslim seats** in the 309-seat provincial legislature, rather than out of the entire house. Source context: https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Language_Movement.
5. **1953 Lahore martial law:** identified as a significant early precedent for military administration of civilian affairs, not asserted to be the first occasion that army authority was ever used in civil order.
6. **1962 constitutional amendment chronology:** distinguished the act's **1963 title and legislative approval** from **presidential assent on 10 January 1964**. The amendment's original legal text: https://molaw.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/Collections/1964.pdf.
7. **1969 transition:** distinguished Yahya Khan's immediate martial-law authority on **25 March 1969** from the **31 March** date for formal assumption of the presidency in a Pakistan Senate historical exhibit. Some official officeholder summaries begin the presidency on 25 March; both conventions are disclosed. Primary-diplomatic and Senate sources: https://www.senate.gov.pk/uploads/documents/Gali-e-Dastoor%20Final.pdf and https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76ve07/d14.
8. **East–West quantitative study:** replaced provisional numbers with a consistently defined estimate of **annual per-capita income at factor cost and 1959–60 prices** (Rs per person). The historical series reports East/West **287/338** (1949–50), **278/366** (1959–60), and **331/537** (1969–70); differences **51, 88, 206 rupees**, or western advantages **17.8%, 31.7%, 62.2%**. Source: Mohammad Niaz Asadullah, Oxford Discussion Paper 63 (2006), Table 1, reproducing Pakistani planning estimates: https://www.nuff.ox.ac.uk/economics/history/paper63/63asadullah.pdf.
9. **Development spending:** gives sourced approximate eastern shares of *central development expenditure*, **20% in 1950–55** and **36% in 1965–70**, while explicitly warning against applying these percentages to total public spending, aid, imports or actual individual benefits. Unverified exact ratios for export share, aid receipt and Bengali army officers were removed from the quantitative table rather than fabricated.
10. **Six Points and 1969:** expanded the proposed constitutional allocation of powers, foreign-exchange safeguards, military vulnerability, the Agartala case, and the roles of the student movement, without presenting an independent Bangladesh as an inevitable objective before 1971. A contemporaneous February 1966 press text is available at https://songramernotebook.com/archives/480659.

## 6. Scope and limitations

1. The eight source windows distinguish authentic short quoted phrases from editorial paraphrases or documentary summaries. They are **not** complete original-document diplomatic transcriptions; the exact wording of every page of every original archival publication has not been independently re-collated.
2. Two map studies remain **text-guided** and do not contain original authenticated cartographic plates; historical boundary/source attribution and rights clearance are required if figures are subsequently added.
3. The final source register identifies official archives and research editions and includes links tested or grounded through current discovery, but **not every external link was checked end-to-end for uninterrupted access**; some institutional sites may block automated retrieval.
4. Quantitative estimates represent one explicitly defined constant-price series. Alternative historical estimates may differ; the text appropriately separates fixed-base calculations from broader qualitative descriptions and avoids unverified exact numeric claims for military recruitment, international aid or export shares.
5. The **live MGU deployment, site-wide styling compatibility, and exhaustive accessibility audits** were not tested. Local Chromium testing establishes the working static HTML and mobile controls, not production deployment.
