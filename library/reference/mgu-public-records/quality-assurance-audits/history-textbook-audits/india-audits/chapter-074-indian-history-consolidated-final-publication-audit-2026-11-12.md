## 4. Historical corrections and targeted refinements

1. **§74.2 — 1878 Cooch Behar marriage:** Names Suniti Devi and Nripendra Narayan. Clarifies that their ages conflicted with thresholds under the 1872 Act but that Suniti's retrospective account says the princely marriage was not celebrated under that statute. Avoids an unsupported assertion of statutory illegality. **Witnesses:** Act III of 1872, §2(2), https://www.indiacode.nic.in/repealedfileopen?rfilename=A1872-3.pdf ; Suniti Devi, *The Autobiography of an Indian Princess* (1921), chapter IV, https://www.gutenberg.org/files/57175/57175-h/57175-h.htm .
2. **§74.4 — Dayananda's *Satyarth Prakash*:** Corrects edition history: first edition 1875, authorial revision in his final years c. 1882–83, varied publication/completion dates (including 1884); the two versions are not treated as identical. **Verification:** J. T. F. Jordens, *Indian Economic & Social History Review* 9.4 (1972), 367–379, https://doi.org/10.1177/001946467200900402 .
3. **§74.14 — *Shetkaryaca Asud*:** Replaces the unsupported categorical 1881 date with composition/presentation in 1882–83 and qualified print chronology; the text contains an 1883 concluding date. The precise date of first printed circulation remains a bibliographic question. The chapter does not infer its printing date from completion.
4. **§74.13 and chronology:** Khalsa College established 1892, but first collegiate classes began 1897; source: https://sanvad.org/about-college .
5. **§74.15 and people reference:** Distinguishes the 10 October 1856 letter mentioning **Fatima** from modern reconstructions of Fatima Sheikh's full biography and teaching roles. Reference: https://theprint.in/india/finding-fatima-sheikh-scholars-point-to-phules-letter-photo-negative-british-era-document/2440053/ .
6. **§74.15:** Frames the Ayyankali public-road protest as traditionally dated 1893 and avoids overclaiming near-contemporary documentation.
7. **§74.16:** Identifies Kadambini Ganguly and Chandramukhi Basu as the first two women graduates of Calcutta University in 1883.
8. **§74.9 and §74.11:** Clarifies Allahabad University affiliation (16 November 1887) for the Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental College and the Kanpur Nadwat al-Ulama public meeting (April 1894).

These adjustments are limited to verifiable editorial points; the chapter's historical interpretations and section boundaries remain the same.
