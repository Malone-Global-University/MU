## Historical corrections completed

1. **Bhutan founding National Assembly (§91.10):** corrected initial membership from an undifferentiated “about 130” to the Parliament of Bhutan’s **36 at the 1953 inaugural sitting: 15 people’s representatives, 16 government nominees, and five monastic members**. Larger retrospective figures are identified as referring to a later/unspecified composition, rather than allowed to replace the contemporary founding count. Source: https://parliament.gov.bt/about
2. **Bhutan changes of 1968–1969 (§91.12):** clarified relinquishment of royal veto on Assembly legislation in 1968 and subsequent no-confidence arrangement in 1969; distinguished these reforms from a modern multiparty parliament. Sources: https://parliament.gov.bt/about and https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/435281468005741668/pdf/PUB4307000Bhut00a0Himalayan0kingdom.pdf
3. **Nepal Panchayat voting (§91.8):** corrected claim that direct voting existed only at village level. Both **village and town** Panchayat representatives were locally elected, with higher levels chiefly indirect or involving appointments. Source: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/842531493140288816/pdf/UNN163000Nepal00development0project.pdf
4. **Maldives first Republic (§91.14):** corrected the misleading assertion that Hassan Nooraddeen II was the reigning sultan in 1952–1953. Clarified his departure in 1943, the absent sultan-elect Abdul Majeed Didi and his death in 1952, the republic beginning 1 January 1953, Mohamed Amin Didi’s presidency ending 21 August 1953, and restoration of the sultanate in 1954. Source: https://presidency.gov.mv/PO/FormerPresident/7
5. **Southern Maldives (§91.15):** distinguished the Addu uprising and declaration at the turn of 1958–1959 from the joining of Huvadhu and Fuvahmulah and the three-atoll United Suvadive political formation in February–March 1959, avoiding a falsely precise January three-atoll proclamation. Source: https://www.qaumiyyath.gov.mv/docs/whitepapers/history/southern%20rebellion.pdf (official state perspective, cross-check with local histories).
6. **Gan treaty chronology (§§91.15–91.16):** distinguished 1956 draft/initialed arrangements, the **14 February 1960 signed agreement**, and the **26 July 1965 independence treaty**, which superseded the earlier agreement. The latter recognized sovereign independence while preserving Gan and Hithadhoo base rights, specified in the agreement until 15 December 1986, and continued certain security restrictions. Source: https://treaties.un.org/doc/publication/unts/volume%20548/volume-548-i-7980-english.pdf
7. **Maldives at UN (§91.16):** specified admission **21 September 1965**, while the country still had a sultan, separating international independence from the **11 November 1968** second republic. Source: https://foreign.gov.mv/index.php/en/multilateral/international-organisations/united-nations?catid=46&id=3116&view=article
8. **Chronology:** reordered 8 August 1949 before December 1949; July 1960 before 15 December 1960; specified the 1964 bilateral citizenship pact date; improved the 1959 Suvadive chronology.
