## 3. Source-backed historical corrections incorporated into corrected baseline

1. **§47.4–5, timelines and back matter — Bengal succession:** Husain Shah's first regnal year is better placed in **1494**, supported by dated inscription/coin evidence and Banglapedia. Habshi rule terminates in the 1493–1494 transition, not definitively 1493. Sources: https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Rule_of_Afghans ; https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Mint_Towns
2. **§47.8 — Maladhar Basu:** *Sri Krishna Vijaya* belongs to the late Ilyas Shahi period, composition c.1473–1480; Basu's honorific is associated with Ruknuddin Barbak Shah. The submitted text wrongly places him under the Husain Shahis. Evidence: https://www.ntm.org.in/download/ttvol/volume16-1/note%202.pdf (literary study); https://www.thedailystar.net/2004/02/21/d40221150285.htm
3. **§47.19 — Hoshang Shah's Odisha expedition:** Chronicles date it c.1421–1422, involving an elephant-acquisition expedition described in a merchant-disguise tale. Preserve the existence of the historical report while qualifying dramatic details. Evidence: https://persian.packhum.org/text/000702052/6 ; R. Subrahmanyam, *The Suryavamsi Gajapatis of Orissa*.
4. **§47.25 and Atala source window — Atala Mosque:** Its late-Tughlaq foundations are better established than the word "tradition" implies; final Sharqi construction/inscription dates to 1408. Earlier temple-site claims remain a distinct evidentiary problem. Evidence: https://jaunpur.nic.in/tourist-place/atala-masjid/ ; https://www.eng.hamarajaunpur.com/2011/05/atala-mosque-built-by-sultan-ibrahim.html
5. **§§47.24, 47.26, timelines and synthesis — Sharqi/Lodi chronology:** The campaign beginning in 1479 and final effective capture around 1483–1484 are differently dated across studies; do not collapse 1479 battle(s) into an uncontested terminal date. A dedicated SOAS dissertation dates Husain Shah Sharqi's exile and death around 1505. Evidence: Richard M. Eaton, *India in the Persianate Age*, https://zenodo.org/record/5119357/files/Eaton%202019.pdf ; M. M. Saeed, *The Sultanate of Jaunpur*, https://soas-repository.worktribe.com/output/415729/the-sultanate-of-jaunpur ; and UNESCO https://en.unesco.org/silkroad/sites/default/files/knowledge-bank-article/vol_IVa%20silk%20road_the%20delhi%20sultanate.pdf .
6. **§47.29 — temple damage attribution:** Distinguish physical evidence of damage from causal attribution to Sikandar's reign. Textual evidence may support coercion, but archaeology alone cannot date or assign every episode.
7. **§47.30 — Zain-ul-Abidin:** He reportedly **prohibited cow slaughter/restricted beef consumption**, not "abolished restrictions on cow slaughter." Corrected while preserving its primary-source qualification. Evidence: https://www.outlookindia.com/amp/story/national/j-k-s-beef-with-beef-why-kashmiris-love-mutton-but-hate-beef-bans-news-193937 ; https://kashmirobserver.net/2015/12/01/post-bihar-budshah%C2%92s-beef-lesson-for-india-2/
8. **§47.31 — maize:** Removed anachronistically undated maize from a general fifteenth-century Kashmir agrarian list. Maize is a New World crop whose arrival and local adoption require period-specific evidence, not an assumption of pre-1500 availability. Evidence: https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/50301000/Races_of_Maize/RoM_India_0_Book.pdf
9. **Rishi vocabulary feature — Nund Rishi:** Updated traditional dates to c.1378–1440 with explicit uncertainty; Lal Ded's exact dates appropriately remain qualified. Evidence: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00856401.2021.1954755 and https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/kashmir/embedded-mystics-writing-lal-ded-and-nund-rishi-into-the-kashmiri-landscape/735710A78E357C6E9FCC59A6AD96403F
