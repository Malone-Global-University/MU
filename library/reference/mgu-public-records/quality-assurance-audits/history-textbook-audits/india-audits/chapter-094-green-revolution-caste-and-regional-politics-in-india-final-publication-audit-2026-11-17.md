## 3. Key historical verification and editorial controls

1. **Green Revolution:** Official Reserve Bank of India crop output series underpin the comparisons of 1960–61 through 1990–91. The chapter explicitly distinguishes increased output, yields, irrigation, regional diversity, and nutrition. Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=17793
2. **Agricultural census:** Operational holdings are correctly distinguished from landownership and do not directly count every landless worker. Source: https://desagri.gov.in/programs-schemes/agriculture-census-scheme/
3. **Mandal Commission:** The appointment (1979), report (1980), and 27% central reservation decision (August 1990) are separate events. The 1992 judgment is identified as later. Sources: https://ncbc.nic.in/Writereaddata/1123.PDF and https://indiankanoon.org/doc/1363234/
4. **Dalit organizations:** The Dalit Panthers (1972) and the BSP (1984) are distinguished from other movement forms. The 1973 manifesto is paraphrased, not reproduced as a purported exact quotation. See Cambridge University Press studies listed in the bibliography.
5. **1984 and 1989 election data:** The manuscript distinguishes the initial **404** Congress wins in December 1984 from the **414** eventual result after delayed 1985 polling, and recognizes Congress's **197** seats in 1989 as a loss of majority, not disappearance. Source: https://www.eci.gov.in/EBooks/Leap_of_faith_Second_Edition/files/basic-html/page93.html
6. **Assam:** The 15 August 1985 Accord's pre-1966, 1966–24 March 1971, and post-24 March 1971 categories are explained without equating community identity with citizenship status. Source: https://assamaccord.assam.gov.in/portlets/assam-accord-and-its-clauses
7. **Nellie:** The killing of civilians on 18 February 1983 is explicitly classified as a massacre. Approximately 1,800 official reported deaths are distinguished from higher estimates; exact totals remain disputed. See reporting and references on the Tewary Commission.
8. **Punjab:** The 1973 Anandpur Sahib Resolution and 1978 revision/endorsement are differentiated, and constitutional Akali demands are separated from armed separatism. Original government positions, parliamentary records and retrospective human-rights work are attributed separately.
9. **Operation Blue Star:** The June 1984 operation, damage to the Akal Takht, and material disagreement about casualties are presented without an invented aggregate death count; government and later critical accounts are separated.
10. **Anti-Sikh violence:** The Nanavati Commission/Ahuja Committee **2,733 Delhi deaths** and Ministry of Home Affairs **3,325 nationwide deaths** are correctly presented as different geographical measures, not competing estimates of the same population. Sources: https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-12/Nanavati-I_eng_08072020%5B1%5D.pdf and https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=116853
11. **Rajiv–Longowal Accord:** Dated **24 July 1985**, not 25 July; the date follows official historical records and the reproduced settlement text. Source: https://www.satp.org/satporgtp/countries/india/states/punjab/document/papers/memorandum_of_settlement.htm

The narrative preserves the distinction between established acts of violence, contested responsibility or motivations, and quantified estimates subject to evidentiary limitations. It does not attribute individual crimes to whole religious or linguistic communities.

## 5. Disclosed limitations, publication scope, and lock

- The two geographic map studies are **text-guided** rather than original illustrated/cartographic images. This edition is explicitly approved as a **textual HTML edition**.
- Several primary-source windows are documented **paraphrases**, not exact transcription of a single archival item. They are labeled accordingly; source links point to institutional records or scholarly locators. The archived 1984 government White Paper and Nanavati Commission reports require contextual scrutiny.
- The 30 references are cited or linked as a publication source register, but this technical audit did **not** independently perform live HTTP access tests for every outbound destination. Future link maintenance may be necessary.
- Statistical data rounded by the original draft retain clear approximate labels where applicable. Complete source-year table reproduction and graphical regional cartography can be developed for a later illustrated edition without reopening the textual editorial lock.
- The manuscript's historical cutoff is the completion of the Part XVII narrative through 1991, with later inquiries explicitly identified as retrospective evidence; it does not claim events after 1991 occurred during that period.
