## Historical corrections and independently identified documents

1. **Sri Lanka's executive presidency:** The presidency with executive powers began on **February 4, 1978** under the **Second Amendment to the 1972 Constitution**. The **September 7, 1978** Second Republican Constitution consolidated the new arrangement; it did not first establish the office. Corrected §96.10, the constitutional source window, and chronology. Source: Parliament of Sri Lanka, *Heads of State*: https://www.parliament.lk/en/learn/handbook-of-parliament/heads-of-state ; 1978 Constitution: https://www.parliament.lk/en/constitution/1978-constitution.
2. **Bhutan's citizenship law:** The **1958 Nationality Law** permitted paternal descent; the **1985 Citizenship Act** required **both parents** to be Bhutanese citizens for citizenship **by birth** and separately addressed citizenship by registration against the December 31, 1958 threshold and naturalization. Corrected §96.19. Source: https://www.refworld.org/legal/legislation/natlegbod/1985/47860 and the text of the 1958 law: https://www.satp.org/satporgtp/countries/bhutan/document/actandordinances/nationality_law_of_bhutan.htm.
3. **Bhutan–Nepal refugee arrivals:** Distinguishes initial arrivals in 1990–1991 (about **5,000 in Nepal by September 1991**, from later retrospective reporting) from much larger movements and UNHCR-supported camp development in 1992. Source: UNHCR, *Report of the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees, 1992*: https://www.unhcr.org/node/1453 . The precise early-arrival totals vary by source and counting point.
4. **Bangladesh's parliamentary restoration:** The Twelfth Amendment Bill was passed **August 6, 1991** and endorsed in the **September 15, 1991 referendum**. Corrected §96.8 and closing chronology. Source: https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Constitutional_Amendments and https://www.idea.int/answer/ans91561913133174 . Do not conflate parliamentary passage and referendum ratification.
5. **Dr. Shamsul Alam Khan Milon:** Corrected the imprecise October/November wording to his **November 27, 1990** killing. Source: https://www.thedailystar.net/news-detail-65139 .
6. **Chronology:** Reordered **May 30, 1981** (Ziaur Rahman killed) before **May 31–June 1, 1981** (Jaffna Public Library fire); moved March 1990 IPKF withdrawal before December 1990 Ershad resignation; retained 35 entries by dropping two low-priority repetitive previews and adding the 1991 constitutional settlement.
7. **Sri Lanka and Black July:** Maintains attributed uncertainty over casualty totals; retains distinctions among Sri Lankan Tamils, Indian-origin Tamils, Sinhalese and Muslims, state forces and multiple armed organizations. Source: https://www.sciencespo.fr/mass-violence-war-massacre-resistance/en/document/massacres-sri-lanka-during-black-july-riots-1983.html .
8. **Bangladesh famine of 1974:** Preserves attribution of mortality and entitlement analyses rather than equating famine with a single rainfall or crop failure event. Source: https://academic.oup.com/book/32827/chapter-abstract/275136196 .

## Disclosed documentary limitations

- The identified 30 clickable reference entries were selected from retrieved public legal, official, intergovernmental, scholarly, and historical pages. **Not every external URL was individually opened in a live browser**; ongoing external URL availability is not guaranteed. Do not construe link inclusion as proof every target is permanently accessible.
- Current online constitutional consolidations can contain **later amendments**. For verbatim period-specific passages, consult contemporaneous versions; the textbook's primary source windows are labelled **paraphrases**, not verified quotations.
- The 1983 Black July window references a retrospective scholarly synthesis alongside its discussion of contemporary evidence, rather than providing a digitized contemporaneous testimony transcript. It should not be misrepresented as a direct eyewitness facsimile.
- The Bangladesh and Sri Lanka quantitative studies use **rounded, explicitly labelled instructional estimates**; they are not source-critical national time series. Census categories and election eligibility/participation may differ; displacement figures should not be aggregated across unrelated dates.
- Map studies contain textual map prompts rather than embedded maps. Illustrated cartography is a potential future enhancement, not a requirement for **scholarly textual HTML** publication.
- The assigned five-division plan is fulfilled and all five countries are treated; later developments remain contextual rather than replacing the 1971–1991 subject.
