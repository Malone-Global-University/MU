## Corrected historical and bibliographic points

1. **1939 ministry resignations versus Section 93.** Eight Congress-led provincial ministries resigned in October–November 1939; seven governors imposed direct rule under Section 93. Assam formed an alternative ministry. All direct narratives, chronology, source vocabulary, synthesis, key terms and instructional-feature claims were conformed. UK Parliament's 18 April 1940 debate explicitly lists seven provinces and contrasts Assam. Source: https://hansard.parliament.uk/commons/1940-04-18/debates/52e08511-7bf1-4121-9486-0b59a0a2faa5/GovernmentOfIndiaAct1935%28ProclamationsUnderSection93%29 .
2. **Reserve Bank series corrected beyond the previous draft audit.** The previously proposed “Rs 171.8 crore at end-December 1939” was itself a mistaken reading of the RBI table: **171.8 belongs to 1935**. Correct *notes in circulation* observations are Rs **236.6** crore at end-December **1939**, Rs **1,137.5** crore at end-June **1945**, and Rs **1,236.9** crore at end-June **1946**. The RBI table expressly notes that 1935–1939 use end-December and later years end-June. This is a note-issue series and should not be presented as total coin-plus-note currency. Corrected in §83.17, Source Window 8, and the register. Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/SCRIPTS/PublicationsView.aspx?id=8202 .
3. **Volunteers versus strength.** Replaced a misleading apples-to-apples comparison of approximately 205,000 men in 1939 with 2.5 million in August 1945. Prewar strength estimates vary with their denominator; approximately 2.5 million men volunteered over the course of the war, a *cumulative recruitment figure* rather than a single August 1945 Army headcount. Navy and air force figures are presented with explicit classification uncertainty. Corrected in opening, chronology, §83.13, Source Window 7, continuity/change, and summary. Sources: https://academic.oup.com/book/26014/chapter-abstract/193878943 ; https://www.gov.uk/government/news/the-worlds-war-in-europe ; https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/LIB23231 .
4. **Casualty measures.** Removed a potentially misleading pairing of Army-specific deaths with wider all-services or commemorative totals as though they were rival estimates of one quantity. The revised §83.14 explicitly distinguishes killed in action, other deaths, wounded, missing, captured, and different service denominators.
5. **Documentary register completed.** Replaced an editorial to-do list called “Verified Source Register” with a finished, linked **Source Register and Documentary Method**. It gives grounded bibliographic particulars for Gwyer and Appadorai (1957), Pirzada's *Foundations* (1970), Mansergh and Lumby (1970), Cripps Cmd. 6350 (1942), Sri Nandan Prasad (1956), the International Labour Office's *International Labour Review* 50(6), 736–750 (1944), RBI's historical table, and identified secondary scholarship, including R. J. Moore's *Churchill, Cripps, and India, 1939–1945* (1979). Explicitly identifies where published documentary locations are verified but exact facsimile/page collation has not occurred. Unverified page claims and self-contradictory 'verify later' publication placeholders were removed. Sources in the HTML register link to supporting catalogs and institutions.
6. **Lahore/Cripps interpretation.** Preserved the cautious distinction between motion and reported adoption of the 23–24 March 1940 Lahore Resolution, and retained Cmd. 6350 / 30 March 1942 for the Cripps Draft Declaration. These documentary windows remain labeled paraphrases.
