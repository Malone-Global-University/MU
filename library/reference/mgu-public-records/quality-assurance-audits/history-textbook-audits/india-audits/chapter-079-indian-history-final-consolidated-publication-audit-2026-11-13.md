## III. Consolidated historical and bibliographic corrections

1. **§79.17 — Malabar Mappila railway wagon tragedy (November 1921):** Corrected the false description of roughly 70 transported people to **100 prisoners transported**, including the reported 97 Mappilas and three Hindus, and **70 eventual deaths**. The chapter attributes the deaths principally to asphyxiation due to dangerously obstructed ventilation and identifies failures of transport supervision. Source: *Report of the Committee Appointed to Enquire into the Death of Certain Mappilla Prisoners* (1922), IOR/L/P&J/6/1774, item 1534; secondary scholarly account in *Modern Asian Studies*.
2. **§79.18 — Chauri Chaura appeal:** Clarified **172 initial death sentences** pronounced by a sessions court in January 1923 versus the **170 death-sentence cases before the Allahabad High Court**, 19 affirmed, 110 commuted to transportation for life, 38 acquitted, three rioting convictions. Nineteen eventually executed. Exact public statement: Hansard, 4 June 1923.
3. **§79.14 — Nagpur Congress, December 1920:** Added **14,583 delegates** as recorded in the official Congress historical synopsis while acknowledging minor published count variance. Preserved the four-anna annual membership and the fifteen-member Working Committee.
4. **§79.17 — Imprisonment estimate:** Retained the approximate 30,000 figure only as a rounded secondary estimate, explicitly not a reconciled jail census.
5. **§79.18 documentary window — Gandhi's published response:** Identified *Young India*, **16 February 1922**, “The Crime of Chauri Chaura,” reprinted in *Collected Works of Mahatma Gandhi*, vol. 26, item 82, rather than the undated title alone.
6. **§79.19 — 1924 aftermath:** Replaced the draft's claim that 1924 developments were beyond the chapter's main period, since its accepted scope ends in 1924. Retained Kohat and Belgaum only as a short endpoint epilogue.
7. **Secondary bibliography:** Supplied fuller publication identifications for Brown, Amin, and Gail Minault. Minault: *The Khilafat Movement: Religious Symbolism and Political Mobilization in India* (New York: Columbia University Press, 1982). Brown: *Gandhi's Rise to Power: Indian Politics 1915–1922* (1972). Remaining entries retained with their existing edition descriptions.
8. **New documentary locator register:** Added selected traceable institutional sources for the key numerical and documentary corrections. The chapter distinguishes primary records, modern institutional summaries, and secondary studies.

## V. Publication limitations (transparent disclosure)

The six primary-source windows are identified **editorial paraphrases**, not reproductions of verified original-language, page-collated quotations. The everyday-life study is explicitly illustrative. Historical map exercises are text-guided and require a separate historical atlas. Bibliographic verification was targeted to the identified corrections and a subset of major works; this audit is not an exhaustive catalogue or archival-facsimile collation. The official Mappila inquiry was verified through published documentation and scholarly treatment, not by inspecting the complete original manuscript. Exact byte-for-byte parity of the separate original Chapter 2 HTML shell was not independently checked; the user's supplied chapter CSS/JS were preserved except additive responsive features. Live website deployment, upstream asset URLs and a full screen-reader/accessibility audit were not performed.
