## Consolidated historical corrections

1. Corrected July 24, 1991 chronology: budget and Statement on Industrial Policy were presented on the same day, after the July 1 and 3 rupee adjustments.
2. Rewrote the gold crisis passage to differentiate May 1991 government 20-tonne gold lease / SBI sale with buyback option from July 1991 RBI pledge of **46.91 tonnes** to the Bank of England/Bank of Japan for approximately **US$405 million** in collateralized borrowing. These are not one undifferentiated gold sale.
3. Clarified the Gulf crisis interrupted employment and remittances rather than absolutely ending all Gulf remittances. Qualified the gradual Soviet-bloc trade unraveling.
4. Replaced the old FEMA-only source window with the **Economic Survey 1992–93 foreign-investment discussion**, documenting 51% automatic approval for priority industries. FEMA remains explained as a later development.
5. Corrected the 1991–92 Economic Survey's publication context: it was a retrospective assessment of the fiscal year, **not a document published alongside the July 1991 budget**.
6. Explicitly distinguished United Front member CPI from externally supporting CPI(M) in reference apparatus; specified a contemporary source describing the CMP release as 4 June 1996, while noting retrospective secondary accounts use 5 June.
7. Removed the stray cross-reference implying that Chapter 97 (which ends in 1991) covers India's May 1998 nuclear tests; referenced Chapter 105 for the subsequent nuclear-security story.
