## Essential historical revisions — completed

1. **Gandhi and Kasturba chronology.** Corrected Kasturba Gandhi's death from 22 February **1943** to 22 February **1944** in the chronology and §84.8; corrected Gandhi's fast breaking to **3 March 1943** in chronology and main narrative. Checked against Mani Bhavan's Gandhi chronology and the Indian government's Rajghat chronology.
   - https://www.gandhi-manibhavan.org/about-gandhi/detailed-chronology.html
   - https://rajghat.gov.in/chronology
2. **Quit India repression figures.** §84.8 now clearly attributes Secretary of State Leo Amery's parliamentary answer of 11 February 1943: **60,229 cumulative arrests by 1 December 1942**; **39,498 still held** as of that date; **470 police** and **68 military firing occasions** reported for the same period. Explains that these categories do not form a combined total or cover the entire movement through 1943. Later retrospective figure of approximately **940 deaths from firing** is separated and qualified, not misdated to the parliamentary return.
   - https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/commons/1943/feb/11/prisoners-and-detainees
3. **Bengal famine mortality estimates.** §84.17 differentiates the Famine Inquiry Commission's estimate of approximately **one million excess deaths during 1943** from the commission's **1.5 million total**, which includes continued excess mortality during **1944**. Subsequent higher demographic estimates retain attribution and methodological caution. Commission original scanned Part II, printed pp. 108–110, reviewed including its page image.
   - https://ir.nbu.ac.in/handle/123456789/1939
   - https://www.indiaofthepast.org/sites/indiaofthepast.org/files/pdf/1940s/bengalfamine/ficreportpart2.pdf
4. **Burma campaign sequence.** §84.16 distinguishes Operation Dracula's parachute landing near Elephant Point on **1 May 1945**, amphibious landings on **2 May**, and entry into Rangoon during **2–3 May**. Also clarified §84.14 that the August 1943 Quebec decision about South East Asia Command preceded the formal assumption of operational responsibility at midnight **15–16 November 1943**.
   - https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/may-1945
   - https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/second-world-war-campaign-narratives/campaigns-in-the-far-east-vol-v-air-supply-operations-in-burma-1942-1945/
5. **Tamluk historical source holding.** Upgraded the vague archive mention to the actual Prime Ministers' Museum and Library **Tamralipta Swadhinata Sangram Itihas Committee** collection (61 files, 1924–1983); source study correctly distinguishes catalogue-level documentation from an item-by-item facsimile transcription.
   - https://pmml.nic.in/downloadNmmlManuscripts
6. **Documentary apparatus.** Replaced the unfinished editorial instruction in the source register with actual institutional links, a source-study locator matrix, and explicit limitations. No fabricated verbatim passages or unverified manuscript page numbers were introduced.
7. **Geography studies.** Reframed two map notes as text-guided exercises, not promised embedded cartography; any future map plate must be separately sourced and licensed.

## Explicit scholarly and deployment limitations

Selected source identities, chronologies and numerical series have been checked, not every printed-page or archival-item claim.

- The final HTML was tested locally in Chromium, but **live Malone University deployment**, website links outside the file, production network settings and exact byte-for-byte parity with Chapter 2 stylesheet were not independently checked.
