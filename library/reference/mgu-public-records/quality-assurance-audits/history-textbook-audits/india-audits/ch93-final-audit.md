## 3. Corrections made and evidence basis

1. **Election results:** the 1977 542-seat Lok Sabha figures remain 295 Janata seats and 154 Congress seats, with the chapter's national vote shares retained. The Rae Bareli defeat margin is now **55,202 votes**, supported by official election return data. The source register now links the Election Commission's actual 1971 and 1977 statistical reports, along with the commission's own reports index.
2. **Detention estimates:** the original approximately 110,000 claim is now scoped to a *cumulative scholarly estimate across the Emergency*, not a single official MISA detention count. The supplementary evidence note contrasts this with Rajya Sabha's **about 17,000 MISA detainees around the election** and **6,851 as of 25 March 1977**, and explains that these are counts on specific dates.
3. **Sterilization statistics:** the main narrative and quantitative study retain **2,624,755 (1975–76)** and **8,132,209 (1976–77)** recorded procedures. An evidence note separately identifies **548 complaints involving unmarried people** and **1,774 deaths reported as linked to sterilization** in the parliamentary summary. These figures cannot establish how many procedures were coercive.
4. **Turkman Gate:** the official six-death count is explicitly classified as a government-reported figure for police firing, not a maximum or an estimate of all casualties or displacement.
5. **Article 352:** an editorial note clarifies that the statutory requirement of **written Cabinet advice** was a safeguard introduced *after* the 1975 proclamation by the Forty-fourth Amendment; it did not apply in June 1975. The chapter distinguishes the legal framework then in force from later reform.
6. **Forty-fourth Amendment:** passage, presidential assent, and staggered commencement are separated to avoid anachronistic statements that all safeguards became operative simultaneously.
7. **1977 state assemblies:** wording changed from "nine northern and western states" to **nine states**, avoiding an inaccurate geographic generalization. Parliamentary and Supreme Court sources were added.
8. **Document status:** source windows for the 1969 banking ordinance, 1971 campaign, and press controls remain **explicit documentary paraphrases**. No unverifiable invented verbatim excerpts were added; the campaign's official election return is clearly labeled *not* the original manifesto.
9. **Source hierarchy:** removed Wikipedia entries from the publication source register. Added parliamentary, official electoral, judicial, government, and curated primary-document links; all seven further-reading items now have bibliographic/catalogue or publisher links. Journalistic sources are retained only as context or testimony, not as substitute official counts.

## 5. Publication limitations, transparently disclosed

- The two map studies are **text-guided geography exercises**, not illustrated cartographic maps. This is accepted for the textual HTML edition, not for an illustrated atlas edition.
- Several source windows quote no full original document because exact public reproductions of the 1971 Congress manifesto and individual censorship circulars were not available; the paraphrase designation remains visible to readers.
- The compilation relies on an attributed mixture of formal documentary records and research literature. The cumulative detention figure and the scope of coercive sterilization are not precise censuses; that uncertainty is disclosed in the text.
- Web references were checked against located authoritative records; the audit is not a warranty that every external host will always serve its files. The archived HTML itself is self-contained except for its outgoing reference links.
- The visual and interaction tests were conducted locally in Chromium, not on the deployed university website. Local hosting, content delivery, and website integration remain the publisher's operational responsibility.
