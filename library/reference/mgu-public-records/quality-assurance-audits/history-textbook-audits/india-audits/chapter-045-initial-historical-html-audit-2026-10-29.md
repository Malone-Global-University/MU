## Priority source-checked claims
1. **Talikota:** 23 January 1565 is a common conventional date (e.g., historical gazetteer), but contemporary/source-derived dating conflicts, including 24 and 26 January proposals. Qualify as a traditional date, not absolutely established. Corrected in attached working HTML. Sources: https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/75359.pdf ; https://www.wisdomlib.org/history/book/further-sources-of-vijayanagara-history/d/doc1887143.html
2. **Krishnadevaraya's 1512 Deccan expedition:** defensible as approximate in conventional reconstructions; dates vary between 1511 and 1512 for Bidar/Gulbarga and Bahmani restoration. Do not confuse with eastern Udayagiri siege, c.1513–14, Kondavidu 1515. Sources: https://ebooks.inflibnet.ac.in/icp01/chapter/the-vijayanagara-empire/ ; https://teluguhistory.org/en/people/krishnadevaraya/
3. **Udayagiri Krishna image:** draft claims installed at Hampi in 1513. Inscription-derived chronology supports **1514**, not 1513; corrected in working HTML. Source: https://www.wisdomlib.org/history/book/further-sources-of-vijayanagara-history/d/doc1887140.html
4. **Madras land grant:** 1639 grant and developments in 1640 are acceptable as 1639–40, but distinguish local Damarla Nayaka grant and subsequent imperial confirmation; do not say merely unnamed local nayaka without explaining actual legal chain. Source: Greater Chennai Corporation, https://portal.chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/about-GCC/about-chennai/origin-and-growth/
5. **Ramamatya:** *Svaramelakalanidhi* completed in **1550** at the request of **Aliya Rama Raya**, with Venkatadri's intervention; vague 'Rama Raya's circle' acceptable but imprecise. 6. **Right- and left-hand caste divisions:** evidence for occupationally and politically organized groupings, especially Tamil-speaking regions; avoid defining them as two rigid all-Indian varnas or equating either wing simply with Vaishnava/Shaiva religions.
