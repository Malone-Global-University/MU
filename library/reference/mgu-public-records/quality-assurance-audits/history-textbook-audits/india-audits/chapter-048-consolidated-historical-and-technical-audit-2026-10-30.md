## 3. Historical and source verification

**General historical interpretation: sound.** The manuscript appropriately distinguishes historically recoverable individuals from hagiographical tradition and later manuscript transmission; avoids an oversimplified unified Bhakti movement; respects the specificity of Sufi orders and Sikh history; and distinguishes arguments about caste from evidence of actual social reform. The manuscript does not rely on fabricated direct quotes in the six source boxes.

Selected checks and editorial repairs:

1. **Bhakti movement historiography (§48.5):** Consistent with John Stratton Hawley's *A Storm of Songs* (Harvard University Press, 2015), which treats the linear south-to-north movement narrative as a historical construction rather than unproblematic fact. https://universityseminars.columbia.edu/books/a-storm-of-songs-india-and-the-idea-of-the-bhakti-movement/
2. **Tamil hymns source window:** Vidya Dehejia, *Antal and Her Path of Love: Poems of a Woman Saint from South India* (SUNY Press, 1990), is real and relevant. https://books.google.com/books/about/Antal_and_Her_Path_of_Love.html?id=baOJTQyTTX8C
3. **Kannada vacanas source window:** A. K. Ramanujan, *Speaking of Siva* (Penguin, 1973), is a real translated selection. https://books.google.com/books/about/Speaking_of_%C5%9Aiva.html?id=lHtkAAAAMAAJ
4. **Chishti source window:** Bruce B. Lawrence's annotated translation *Morals for the Heart* (Paulist Press, 1992), of Amir Hasan Sijzi's *Fawa'id al-Fu'ad*, is correctly identified. https://books.google.com/books/about/Nizam_Ad_din_Awliya.html?id=aPCvzgEACAAJ
5. **Guru Nanak and Sikh scripture (§§48.37–48.41):** Pashaura Singh's *The Guru Granth Sahib: Canon, Meaning and Authority* is a study of Sikh scripture and textual canon formation, **not a verified translation of the specific Japji/Babarvani passages**. The corrected source box differentiates scholarship from an actual verified translation. https://academic.oup.com/book/10787
6. **Adi Granth of 1604:** Distinguish Guru Arjan's compilation from later recensions incorporating the compositions of Guru Tegh Bahadur and later scriptural authority. Clarified in corrected source-box edition note. https://academic.oup.com/book/10787/chapter-abstract/158925374
7. **Guru Arjan, Jahangir, and 1606 (§48.41):** The manuscript's cautious account agrees in broad outline with Pashaura Singh's treatment of the controversial execution, which discusses both the accusation concerning Khusrau and religious/political factors. https://academic.oup.com/book/4017/chapter-abstract/145673953
8. **Bhai Mardana's instrument (§48.38):** Corrected the misleading word *rebec* to **rabab, a plucked lute**. Scholarship on Sikh shabad kirtan identifies Mardana's accompaniment as a rabab. https://punjab.global.ucsb.edu/sites/default/files/sitefiles/journals/volume18/Volume18_FULL.pdf
9. **Gurmukhi (§48.39):** Tempered the unqualified claim that the script made the Guru's words independent of Persian and Devanagari; historical continuity with earlier North Indian writing systems is already recognized in the same section.
10. **Caste (§48.43):** Replaced a claim that the vacana teachers *dismantled* social hierarchy in their community with a historically defensible distinction between critique of hierarchy and evidence about practice.
11. **Chronology:** Moved the 1564–1624 Sirhindi entry from after 1606 to the correct chronological region, and adjusted the entry containing Baba Farid (d. 1265) from “late 13th” to “mid-13th”. Overlapping life-span ranges in an overview timeline are unavoidable and explicitly approximated.

**Verification limit:** These are selected checks against public scholarly/bibliographic sources, not a source-by-source proof of every historical proposition in 44 narrative sections. Source windows remain paraphrases, not verified excerpts from particular verses or manuscript folios. The final expansion should identify individual verses/passages and dependable editions where the claims depend on a specific text; never invent quotation wording or manuscript shelfmarks. The chapter's three map studies are textual atlas exercises, not embedded map assets; acceptable where the model does not prescribe image assets, but actual map artwork would require a separate authorized production step.
