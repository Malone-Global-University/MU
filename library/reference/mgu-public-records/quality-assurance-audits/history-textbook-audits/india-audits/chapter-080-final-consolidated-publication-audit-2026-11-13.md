## 2. Historical corrections completed

1. **Mahad chronological distinction (§80.15):** Clarified the Bombay Legislative Council's Bole resolution of **August 1923** versus the **Mahad municipal resolution of 5 January 1924** permitting access to the Chavdar tank. Removed the malformed unsourced `(talen)` reference. The account now avoids asserting a citywide municipal declaration covering all water sources.
2. **Mahad litigation (§80.15):** Clarified the municipality's reversal of the access resolution on **4 August 1927**, the exclusive-use claims of caste-Hindu litigants, and the Bombay High Court's decision of **17 March 1937** rejecting the exclusionary claim. Distinguished an asserted private-ownership theory from the municipal nature of the tank. The 1937 reference is a historical consequence, not a separate post-1929 chapter narrative.
3. **AITUC (§80.6):** Retained the Bombay inaugural session of **31 October 1920** and Lala Lajpat Rai as first president. Distinguished organizer **N. M. Joshi**, early general secretary **V. M. Pawar**, and **Dewan Chaman Lall** as his successor in 1921, following published AITUC executive records. This resolves a prior imprecise inaugural officer formulation.
4. **1929 Child Marriage Restraint Act (§80.18):** Distinguished legislature passage in September 1929, Governor General's assent on **1 October 1929**, and **commencement on 1 April 1930**. Retained the contemporary original age thresholds (girls under fourteen and boys under eighteen), as opposed to thresholds from later amendments.
5. **Dange bibliographic precision (§80.8):** Identified S. A. Dange's 1921 text by cataloged title, *Gandhi vs. Lenin*, rather than a generic reference to a pamphlet on Gandhi and Lenin.
6. **Documentary infrastructure:** Added a seven-entry *Primary-Source Register and Documentary Locators* and sidebar link before the existing fifteen-title *Further Reading* list. The register supplies traceable volumes/editions and URLs where verified, and explicitly flags uncollated primary files or newspaper issues instead of inventing page references.
