## Material historical and source corrections implemented in the working master

These are localized, fact-directed changes; all approved sections and original instructional features remain. The working master has **4,018** direct historical narrative words after corrections; it is **not** a full-depth manuscript.

1. **§51.20 Gujarat chronology:** The draft placed Akbar’s defeat of **Ibrahim Husain Mirza at Sarnal** in September **1573**, confusing the first campaign with the rapid second expedition. Sarnal belongs to **1572**, during the initial campaign. The famous **1573** forced return march was directed against **Mirza Muhammad Husain and Ikhtiyar-ul-Mulk**, who besieged **Ahmadabad**. Distinguish the engagements, dates, and opponents. Sources: https://www.cyberlibrary.org/en/books/history-of-gujarat-gazetteer-of-the-bombay-presidency-volume-i-deabd6e2/chapters/20/ ; https://persian.packhum.org/text/000701023 .

2. **§51.24 Isa Khan:** The original categorically asserted that Isa Khan had accepted Mughal authority before his death in 1599. This cannot be established by the selected source base; Richard Eaton documents the defeat of imperial river forces by Isa Khan’s coalition in **1597** and continuing local resistance. Replace categorical submission with evidence-based language and require a specific contemporary record before reinstating it. Source: https://publishing.cdlib.org/ucpressebooks/public/book/the-rise-of-islam-and-the-bengal-frontier-1204-1760.html .

3. **§51.30 Sindh:** Abd al-Rahim Khan-i Khanan’s operation extended through **1592**; Jani Beg’s negotiated surrender is commonly dated **1592**, rather than presenting 1590–91 as the full campaign. Sources: https://persian.packhum.org/text/080201015/4 ; https://persian.packhum.org/text/000702051/6 .

4. **§51.15 Chittor:** Avoid both uncritical acceptance of exact massacre figures and dismissing them as “likely rhetorical” in a way that minimizes well-attested large-scale violence. Abu’l-Fazl explicitly reports nearly 30,000 killed, although the exact total and social categories require scrutiny. State the reported estimate, the event’s documented severity, and the source’s attempt at justification. Source excerpt: https://ethicsofsuicide.lib.utah.edu/selections/abul-fazl-ibn-mubarak/ .

5. **§51.32 Khandesh:** Nominal recognition of Akbar’s supremacy in the early 1590s should not be conflated with completed incorporation, which required conquest culminating at Asirgarh in **1601**.

6. **Timeline:** Corrected overstatements that Chittor ended Mewar resistance and that the northwest was fully secured by 1588.

7. **Mobile chronology:** Added only a narrowly scoped CSS media rule to make the wide timeline table internally horizontally scrollable at phone widths.


## Historical fact-check priorities for the full-depth manuscript

- **Accession and 1556 (§§51.1–51.6):** Confirm Kalanaur succession, Hemu’s role under the Sur regime, battlefield source traditions, and the political stages between Bairam Khan’s dismissal and Akbar’s personal control. Do not rely on simplistic religious categories for Hemu’s following.

- **Chittor, Haldighati and Mewar (§§51.12–51.18):** Identify Jaimal and Patta precisely; separate imperial and later Rajput versions; acknowledge the Chittor massacre and treatment of civilians; keep the tactical battlefield result at Haldighati distinct from Maharana Pratap’s long-term resistance. Check alleged Rajput wedding identities and Nagaur assembly chronology before supplying precise names or dates. For Nagaur pilgrimage, see Cambridge JRAS research: https://doi.org/10.1017/s1356186322000153 .

- **Gujarat (§§51.19–51.21):** Distinguish events of 1572 and 1573 (see corrected baseline); explain multiple local elites and maritime interests rather than asserting immediate control of all ports or territorial interiors.

- **Bengal and Odisha (§§51.22–51.25):** Distinguish the 1575 Tukaroi treaty from 1576 Rajmahal, later Afghans and Baro-Bhuiyans; treat riverine military power and Man Singh’s 1594 appointment in detail. Validate claims of Isa Khan’s submission separately.

- **Northwest (§§51.26–51.31):** Kashmir’s Yusuf Shah Chak, Yaqub Shah Chak and the contested 1586 submission/detention need careful sequencing; **Akbar’s 1589 Srinagar visit** is supported by the Persian chronicle dating (some references differ). Separate northern Sindh’s earlier absorption from the 1590–92 conquest of Jani Beg’s lower Sindh. Kandahar’s 1595 transfer should be explained as diplomacy/de facto incorporation, not just conquest.

- **Deccan (§§51.32–51.37):** Verify Chand Bibi’s role, the 1596 Berar settlement, 1597 fighting, her death in 1600, and the Ahmadnagar fort’s capture; state clearly that the Nizam Shahi political entity and Deccani resistance survived. Explain the difference between nominal Khandesh allegiance and the 1601 Asirgarh capitulation.

- **Mansabdari, jagirdari (§§51.38–51.44):** Explain chronology and historical debate around ranks (including dual zat/sawar grades), military inspections and revenue assignments. Avoid anachronistically treating the mature system as present in 1556, and reserve detailed zabt/dahsala revenue methodology for Ch52.

- **End of reign (§§51.45–51.47):** Differentiate claimed versus actual sovereignty, Salim’s court, Abu’l-Fazl’s killing in 1602, and accession responsibilities transferred to Ch53.
