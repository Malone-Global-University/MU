## Historical corrections incorporated

1. **Article 340:** clarified presidential authority is permissive, not an absolute duty to appoint a standing commission.
2. **1990 Ayodhya police firing:** source discrepancy is now explicit. A contemporaneous communication reported in a UN source records sixteen fatalities including a security-force member; later sources commonly state seventeen protesters. Neither is presented as universally definitive.
3. **Mumbai January 1993:** the new wave was already under way before the Radhabai (Gandhi) Chawl murders; the Bane-family arson is now correctly dated to the early hours of **8 January 1993**, consistent with the Srikrishna Commission.
4. **1998 Pokhran tests:** removed the erroneous Chapter 97 cross-reference; directs readers to Chapter 105 for the post-1991 security chronology.
5. **Election contested-seat totals:** 1984 main poll **224** seats contested and five more in 1985; 1991 main poll **468** and nine more in 1992, consistent with the detailed party-archived electoral series. Clarified distinction between reporting by election year and full election cycle and retained the corresponding vote-share denominators.
6. **Gujarat 2002:** maintained official 2005 reported death figures (790 Muslim, 254 Hindu) with attribution and without pretending figures are uncontroversial.
