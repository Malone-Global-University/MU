## 3. Historical corrections, documentary verification and expanded interpretation

1. **Historical plan rates precisely sourced:** Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation's historical summary supplies target/achieved rates: First Plan **2.1%/3.6%**, Second **4.5%/4.3%**, Third **5.6%/2.8%**. The growth table names its source. Alternate historical national-accounts series give moderately different retrospective estimates and are not conflated.
   - MoSPI: https://www.mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/Statistical_year_book_india_chapters/Five_Year_Plan.pdf
   - NITI/Planning Commission alternate retrospective series: https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-08/10th_vol1.pdf
2. **Foodgrain production supported by RBI historical series:** **50.82** million tonnes (1950–51), **66.85** (1955–56), **82.02** (1960–61), **72.35** (1965–66). Tables explain agricultural-year versus census-year observations, and narrative discusses the 1964–65 peak of 89.36 and vulnerability to drought.
   - RBI: https://rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=12708
   - Agriculture official historical series: https://desagri.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Agricultural-Statistics-at-a-Glance-2021.pdf
3. **Industrial policy source identification corrected:** Government of India's **final** 30 April 1956 Industrial Policy Resolution is separated from Nehru's **15 April preparatory draft**. Both linked in primary-source window and register.
   - Final parliamentary record: https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/994752/1/01_XII_30-04-1956_p47_p52_PII.pdf
   - Draft and editorial apparatus: https://nehruarchive.in/documents/the-industrial-policy-15-april-1956-yjz8l8
4. **First Five-Year Plan editions distinguished:** NITI Aayog catalogs the **1951 summary** separately from **December 1952 plan-related material**; the primary-source window now distinguishes the publications and links both.
   - 1951 summary: https://digitallibrary.niti.gov.in/items/a9861f42-e35d-483f-b3f0-afffed09b8f6
   - December 1952 volume: https://digitallibrary.niti.gov.in/items/750a2df5-8a9d-4373-876e-4dae00aa9a81
5. **1955 States Reorganisation Commission report:** Replaced former failing MHA PDF link with the currently linked ministry file; separately links 1956 Act.
   - https://www.mha.gov.in/files/commissions_committees_documents/State_20Reorganisation_20Commisison_20Report_20of_201955_270614.pdf
   - https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/1680?view_type=browse
6. **Agrarian and social legislation:** Added detailed but era-bounded analysis of tenancy and records of rights, distribution versus abolition, the Kalelkar Commission's contested criteria, and the original Hindu law reforms versus the 2005 amendment. India Code is identified as a **consolidated/amended** statute source, not a facsimile of the original enactment.
7. **Linguistic reorganization:** Added analysis of the distinction between language identity and administrative boundaries, the 1953 Andhra versus 1956 Andhra Pradesh settlements, SRC consultation limitations, and linguistic minorities inside reorganized states.
8. **Outcome analysis:** Deepened §89.18 with economic measurement methodology, per-capita welfare, foodgrain volatility, employment, industrial capacity, and the consequences of incompatible statistical denominators.
9. **Chronology and tables:** Corrected out-of-order Ambedkar death and IIT development chronology entries; three comparative tables renumbered 1–3. Preserved both separate quantitative studies.
