## 3. Direct historical and bibliographic refinements

1. **Junayd–Uzun Hasan marriage:** The chronology table and §56.9 now follow Kathryn Babayan's published reconstruction that Junayd's marriage to Khadija Begum occurred **no earlier than 1456**, rather than asserting an unqualified mid-1450s date. The relationship itself is preserved.
2. **Husayn Beg Shamlu:** §56.18 distinguishes his personal name from **lala**, the guardianship/tutorship office. The earlier phrasing could mislead readers into treating it as a surname.
3. **Ibn Bazzaz textual transmission:** The source window now identifies Ayasofya MS 2123 (dated 914 AH/1508 CE), a manuscript already showing ideological alteration; the Bombay lithograph of 1329 AH/1911 CE; the Gholam-Reza Tabataba'i Majd 1994 edition; and Heidi Zirke's 1987 critical treatment of a chapter. **The 1911 lithograph is not classified here as a particular revised recension without collation.**
4. **Tihrani's *Kitab-i Diyarbakriyya*:** The edition by Necati Lugal and Faruk Sümer (Ankara, Türk Tarih Kurumu, 1962–1964) is identified as an edited **Persian chronicle, not an English translation**. The marriage study now carefully distinguishes Tihrani's Aq Qoyunlu court perspective from marriage claims discussed by Kathryn Babayan with reference to Khunji. No unlocated marriage passage is represented as directly checked in Tihrani.
5. **Headgear study:** The editorial description now distinguishes the later dynastic narrative from hostile late-fifteenth-century polemical evidence and its differing viewpoint, rather than claiming that all witnesses describe identical physical details or symbolism.
6. **Khwandamir:** The source study specifies **1524** as the completion year of *Habib al-Siyar*, and the succession study identifies **British Library Or. 3248, fols. 28b–29a**, as a published source locator for the traditional transfer of authority to Ismail.
7. **Glossary:** Harmonized *Alid devotion* and *Derbent and Tabasaran* across preview/review and added historically relevant *Zaydi* to the preview (25 preview terms, 29 end glossary entries).
