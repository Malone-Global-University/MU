## Historical/source corrections made directly

1. **Saman Khuda's provenance:** §§35.2 and 35.30 and the glossary no longer assert Balkh provenance as securely proven; regional origin near Balkh versus Samarkand is disputed. Sources: C. E. Bosworth, *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Khorasan vi”; https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khorasan-vi-history-in-the-taherid-and-samanid-periods/.
2. **Abu'l-Fadl Balʿami:** §§35.7 and 35.11 revised from “late 930s” to his vizierate approximately **921–938**. The older/younger Balʿamis remain distinct. Source: Bosworth, *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Balʿami, Abu'l-Fazl”; https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/balami-abul-fazl-mohammad-b/.
3. **Abu Shakur's *Āfarīn-nāma*:** §35.18 qualified the date: surviving verses point to **944**; a later biographer gives **948**; former categorical “947” was too precise. Source: Jalal Matini, *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Āfarīn-nāma”; https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/afarin-nama-a-poem-in-the-motaqareb-meter-by-the-4th-10th-century-poet-abu-sakur-balki-q/.
4. **Sebuktegin's relationship:** §35.27 replaced unsubstantiated “son-in-law of one of [Alptigin's] successors” with the well-attested slave soldier and protégé relationship with Alptigin. Source: Bosworth, *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Sebüktegin”; https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/sebuktegin/.
5. **992 recovery versus later military aid:** §35.28 now distinguishes Nuh II's recovery of Bukhara after Bughra Khan's withdrawal in **992** from Sebuktegin's subsequent intervention against Faiq and Abu ʿAli Simjuri. Source: Bosworth, *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Nuh II”; https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/nuh-ii-b-mansur-i/.
6. **Mahmud's accession:** §35.29 revised: Sebuktegin died in **997**, his son Ismaʿil briefly succeeded him, and Mahmud took power at Ghazna in **998** (not 997). Source: Bosworth, *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Mahmud b. Sebuktegin”; https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/mahmud-b-sebuktegin/.
7. **Narshakhi source window:** Removed unsupported embellishment about impressed visitors and crowded petitioners; distinguished offices actually documented in Narshakhi from military-administrative reconstruction. Source: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Dīvan ii”; https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/divan/divan-ii-government-office/.
8. **Balʿami source window:** Removed unsupported claims about Transoxianan scholars and an exact account of abridgment in the *preface*; distinguished attested patronage in prefaces from the editorial omissions established by modern text comparison. Sources: Elton L. Daniel, “Manuscripts and editions of Balʿami’s Tarjamah-i Tarikh-i Tabari”; https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-royal-asiatic-society/article/abs/manuscripts-and-editions-of-balamis-tarjamahi-tarikhi-tabari1/3382EA9F03BC39FA8BA2487B92885B24 ; *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Historiography iii”; https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/historiography-iii/ ; LOC manuscript description https://www.loc.gov/item/2019714509/.
