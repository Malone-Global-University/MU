## Historical audit and corrections

1. **Grain recovery:** Official historical foodgrain output was 195.05 million tonnes (1957), 160.00 (1962), 187.50 (1964), 194.53 (1965), and 214.00 (1966). Repaired misleading suggestions that the 1957 peak was reached or exceeded in 1964–1965; the official series clearly exceeds the 1957 total in 1966. Distinguished aggregate output from per-capita supplies and household access.
2. **Urban resettlement:** Explained that the frequently cited 20 million is approximate and depends on workforce versus resident scope. The party-history chronology cites **26 million urban residents resettled** by June 1963, alongside **18.87 million employees removed from the workforce**, categories not directly interchangeable.
3. **Soviet withdrawal:** Approximately **1,390** is the contemporary Chinese side's figure for experts withdrawn in 1960, independently rounded to around **1,400**. The original summary's assumption that all took their technical documents was replaced with more careful language about disrupted designs and cooperation.
4. **Ili–Tacheng:** Approximately **60,000** cross-border migrants in spring 1962 is supported by Charles Kraus's archival article. Framed state accusations separately from refugees' local reasons for departure.
5. **First nuclear test:** October 16, 1964, tower test at Lop Nur. Estimated yield varies by source, around **20–25 kilotons**; changed from an unqualified 20 kilotons to the documented estimate range.
6. **Deng's proverb:** July 7, 1962 text cites an older Sichuan proverb about a **yellow or black cat**, attributed to **Liu Bocheng**. It is not Deng's original invention or the later popular **white or black** wording.
7. **Liu's 30/70 comparison:** His January 27, 1962 oral speech reports a proportion used by **peasants interviewed in Hunan**, not a precise national causal estimate that Liu calculated himself. The written conference report and accompanying oral remarks are distinguished.
8. **Anti-Rightist background:** The draft's vague 'thousands' was corrected to **hundreds of thousands**.
9. **Chapter numbering:** Removed references to Chapters 75 and 76, which exceed the supplied 72-chapter China plan; retained nonspecific forward links to later relevant discussions.
10. **Factual scope cautions:** Preserved clear 1965/1966 handoff, PRC/ROC distinction, 1962 Sino-Indian War perspectives, local agency in frontier migration, party-state vs personal leadership, and contemporary vs subsequent meanings of political-campaign terms.
