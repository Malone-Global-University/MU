## III. Historical verification

### Quantitative checks

1. **Grain output:** 1978 **304.77 million tonnes**, 1984 **407.31 million**, 1985 **378.98 million**, 1988 **394.08 million**. Published chapter uses appropriately rounded 305, 407, 379 and 394. National Bureau of Statistics and academic reproductions confirm these figures: https://www.stats.gov.cn/yearbook/1999/l15c.htm and https://academic.oup.com/view-large/426385240 .
2. **Rural per capita nominal net income:** 1978 approximately **134 yuan**, 1984 approximately **355 yuan**, 1985 approximately **398 yuan**, 1988 approximately **545 yuan**. The chapter correctly labels them nominal and distinguishes price adjustment: https://english.moa.gov.cn/overview/201910/t20191009_296600.html .
3. **TVE employment:** approximately **28 million (1978), 52 million (1984), 95 million (1988)**, but the 1984 broadening of the classification complicates simple comparisons. NBS reports about 95.46 million for 1988: https://www.stats.gov.cn/zt_18555/ztfx/xzg50nxlfxbg/202303/t20230301_1920444.html .
4. **TVE gross output:** documented rounded figures (nominal billion yuan) of **49.3 / 170.9 / 649.6** for 1978 / 1984 / 1988. Substituted these for an insufficiently specified row on TVE **share of national industrial output**, a different measure with inconsistent denominators. Historic figures summarized at https://www.china.com.cn/opinion/think/2018-07/03/content_54625658.htm and contemporary 1984 data at https://cn.govopendata.com/renminribao/1985/10/04/5/ .
5. **Retail prices:** increase of approximately **2.8% (1984), 8.8% (1985), 6.0% (1986), 7.3% (1987), 18.5% (1988)**. Rounded narrative and table values are suitable. This is retail-price inflation, not identical to 1988 consumer-price inflation of 18.8%. National Bureau of Statistics original series: https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/ndsj/2001c/i0901c.htm .

### Documentary chronology and interpretation

- Rural No. 1 central documents: corrected the mistaken implication of an unbroken annual rural-document series since 1982. Five consecutive documents addressed rural affairs **1982–1986**; the sequence resumed **2004** after a gap: https://www.jjdl.com.cn/CN/abstract/article/1000-8462/71688 and https://www.chinacoop.gov.cn/HTML/2010/01/25/39580.html .
- Special Economic Zones: the August 26, 1980 Guangdong regulations explicitly concern Shenzhen, Zhuhai and Shantou; separate arrangements covered Xiamen: https://english.court.gov.cn/2016-04/15/c_761530.htm .
- October 20, 1984 CCP Decision on economic-structure reform: original policy formulation checked against the *Beijing Review* archived English text: https://www.bjreview.com/special/2011-04/12/content_350426_5.htm .
- Sino-British Joint Declaration: December 19, 1984 signing and May 27, 1985 entry into force checked against UN treaty registration 23391: https://treaties.un.org/Pages/showDetails.aspx?objid=08000002800d4d6e .
- Compulsory Education Law: April 12, 1986 adoption and July 1, 1986 commencement. A specific reproduction of the *1986 original* is identified, rather than assuming a post-2006 amended law presents the 1986 text: https://www.edu.cn/english/education/laws/21st/200603/t20060323_115357.shtml .
