## Independent factual and documentary verification

1. **April 22 petitioners and first student federation chair:** The names Guo Haifeng, Zhang Zhiyong, and Zhou Yongjun and Zhou's election as early Federation chair are independently supported by the June Fourth Memory/Human Rights Museum's contemporary-source timeline. https://8964museum.com/time/en/t-d09-002/ ; https://8964museum.com/time/en/t-d10-001/ . **Retained.**
2. **June 1–2 cyclist collision:** Original §73.24 wrongly dated the collision as the night of June 2. A U.S. Army Intelligence Center of Excellence account places the PAP vehicle collision killing three cyclists on **June 1** and the subsequent unarmed troop movement on **June 2**. **Corrected in §73.24** with explicit attribution; https://www.dvidshub.net/news/446220/military-attaches-witness-tiananmen-massacre-4-june-1989 .
3. **June 3–4 geographic reconstruction:** The National Security Archive's June 22 U.S. Embassy cable places many and possibly most deaths on Chang'an Avenue and other approaches, rather than strictly inside the Square. The account preserves both the documented shootings of civilians and uncertainty over elements of the Square's final clearing; https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB16/index.html and document 31 in its archive.
4. **June 30 official casualty claims:** Mayor Chen Xitong's report said more than 200 civilians died, including 36 students, more than 3,000 civilians were wounded, and several dozen soldiers and police were killed (with more than 6,000 injured). These are official claims, not authenticated totals. The earlier published list of ten honorees should not be conflated with the wider casualty category. **Clarified §§73.29–30.** https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/1236917.html ; https://www.deseret.com/1989/6/30/18813554/chinese-raise-casualty-figures-to-over-200-dead-br-zhao-stripped-of-last-remaining-post/ .
5. **Independently documented casualties:** The precise total of persons killed is unknown. The manuscript describes Amnesty's 1989 minimum and other figures as attributed assessments, distinguishes geographies and methods, and explicitly states that lethal violence against civilians is independently established. U.S. State Department Office of the Historian https://history.state.gov/milestones/1989-1992/tiananmen-square .
6. **May 20 order / April 26 editorial / May 13 hunger strike / May 19 Zhao appeal:** Traceable documentary locations added to the source notes. https://cn.govopendata.com/renminribao/1989/05/20/1/ ; https://crawfordsworld.com/rob/apcg/China/HeavenlyPeace/April26ed.html ; https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/main_pop/ps/ps_china-tiananmen-hunger-strike.htm ; https://cn.govopendata.com/renminribao/1989/05/19/1/ .
7. **Shanghai June 6 rail incident:** Six persons killed and three subsequent death sentences are supported by contemporary reporting and U.S. State Department documentation; retained with attribution. https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/1236917.html .
8. **The Tiananmen Papers:** Preserves debate over authenticity, including Alfred L. Chan and Andrew J. Nathan's 2004 scholarly critique; the work is not treated as a certified transcript of senior leadership proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305741004000116 .
9. **Zhao's position and 1989 leadership:** The main chronological distinction is preserved between sidelining after May 19 and **formal removal June 23–24**, and between **martial law May 20** and **military action June 3–4**. The U.S. State Department chronology corroborates the overall sequence. https://history.state.gov/milestones/1989-1992/tiananmen-square .

## Remaining disclosure and publication decision

Some specific minute-by-minute events of June 3–4 and the attribution of particular shootings to individual PLA units remain disputed. The manuscript appropriately uses approximate times, avoids overconfident unit assignments, and states evidentiary limitations. No complete primary-source edition and page-level collation has been performed; the seven windows remain labeled paraphrases, now supported by traceable documentary locations. Exact references should be cross-checked with whichever print editions MGU ultimately publishes.
