## 3. Independent historical and documentary checks

**College entrance examination (§71.11).** The 1977 figures of approximately 5.7 million candidates and 273,000 admissions are supported. The initially vague 1978 figures have been specified as approximately **6.1 million candidates and 402,000 admissions**. Education access and selection remain distinct in the narrative. Evidence: Academy of Contemporary China and World Studies, `https://english.scio.gov.cn/featured/chinakeywords/2022-07/13/content_78321938.htm`; *China Culture*, `https://en.chinaculture.org/info/2013-06/21/content_464370.htm`.

**Third Plenum (December 18–22, 1978; §§71.16–71.20).** Confirmed from the contemporaneous communiqué, adopted December 22 and published in *Peking Review*, vol. 21 no. 52 (December 29). The text credits Hua with the Political Bureau proposal to shift Party emphasis, includes early agricultural proposals for discussion and trial, announces personnel changes, and emphasizes legal and administrative rebuilding. The chapter distinguishes 1978 policy redirection from full implementation. Evidence: *Beijing Review* historical archive `https://www.bjreview.com/special/30yearsofreform/2008-11/29/content_167170.htm` and continuation pages.

**Deng's December 13, 1978 speech (§71.17).** The date, title, advocacy of practical judgment, economic decentralization and allowing some regions and workers to improve their living standard before others are verified against an accessible English transcription. Its original speech and the later shorthand 'getting rich first' are not treated as identical. Evidence: `https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/deng-xiaoping/1978/110.htm` and `https://dangshi.people.com.cn/n/2014/0310/c85037-24589817.html` (Central Archives recording excerpts).

**Xiaogang (§71.29).** The Claude draft conflated the reported twenty households with eighteen signatories. The edited chapter now distinguishes the **roughly twenty households**, the **eighteen representatives reportedly present** in some accounts, and conflicting scholarly descriptions, and flags the agreement's date and documentary history. This is a source-critical correction rather than the assertion of one newly certain count. Evidence: Cambridge University Press, *Journal of Institutional Economics*, `https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-institutional-economics/article/resolving-douglass-c-norths-puzzle-concerning-chinas-household-responsibility-system/7627E68395A4A2FC24566233F08F287C`; Beijing Municipal Cultural Heritage Bureau, `https://wwj.beijing.gov.cn/bjww/362760/362767/bwgxh/spzl/326039637/index.html`.

**March 1979 newspaper controversy and September Fourth Plenum (§71.28).** Confirmed an editorialized reader letter signed Zhang Hao appearing in the *People's Daily* on **March 15, 1979** and a corrective response **March 30**. The September 25–28 Fourth Plenum authorized a national agricultural development decision that preserved the production-team framework while debates and experiments continued; the chapter no longer asserts loosely sourced, exact universal exceptions. Evidence: `https://cpc.people.com.cn/GB/68742/70427/70428/5566016.html`; contemporary Fourth Plenum communiqué reproduced `https://japanese.beijingreview.com.cn/90th/2011-04/26/content_357494_4.htm`.

**Hua Guofeng and the 1977 Congress (§§71.6–71.10).** Paraphrase of the 1977 Party congress report was tightened: Hua presented the 1976 arrests as marking the **triumphant conclusion of the first Cultural Revolution**, not merely its 'first stage'. The official declaration that the Cultural Revolution had concluded did not at that point amount to repudiating it. Evidence: contemporaneous report *Washington Post*, August 21, 1977, `https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1977/08/21/chinese-congress-stresses-unity-end-to-years-of-conflict/3d372175-6b8d-4208-94cc-21ac096fc33a/`; CCP published congress materials `https://cpc.people.com.cn/GB/64162/64168/64563/65449/4441883.html`.

**Trial of Gang of Four and associates, 1980–1981 (§71.5).** The ten defendants, division between the Gang of Four and alleged Lin Biao-associated military group, suspended death sentences for Jiang Qing and Zhang Chunqiao, and the others' terms are consistent with contemporary reporting. The trial is presented as both a legal proceeding and a politically bounded form of reckoning, not a neutral judgment on all Mao-era responsibility. Evidence: *Washington Post*, January 25, 1981, `https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1981/01/25/jiang-will-face-execution-threat-for-two-years/892b4f58-233f-4c1b-8907-9a6bb25d80fa/`.

**1981 historical resolution (§§71.21–71.22).** Confirmed adoption on **June 27, 1981**. Its interpretative box remains explicitly marked as retrospective and preserves the distinction between the official CCP account and independent scholarly assessment. Evidence: USC China documentary source, `https://china.usc.edu/node/20941`.
