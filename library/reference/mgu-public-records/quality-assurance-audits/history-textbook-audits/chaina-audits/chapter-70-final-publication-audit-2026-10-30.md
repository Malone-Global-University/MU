## 4. Source-backed and factual repairs

1. **Official trial statistics (§70.11):** Retains the 729,511 persecuted / 34,800 dead indictment figures as reported by Alexander C. Cook's study. Explicitly confines them to cases charged against the defendants, not all deaths nationally. Contemporary reporting also carried variants (e.g., 727,420 / 34,274), demonstrating the need to identify the publication and accounting stage.
2. **Walder–Su rural mortality (§70.11):** Corrected a misleading phrase implying 750,000–1.5 million deaths occurred only within the counties they studied. This was an **extrapolated estimate for rural China based on 1,520 county annals**, with most deaths concentrated in 1968–1971. The 2014 Walder national study is separately mentioned for methodological comparison.
3. **Guangxi (§70.17):** Gives confirmed deaths in a later investigation as **89,810** and notes almost 20,000 additional missing persons. Avoids treating all unconfirmed missing people as established deaths; cites Walder's 2022 research.
4. **Tangshan (§70.43):** Clarifies divergent historical death totals and magnitude estimates; qualifies 242,419 as a figure from a published compilation rather than a definitive or uncontested count. The USGS describes roughly 240,000 official deaths and catalog magnitude around 7.8.
5. **Source edition metadata:** Strengthened ninth-party-congress citation (Lin Biao, Foreign Languages Press, 1969), Zhou Enlai's January 13, 1975 report, and the armed-forces arts forum (February 1966, CCP circulation April 10, 1966, English translation 1967). All source windows continue to label their textual presentations **paraphrases, not direct quotation**.
6. **Movement's chronology:** Distinguishes the chapter's September 1976 narrative endpoint from the CCP 1981 historical resolution's October 1976 terminal date. The post-Mao arrest chronology remains reserved for Chapter 71.

The other principal dates and document types—including the May 16 Circular, August 8 Sixteen Points, September 1971 Lin Biao aircraft crash, October 1971 UN Resolution 2758, February 1972 Shanghai Communiqué, January 1975 government report and 1976 deaths—were checked for consistency with historical chronology. No claim is made that every numerical claim was independently reconstructed from archival datasets.
