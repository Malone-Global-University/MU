## Targeted historical correction register
1. **§61.32 refugee chronology:** Replaced the unsupported claim that most eventual refugees had departed by 15 May 1948. Explicitly distinguishes end-of-May Israeli intelligence estimates from end-of-war totals and adds methodological caution about observation dates, internal movement, and repeat displacement. Contemporary IDF analysis (30 June report, through 1 June) is not a headcount for 15 May; it estimates roughly 391,000 for the period through June 1 and should be read with the report’s error margin.
2. **§61.47 UN partition geography:** Corrected the false assertion that Resolution 181 assigned all of Galilee to the Arab state: the resolution distinguished areas of eastern Galilee allocated to the Jewish state from much of western Galilee allocated to the Arab state. Reframed the discussion of territory with three separate layers: proposed partition, wartime control, and 1949 demarcation.
3. **§61.19 Ad Halom:** Removed the single-cause claim that the 29 May Israeli air mission alone halted the Egyptian advance; identified the destroyed bridge, logistics, Israeli resistance, and the aerial attack as interacting circumstances while preserving the separation from failed June ground counterattacks.
4. **§61.29 Operation Hiram:** Removed the misleading “about a dozen villages” account; distinguished extensive occupations across Galilee from temporary occupation of some villages inside Lebanon and subsequent withdrawals.
5. **Chronology, Operation Nachshon:** Changed a falsely precise 2–20 April campaign span into “early–mid April” and described an attempt to open the Jerusalem road without implying permanently unrestricted passage.
6. **UN source window A/689:** Reidentified the 18 October 1948 refugee relief document as the humanitarian *Supplement to the Progress Report of the United Nations Mediator on Palestine* (A/689); confirmed the 472,000 figure against the published UN text.
7. **Economic Survey Mission:** Identified its final report as **A/AC.25/6, Part I** (28 December 1949) and distinguished it from the **November first interim report** (A/1106) used in December 8 UNRWA deliberations. Updated original window and source register, avoiding retroactive causation.
8. **Resolution 302:** Clarified that its December 8 decision preceded the Economic Survey Mission’s final report; the November interim report informed the agency’s creation.
9. **Source register and reader’s guide:** Identified May 15 Arab States cable as **S/745**, corrected Egyptian armistice record identifiers (S/1264 and corrigendum), retained clear designations of paraphrases and outstanding page-checks, and added publicly accessible official sources.
