## 3. Historical and documentary verification

The revision corrected the principal factual conflation in Claude's draft and materially deepened evidence-based explanation. Cross-checks were made using historical primary/official institutional records, including:

1. **Tunisia, 2014 constitution:** adopted January 26 and promulgated January 27. Checked against Venice Commission constitutional records and an English translation.
2. **Egypt, 2011–2013:** SCAF's March 30, 2011 declaration; June 2012 election; July 3, 2013 military removal of Morsi; August 14 dispersals. Explicitly attributes HRW's documented minimum of 817 deaths at Rabaa and its larger assessment to the investigating organization, without implying that a judicial determination has been entered.
3. **Bahrain, March–November 2011:** GCC personnel arrived beginning March 14; BICI findings concerning force, detention, torture, and accountability. Foreign deployment is not conflated with all subsequent conduct of Bahraini security agencies.
4. **Syria, 2011–2020:** distinguish original peaceful protests from later factions, Russia's 2015 intervention, Security Council Resolution 2254, and the August 2013 Ghouta investigation from separate chemical-weapons responsibility investigations. Resolution 2118 adopted September 27, 2013.
5. **ISIS and Yazidis:** seizure of Mosul in June 2014, genocide against Yazidis beginning in August 2014, UN commission's A/HRC/32/CRP.2 issued June 15, 2016, and ISIS's loss of the final substantial territorial enclave at Baghuz in March 2019. Do not equate territorial defeat with elimination of the organization.
6. **Libya:** **Resolution 1970 (February 26, 2011)** imposed sanctions, an arms embargo, and ICC referral; **Resolution 1973 (March 17, 2011)** separately authorized specified civilian protection and no-fly-zone measures, excluding a foreign occupation force. These are no longer wrongly combined. The countrywide ceasefire was signed on **October 23, 2020** by the 5+5 Joint Military Commission; signature is distinguished from implementation.
7. **Yemen:** GCC Initiative and Implementation Mechanism were signed on November 23, 2011; Saudi-led coalition intervention began March 26, 2015. UN Resolution 2216 and the Stockholm Agreement of December 2018 are carefully distinguished from a fully implemented national settlement.
8. **Sudan, Algeria, Lebanon, Iraq:** the 2018–2020 protest chronology, Sudan's August 17, 2019 Charter, and the August 4, 2020 Beirut port explosion are placed within the cutoff and differentiated by national institutional circumstances.
9. **Displacement:** UNHCR's estimate of approximately 13.5 million Syrians forcibly displaced at the end of 2020, including more than 6.7 million IDPs, is identified as a date-specific reported estimate; refugee, IDP, direct-death and excess-mortality definitions are kept separate.

**Method limits:** This is a targeted source-based historical and documentary audit of major statements, primary windows, chronology and the most consequential allegations. It is not exhaustive archival collation of every claim against original Arabic sources, nor a formal peer-reviewed monograph. All source-study excerpts are editorial paraphrases, not certified facsimile-collated quotations. Human-rights investigative findings and allegations are attributed, and crime labels depend on specifically named authoritative investigative evidence.

## 5. Editorial limitations and publication policy

- **Map studies:** All three are text-only; no historical map plates are embedded. Students must use appropriately dated, licensed historical atlases. The chapter does not invent front-line boundaries or geographic map imagery.
- **Primary sources:** Eight studies provide clearly labeled editorial paraphrases tied to identified documents. Page-level original-language manuscript or facsimile collation was not performed. Students may open the linked source for original text and should cite the actual edition they consult.
- **Quantitative data:** Casualties, disappearances, displacement and famine-related mortality have differing methodologies. The chapter avoids unsupported single totals for debated human losses; where specific figures are used they are dated and attributed.
- **Scope:** The narrative ends December 2020. It deliberately does not extend into political and military developments covered by the next chapter.
