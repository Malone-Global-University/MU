## 3. Historical evidence and spot verification

The build uses named historical actors, laws, dates, organizations, and social structures, avoiding unqualified assertions of one universal nationalist program. The following matters received targeted documentary confirmation:

1. **Egyptian independence and the four 1922 reserved matters.** Official British Hansard record of 28 February 1922 confirms imperial communications, defense, foreign interests/minorities, and Sudan: https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/commons/1922/feb/28/statement-by-the-prime-minister
2. **Egyptian 1923 constitution.** Egyptian Parliament historical reference confirms the bicameral structure, ministerial accountability, royal powers, and subsequent interruptions: https://www.parliament.gov.eg/Documents/History/dostor_1923.aspx
3. **1936 treaty.** The contemporary British parliamentary discussion supports the distinction between expanded Egyptian diplomatic status and retention of British canal defenses: https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/1936-11-24/debates/f7ea7658-7a88-4e41-ad5a-f377f4d343e6/Anglo-EgyptianTreaty
4. **Sudanese national petition of May 1924.** Elena Vezzadini's *Lost Nationalism* reproduces an archived translation, identifies the signatories, and explains its limits: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/lost-nationalism/telegrams-of-the-white-flag-league-and-other-protesters/69A2D7C2B013310765409D76C8B4872E
5. **Italian mass internment in Cyrenaica.** A Cambridge historical study reports 100,000–120,000 confined and estimates up to 40,000–48,000 deaths within the camps, with limitations; the chapter specifically does not confuse these with wider wartime demographic losses: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/international-review-of-social-history/article/other-at-home-deportation-and-transportation-of-libyans-to-italy-during-the-colonial-era-19111943/FAFA1592C6350550622B210623CF57CE
6. **Badoglio's 1930 directive.** An Italian regional legislative educational resource reproduces and attributes the directive and its purpose: https://www.assemblea.emr.it/cittadinanza/per-approfondire/formazione-pdc/viaggio-visivo/lideologia-nazista-e-il-razzismo-fascista/il-razzismo-fascista/limperialismo-fascista/approfondimenti/lordine-del-generale-badoglio
7. **Algerian 1919 law.** French government legislation record verifies the existence and date of the law: https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000000869156
8. **Moroccan Berber Dahir.** Historical-legal scholarship confirms the May 16, 1930 decree and notes that it was not entirely repealed in 1934: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/comparative-studies-in-society-and-history/article/berber-law-by-french-means-customary-courts-in-the-moroccan-hinterlands-19301956/B10C1347D17D997E74AB657853E0E39C
9. **1934 Moroccan Action Committee plan.** A cataloged scan identifies its title, date, and author, but the full original page text was not read line by line: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plan_des_R%C3%A9formes_Marocaine.pdf

**Archival limitations:** This report is a *targeted evidence review*, not a claim to have verified every sentence or consulted all original archival materials. Several source windows are explicitly labeled as paraphrases based on published scholarly descriptions or catalog records, including the 1938 Sudanese Congress materials, early Tunisian petitions, 1934 Neo-Destour congress documents, and 1934 Moroccan reform program. They require original page-level inspection before any verbatim documentary excerpts are published. No quotation is fabricated to close these gaps. The academic SOAS PDF on the 1938 Sudanese Congress was text-inspected from the indexed excerpt; browser screenshot retrieval failed, and the relevant original newspaper pages were not examined.
