## Verified historical points

1. **Transjordan National Congress:** July 25, 1928, supported by the contemporary political chronology in the Arab parliamentary history and a UN historical submission: https://library.fes.de/pdf-files/bueros/vifa-nahost/a99_00977.pdf and https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/180339/files/CAT_C_16_Add.5-EN.pdf.
2. **The 1928 Anglo-Transjordan Agreement:** signed **February 20, 1928** at Jerusalem. Published as Treaty Series No. 7 (1930), Cmd. 3488; ratifications exchanged October 31, 1929. The Basic Law was issued April 16, 1928. https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Agreement_between_His_Majesty_and_the_Amir_of_Trans-Jordan and https://library.fes.de/pdf-files/bueros/vifa-nahost/a99_00977.pdf.
3. **Philby and Rashid Tali'a:** Philby served as British representative in Transjordan from 1921 to 1924, and Rashid Tali'a led the first government in April 1921. https://www.qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100074457097.0x0000c2 and https://www.rhdc.jo/primeministers/the-government-the-government-of-of-his-excellency-mr-rashid-talia-1921/.
4. **1924 Ikhwan attack:** documented against Transjordan in August 1924; a 1924 official mandate report identifies the engagement at **Umm el-Amad south of Amman**. The supplied draft's 'Tuwal near Amman' was replaced. https://ismi.emory.edu/resources/primary-source-docs/1924report.pdf and https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/041-000546581.
5. **Sanaa treaty:** signed **February 11, 1934**, ratified **September 4, 1934**; the treaty maintained the existing Aden–Yemen frontier situation pending further negotiation. https://hansard.parliament.uk/commons/1934-02-26/debates/382b4797-19ba-40d4-8a22-5e804cfc7750/Yemen%28Treaty%29 and https://qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100000000555.0x00029a.
6. **Treaty of Taif:** UN treaty record dates conclusion to **May 20, 1934 at Jeddah** and entry into force to June 22, 1934; renewal after 20 years unless revised is supported by scholarly policy analysis. https://treaties.un.org/Pages/showDetails.aspx?objid=08000002800667a7 and https://sanaacenter.org/publications/policy-research/17828.
7. **Aden Protectorate Levies:** established **1928** as recorded in contemporary government reporting. https://www.qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100029481177.0x000016.
8. **Yemeni training and Iraqi military missions:** selected Yemeni cadets began receiving training in Baghdad in 1935; large Iraqi military missions within Yemen are documented primarily in the 1940s. The draft's implication of an early Iraqi role in building the interwar Yemeni army was qualified. https://alternatifpolitika.com/eng/makale/a-political-history-of-civil-military-relations-in-yemen and https://publishing.cdlib.org/ucpressebooks/public/book/the-calligraphic-state-textual-domination-and-history-in-a-muslim-society.html.
9. **Soviet recognition:** Soviet recognition of Ibn Saud as king of the Hijaz occurred February 1926. British archival record reproduces a letter dated February 16, 1926. https://qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100079351204.0x000081.
10. **United States recognition:** May 1, 1931, of the **Kingdom of the Hijaz and Nejd and its Dependencies**, not yet the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (proclaimed 1932). https://history.state.gov/countries/saudi-arabia and https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1931v02/d532.
11. **Simele:** the August 1933 massacre of Assyrian civilians is established; the main killings at the town of Simele are specifically associated with **August 11**, whereas broader attacks extended across the district. https://assyrian-history.com/articles/simele-1933 and modern scholarship, Arbella Bet-Shlimon, *Review of Middle East Studies* (April 2026), https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/review-of-middle-east-studies/article/propaganda-of-the-victims-atrocity-denial-ethnic-nationalism-and-the-disparagement-of-assyrians-in-middle-east-studies/47263C562C196EEEC129BCF3055E6ED0.
12. **1920 British report:** Gertrude Bell prepared *Review of the Civil Administration of Mesopotamia*, Cmd. 1061 (London: HMSO, December 1920), 149 pp., for the acting Civil Commissioner. https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000545916.
