## 2. Verified publication-critical historical findings

**A. Section 61.1 — Incorrect ownership of December 1947 Jerusalem shops (FACTUAL ERROR).** Draft says Arab shops near Jaffa Gate were looted and burned during the opening unrest. This reverses the victim group in the documented December 2 attack on the predominantly **Jewish-owned commercial centre of Mamilla**, attacked by Arab demonstrators. The neighborhood was mixed, and retaliatory attacks on Arab property should be discussed separately. Correct the passage and source it to British parliamentary record, contemporary Jewish Agency complaint, and scholarly reconstruction.
- British Commons, Dec. 3, 1947: https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/commons/1947/dec/03/palestine-disturbances
- Benny Morris, *Birth of the Palestinian Refugee Problem Revisited*, chapter on first wave: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/birth-of-the-palestinian-refugee-problem-revisited/first-wave-the-arab-exodus-december-1947-march-1948/9738BA54B80E72789DF95E6618988F8C

**B. Section 61.8 — Bayt Mahsir attributed to the wrong operation (FACTUAL ERROR).** The village was attacked or targeted during Nachshon in April but was *not* captured then. The capture and depopulation occurred in **Operation Maccabi** in May, with assaults around May 8–12 and sources differing about exact possession dates. The passage's statement that it was depopulated during Nachshon is incorrect. Differentiate operations.
- Palmach operation narrative: https://palmach.org.il/en/history/database/?itemId=5843
- Palmach entry: https://www.palmach.org.il/en/history/database/?itemId=5623

**C. Section 61.19 — Ad Halom chronology and engagement outcome (FACTUAL ERROR/CONFLATION).** Egyptian advance reached its northern limit near Isdud/Ad Halom around **May 29, 1948**, when Israeli air attacks took place. Israeli ground attacks around June 2–3 did **not simply defeat the Egyptians**; Operation Pleshet involved failed Israeli ground assaults, even though Egyptian forces did not advance farther north. Replace 'stopped by the Givati Brigade on June 2–3' with a differentiated sequence of halt, air attack, Israeli counterattack and subsequent stalemate.
- Event reconstruction: https://www.jpost.com/arab-israeli-conflict/article-744635

**D. Section 61.42 — Lausanne Protocol/UN admission causal chronology (FACTUAL ERROR).** It states Israel signed the **May 12, 1949** protocol 'in part to obtain admission to the United Nations a day earlier, on May 11.' A signature cannot retroactively obtain admission. Pre-signing diplomatic discussions were linked to international membership negotiations, but the 12 May protocol's purpose and reservations must be stated from UNCCP records rather than as an impossible cause. Israel and Arab delegations signed separately and reserved their positions; the 1947 partition map was a basis for discussion, not agreed final borders.
- UNCCP 12 May record: https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-211969/
- US FRUS report: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1949v06/d649

**E. Section 61.9 — Hadassah convoy toll (PRECISION).** Draft says 'some seventy-nine' killed. The institution's official commemorative count is **78 killed on April 13, 1948**. Unless citing another count specifically, use 78.
- Hadassah history: https://www.hadassah.org/story/76-years-after-hadassah-convoy-massacre-honoring-the-victims

**F. Section 61.27 — Dahmash mosque (HISTORIOGRAPHICAL PRECISION).** The draft presents men killed in or near the mosque as a relatively settled event and says 'Israeli estimates run to several hundred' without identifying what that estimate counts. Mass killing in the wider Lydda fighting and the subsequent forced expulsion have substantial evidence. The precise claim concerning killings *inside the Dahmash mosque*, the number of noncombatants, and the interpretation of the July 12 engagement are disputed in a named peer-reviewed study. Identify, compare, and attribute the competing interpretations; distinguish military casualty totals for the town from deaths at the mosque. Do not present a contested site-specific scenario as universally established.
- Kadish and Sela, *Middle East Journal* (2005) abstract: https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/myths-and-historiography-of-the-1948-palestine-war-revisited-the-/
- Gershom Gorenberg discussion of Benny Morris's reading: https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2009/05/28/the-war-to-begin-all-wars/

**G. Section 61.47 — Green Line described as a 'border' (LEGAL PRECISION).** Near the end, it calls the Green Line 'the border of the Israeli state' even though the chapter elsewhere correctly says it was an armistice demarcation *without prejudice* to ultimate borders. Change this sentence to 'the armistice demarcation between territory held by Israel and the areas administered by Jordan and Egypt.' Be consistent throughout.
- UN truce supervision/armistice overview: https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-176894/
