## 3. Material historical corrections applied directly

1. **Chemical-weapons document metadata (§69.7):** corrected UN document `S/18852` from an erroneous “June 1987” reference to **8 May 1987**. This is the original report date; the corrigendum is a different document dated 26 May 1987. The in-chapter archival links now include `S/16433` (1984), `S/17127` (1985), `S/17911` (1986), and `S/18852` (1987). Sources: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/63325 , https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/82331 , https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/114580 , https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/134521 .
2. **Shatt al-Arab / Umm Qasr (§69.2):** corrected the geographical claim that Umm Qasr is a port on the Shatt al-Arab. Its port approach is through **Khawr Abd Allah and Khor al-Zubayr**, distinct from the Shatt al-Arab leading to Basra. The chapter no longer presents the Shatt as Iraq's near-exclusive maritime outlet.
3. **Halabja versus formal Anfal operations (§69.7):** explicitly distinguishes the **16 March 1988 Halabja massacre**—part of the broader Iraqi anti-Kurdish genocidal violence—from **the eight officially named Anfal operations**. The draft already attributed its casualty range instead of pretending that one figure was uncontested. Research: https://www.hrw.org/report/1994/05/01/iraqs-crime-genocide-anfal-campaign-against-kurds .
4. **1971 Abu Musa / Tunbs diplomatic record (§69.2):** differentiates the Sharjah–Iran arrangement governing the Iranian landing on Abu Musa from the Iranian occupation of Greater and Lesser Tunb **without agreement from Ras al-Khaimah**. Contemporary documentary background: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v24/d110 .
5. **Iranian Revolution wording (§69.2):** distinguishes the Shah's **departure in January 1979** from the monarchy's **collapse in February**, with the Islamic Republic proclaimed in April.
6. **Arab League emergency summit (§69.12):** clarifies that Iraq, Libya and the **PLO delegation** were reported among the opponents of the 10 August 1990 Cairo summit motion, while abstentions and qualified reservations are not equivalent; it does not falsely insist on a disputed exact full voting roster.

**Core claims retained after verification:** UN Resolution 598 of **20 July 1987** and operational ceasefire on **20 August 1988** are separately dated; invasion of Kuwait **2 August 1990**; Security Council resolutions 660, 661, 678, 688 and 687 have their correct dates and general mandates; Resolution 986 was adopted **14 April 1995**, with implementation beginning in 1996. The draft's account of Anfal genocide is attributed to Human Rights Watch, and the discussion of the 2017 sanctions-mortality reappraisal distinguishes disputed statistics from established civilian suffering.
