Chapter-level assessment records for the Malone Global University Arab World History textbook series.

Collection Overview

This index lists the Arab World History chapter audits identified in the institutional directory inventory, grouped by chapter. It preserves links to both final reviews and earlier evaluation or revision records where multiple files exist.

Inventory scope: 18 chapters (55–72); 25 audit and verification records. These are links to the recorded .md files, not PDF editions. A filename describing an audit as “final” does not, by itself, establish independent scholarly peer review.
