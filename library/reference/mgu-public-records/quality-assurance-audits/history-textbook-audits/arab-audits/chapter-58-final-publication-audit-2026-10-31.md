## 2. Corrections integrated into the publication-final HTML

1. **§58.6: Battle of al-Mazraʿa.** Changed the incorrect “early September” date to **2–3 August 1925**. Early September would materially distort the revolt's opening chronology. Reference: [Sultan al-Atrash biography](https://www.islam.museum/en/person/sultan-al-atrash/) (21 July al-Kafr; 2 August al-Mazraʿa); see also [battle chronology](https://comesyria.com/en/places/battle-of-al-mazraa-memorial).
2. **§58.33: 1943 Syrian National Bloc.** Removed the anachronistic statement that the Bloc was “reorganized as the National Party” during the 1943 elections. The Damascus-based **National Party emerged in 1947**. Reference: Mahmoud Haddad, [“National Party (Syria)”](https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/asia-and-africa/middle-eastern-history/national-party), identifying 1947 and the earlier National Bloc.
3. **§58.25: 1936 Alawite petition.** Qualified the petition as representing a separatist faction rather than the entire community, and distinguished archival documentation from distorted, widely circulated copies and pro-unity petitions. Reference: Yaron Friedman, “The separatist Alawi petition to the French Prime Minister Léon Blum (1936): reliability, background and aftermath,” *Middle Eastern Studies* 60 (2024): 444–463, [DOI 10.1080/00263206.2023.2204517](https://doi.org/10.1080/00263206.2023.2204517).
4. **§58.35: Syria's 1946 military evacuation.** Corrected the claim that British troops departed Syria “later that year” after the French withdrawal. **French and British forces completed evacuation in April 1946**. Reference: [FRUS 1946, vol. VII](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1946v07/d609), recording that French and British troops had departed Syria by April 15.
5. **§58.35: Lebanon's 1946 military evacuation.** Distinguished the departure of principal French forces by **August 1946** from residual officers and technical/liquidation personnel remaining until **December 1946**. Reference: [FRUS 1946, vol. VII](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1946v07/d609) and the [contemporaneous UN evacuation timetable](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/659300).

These edits are strictly localized. The full section architecture and instructional apparatus were not altered.

## 3. Additional documentary cross-checks

* **Lebanese census, 1932 (§58.19).** Retain 396,946 resident Christian citizens and 396,450 resident non-Christian citizens, a difference of 496. When registered emigrants were included, the Christian share was 58.5%. Data match Rania Maktabi, [“The Lebanese Census of 1932 Revisited”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/249004123_The_Lebanese_Census_of_1932_Revisited_Who_Are_the_Lebanese). No correction needed.
* **Syrian Constitution of 1930 (§58.13).** Decree no. 3111 dated May 14, published at Damascus May 22, with Article 116 reserving mandate obligations: consistent with the League-published constitutional record, [C.352.1930.VI](https://deriv.nls.uk/dcn23/1939/7130/193971308.23.pdf). No correction needed.
* **Franco-Syrian treaty of 1936 (§58.26).** Pierre Viénot's participation is confirmed by contemporary French analysis in [*Politique étrangère*, “Le traité franco-syrien” (1936)](https://www.persee.fr/doc/polit_0032-342x_1936_num_1_5_5657). No correction needed.
* **1925 diplomatic window.** The attributed October 19, 1925 Knabenshue telegram exists and reports the bombardment, the telephone reports from Damascus, and rumors distinguished from verified findings: [FRUS 1925, vol. II, document 77](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1925v02/d77). No correction needed.
* **1945 diplomatic window and reinforcements.** May 29 Wadsworth telegram and May 31 Henderson memorandum exist and match the paraphrases: [FRUS 1945, vol. VIII, document 1082](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1945v08/d1082) and [document 1088](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1945v08/d1088). The May 6 and May 18 reinforcement dates are explicitly in Henderson's memorandum. No correction needed.
* **Lebanese language provision, 1943.** Constitution Article 11 as amended November 9, 1943 confirms Arabic as official national language and allows French in cases determined by law: [Lebanese Presidency constitutional text](https://presidency.gov.lb/lebanon-system/3). No correction needed.
* **February 1946 Security Council.** The resolution on foreign troop withdrawals was vetoed by the Soviet Union and not adopted; this is verified in the [UN's vetoed-draft record](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-185503/). No correction needed.
* **Armenian settlement.** The movement of Musa Dagh Armenians to Anjar in 1939–41 is supported by Vahram Shemmassian, [Haigazian University study](https://www.haigazian.edu.lb/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/ArmeniansinLebanon.pdf). The Catholicosate of Cilicia's Antelias establishment in 1929 is supported by Armenian ecclesiastical records. The chapter uses these details without introducing impossible dates.
* **Alexandria meetings, 1944.** Syrian participation is supported by the October 7, 1944 [Alexandria Protocol](https://jvl.levit.dev/the-alexandria-protocol-october-1944).
* **Ibrahim Hananu trial.** His 1922 release following a French court proceeding is attested by [biographical records](https://www.islam.museum/en/person/ibrahim-hanano/); the account does not require a corrective rewrite.

The review uses targeted independent documentary verification and manuscript inspection. It does not represent a line-by-line archival authentication of every factual assertion or a comparison of all interpretive scholarship.
