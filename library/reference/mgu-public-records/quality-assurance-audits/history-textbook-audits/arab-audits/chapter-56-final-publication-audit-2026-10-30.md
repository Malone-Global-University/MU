## 2. Historical and documentary audit — key verified claims

1. **League Covenant Article 22**: provisional recognition for former Ottoman communities, the mandate system, annual reporting and supervision. Yale Avalon primary source: https://avalon.law.yale.edu/20th_Century/leagcov.asp
2. **Faisal memorandum and Palestine**: memorandum dated 1 January 1919 distinguished Palestine as a sensitive case potentially requiring a special trustee; it should not be summarized as Faisal unconditionally giving Palestine to Zionist institutions. UN historical study reproducing the relevant passage: https://www.un.org/unispal/es/history/origins-and-evolution-of-the-palestine-problem/part-i-1917-1947/
3. **Conditional Faisal–Weizmann agreement**, January 3, 1919: the appended independence qualification is genuine, distinct from the memorandum chronology: https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-207006/
4. **San Remo resolutions**, 25 April 1920: assign Syria and Lebanon to France and Mesopotamia and Palestine to Britain, while treating Syria and Mesopotamia through the provisional-independence language of Article 22: https://www.palquest.org/en/node/37930
5. **1920 Franco-British boundary convention**: December 23, 1920 paper boundary and Article 2's commission are distinct from Paulet–Newcombe demarcation and the 1923 agreement: https://palquest.org/en/node/39761 ; https://www.palquest.org/en/overallchronology-grid?nid=37742&sideid=6524
6. **Palestine Mandate**: July 24, 1922 League approval and September 29, 1923 operation, Articles 2, 4, 6, and 25: https://avalon.law.yale.edu/20th_century/palmanda.asp ; https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1924v02/d174
7. **Iraq's separate treaty-based mandate**: 1922 Anglo-Iraqi treaty and 1924 League Council's substitution arrangements, not the same final mandate document as Syria/Palestine: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1925v02/d189 ; https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-law-journal/article/abs/mandates1/1478D7F38AA28212105EC18A3E156B45
8. **Ibrahim Hananu**: French trial and acquittal in March 1922: https://www.islam.museum/en/person/ibrahim-hanano/ ; https://history.ucsd.edu/doc/review/current/mp/A-1-2.pdf
9. **Gouraud ambush**: June 23, 1921 near Quneitra; Gouraud himself unharmed, one officer killed, per French diplomatic archive: https://archivesdiplomatiques.diplomatie.gouv.fr/ark%3A/14366/c9fdq7bptv82
10. **1920 Iraqi Revolt**: Rumaitha at end of June, influential al-Shirazi rulings, local and nationalist participants, and high suppression costs; dates and estimates qualified: https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.7560/739246 ; https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/iraq-x-shiites-of-iraq/
11. **Palestinian Arab congresses**: Arab Executive at Haifa in December 1920; Palestinian Legislative Council electoral boycott in February–March 1923: https://www.palquest.org/en/overallchronology ; https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-193812/
12. **Hijaz and Egypt**: King's British subsidy ended February 1920 according to contemporary parliamentary evidence; Britain's 28 February 1922 Egypt declaration reserved specified strategic matters: https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/lords/1922/feb/28/the-king-of-the-hejaz ; https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/lords/1922/feb/28/statement-on-egypt
