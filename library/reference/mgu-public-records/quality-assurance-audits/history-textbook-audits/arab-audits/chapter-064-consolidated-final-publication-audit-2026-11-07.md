## 2. Consolidated historical revisions applied

1. **§64.1 — Regional coup chronology:** Removed the false assertion that Husni al-Za'im's March 1949 takeover was the first military coup in the modern Arab world; identified Iraq's **29 October 1936 Bakr Sidqi coup** as an earlier successful precedent. Support: Adeed Dawisha, *Iraq: A Political History*, ch. 5 (https://doi.org/10.23943/princeton/9780691157931.003.0005).
2. **§64.2 and Syrian constitutional source window:** Explicitly separated the **1950 Syrian constitution Article 3(2), “the main source”** of legislation from the **1973 constitution Article 3(2), “a main source”**, while identifying 1973 Article 8's Ba'athist party supremacy. Source identities: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Syria_(1950) and https://www.refworld.org/legal/legislation/natlegbod/1973/en/38517. The window remains an editor's paraphrase, not a verbatim transcription.
3. **§64.10 — Saddam Hussein's 1979 purge:** Replaced “twenty-two were executed” with contemporary reporting of **21 officials executed on 8 August 1979**, including five Revolutionary Command Council members, while acknowledging ambiguity about the wider arrests and purges. Support: U.S. State Department, *Foreign Relations of the United States*, https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v18/d139, note 3.
4. **Chronology — South Yemen, 1967–1970:** Correctly separated the **30 November 1967** independent People's Republic of Southern Yemen, **June 1969** left-wing leadership takeover, and **1970** reorganization as the People's Democratic Republic of Yemen. Support: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v21/d117 ; https://sanaacenter.org/publications/policy-research/14140 ; https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-199486/ .
5. **§64.12 — Chemical warfare in Yemen:** Clarified that the historical record establishes Egyptian **use of chemical weapons**, especially in 1966–1967; preserved uncertainty about the precise chemical agents, individual incidents, and casualty totals. Specialist reference: Asher Orkaby, *Beyond the Arab Cold War*, ch. 6, https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780190618445.003.0007.
6. **Libyan 1977 source window:** Removed unsupported attribution of an explicit RCC-power-transfer mechanism to the declaration. Now accurately describes the proclaimed popular congresses/committees and the Quran's status in the text. Source: https://security-legislation.ly/latest-laws/resolution-of-1977-on-the-establishment-of-the-authority-of-the-people-and-its-enabling-decisions/.
7. **South Yemeni source window:** Replaced an unidentifiable composite of “revolutionary documents, 1967–1970” with a named and dated **Constitution of the People's Democratic Republic of Yemen**, proclaimed **30 November 1970**, published by **Republic Press (1970, 60 pp.)**, with explicit focus on economic Articles 12, 14 and 21. Bibliography: https://books.google.com/books/about/Constitution_of_People_s_Democratic_Repu.html?id=gzREAQAAIAAJ. Article summaries cross-referenced by JICA at https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/10834703_01.pdf; original complete pages remain uncollated.
8. **1962 Yemen broadcast metadata:** Clarified that surviving versions include later textual reproductions and are not equivalent to authenticating an original audio recording; cross-reference: https://althawrah.ye/archives/96869 and U.S. diplomatic records.

There was **no wholesale narrative expansion**, no new historical section, and no restructuring of the chapter's country sequence. The main historical claims and chapter boundaries assessed in the initial audit were retained.
