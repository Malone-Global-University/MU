## 3. Historical and source verification

**Critical correction 1 — §§57.6, 57.9: Shi'i clerical expulsions.** The text says the clerics opposing elections were expelled "that year" following the 1924 Constituent Assembly vote. The prominent documented case is the **June 25, 1923** deportation of Mahdi al-Khalisi, accompanied by close relatives; other clerics departed in protest. Clarify the different circumstances and avoid collectively assigning expulsions to 1924. Contemporary UK parliamentary record: https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/1923-07-30/debates/66a8d27b-91e8-4530-90ad-ff656dccd1f6/IRAQ%28DEPORTATION%29 ; scholarly account: https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khalesi-mahdi/

**Critical correction 2 — §§57.6, 57.10, 57.12: Iraq treaty revisions.** The original **October 10, 1922** Anglo-Iraqi Treaty contemplated a 20-year period; the **April 30, 1923 protocol** sharply limited its term; the **January 13, 1926 agreement**, arising from the Mosul settlement, provided for the longer mandate framework unless League admission occurred sooner. The **June 30, 1930** alliance became effective upon termination of the mandate/League admission in 1932. Do not make the revision story sound like a single continuing shortened treaty. Contemporaneous 1923 protocol: https://www.qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100111367151.0x00009d ; US State Department historical summary: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1919Parisv13/ch10subch1 ; accessible scholarly legal discussion: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-law-journal/article/abs/mandates1/1478D7F38AA28212105EC18A3E156B45

**Critical correction 3 — §57.34, Source Window `source-seeb`: Treaty of Seeb terms.** It is accurate that the **September 25, 1920** accord produced a working division of authority and noninterference, but avoid portraying the treaty as explicitly defining the Sultan's sovereignty over the coast or granting international recognition to an independent Omani state. The substantive clauses provided for **no more than 5% customs duty** on products entering coastal towns from interior Oman, freedom of movement and security, noninterference in interior affairs, reciprocal peace, protected trade, and treatment of fugitives. Define the competing readings of political sovereignty versus autonomy. A historical scholarly account: https://dokumen.pub/a-history-of-modern-oman-hardcovernbsped-1107009405-9781107009400.html ; British Library catalogue with contemporary texts: https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000242760 ; UN archive records the agreement text (consult original before quoting): https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/768875/files/A_5846-EN.pdf

**Critical correction 4 — §57.22: British/Rashidi relationship.** "Loss of Ottoman and then British support after 1918" is overcompressed and implies sustained British sponsorship. Ottoman-Rashidi partnership was central; the British considered Hail as a possible counterweight but ultimately supported Saudi campaigns. Rewrite with documented sequencing, avoiding an unqualified claim of lost British support. Peer-reviewed study: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-perspectives-on-turkey/article/tribal-partners-of-empire-in-arabia-the-ottomans-and-the-rashidis-of-najd-18801918/AF3C3A2725F297AF9371DF46799D9725

**Critical correction 5 — §§57.1 and 57.10: Historical precision.** "Iraq did not exist as a unit before 1918" is misleading if read as denying a historical geographical identity. Say that the *modern territorial state* did not yet exist with its later borders, despite long-standing geographical usages of Iraq and Ottoman provincial traditions. In §57.10, don't make Shaykh Mahmud Barzinji's defeat in 1924 sound like the final end of Kurdish resistance; contextualize his later campaigns.

**Important nuance — §57.13 Simele.** An honest lower-bound estimate should say *at least approximately 600 Assyrians killed* in the wider 1933 atrocities, while explaining that higher totals depend on geographic and temporal scope. The mass killing of civilians by Iraqi forces is firmly established; disagreement over totals and central intent is not disagreement that massacres occurred. Peer-reviewed historical study: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11792212/
