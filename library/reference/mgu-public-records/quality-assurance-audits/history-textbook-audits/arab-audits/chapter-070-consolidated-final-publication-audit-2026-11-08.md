## 4. Independent historical and documentary review

### Historical corrections applied

1. **Oslo II stage-specific territorial percentages (§70.4):** Removed the misleading projection of approximately 18/22/60 into 1995. The text now distinguishes the UN 1995 estimate **A 3%, B 24%, C 73%** from the later 2000 stage **A 18.2%, B 21.8%, C 60%**. These are estimated maps for different historical stages, not permanent treaty percentages. Source: [UN, *Origins and Evolution of the Palestine Problem*, Part V, Annex V](https://www.un.org/unispal/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/OE-Part-V.pdf) and [UN institutional landing page](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-209471/).
2. **Arab Peace Initiative context (§70.10):** Replaced the ambiguous claim of adoption after a major Israeli operation with explicit dates: March 27, 2002 Netanya hotel attack; March 28 Arab Peace Initiative; March 29 beginning of Operation Defensive Shield. Source: [UN Arab Peace Initiative](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-207494/).
3. **Human Development Report question (§70.15):** Corrected an anachronistic question asking a 2002 report to establish late-2000s conditions. It now interrogates early-2000s debates and the need for later comparative evidence.
4. **Mutual recognition documents:** Added a companion documentary link for the reciprocal Israel–PLO communications signed/dated September 9–10, 1993, beside the 1993 Declaration of Principles. Source: [UN institutional compilation](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-195501/).

### Additional foundational facts checked

- Madrid conference opened October 30, 1991; U.S.–Soviet invitation dated October 18, distributed/published on differing dates. [UN Madrid documents](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-206738/).
- Declaration of Principles signed September 13, 1993; Oslo II signed September 28, 1995. [UN Declaration](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-180015/) and [UN Oslo II](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-185434/).
- Jordan–Israel peace treaty signed October 26, 1994; Article 9 identifies Jordan’s special role in Muslim holy shrines in Jerusalem. [Treaty](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-179122/).
- Quartet Road Map released April 30, 2003; later circulated as UN S/2003/529. [UN Road Map](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-186742/).
- Palestinian Central Elections Commission 2006 parliamentary final results: Change and Reform 74 of 132 seats, Fatah 45; *list vote share* and *total seats* must be distinguished. [CEC](https://www.elections.ps/TabId/1083/ArtMID/9183/ArticleID/889/Default.aspx).
- July 9, 2004 ICJ advisory opinion addresses the wall and associated regime in occupied Palestinian territory. [UN/ICJ synopsis](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-213932/).
- UNDP reports: 2002 *Creating Opportunities for Future Generations*, and 2009 *Challenges to Human Security in the Arab Countries*. [2002](https://hdr.undp.org/content/arab-human-development-report), [2009](https://hdr.undp.org/content/challenges-human-security-arab-countries).
