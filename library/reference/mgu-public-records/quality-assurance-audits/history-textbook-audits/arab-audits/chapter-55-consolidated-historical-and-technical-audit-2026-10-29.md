## Source-checked points

- **Kut surrender:** roughly 13,000 prisoners on 29 April 1916 is supported by Britain's National Army Museum: https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/mesopotamia-campaign
- **Aley tribunal and 1915 hangings:** Hasan Kayalı cites eleven leaders executed in Beirut on 21 August 1915; differences among other accounts exist: https://publishing.cdlib.org/ucpressebooks/public/book/arabs-and-young-turks-ottomanism-arabism-and-islamism-in-the-ottoman-empire-1908-1918.html
- **Wejh:** captured 23–24 January 1917 in an Arab/British naval operation: https://ijnh.seahistory.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2012/01/Article-Gilbert.pdf
- **Tafila:** Arab victory January 1918: https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/lawrence-arabia-man-behind-robes
- **Faisal in Damascus in 1915:** contemporary reporting identifies the visit, but the later Damascus Protocol's detailed provenance remains difficult and the chapter appropriately qualifies it: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1918Supp01v01/d177
- **Allenby–Faisal:** 3 October 1918 Damascus meeting, documented in contemporary accounts and a detailed record later written by Chauvel: https://www.pbs.org/lawrenceofarabia/players/feisal.html
- **Lahej 1915:** contemporary British India Office biographical material mentions Said Pasha and Ottoman activity around Lahej. British Library/Qatar Digital Library: https://www.qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100025595977.0x000019
- **Mount Lebanon famine:** interacting blockade, Ottoman requisition, military provisioning, transport collapse, 1915 locust disaster, and food entitlements; uncertain mortality estimates require continued caution: https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4bs8383d and https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00263206.2014.976624
