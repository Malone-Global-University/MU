## Historical correction register

1. **Shaykh Khalifa:** The Claude draft wrongly said he was executed in 1342. Corrected to **736 AH / 1335**, documented by the religious-history article in *Encyclopaedia Iranica*; distinguished from his disciple Hasan Juri's death in **July 1342 at Zava**.
2. **Hasan Juri at Zava:** The killing by a Sarbadar soldier is recorded; suggestions that Wajih al-Din Masʿud arranged it are **attributed and contested**, not recast as a demonstrated order. The battle is dated **18 July 1342** by Denise Aigle.
3. **Wajih al-Din Masʿud:** Removed the false description of death during warfare with Togha Temur in the mid-1340s. The death followed an unsuccessful **Mazandaran campaign**; **Aigle dates it April 1343**, whereas **Madelung's Bavandid account gives 1344** for the Mazandaran confrontation. Both chronology traditions are explicitly identified; the disagreement is not concealed.
4. **Kartid endpoint:** Separates Timur's occupation of Herat in **April 1381** from the final elimination of Pir ʿAli in **1389**; corrected the introductory reference entry and §51.16.
5. **Chobanid succession chronology:** Adds **16 July 1338** for Alataq (Alāṭāq); clarifies Sati Beg's brief nomination and political marriage; adds **December 1343** for Hasan Kuchak's death; maintains the Chobanid collapse in 1357.
6. **Malek Ashraf's figurehead:** Identifies **Nushirvan (Anushirvan-i Adil)** as a former **wardrobe keeper and non-Chinggisid figurehead** who invoked a Sasanian royal name, rather than treating every Chobanid nominal ruler as a Chinggisid prince.
7. **Muzaffarid chronology:** Identifies recognition of nominal Chinggisid sovereignty in early coinage, Kerman governorship and mint issues c. **1340–41**, taking Shiraz in **1353**, and Isfahan in **1356**.
8. **Mazandaran:** Clarifies the Bavandid ruler's death in **April 1349**, Afrasiyabid government, and the Marʿashi coalition's victory in **1359**.
9. **Evidentiary precision:** Distinguishes territorial control from a claim represented by coins, the reliability of hostile dynastic chronicles, and the claims that can responsibly be drawn from Hafiz's literary career.

These are targeted corrections, not a claim to have exhaustively collated every medieval Arabic or Persian source.
