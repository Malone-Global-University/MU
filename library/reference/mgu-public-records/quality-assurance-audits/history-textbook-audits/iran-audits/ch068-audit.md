## Consolidated historical corrections and substantive additions

1. **§68.8:** Battle of Karnal is now dated **24 February 1739 Gregorian**, with **13 February Old Style** identified explicitly; clarified military conduct, Delhi massacre, evidence limits and political consequences. Source: [Encyclopaedia Iranica, India vii](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/india-vii-relations-the-afsharid-and-zand-periods/).
2. **§68.16:** Clarified **Mohammad Hasan Khan Qajar's defeat of Azad Khan near Urmia in 1757** as distinct from Karim Khan's later campaign toward Tabriz and Urmia culminating in **1763**. Source: [Iranica, Karim Khan Zand](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/karim-khan-zand/).
3. **§68.19:** Reconstructed succession from **Karim Khan's death in 1779** through disputes over **Abu'l-Fath Khan and Mohammad Ali Khan**, Zaki Khan, Sadeq Khan, Ali Morad Khan, Jafar Khan, and Lotf Ali Khan, without taking over the full Qajar narrative. Sources: [Iranica, Zand Dynasty](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/zand-dynasty/) and [Abu'l-Fath Khan Zand](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/abul-fath-khan-zand/).
4. **§68.18:** Specified **Mir Muhanna's capture of the Dutch fort on Kharg Island on 1 January 1766**, differentiating local maritime politics from Zand sovereignty. Source: [Iranica, Kharg Island ii](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/kharg-island-02/).
5. **§68.18:** Added the **July 1763 East India Company commercial agreement and silver-outflow concern**, the **1775 Fars/Isfahan famine and opening of government grain stores**, and the 1775–79 Basra episode. Sources: [Iranica, Karim Khan Zand](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/karim-khan-zand/) and [East India Company ii](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/east-india-company-british-ii-the-afsharid-zand-and-qajar-periods/).
6. **§§68.5, 68.10–68.12:** Strengthened analysis of Moghan legitimacy, the Jaʿfari negotiations, fiscal pressures, the 1744 Taqi Khan revolt and the distinction between Nader's assassination and institutional imperial fragmentation. Sources: [Iranica, Nader Shah](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/nader-shah/) and [Army iv](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/army-iv/).
7. **Primary sources:** Identified Anand Ram Mukhlis's **Badāʾeʿ-e waqāʾeʿ**, Part I; refined the Mirza Mahdi Khan source into a labeled overview; and anchored the Nami study to a **specific reported account of choosing Shiraz as the government seat**, discussed by Christoph Werner (2011, pp. 223–224). These are explicitly described as source overviews, paraphrases or mediated source interpretation—not invented quotations. References: [Iranica, Anand Ram Mokles](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/anand-ram-mokles/), [Jahangosha-ye Naderi](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/jahangosa-ye-naderi/), [Werner in Court Cultures](https://www.routledge.com/Court-Cultures-in-the-Muslim-World-Seventh-to-Nineteenth-Centuries/Fuess-Hartung/p/book/9781138788909).
8. **Bibliography:** Replaced provisional “verify editions” instructions with complete bibliographic records for Axworthy (2006), Tucker (2006), Lockhart (1938), Perry (1979), Matthee (2012), Werner (2011), relevant Iranica entries and identified primary-source traditions. Publisher and catalog records were checked.
