## Consolidated historical corrections

1. **Gilanentz testimony:** Distinguished compiler Petros di Sarkis Gilanentz, who was based outside Isfahan, from firsthand informants including Joseph Apislamaian. Corrected introduction, §67.9, actor identification and review references.
2. **Kerman chronology:** Reconstructed the separate 1719–1720 occupation/devastation, subsequent recovery and 1721–February 1722 campaigns; distinguished temporary military presence from durable control and highlighted varying Zoroastrian experiences.
3. **Shiraz:** Specified resistance after the fall of Isfahan, the Afghan siege begun in 1723 and the city's capture in 1724.
4. **Georgians at Gulnabad:** Clarified that Rostam Khan's Georgian soldiers fought at Gulnabad although the larger force expected from Vakhtang VI did not arrive.
5. **Russian campaign dating:** Distinguished Derbent's late-August Julian entry from early-September Gregorian dating; corrected the timing of the Russian garrison at Rasht to early 1723 and clarified the geographic limits of Russian occupation.
6. **Bibliographic precision:** Corrected Krusiński's English edition title and integrated selected scholarship and primary-source editions into the research apparatus.
7. **Source pedagogy:** Required the research exercise to identify the actual originators of reports transmitted via Gilanentz.
8. **Bibliographic correction after second review:** Corrected the English 1728 edition to *The History of the Revolution of Persia* (singular), as catalogued in original-edition library records.
9. **1723 treaty legal precision:** Clarified that non-ratification by Tahmasp II did not prevent Russia from occupying and retaining portions of the territories it claimed; signing, sovereign assent, and effective occupation are distinct.

The chapter already correctly distinguished 8/9 March as competing dates for Gulnabad; separated the October 1722 stages of surrender, abdication and Mahmud's entry; treated the 1723 Russo-Safavid treaty as signed by the envoy but not ratified by Tahmasp II; and reserved the later reconstitution of Iranian authority chiefly for Chapter 68. No unsupported casualty totals or purported direct primary-source quotations were introduced.
