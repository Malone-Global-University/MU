## 1. Governing materials and method

The consolidated revision uses the uploaded Claude draft `Pasted text(20261127-163901).txt`, the authoritative `iran-outline-revised(5).txt`, the `iran-master(4).md` governing instructions, the approved Chapter 100 twenty-section plan, and the completed Chapter 99 HTML design as the immediately preceding implementation reference. Claude's compressed handoff was accepted under the user's revised resource-allocation protocol. It was not treated as a completed publication-ready manuscript. The revision retained the chapter identity, twenty section assignments and feature assignments, but substantially rewrote the highly compressed paragraphs into a full narrative. The opening, closing, documentary, quantitative and navigational components were completed in the same consolidated pass.

This is a historical textbook synthesis, not an exhaustive archival investigation. The principal claims and quantitative observations were checked against identifiable constitutional, electoral, economic, historical and documentary sources; analytical explanations were developed independently. The 55 numbered references are linked from the text and instructional features. No direct source quotations have been reconstructed from memory; source windows contain labeled paraphrases. Some documents were available only through indexed excerpts or reproductions rather than full original editions; limits are recorded below.

### Quantitative reconciliation and limitations

- Khatami's 1997 total (20,138,784) and 2001 total (21,659,053) are the Iran Data Portal series; its 1997 record notes a competing published series with slightly different candidate counts. No unsupported reconciliation is offered. Reported turnout is 79.92% and 66.77%, respectively. The chapter distinguishes ballots cast from eligible voters and supports comparison with 1993.
- 2000/2004 parliamentary figures include 67.35%/51.21% turnout and 83.78%/66.69% approved shares of registered candidates. Final approvals include the completed vetting process, not solely Guardian Council disqualifications; initial exclusions, reinstatements and final approvals are not interchangeable.
- The 2003 council record reports 49.96% turnout, but its 20,235,898 ballots and 41,501,783 eligible voters imply 48.7591%. Both figures are explicitly labeled. The discrepancy is not resolved by inventing a new denominator. Both observations support the broad direction of change from 1999.
- The economic table uses the IMF's 2006 historical reporting vintage. It includes 2000/01–2004/05 data for real GDP growth at factor cost, CPI inflation, unemployment, current account and exchange rates. Additional oil price, oil and gas export receipts and nominal dollar GDP per capita observations are separately attributed. Nominal USD GDP per capita is not described as real growth; export earnings are not treated as identical to government oil revenue.
- UNESCO UIS/UNdata observations on female tertiary enrollment and share of tertiary students and ITU internet-use observations are included as supplementary social indicators. Comparable full series for youth unemployment, newspaper circulation and other requested social indicators were not assembled; the discussion remains qualitative on those points. No fabricated series or demographic breakdowns have been inserted.
- Numerical election figures are reproduced from the identified historical compilation rather than represented as newly independently certified official returns. No comprehensive protest casualty total, factional seat allocation or disputed attribution is asserted without an adequate basis.

## 6. Decisions and residual qualifications

- Preserved the approved twenty-section architecture and used the established Chapter 99 design baseline while expanding almost all narrative text from the compressed draft. The overall length exceeds the indicative target because the required scholarly depth and instructional material were prioritized.
- The chapter incorporates labeled documentary paraphrases; original Persian texts and every source edition were not fully consulted. The Paris Agreement English PDF could not be opened directly, but indexed identification and a reproducing IAEA resolution support the limited account given. Historical scholarship was checked selectively; no claim of exhaustive verification is made.
- The municipal turnout inconsistency and alternative 1997 election totals are expressly disclosed. Additional detailed social indicators remain qualitative where reliable comparable observations were not obtained.
- The actual geographical maps remain an independent editorial task. The adjacent Chapter 101 filename is inferred from the authoritative outline and must be checked against deployment; Chapter 99's filename follows the completed preceding chapter. External hyperlinks are traceable references, not a guarantee of permanent availability.
- No separate Claude revision was required. No further general narrative expansion or routine micro-revision cycle is recommended.

**Final disposition:** Completed manuscript satisfies the twenty-section production plan and the direct historical narrative target. Recommended for editorial locking on acceptance of the documented qualifications and routine deployment verification.
