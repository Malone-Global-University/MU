## II. Historical and source remediation

1. Tahmasp’s first repentance is distinguished from the second, decree-backed moral reform in **1556**, based on the specialist *Encyclopaedia Iranica* biography.
2. The chronicle inventory of Abbas I’s **1611** Ardabil gift records **1,162 Chinese porcelain objects**. This is described as an inventory rather than as a contemporary independent assessment of every piece surviving today.
3. Mir Damad’s death is placed in **1040 AH / 1630–1631 CE**; Mulla Sadra’s traditional **1050 AH / 1640–1641** date is distinguished from the reported **1045 AH / 1635–1636** alternative. Mir Findiriski’s approximate death date is separately retained, not conflated with Mulla Sadra.
4. The 1711 Madrasa-ye Sultani source study identifies Maryam Moazzen’s published study and dated deeds (2 July and 9 November 1711). The Quantitative Study now cites documented planned allocations of **30 tumans for Muharram**, **3 tumans for Arba‘in**, **1 tuman and 4,000 dinars for a 21-Ramadan observance**, and **14 tumans for each of two separate birthday observances**. The broad stated commemorative allowance of **351 tumans and 8,100 Tabrizi dinars** is identified but not incorrectly added to its components or described as total madrasa income.
5. Numerical interpretation distinguishes the convention **1 tuman = 10,000 dinars; 1 abbasi = 200 dinars** from changing regional denominations. Only directly comparable unit-specific ratios are calculated; no invented conversion of Tabrizi dinars or riyals is performed.
6. Primary-source windows for al-Karaki and Majlisi remain properly labeled editorial paraphrases with verified edition or heading references, **not** falsely presented as manuscript-collated quotations.
7. The independent bibliography distinguishes the original texts, printed documentary editions, modern analysis, and digital reference entries.
