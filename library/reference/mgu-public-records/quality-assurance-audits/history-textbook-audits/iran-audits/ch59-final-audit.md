## Historical corrections, including further independent checks

1. **Death of Muhammad Khan Ustajlu:** Claude's original draft placed his death in 1515. He was killed **at the Battle of Chaldiran on 23 August 1514**. His successor and brother **Qara Khan (Kara Han) Ustajlu** died at **Koçhisar (Dede Kargın) in May 1516**. Sections 59.8 and 59.11 and end matter now distinguish them correctly. Historical verification: TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi articles “Diyarbakır,” “Mardin,” and “Bıyıklı Mehmed Paşa.”
2. **Ghujdawan commander — newly discovered substantive error:** §59.5 incorrectly listed **Mir Najm al-Din Masʿud** among the officials killed in November 1512. **Amir Yar-Ahmad (Najm-e Thani)**, the Safavid *vakil* commanding the failed campaign, was captured and executed. His predecessor **Najm al-Din Masʿud Gilani** had died around 1509–1510. Revised with explicit differentiation; checked against Michel M. Mazzaoui, “Najm-e Thani,” *Encyclopaedia Iranica*.
3. **Amid and Mardin chronology:** Following Amid's anti-Safavid uprising and Safavid siege, **Amid came under Ottoman authority on 10 September 1515**. Mardin's **town** was negotiated into submission in **October 1515** but its **citadel** remained contested; Qara Khan regained the town before his defeat at **Koçhisar in May 1516**. The fortress finally passed from Safavid control in the **late-1516 to 1517** timeframe, with differences among secondary accounts on the precise date. Section 59.11 and the preview chronology now distinguish these stages and do not misleadingly describe a single simultaneous conquest.
4. **Chaldiran and Tabriz dates:** The battle was **23 August 1514 (2 Rajab 920 AH)**. Selim occupied Tabriz in **early September 1514**, approximately two weeks after the battle, and withdrew in mid-September. The opening and §59.9 were corrected accordingly. Date checked against Michael J. McCaffrey, “Čālderān,” *Encyclopaedia Iranica*.
5. **Ottoman expedition departure:** The chapter distinguishes Selim's movement from **Edirne in March 1514** and departure from **Istanbul toward Anatolia in April**; this avoids the original conflation of staging places and departure months.
6. **Civilian experience and source limitations:** Removed an unsupported inference about what individual Tabrizi households “must have” done. In §59.16 added documentary context from the **1518 Ottoman tax survey of Amid**, including **1,220 Muslim and 1,093 non-Muslim households** and 237 unmarried taxpayers. Clarified that fiscal categories are not total population figures or evidence of wartime demographic losses. The instructional everyday-life scenarios are explicitly labeled possible reconstructed choices, not actual recorded actions.
7. **Anatolian Qizilbash communities:** Qualified generalized claims about public concealment and integration into Ottoman military service; later devotional traditions are not treated as automatic evidence for each community in 1514.
8. **Historical method and sources:** Expanded §59.19 to distinguish Ottoman campaign narratives, retrospective Safavid chronicles, foreign reports, and post-conquest tax registers. Added the relevant Iranica and TDV entries to a seven-link scholarly guide. The Primary Source Windows identify themselves as editorial studies where no original passage has been collated.
