## 3. Consolidated historical corrections

1. **IRGC:** Removed the premature claim of a separate ministry in 1979–80 and avoided asserting one definitive founding-decree date. Distinguished spring 1979 formation, constitutional Article 150, and the September 1982 organizational statute.
2. **Basij:** Distinguished the November 1979 mobilization call, July 1980 legislation, January 1981 incorporation into the Guards, and later organizational development. Added linked legislative references.
3. **Courts:** Added original constitutional Articles 32 and 34–39 and their relevance to individualized responsibility, fair trial and detention. Linked contemporary human-rights documentation; retained distinction between regular and revolutionary courts.
4. **Property:** Added a distinction between the recovery of unlawfully acquired assets under Article 49, nationalization, and administrative management by foundations. No inference of unlawful ownership from mere association with the monarchy.
5. **Baháʼí persecution:** Added attributed dates of the December 1981 arrests and executions and distinguished the successor assembly from its predecessor. Explained Articles 12 and 13 without conflating Sunni Muslims and unrecognized religious communities.
6. **Education:** Added the April 1980 campus directives and June headquarters creation. Iranica gives 12 June while other chronologies give 13 June; the discrepancy is disclosed rather than artificially resolved. Retained the distinction between the original headquarters and the 1984 council.
7. **Social regulation:** Added the distinction among 1979 demands, administrative enforcement in 1980 and subsequent penal legislation in 1983. Preserved diverse women's political agency and voluntary versus compulsory observance.
8. **Presidential removal:** Replaced general language with the official parliamentary notification: 177 in favor, one against, twelve neutral, 190 participants. Distinguished 21 June vote and 22 June dismissal. Explained original Article 110(5) and avoided applying the amended 1989 Article 89 retrospectively. Corrected the duration of Bani-Sadr's post-dismissal hiding and removed an unsupported superlative about his electoral mandate.
9. **Succession:** Distinguished the July 24 and October 2 elections and October 29 approval of Mousavi; included the rejection of the initial prime-ministerial nominee. Removed unnecessary details of attacks. Corrected Beheshti's office to head of the Supreme Court where appropriate.
10. **Quantitative evidence:** Added an actual evidence table and interpretive exercise to Feature 17. Used directly corroborated Amnesty figures: 709 known executions in 1980; over 1,600 recorded after June 20 through the October 1981 report's cutoff; approximately 400 in 1983; 5,447 cumulatively through the end of 1983. These are documented executions generally, not exclusive political execution totals. Overlapping intervals must not be summed. The earlier audit's exact full-year 1981 figure was not retained because the directly retrieved sources here provide stronger support for the partial-year count. This is a deliberate evidentiary choice.
11. **Tudeh:** Added successive arrests in February and April 1983, televised statements and May 4 proscription. Distinguished international political connections from allegations of individual crimes and stressed the need to corroborate confessions.
12. **Eight-Point Decree:** Expanded the paraphrase to encompass all eight points, including the explicit security exceptions and complaint arrangements. Distinguished administrative complaint mechanisms from independent judicial oversight and the existence of the decree from effective implementation.
13. **Political legitimacy:** Qualified claims of broad popular allegiance and retained the thesis that consolidation rested on both support and coercion. Neither electoral participation nor social spending alone measures public opinion.

## 4. Source verification and limitations

Directly retrieved in the final revision:

- [Iran Data Portal, Dismissal of Bani Sadr as President](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/dismissal-of-bani-sadr-as-president/): official parliamentary notification and decree; vote figures confirmed.
- [Amnesty International, October 7, 1981 report](https://www.iranrights.org/library/document/144/executions-in-iran-in-light-of-international-human-rights-standard-june-september-1981-more-than-1600-executions): partial-period recorded executions and reference to May 1980 tribunal investigation.
- [Amnesty International, 1980 execution figures](https://www.iranrights.org/library/document/174/amnesty-international-seeks-to-send-mission-to-iran-in-effort-to-stop-executions): 709 known executions in Iran in 1980.
- [Amnesty International, report covering 1983](https://www.iranrights.org/library/document/98/amnesty-international-iran-annual-report-1983): cumulative 5,447 and approximately 400 in 1983; undercount warning.
- [Amnesty International, Human Rights Violation in Iran](https://www.iranrights.org/library/document/95/human-rights-violation-in-iran): detention and execution evidence and reporting limitations.
- [Encyclopaedia Iranica, Khomeini: Life](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khomeini-i-life/): Cultural Revolution chronology, including June 12 dating.
- [Encyclopaedia Iranica, Chronology of Iranian History Part 4](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/chronology-of-iranian-history-part-4/): general chronology.
- [Wilson Center, New Sources on Iran’s Tudeh Party](https://www.wilsoncenter.org/blog-post/new-sources-irans-tudeh-party-1978-1988): archival materials on party connections and state allegations.
- [Encyclopaedia Iranica, Hostage Crisis](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/hostage-crisis/): factional context.

Additional document links from the preceding audit were integrated, including the original constitutional collection, Guards statute, Basij incorporation legislation, Eight-Point Decree, Iranica feminist movements and the IHRDC Baháʼí study. Not all were freshly reopened or independently checked for exact wording in this final pass. The bibliography distinguishes recommended monographs from consulted documents. No claim is made to have read Abrahamian, Bakhash, Arjomand or Schirazi in full or to have verified every paragraph against archival sources. Regional histories and specific court cases are not exhaustively collated. The exact founding-decree date of the IRGC remains deliberately qualified.
