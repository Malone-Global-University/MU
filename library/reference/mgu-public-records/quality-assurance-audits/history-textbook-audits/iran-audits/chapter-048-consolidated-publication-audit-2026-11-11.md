### Historically material interventions

- **Conversion chronology:** Retained June 1295 as secure; identified the scholarly dating of 2 Shaʿban 694/17 June and an alternative tradition associated with 4 Shaʿban/19 June, without conflating a conversion profession with subsequent accession ceremony.
- **Religious policy:** Made clear that Ghazan was already moderating some measures toward Christians by July 1296, before Nawruz's execution in 1297; avoided an over-simple post-1297 turning-point claim.
- **Vizier chronology:** Corrected Savaji's execution in 1312 under Öljeitü and Rashid al-Din's execution in 1318 under Abu Saʿid.
- **Coin evidence:** Reconciled coin HCR15280 with the Ashmolean catalogue: AH 699; silver dirham, 2.03 g; *unknown mint* with blundered mint legend. Distinguished the specimen's measured weight from the circa 2.16 g silver standard described in specialist numismatic literature.
- **Institutional precision:** Expanded registration and scheduled taxation, the <em>diwan-i khalisa</em> and land restoration, military <em>iqtaʿ</em> assignments of 703 AH, coercive labor implications, <em>yam</em> authorization, and the evidentiary limits of claims about reform results.
- **Historical boundaries:** Preserved Rashid al-Din's manuscripts and broad historiographical program for Chapter 49, agrarian/urban recovery for Chapter 50, and fragmentation for Chapter 51.
