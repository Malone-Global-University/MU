## 3. Consolidated historical correction record

### A. Muhammad Baqir Majlisi and the *mulla-bashi* office — corrected

The draft falsely implied Majlisi was a *mulla-bashi*, while at the same time treating his documented office as vague. §58.19 now accurately states his appointment as **shaykh al-Islam of Isfahan in 1687**, an office he held until **1699 or 1700**. It distinguishes the *mulla-bashi* office, founded **1712**, after Majlisi's death, and avoids uncorroborated claims that Majlisi held both chief *sadr* offices. Adds a focused discussion of his scholarly network, court backing, anti-Sufi polemics and the limits of evidential claims about later royal edicts.

**Verification:** Rainer Brunner, “Majlesi, Mohammad-Baqer,” *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, <https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/majlesi-mohammad-baqer/>; “Shi'ite Doctrine ii. Hierarchy in the Imamiyya,” <https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/shiite-doctrine-ii-hierarchy-emamiya/>.

### B. Muhammad Amin al-Astarabadi chronology — corrected throughout

Replaces an unqualified **1623–1624** death with **1623–1624 or 1626–1627** wherever relevant, including the main narrative, chronology preview, timeline feature, major people entry and review entry.

**Verification:** Etan Kohlberg, “Astarabadi, Mohammad Amin,” *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, <https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/astarabadi-molla-mohammad-amin-b/>.

### C. Ali al-Karaki’s movement and royal recognition — corrected

The manuscript previously suggested al-Karaki's travel to the Iranian court c. 1504 without evidence. The revised narrative distinguishes his departure from Jabal Amil for **Najaf around 1504** from his documented recognition by **Shah Ismail around 1510**, while he was in Najaf. His later visits to the Safavid court are distinguished from those earlier events. The chronology preview agrees.

**Verification:** Rula Jurdi Abisaab, “Karaki,” *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, <https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/karaki/>.

### D. Ardabil porcelain endowment — corrected

§58.11 now dates Abbas I's specific Chinese porcelain gift to Ardabil to **1017 AH / 1608**, not c. 1611, while distinguishing that donation from the broader set of endowments and associated gifts.

**Verification:** *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Ceramics xv,” <https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/ceramics-xv/>; Kishwar Rizvi, discussion of 1608 waqf and shrine architecture, <https://arthistory.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/1-Rizvi_Shah2.pdf> (referenced bibliographically; the PDF's images were not examined for this editorial audit).

### E. Shaykh Lotfallah Mosque — corrected

Opening and §58.16 now identify the mosque as a **private royal worship space**, built **1602–1603 to 1618–1619**, and distinguish it from the large public congregational royal mosque and from madrasas. The text no longer conflates its private royal function with the scholar's separate teaching career.

**Verification:** Sussan Babaie, “Isfahan x. Monuments (3) Mosques,” *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, <https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/isfahan-x3-mosques/>.

## 4. Documentary source-study improvements

All six existing primary-source/object windows remain **explicit editorial paraphrases, not purported verbatim translations**. The revision adds identifiable bibliographical and catalogue directions rather than inventing passages or folios.

1. **Hasan Rumlu:** English translation of the early Safavids by C. N. Seddon (Baroda, 1934), accession-era narrative under 907 AH; exact original-language passage not recollated.
2. **Sadr administration:** *Tadhkirat al-Muluk*, British Library Or. 9496, Minorsky (1943); specifically identifies *sadr-e khassa* and *sadr-e ʿamma* and stresses the manual's **c. 1725** date and late institutional perspective.
3. **Friday prayer:** locates al-Karaki's published juristic treatise in *Rasaʾil al-Muhaqqiq al-Karaki*, ed. Muhammad Hassun (1988–89), pp. **77–136**, and Zayn al-Din al-Amili, *Risala fi salat al-jumʿa* (2000), pp. **173–248**; identifies Devin J. Stewart's peer-reviewed article in *Bulletin of SOAS* **72.3 (2009):425–457**, DOI `10.1017/S0041977X09990024`, for discussion of legal and patronage disputes. No claim to have directly collated al-Qatifi's text.
4. **Kashifi:** *Rawzat al-Shuhada*, dated **908 AH / 1502–1503**, ed. Abu al-Hasan Sha'rani (Tehran, reprint 2000–2001), and Princeton University Library Garrett no. **457L** manuscript copied **1669–1670**. The manuscript is later and not an autograph.
5. **Mashhad:** Iskandar Beg Munshi, trans. Roger Savory, *The History of Shah Abbas the Great* (Boulder, **1978**, 2 vols.), and Caroline Mawer, *Iran* **49 (2011): 123–147**, DOI `10.1080/05786967.2011.11834433`.
6. **Majlisi:** identified **2004 Qum edition** of *Jala al-Uyun*, a catalogued later copy at British Library **IO Islamic 3816**, and the 1963 Tehran printing of *Hayat al-Qulub*.

**Limit of certification:** Bibliographical witnesses and scholarship have been checked selectively, but primary source prose here is editorial paraphrase; individual source windows do not substitute for facsimile/edition page-by-page original-language collation. Students are directed to check the cited passages before quoting.
