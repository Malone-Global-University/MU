## Consolidated historical corrections

1. **Enthronement:** Corrected misleading claims that Abbas was enthroned in the autumn of 1587. The formal transfer of power at Qazvin is dated **1 October 1588** by Roger Savory in *Encyclopaedia Iranica*. The political crises and Uzbek offensive of 1587 are distinguished from the formal ceremony. Older broad labels of the reign as beginning in 1587 are disclosed.
2. **1581 proclamation and 1585 guardianship:** The early Khurasan proclamation is now assigned to a coalition led prominently by Ali Quli Khan Shamlu, not wrongly attributed to Murshid Quli Khan. Murshid Quli Khan's seizure of the prince in **1585** is distinguished from his later action in 1588.
3. **Uzbek conquest chronology:** Removed the erroneous claim that Mashhad fell in 1587. *Encyclopaedia Iranica* treatments disagree about the exact capture of Herat (1588 versus February 1589) and Mashhad (late 1589 versus spring 1590); the new text identifies the dispute rather than inventing false precision. The 1598 Safavid reconquest is retained.
4. **Iskandar Beg Munshi:** Distinguished the history's first portion (c. 1616) and the author's own second portion (1629) from the later continuation concerning Shah Safi (partly written by someone else).
5. **Silk tribute:** Replaced the false statement that the 1618 Serav settlement removed the silk obligation. Scholarly studies discuss its **reduction from 200 to 100 loads** annually, while the actual delivery of silk was a separate matter.
6. **Georgia:** Corrected the identification to **Luarsab II**, executed in 1622, and the major Giorgi Saakadze-associated uprising to **1625**, including Martqopi and Marabda.
7. **Baghdad:** Clarified that the 1534 Ottoman conquest occurred during **Shah Tahmasp I's reign**, not his successors' reigns; standardized the Safavid recovery to 1623.
8. **Burial:** Corrected Abbas's burial from Mashhad to the **shrine of Habib ibn Musa in Kashan**, while noting scholarly caution about the identification of a particular surviving cenotaph.
9. **Cross-references:** Removed erroneous claims that Chapter 62 would narrate the later history of Kandahar, the 1638 loss of Baghdad, or Shah Safi's political reign. Chapter 62 is about Isfahan's capital, court, architecture, and urban life. The correct Chapters 63–64 commercial/social boundaries remain.
10. **Bibliography:** Added Bournoutian's 2010 date and specific Armenian history chapter loci, corrected a modern scholarly article title, and added traceable reference works and a 1622 East India Company correspondence record.

## Expansion scope

Expansion was added principally in §§61.9, 61.14, 61.17–61.20, including campaign logistics, disputed sovereignty, the administrative geography of Isfahan, effects on rural communities, captive and military labor, dynastic confinement, and an analytical synthesis distinguishing royal achievement from durable institutional resilience. The expansion avoids invented eyewitness speech and ungrounded numerical estimates.
