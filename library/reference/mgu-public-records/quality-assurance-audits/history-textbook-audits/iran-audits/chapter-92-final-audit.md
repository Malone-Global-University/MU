## 1. Scope and method

This is the consolidated independent revision of the supplied Claude-authored HTML. The audit uses the reported chapter plan and the preceding editorial review as the governing revision brief. The original standalone outline and Chapter 1 model were not supplied for independent side-by-side verification in this turn. The existing chapter shell, stylesheet, sidebar architecture, feature classes, review apparatus and footer were retained; no wholesale redesign or discretionary expansion was performed.

The editor inspected the supplied manuscript, checked specific claims against accessible primary and secondary sources, revised the three primary-source windows, checked the consistency of the narrative and opening apparatus, and parsed and rendered the completed HTML. The purpose was to resolve the identified publication-critical issues in one consolidated pass, not to claim exhaustive sentence-level verification of all historical literature.
