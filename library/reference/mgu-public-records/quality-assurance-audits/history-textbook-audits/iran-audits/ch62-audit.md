## Historical corrections and evidence

1. **Ali Qapu and its projecting columned terrace:** Corrected §62.6, the opening, and §62.18 to separate the earlier palace gateway/roof-level view under Abbas I from the projecting *talar* generally dated **1643–1644, Abbas II**. Source: Priscilla P. Soucek, *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “ʿAli Qapu,” https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/ali-qapu-a-five-storied-building-overlooking-the-maydan-e-sah-of-isfahan/.
2. **Abbas I's accession:** §62 context, chronology and reference blocks distinguish accession struggle from **1 October 1588 formal enthronement**. Source: Roger Savory, *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “ʿAbbas I,” https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/abbas-i/.
3. **Vank Cathedral:** Corrected to **approximately 1653–1664** for the extant All Savior's cathedral; differentiated earlier church/monastery from later major building, and identified Khwaja Avetik as patron. Source: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Julfa iv. Architecture and Painting,” https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/julfa-iv-architecture-and-painting/.
4. **Armenian printing:** Identified Khachatur Kesaratsi's 1636 press and 1638 printed Psalter, rather than loosely conflating the introduction of the press with the production of its first book. Source: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Julfa i. Safavid Period,” https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/julfa-i-safavid-period/.
5. **Zayandeh Rud diversion:** Identified the Kuhrang/upper Karun watershed scheme, the 1618 survey and subsequent abortive work c.1620–1621; distinguished Tahmasp-era attempts from Abbas-era initiatives. Source: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, entry on Zayandeh Rud; R. D. McChesney, “Four Sources on Shah Abbas's Building of Isfahan,” *Muqarnas* 5 (1988): 103–134.
6. **Persecution of Jews:** Corrected vague attribution to describe **the 1656–1662 forced conversions under Abbas II** and official Mohammad Beg, with reference to Baba'i ben Lotf's *Ketab-e Anusi*. Sources: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, “Baba'i ben Lotf,” https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/babai-ben-lotf/, and “Conversion iv,” https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/conversion-iv/.
7. **Source-window verification:** Identified the translator and the correct title *Book of History*, as well as Arakel's chapters **4–5** on deportation and the exile of Julfa; confirmed publisher's 2010 record: https://www.mazdapublishers.com/book/arak-el-of-tabriz-book-of-history. Corrected Iskandar Beg's continuation chronology and cited McChesney's analysis, and identified the Bibliothèque nationale de France's 1711 Chardin edition: https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb31930144k.
8. **Map study:** Added grounded references to UNESCO's monument record and to Blake's historical city plans, with an explicit statement that a modern property plan does not replace a full seventeenth-century urban plan.
9. **Mobile navigation and header:** A native `<details>` chapter contents menu now closes on link selection, and the decorative chapter number is hidden at mobile widths to prevent series-metadata overlap.
