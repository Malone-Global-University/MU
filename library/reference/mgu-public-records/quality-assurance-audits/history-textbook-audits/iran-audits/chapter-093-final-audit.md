## 1. Assignment and scope

The authoritative revised Iranian chapter outline assigns this chapter constitutional drafting, the referenda, velayat-e faqih, leadership, presidency, Majles, Guardian Council and tensions between elected representation and clerical supervision. The approved twenty-section, five-division structure has been retained. The revised narrative primarily covers February 1979 through the formation of the constitutional institutions in 1980, with narrowly scoped references to 1982 and 1989 for institutional differentiation. Chapter 94 retains the hostage crisis and political struggle; Chapter 95 retains the detailed history of repression; Chapters 96–97 retain war history; Chapter 98 retains the 1989 constitutional settlement and succession; Chapter 105 retains the later political economy of the Revolutionary Guards and foundations.

The draft's central historical argument is retained but sharpened: popular participation was institutionalized within a framework of unequal constitutional authority, rather than an undifferentiated transfer of power or a system of equal institutional sovereignty. This is a historical analysis of the original constitutional arrangement, not an assessment of current Iranian political conditions.

| Broadcasting | Corrected original Art. 175 to joint supervision by judicial, legislative, and executive branches. Direct appointment of broadcasting head by Leader belongs to 1989 version. |

| Guardian Council review | Added Arts. 92–99: six-year terms and staggered renewal, parliamentary-validity exceptions, ten-day deadline and possible ten-day extension, differentiated voting rules, participation in deliberations, three-quarters interpretation requirement, and original scope of election supervision. |

### Source review actually performed

1. [Iran Data Portal — Constitutions and Constitutional Debates](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/constitutions-and-constitutional-debates/): checked archive identification of original and amended texts and assembly debate collection.
2. [Iran Data Portal — Persian constitutional compilation](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/wp-content/uploads/constitution-persianislamic-republic-of-iran.pdf): inspected extracted former-article wording and page images for the original appointment rule (Art. 91), executive provisions (Arts. 124–126) and broadcasting provision (Art. 175). The compilation is comparative, not an unamended standalone edition despite its archive label. Other selected provisions were checked against the original institutional account and extracted text. No claim is made of exhaustive article-by-article transcription or full archival collation.
3. [Encyclopaedia Iranica — Constitution of the Islamic Republic](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/constitution-of-the-islamic-republic/): checked the drafting sequence, preliminary review body, clerical proposals, constitutional architecture, leadership succession, judicial organization and original parliamentary designation.
4. [Iran Data Portal — Referenda](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/referenda): checked recorded participation in the March and December 1979 votes; the chapter attributes the numbers to official records rather than treating them as independent certification.
5. [Iran Data Portal — 1979 constitutional assembly election](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/the-1979-assembly-of-experts-for-the-drafting-of-the-constitution-election): checked turnout, seats, candidate registrations, jurists' representation, Qasemlou's credential rejection and Gorji's listing.
6. [Iran Data Portal — 1980 presidential election](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/1980-presidential-election): checked vote totals, candidate registration and withdrawal/disqualification counts, and provincial participation. The portal's generic note on contemporary Guardian Council vetting is not used as evidence for early 1980 procedure.
7. [Iran Data Portal — 1980 parliamentary election](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/1980-parliamentary-election/): checked first-round participation, seat count, registered candidates and women elected. Later supervisory practices are not inferred from its general notes.
8. [Encyclopaedia Iranica — Elections under the Islamic Republic](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/elections/elections-ii-under-the-islamic-republic-1979-92/): checked referendum reporting, early electoral context and the subsequent interpretation of supervisory authority.
9. [Encyclopaedia Iranica — Guardian Council](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/guardian-council/) and [Iran Data Portal — Guardian Council](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/the-guardian-council/): checked legislative review and commencement of council activity in 1980.
10. [Encyclopaedia Iranica — Feminist Movements in the Islamic Republic](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/feminist-movements-iv/): checked Gorji's sole female membership and the context of women's participation.
11. [Khomeini, Islamic Government](https://al-islam.org/islamic-government-governance-jurist-sayyid-ruhullah-musawi-khomeini): checked identity, date, and general argument of the lecture collection; feature uses an identified paraphrase, not a verbatim quotation.
12. [Abdorrahman Boroumand Center — The Clash of the Ayatollahs](https://www.iranrights.org/library/document/3906): reviewed contextual evidence on clerical disagreement and constitutional opposition.
13. [Iranian Studies — The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Iran (1989 Edition)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00210862.2013.825505): search-indexed comparative original-article notes supplemented the Persian compilation; full publisher text returned an access restriction, so it was not treated as fully consulted.

Primary-source windows use clearly marked paraphrases. The original March ballot facsimile, complete Persian constitutional assembly proceedings, preliminary draft manuscript, and complete books by Arjomand, Schirazi, Abrahamian and Bakhash were not independently collated. The chapter's bibliography identifies these books as further reading, not as directly verified quotations or page-specific claims.
