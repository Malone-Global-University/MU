## Independent historical findings

### Required factual/source corrections

1. **§44.24 Zhang Wenhu convoy:** The text claims the Yuan source says Zhang's fleet “fought through and reached the main army.” This contradicts *Yuan shi*, juan 209, which records Zhang dumping rice into the sea and retiring toward Qiongzhou (Hainan), and also records Yuan generals observing that his grain fleet had not arrived. The sources may disagree on responsibility and losses, but they do not support successful delivery of Zhang's cargo. Correct the narrative and the Vân Đồn Logistics Study to distinguish the Vietnamese account of interception from the Yuan description of abandoned cargo and withdrawal.
   - Primary text: https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=6544803&if=en (juan 209, paragraphs 48–49)
   - Specialist comparison: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/ming-china-and-vietnam/brief-history-of-annan/CD6E5A787696821ABFAFED6C47BC49A1
2. **§44.38 Hồ Quý Ly's reform chronology:** The phrase describing his “mainly 1390s” measures includes a limit on the number of dependent laborers. The **hạn nô** dependent/slave restriction belongs to **1401** under the Hồ, beyond the chapter's 1400 political cutoff. Differentiate 1396 paper currency, 1397 land regulation/capital construction, and post-1400 reforms; discuss the 1401 measure only as a prospective note explicitly outside this chapter's scope.
   - Vietnam National Museum of History: https://baotanglichsuquocgia.vn/vi/Articles/3098/14720/nhung-cai-cach-cua-ho-quy-ly-cuoi-the-ky-xiv-djau-the-ky-xv.html
   - Vietnam National Museum currency background: https://baotanglichsuquocgia.vn/vi/Articles/3096/6767/ong-to-cua-tien-giay-viet-nam.html
3. **§44.2 founding-date conversion:** The chapter presents January 1226 in the Gregorian calendar as a settled date for the 1225 lunar transfer. Specialist chronology notes possible late-December 1225 **or** early-January 1226 conversions. State the traditional 1225 date without an unwarranted single Gregorian-day/year assertion.
   - https://www.archontology.org/nations/vietnam/00_1225_1400_s.php
4. **§44.27 magnitude of Yuan losses:** Avoid “the Yuan lost an army and a fleet” as if the land army was totally destroyed. The naval force was decisively defeated; Toghon and surviving land forces withdrew with losses.
5. **Archaeology window:** Complete the toponym **Đồng Má Ngựa** (not merely “Đồng Má”); distinguish Yên Giang, Đồng Vạn Muối, and Đồng Má Ngựa. Report broad radiocarbon ranges and the archaeological limitations, not firm attribution to 1288.
   - Study: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/259544538_Naval_Battlefield_Archaeology_of_the_Lost_Kublai_Khan_Fleets
