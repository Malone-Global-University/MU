## Evidence and publication limits

- The three primary-source windows use labeled editorial paraphrase/source criticism. Neither ancient manuscript facsimiles nor original-language inscriptions and chronicle passages have been independently collated word-for-word for this edition.
- The geography and mapping activities are textual; they require students to consult a suitable external historical atlas. No embedded atlas plates are claimed.
- Archaeological results concerning Angkor Thom and Angkor Wat are localized and are not extrapolated to exact metropolitan population loss, universal inundation, or simultaneous settlement abandonment.
- The 1431–1432 date is conventional and partly dependent on retrospective chronicles; Buddhist sculptural style is not a stand-alone exact chronology for a garrison or military occupation.
- Publication approval applies to a defensible first-edition scholarly historical text and functioning standalone HTML, not to exhaustive original-language archival collation or production-host integration.
