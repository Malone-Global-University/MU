## Historical verification and corrections consolidated into final HTML

1. **Ta Prohm K.273 and Preah Khan K.908 inscription languages.** Corrected claims that the two foundation steles combine Sanskrit verse with Old Khmer administrative prose. Scholarly editions describe 145 Sanskrit verses in Ta Prohm K.273 and 179 in Preah Khan K.908; the administrative inventories themselves appear in Sanskrit. Similarly corrected the hospital inscription source window and the explanatory vocabulary and narrative statements. Distinguished language from Khmer script.
2. **Inscription authorship.** Where source windows previously named only anonymous court scribes, identified the eulogies as attributed to Princes Śrī Sūryakumāra (Ta Prohm) and Śrī Vīrakumāra (Preah Khan), in court context. The paraphrases are still explicitly labeled as paraphrases, not quotations.
3. **The 1177 Khmer–Cham crisis and accession chronology.** Aligned the opening, §38.2, §38.4, chronology, conclusion, and summary with the real documentary dispute. Preserved conventional 1177 and the inscription-supported 1181 royal regnal date, but no longer states the purported naval sack, four-year occupation, or route as independently established fact. Corrected an over-narrow characterization of Michael Vickery's argument: he challenges the great conquest/sack and prolonged occupation, not merely the exact length of occupation.
4. **Identity of Cham rivals.** Distinguished Jaya Indravarman of Grāmapuravijaya from the ruler associated with the events of 1190, Jaya Indravarman of Oi Vatuv, instead of treating the modern ordinal “Jaya Indravarman IV” as conclusively one individual across both events. Kept the 1190 episode and Vidyānandana's participation.
5. **Preah Khan dating.** Preserved c. 1191 as the conventional date and identified uncertainty about the Śaka 1113/1114 reading, corresponding to approximately 1191–1193. Updated section 38.14, source window, and chronology preview.
6. **Infrastructure figures.** Confirmed, retained, and distinguished **102 hospitals from Ta Prohm K.273** and **121 rest houses from Preah Khan K.908**. Both are royal inscriptional claims, not independently verified counts of facilities.
7. **The Bayon.** Confirmed that the Buddhist origin, phases of construction, uncertain identification of monumental faces, and subsequent Hindu reuse/alteration under Jayavarman VIII are differentiated in the chapter. No additional alteration required.
8. **Small responsive stylesheet fix.** Added a narrowly scoped `@media (max-width:600px)` rule so chronology tables scroll horizontally within their own width on phones. All existing CSS rules and the JavaScript remain unchanged. This is the only CSS addition relative to Claude's submitted file.
