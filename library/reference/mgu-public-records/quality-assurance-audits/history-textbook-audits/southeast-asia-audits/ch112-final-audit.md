## Historical-source verification and data provenance

1. **Paul Doumer, 1898 budget and 1902 fiscal record.** The decree of July 31, 1898 and Doumer's *Situation de l'Indo-Chine (1897–1901)* (Hanoi, 1902) are identified in the chapter. The 1899–1902 planned general-budget series is sourced from Doumer p. 166 as reproduced by Gerard Sasges, *Journal of Southeast Asian Studies* 43(1), 2012, pp. 133–157, table 1. The chapter **labels figures as budget estimates**, not realized net receipts; the 1900–1902 proportional revenues from the three régies derive from a *separate* series, also interpreted by Sasges. Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022463411000695.
2. **Rice shipment comparison.** The 1906 report of approximately 548,000 metric tonnes of Cochinchina rice shipped, including 149,649 tonnes to Europe, derives from the United States *Monthly Consular and Trade Reports* (1907). It is labeled *shipments from Cochinchina*, not total Indochinese harvest or an exact net-export census. The remaining destination total is explicitly an arithmetic residual, not a separate published entry. Source: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/SERIALSET-05331_00_00-002-0591-0000/pdf/SERIALSET-05331_00_00-002-0591-0000.pdf.
3. **Tonkin coal figures.** The 1907–1909 numbers, 344,512; 366,800; and 449,075 tonnes, are cited as a contemporary industry compilation, covering coal extraction in Tonkin, not all Indochinese minerals or exports. The firm's reported 308,086 tonnes in 1909 is separately identified as a company-specific component, not added to the total. Source: https://www.entreprises-coloniales.fr/inde-indochine/Charbonnages_Trang-Bach.pdf.
4. **Indochina trade value.** The approximate £6.796 million in 1896 and £16.933 million in 1905 come from the contemporary 1911 *Encyclopaedia Britannica* entry, as period nominal sterling figures and a union-level comparison. No ungrounded inflation or exchange conversion is attempted. Source: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/1911_Encyclopaedia_Britannica/Indo-China,_French.
5. **Labor law.** The 26 August 1899 Tonkin order on specified contracts and worker booklets, the 5 February 1902 extensions, and 31 December 1911 Laos extension are identified from a reproduction in the historical legal collection *Réglementation du travail en Indochine*. The chapter distinguishes the original dated instrument from later retrospective annotations and avoids treating later 1920s rules as if effective in 1900. Source: https://humazur.univ-cotedazur.fr/files/original/04f725d83b677444f428b7ea0988dde956e29668.pdf.
6. **Railway.** Completion of the Haiphong–Yunnan line in 1910 is supported by contemporary coverage and later technical/labor scholarship. No precise fatality total is asserted because sources and methods disagree; an approximate 850–860 km range is identified as dependent on endpoints and definition. The 1910 *Le chemin de fer du Yunnan* is an identifiable company publication in the BnF catalogue; the document's *full original text was not collated line-by-line* for this edition. Sources: https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb33315216n ; https://www.nature.com/articles/084177a0 ; https://doi.org/10.1017/S0147547921000120.
7. **Education.** The 31 May 1906 Nguyễn royal educational measure is identified through Nguyễn Mậu Hùng's 2022 scholarly analysis; the source window explicitly states that the complete original decree was *not independently collated*, and it makes no claim of a verbatim translation. The colonial territories had different educational institutions and the text avoids describing 1906 as instant uniform reform. Source: https://ced.revistas.deusto.es/article/download/2557/3070.
8. **Period boundary.** The chapter stops in 1914, distinguishing the early rubber sector from interwar plantation development and the Yunnan railway from the later completion of the Vietnamese north–south route in 1936.

## Documentary limitations, preserved in the release

- Four source windows name specific documents, identify creator/date/purpose, mark analytical paraphrases, acknowledge perspective and omissions, and supply five questions each. **The 1910 railway company publication and the complete 1906 royal education text have been identified via institutional catalog and scholarly discussion but not fully diplomatically collated.** This is disclosed directly in their windows rather than hidden or represented as a full-text verification.
- The historical labor compilation is a later reprint containing original dated laws together with amendments; its later provisions are **not** projected backward into the chapter period.
- The budget, rice, trade and coal datasets are drawn from different original and secondary compilations. They must not be combined into a mathematically uniform series.
- This is a scholarly *textual HTML* publication readiness judgment, **not** independent archival certification, peer review, automated accessibility certification or a claim of live website deployment.
