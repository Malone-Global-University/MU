## II. Consolidated historical and documentary corrections

1. **The Sukamerta charter (§49.14 and feature):** Identifies **Sang Apañji Patipati Pu Kapat** by name, the restoration of Sukamerta as hereditary *sīma*, the plate 5r2–5r5 wording, the beneficiary's service, and the 1296 date; source window now identifies its paraphrased passage and explains the difference between predominantly Old Javanese and the Sanskrit elements marked in the DHARMA critical edition. The claim that Kudadu and Sukamerta are the only surviving charters of the reign has been withdrawn; other charters are acknowledged.
2. **Yuan penalties (§49.10 and feature):** Separates Shi Bi's seventeen strokes and one-third family-property confiscation in *Yuan shi* 162 from Yiheimishi's separately documented one-third confiscation in *juan* 131. Restitution and Gao Xing's contrasting treatment are explained. A later text mentioning identical corporal penalties is labeled later rather than silently assigned to either biography.
3. **Fleet departure date (§49.5, introductory chronology, timeline):** Explains the final lunar month of Yuan year 1292 versus the reconstructed Gregorian date **22 January 1293**. All chronology tables updated consistently.
4. **Maritime navigation (§§49.6, 49.8 military geography):** Replaces generic Belitung wording with qualified Natuna–Tambelan–Serutu/Karimata–Pulau Gelam–Karimunjawa–Tuban reconstruction; differentiates coastal landing at Tuban from lower Brantas/Hujung Galuh waterway operations and clearly limits what archaeological traces can prove.
5. **Harṣawijaya and the founder (§49.3, genealogical debate, chapter opening and review):** Separates the founder's attested Narāryya Saṅgrāmawijaya identification from the independently attested earlier Śrī Harṣawijaya in inscriptional evidence. Hadi Sidomulyo's proposed Dyah Lembu Tal identification is treated as scholarship, not certain fact.
6. **Jayanegara and dynastic maternity (§§49.13, 49.19):** Explicitly contrasts the *Pararaton*'s Dara Petak tradition with contemporaneous Sukamerta (1296) and Balawi (1305) presentations linking the prince to Tribhuwaneswari. Distinguishes official dynastic affiliation from a claim of conclusively established biological maternity.
7. **Posthumous religious commemoration (§49.18):** Identifies Simping/Sumberjati and Harihara-Śaiva remembrance, Antahpura and Buddhist/Jina association, and the distinction between the founder's reign and later fourteenth-century commemoration and renovation under Hayam Wuruk.
8. **Rivalries (§49.17):** Clarifies literary narrative versus dated evidence of official appointments, including the difficulty of dating Ranggalawe, Sora and Nambi; does not force Nambi's later downfall into Kertarajasa's reign.
9. **Historical interpretive safeguards:** Maintains non-teleological separation between 1293 founding and Chapter 50's later expansions; no modern national-state framework is projected backward. Retains qualified consecration traditions and Trowulan archaeological chronology.
10. **Source apparatus:** Adds a 10-item primary/documentary and scholarship register with working-source URLs, source type, period, and application. All three primary-source teaching windows remain **editorial paraphrases**, not invented quotations or claims of complete original-manuscript collation.
