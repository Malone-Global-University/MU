## 4. Historical corrections included

1. **Huế's construction:** Distinguished earlier European-influenced bastion expertise from construction after 1802. Olivier de Puymanel died in 1799 and did not personally direct the imperial citadel works. Reemphasized Vietnamese administration, technical work, and coerced labor.
2. **Northern governorships:** Replaced the incorrectly listed Nguyễn Hữu Thận with a source-grounded succession involving Nguyễn Văn Thành, Nguyễn Huỳnh Đức, and Lê Chất. Avoided overprecision where studies disagree on transitions or vacancies.
3. **Gia Định Thành:** Kept formal establishment in 1808; clarified the development of its five *trấn*, including Hà Tiên, and the appointments of Nguyễn Văn Nhân, Lê Văn Duyệt, Trương Tấn Bửu, Nguyễn Huỳnh Đức, and Nguyễn Văn Nhân again. Duyệt's second appointment is identified as belonging to 1820 and the succeeding reign.
4. **Cadaster chronology:** Distinguished surviving northern 1805–1806 registers, central surveys of subsequent years, and the extensive surviving 1836 southern survey. Removed the implication of one completed nationwide 1805 survey.
5. **Legal chronology:** Distinguished the 1811 commission, Gia Long's 1812 preface, catalogued 1813 printed copies, and later distribution and implementation phases. Added archival catalogue locators from the Hán-Nôm Institute and EFEO.
6. **French diplomatic chronology:** Removed the incorrect statement that Chaigneau returned as an official French agent in 1819; he left Vietnam in 1819 and returned in May 1821, after Gia Long's death.
7. **Nguyễn Văn Thành:** Identified his suicide in 1817 following the court prosecution and treated alleged conspiracy as an allegation rather than proved guilt.
8. **1820 epidemic:** Distinguished the devastating epidemic recorded during Minh Mạng's first year from Gia Long's final-year events.
9. **Earlier Nguyen and Le relations:** Qualified the statement of nominally shared loyalty to the Lê, recognizing progressive Nguyễn political separation and royal status from 1744.
10. **Historical method:** Added source and evidence cautions concerning women, Khmer and Cham populations, the differences between administrative aspirations and actual enforcement, and regional differences.
