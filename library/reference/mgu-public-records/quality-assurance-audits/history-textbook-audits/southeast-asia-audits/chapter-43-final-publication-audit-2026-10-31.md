## Independent historical verification

1. **Cơ Xá dike, 1108:** Supported in Vietnamese historical sources and Vietnamese National Museum of History. Appropriate caveat maintained that the record does not prove a comprehensive hydraulic system. Compare Yumio Sakurai, “The Red River Delta during Lý Dynasty (1010–1225)” (1980), *Southeast Asian Studies*, 18(2), 271–314. https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tak/18/2/18_KJ00000133587/_article/-char/en and https://baotanglichsuquocgia.vn/vi/Articles/2001/65358/thang-long-thoi-nha-ly-1009-1225.html
2. **Vân Đồn, 1149:** The official establishment date is supported by provincial/government heritage materials. Maritime activity existed earlier; the chapter does not conflate the official designation with the origin of trade. https://quangninh.gov.vn/Sites/en-us/Trang/ChiTietTinTuc.aspx?nid=4952 and https://www.ippasecretariat.org/ippa2026/sessions/s02/s02-a1
3. **1092 household census:** The exact claim is **not made in the submitted chapter**. No reason to insert or validate a fact omitted from the manuscript.
4. **Nùng Trí Cao:** Broad chronology and description consistent with James Anderson, *The Rebel Den of Nung Trí Cao* (2007), which documents kingdoms in 1042 and 1048, the 1052 campaign, and defeat in 1053. The chapter treats disputed later details with caution. https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctvcwn5gr
5. **Văn Miếu 1070; first examination 1075; Quốc Tử Giám 1076; Hàn Lâm Viện 1086:** Supported; the education section correctly notes that examination appointments were irregular and did not immediately become a generalized merit bureaucracy. See “Vietnamese Confucianism” (*Asian Studies*, 2020), https://viet-studies.com/ConfucianismVN_AsianStudies2020.pdf (publication details verified from search abstracts; full PDF was not visually audited) and https://www.voer.edu.vn/m/giao-duc-khoa-cu-dai-viet-thoi-ly/6e4626d1
6. **Song war 1075–1077:** Guo Kui and Zhao Xie are grounded in published histories; the commonly given Yongzhou siege duration is 42 days. The original wording erroneously suggested that Song naval support reached the contested crossing. The final text recognizes that river craft/bridging failed to provide dependable crossings, while preserving the Song successes in the northern passes and the Lý defensive and counteroffensive costs. Compare K. W. Taylor, *A History of the Vietnamese* (Cambridge University Press, 2013), chapter 2, https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/a-history-of-the-vietnamese/2269255F3CF32ECD6827399643AB68D3 and https://baotanglichsu.vn/vi/Articles/2001/65720/ly-thuong-kiet-noi-kinh-hoang-cua-quan-tong.html
7. **Quách Bốc crisis:** The original text placed the seizure of Thăng Long in 1208; accounts based on the *Việt sử lược* date it to **1209**. Corrected chapter chronology, §43.34, and §43.35 while retaining the earlier 1207–08 phase of escalating regional unrest. https://scov.gov.vn/ban-sac-van-hoa/viet-su-giai-thoai/cuoc-nao-loan-kinh-thanh-nam-ki-ti-1209-.html
8. **Sùng Thiện Diên Linh stele, 1121:** Correct date at Long Đọi Sơn. The metadata now includes text attributed to Nguyễn Công Bật and calligraphy to Lý Bảo Cung; the paraphrase remains expressly non-verbatim. https://en.nhandan.vn/sung-thien-dien-linh-stele-recognised-as-national-treasure-post23582.html and https://baotangtinh.ninhbinh.gov.vn/bao-vat-quoc-gia/bia-sung-thien-dien-linh-bao-vat-quoc-gia-thoi-ly.htm
9. **Scholar attributions:** Sakurai's decentralization and agrarian variation thesis verified from the published journal abstract. James Anderson's emphasis on Nùng frontier agency verified through his university-press book. Momoki Shiro's regional/maritime research is real; maritime-change interpretation also supported by John K. Whitmore (2006) and Li Tana (2024), https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/maritime-vietnam/maritime-resurgence-and-the-rise-of-dai-viet/0F28317A14DE7AEE8EC18C4DE84BD527.
10. **Lost Hình Thư, 1042; 1010 capital edict; *Nam quốc sơn hà*:** Manuscript explicitly separates surviving later transmissions from contemporary evidence and avoids falsely attributing an authenticated original text or definitive poem authorship.
