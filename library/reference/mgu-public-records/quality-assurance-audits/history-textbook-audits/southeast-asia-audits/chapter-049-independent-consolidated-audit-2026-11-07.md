## Historical verification and direct correction requirements

### 1. Sukamerta charter §49.14 and `#source-sukamerta`: substantive specificity
The draft's description as a loyalty reward / restoration of village status is correct but too generic for a critical primary-source window. National Museum Indonesia documents **11 copper plates, dated 29 October 1296 (1218 Śaka)**, recording a request by **Sang Apañji Patipati Pu Kapat** to restore Sukamerta's *sīma* status, in connection with his father's prior service under Wisnuwardhana and Kertanagara and his own extraordinary loyalty to Wijaya. DHARMA's edited Old Javanese passages at plates **5r2–5r5** identify *sukāmr̥ta*, *sīma*, *saṅ apañji patipati, pu kapat*, and inheritance by descendants. Cite/document specifics. The DHARMA critical edition identifies both **Old Javanese and Sanskrit** in the inscription; avoid unqualified 'Old Javanese only' as a comprehensive language description. Hall's study also notes the connection to a Śaiva shrine and priestly status; treat that as Hall's interpretation, not automatically an uncontested translation.

Sources: https://dharmalekha.info/texts/INSIDENKSukhamerta ; https://repositori.kemendikdasmen.go.id/24363/1/Prasasti%20dan%20raja-raja%20nusantara.pdf (Museum Nasional, 2015, printed p.69); https://www.persee.fr/doc/arch_0044-8613_2000_num_59_1_3554 (Kenneth R. Hall 2000).

### 2. Yuan punishments §49.10 and `#source-yuanshi`: precise differentiation
The manuscript correctly says Shi Bi and Yiheimishi were punished while Gao Xing was treated differently, but it lacks the documented details and differences. *Yuan shi* **juan 162** (Shi Bi) describes **17 strokes** and confiscation of **one-third of his family's property**, later restored; *Yuan shi* **juan 131** (Yiheimishi) explicitly notes confiscation of **one-third of his family's property**, later restored, and links censure to the release of Wijaya. Do not attribute an identical flogging to Yiheimishi solely on the authority of his own biography; if adducing a later account describing both as beaten, identify it as a different text. Gao Xing was praised for warning against the decision. Distinguish the respective biographies and chapter on Java.

Sources: https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=1280013&if=en&remap=gb ; https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=5477754&if=gb ; https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=885513&if=gb&remap=gb (later chronicle).

### 3. Yuan route §49.6 and military-geography feature: avoid obsolete identification
The manuscript refers loosely to 'the Karimata and Belitung area' and groups Tuban and Hujung Galuh as if interchangeable landing regions. Scholarship reconstructs passage by the **Natuna–Tambelan–Karimata** region and **Pulau Gelam/Serutu** (formerly often misidentified as Belitung), then **Karimunjawa**, with a **landing at Tuban** and water-route operations through the Hujung Galuh/Kali Mas area. Chinese name-to-place identifications remain partly debated, so name the grounded itinerary and distinguish confirmed descriptions from hypotheses. Archaeological work (Hung et al. 2022) supports revising the older Belitung convention; do not claim a direct proven precise itinerary from every wreck site.

Sources: Hung et al. (2022), 'Mongol fleet on the way to Java: First archaeological remains from the Karimata Strait in Indonesia', *Archaeological Research in Asia* 29, 100327; *Yuan shi* juan 210; Indonesian study https://repository.ut.ac.id/10017/1/jurnal%20Abad_vol2_no%202_Des_2018.pdf .

### 4. Early royal family and succession §49.19: surface the contested maternity
The text accurately *attributes* to the *Pararaton* the claim that **Jayanegara was the child of Dara Petak**. It needs a brief contrast with **Balawi (1305)** and other inscriptional/literary traditions associating the royal line differently, including Tribhuwaneswari. Do not present the *Pararaton* tradition as a settled modern genealogical conclusion. Compare recent research on the four daughters of Kertanagara and the founder's marriages.

Source: Titi Surti Nastiti, 'Kṛtanagaraduhita: Peranan dan Kedudukan Mereka di Kerajaan Majapahit,' *Amerta* 30(2), 2012; https://garuda.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/documents/detail/4008690 .

### 5. Religious remembrance §49.18: anchor later sources with names and dates
Broad claims about Śaiva/Buddhist patronage are appropriate but vague. Kenneth R. Hall (1996) and John N. Miksic discuss royal **posthumous** ritual networks. Distinguish commemoration of **Kertarajasa at Simping/Sumberjati** in Śaiva/Harihara context and references to Buddhist commemoration at **Antahpura** from any claim that these full institutions already existed under Wijaya during his lifetime. Later Hayam Wuruk-era renovations and images should be dated as later, not contemporaneous with 1293–1309.

Source: https://www.persee.fr/doc/arch_0044-8613_1996_num_52_1_3357 ; research review https://hasp.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/catalog/view/366/517/81624 .
