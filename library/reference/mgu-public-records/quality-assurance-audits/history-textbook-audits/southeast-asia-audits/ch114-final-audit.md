### 3. Historical and documentary corrections

1. Corrected the 1893 French signatory's first name to **Charles-Marie Le Myre de Vilers** in §114.8. The signed treaty is available at [FRUS 1893](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1893/d543).
2. Specified **9 January 1905** as the final French evacuation of Chanthaburi, separating that development from continued French control of Krat/Trat. Primary evidence: [FRUS 1905](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1905/d927).
3. Linked all four source windows to identifiable diplomatic texts or document editions: 1893 treaty, 1896 declaration, 1907 treaty, and 1909 treaty. The 1896 document remains correctly described as an **Anglo-French declaration** to which Siam was not a signatory, not an unconditional international guarantee.
4. Linked the **complete 1909 treaty**, including Article VIII on ratification, alongside the U.S. reproduction that omits the article. Original treaty text: [Complete 1909 treaty](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Treaty_between_the_United_Kingdom_and_Siam_(1909)); reproduction: [FRUS 1909](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1909/d498).
5. Expanded §114.19's comparative chronology, distinguishing signing, ratification, territorial demarcation, military withdrawal, and implementation and emphasizing differences among legal sovereignty and effective political control.
6. The 1907 territorial and jurisdictional provisions remain grounded in [FRUS 1907, Part II](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1907p2/d373).
