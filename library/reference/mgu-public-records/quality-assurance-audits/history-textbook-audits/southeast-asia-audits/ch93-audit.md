## Publication-critical historical and documentary revisions
1. **Riau:** distinguished Raja Haji's 1782–1784 Dutch–Bugis struggle from the **May 1787 Iranun attack**, summoned with Sultan Mahmud's assistance; acknowledged **Dutch return in 1788**, not permanent expulsion in 1787. Contemporary and retrospective Malay/Dutch evidence should still be assessed in specialized editions. See Warren's work on Iranun raiding and archival history of Riau.
2. **Koh Keng We:** corrected publication type and particulars: *Journal of Southeast Asian Studies* **48**, no. **3** (2017), **390–413**, DOI **10.1017/S0022463417000558**; not a standalone monograph.
3. **Penang documentary study:** distinguishes British Library **IOR/G/34/2, pp. 147–296, 1786** from the **USM Light Letters Volume 9 (1785–1791)** and the British Library's **1787 proceedings, pp. 489–528**. Adds linked catalogues and historical source limitations. The **1791 payment of 6,000 Spanish dollars** is described separately, not assigned to a 1786 letter.
4. **Source honesty:** the Penang feature is an attributed documentary summary, **not** an independently transcribed, translated, facsimile-collated, or directly quoted primary source. Do not present it as such.
5. **Bibliography:** eight separately linked source entries with verified publication particulars; selected catalog records and secondary sources cross-checked externally. No fabricated manuscript folio number or page-specific archival quotation inserted.
