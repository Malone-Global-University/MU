## Historical editorial corrections and source policy

1. Preserved and explained Anthony Reid’s c. 1450–1680 Age of Commerce interpretation; distinguished Geoff Wade’s c. 900–1300 prior commercial age and Atsushi Ota’s interpretation of changing state and nonstate trade networks.
2. Explained that the Portuguese conquest (1511) and the VOC takeover (1641) concern **control of Melaka proper**; Aceh and Johor were commercial and military competitors, not successive direct possessors of Melaka.
3. Built commodity-specific explanations of spices, Indian cotton cloth, rice, metals, forest products, labor, customs, and commercial credit.
4. Strengthened the comparison of Aceh, Johor, Patani, Banten, Makassar, Batavia, Ayutthaya, Pegu, and Hội An and the limits of the VOC’s monopoly enforcement.
5. Explicitly rejected the claim that Europeans first introduced Southeast Asia to firearms, drawing on Sun Laichen’s historical study.
6. Rebuilt the Pires `Suma Oriental` source window using the historically documented **four syahbandars** as a specific institutional example; **this is labeled an editorial paraphrase, not a collated quotation**. The quoted source wording was not fabricated.
7. Kept after-1680 commerce differentiated geographically and temporally; did not project the broad later eighteenth-century Chinese-oriented expansion into 1700.
8. Sources register contains ten traceable reference entries with links to publisher, museum, university, or bibliographic records. It does **not** claim comprehensive page-by-page collation of all monographs or original Portuguese/Dutch primary texts.
