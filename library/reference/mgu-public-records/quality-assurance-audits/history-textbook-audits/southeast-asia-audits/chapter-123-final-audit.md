### Influenza demographic interpretation

Chandra's 2013 model estimates a population loss of 4.26–4.37 million in the directly governed Java/Madura study area, using a baseline growth rate around 1.75%. Van der Eng's 2023 working paper challenges that assumption, proposing approximately 1.1% and deriving about 1.47 million deaths in Java in 1918, and an approximate two million excess deaths across Indonesia in 1918–1919. The estimates are competing reconstructions with different definitions, time periods and geographic coverage. They are neither additive nor directly interchangeable. The revision does not manufacture a consensus estimate.
