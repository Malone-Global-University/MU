## Historical revisions and verification

1. **1826 Treaty of Yandabo.** Source Window II now identifies Article II (Assam, Cachar, Jaintia, Manipur), III (Arakan), IV (Tenasserim), V (one-crore-rupee indemnity), VII (envoys and trade), and XI (ratification and prisoners). Treaty provisions are **labeled analytical paraphrases**, not fictitious verbatim transcriptions. British Library catalogue for Aitchison, 5th ed., vol. XII (1931): https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000565019; reproduced treaty digest: https://roadevar.ro/en/news-roadevar/treaty-of-yandabo-24-february-1826.
2. **First War.** Expanded the May 1824 landing, military manpower and supply, Danubyu, campaigns in the northeast, monsoon disease, indigenous experiences, and historiographical uncertainty about casualty denominators and financial costs. National Army Museum https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/first-burma-war; Thant Myint-U, *The Making of Modern Burma* (2001).
3. **1852–1853 annexation.** Separated Dalhousie's annexation decision and December 1852 announcements from the public proclamation dated 20 January 1853 in National Army Museum's history. Historical accounts differ in the labels they assign to the December reading and January announcement; uncertainty is stated instead of suppressing the discrepancy. Military campaign dates: Martaban 5 April; Rangoon 12–14 April; Bassein 19 May; Pegu 3 June; Prome 9 October 1852. https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/second-burma-war.
4. **1879 Mandalay court violence.** Differentiated documented execution of royal relatives from varying totals and unfounded assumptions about personal responsibility; noted Thant Myint-U's documentation of 31 of Mindon's sons and nine daughters among victims. https://www.academia.edu/30017582/The_making_of_modern_burma_PDF.
5. **1885 teak dispute.** Identified the commonly reported corporate penalty of about 23 lakh (2.3 million) rupees as an allegation disputed by interested parties, not a proven assessment of commercial guilt. National Army Museum https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/third-burma-war.
6. **1885 ultimatum and reply.** Corrected the mistaken late-October reply date: ultimatum 22 October; reply required by 10 November; official annual report records receipt at Rangoon on 9 November. Explicitly distinguished drafting, transmission, receipt, and British interpretation. Government of India parliamentary progress report: https://dspace.gipe.ac.in/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10973/17790/GIPE-014389-4.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3.
7. **November 1885 campaign.** Expanded Minhla on 17 November, the Irrawaddy flotilla, the 26 November surrender order, the 28 November British occupation of Mandalay, and Thibaw's subsequent submission. https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/third-burma-war.
8. **Post-1885 resistance.** Expanded the analysis of local political agency, the use of the category “dacoit,” and continuing operations through approximately 1895. Ian F. W. Beckett (2019), DOI 10.1080/09592318.2019.1638552.
9. **Bibliography correction.** Corrected the author of the 1827 *Narrative of the Burmese War* to **John James Snodgrass**, rather than Thomas; Cornell catalogue: https://digital.library.cornell.edu/catalog/sea310. Replaced grouped references with 15 separately identified citations or documentary entries.

## Outstanding limitations / disclosures

- Primary-source windows remain **accurate, clearly labeled paraphrases and document studies**, not claimed verbatim translations of Burmese-language originals. Full collation against original royal orders, manuscript witnesses, and all parliamentary paper pages has **not** been completed.
- The exact provenance of each 1823–1824 frontier letter and each December 1852 proclamation variant is not claimed to have been independently authenticated. The narrative explicitly distinguishes recoverable documentary facts and disputed chronology.
- Historical casualty and cost totals vary by accounting basis. The chapter does **not** present contested estimates as exact findings.
- The three Geography Studies are textual exercises, not embedded cartographic reproductions. Historical map licensing, placement, and captioning remain optional enrichment.
- Several external bibliography destinations are catalogues or search paths; their live accessibility was not exhaustively tested and none should be mistaken for fully digitized archival holdings.
- Exact byte-by-byte identity of CSS with a separately supplied Chapter 105 model was not audited; inherited design tokens, markup classes, original sidebar styling and script behavior were preserved. No live website deployment or institutional review beyond this textual/technical audit is claimed.
