## 3. Verified documentary and historical corrections applied directly
### Shin Sawbu: separate contemporary from later inscription (§60.15)
- Added **Kyaikmaraw I (1455)**, the direct record of land dedicated by ruling queen **Bañā Thau / Shin Sawbu**, identified in H. L. Shorto, “The Kyaikmaraw Inscriptions,” *Bulletin of the School of Oriental and African Studies* **21**, no. 2 (1958): **361–367**, DOI **10.1017/S0041977X00072736**. The specific date **25 September 1455** is reported by Than Tun, *Royal Orders of Burma*, part II (1985), introduction p. x, citing *Mon Inscriptions* (1965), II, p. 60. Shorto distinguishes damaged Kyaikmaraw II from Kyaikmaraw I. The feature identifies its account as an **editorial summary through Shorto**, not an independently collated original-language transcription.
- Separately identified the **1485 Shwedagon inscription erected under Dhammazedi**, written in Mon, Pali, and Burmese, as retrospective royal narration of earlier shrine history and patronage. It does not itself constitute a record issued by the queen in 1455. This distinction is reinforced in the narrative and teaching questions.
- Added both **1455 and 1485** to the chronology. The section now has clear direct vs retrospective source chronology without introducing invented quantities, quotations or monarch-specific claims.
**Evidence:** https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-the-school-of-oriental-and-african-studies/article/abs/kyaikmaraw-inscriptions/3B408434C4315EEAAD8E096E7CAABBF0 ; https://www.shwedagonpagoda.org.mm/en/project/%E1%80%93%E1%80%99%E1%80%B9%E1%80%99%E1%80%85%E1%80%B1%E1%80%90%E1%80%AE%E1%80%80%E1%80%BB%E1%80%B1%E1%80%AC%E1%80%80%E1%80%BA%E1%80%85%E1%80%AC/ ; https://www.myanmar-law-library.org/IMG/pdf/than_tun-1985-royal_orders_of_burma-02-en_bu-red.pdf

## 4. Historical interpretation and chronology reassessment
- Retained the necessary distinction between **Wareru’s Martaban-centered polity** and later **Pegu-centered Hanthawaddy**, and treated “Mon identity” without anachronistically positing ethnic uniformity.
- Kept the 1287 foundation, 1307 accession change, c. 1369 shift toward Pegu, 1380s–1420s Ava wars, c. 1453/1454 Shin Sawbu accession, 1471/1472 Dhammazedi transition and 1492 Binnya Ran II transition as conventional or source-dependent dates.
- Preserved the separation of **the 1470s Kalyani ordination reform** from the **1476–1479** chronology of commemorative inscription activity; no unverified verbatim text was inserted into the Kalyani window.
- Maintained the distinction between the **ancient religious-historical Ramannadesa/Suvannabhumi claims** and the documented history of Mon-speaking communities. Preserved competing scholarly interpretations, including Aung-Thwin, without asserting an unbroken ancient Mon state.
- Kept scope principally c.1280–1500; the later Toungoo conquest is background/cross-reference, not imported into the chapter narrative.

## 6. Disclosed first-edition research limitations
- Three source windows are **explicit editorial paraphrases**, not authenticated original-language quotations. The original Mon and Burmese manuscript witnesses, including the 1910 and 1967 editions, were **not exhaustively collated**. Their identification in the source register is bibliographical, not an assertion that their exact wording was compared manuscript by manuscript.
- Shin Sawbu’s 1455 donation is established through Shorto’s published analysis; this edition does **not** claim direct inspection of the original inscription or independently recover land boundaries, gift amounts, or full textual variants.
- The chapter’s fourteenth-century dates and detailed court narratives often derive from later chronicles, as marked. No embedded historical atlas plates are present; map studies are text-based exercises requiring an external map.
- All 15 bibliography entries are identified publications; this does not mean every cited original volume was read page by page or every document was facsimile collated. No external live-site or publishing-system deployment testing has been performed.
