## Substantive editorial corrections

1. Expanded all twenty narratives, especially Rizal's two novels, the reform program and transnational newspaper network, the origins and limits of assimilation, and the final reformist years. The final narrative occupies the approved 12,000–14,000 range without adding new sections.
2. Clarified that **Elías** in *Noli Me Tangere* is a political interlocutor whose argument cannot accurately be reduced to advocacy of violence as the only possible solution.
3. Incorporated the specific **Schumacher interpretation** in §116.16: after an earlier reformist phase, leading propagandists increasingly contemplated eventual independence after the mid-1880s. This is attributed as Schumacher's interpretation and compared with more cautious readings; it is not presented as an uncontested fact. The precise argument was checked against a contemporaneous published review in *Social Science Information*, 26(1), 1998, p. 66, **not** claimed as a page-specific quotation from Schumacher's book.
4. Distinguished the historical confirmation of **Pío Valenzuela's visit to Dapitan** from the disputed substance of the exchange as described in conflicting statements and retrospective accounts.
5. Clarified the founding of **La Liga Filipina** on July 3, 1892, at a house on Ilaya Street, Tondo, associated with Doroteo Ongjunco; did not invent a precise modern street number.
6. Specified the **1898 mourning decree** as dated December 20, 1898, and the **Rizal Law** as Republic Act 1425, June 12, 1956. Correctly distinguished **Claro M. Recto's authorship** of the Senate initiative from **José P. Laurel's principal legislative sponsorship**.
7. Clarified that the **1893 Maura municipal reform** principally applied to towns of Luzon and the Visayas outside the separate Manila municipal arrangements, and that partial reform did not provide parliamentary representation.
8. Retained supported details concerning *Fray Botod*, Máximo Viola, Valentín Ventura, and publication of the two novels; no unsupported trade or readership statistics were added.
9. Extended source metadata for each primary-source window with a traceable document/edition, retaining proper separation of paraphrase from quotation.
10. Removed the unresolved general editorial placeholder from the bibliography; corrected Schumacher (rev. ed., 1997), the 1979 location of Ileto's book, and the 1998 periodical title. Added references to the 1996 Fundación Santiago edition of *La Solidaridad* alongside the earlier UP Press volume and provided documentary links.

## Disclosure and disposition

This is a consolidated **editorial and technical** audit of a historical textbook chapter, not a representation that every historical sentence has been independently corroborated against unpublished archives or that Schumacher's entire book has been consulted page by page. Primary-source windows intentionally remain *paraphrases* because translated, copyrighted, or edition-dependent wording was not reproduced as unverified quotation.
