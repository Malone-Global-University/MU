## 3. Specific historical corrections and additions

1. **Final-assault chronology:** Retained **7 April 1767** as the conventional Siamese date but now explicitly identifies the different **March 1767** dating in the testimony recorded in 1768. Avoided an unsupported reconciliation.
2. **Boeles's documentary record:** Correctly distinguishes the siege and destruction in 1766–1767, testimony recorded and signed by harbor master P. van der Voort on **26 April 1768**, and J. J. Boeles's **1968** edition/translation. Added attributable paraphrases of the reported encirclement (July/August 1766), night attack with boats and ladders, alleged help from Burmese prisoners inside, destruction of the Dutch company lodge, captivity, escape, and subsequent mobility. Testimony claims are not converted into independently verified facts.
3. **Disputed royal deaths:** Explained Boeles's reservations about identifications and versions of royal deaths; did not supply an invented scene for Ekkathat or Uthumphon.
4. **Siege strategy and geography:** Expanded the northern and western campaigns, the vulnerability of provincial communication, military logistics, the uncertain onset of the encirclement, the annual flood, and the social differentiation of scarcity.
5. **Aftermath and continuity:** Expanded religious, social, commercial, and household consequences and the survival of administrative knowledge, monks, trade practices, regional political authority, and merchant skills. Distinguished losses to built fabric from later abandonment and reuse.
6. **Bibliography:** Added verifiable links to eight listed scholarly or archival sources, including the original published Boeles document. No quotations were invented or attributed to sources not independently checked.
