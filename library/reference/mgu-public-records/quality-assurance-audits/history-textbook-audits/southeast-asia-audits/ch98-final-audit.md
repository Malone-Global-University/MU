## Historical corrections and expansion

1. Corrected Gia Định chronology: Tây Sơn recapture and subsequent Nguyễn loyalist recovery in **1782**, followed by another Tây Sơn offensive and Nguyễn Ánh's flight in **1783**. Reconstructed the region's trade, mobility, river supply, and diverse social alignments.
2. Identified the major Siamese commander, **Krom Luang Thepharirak** (Chiêu Tăng in Vietnamese accounts), while preserving uncertainty over force strength, casualty numbers, and exact tactical reconstructions. The January 1785 outcome remains securely described.
3. Contextualized **22 December 1788** as a source-associated Gregorian conversion for Quang Trung's accession. Clarified the commonly cited **30 January 1789** date for the main Ngọc Hồi–Đống Đa victories and continued to withhold spurious numerical certainty.
4. Re-dated and reattributed the **Chiếu lập học** research discussion to Quang Trung's **early reign, approximately 1788–1789**, with Ngô Thì Nhậm identified as drafter in Nguyễn Thúy Nga's published research. Distinguished this from the **1791 Sùng Chính scholarly institute** and documented the reported 1789 examination activity without assuming universal Nôm-language practice.
5. Distinguished Nguyễn Quang Toản's **1792 succession** from the **1793 start of the Cảnh Thịnh era**; made the **1795 Bùi Đắc Tuyên coup and killing** explicit.
6. Clarified the *constitutional* difference between the northern Trịnh administration's invocation of the Lê imperial name and the southern Nguyễn's increasingly independent claim, particularly after the **1744 royal assumption**.
7. Expanded 20 sections with added treatment of tax collection, local brokers, military logistics, source limitations, social diversity, Cham, Khmer, upland and Chinese communities, legal/religious practice, the uneven reach of reforms, political institutions, household life, competing historical memories, and non-teleological causation.
8. Preserved Chapter 97's Siamese historical perspective as a cross-reference and Chapter 99's Nguyễn unification and subsequent state-building as a clearly defined chapter boundary.
