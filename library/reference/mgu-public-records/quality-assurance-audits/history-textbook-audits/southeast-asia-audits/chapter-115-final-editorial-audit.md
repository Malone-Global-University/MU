## Substantive corrections

1. **Cebu:** July 30, 1860 royal authorization versus port operation in 1863, supported by Philippine Institute for Development Studies account.
2. **Tribute and cédulas:** Tribute abolished 1884; cédulas personales instituted 1885, per Aguilar (2023).
3. **Polo:** Original royal decree signed July 12, 1883 and published July 13 reduced 40 to 15 days, outlawed cash redemption of the remaining service and allowed person-for-person substitution; companion annual provincial levy of 1 peso 50 centimos. Historical accounts sometimes treat 1884 as the local regulatory period. Corrected §115.11, §115.18, glossaries, timeline, and review questions.
4. **Malolos:** 20 women petitioned for Spanish-language instruction December 12, 1888; NHCP-hosted historical account.

6. **Maura Law:** Intended coverage Luzon and Visayas excluding Manila; prospective Jan. 1, 1894 commencement; distinct from actual implementation. Clarified statute versus practice.
