## 4. Primary-source verification

### 4.1 Indonesian proclamation (§146.9)

The prior independent audit identified the archival reproduction in ANRI's presidential archival publication, page 7, Teks Proklamasi I. The consolidated revision inspected ANRI's institutional comparison of the handwritten draft, typed text and later audio account, and linked that comparison and the archival publication. The feature retains the short opening clause in historical orthography with a labeled textbook translation, paraphrases the remaining substantive content, and distinguishes draft, final document, and later recorded reading. Corrected the date formula (expanded, not shortened), the spelling change from *tempoh* to *tempo*, signature formula, and the Japanese imperial-year numbering (2605, not the Shōwa era). The ANRI commentary dates the recording approximately five years later, while RRI identifies 1951. Neither is an original event recording; the discrepancy is disclosed. The interpretation of the transfer-of-authority clause is limited to what the text supports rather than asserting a precise undocumented intention. All five student questions remain.

### 4.2 Vietnamese declaration (§146.12)

The Fordham University Internet Modern History Sourcebook reproduces Hồ Chí Minh's declaration as printed in *Selected Writings* (Hanoi: Foreign Languages Publishing House, 1977), pp. 53–56. The complete online text was inspected directly in this revision. The feature identifies that edition rather than conflating it with *Selected Works*. It uses short excerpts and an explicitly labeled paraphrase, corrects the final quoted clause to match the inspected edition, and distinguishes the 1789 French rights declaration from the 1791 date used in the source. The exercise treats the text as advocacy and does not infer unanimous popular consent or actual territorial control from its claims. All five questions remain. The original Vietnamese facsimile and all other translations have not been independently compared in this pass.

- [Fordham text](https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/mod/1945vietnam.asp)

Both features retain context, creator, document type, interpretation, limitations, perspective, and five questions. The comparative declaration table remains intact. The document-based exercises are suitable for classroom analysis with the specified editions and stated limitations.
