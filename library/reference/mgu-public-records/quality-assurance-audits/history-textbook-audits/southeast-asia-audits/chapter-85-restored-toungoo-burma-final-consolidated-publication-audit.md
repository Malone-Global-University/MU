## Consolidated editorial work completed

1. Preserved the corrections to Nyaungyan's Shan campaigns, Anaukpetlun's 1614–1615 Lan Na intervention, Thalun's accession after the 1628 murder, the Prome chronology variation, the early-1660s Lan Na/Siamese sequence, the Yongli episode, and the 1740–1752 Hanthawaddy crisis.
2. Removed a duplicated succession sentence in §85.5; distinguished Thalun's reign from his frontier intervention in **1631** and distinguished Kaunghmudaw's 1636 commencement from its 1649 completion under Pindale.
3. Added source-grounded substance to eighteen historical sections, raising the direct narrative from about 7,505 words to about 9,600. Expansion centers on protections against official extortion, administrative legal procedure, local headman appointment, service categories and annual registers, village labor, the fiscal-religious boundary, the Irrawaddy river economy, and the historiography of later reigns.
4. Corrected the treatment of **U Kala**: he is presented as an apparently independent compiler, not as a presumed court historian; the continuous narrative of the *Maha Yazawin* ends in **1711**.
5. Retained the 1638 inquest as historically known through later references rather than an extant complete register. The VOC source window explicitly describes an attributed paraphrase of Hall's account of identifiable historical records and does **not** claim a collated original quotation.
6. Added 23 clear working definitions to the front-matter Core Vocabulary Preview and improved the bibliographic source note, without creating new numbered narrative sections.
7. Repaired the actual mobile overflow caused by chronology tables without altering the broader visual template or JavaScript behavior.
