## Revision log and historical verification

1. **Harrison's military order:** §89.15 and Chronology Preview now state **13 November 1890**, separately from troop deployments and arrivals in the latter part of the month. Cross-check: Nebraska State Historical Society, https://history.nebraska.gov/the-military-buildup-to-wounded-knee/ ; James Mooney, *The Ghost-Dance Religion* (1896), documentary notes identifying Harrison's Nov. 13 order, https://www.gutenberg.org/files/74335/74335-h/74335-h.htm .
2. **Spotted Elk's group:** §89.16 and chronology distinguish the **28 December 1890 interception** by Seventh Cavalry from the **29 December massacre**, explicitly reserving the latter's history for Chapter 90. Cross-check: U.S. Senate Committee on Indian Affairs, Senate Report 118-208 (2024), pp. 1–2, https://www.congress.gov/118/crpt/srpt208/CRPT-118srpt208.pdf ; historical report provenance available separately. This modern congressional report is corroborative, not itself an 1890 eyewitness record.
3. **Indian Shaker Church:** §89.17 distinguishes its formal organization at Mud Bay on **7 June 1892** from incorporation under Washington State law in **December 1910**. Cross-check: Washington State Historical Society's archival description presented by SNAC, https://snaccooperative.org/view/35568497 ; HistoryLink, https://www.historylink.org/File/2640 .
4. **Mooney:** chronology and Source Window 1 distinguish his principal 1891–93 investigations from related **1894** song recordings; publisher imprint **1896** retained with the catalogue-year 1897 caveat. The recordings are not treated as original 1890 ceremony performances. Smithsonian/NAA contextual data and Library of Congress item 2014655251.
5. **Everyday Life (§89.12):** replaced undifferentiated hypothetical/synthetic gathering with a **named, dated 1891 Mooney Arapaho ceremonial photograph** in the Smithsonian National Anthropological Archives, NAA INV.00357300 (Photo Lot 24 SPC Plains Arapaho BAE No # Mooney 00357300). The catalogue gives location and year but not participants' individual names or the exact day. Text clearly distinguishes what the photograph actually documents from wider accounts of ceremonial practice. Source: https://www.si.edu/object/archives/components/sova-naa-photolot-24-ref697 . No image is reproduced, avoiding unverified reproduction rights/cultural-protocol concerns.
6. **Source access:** inserted documentary access links in **all five Primary Source Windows**, preserving editorial-paraphrase labeling. The McLaughlin study distinguishes the reported letter, its **published summary in Mooney**, and the **MHS finding aid for papers**, which does *not* itself reproduce the original letter. Updated Source Register now contains **15 entries** and traceable links, with an explicit evidence-provenance note.
7. **Mobile/technical:** added an accessible sticky mobile contents menu copied from the chapter's own actual sidebar anchors, including section and feature navigation. Added horizontal scroll regions around both wide chronology tables. Original page CSS, sidebar layout, and intersection-observer scroll-highlighting are retained.

## Remaining disclosed limitations (not publication blockers)

- Five source windows remain **editorially attributed paraphrases / source studies**, not collated original-language quotations, full original transcriptions, or facsimiles. Published texts or archival descriptions are linked, but catalogues/finding aids are not substitutes for original manuscript inspection.
- Three geography studies remain **text-guided**; no historical atlas/map raster is embedded.
- The 1891 Arapaho case relies on a catalogued photographic record; the archive's ethnic identification was reviewed in 1992. Neither the precise day nor the participants' personal names is specified. Image reproduction and community protocols were not cleared, so the image is **linked rather than embedded**.
- Bibliographic authentication was **selective**; critical-edition page-level, translation, and independent full-archive collations were not undertaken.
- Exact *Chapter 1* source shell/CSS **byte-for-byte parity** could not be proved without an independent archived copy and direct comparison; desktop behavior and visual styling were inspected.
- Live website deployment, external-link click-through from a public server, and live-site path testing remain unverified.
