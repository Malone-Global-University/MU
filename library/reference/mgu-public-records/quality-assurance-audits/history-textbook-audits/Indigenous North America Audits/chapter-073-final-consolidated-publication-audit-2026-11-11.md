### Historical methodology and known areas of uncertainty

- The five source windows are explicitly identified **editorial paraphrases and source interpretations**, not new transcriptions collated against every archival original; the source links facilitate future classroom study with the documents.
- Civilian casualty totals, prison mortality, and some battlefield wounded counts are treated as estimates or source-contingent numbers. The chapter does not turn the reconstructed mortality of Dakota noncombatants into a body count established for each individual.
- Chaska's execution and the associated name confusion are **not** asserted to be proven intentional wrongdoing; the contemporaneous Wakefield letter and later competing explanations are differentiated.
- The direct involvement of participants in particular civilian killings should not be inferred from Dakota ethnicity, the presence of a Dakota soldier in battle, or the commission's problematic conviction rate.
- The chapter does not duplicate the broader 1863–1865 northern Plains campaigns assigned to later chapters.
