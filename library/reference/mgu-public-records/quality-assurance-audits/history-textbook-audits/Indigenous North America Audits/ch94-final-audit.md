All windows are labeled editorial paraphrases, and source-type distinctions are explicit.
2. **Gilder Lehrman's 1920 veteran questionnaire:** a published digitized transcription and source overview were accessed. The original manuscript held in archival collections was not separately examined; no direct quotation is reproduced in the finished text.
3. **Geography studies are text-guided.** Publication may later add original historical atlas maps, but no unverified map has been inserted.
4. **External links were researched and matched to identifiable sources**, but source-provider URL availability may change after publication. The local archive and standalone HTML are fully navigable without a network connection; direct external documentary links naturally require internet access.
5. **Live-site deployment, server response, shared MGU Chapter 1 CSS byte parity, and comparison with the user's deployed website** have not been independently checked. Chromium checks apply to this self-contained chapter, not production hosting.
