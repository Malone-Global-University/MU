## 4. Source-verified claims and judgments

- **Richard Waldron, September 1676:** Dover/Cocheco seizure supported. Waldron’s own September 6 report lists 350 people; later histories distinguish smaller groups sent to Boston and different arrest figures. Claude’s broader “several hundred” remains defensible. Sources: https://indigenousnh.com/2023/03/03/native-retribution-against-maj-waldron/ and https://dover.nh.gov/government/city-operations/library/research-learn/history/the-cocheco-massacre/.
- **Casco Bay agreement, 1699:** January and September 1699 treaty renewals confirmed (alongside 1693 settlement and 1678 agreement). Sources: https://cirnac.gc.ca/eng/1100100028966/1564584576068 and https://data2.archives.ca/rcap/pdf/rcap-182.pdf.
- **Abenaki at Montreal, 1701:** Abenaki explicitly named in the August 4 Great Peace source: https://pacmusee.qc.ca/en/treaty-great-peace-montreal/. Claude’s “some Abenaki and Wabanaki representatives” is not an exact numerical claim.
- **Pequot captives, 1638:** The 80 Mohegan / 80 Narragansett / 20 Eastern Niantic distribution is reported in contemporary historical accounts and Connecticut educational materials, though administration varied; Claude appropriately calls figures approximate and notes incomplete implementation. Sources: https://pequotwar.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Grade-8-Treaty-of-Hartford-Guiding-Questions-Avery.pdf and https://portal.ct.gov/-/media/sde/publications/native-americans/mashantucket/mashantucketpequot_cycle3_lesson07_treatyofhartford_s2_closereading_classwork_lessonplan.pdf.
- **Pequot sachem Tatobem, 1633:** Dutch seizure and killing corroborated by scholarly interpretation: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/godly-violence-in-the-puritan-atlantic-world-16361676/mystic-massacre-1637-divine-slaughter-by-the-hand-of-the-english/637F7329E7A72CD6A7319CFB4510D57C.
- **Carolina / Spain:** Major documented Spanish armed destruction of English Stuart Town was **1686**, not an undifferentiated campaign of Spanish raids in the **1670s**. The latter unsupported clause corrected in §48.43; Spanish disputed frontier retained. Source: https://www.ccpl.org/charleston-time-machine/anglo-spanish-hostility-early-south-carolina-1670-1748.
- **Yamasee migration:** Yamasee began settling near Port Royal in **1683**, with villages by late 1684; Spanish attacks devastated Stuart Town and associated Indigenous towns in 1686. Correct in chronology after removing misleading generalization. Sources: https://sc.edu/study/colleges_schools/artsandsciences/sc_institute_archeology_and_anthropology/documents/sciaa_legacy_vol19no2.pdf and https://nationalregister.sc.gov/SurveyReports/WilltownSM.pdf.
- **Westo War and Maurice Mathews:** War of 1680 and Savannah/Shawnee participation supported. Goose Creek traders supported anti-Westo policy; evidence for attributing personal operational command to Mathews is weaker than evidence for his role among the Goose Creek faction. Final §48.44 distinguishes factional backing and Westo loss of regional power rather than asserting a singular personal command or extinction. Source: https://www.georgiaencyclopedia.org/articles/history-archaeology/westo-indians/.
- **Recognition years:** Narragansett 1983; Mashantucket Pequot 1983; Mohegan 1994; Aquinnah 1987; Mashpee 2007; six Virginia nations via Congress 2018. Pamunkey acknowledgment **determined/published in 2015**, **effective January 28, 2016**. Corrected both §48.47 passages. Sources: https://www.bia.gov/as-ia/ofa/323-pamunk-va and https://pamunkey.org/; https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IG10038.html.
- **Prior five corrections retained:** §48.20 ally reaction at Mystic, §48.39 Treaty of Middle Plantation distinction 20 beaver skins vs three arrows, §48.42 Casco 1678 annual **English-to-Wabanaki** corn payments, §48.43 Santa Elena Spanish abandonment 1587, and mobile chronology CSS.
