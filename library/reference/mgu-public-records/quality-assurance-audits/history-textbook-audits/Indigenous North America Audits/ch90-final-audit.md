## Consolidated correction and historical verification log

1. **December 29 versus December 30:** White Clay Creek/Drexel–Holy Rosary Mission fighting is explicitly dated December 30 and distinguished from the Wounded Knee Massacre. Captain Allyn Capron's name is retained as correct. NYPL finding aid: https://archives.nypl.org/mss/19070 ; historic award citations: https://armyatwoundedknee.com/gallantry-in-action/ .
2. **February 11, 1891 delegation:** §§90.7 and 90.11 and Source Window 2 identify differences among Spotted Horse (claimed firsthand presence as a scout), American Horse (arrived after shooting, inspected scene), Turning Hawk (reported information), and Captain Sword (did not expressly claim to see the first shot in the extract). The distinction between witnessed and reported evidence is made explicit. Full published transcription: https://www.historyisaweapon.com/defcon1/turninghawkmassacrewoundedknee.html ; edited American Yawp extract: https://www.americanyawp.com/reader/17-conquering-the-west/turning-hawk-and-american-horse-on-the-wounded-knee-massacre-1890-1891/ .
3. **Recovery and casualty figures:** NPS-documented Indigenous rescue party reached the field January 1; principal burial operations occurred January 3–4; 146 people were interred in the common grave. A widely repeated breakdown of 84 men and boys, 44 women, 18 children is included with caution that it is not an exhaustive total of Lakota deaths. NPS: https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NHLS/Text/66000719.pdf ; contemporary Congress: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-CRECB-1892-pt2-v23/pdf/GPO-CRECB-1892-pt2-v23-8.pdf .
4. **Dewey Beard and Horn Cloud brothers:** §§90.11 and 90.18 expand the survivor case and explicitly identify the relationships among Dewey Beard, Joseph Horn Cloud and Daniel White Lance, with limitations of retrospective Ricker recordings. History Nebraska: https://history.nebraska.gov/eli-ricker-wounded-knee-interviews/ .
5. **Memorial dates:** §90.18 identifies a Wounded Knee Survivors Association dated **1901** in a Smithsonian National Museum of the American Indian account. NPS records a **May 28, 1903** monument dedication, though Smithsonian's later retrospective lists **1905** as an erection date. The discrepancy is acknowledged without pretending both publications say the same thing. Smithsonian: https://www.americanindianmagazine.org/sites/default/files/2018-01/NMAI-SUMMER-2007.pdf ; NPS above.
6. **Medals of Honor:** §90.20 distinguishes historical totals from the **19 Wounded Knee-specific awards** reviewed in 2024–2026; two White Clay Creek recipients were excluded, an additional Wounded Knee recipient added. The 3–2 reviewer split, Secretary Hegseth's September 25, 2025 retention decision, and Senate report's requested **February 1, 2027** disclosure are stated separately. Panel report: https://southdakotasearchlight.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Wounded-Knee-Medal-Report.pdf ; Senate Committee report: https://www.armed-services.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/fy27_ndaa_report_language_committee_filing_version.pdf .
7. **Sacred site:** The **December 19, 2025 Wounded Knee Massacre Memorial and Sacred Site Act (Public Law 119-61)** directs restricted-fee status for approximately forty jointly owned Oglala Sioux/Cheyenne River Sioux acres, subject to tribal covenant and other statutory provisions. The statutory deadline is 365 days, not evidence that implementation was already complete as of the cutoff. Statute: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ61/html/PLAW-119publ61.htm .
8. **Historical argument and source provenance:** Added contextual explanation of troop placement, artillery, civilian flight, evidence limits, institutional inquiries, archival photographs, and historical terminology. Greene and Grua appear as scholarly bibliographic resources, not purported independently inspected quotations or exact page-level attributions.

## Disclosed limitations (not publication blockers)

- Five source windows are attributed editorial **paraphrases/source studies**, not verified original manuscript facsimiles or full diplomatic transcriptions; direct access to select published records is linked.
- No sensitive historical imagery is embedded. The historical photographs and archival descriptions are linked for students to consult under their originating institutions' rules and cultural protocols.
- Three geography studies are text-guided; no historical map raster or first-hand field survey was commissioned.
- Published sources differ on the precise memorial construction/dedication year. NPS 1903 and Smithsonian 1905 have been identified; a critical-edition reconciliation would require additional archival collation.
- Historians Greene and Grua were not independently collated by chapter page. This first edition avoids invented exact quotes or page-specific claims from their books.
- Chapter 1 HTML shell/CSS byte-for-byte parity was not independently audited, but the supplied theme and approved Chapter 89 responsive behavior are preserved.
- No live-site deployment or public-server path test was performed. Links were selected against identifiable archives, documents and publications, without exhaustive manual click-through from a deployed host.
- No later developments after November 15, 2026 are asserted, and implementation of the 2025 land-status law has not been independently verified complete.
