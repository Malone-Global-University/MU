## 3. Independent factual verification — sampled targeted claims

1. **Champlain, Tadoussac 1603 and Savignon 1610–1611:** supported by the *Dictionary of Canadian Biography* (Champlain biography). The 1603 Innu/Algonquin/Etchemin meeting is accurately distinguished from later alliances. Savignon's 1610 visit to France and 1611 return are supported.
2. **Brébeuf 1637 advice:** genuine source, *Instructions for the Fathers of our Society Who Shall Be Sent to the Hurons* in the 1637 *Jesuit Relations*. Chapter's paraphrase broadly corresponds to source; it is correctly distinguished from a direct quotation.
3. **Wendat dispersal and Wendake:** the successive refuge settlements and final Jeune-Lorette/Wendake location in **1697** are corroborated by Parks Canada, Quebec heritage records, and Huron-Wendat sources. No change warranted.
4. **Saint-Lusson, 1671:** numerically divergent French sources are real: the *Jesuit Relations* describe 14 nations at the June assembly, while Document 1 of *Edge of Empire* is dated **May 16, 1671** and describes 17 represented nations. **Additional date conflict:** the 1671 Jesuit narrative and *Dictionary of Canadian Biography* use **June 4** for the ceremony; some later secondary histories and local museums give **June 14**. The original uploaded §45.25 stated June 14 as unqualified fact. Corrected to **“In June 1671”** and added explicit date qualification. Preserve the distinction between the 16 May document and the June ceremony.
5. **Michilimackinac:** *Edge of Empire* is a genuine documentary edition comprising 61 translated French-language archival records. It contains **Document 1, the Saint-Lusson act (16 May 1671, pp. 1–2)**, but that act concerns Sault Sainte-Marie rather than a specific Michilimackinac council. Corrected §45.26 metadata to name the document and its limits. The feature remains an **Evidence Study**, not a false quotation or unattributed Primary Source Window; it asks students to select another identifiable document from the edition.
6. **Kondiaronk 1688:** episode is documented, but must not be made the single cause of war; chapter's qualified interpretation is defensible (DCB Kondiaronk and Lahontan entries).
7. **Fort Pontchartrain:** **July 24, 1701** established; source Detroit Historical Society. The chapter's date is correct.
8. **Great Peace of Montreal, August 4, 1701:** date and role of Indigenous diplomacy supported; treaty arrangements and neutrality should not be simplified as an unconditional surrender. Chapter's treatment is appropriate as a high-level synthesis.

This verification targeted the flagged and consequential claims; it is not a claim that every historical assertion in the 21,860-word HTML has been independently reconciled to all primary literature. Claims about Wendat population and mortality are properly presented as uncertain.
