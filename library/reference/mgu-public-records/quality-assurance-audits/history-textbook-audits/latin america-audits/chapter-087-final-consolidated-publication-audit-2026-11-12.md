## 3. High-consequence historical audit and corrections

The completed chapter and source apparatus were checked selectively against identified contemporary legal, electoral, intelligence, diplomatic, journalistic, human-rights and judicial evidence. The following material distinctions were incorporated:

1. **Uruguay's June 1973 regime:** Bordaberry participated in the dissolution; Decree 464/973 is presented as the legislative rupture without misattributing separate censorship provisions to its own text. The CNT general strike and the Tupamaros' earlier defeat are independently contextualized. **1980 referendum:** official Court of Elections figures are distinguished as 945,176 No and 707,118 Yes. Sources: [IMPO decree](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/decretos/464-1973), [Uruguayan Electoral Court](https://www.gub.uy/corte-electoral/comunicacion/publicaciones/plebiscito-constitucional-1980).
2. **Paraguay's distinctive chronology:** 1954 takeover, Colorado Party and military patronage, 1967 Constitution and 1977 Article 173 amendment, 1976 repression of Christian Agrarian Leagues, Itaipu, and February 1989 intra-regime overthrow are distinguished from the later Condor alliance. Sources: [constitutional compilation](https://www.ipdc.org.py/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/CN_1967-.pdf), [Paraguay's National Secretariat of Culture](https://cultura.gov.py/2011/05/la-pascua-dolorosa-de-1976/).
3. **Bolivian regime sequence:** differentiates the 1964, 1971, 1978–1980 and 1980–1982 political periods, including Banzer's state-centered economic features, the García Meza coup and restoration of civilian rule on 10 October 1982; does not claim a single continuous dictatorship or treat a **1981 IACHR report as an on-site inspection**. Sources: [Bolivia national statistical office](https://www.ine.gob.bo/index.php/bolivia/aspectos-historicos/), [IACHR investigation](https://hrlibrary.umn.edu/iachr/country-reports/bolivia1981-intro.html).
4. **Condor founding chronology:** October 1975 invitation is distinct from the 25–28 November meeting and the 28 November minutes. The five founding participants are listed separately from Brazil's observation/later collaboration and Peru/Ecuador's subsequent involvement. Sources: [invitation](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/32168-document-11-dina-letter-invitation-dina-director-manuel-contreras-paraguayans-attend), [minutes](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/21754-document-01).
5. ***Teseo* documentary chronology:** the proposed September 1976 operations regulation is differentiated from its reproduction in a 16 August 1977 CIA report. Written planning is **not** presented as direct proof that all proposed assassination missions occurred. Source: [National Security Archive](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/33628-document-10-cia-cable-text-september-1976-agreement-condor-countries-regulating).
6. **Assassination categories:** the September 1974 Carlos Prats/Sofía Cuthbert murders **predate Condor's formal establishment**; the September 1976 Letelier–Moffitt murders are described as a DINA operation with international facilitation rather than casually grouped with every bilateral Condor operation. The 1976 Buenos Aires killings of Michelini, Gutiérrez Ruiz, Barredo and Whitelaw and the Automotores Orletti detainee-transfer network receive separate, source-grounded treatment.
7. **Disputed May 1976 body-discovery date:** published institutional accounts disagree between **20 and 21 May** (including commemorative and investigative sources). The prose now states **“in late May”**, avoiding an unsupported false precision. See [Parque de la Memoria](https://parquedelamemoria.org.ar/) and [Uruguay's diplomatic note catalog](https://sitiosdememoria.uy/archivo-estatal/smdc-uymrree-000029).
8. **1992 Paraguayan Archives of Terror:** record discovery on 22 December 1992 is not described as the *beginning* of knowledge of Condor; it is presented as a major strengthening of already existing investigative evidence. A police dossier's authenticity is distinguished from the truth of claims made inside it. Source: [Paraguayan judiciary](https://www.pj.gov.py/contenido/698-archivos-del-terror-20-anos/698).
9. **Bibliographic corrections:** the second Francesca Lessa book is now identified with its publisher's 2013 book page; all thirty entries have linkable reference leads. See [Palgrave / Springer book record](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1057/9781137269393).

**Verification scope:** These are targeted external checks and manuscript corrections, **not exhaustive independent facsimile collation or full bibliographic catalog auditing of every edition**. The document windows are editorial summaries with their authoring method disclosed, not reproduced first-hand transcriptions. No novel direct primary-source quotations were fabricated.
