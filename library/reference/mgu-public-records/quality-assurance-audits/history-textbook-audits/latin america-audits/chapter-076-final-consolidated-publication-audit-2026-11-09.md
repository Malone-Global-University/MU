## 3. Integrated historical and documentary corrections

### 3.1 Women's suffrage and voting age — §76.4
- Corrected an additional factual error in the initial draft: the **1932 Electoral Code did not lower the voting age to twenty-one**. Its minimum voting age was twenty-one; the **1934 Constitution reduced it to eighteen**.
- Replaced the assertion that the 1932 Code itself explicitly required a married woman to obtain her husband’s permission. The Code’s enacted voter definition recognized registered electors **without distinction of sex**; civil-status constraints, administrative practices, and practical access are separately identified rather than falsely inserted into Article 2.
- Added the 1934 Constitution’s distinction concerning compulsory voting: mandatory for men and for women in remunerated public service, not mandatory for every woman.
- Aligned the Electoral Code and 1934 Constitution source-window paraphrases and added links to the original text.

**Primary verification:**
- [Brazilian Chamber of Deputies, Decree No. 21,076 of 24 February 1932](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/decret/1930-1939/decreto-21076-24-fevereiro-1932-507583-publicacaooriginal-1-pe.html).
- [Brazilian Chamber of Deputies, Constitution of 1934, arts. 108–109](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/consti/1930-1939/constituicao-1934-16-julho-1934-365196-publicacaooriginal-1-pl.html).
- [Tribunal Superior Eleitoral, Voto da mulher](https://www.tse.jus.br/servicos-eleitorais/glossario/termos/voto-da-mulher).

### 3.2 Indigenous administration — §76.12
Replaced the vague statement about the Indian Protection Service (SPI) shifting among ministries with verified legal chronology: **12 July 1934** transfer to the Ministry of War (Decree No. 24,700), and **1 January 1940** effective transfer to the Ministry of Agriculture (Decree-Law No. 1,736, issued 3 November 1939). This administrative distinction does not imply that Indigenous peoples enjoyed secure land rights or comprehensive federal protection.

**Primary verification:** [1934 transfer decree](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/decret/1930-1939/decreto-24700-12-julho-1934-519729-publicacaooriginal-80372-pe.html); [1939 decree-law](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/declei/1930-1939/decreto-lei-1736-3-novembro-1939-411705-publicacaooriginal-1-pe.html).

### 3.3 Brazil’s World War II legal chronology — §76.16
Clarified that Brazil recognized belligerency against Germany and Italy on **22 August 1942**, whereas **Decree No. 10,358 of 31 August 1942** declared a **domestic state of war throughout Brazilian territory and suspended enumerated constitutional guarantees**. The decree’s operative provisions do not identify enemy governments; it must not be offered as the only instrument proving the preceding diplomatic action. Updated the narrative, source-window title, context, and analysis.

**Primary verification:** [Chamber of Deputies, Decree 10,358](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/decret/1940-1949/decreto-10358-31-agosto-1942-467907-publicacaooriginal-1-pe.html); publication in *Diário Oficial da União*, 1 September 1942, p. 13,413.

### 3.4 FEB fatalities — §76.16
Retained **approximately 450** personnel as the responsible approximate tally but explicitly disclosed that published totals differ depending on service and mortality scope. The number is a rounded teaching figure, *not* an asserted single precise official total. The verification notes identify the Brazilian diplomatic-historical source collection.

**Comparative documentary lead:** [Fundação Alexandre de Gusmão / Itamaraty, FEB study](https://funag.gov.br/biblioteca-nova/pdf/mostraPdf/1/751/itamaraty_e_a_forca_expedicionaria_brasileira_feb%3A_o_legado_da_participacao_do_brasil_na_segunda_guerra_mundial_como_ativo_de_politica_externa_o).

### 3.5 *Carta-testamento* provenance — §76.20
Distinguished the **publicly circulated, broadcast typescript** from the **surviving earlier handwritten draft** preserved among Vargas’s papers. Acknowledged differing testimony on the drafting or editorial assistance of **José Soares Maciel Filho**. The famous concluding sentiment is attributed to the circulated testament rather than presented as a transcription certified against an uncontested Vargas holograph. Aligned both the narrative and the Primary Source Window, with direct archival provenance leads.

**Verification:** [FGV Atlas documentary discussion](https://atlas.fgv.br/verbete/5759); [FGV photograph/archive record of an earlier handwritten version](https://atlas.fgv.br/marcos/segundo-governo-vargas-1951-1954/midias/versao-anterior-da-carta-testamento-de-getulio-vargas).

## 5. First-edition limitations and publication classification

1. The nine Primary Source Windows use **labeled editorial paraphrases**, not fully collated verbatim original-language excerpts. Their existence does not establish that every archival facsimile or published edition was consulted in full.

5. Exact historical casualties, particularly FEB losses, can vary by reporting category. The chapter acknowledges rather than conceals the remaining measurement limits.
