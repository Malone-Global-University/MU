## II. Verified historical findings

1. **Zambada**: arrested July 25, 2024; guilty plea August 25, 2025; life sentence July 20, 2026. The July sentence is confirmed by the U.S. Attorney, Eastern District of New York: https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/co-founder-sinaloa-cartel-ismael-el-mayo-zambada-garcia-sentenced-life-prison-engaging .
2. **Joaquín Guzmán López**: guilty plea **December 1, 2025**, reported by DOJ December 2, 2025. DOJ reports that he **stipulated to an international kidnapping offense**; do not state that he was convicted of a separate kidnapping count if his plea counts were drug conspiracy and continuing criminal enterprise. https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndil/pr/joaquin-guzman-lopez-son-el-chapo-and-head-sinaloa-cartel-pleads-guilty-federal-drug .
3. **Mexican sovereignty investigation**: Mexico's FGR public statement of July 8, 2026 documents inquiry into the alleged July 2024 seizure/transfer and contested circumstances. U.S. participation should remain a disputed question, not a finding. https://fgr.org.mx/es/FGR/Nacional/_rid/61/_mod/story?categoria=Nacional&f=0&ord=desc&p=1&suri=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.FGR.swb%23fgr_Boletin%3A26954 .
4. **February 2025 terrorism designations**: U.S. FTO notice under INA section 219 is **2025-02873**, and separate SDGT notice under EO 13224 is **2025-02870**, both published February 20, 2025 (90 FR 10030). The single source window currently confounds their distinct statutory bases and should cite both explicitly. FTO: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/02/20/2025-02873/foreign-terrorist-organization-designations-of-tren-de-aragua-mara-salvatrucha-cartel-de-sinaloa ; SDGT: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/02/20/2025-02870/specially-designated-global-terrorist-designations-of-tren-de-aragua-mara-salvatrucha-cartel-de-sinaloa . The alias *Los Zetas* in the notice belongs under *Cártel del Noreste* and is a U.S. legal treatment of names, not proof that the historical Zetas and every CDN faction are one continuous organization.
5. **Arellano Félix cases**: Ramón Arellano Félix killed in police shootout in Mazatlán February 2002; Benjamín arrested March 9, 2002; Francisco Javier captured 2006; Eduardo arrested October 25, 2008. Supporting U.S. sources: https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/last-arellano-felix-brothers-sentenced and https://www.justice.gov/archive/opa/pr/2002/March/02_ag_135.htm .
6. **Los Zetas and Gulf split**: 2010 rupture supported by federal court documents; Lazcano died October 2012, Miguel Treviño Morales arrested July 2013. Treat military training and number of original recruits as estimates and distinguish formal GAFE membership from broader former military/police recruitment. DOJ case source: https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/media/1373606/dl .
7. **San Fernando**: 72 migrants killed in 2010, 193 bodies found in 2011 mass graves. The 2011 body-identification effort was incomplete; do not assume all 193 were migrants. Amnesty: https://www.refworld.org/reference/annualreport/amnesty/2012/en/85553 .
8. **Allende**: precise 2011 death/disappearance count is disputed; avoid a fixed undocumented toll. **Petén**: UN special procedures cite 27 agricultural workers found murdered May 15, 2011, with suspected Zetas responsibility. https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/721892/files/A_HRC_19_44-EN.pdf .
9. **Casino Royale**: Mexican SEDENA attributed the August 25, 2011 attack to Zetas-linked suspects; Mexican CNDH records **52 deaths**. https://www.gob.mx/defensa/prensa/sedena-detiene-a-carlos-oliva-castillo-la-rana-encargado-del-grupo-delictivo-los-zetas .
10. **Wachovia/HSBC**: Wachovia settled for **$160 million in March 2010**, of which $110 million was forfeiture plus a $50 million fine; $420 billion refers to broader inadequately monitored exchange-house transactions, **not** an adjudicated amount of drug money. HSBC resolved related U.S. violations in December 2012 with penalties exceeding $1.9 billion. https://www.justice.gov/archive/usao/fls/PressReleases/2010/100317-02.html and https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/tg1799 .
11. **Operation Casablanca**: contemporaneous Treasury release May 18, 1998, documented indictments, bank officials, and seizures; link and use exact dated figures rather than relying on prose alone. https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/rr2452 .
12. **Posadas Ocampo (1993) and Colosio (1994)**: historical significance is real, but the chapter should not imply an adjudicated cartel explanation for either contested killing. Maintain evidentiary distinctions and do not let these events displace cartel organizational analysis.
