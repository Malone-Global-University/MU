## III. Other publication-critical historical corrections completed

1. **UN disappearance procedure:** distinguish initiation of Article 34 review in March 2025 from committee decision of **17 April 2026** (CED/C/MEX/A.34/D/1). Treaty-body action is not a criminal conviction or categorical finding of responsibility in every case.
2. **RNPDNO:** precisely identify the Mexican government's March 27, 2026 snapshot: 132,534 historical entries since 1952; 130,178 with missing status for records since 2006; 46,742 incomplete, 40,308 showing subsequent administrative activity (not proof of life), 43,128 without later administrative matches. Criticism and methodological limits retained.
3. **Mérida appropriations:** identify initial 2008 Public Law 110-252 funding and the CRS approximate **$3.6 billion** of broader security cooperation FY2008–FY2023, including successor programs; avoid falsely labeling all spending Mérida-only.
4. **Tlatlaya:** cite CNDH **Recommendation 51/2014**, date October 21, 2014; do not conflate human-rights findings with individual convictions.
5. **Ayotzinapa:** retain independent forensic identifications of three students and ongoing unresolved fate of the others; remove duplicate Mexican Revolution wording and preserve distinctions between investigative findings and accusations.
6. **Teuchitlán:** mark claims about a ranch's purposes, objects, and human remains as contested and dependent on specific forensic investigation.
7. **2025–2026 U.S. security actions:** CRS R48859 records six Mexican FTO designations in February 2025, two in July 2026 and 92 suspects/traffickers transferred into U.S. custody by January 2026; transfer is not synonym for court-ordered extradition or conviction.
8. **El Mencho operation (Feb. 22, 2026):** distinguish Mexican military execution from U.S. intelligence support; retain distinct treatment of the CJNG's organizational history for Chapter 104.
9. **Sheinbaum security reporting:** contrast SESNSP 86.9 daily average in September 2024, 40.8 in August 2026, and **43.4 in October 2026 (reported November 10)** with INEGI's death-registration series. No unwarranted causal claim drawn from temporal movement alone.
10. **Division scope:** original Chapter 102 / 103 / 104 boundaries retained; modest evidence-based expansion in §§103.6, 103.18, and 103.20.
