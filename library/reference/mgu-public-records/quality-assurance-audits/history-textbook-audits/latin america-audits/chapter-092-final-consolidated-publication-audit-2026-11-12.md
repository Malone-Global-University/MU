## Historical factual corrections applied
- Election conducted **7 May 1989**; annulled by Electoral Tribunal Decree No. 58 **10 May 1989** (IACHR contemporary account).
- Civilian deaths presented as source-dependent **U.S. Southern Command 202** versus **Americas Watch approximately 300**, without creating a false universal total.
- **23 U.S. military fatalities** kept distinct from Panamanian civilian fatalities and other casualty categories.
- **UN General Assembly Resolution 44/240** dated 29 December 1989, adopted **75–20–40**, distinguished from binding judicial decisions.
- **IACHR Report 121/18** integrated as later findings concerning civilian casualties, property, precaution and international responsibility, with U.S. objections acknowledged.
- **14 October 1977 neutrality-treaty interpretive statement** introduced to distinguish defense of canal transit from domestic intervention.
- **Noriega convicted 9 April 1992 on eight counts**, not conflated with 1988 indictments or used to validate intervention retrospectively.
- Military defeat in December 1989 distinguished from the **1994 constitutional abolition** and **31 December 1999 canal transfer**.
- Historic 1964 deaths, contested 1984 election, Spadafora accountability, alleged Torrijos-crash conspiracy, U.S. cooperation and military-civilian issues treated with evidentiary qualifications.
