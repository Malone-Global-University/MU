## Historical verification and corrections applied

1. **1943 vs. 1948:** Preserved the 1943 hydrocarbons-law distinction; identified the 1948 fifty–fifty income-tax reform as approved November 11 and dated November 12, preceding the November 24 coup. Supported by *Oil Policies and Budgets in Venezuela, 1938–1968* (Latin American Research Review): https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/0D89BAD97329944206932B468C217C8E/S0023879100026200a.pdf/oil_policies_and_budgets_in_venezuela_19381968.pdf .
2. **Sierra Nevada:** Replaced erroneous “Congress censured Pérez in 1979” with the **May 1980** congressional *political responsibility* finding, not a criminal conviction. Source: https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/_custom/static/cronologia_hv/zoom/s20-10/1980-9.html .
3. **1986 bank debt:** Identified the **February 26, 1986** restructuring of approximately **US$21.2 billion in existing public-sector foreign bank debt**, not total external indebtedness or newly loaned dollars. Contemporaneous: https://www.upi.com/Archives/1986/02/26/Venezuela-signs-debt-pact-warns-Latin-nations-will-need-help/1885509778000/ .
4. **Currency system:** Corrected 1964–1983 fixed-rate account: mid-1960s around Bs 4.45/US$, later Bs 4.30/US$; February 1983 controls and differentiated rates. Source: https://manifold.bfi.uchicago.edu/read/a-monetary-and-fiscal-history-of-latin-america-1960-2017/section/d2c8d793-bd56-4e90-bd22-5d07377b372a/resource/a3111bce-c75f-44ae-a059-f89094a7d5d6 .
5. **1989 IMF chronology:** Differentiated government policy announcement of **February 16**, IMF first-credit-tranche approval **March 23**, and EFF authorization **June 23, 1989**, avoiding an assertion that full IMF approval preceded February 27. World Bank: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/589951468130819872/pdf/multi0page.pdf .
6. **Caracazo emergency powers:** Dated Decree No. 49 to **February 28, 1989**, partial restoration via Decree No. 67 on **March 8**, final restoration **March 22**; specified major categories of suspended guarantees. Source: https://cofavic.org/espacio-memoria-y-verdad/caracazo/hechos-generales/ and court proceedings.
7. **La Peste:** Corrected the mistaken implication of grave discovery in the immediate aftermath; judicial inspections **November 5, 1990**, exhumations began **November 13**, and the public findings followed later that month. Inter-American Court, 2002 reparations decision §§66.7–66.8: https://hrlibrary.umn.edu/iachr/C/95-esp.html .
8. **Caracazo death counts:** Retained 276 as a **commonly cited official tally**, not a definitive total; separated official count, disputed higher estimates, exhumations, and cases judicially established. The Court did not report an exhaustive national death figure.
9. **Chronology preview:** Placed Gómez's 1908–1935 dictatorship before the 1914 Zumaque row, instead of out of sequence.

## Three quantitative studies — provenance

- **Study I — petroleum exports and government revenue, 1921–1960:** Selected rows from IBRD / World Bank *Economic Position and Prospects of Venezuela* (June 20, 1961), Statistical Appendix **Table 8**. Outputs are percent shares and oil output in million barrels, with 1960 marked as estimated, and 1957 special concession-revenue caveat explicitly noted. https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/779831468129884490/pdf/multi0page.pdf .
- **Study II — prices, debt, exchange:** Dated nominal crude benchmarks based on IMF International Financial Statistics compilation, clearly marked series switch to average crude price in 1986; independent exchange-rate overview; accurately scoped 1986 bank-refinancing amount. The tables are **not** a single consistent time series or comprehensive model of economic performance. See exact linked documents in the HTML.
- **Study III — Caracazo record comparison:** Source-population and date distinctions; separates the official cited 276 baseline, later unstandardized allegations, 1990 burial/exhumation records, and adjudicated victims. The numerical row values should never be summed or claimed as comprehensive mortality.
