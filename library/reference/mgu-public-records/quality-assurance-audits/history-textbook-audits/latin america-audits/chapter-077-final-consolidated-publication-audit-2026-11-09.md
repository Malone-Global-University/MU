## 3. Completed factual and documentary corrections

1. **§77.1 — Roca–Runciman Agreement.** Reversed trade direction corrected: the 1 May 1933 treaty concerned **British imports of Argentine chilled beef**, with conditions tied to a 1932 benchmark, in exchange for Argentine commercial and foreign-exchange concessions. The original formulation incorrectly spoke of “Argentine beef purchases” by Britain as if Argentina were the purchaser. Official treaty: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-11693-293697/texto . Comparative scholarly research: https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10179560/1/McCulloch_ROCA-RUNCIMAN%20REVISITED%2C%201933-43%20-%20FINAL%20DRAFT.pdf .
2. **§77.6 — CGT general secretary chronology.** Luis Gay's 1946–1947 tenure corrected; **Aurelio Hernández** briefly replaced him, followed by **José Espejo** from late 1947 to 1952 and **Eduardo Vuletich** thereafter. Gay's removal is interpreted as a contested exercise of government influence, not an automatic leadership succession. Contemporary/retrospective source: Marysa Navarro, *Evita*, publicly accessible at https://pergamo.legislatura.gob.ar/pgmedia/biblioteca/LIBROS%20DIGITALIZADOS/036285_D.pdf . Additional institutional/union history: https://www.upcndigital.org/files/publicaciones/3/250-EscenariosN%C2%B020-ElEstadoArgentinoentreBicentenarios.pdf .
3. **§77.13 — The Constitution of 1949.** Article 37 recognized workers' rights including professional association, **but did not expressly constitutionalize a right to strike**. The main narrative, the source-window commentary and one primary-source question were synchronized. 1949 printed constitutional text: https://bcn.gob.ar/uploads/constitucion1949.pdf . Specialist historical study: https://trabajosycomunicaciones.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/article/download/TyCe105/11789 .
4. **§77.15 — Election category.** Two personal **presidential** victories for Perón (1946 and 1951), not three; the April 1954 **vice-presidential** election for Alberto Teisaire is identified separately, without introducing a false third presidential contest. See a reproduced contemporary statistical study at https://cdi.mecon.gob.ar/bases/docelec/fc1435.pdf (national counts differ across later compilations; the manuscript does not introduce disputed fine totals).
5. **§77.18 — Women's participation in the 1951 presidential election.** Replaced the false statement that male/female returns cannot be separated. The chapter now reports **3,816,654 women voting** and **2,441,558 female Peronist ballots** (about **64% of women who voted**, not 64% of the entire Peronist vote). Female turnout is described as approximately 90%, with acknowledgment of differences among tabulations and denominators. Documentary educational study referencing Carolina Barry: https://museoevita.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Propuesta-didactica-Museo-Evita.pdf ; official context: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/voto-femenino-historia-y-acciones .
6. **§77.19 — June 16, 1955 bombing.** Replaced an indefensible superlative with an accurate minimum of **at least 309 fatalities** from the **bombing and related armed fighting**, and described the substantial civilian losses without minimizing earlier mass killings in 1919 and Patagonia in 1920–1921. Archive finding aid: https://catalogo.jus.gob.ar/index.php/ar-anm-b1955%3Bisad?sf_culture=es .
7. **§77.20 — September 19 message and claimed resignation.** Explained that Perón sent a withdrawal/negotiation message to Army Minister Franklin Lucero, senior officers treated it as a resignation, and its precise constitutional and documentary status is contested; no uncomplicated formal resignation is asserted. Archival/diplomatic background: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1955-57v07/d192 . Research on the military interpretation: https://perio.unlp.edu.ar/sistemas/biblioteca/files/Diaz_Cynthia__Rodolfo-Walsh-trama-y-tensin-_online.pdf .
