### 3. Consolidated historical verification and editorial results

The manuscript distinguishes resistance to dictatorship from the national structure of the military regimes covered in Chapters 84–87, from the full Central American war narratives assigned to Chapter 91, and from post-authoritarian justice assigned to Chapter 94. It treats religious collaboration/accommodation alongside religious opposition; distinguishes Mothers from Grandmothers; recognizes Indigenous organizations as independent political actors; and does not claim international human-rights advocacy alone ended the dictatorships.

Targeted verified points include:

- **Mothers of Plaza de Mayo:** Saturday 30 April 1977 first gathering of fourteen women; Thursday marches developed later. Argentina's Secretariat for Human Rights institutional history: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/las-madres-de-plaza-de-mayo-cumplieron-45-anos-de-lucha
- **Romero:** 23 March 1980 Sunday homily, followed by his assassination on 24 March; not conflated with his final shorter homily on 24 March. Archbishop Romero Trust: https://www.romerotrust.org.uk/1980-homilies/the-church-serves-personal-communal-and-transcendent-liberation/
- **Vicaría de la Solidaridad:** formally active from 1 January 1976 following the ecumenical Committee for Peace. Chilean institutional records: https://www.vicariadelasolidaridad.cl/noticias/enero-de-1976-nace-la-vicaria-de-la-solidaridad
- **Bolivian mining hunger strike:** the original four initiators in December 1977—Aurora de Lora, Nelly de Paniagua, Luzmila de Pimentel and Angélica de Flores—are separately identified; Domitila Barrios de Chungara is not falsely placed among the initial four. Bolivian electoral authority historical collection: https://web.oep.org.bo/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/El_Camino_de_las_Democracias_2022.pdf
- **Brasil: Nunca Mais:** 707 judicial proceedings (complete and incomplete), approximately 900,000 copies, 543 microfilm reels, with the archival distinction between copied documents, collection and 1985 publication. UNICAMP Arquivo Edgard Leuenroth: https://ael.ifch.unicamp.br/guia-acervo?title=Nunca
- **Indigenous assemblies:** CIMI's 1972 foundation and April 1974 Diamantino first assembly; leader and community agency emphasized. CIMI historical educational publication: https://www.cimi.org.br/pub/MS/escolas/Manifesto_EducacaoEscolarIndigena.pdf
- **Amnesty International mission to Argentina:** 6–15 November 1976; report first published March 1977, with archival identifier **AMR 13/083/1977**: https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/amr13/083/1977/en/
- **IACHR:** 6–20 September 1979 Argentina visit, report published April 1980; the text distinguishes official facilities visited from the clandestine network not fully accessible to investigators: https://hrlibrary.umn.edu/iachr/country-reports/argentina1980.html
- **UN Working Group:** Commission on Human Rights Resolution 20 (XXXVI) of 29 February 1980; first report E/CN.4/1435 dated January 1981, not conflated with 1992 General Assembly declaration: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/20572
- **Gustavo Gutiérrez:** Spanish *Teología de la liberación* (1971), English *A Theology of Liberation* (1973), with edition distinction maintained.
