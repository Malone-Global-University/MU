## 3. Historical verification and corrections

1. **Origins and development:** Preserve differences between the FARC and ELN, their 1960s origins, FARC's 1982 conference and subsequent organizational expansion, and distinctions between armed presence and uncontested territorial control. Documentary comparator: CNMH FARC training source, pp. 76–77, https://centrodememoriahistorica.gov.co/descargas/informes2015/cajaHerramientasMaestros/texto-para-estudiantes-el-salado.pdf; and Alejo Vargas Velásquez, *Controversia* 173 (1998), https://revistacontroversia.cinep.org.co/index.php/controversia/article/view/335 .
2. **AUC formation:** April 18, 1997 founding meeting and staged confederation; additional affiliation in May 1998. Colombian Truth Commission: https://www.comisiondelaverdad.co/de-los-pepes-las-auc-0 . No homogenization of different blocs or unproven attribution to all state personnel.
3. **CONVIVIR:** Corrected inaccurate implication of immediate universal dissolution. Constitutional Court C-572/97 upheld regulated private security subject to constitutional constraints; administrative limitations, nonrenewals, and reorganization followed in 1997–1999. See https://www.corteconstitucional.gov.co/relatoria/1997/c-572-97.htm .
4. **Massacres:** Mapiripán's approximately 49 executed or disappeared persons is not a verified count of individually identified homicide victims; see CNMH https://centrodememoriahistorica.gov.co/21-anos-de-la-masacre-de-mapiripan/ . El Salado's broader CNMH count is 60; see https://centrodememoriahistorica.gov.co/micrositios/recorridos-por-paisajes-de-la-violencia/montes-maria-piedemonte.html .
5. **Bojayá:** May 2, 2002; 79 direct victims of the church attack in one defined accounting, distinct from larger event-period estimates. The narrative names the FARC-fired projectile, the surrounding armed confrontation, civilian vulnerability, and later community-led memorial work.
6. **Caguán and Plan Colombia:** February 20, 2002 termination of negotiations; the Colombian plan announced in 1999 distinguished from the U.S. regional FY2000 supplemental. The approximately $1.3bn regional/$860.3m Colombia-specific figures describe the initial package, **not** cumulative program spending. GAO assessment: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-09-71 .
7. **Displacement:** Explicitly distinguish CNMH 2015 RUV cutoff (2002: 618,058), later Truth Commission narrative RUV counts (above 800,000 for 2002), and separately reconstructed JEP–CEV–HRDAG data (730,904); they are not an additive series. See https://centrodememoriahistorica.gov.co/descargas/informes2015/nacion-desplazada/una-nacion-desplazada.pdf and https://comisiondelaverdad.co/el-exodo .
8. **JEP Case 03:** 6,402 in 2002–2008 is a *preliminary* count of persons killed and falsely reported as combat fatalities, not a sum of final individual convictions. See https://www.jep.gov.co/macrocasos/caso03.html .
9. **AUC demobilization / Law 975:** Distinguish registered demobilization from verified, lasting disarmament; legal benefits are conditional and consolidated online statutes include later modifications. See https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=17161 .
10. **2012 negotiations dates:** August 26 general framework signed; reports emerged August 26–27; President Santos's formal national announcement occurred **September 4, 2012**, with Oslo opening in October. See CNMH https://centrodememoriahistorica.gov.co/micrositios/fin-del-conflicto/diez-momentos-del-proceso-de-paz.html .
