### 3. Historical factual changes completed

1. **§75.2 — trade collapse:** replaced a loosely rounded “about half” assertion with the verifiable UN ECLA series (1965, Table 20). 1929 exports US$2,714.0 million; 1932 exports US$1,076.6 million; 60.3% fall in **current-dollar merchandise export value**, not physical quantity or national production. The separate 48.1% decline between the 1925–29 and 1930–34 annual averages is identified as a different comparison.
2. **§75.3 — international sugar plan:** attributed the international Chadbourne proposal to Thomas L. Chadbourne in negotiation with Cuban and other interests; specified the Brussels agreement of May 9, 1931, rather than incorrectly calling it solely Cuban-led.
3. **§75.14 — Alessandri:** clarified Chilean president Arturo Alessandri's second term, beginning in late 1932, and revised syntax.
4. **§75.7 and Chile's documentary window — labor institutions:** distinguished legal recognition from actual bargaining power; identified the 1924 antecedents and Chile's DFL No. 178, promulgated May 13 and published May 28, 1931.
5. **§75.11 and CORFO source window:** specified Chilean Law No. 6,334, promulgated April 28 and published April 29, 1939; distinguished the reconstruction-and-relief corporation from the separate production-development corporation created by the same law.
6. **Roca–Runciman documentary window:** replaced an incorrect “minimum share” formula with the annual refrigerated-beef import floor of **390,000 metric tons**, equivalent to 1932 volumes, distinguished from the agreement's foreign-meatpacking provisions and other negotiations.
7. **Volta Redonda documentary window and chronology:** distinguished the 1940 tentative bank financing, the May 22, 1941 contract and US$20m guarantee from the later **up to US$45m** guaranteed financing recorded in Brazil's 1943 Decree-Law No. 5,659; retained 1946 for plant output.
8. **§75.18 and 1949–50 chronology:** cited Prebisch's 1949 Spanish UN E/CN.12/89 and the separate 1950 English revised issue E/CN.12/89/Rev.1 rather than treating the two as an undifferentiated publication.
9. **§75.1 and Statistical Study:** removed unsupported commodity export-share percentage approximations; substituted a documented UN regional trade table and a separate comparative price/volume-index table from Rosemary Thorp (1998, Table 4.1). Distinct units, denominators, and time bases are explicitly disclosed.
