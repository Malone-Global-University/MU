## 2. Historical verification and corrections applied

1. **Constitution of 1824:** Confirmed the original constitutional charter bears **25 March 1824**, not 24 March. Official reference: [Brazilian Presidency](https://legislacao.presidencia.gov.br/atos/?ano=1824&ato=1db0TPn5UMBRVT9c5&tipo=COI); [Brazilian Senate](https://www25.senado.leg.br/web/atividade/anais/constituintes). No alteration required.
2. **1872 census:** Confirmed rounded classifications 38.1% white, 38.3% *pardo*, 19.7% Black, and 3.9% *caboclo*, total roughly 9.93 million. These are contemporary census categories, not neutral indicators of ancestry. Reference: João Pacheco de Oliveira, [*Horizontes Antropológicos* (1997)](https://www.scielo.br/j/ha/a/fh9cpRfmbxt4QNkmvnZyffg/).
3. **Cabanagem death toll (§61.13):** Replaced a poorly contextualized 20,000–40,000+ range with a clearly qualified commonly cited order of **30,000–40,000**, without implying a reliable census-based total. References: [scholarly article](https://www.scielo.br/j/tem/a/ZX5z5skg9g7YyC47qtn533N/?format=html), [UFF historical review](https://www.historia.uff.br/impressoesrebeldes/revista/cabanagem-plural-e-radical/).
4. **Justices of the peace (§61.8):** Corrected the claim that the **1832 Code** created the elected office. The **1824 Constitution** anticipated it, a law of **15 October 1827** established organization, and the **1832 Code** expanded authority. Reference: [Brazilian National Archives](https://mapa.an.gov.br/index.php?catid=65&id=282&option=com_content&view=article).
5. **National Guard source window:** Corrected the characterization of Guard eligibility as simply volunteer enrollment. The law of **18 August 1831** imposed service duties on eligible citizens subject to statutory conditions and exemptions. Reference: [original law](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/lei_sn/1824-1899/lei-37497-18-agosto-1831-564307-publicacaooriginal-88297-pl.html).
6. **Paraguayan War (§61.42):** Corrected the conflation of cumulative wartime mobilization with a peak standing-army figure. Scholarship varies on the war-wide total; no unsupported simultaneous-peak number remains. References: [University of London Press](https://read.uolpress.co.uk/read/brazil/section/0c2155f3-0885-42c3-afda-319d122d8b63), [Brazilian National Library](https://bndigital.bn.gov.br/dossies/guerra-do-paraguai/contexto-historico/).
7. **Free-Womb Law (§61.43):** Replaced the unanchored “on the order of a hundred” description with **113 children surrendered to the state for compensation in records covering 1871–1885**, distinguishing the 1885 administrative outcome from the 1871 legislation itself. Sources: [original Law 2,040](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/leimp/1824-1899/lei-2040-28-setembro-1871-538828-publicacaooriginal-35591-pl.html), [historical educational research in SciELO](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbedu/a/8rz8S3Dxm9ZLBghPZGKtPjv/?format=html&lang=pt).
8. **Decree 523 of 1847 — source window:** Narrowed the paraphrase to what the decree actually says: the office of President of the Council of Ministers was created and the Council was charged to prepare regulations for imperial approval. Appointment practice is not all enumerated in that short decree. Reference: [original text](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/decret/1824-1899/decreto-523-20-julho-1847-560333-publicacaooriginal-83096-pe.html).
9. **Law 581 of 1850 — source window:** Corrected the sweeping phrasing that could imply all domestic transport/sales were treated as piracy; the law concerned prohibited **importation**, maritime seizure and enforcement. Re-export is discussed as statutory provision, distinguished from implementation. Reference: [original text](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/leimp/1824-1899/lei-581-4-setembro-1850-559820-publicacaooriginal-82230-pl.html).
10. **Land Law 601 of 1850 (§61.32 and source window):** Clarified purchase was the general rule for *terras devolutas*, subject to an express foreign-frontier exception, revalidation of qualifying prior grants and occupation, special provisions for Indigenous settlement, and statutory authorization of a land office. Reference: [original law, Articles 1, 4–5, 12, 19–21](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/leimp/1824-1899/lei-601-18-setembro-1850-559842-publicacaooriginal-82254-pl.html).
