## 3. Historical precision corrections and independent checks

**A. Recopilación chronology corrected.** The *Recopilación de leyes de los reynos de las Indias* was sanctioned/authorized in 1680, while its **first complete printed edition was published in Madrid by Julián de Paredes in 1681**. The chronology, source study, timeline and register now distinguish these dates. Library of Congress original-edition record: https://www.loc.gov/item/31010935/ ; Universal Short Title Catalogue: https://ustc.ac.uk/editions/5067582 .

**B. Pernambuco instruments distinguished.** The Portuguese Crown's donation to Duarte Coelho dates to **10 March 1534** and the separate foral dates to **27 September 1534**, documented by the Brazilian National Library: https://bdlb.bn.gov.br/acervo/handle/20.500.12156.3/37105 and https://bdlb.bn.gov.br/acervo/handle/20.500.12156.3/37106 . The text no longer treats them as a single instrument bearing an unspecified 1534 date.

**C. Scope precision.** The opening no longer claims a directly demonstrated Potosí supply network extending to 'three oceans'; it accurately describes Atlantic and Pacific routes with broader Eurasian connections. The estate definition separates colonial Brazilian cattle/cotton fazendas from the later expansion of coffee production. Haciendas, estancias, fazendas, and export plantations are differentiated; mines are treated as generators of regional demand, not re-narrated as technical silver history.

**D. Sugar and global trade.** Antonil's *Cultura e Opulência do Brasil* is confirmed as a 1711 Lisbon publication with Part I on sugar: John Carter Brown Library copy, https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/106007 . Published scholarship and edited sources confirm the **1593 Manila–Acapulco legal ceilings** of approximately 250,000 pesos in export merchandise and 500,000 pesos in silver returns, with subsequent changes and evasion; these values are never described as certain actual cargo totals. Published-edition evidence: https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/15022/pg15022-images.html and https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/spanish-pacific-15211815/royal-decree-of-philip-iii-regulating-trade-between-the-philippines-and-new-spain-1604/0919A21D832E021BE17247D7A1794517 .

**E. No claim of comprehensive original-document collation.** Independent work verified important bibliographic identities, document dates, archive series, and published scholarly arguments. It did not include a folio-by-folio reading of the manuscript original for every source window, exhaustive checks of all 30 references, or an exhaustive multilingual specialist peer review. The final manuscript reflects those limits rather than concealing them.

## 7. Scope of publication lock and known limitations

**APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION — LOCKED** as a first-edition independent historical-text standalone HTML with disclosed limits. Specifically:

- The **three Map Studies** are text-guided atlas tasks without authenticated embedded map plates.
- The **Documentary Image Study** is an attributed text-guided exercise; **no archive photograph, engraving or licensed visual plate** is embedded. The source provides identification of an image type, not an independently accession-verified illustration.
- All nine source windows are **editorial paraphrases or source-identification studies**, not audited translations of original pages. Some are directly tied to published scholarly analyses or catalog entries where full manuscript folios were not read.
- The 30-entry bibliography is selectively catalogued and linked; **not every monograph edition or page number** was independently confirmed against physical holdings.
- Source HTML maintains the provided Chapter 31-like CSS; **exact parity with a separately supplied Chapter 31 original** was not verified.
- No actual deployment to maloneuniversity.org or production CDN was tested; live links will be subject to host policies and future website configuration.
