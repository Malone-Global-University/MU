## C. Historical corrections applied directly to the HTML

**H1 — Bay of Pigs battlefield geography, §81.6.** The draft's wording implied an additional amphibious landing at San Blas. This was corrected: the principal landings were at Playa Girón and Playa Larga; the First Battalion's **paratroopers dropped inland at 7:30 a.m. April 17** and seized the San Blas road center. Official contemporary operational chronology: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v10/d112 .

**H2 — Castro's five demands, §81.16, Primary Source Window 8, and key-term glossary.** All five points are now separately and consistently identified: (1) end the embargo and economic pressure; (2) end subversive actions; (3) end attacks from U.S. and Puerto Rican bases; (4) stop violations of Cuban airspace and territorial waters; (5) evacuate and return Guantánamo. The omitted third point was restored. Documentary account: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v11/d133 (footnote 3). The five points were Castro's demand, **not conditions conceded by Kennedy and Khrushchev**.

**H3 — Guevara's Bolivia campaign, §81.19.** Replaced the overly broad claim that area residents spoke Quechua and Guaraní rather than Spanish. The revision distinguishes the guerrillas' **Quechua-language preparation** from **Guaraní-speaking communities and Spanish speakers in the Ñancahuazú region** and does not falsely generalize all peasant language use. Specialist treatment: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/blar.12435 .

**H4 — ten-million-ton 1970 sugar goal, §81.20.** Replaced the inaccurate statement that Castro first announced the goal in 1969. The revised narrative says it was advanced **as early as 1963** and became the overriding national mobilization in 1969. The approximately 8.5-million-ton 1970 harvest remains identified as a shortfall relative to the official target. Contemporary context: https://www.nybooks.com/articles/1970/09/24/this-shame-will-be-welcome-a-speech-by-fidel-castr/ and https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76ve10/d207 .

**Other historical anchors retained:** the two separate agrarian-reform laws of May 1959 and October 1963; the April 16, 1961 socialist declaration before the April 17 invasion; CIA-organized Cuban exile invasion as distinct from the later Operation Mongoose; Mongoose planning approved in November 1961; the public settlement and secret Turkey-Jupiter element in 1962; October 28's agreement as distinct from subsequent IL-28, inspection and quarantine developments; the 1965 Communist Party name and 1965–1968 UMAP coercive-labor system. Contested casualty and detainee figures remain qualified rather than falsely precise.

## D. Primary-source apparatus, provenance, and citation improvements

The nine Source Windows are **clearly marked editorial descriptions or paraphrases**, not verified verbatim quotations, full translations, or line-by-line facsimile transcriptions. Each has precisely four analytical questions.

**Window 3 — Bay of Pigs intelligence review:** Explicitly distinguishes **Lyman B. Kirkpatrick's CIA Inspector General's Survey of the Cuban Operation (October 1961)** from the **separate June 1961 Taylor Study Group inquiry**. The narrative is a labeled comparative paraphrase, not a falsely attributed single report. A direct FRUS edition of Kirkpatrick's survey was added: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v10-12mSupp/d273a . The operational chronology is separately linked at https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v10/d112 .

**Window 5 — 1961 National Literacy Campaign:** The principal identifiable source is **_Cartilla Venceremos_** (1961 learner's primer). The separate **_Manual Alfabeticemos_** (instructor's guide), participants' letters, oral recollections and later scholarly reports are not misrepresented as excerpts from the primer. UNESCO's Memory of the World documentary register names these different collections: https://www.unesco.org/en/memory-world/lac/national-literacy-campaign-its-international-legacy . A student must consult the actual original booklet for page-level close reading; the present study is an editorial teaching description.

**Window 8 — Missile crisis:** The source-study paraphrase now carries all five demands and a FRUS locator, matching the revised narrative and glossary. Other windows gain a documentary locator or published collection link, with the explicit notice that linking to a published record does not establish independent source collation.

**Selected other direct documentary locators used:**
