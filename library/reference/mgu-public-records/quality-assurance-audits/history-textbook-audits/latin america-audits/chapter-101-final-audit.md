## 4. Quantitative reconciliation

### Study I — AGN, PGR reports for 1982–1984

Preserved AGN numbers: **16,776 hectares** of poppy destroyed in 1982 and **24,333 hectares** in 1984; actual change **7,557 ha / +45.05%**, not “almost doubled.” Reported arrests **3,873** (1982) and **10,016** (1984); change **+158.61%**. The official AGN article itself uses the “casi se duplicó” description and provides these incompatible values. The chapter retains the figures and clearly identifies the arithmetic discrepancy. The record is identifiable: Dirección de Reconocimiento y Verificación, *Cuadro de plantíos asperjados y superficie nacional, 1982–1984*, January 8, 1985, cited as image 3 in the AGN illustrated article and described within the PGR statistical dossier. **No unverified 1983 figure was inserted.** Source: https://www.gob.mx/agn/articulos/los-anos-germinales-de-la-politica-de-guerra-contra-las-drogas-en-mexico .

### Study II — Historical demand surveys

Original NHSDA cocaine tables were preserved. Added a transparent comparative table for marijuana and cocaine in school surveys, documenting marijuana **37% (1978) to 12% (1992)** and cocaine **6.7% (1985) to 1.3% (1992)** past-month prevalence among high-school seniors. Also identifies the ONDCP 1979 NHSDA marijuana benchmark (ages 12–17 **14.2%**) as a distinct population and survey design. Explicitly warns that benchmark years differ and school data cannot be treated as a national supply index. **No unsupported heroin prevalence values were invented; a matched heroin series remains outside this particular quantitative dataset.** Official sources: https://bjs.ojp.gov/drugs-and-crime-facts/drug-use and https://bjs.ojp.gov/drugs-and-crime-facts/drug-use/cocaine-table ; ONDCP fact sheet https://www.ojp.gov/ondcppubs/publications/pdf/drugusetrends.pdf .

### Study III — 1987–1988 program performance, replacing unattributed excerpt

**Fully replaced** the previously unverifiable “1987 State Department report excerpt” table, including its unattributed cocaine-seizure and mixed acres/hectares field totals. The new table draws on two identifiable, dated GAO publications:

- GAO **T-NSIAD-87-42** (August 5, 1987), which reports observed aircraft utilization **48 hours/month against an 80-hour benchmark** (60%) and as few as **40%** of aircraft operationally available; https://www.gao.gov/products/t-nsiad-87-42 .
- GAO **NSIAD-88-73** (January 11, 1988), reports observed monthly aircraft utilization **46 against 80 hours** (57.5%) and deficiencies in maintenance, baseline cultivation estimates, performance targets, and verification of eradication; https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad-88-73 .

These are separately dated GAO observations, **not** a matched experimental series. Flight hours, crop area, seizures, and volume of illicit commerce are different quantities; no numeric inference of total market size is drawn.
