## Consolidated localized publication repairs

1. **§56.23, British treaties:** Corrected conflation of the 23 November 1826 slave-trade convention with the separate 17 August 1827 bilateral friendship/navigation/commerce treaty; the trade treaty is a follow-on event rather than part of the 1825 recognition settlement.
2. **§56.23 and treaty source window:** Clarified that the total £2 million in the additional convention **includes** Brazil's assumption of Portugal's 1823 London loan, rather than being £2 million plus the loan.
3. **Opening-of-ports source window:** Identified the 28 January 1808 *carta régia* more exactly, including the general 24% customs duty and restrictions for brazilwood and other crown-monopoly goods.
4. **Lisbon Cortes source window:** Anchored its 1821 paraphrase to the dated 29 September Cortes decision promulgated on 1 October, and distinguished Pedro's return from separate Brazilian provincial-government decrees. Removed unfinished 'verify exact wording' editorial note.
5. **1822 and 1824 source windows:** Strengthened document context and perspective, noted limitations of later reconstructions of 7 September, and specified Articles 98–101 of the 1824 Constitution and the electoral distinctions of Articles 90–95.
6. **Maps / Porto Revolution:** Qualified a map prompt that overstated northern provinces' formal administrative separation from Rio until 1823, and a sentence generalizing all Porto revolutionaries' trade goals.
7. **Instructional placement:** Moved all 15 existing feature boxes adjacent to their related narrative sections rather than leaving them in an end-of-chapter feature appendix. IDs and sidebar links preserved.

These repairs did not change the approved section titles, count, chronology, or narrative architecture. No wholesale expansion was needed.
