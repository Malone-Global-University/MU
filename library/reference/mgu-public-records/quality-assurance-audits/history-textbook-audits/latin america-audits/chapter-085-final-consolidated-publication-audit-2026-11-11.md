## Applied targeted historical corrections

1. **René Schneider (1970):** distinguished CIA contact with different Chilean coup groups, the Valenzuela group receiving guns, and the plot attributed by Chilean courts to the Viaux group. The CIA supported overthrow efforts but the congressional inquiry did not establish that it ordered Schneider's killing. Did not merge this with direct military decision-making in September 1973.
2. **September 1973 junta:** clarified that the proposed rotation of the junta presidency was not implemented. Pinochet was named junta president from the founding day; Decree Law 527 (June 1974) and Decree Law 806 (December 1974) formalized his later titles.
3. **Víctor Jara:** distinguished 2018 judicial convictions from the definitive August 2023 Supreme Court decisions concerning Jara and Littré Quiroga.
4. **Poverty:** corrected the denominator-confused comparison. The retrospective 1970 estimate of **17%** and 1987 estimate of **38.1%** refer to households; the **44.4%** 1987 estimate refers to people. Comparison limitations are explicit.
5. **1988 electoral record:** preserved certified totals of **3,967,579 No** and **3,119,110 Yes**, and explained differences in percentage denominators.
6. **1989 constitutional reform:** specified 54 reforms and **6,069,449** Apruebo vs **581,605** Rechazo votes. Approximately **85.70%** of all ballots were affirmative; approximately **91.25%** of substantive-option votes were affirmative.
7. **Scholarly Register:** clarified Stern's *Memory Box of Pinochet's Chile* as a trilogy label; expanded individual publication details and primary-document entries; preserved Edwards and Cox Edwards's *The Chilean Experiment* title.
8. **Source Window 7:** replaced generic periodical description with an identifiable documentary case—*Solidaridad* no. 1 (May 1976), its Vicaría archive, and limitations of the archival evidence.
9. **Brazil comparison:** retained and qualified documented Brazilian recognition/cooperation, without importing Chapter 84's historical narrative.
