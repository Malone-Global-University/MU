## 3. Corrected historical and interpretive errors

**H-01 — Governor at Plaza, primary-source verified.** Replaced three erroneous mentions of “Maeno Haruo” with **Sumita Satoshi** in the opening institutional reference, §96.17 and closing people review. The Bank of Japan lists **Maekawa Haruo** as governor December 17, 1979–December 16, 1984; **Sumita Satoshi** December 17, 1984–December 16, 1989; and **Mieno Yasushi** December 17, 1989–December 16, 1994. The 1986 **Maekawa Report** takes its name from the former governor, not from a governor serving at the September 1985 meeting. Source: https://www.boj.or.jp/en/about/outline/history/pre_gov/index.htm

**H-02 — Plaza communiqué and Japanese monetary intentions, directly checked.** Removed the incorrect statement that the original communiqué contained no Japanese monetary-policy material. The original Japan-specific **Policy Intentions** expressly call for flexible management of monetary policy with due attention to the yen rate, intensified financial-market liberalization and domestic-demand stimulation, including the consumer/mortgage credit markets. The revised narrative, source analysis and student question all distinguish these **broad commitments** from any **specific discount-rate reductions in 1986–1987**, which the communiqué does not require. The historical conclusion remains multifactorial. Original 1985 diplomatic text: https://g7.utoronto.ca/finance/fm850922.htm

**H-03 — Emergency oil statutes checked against the enacted texts.** The chapter now gives **December 22, 1973**, **Act No. 121**, *Emergency Law for the Stabilization of Living*, and **Act No. 122**, *Petroleum Supply and Demand Optimization Law*, with direct House of Representatives links. Removed “to be confirmed” because the legislative record resolves the point. Originals: https://www.shugiin.go.jp/internet/itdb_housei.nsf/html/houritsu/07219731222121.htm and https://www.shugiin.go.jp/internet/itdb_housei.nsf/html/houritsu/07219731222122.htm

**H-04 — Precise 1979 law-version control.** The law is identified as **Act No. 49 of June 22, 1979**. Its source window links the **original enacted** text and warns that English-language amended consolidations must not be treated as the 1979 statutory wording. Original: https://www.shugiin.go.jp/internet/itdb_housei.nsf/html/houritsu/08719790622049.htm

**H-05 — Heisei chronology clarification.** The chapter chronology now distinguishes Emperor Shōwa’s death and Akihito's accession on **January 7, 1989** from the **Heisei era beginning on January 8, 1989**. §96.20 already made this distinction. Government accession chronology: https://www.gov-online.go.jp/eng/publicity/book/hlj/html/201904/201904_06A_en.html

**H-06 — First 1976 historical preservation districts.** §96.16 now names verifiable initial selections: Tsumago-juku (Nagano), and Kyoto's Sannei-zaka and Gion Shimbashi, while preserving the distinction between the **1975 legal mechanism** and **1976 first selected districts**. Agency for Cultural Affairs district list: https://www.bunka.go.jp/english/policy/cultural_properties/introduction/historic_buildings/list.html

## 4. Quantitative-data reconciliation

**Data Study A — energy supply, fixed definition and fiscal years.** Replaced mixed round figures and the undated “late 1980s” column with a single chart-derived three-year fiscal series: FY1973, FY1980 and FY1990. Values are proportions **of primary energy supply**, not electricity-generation shares, and are from the FEPC 2010–2011 Japanese-language booklet **図表で語るエネルギーの基礎** (p. 11), reproducing Agency for Natural Resources and Energy FY2008 final statistics. The source methodology and original PDF link are now adjacent to the table.

| Share of Japan's primary energy supply, % | FY1973 | FY1980 | FY1990 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Petroleum | 77.4 | 66.1 | 56.0 |
| Coal | 15.5 | 16.9 | 16.8 |
| Natural gas | 1.0 | 6.1 | 10.7 |
| Nuclear | 0.6 | 4.7 | 9.6 |
| Hydropower plus other | 5.3 | 6.3 | 6.9 |

The totals differ from 100 by at most 0.2 percentage points because the source independently rounds categories. The exact composition of the underlying supply categories, the fiscal-year designation and historical statistical vintage are disclosed. Other white-paper vintages sometimes give a different FY1973 petroleum percentage; the chapter does not silently combine them. Source and screenshot-reviewed chart: https://www.fepc.or.jp/pr/pamphlet/pdf/enekiso2011.pdf (PDF p. 11).

**Data Study B — reproducibility instead of guessed employment data.** Retained the student research assignment and strengthened its rules: use a consistent geographic scope, years, industry classification and distinction between ships completed, orders and the order book, plus units such as gross tons vs compensated gross tons. No invented labor or production figures were introduced.

**Data Study C — the yen.** Approximate exchange-rate examples remain pedagogical scenarios. Text now distinguishes year-end closing observations from calendar-year averages, and requires a single rate series rather than blended year-end and annual statistics. These are **illustrative numbers**, not a newly certified Bank of Japan transcription.

**Data Study D — financial assets and interest rates.** Kept the suitably rounded Nikkei year-end index closes and the year-end official rates. Added the source series directly to the exercise: FRED's historical Nikkei daily price series and the Bank of Japan's dated changes to discount rates. BOJ official data show 2.50% from **February 23, 1987**, with the next rise to 3.25% **May 31, 1989**. The Nikkei’s December 29, 1989 close is 38,915.87; the chapter appropriately presents roughly 38,900 in rounded-year-end exercises. Sources: https://www.boj.or.jp/en/statistics/boj/other/discount/discount.htm and https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/NIKKEI225

**Land prices:** The 19-source register includes the Japan Real Estate Institute index's official publication/methodology page (https://www.reinet.or.jp/visitors-report.html). The source-study distinction between appraisal/sample indexes and registered transaction values remains explicit. Land and stocks are not given a false common peak date.
