## 3. Historical verification and corrected precision

### Company dissolution and the competition framework

1. **SCAPIN-244, November 6, 1945**, approves the Japanese four-holding-company plan in general, reserves SCAP power over implementation and seeks wider ownership and competition. Original record: https://jahis.law.nagoya-u.ac.jp/scapindb/docs/scapin-244.
2. **HCLC**, established under the Japanese ordinance of **April 20, 1946**; SCAPIN-859 of April 4 addresses the proposed ordinance. Verified via JACAR: https://www.jacar.go.jp/english/exhibition/glossary_en/term1/0110-0010-0050-0010-0020.html. Commission wound down in July 1951.
3. **SCAPIN-1363, November 26, 1946**, extends commission jurisdiction over specified zaibatsu family property: https://jahis.law.nagoya-u.ac.jp/scapindb/docs/scapin-1363.
4. **Antimonopoly Act**, Law No. 54 promulgated **April 14, 1947**; enforced and Japan Fair Trade Commission established **July 20, 1947**, per JFTC https://www.jftc.go.jp/en/about_jftc/index_3_250723.html and historical statute https://jahis.law.nagoya-u.ac.jp/lawdb/l/322a0054en.
5. **Excessive Concentration Law**, Law No. 207 promulgated **December 18, 1947**, distinct from the Antimonopoly Act: https://jahis.law.nagoya-u.ac.jp/lawdb/l/322a0207en.
6. **Initial 325 company designations** (257 plus 68) and **18 ultimate reorganizations, six actual splits**, follow the Ministry of Finance's 2010 retrospective, printed page 50 (web PDF p. 56). These count different administrative stages, not 325 actual breakups: https://www.mof.go.jp/english/pri/publication/policy_1945-1971/Full_edition1945-1971.pdf. The original Ministry volume's page image was inspected.
7. **SCAPIN-1741, July 3, 1947**, directs liquidation of Mitsui and Mitsubishi trading companies; corroborated by contemporary FRUS correspondence: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1947v06/d240.
8. **SCAPIN-2158, June 21, 1951**, modified and rescinded specified liquidation rules without retroactive cancellation. Its accompanying memo reports **more than 166 million shares** disposed of; a shares count is not a number of individual shareholders. https://jahis.law.nagoya-u.ac.jp/scapindb/docs/scapin-2158.

### Labor, strategy, financial measures and political exclusion

9. **January 31, 1947:** MacArthur stopped the planned February 1 general strike, according to the National Diet Library chronology, https://www.ndl.go.jp/modern/e/utility/chronology.html.
10. **July 22, 1948:** MacArthur public-service letter; **July 31:** Cabinet Order No. 201. Distinguished from the 1947 National Public Service Law and from continuing private-sector union rights. Contemporary FRUS: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1948v06/d573.
11. **NSC 13/2, October 7, 1948:** source distinguishes Washington's strategic recommendations from Japanese measures actually implemented: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1948v06/d588.
12. **Nine-Point Program (December 1948)** and **Dodge Line (1949):** separate announcement and implementation phases, evidenced by https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1948v06/d696.
13. **SCAPIN-1997 issued April 23, 1949; ¥360/US$ effective April 25:** original directive https://jahis.law.nagoya-u.ac.jp/scapindb/docs/scapin-1997. Rate is not presented as unrestricted convertibility.
14. **June 6, 1950** action against Communist Party leaders preceded the **June 25 Korean War**; the manuscript avoids a falsely exact all-sector purge total: https://www.ndl.go.jp/modern/e/cha5/description12.html.
15. **Shoup Mission 1949:** 1949 proposals (including 55-percent top income-tax recommendation and a separate net-worth proposal) distinguished from Japanese 1950 implementation and later revisions; contemporary FRUS https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1949v07p2/d131 and Japanese MOF https://www.mof.go.jp/tax_information/qanda015.html.
16. **Historical industrial growth data:** METI's retrospective 1950-base series for annual mining/manufacturing output change, 1945–1950: https://www.meti.go.jp/statistics/toppage/report/featurearticles/fa_kako/20161007base1950_iip.html. It is labeled year-on-year percentage change, explicitly not an absolute production level or a wage/inflation rate.

### Interpretive guardrails

- Family holding companies distinguished from operating enterprises and banks; mature later *keiretsu* not projected backward into 1946.
- Law No. 54 and Law No. 207 treated as separate legal programs; designated companies not conflated with split companies.
- Public-service labor restrictions not equated with termination of private unions; ordinary layoffs not conflated with politically motivated dismissals.
- Causal caution retained: production growth does not prove Dodge alone caused recovery, and lower inflation does not demonstrate improvements to all household living standards.
- Chapter 90's Korean War procurement and peace-treaty narrative and Chapter 91's high-growth institutions not prematurely absorbed.
