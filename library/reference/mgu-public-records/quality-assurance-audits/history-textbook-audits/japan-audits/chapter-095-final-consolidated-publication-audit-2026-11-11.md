## 3. Historical corrections implemented and verification basis

**3.1 Opening chronology.** Corrected the introductory claim from “seven years” after the firebombing of Japanese cities to approximately six years before the September 1951 Venice award. The film's 1950 release and 1951 Venice recognition remain unchanged. Historical background: National Film Archive of Japan, https://www.nfaj.go.jp/english/exhibition/historyofjapanesefilm/ .

**3.2 Japanese cinema admissions, §95.6.** Replaced “roughly a quarter” of the 1958 peak “by the early 1970s” with Eiren's documented numbers: 1958, **1.127 billion** admissions; 1970, **254.799 million** (approximately **23%** of 1958); 1972, **187.391 million** (approximately **17%**). This avoids conflating 1970 and 1972. Source: Motion Picture Producers Association of Japan, https://www.eiren.org/statistics_e/ .

**3.3 Kara Jūrō, §95.10.** Corrected the inaccurate claim that Situation Theater was founded in 1967. The verified sequence is **Situation no Kai founded in 1963; renamed Jōkyō Gekijō in 1964; Red Tent production at Shinjuku's Hanazono Shrine in 1967**. Recast the affected passage as grammatical, continuous historical prose. Source: Digital Script Archives, https://www.nkac-da.jp/database/121 .

**3.4 Hijikata's *Kinjiki*, §95.9 and source study.** Added **May 24, 1959** and **Daiichi Seimei Hall, Tokyo**, to the original performance, identified the original duet as **Hijikata Tatsumi and Ōno Yoshito**, and distinguished the September revised version that involved **Ōno Kazuo**. The source-study metadata now names Dance Archive Network's **event PFM21** and associated photographic/leaflet item **PIC290**, instead of depending on unnamed archival holdings. Sources: https://dance-archive.net/en/archive/works/pf21.html ; https://dance-archive.net/en/archive/data/pt290.html ; https://dance-archive.net/en/archive/works/wk50.html .

**3.5 *Godzilla* suit performance, §95.5.** Retained **Nakajima Haruo as the principal suit performer**, and acknowledged **Katsumi Tezuka's additional suit work**, with an explicit warning that individual scene assignments were not established in this edition. Sources: retrospective Nakajima interview, https://www.nippon.com/en/articles/-/12541 , and published production-history summary https://www.scifijapan.com/godzilla-toho/godzilla-50th-anniversary-pressbook . This is a qualified credit, **not** a claim of direct inspection of Tōhō's original production paperwork.

**3.6 *New Treasure Island*, §95.17.** Named **Sakai Shichima** and distinguished his original story concept from Tezuka's collaboration. Added the official archive's **January 30, 1947** publication date. The manuscript does not add an inflated circulation figure. Source: Tezuka Osamu official manga archive, https://tezukaosamu.net/en/manga/207.html . Note that this same source discusses material alterations in later editions, relevant to student comparison of original and later texts.

**3.7 1956 Gutai manifesto.** Expanded the source metadata to identify **Yoshihara Jirō, “Gutai bijutsu sengen,” *Geijutsu Shinchō* 7, no. 12 (December 1956), pp. 202–204**, and attribution of the linked English translation to Reiko Tomii. The window retains its clear editorial-paraphrase label. Citation and archival pointers: https://web.guggenheim.org/exhibitions/gutai/data/manifesto.html ; Guggenheim museum teaching guide https://www.guggenheim.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/guggenheim-teaching-materials-gutai.pdf . The archived manifesto text URL was not independently available for a full-line collation in this turn; the original publication reference is confirmed through the Guggenheim's published bibliography. **Do not treat a link as completed collation.**

**3.8 Additional source context.** The source register explicitly distinguishes original artworks, institutional catalogs, later museum surveys, interviews, and critical essays; it does not claim all resources are primary sources. Interpretive subjects including the New Wave, Hi-Red Center, Mono-ha, Olympic design, Metabolism and television *Astro Boy* have identifiable research pointers. Key independently consulted resources: BFI https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/where-begin-japanese-new-wave ; MoMA https://www.moma.org/artists/36946-hi-red-center ; National Museum of Art Osaka https://www.nmao.go.jp/archive/en/exhibition/2005/id_1025055227.html ; Mori Art Museum https://www.mori.art.museum/english/contents/metabolism/about/index.html ; Japan House London https://www.japanhouselondon.uk/press-area/exhibitions/tokyo-1964-designing-tomorrow/ ; Tezuka official animation archive https://tezukaosamu.net/en/anime/30.html .
