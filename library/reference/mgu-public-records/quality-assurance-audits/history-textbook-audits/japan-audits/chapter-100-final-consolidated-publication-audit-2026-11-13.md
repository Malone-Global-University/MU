## 3. Consolidated historical and legal corrections

1. **Women in university education (§100.4).** Replaced the incorrect “more than half of university entrants” with MEXT’s **46.1% female undergraduate enrollment in 2025**, explicitly distinguishing this from the broader OECD tertiary entrants measure. Government educational source: https://www.nicjp.niad.ac.jp/en/news/schoolbasicsurvey2025.html .
2. **Gender-gap index editions (§100.4).** Retained the accurate 2025 WEF figure, **118/148, score 0.666**, and added the distinct 2026 edition, **117/145, score 0.670**, with a caveat that rankings involve different country universes. Sources: https://www.weforum.org/publications/global-gender-gap-report-2025 and https://www.weforum.org/publications/global-gender-gap-report-2026/benchmarking-gender-gaps-2026 .
3. **Joint parental authority (§100.4).** Corrected the 2024 family-law amendments from future tense to **effective April 1, 2026**, and distinguished parental legal authority from a legal requirement of equal physical residence. Ministry of Justice: https://www.moj.go.jp/MINJI/minji07_00357 .
4. **Same-sex marriage litigation (§100.4).** Named the high-court decisions finding the exclusion unconstitutional (Sapporo, Tokyo first case, Fukuoka, Nagoya, Osaka), contrasted the **November 28, 2025** contrary Tokyo High Court ruling, and retained the position as of cutoff that no general right to marry had been created by the judiciary. A public secondary case chronology is linked in the source study: https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2025/11/japan-last-high-court-ruling-a-damaging-step-backwards-on-same-sex-marriage/ . Individual original court judgments have **not** been original-language page-collated in this edition.
5. **Foreign-population statistics (§§100.3, 100.6).** Distinguished the OECD’s **3.8 million comparative “foreign population” for 2025** (International Migration Outlook released Nov. 12, 2026) from the Japanese Immigration Services Agency’s **4,125,395 Dec. 31, 2025** and **4,281,026 June 30, 2026** administrative foreign-resident totals. Defined the latter’s mid-to-long-term and special-permanent-resident coverage and the exclusion of short-term visitors. Official ISA release: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/publications/press/13_00070.html ; OECD: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/international-migration-outlook-2026_a9eab36b-en/full-report/japan_5e2440bd.html .
6. **2025 population census (§100.3).** Dated the 123.05 million preliminary series to the **May 29, 2026** release and specified decreases in **1,558 of 1,719 municipalities (90.6%)**. Later 2026 basic-tabulation releases are not silently equated with the preliminary series. Statistics Bureau: https://www.stat.go.jp/data/kokusei/2025/kekka.html .
7. **Assassination proceedings (§100.1).** Clarified Yamagami Tetsuya’s **January 21, 2026** life sentence at Nara District Court and **February 4** appeal to Osaka High Court. This avoids describing the initial conviction as the final appellate disposition. Report: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/assassin-japans-former-pm-abe-appeals-life-sentence-kyodo-reports-2026-02-04/ .
8. **APEC planning and cutoff (§100.13).** Replaced generic “reported to be preparing” language with the **Nov. 13** announcement of a scheduled **Nov. 16–19** visit to the Philippines and China and the **Nov. 18–19** Shenzhen APEC meetings. Future events are not written as accomplished history. Contemporaneous source: https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/en/component/k2/1877662-20261113.htm .

### Substantive historical framing preserved

The differentiation between the Unification Church corporate-status dissolution and restrictions on private religious practice remains clear; the 2026 June Supreme Court order is distinguished from the 2025 and March 2026 lower-court orders. The manuscript continues to distinguish Japan’s demographic forecasts from outcomes, official state perspectives from independent historical evidence, contested interpretations of empire from established historical findings, cultural market revenues from “soft power,” and foreign-resident registers from tourist arrivals.
The final 2015 and 2025 wartime-anniversary statements are individually linked; the 2024 Nihon Hidankyō Nobel recognition and 2013 UNESCO washoku inscription retain their documentary context; 2024 arrivals and 2025 Expo cumulative admissions remain carefully defined, not mislabeled unique persons.

## 6. Scholarly and deployment limits

- **Scope of approval:** first-edition scholarly textual HTML publication. It is *not* an assertion of exhaustive original-language Japanese legal transcript collation, authenticated archival facsimile comparison, or exhaustive scholarly bibliography verification.
- **Press vs court documents:** the June 2026 Unification Church Supreme Court outcome is supported by contemporary reporting, but no original Supreme Court decision PDF was independently collated line by line. Source windows are transparent about this distinction.
- **Legal and political cutoff:** the 2026 cases and November 2026 diplomacy are treated only through November 13, 2026. Appeals, legislative revisions, expected visits, and evolving statistics may change afterward.
- **Data provenance:** the statistical exercise tables remain pedagogical and require students to retrieve compatible source releases. No unsupported quantitative extrapolations have been introduced to fill blank series.
- **Bibliographic links:** links were selected and cross-checked for relevance to public sources in this editorial session, but complete future HTTP uptime and link permanence were not independently tested.
- **Template parity:** base CSS and script were preserved against the uploaded Claude chapter. Byte-for-byte comparison to an independently authenticated Chapter 2 template remains unverified.
- **Maps and images:** historical geographical exercises are text-guided; no external atlas plates or rights-cleared copyrighted illustration is embedded. No live MGU website upload or deployment has occurred.
