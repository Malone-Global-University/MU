## Localized historical and editorial corrections

1. **§62.1:** Corrected the Eulsa Five to Yi Wan-yong, Yi Ji-yong, Yi Geun-taek, Gwon Jung-hyeon, and Foreign Minister Park Je-sun. Removed the unsupported claim that Park objected and his seal was stolen; distinguished signed text from coercive circumstances and lack of Gojong's approval.
2. **Chronology and Eulsa source box:** Changed misleading "1905 Protocol" labeling to "Second Japan–Korea Convention"; identified Hayashi Gonsuke and Park Je-sun as signatories.
3. **1905 source window:** Corrected Article V's "welfare and dignity" wording and linked the English text as reproduced by *Foreign Relations of the United States, 1905*.
4. **1910 source window:** Added a precise archival source location in *Foreign Relations of the United States, 1910*.
5. **1919 source window:** Tightened the interpretive paraphrase and identified Han-Kyo Kim's published translation in *Sources of Korean Tradition*, volume 2, reproduced by Columbia University's Asia for Educators.
6. **§62.23:** Specified that Kang Ju-ryong's 1931 rooftop protest occurred atop the **Eulmildae Pavilion** in Pyongyang, rather than leaving its location ambiguous.
7. **§62.32:** Corrected the oversimplified claim of naval conscription "following in 1945" to distinguish the 1943 naval special-volunteer program, army conscription beginning in 1944, and wartime naval volunteer/conscription channels.
8. **§62.34:** Replaced the claim that Japan merely "promised" Diet seats in April 1945 with the fact of the enacted electoral provision (23 Korea/5 Taiwan, restrictive franchise); clarified its failure to take effect or produce elections.
9. **1939–1940 naming source window:** Added the identifiable 1939 decrees No. 19 and 20 and the National Institute of Korean History/Government-General Gazette record.
10. **CSS:** Contained all three chronology/comparison tables on narrow screens, preserving horizontal table scrolling without horizontal page overflow.
