## Historical and factual corrections

1. **§72.36, Reiyūkai:** Removed the unsupported claim of incorporation “around 1930.” Distinguished formative initiatives of the 1920s from the organization’s own July 13, 1930, inauguration (104 participants including children). Source: https://reiyukai.jp/history and https://reiyukai.jp/profile .
2. **§72.35, Ōmoto:** Distinguished the February 12, 1921 arrests; 126 days of custody and bail release; continued appeals; and final May 17, 1927 amnesty. Do not conflate bail release with resolution of the judicial proceedings. Source: https://oomoto.or.jp/wp/english/history-of-oomoto/the-first-and-second-oomoto-incidents/ .
3. **§72.37, Sōka Kyōiku Gakkai:** Distinguished November 18, 1930, commemorated publication/founding date from the 1937 inaugural meeting. Source: https://www.nichirenlibrary.org/en/dic/Content/S/232 .
4. **§72.8, Mingei:** Retained the independently supported 1925 coinage while avoiding unwarranted specificity about an exact Kōyasan itinerary. Sources: https://mingeikan.or.jp/special/ex202606/?lang=en and https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/jikihitsu/part2/s10_1/ .
5. **§72.7, Yanagi Kaneko’s benefit performances:** Corroborated the underlying fundraising role via the Academy of Korean Studies’ reference entry for the Korean Folk Art Museum; museum opened April 9, 1924. Source: https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0071583 .
6. **§72.12, Japan Mingei Association:** The manuscript’s 1934 formation is supported by the Japanese National Diet Library’s corporate-authority record, notwithstanding some secondary references to 1931 as a broader associational beginning. Source: https://id.ndl.go.jp/auth/ndlna/00294272 .
7. **§72.25, Chiri Yukie:** Distinguished her own transcription, translation, and editorial agency from Kindaichi’s support; included her thirteen Ainu narratives. Source: https://uhpress.hawaii.edu/title/ainu-spirits-singing-the-living-world-of-chiri-yukies-ainu-shinyoshu/ .
8. **§§72.18–72.19:** Expanded identifiable works and historical publishing networks, including the 1924 *Bungei Jidai* group and the 1926 Kyōdō Printing strike background, rather than relying on movement labels. Source: https://www.japanesestudies.org.uk/ejcjs/vol12/iss1/omura.html .
