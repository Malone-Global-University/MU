## Historical verification and targeted revisions

1. **Growth ranges and definitions:** the text now differentiates the Cabinet Office's approximate 9.3% annual real growth for 1955–1972 from the Bank of Japan's 9.7% annual real GDP rate for 1956–1970, avoiding misleading one-period precision.
2. **Economic causes:** domestic demand, bank finance, capital formation, technology learning, and exports are analyzed as complementary rather than turning the chapter into a simplistic export-led account.
3. **1960 Income Doubling Plan:** the cabinet decision is explicitly dated 27 December 1960, growth targets are described as planning rather than observed outcomes, and institutional preparation and social objectives are explained.
4. **Zaibatsu and keiretsu:** horizontal/vertical forms are distinguished. Dai-Ichi Kangyo's 1971 merger chronology is preserved; group structure is not projected unchanged backward.
5. **Sony:** its 1955 TR-55 is attributed to the firm legally known as Tokyo Tsushin Kogyo, with a later 1958 corporate name change and distinct 1957 TR-63.
6. **Labor:** shuntō is traced to 1955. Lifetime employment is treated as uneven, developing, and inclusive of some large-firm production workers; the unsupported one-fifth labor-share estimate is removed.
7. **1973:** the yen float is specifically placed in February 1973, before the October energy shock; the real contraction of 1974 is not dated to 1973.
8. **Documentary controls:** four evidence windows are now included, along with a thirteen-item research register. Direct quotation is restricted to short, verifiable text; incomplete original-language collation is disclosed.
