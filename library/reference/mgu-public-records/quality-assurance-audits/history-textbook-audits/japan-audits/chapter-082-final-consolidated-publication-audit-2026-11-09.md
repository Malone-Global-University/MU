## 3. Historical corrections directly made

1. **§82.2, September 2–3 proclamations:** Replaced the vague account with the three documented proposals—general occupation government authority, military tribunals, and supplemental B-marked military currency—and correctly dated MacArthur's retraction to September 3 after Japanese representations. Removed the unsupported inference that the Japan proposals included an English-official-language clause. **Document:** JACAR, Foreign Ministry archive **B17070000700**: https://www.jacar.archives.go.jp/das/meta-en/B17070000700 and JACAR's research account: https://www.jacar.go.jp/wp/newsletter_en/newsletter_045e/newsletter_etc_045e/.
2. **§82.3, Washington directives and first source window:** Distinguished the **September 6, 1945 SWNCC 181/2 / JCS 1380/6 authority message** (explicitly describing the relationship as noncontractual) from the **November 3 JCS 1380/15 detailed occupation directive** (conditional indirect governance, broad supreme authority). **Documents:** https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1945v06/d491 and https://www.ndl.go.jp/constitution/e/shiryo/01/036/036tx.html.
3. **§82.16, censorship and third source window:** Corrected the text of the **September 19 SCAPIN-33 Press Code** to distinguish **false/destructive** or **destructive** criticism from blanket restrictions later enforced through internal CCD guidance. Corrected a misleading assertion that the ten-paragraph Press Code itself expressly forbade mention of censorship. **Document:** https://jahis.law.nagoya-u.ac.jp/scapindb/docs/scapin-33.
4. **§82.18, Everyday Life feature:** Replaced the hypothetical composite prefectural team with the **documented Kyoto Military Government Team** and physicians **John D. Glismann** and **Tadayoshi Tsuchiya**. The source-backed narrative also discusses the 1948 defective diphtheria vaccine episode (68 child deaths) and the uneven consequences of public-health supervision. **Study:** Sey Nishimura, *American Journal of Public Health* 98, no. 3 (2008), 424–434, DOI 10.2105/AJPH.2006.088906: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2253585/.
5. **§82.7, Data Study:** Seven directive numbers are now linked individually within six table rows: SCAPIN-33, -93, -448, -548, -550, -677, and -775. Retained the distinction between official issuance and demonstrable local effect. SCAPIN-775's explicit requirement for monthly prefectural reporting was incorporated into the analysis: https://jahis.law.nagoya-u.ac.jp/scapindb/docs/scapin-775.
6. **§82.20:** Expanded the synthesis and included a specific administrative lifecycle through SCAPIN-775, with a second example from Kyoto public health.
7. **Historical and textual cleanups:** Corrected a cross-chapter reference in a writing exercise; corrected the chronological treatment of the September 3 proclamation withdrawal; distinguished 1945 field arrangements from their 1946 reorganizations; added fuller treatment of Korean residents and Okinawa's separate jurisdiction to avoid equating Tokyo reform with all former imperial populations.
8. **Primary-source provenance:** The three source windows remain clearly labeled **editorial paraphrases**, not verbatim translations or original-document excerpts; their identified records are linked.
