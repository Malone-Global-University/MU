## 3. Targeted historical corrections completed

**3.1 Interval between the two 1995 crises.** Corrected *sixty-three days* to **sixty-two elapsed days**, January 17 to March 20, 1995. Does not imply a causal connection.

**3.2 JMA seismic intensity.** Replaced the assertion that a station recorded intensity 7 in 1995. Contemporary station reports gave **intensity 6 in Kobe and Sumoto**; the JMA's subsequent field survey identified areas at **intensity 7**. This distinction is now explicit in §98.1, including the role of field-assessed intensity under the then-prevailing system. Independent basis: Japan Meteorological Agency, [1995 event overview](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/eqev/data/1995_01_17_hyogonanbu/gaiyo.html), [observations](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/eqev/data/1995_01_17_hyogonanbu/data.html), and [intensity reforms](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/eqev/data/1995_01_17_hyogonanbu/rireki.html).

**3.3 Kobe fatality accounting.** Identifies **6,434 deaths in the official December 2005 accounting** reported by the JMA and Cabinet Office, and the distinct **6,437 total in the 2026 national disaster white paper**, without treating these as interchangeable or claiming to know the basis of all three additional cases. Hyōgo's 2005 prefectural accounting of **919 related deaths** is explicitly identified as prefectural. Documentary basis: [Cabinet Office historical overview](https://www.bousai.go.jp/kyoiku/kyokun/hanshin_awaji/earthquake/index.html) and [2026 disaster white paper](https://www.bousai.go.jp/kaigirep/hakusho/r08/honbun/t1_1s_02_03.html).

**3.4 Volunteer measurement.** Replaces ambiguous references to a number of unique people with **more than 1.3 million cumulative volunteer participations**, as described in Japan's 2026 retrospective. Numbers are not assumed to represent distinct individuals.

**3.5 SDF dispatch chronology.** Places the **formal Hyōgo request at approximately 10:00 a.m.** on January 17 while preserving the distinction between preliminary contacts, administrative authorization and actual arrival in affected neighborhoods. [Cabinet Office chronology](https://www.bousai.go.jp/kyoiku/kyokun/hanshin_awaji/nenpyo/index.html) and published Hyōgo emergency logs substantiate the broad sequence. No claim is made that all earlier or local rescue actions were absent.

**3.6 Matsumoto attack.** Retains the source-vintage distinction between **seven immediate 1994 deaths and eight after a later death in 2008**. No substantive correction required.

**3.7 1996 TBS affair.** Retains the verified 1996 disclosure and distinguishes journalistic misconduct from proof of a specific causal role in the murders. Supplemental contemporary locator: [UPI, April 3, 1996](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1996/04/03/Japan-TV-network-fights-ethics-charges/3444828507600/).

**3.8 Chūetsu 2004.** Gives **JMA magnitude 6.8 and 68 deaths in final Niigata Prefecture accounting**, including disaster-related fatalities; avoids presenting post-Kobe reforms as the demonstrated sole cause of differences in casualties. [Niigata Prefecture disaster archive](https://www.pref.niigata.lg.jp/site/saigai-archive/tyuuetudaishinzai.html).

**3.9 Victim participation.** Gives **December 1, 2008** for the commencement of the trial-participation provisions enacted in the 2007 criminal-procedure reform; does not confuse this with the 2004 Basic Act on Crime Victims. [Ministry of Justice retrospective](https://hakusyo1.moj.go.jp/en/61/nfm/n_61_2_5_2_1_4.html).

**3.10 Reported crime.** Newly added narrative supplies the Ministry of Justice's official 2002 totals, **2,854,061 general Penal Code offenses**, **3,693,928 total offenses including specified traffic negligence**, **476,573 general offenses other than larceny**, and a **20.8% general-case clearance rate**. Explains why reporting, recording practices, actual incidents and offense composition cannot be assigned one universal causal weight. [White Paper on Crime 2003](https://hakusyo1.moj.go.jp/en/49/nfm/n_49_2_1_1_1_1.html).

**3.11 Religious freedom and security law.** Newly added context distinguishes criminal responsibility, loss of religious-corporation status, the **1997 rejection of a dissolution request** under the Subversive Activities Prevention Act, and subsequent **2000 administrative surveillance** under the 1999 statute. [Public Security Examination Commission](https://www.moj.go.jp/EN/kouanshin/kouanshinsa_index.html). No claim that membership in lawful religious associations was a criminal offense.

### Limits of validation

1. The assessment is a **first-edition scholarly historical-text** audit; neither exhaustive original-language archival transcript/facsimile checks nor full source-quotation collation were performed. Six source windows remain explicitly editorial explanations or paraphrases, not collated excerpts.
2. Reproduction rights for historical photographs, documents, and artwork were not assessed; none was embedded in the HTML. Geographic activities remain **text-guided** and require appropriate atlases or maps.
3. Published statutes available on government portals may be consolidated as amended through later years. The register flags the distinction from original 1995, 1998, 1999, 2000 and 2004 enacted texts.
4. This audit verifies the unchanged baseline CSS and script **relative to the original Claude Chapter 98 upload**, not byte-exact identity with the separate original Chapter 2 template. No live website deployment, production-host link integrity, or cross-browser test was performed.
5. External domains and government document portals may limit automated or international access; having a document locator is not proof that every user can open every link. No assertion of a fully tested external-link crawl.
6. National and prefectural fatality reporting differ in geography, methodology and statistical revision; interpretations should continue to cite the specified dated series.
