## 3. Primary-source and statistical corrections

### Quantitative Study I — UNODC coca cultivation

Retained the checked 1990–1993 and 1997 series for Bolivia, Colombia and Peru from **UNODC, World Drug Report 2008, historical Table 5 (section 1.3.2)**. The HTML includes an identifiable report link, notes retrospective reconstruction, and preserves the differences between UNODC's 1992 Bolivia value of 45,300 hectares and an alternative U.S. series of 45,500. **Primary series:** https://unodc.org/documents/wdr/WDR_2008/WDR_2008_eng_web.pdf

### Quantitative Study II — U.S. cocaine use and availability

Retained the directly verified past-month age-group percentages in the National Household Survey on Drug Abuse as published by ONDCP (e.g. ages 18–25: 9.9% in 1979; 7.0% in 1982; 8.1% in 1985; 4.8% in 1988). Replaced the poorly identified DAWN national emergency totals and purity mini-series with a contemporaneous **NNICC April 1988** statement: projected cocaine-related emergencies in 1987 nearly four times the 1984 level, based on nine months of 1987 observations. This is explicitly a projection. A separate DEA seizure retrospective and the DEA's 1983–1984 Western European seizures are identified as distinct measures with their respective provenance. The text now warns against treating emergency projections, seizures and household prevalence as one continuous quantity.

**Documents:** https://www.ojp.gov/ondcppubs/publications/pdf/drugusetrends.pdf ; https://www.ojp.gov/pdffiles1/Digitization/111482NCJRS.pdf ; https://www.ncjrs.gov/pdffiles1/Digitization/101766NCJRS.pdf ; https://www.dea.gov/sites/default/files/2018-07/1985-1990%20p%2058-67custom2.pdf
