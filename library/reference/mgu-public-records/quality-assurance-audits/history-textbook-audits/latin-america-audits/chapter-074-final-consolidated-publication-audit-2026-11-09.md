## B. Historical correction register

1. **§74.5 / 1934 versus 1937:** Removed the false attribution of full hacienda-worker petition rights to the 1934 Agrarian Code. Identified the **August 9, 1937** amendment to Article 45, while distinguishing prior regional administrative action in La Laguna in 1936. Source: [published transcription](https://www.memoriapoliticademexico.org/Textos/6Revolucion/1937-LC-D-RCA.html); corroboration in Mexican agrarian court history.
2. **§74.6 / La Laguna area:** Replaced roughly 150,000 ha as purported total with approximately **447,515 ha overall, including 146,277 ha irrigated**, 296 ejidos, approximately 34,816 beneficiaries under the cited series. Other close counts explicitly distinguished. Source: [Lourdes Romero Navarrete, *Región y sociedad* (2006)](https://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?lng=en&nrm=iso&pid=S1870-39252006000200005&script=sci_arttext).
3. **§74.7 / Yaqui resolutions:** Identified the October 30, 1937 publication and October 22, 1940 definitive-titling publication, while distinguishing paper recognition from enforcement. Source: [Diario Oficial retrospective citing both decrees](https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/docFuente/5631177).
4. **§74.13 / technical personnel:** Removed the implication that the newly founded 1936 IPN had already produced large numbers of graduates managing Pemex in 1938; preserved the larger technical-training story.
5. **§74.13 / Britain:** Distinguished 1941 announcement of resumed diplomatic contact from fuller later relations; the British-connected oil compensation agreement is placed in **1947**, not vaguely in the mid-1940s. Source: [U.S. Department of State, Office of the Historian](https://history.state.gov/milestones/1937-1945/mexican-oil).
6. **§§74.13, 74.18 / U.S. compensation:** Separated the **November 19, 1941** negotiating framework, **April 1942** Cooke–Zevada appraisal, and ongoing implementing installments. Removed implication that all bilateral petroleum claims were fully settled in 1942. Source: [FRUS 1946, XI, document 837](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1946v11/d837).
7. **§74.18 / social insurance:** Corrected the law's publication to **January 19, 1943**, and clarified that IMSS services began in 1944. Source: [official *Diario Oficial* entry](https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/4555563).
8. **§74.19 / military sector:** Dated withdrawal of the PRM military sector to **December 1940**, not an undifferentiated “early 1940s.” Source: [El Colegio de México, *Nueva historia mínima de México*, chapter 7](https://www.nhmdemexico.colmex.mx/cap-7.php).
9. **§74.19 / 1946 PRI:** Distinguished **January 18–19** convention, the January 18 founding account, an unresolved January 26 date found in another chronology, and **March 30** party-registration date. No unproven explanation was supplied for the January 26 discrepancy. Sources: [Memoria Política de México](https://www.memoriapoliticademexico.org/Efemerides/1/18011946.html); [INE](https://www.ine.mx/partidos-politicos-nacionales/).
10. **§74.19 / Alemán nomination:** Identified Alemán's labor-sector support in June 1945 and subsequent pre-PRI candidacy; new PRI inherited that nomination in January 1946. Qualified the “first civilian” formulation. Source: [El Colegio de México study of the Ávila Camacho reforms](https://repositorio.colmex.mx/downloads/q237hs684?locale=en).
11. **Bibliography:** Added González's volume 15, corrected Senior's title/publisher, corrected Silva Herzog's book year from 1963 to **1964**, and specified the translation of Meyer's 1977 English edition. Sources: publisher/catalog records and [Colmex oil bibliography](https://petroleo.colmex.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/bibliog-SHCP.pdf).

## E. First-edition limits and editorial disclosure

- Nine Primary Source Windows contain **explicitly labeled editorial paraphrases**, not verbatim verified editions or original-language manuscript transcription. Original facsimile-by-facsimile collation of all nine primary documents has **not** been represented as complete. Their questions and documentary-context labels remain useful for critical source evaluation, not textual scholarship requiring exact quotes.
- Three Map Studies are **text-based atlas exercises**; historical map plates are not embedded. The Documentary Image Study contains **no** reproduced photograph and instructs students to verify provenance before using an external image.
- The 26 standard scholarly and documentary bibliography entries are **not all independently inspected edition-by-edition**. Selected book metadata and nine online documentary checkpoints were checked and directly linked. No invented pagination is supplied.
- The source Claude called the Chapter 31 HTML model was **not supplied in this conversation**, so pixel-/template-equivalence to that earlier source is not claimed. Original chapter-specific stylesheet, sidebar, feature classes and JavaScript were preserved and the finished chapter rendered as standalone HTML.
- The 19-event timeline exceeds the master instructions' approximate 6–10 events, but maintains distinct agrarian, labor, diplomatic, economic and party strands. Its analytical questions were added. This is a deliberate instructional exception rather than an accidental omission.
