### 3. Material historical corrections applied
1. **§86.19 South Atlantic chronology** — Corrected the false assertion that South Georgia surrendered after June 14. South Georgia was retaken on **April 25, 1982**; the remaining Argentine presence on **Southern Thule, South Sandwich Islands**, surrendered **June 20**. Replaced imprecise 'fighting began April 25' phrasing with a specific description of the South Georgia operation. Verification: U.S. State Department South Atlantic timeline and contemporaneous records: https://history.state.gov/milestones/1981-1988/south-atlantic ; https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1981-88v13/d357
2. **Primary Source AI-4, IACHR** — Removed the categorical and inaccurate claim that the commission could not enter detention facilities. It visited prisons and military facilities including ESMA, but lacked free access to clandestine detainees and concealed facilities. Contemporary documentary record: https://hrlibrary.umn.edu/iachr/country-reports/argentina1980-intro.html ; https://hrlibrary.umn.edu/iachr/country-reports/argentina1980-ch5.html
3. **Primary Source AI-8** — Identified the **May 21, 1982** FRUS item correctly as a **cover memorandum from Vice President George Bush to President Reagan** with **Secretary Alexander Haig's memorandum attached as Tab A**. Replaced unspecific synopsis with document-grounded account of UN votes, support for British materiel requests short of direct operational involvement, public messaging, contingencies for quick surrender or protracted combat, and American safety precautions: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1981-88v13/d281
4. **Walsh reference** — Replaced misleading 'former Montoneros member' in the people index with description acknowledging Walsh's militant involvement and strategic criticism; his clandestine 1977 letter and killing remain accurately described. Source: https://conti.derhuman.jus.gov.ar/areas/b/carta-abierta-walsh.php
5. **Economy, §86.10** — Replaced the 1976-to-1983 debt comparison with a source-defined **1975 to 1983** comparison of approximately **US$7.7bn to US$45.9bn**, with an inline link to the cited Argentine government historical study and a caveat about series definitions. https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/2022/10/resp_civil_0610-web.pdf
6. **Bibliography** — Corrected the first-edition date and title of Emilio Crenzel's **2011** Routledge work, *The Memory of the Argentina Disappearances: The Political History of Nunca Más*. Publisher record: https://www.routledge.com/The-Memory-of-the-Argentina-Disappearances-The-Political-History-of-Nunca/Crenzel/p/book/9780415886161
