## Historical correction and verification log

1. **2016 referendum upgraded to final certified electoral results.** Original draft mixed election-night preliminary figures with a plan to seek final departmental results. The Registraduría Nacional del Estado Civil's 2023 retrospective [official departmental table, pp. 1–2](https://www.registraduria.gov.co/IMG/pdf/20230815_historico-plebiscito.pdf) gives **NO 6,438,552; YES 6,382,901; margin 55,651; valid votes 12,821,453; total ballots 13,062,917; null 168,589; unmarked 72,875.** Original election-night precount NO 6,431,376 and YES 6,377,482 (margin 53,894) is explicitly identified as *preliminary*, not the final tally. All 34 territorial/consular categories were transcribed into Quantitative Study I, and their column sums reconcile exactly to the official national totals. The final turnout remains ~37.43%, with 34,899,945 registered voters (the source's historical roll). The original source window and summary have been harmonized.
2. **2016 agreement chronology and legal implementation.** Original text and revised texts linked directly in the JEP Normograma: [initial agreement](https://jurinfo.jep.gov.co/Normograma/compilacion/docs/acuerdo_paz_acuerdopaz.htm), [revised agreement](https://jurinfo.jep.gov.co/Normograma/compilacion/docs/acuerdo_paz_acuerdopaz_final.htm); UN transmittal [S/2016/1063](https://docs.un.org/S/2016/1063); Colombian Court [C-332/17](https://jurinfo.jep.gov.co/Normograma/compilacion/docs/c-332_2017.htm), restricting parts of the fast-track process. Added comparative explanation of changes and retained the distinct dates of September 26, October 2, November 24 and November 29–30, 2016.
3. **Nobel Peace Prize.** Deleted the unsupported assertion about the Nobel Committee's private decision date; the historically established public award announcement was October 7, 2016.
4. **JEP's 2025–2026 judicial record.** [Case 01 page](https://www.jep.gov.co/macrocasos/caso01.html) and the [July 2026 jurisprudence bulletin](https://relatoria.jep.gov.co/documentos/providencias/17/23/Edicion-07_Boletin-jurisprudencial_julio-2026.pdf) establish the September 16, 2025 first-instance restorative judgment and June 26, 2026 appellate decision TP-SA-SP-620. Added a separate source note. The **21,396** reported victimizing incidents should not be equated automatically with 21,396 unique people; judgment versus execution of sanctions remains a separate distinction.
5. **Truth Commission statistical caution.** Clarified that **450,664 reported conflict-related deaths for 1985–2018** and an earlier CNMH estimate of **approximately 80% civilians** do not derive from exactly the same historical statistical exercise. The Commission says its integrated project could not reliably extract that specific civilian share; a differently specified series or expanded missing-person accounting yields different proportions. See [2022 Hallazgos y recomendaciones, pp. 126–127](https://www.comisiondelaverdad.co/sites/default/files/descargables/2022-08/FINAL%20CEV_HALLAZGOS_DIGITAL_2022.pdf). CNMH's approximately 220,000 for 1958–2012 is a different period and methodology and is not to be added to 450,664.
6. **Former-combatant security.** Updated to **501 verified killings, 185 attempted killings and 56 enforced disappearances since signing**, UN Mission October 2026. Kept counts separate, distinguishing approximately 13,000 historically accredited participants from more than 11,000 still in reintegration at the October 2026 reporting point. [UN Secretary-General/Mission update](https://colombia.unmissions.org/en/node/136964).
7. **Coca cultivation.** Added latest pre-cutoff official series: **2024: 261,000 hectares** versus **2023: 253,000 hectares**, a **3.5%** increase. This is the 2024 observed area, published June 2026, not cocaine-output tonnage. Also documented regional differences and limitations of crop-area data. [UNODC–SIMCI / Colombian Ministry of Justice, p. 11](https://www.minjusticia.gov.co/programas-co/ODC/Publicaciones/Publicaciones/Informe_monitoreo_cultivos_Colombia_2024.pdf).
8. **Kroc 2026 monitoring.** Preserved 578 total stipulations and published categories (**36% complete, 21% intermediate, 35% minimum, 8% not initiated**) and added exactly **130 gender-related and 80 ethnic-related** stipulations. Monitoring data through November 2025, report released May 2026; figures are *stipulation ratings*, not share of the population at peace. [Kroc Institute 2026 release](https://kroc.nd.edu/news-events/news/navigating-the-shift-new-kroc-institute-report-maps-the-future-of-colombian-peace-amidst-pivotal-political-transition/).
9. **UN resolution and historical cutoff.** [Resolution 2833](https://mandates.un.org/mandate/S/RES/2833%20%282026%29), adopted October 30, 2026, extends the UN Verification Mission to October 31, 2027 with reduced verification priorities. The revision explains that removing international monitoring of rural reform does **not** repeal the underlying Colombian obligations. November 24, 2026 tenth anniversary remains in the future relative to the editorial cutoff.
10. **2026 administration fact-check.** August 7 inauguration of Abelardo de la Espriella in Cali independently [reported by EFE](https://efe.com/english/latest-news/2026-08-08/abelardo-de-la-espriella-sworn-in-as-president-of-colombia/). The documented election-transition controversy is sourced to [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/659cd24bbaaa6669137b1a8b78a2a6a2); official [August 30 statement](https://www.presidencia.gov.co/prensa/Paginas/Gobierno-del-Presidente-De-La-Espriella-pone-fin-a-tres-procesos-de-falso-260830.aspx) confirms the closure of three specified *Paz Total* dialogues. These decisions must not be mischaracterized as cancellation of the 2016 agreement itself.
