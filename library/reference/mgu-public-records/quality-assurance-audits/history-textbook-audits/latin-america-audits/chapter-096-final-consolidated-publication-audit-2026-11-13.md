## 3. Targeted historical and legal changes

1. **Chiapas, 1824:** Corrected the erroneous suggestion that its Mexican incorporation occurred after the breakup of the Central American federation. The new context describes September 1824 as a contested decision while the federation existed and notes the disputed Soconusco question. Comparison: INEGI regional chronology and diplomatic sources named in prior audit.
2. **PRONASOL, 1988:** Corrected “launched 1992” to December 1988, preserving discussion of its early-1990s activities. Source: UNAM and El Colegio de México historical studies cited in prior audit.
3. **1992 agrarian reform:** Replaced the sweeping “rent, sell, mortgage” description with specific categories in the **original** February 26, 1992 Agrarian Law: art. 79 (use/lease), art. 80 (limited parcel-right transfers), arts. 81–83 (assembly pathway to *dominio pleno* and later registration), and art. 46 (security in usufruct rights). The constitutional reform is dated January 6, 1992. Original texts: https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/4643312 and https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/4652944.
4. **Guatemala 1999 referendum:** Inserted May 16, 1999, its four-question character, turnout of approximately **18.55% of registered voters**, and a caution that the widely quoted overall **55.25% No** estimate is not the separate percentage for each question. IACHR Guatemala country chapter X, ¶¶10–12: https://hrlibrary.umn.edu/iachr/country-reports/guatemala2001-chap10.html.
5. **2023 reorganization:** Corrected original notice to **November 5** (as archived by the EZLN), subsequent design announcement to **November 12**, and gave the three distinct names GAL, CGAZ and ACGAZ. Clearly marked announcements as movement testimony, not independent proof of implementation in every community. Original notices linked in §96.20.
6. **Bibliographic spelling:** Corrected **Shannan L. Mattiace**, rather than Shannon Mattiace, wherever found.
7. **Acteal study:** Replaced unbounded composite descriptions with a specified UN investigator and documentary identifier: **Asma Jahangir, E/CN.4/2000/3/Add.3 (November 25, 1999), ¶¶24–37**, and a separate IACHR 1998 Mexico report, chapter II, ¶¶164–165. Distinguishes the missions' concerns, official counterarguments, and legal findings without presuming judicial guilt from a report alone. Report catalog: https://www.refworld.org/reference/mission/unchr/1999/101366.

## 4. Verified statistical completion

**Quantitative Study I:** Exact INEGI Indigenous-language speaker counts, age **5 and over**: **1990, 716,012 (26.4%)**; **2000, 809,592 (24.6%)**; **2005, 957,255 (26.0%)**. Shares use the corresponding population aged five and older. This is not a count of all persons self-identifying as Indigenous. Sources: https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825497590/702825497590_2.pdf and https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/censos/conteo/2005/perfiles/Perf_Soc_Chipas.pdf. The series is not directly comparable with post-2010 language counts based on age 3+ without adjustment.
