### 3. Historical and legal verification and consolidated corrections

**1981 initiation of U.S. covert support:** Chronology distinguishes November–December 1981: the Reagan administration's presidential authorization dated November 17 and CIA operational authorizations in December, rather than presenting a single unsourced December inception. Congressional and declassified intelligence evidence grounds the sequence.

**Caribbean Coast and Miskitu rights:** The text differentiates the approximately **8,500 Río Coco–Tasba Pri relocations in early 1982** from additional displacements and flight to Honduras. It treats Miskitu, Mayangna, Rama, and Creole political agency separately, identifies disagreement between Indigenous leaders and organizations, and cites the 1983 IACHR investigation. The latter documents some confirmed abuses and expressly withholds conclusions on disputed acts insufficiently corroborated. The 1987 autonomy statute is distinguished from the earlier coercive relocation regime.

**Contra organizations and civilians:** FDN, ARDE, and Indigenous resistance groups are not collapsed into a single organization or treated as uniformly former National Guardsmen. The section on atrocities assesses allegations with the same evidentiary standards for Contra and government forces, while distinguishing patterns, responsibility, and motivation.

**International Court of Justice, Nicaragua v. United States (1986):** Separate the finding of unlawful U.S. intervention and use of force, mining, infringement of sovereignty and maritime commerce from the requirement of **effective control** for attributing individual Contra operations. The judgment does **not** establish automatic U.S. legal responsibility for every Contra act.

**1984 elections and chronology:** **October 1984 Boland restriction is positioned before the 4 November 1984 Nicaraguan election** (a confirmed ordering defect in the first build). The chapter distinguishes polling and voting/counting, campaign advantages, participation, withdrawals, and observers' divergent interpretations rather than calling the contest simply free or fraudulent.

**Boland provisions:** Described as **successive differently worded appropriations restrictions**, not one permanent universal prohibition. The 1987 White House legal-chronology reproduction is appropriately identified as a retrospective compilation, not an original statute.

**Iran-Contra memorandum:** The Oliver North memorandum often indexed **4 April 1986** is identified as an **unsigned and undated surviving document**, prepared **approximately 4–7 April 1986** according to later investigation. The source window does not infer presidential approval from the memorandum alone. The separate Iran-arms/hostage initiative and Contra-assistance policy are joined through the diversion of profits, not described as open Iranian state funding of the Contras.

**UN psychological operations manual (AI-2):** The archive has now been identified to the correct UN document, **A/39/596–S/16789**, a **20 October 1984 letter** from Nicaragua's representative, distributed **22 October 1984**, transmitting the *Psychological Operations in Guerrilla Warfare* manual. A direct UN English archival PDF is linked instead of relying on an ambiguous catalog path. The diplomatic allegations are distinguished from proof that every suggested field act was carried out.

**Economic crisis:** War damage, monetary/fiscal policy, sanctions, and structural vulnerability are treated jointly. The U.S. embargo under Executive Order 12513 was issued **1 May 1985**, effective **7 May 1985**. The time basis of the central-bank price series is explicit.

**Diplomacy and transition:** Esquipulas II **7 August 1987**, Sapoá **23 March 1988** (initial suspension of offensive operations **1 April**), 1990 electoral observation, Chamorro's roughly **54.7%** presidential vote share and Ortega's roughly **40.8%**, and transfer of power **25 April 1990** are distinguished from the separate pace of Contra demobilization.

### 6. Residual publication limitations

1. **Original-record collation.** Not every document has been independently transcribed or matched line by line to a manuscript scan. Primary Source Windows are clearly labeled attributed analytical paraphrases or source studies, never falsely presented as authenticated quotations.
2. **Historical maps.** The three geography studies are text-guided and require an external historical atlas or verified plates; they are not represented as embedded finished maps.
3. **Documentary photograph.** Public-domain U.S. government photographic attribution and catalog identifier are provided. The HTML links a Wikimedia-hosted thumbnail, not an embedded binary file; check the remote asset loads after deployment, and preserve provenance/rights metadata.
4. **Bibliography.** The register is substantively complete for first-edition teaching use but catalog verification is selective; some secondary entries use publisher/library search-homepage links, not a verified item permalink. Do not represent this as exhaustive bibliographic or archival authentication.
5. **Site parity.** Standard structural and responsive browser checks passed; an exact comparison to the canonical Chapter 31 file and actual live deployment were outside the local audit.
6. **Quantitative interpretation.** Inflation figures refer to the Banco Central's explicitly selected **Managua year-end cumulative** series, which is not interchangeable with annual-average inflation or a different base-date calculation; presidential and legislative election figures have separate sources and denominators.
