## 2. Historical and source-specific corrections

1. **Canadian pass versus permit system:** §§91.2, 91.5, chronology and the Pass System glossary entry now explicitly distinguish produce-sale permits supported by legislation from the administratively imposed Prairie pass system, which had no statutory basis. The movement rules varied in actual enforcement. Grounding: the University of British Columbia Open Textbook *Post-Confederation*, “Living with Treaties,” https://opentextbc.ca/postconfederation/chapter/11-6-living-with-treaties/ .
2. **Sonora railroad labor (1883):** §91.13, chronology and Source Window 4 name Miguel Tinker Salas, *In the Shadow of the Eagles* (University of California Press, 1997), chapter 6, printed pp. 134–135. The reported employer count is **1,949 workers: 810 Yaquis, 664 Mexicans, 475 Americans**; the study describes differentiated wages and Yaqui employment in track repair/construction. The original employer return was not separately collated. Full-text access: https://publishing.cdlib.org/ucpressebooks/public/book/in-the-shadow-of-the-eagles-sonora-and-the-transformation-of-the-border-during-the-porfiriato.html .
3. **Kahnawà:ke ironworkers:** §91.13 specifies the **29 August 1907** Québec Bridge collapse and **76 fatalities, including 33 Mohawk workers**. Government of Canada: https://www.canada.ca/en/housing-infrastructure-communities/news/2021/04/the-history-of-the-quebec-bridge.html .
4. **Hubbell chronology and archive:** chronology and reference description avoid an over-certain 1878 establishment date; sources differ between 1876 and 1878. Source Window 2 names the University of Arizona Libraries **AZ 375 Hubbell Trading Post records**, dates 1882–1968, and documented material types (daybooks, cashbooks, accounts, inventories). An archival finding aid is *not* represented as inspection of an individual ledger. https://lib.arizona.edu/special-collections/collections/hubbell-trading-post-records .
5. **Mountain Wolf Woman:** the Everyday Life study and Source Window 5 identify an edited, retrospective **first-person oral autobiography**, recorded in **1958** and published in **1961**, not a secondary scholarly reconstruction. The chapter points to the author’s **“Livelihood” (pp. 8–17)** and Lurie’s editorial commentary (pp. 92–108). Accessible edition: https://www.fulcrum.org/concern/monographs/6t053k26q?locale=en ; specific chapter record: https://www.jstor.org/content/oa_chapter_edited/10.3998/mpub.7669.7 .
6. **Source Window 1:** identifies the 1928 Meriam Report chapter X, “General Economic Conditions,” printed pp. 430–431 onward, with a digitized original text and caution concerning paternalistic assumptions. The 1908 Commissioner's annual report is linked as an authenticated comparison, but no invented farming figure from an uninspected volume is reproduced. https://narf.org/nill/documents/merriam/p_meriam_chapter10_part1_economic.pdf .
7. **Source Window 3:** identifies the specific **F. Dundas Todd photograph (1913)** of Indigenous women and children at Imperial Cannery, Steveston; Vancouver Public Library Local History Collections accession **2067**. The item is interpreted through catalogue metadata, without claiming identification of individual workers or reproducing the image. https://localhistory.vpl.ca/link/photos18554 .
8. **Bibliography:** replaces generic institutional homepage links with item-, chapter-, and collection-specific links; includes the full title and publication of Thomas Biolsi's *Organizing the Lakota* (University of Arizona Press, 1992), with a warning about its **1933–1945** New Deal focus.

## 5. Publication limitations and final decision

- Source windows are carefully attributed editorial paraphrases, catalogue-based studies, or modern scholarly reconstructions; **none is presented as a new original manuscript transcription**. The Meriam report's identified pages and Tinker Salas's published chapter were consulted for targeted facts. The Hubbell archive finding aid, cannery photo catalogue and edited Mountain Wolf Woman autobiography are linked with clear source classification.
- The cannery photo is not embedded. Cultural protocols and reuse permissions for archival visual material have not been separately investigated.
- Geography studies remain text-guided; no historical atlas is embedded.
- Bibliographic and item authentication was targeted; there was no full archive collation, exhaustive original-language textual comparison, or live website deployment check. External-link availability can change.
- The exact original Chapter 1 shell was not independently compared byte-for-byte; the supplied Chapter 91 HTML shell was retained and extended minimally to repair mobile navigation.

**Publication determination:** All identified publication-critical factual provenance and mobile production issues in the prepublication audit have been addressed. The chapter meets the 20-section structure and narrative-length target. **APPROVED FOR FIRST-EDITION SCHOLARLY TEXTUAL HTML PUBLICATION — EDITORIAL LOCK, 15 NOVEMBER 2026 (PACIFIC).** Reopen only for consequential newly documented factual errors or deployment defects.
