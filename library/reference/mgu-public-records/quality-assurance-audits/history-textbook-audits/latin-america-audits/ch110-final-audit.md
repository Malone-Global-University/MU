## 2. Publication-critical corrections implemented

1. **Argentina 2024 repeal initiative:** Corrected the misleading February 2026 claim to deputy Rocío Bonacci's filed 5 February 2024 parliamentary item 5172-D-2023. Distinguished proposed repeal from an enacted law or executive bill. Linked the official Chamber record and later implementation reporting.
2. **Chile 2019 transit and emergency chronology:** Distinguished 4 October fare announcement, effective change around 6 October, student fare evasion, October 18 violence and initial Metropolitan Region state-of-emergency decree, and subsequent October 19 measures. Linked OHCHR investigation and Chilean government response.
3. **Chile's constitutional process:** Clarified the two different questions asked in 2020; the two separate draft-rejection votes in 2022 and 2023; compulsory versus voluntary voting; different drafting bodies and distinct interpretations of each rejection. Kept turnout inference separate from valid-vote shares.
4. **Ecuador 2025:** Added the dated October 22, 2025 CONAIE announcement ending highway blockades while distinguishing the unresolved controversy over fuel subsidies from the termination of one protest tactic.
5. **Mapuche and 2026:** Removed anachronistic 'after 2026' phrasing. Differentiate community claims, political organizing, documented police misconduct, allegations of violence and state security responses.
6. **Marielle Franco case:** Differentiated convictions and sentences of Domingos and Francisco Brazão from Rivaldo Barbosa's convictions for corruption and obstruction rather than direct involvement in the killings, and dated the June 2026 appellate notice. Linked STF decisions and docket.
7. **Reproductive-law comparison:** Distinguished Argentina's statutory route, Colombian judicial holdings, Mexican federal and state jurisdiction, and Chile's three-grounds statute. Highlighted the difference between a legal right and accessible services.
8. **Environmental law:** Added direct links to the ILO Convention 169, Sarayaku judgment, Escazú Agreement and Ecuador's CNE certification of Yasuní's 58.95/41.05 national referendum result.

## 5. Historical and evidence limitations

- The manuscript is cut off as of **November 16, 2026**. Future reinterpretation or changes to court cases, political leadership or implementation policy require a new edition.
- Electoral shares, judicial judgments and legislative acts are dated. They must not be conflated with public opinion as a whole or with service delivery.
- Source windows deliberately paraphrase linked primary materials; they do not imply verbatim quotations. Reproduction permissions for any later photographs must be separately cleared. This textual release embeds no third-party copyrighted documentary photographs.
- Mechanical navigation and responsive browser checks were performed locally. The audit does **not** certify that every external URL will remain operational indefinitely, nor does it constitute independent peer review of every interpretive sentence.
