Latin American History Audits

Chapter-level assessment and publication review records for Malone Global University's Latin American History textbook series.

Collection Overview

This register links only to Latin American History audit records identified in the supplied institutional directory inventory. Records are arranged by chapter; chapters with no recorded audit are omitted. Where multiple audits exist for a chapter, each is listed.

Inventory scope: 59 chapters (Chapters 43–115); 63 existing audit and revision records. The linked records carry .md extensions and have not been converted into PDF editions. The separate Chapter 105 production plan is excluded from this audit register. Final audit labels reflect file titles and do not independently certify external peer review.
