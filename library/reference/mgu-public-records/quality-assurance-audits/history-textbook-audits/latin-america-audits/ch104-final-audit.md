## Historical/source corrections and methodology

1. **Founding chronology:** CJNG's emergence is described as a process around 2010–2011 from Milenio-linked and earlier regional relationships. An organization created around one individual and a single founding date is not asserted as proven.
2. **2015 sanctions:** Treasury's April 2015 CJNG and Los Cuinis action and August 2015 business-network action are separately linked and presented as administrative determinations, not convictions.
3. **Synthetic drugs:** Methamphetamine's longer history is separated from the later expansion of illicit fentanyl. Medical fentanyl is distinguished from the illicit market; no instructions for illicit drug production are included.
4. **Chemical sourcing and finance:** December 18, 2025 GAO report GAO-26-107918 is linked in its full and summary forms. It grounds the trade and finance discussion; its selected cases are not converted to global market-share estimates.
5. **Overdose statistics:** CDC's May 13, 2026 estimates are presented as provisional: 69,973 all-drug overdose deaths in 2025 versus 81,313 in 2024, and 38,084 versus 48,913 involving synthetic opioids. Overlap and inability to infer single-cartel revenues are explicit.
6. **2025 legal designation:** February 20, 2025 FTO and SDGT notices are linked separately from the Federal Register with the respective INA §219 and EO 13224 authorities.
7. **2026 Tapalpa operation:** Mexico's February 22 defense communiqué is identified as the government’s initial participant account. Operational deaths are not conflated with later nationwide retaliatory fatalities. U.S. intelligence participation is attributed rather than framed as direct command of the operation.
8. **Succession:** The U.S. Treasury's July 23, 2026 identification of Juan Carlos González / Juan Carlos Valencia González as a successor is reported as a **dated U.S. government assessment**, not a verified nationwide chain of command. The release's inconsistent given name for Oseguera is not uncritically repeated.
9. **Audias Flores Silva:** The April 27, 2026 arrest is separated from the Justice Department's May charges; accusations remain accusations.
10. **Documentary image:** The OFAC May 2019 organization diagram was inspected as a visual source. The HTML links the authenticated one-page government graphic and describes its arrows, portraits, boxes and framing as government representations, without embedding possibly third-party individual photos or treating the diagram as judicial proof.
11. **Quantitative limitations:** The selected GAO financial filings (including 1,457 sampled reports; 24 to 293 suspicious transfer references for specified years) and CDC statistics are identified as measures of recorded activity, **not total production, trafficking volume or CJNG revenue**.

Sources include the GAO, CDC NCHS, CRS, Treasury/OFAC, FinCEN, Justice Department, DEA, the Federal Register, and the Mexican Defense Ministry, all named in the chapter bibliography. The in-document links are public and identifiable; a live-site crawl or guarantee of future URL persistence was not performed.
