## 4. Historical/documentary corrections implemented

1. **Commodity prices:** Replaced mixed spot-price and annual comparisons with a uniform 2002/2008/2012/2015 annual table based on the World Bank's historical Pink Sheet series, with explicit commodity units and distinction between prices, output and export revenue. Crude-oil benchmark is the three-price average, not Brent monthly high.
2. **Brazil, Bolsa Família:** Replaced estimated coverage with actual December beneficiary-family counts: 6,571,839 (2004); 10,965,810 (2006); 12,778,220 (2010); 13,902,155 (2012). Added national population poverty and indigence indicators 2005–2012 from the ECLAC 2014 edition; these are *people*, not beneficiary families.
3. **Cross-country social outcomes:** Added five-country table for Brazil, Bolivia, Ecuador, Chile, and Colombia, using ECLAC's published poverty survey-year pairs and an additional 2008/2013 Gini comparison in index points out of 100. Explicitly distinguished survey years and measurement units. Removed the mistaken treatment of 28.1% as a universal 2012 figure; the 2013 ECLAC edition reports approximately 28.2% in 2012, while retrospective vintages can differ.
4. **Bolivia 2006:** Clarified that the temporary 82% state share applied to fields satisfying the decree's production threshold; it was not a permanent blanket rate on all gas fields. Identified mandatory YPFB delivery and renegotiation separately from expropriating every company's assets.
5. **Ecuador's constitution:** Expanded legal treatment of rights of nature and restoration under Articles 71–72, separating constitutional recognition from enforcement.
6. **Yasuní-ITT:** Linked the 2010 UNDP–Ecuador fund memorandum and Ecuadorian National Assembly's contemporary account of Decree 74 of August 2013. Clearly labeled 2023 Yasuní referendum as outside the core chronology when referenced retrospectively.
7. **Argentina debt exchange and YPF:** Linked the original authorizing 2004 debt decree and 2012 Law 26.741; maintained the precise 51% controlling-shareholding distinction.
8. **UNASUR:** Linked the 2008 treaty reproduced in the Colombian ratification law, differentiated the 2008 signing from its 2011 legal entry into force, and identified the Educ.ar/Télam summit photograph without claiming an individually named photographer or license.
9. **Elections:** Linked Brazil's TSE 2002 returns, Bolivia's electoral authority 2005 record, and Ecuador's electoral authority 2006 record. Do not equate first-round with second-round vote shares.
