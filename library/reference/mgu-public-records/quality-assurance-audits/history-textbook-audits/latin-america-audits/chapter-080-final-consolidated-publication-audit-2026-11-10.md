## 3. Historical accuracy, source-specific checks, and major corrections

**Corrected foundational chronology.** The original falsely collapsed the end of Spanish sovereignty in 1898 into Cuban state independence. The revision distinguishes 1898, U.S. occupation in 1899–1902, and the Republic of Cuba taking office on 20 May 1902. It also distinguishes Batista’s constitutionally elected 1940–1944 presidency from his 10 March 1952 coup government.

**Legal and ideological source handling.** The published *History Will Absolve Me* is explicitly identified as a reconstructed and edited pamphlet derived from the October 1953 defense, rather than an assured verbatim trial record. Published constitutions and movement manifestos are treated as statements of rights or political aims, never as proof that the declared program was enforced or that later socialist institutions were already established in the 1950s.

**Military and political sequence.** Moncada (26 July 1953) and Bayamo are distinguished; the 30 November 1956 Santiago action is separated from the delayed 2 December landing and 5 December Alegría de Pío defeat. Guerrilla survival, city organizing, FEU/Directorio operations, the April 1958 failed strike, Batista’s summer offensive, the July Caracas Pact, the central campaign, January 1 departure, and Urrutia’s provisional government appear in their historical order.

**Avoided false precision.** Moncada casualty figures, the roster of Granma survivors, total summer-offensive troop strength, and deaths attributable to security repression are contextualized instead of repeated as undifferentiated, certain single counts. Outcomes at El Jigüe, Las Mercedes, Yaguajay, and Santa Clara are described as parts of broader campaigns rather than miraculous standalone victories.

**U.S. policy verified and qualified.** The March 1958 decision to halt certain combat-arms shipments is distinguished from a total break in relations or support for Castro. Relevant records include FRUS vol. VI docs. 36 and 41; doc. 49 for April 9; doc. 124 for the movement after the strike; doc. 107 for the Caracas Pact; and docs. 213 and 217 for January 1959. These establish what U.S. officials recorded, not a complete or neutral Cuban social history.

**Civic and social agency restored.** Frank País and the urban movement receive an independent narrative section. The FEU and José Antonio Echeverría are discussed apart from Castro’s command, including the identified 1956 Carta de México and the 1957 political testament. Women organizers and rural civilian households are integrated into direct narrative, rather than confined to decorative boxes.

2. **History Will Absolve Me: Constitutional Defense and Social Reform** — Fidel Castro; defense delivered 16 October 1953, subsequently reconstructed and published; excerpted teaching edition by Brown University. https://library.brown.edu/create/modernlatinamerica/chapters/chapter-4-cuba/primary-documents-w-accompanying-discussion-questions/document-no-10-history-will-absolve-me-by-fidel-castro-ruiz/

4. **The Sierra Maestra Manifesto of 12 July 1957** — Fidel Castro, Felipe Pazos, and Raúl Chibás; text printed in Bohemia on 28 July 1957 and reproduced in public documentary editions. https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Manifiesto_de_Sierra_Maestra
5. **José Antonio Echeverría’s Political Testament, 13 March 1957** — José Antonio Echeverría; statement dated 13 March 1957, CeDeMA digital documentary reproduction. https://iwww.cedema.org/digital_items/3140
6. **American Intelligence and the July 26 Movement after the April Strike** — U.S. Department of State, Bureau of Intelligence and Research; Report No. 7780, 15 August 1958; FRUS 1958–1960, Cuba, vol. VI, document 124. https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1958-60v06/d124
7. **The General Strike of 9 April 1958 in Embassy Reporting** — U.S. embassy and State Department dispatches, consolidated in FRUS 1958–1960, Cuba, vol. VI, document 49. https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1958-60v06/d49
8. **The Caracas Pact and the Coalition against Batista** — Frente Cívico Revolucionario signatories, 20 July 1958; documented in FRUS 1958–1960, Cuba, vol. VI, document 107. https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1958-60v06/d107
9. **Recognition of the Urrutia Government, 7 January 1959** — U.S. Secretary of State to the President, 7 January 1959; FRUS 1958–1960, Cuba, vol. VI, document 217. https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1958-60v06/d217

Each is an attributed **editorial paraphrase** and not an independently collated original-language quotation or certified translation. The 1955 Yale manifesto appears as an authenticated catalogued holding in the 30-entry register but is not falsely represented as directly read in a primary source window. The 1940 constitutional source is a published English-language reproduction; original assembly manuscript facsimiles were not collated. The image study is a separate pedagogy box and not miscounted as a tenth source window.

## 6. Bibliographic and visual limitations

- The 30-entry register includes 12 published or catalogued documentary records and 18 scholarly books. Major titles, selected publishers, dates, and direct documentary URLs were verified with university/publisher catalogs and the U.S. Department of State. This is not a guarantee of exhaustive edition-by-edition bibliographic validation; several titles explicitly require selection of a particular edition before page-specific citation.
- All primary-source passages are labeled editorial paraphrases. No original-language facsimiles, handwritten records, or full page-by-page manuscript collation were conducted. FRUS documents are reproduced from the Department of State’s published collection.
- All three map exercises are text-guided and require an external historical atlas; no copyrighted cartographic plates are embedded. Documentary Image Study directs students to authenticated, properly catalogued imagery, but the HTML does not claim to reproduce a specific photograph or clear image rights.
- Exact visual parity against an independent Chapter 31 reference artifact was not established. The structure and responsive mobile behavior follow the verified Chapter 79 publication template. Live deployment remains a separate operational step.
- This audit does not certify that every interpretive claim is undisputed; competing interpretations of labor organization, the role of the PSP, and the relative importance of armed forces and civic opposition are identified as historical debates.
