## III. Historical and source corrections completed

1. **La Higuera:** October 8 capture and October 9 execution, with the October 11 Helms memorandum explicitly distinguished from later memoir claims and October 13 records. See https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v31/d171 .
2. **Foco theory:** Guevara's developing theory distinguished from Régis Debray's 1967 systematization, guided by Matt D. Childs, *Journal of Latin American Studies* 27 (1995), 593–624, https://scholarcommons.sc.edu/hist_facpub/191/ .
3. **Guatemala:** the MR-13's 1960 origins, the late-1962 FAR coalition, internal PGT relationships, mid-1960s factional disputes, and Turcios Lima's March 6, 1965 letter treated through clearly attributed scholarly access, **not a fabricated source transcription**.
4. **Venezuela:** Carúpano (May 4, 1962), Puerto Cabello (June 2), electoral arrangements, and subsequent FALN operations distinguished. https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/g/guerrillas/ .
5. **Peru:** failed 1963 ELN incursion separated from the MIR's 1965 fronts in Cusco, Junín and Piura and the reconstituted ELN front in La Mar, Ayacucho. https://lineadetiempo.iep.org.pe/public/6/las-guerrillas-del-mir%26lang%3Den .
6. **Congo and Bolivia:** national conditions, fractured political alliances, transnational intervention, local supporters' and civilians' positions, and uncertain diary editions distinguished.
7. **Argentina and Brazil:** Masetti's 1963–1964 expedition, the distinct ERP/Montoneros histories, and the September 1969 ALN/MR-8 Elbrick kidnapping presented without falsely attributing the kidnapping's direct authorization to Marighella. https://guides.loc.gov/brazil-us-relations/ambassador-charles-burke-elbrick-kidnapping .
8. **Uruguay:** Tupamaros' 1972 defeat distinguished from 1973 dictatorship; political causes not flattened to a single guerrilla explanation.
9. **OLAS poster:** Library of Congress **item 2017649191**, Yanker Poster Collection, call **POS 6 – Cuba, no. 15 (C size)**. Official catalog and documented bullet-hole imagery linked at https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2017649191/ . The original image **is not embedded** because publication rights may be restricted.
10. **Documentary honesty:** no invented dialogue, diary lines or alleged verbatim copies. Source-window descriptions explicitly distinguish identified edited editions, a later 1967 Congressional Record reprint, scholarly contextual links, and direct contemporaneous evidence.

## V. Explicit limitations

1. **Original Chapter 31 shell/template not supplied.** The rebuilt page retains the MGU parchment/ink aesthetic and standard structural concepts with restored responsive behavior, but exact CSS/HTML byte parity with Chapter 31 cannot be independently certified.
2. **Sources are not manuscript-facsimile-collated.** All nine windows are documented editorial paraphrases or contextual source studies. The March 6, 1965 Turcios Lima letter is discussed through Arturo Taracena Arriola's scholarship, not quoted from an independently verified full original. The Congo diary window identifies a translated book edition. The Tricontinental declaration link is a contemporary *Congressional Record* reproduction, not the conference's first manuscript or necessarily a neutral translation.
3. **Three map exercises are text-guided atlas studies**, not original rendered historical-cartography plates. This fact is clearly disclosed in the chapter.
4. **Rights restrictions:** the LOC OLAS poster is linked and analyzed without embedding copyrighted imagery. The LOC warns that publication may be restricted.
5. **Scholarly bibliography:** 28 named identifiable primary, secondary, and reference resources, some accessed through publisher/catalog/study pages. Full archival/pagination verification of every edition and live HTTP-success testing for every external URL were not performed. Access leads are labeled, avoiding misleading facsimile claims.
6. **Live-site deployment** at maloneuniversity.org was not tested. Only standalone HTML behavior was verified.
