## 3. Major new historical omission: the June 24, 2026 Venezuela earthquakes

The manuscript and prior approved outline both omit a historically central disaster that occurred **within the chapter cutoff**. On **24 June 2026**, two destructive earthquakes of magnitude **7.2 and 7.5** struck north-central Venezuela. PAHO's 14 July report documented **4,333 deaths**, **16,740 injuries**, and **17,907 people displaced** at that reporting date. Later UN documentation reports **6,125 deaths**; other subsequent investigations use still higher counts. These are dated measures, not interchangeable final totals. The disaster severely affected housing, hospitals, public services, displacement, humanitarian response, and the interim government's performance.

**Mandated integration:** add a substantial chronologically situated earthquake account to §115.12 (everyday life/services); include affected locations and internally displaced people in §115.18; discuss the disaster's effects on institutional legitimacy in §§115.7, 115.17 and 115.20 as appropriate. Add June 24 and an updated reporting date to the chronology. Use PAHO/UN/OCHA records in the geography, quantitative, source-study and final-reflection apparatus. This is a necessary addition to the previous plan, rather than optional thematic expansion.

Sources: USGS https://www.usgs.gov/programs/landslide-hazards/science/2026-venezuela-sequence-earthquake-triggered-landslide-hazards ; PAHO 14 July https://www.paho.org/en/news/14-7-2026-venezuela-earthquake-health-response-enters-early-recovery-phase-focus-shifts ; UN Fact-Finding Mission A/HRC/63/57 https://www.ecoi.net/en/file/local/2145085/g2612099.pdf ; UNICEF 3 August https://www.unicef.org/media/183521/file/Venezuela-Humanitarian-Situation-Report-No.7-%28Earthquake-Response%29%2C25-July-3-August-2026.pdf.pdf

## 4. Factual verification and corrections

### A. Established / properly attributed matters

1. **2024 election:** Carter Center's final report rejects the integrity of the official process and emphasizes the lack of published polling-station results. The chapter's high-level description is appropriate, but §115.2 needs figures, dated evidence and substantial context. Primary report: https://www.cartercenter.org/publication/final-report-observation-of-the-2024-presidential-election-in-venezuela/
2. **3 January operation and 5 January interim inauguration:** established as events; do not equate the U.S. justification with legal authorization. Official Venezuelan inauguration reporting: https://mppre.gob.ve/publicacion/8194-0
3. **Casualties:** roughly 75 in an initial U.S. assessment as reported January 6 versus 100 stated by Venezuelan Interior Minister Diosdado Cabello in reporting January 7/8. The draft appropriately attributes figures; further develop their provenance and uncertainty. https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/01/06/maduro-raid-death-toll/ and https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/venezuelas-interior-minister-says-100-people-died-us-attack-2026-01-08/
4. **TSJ succession:** constitutional judgment No. 0001, issued 3 January 2026 and interpreting Articles **234 and 239** in light of Article **335**; text can be cited directly. https://accesoalajusticia.org/la-sc-ordena-que-delcy-rodriguez-presidencia-de-la-republica/ ; court link reported as https://historico.tsj.gob.ve/decisiones/scon/enero/352131-0001-3126-2026-26-0001
5. **February 19 Amnesty Law:** confirmed, but describe substantive exclusions and the law's implementation. The EUAA July 2026 country report also discusses the 24 April announcement concerning its application. https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2026-05-19/venezuela-law-grants-amnesty-for-past-political-acts/ ; https://www.euaa.europa.eu/venezuela-country-focus/141-amnesty-law
6. **September 2026 UN fact-finding report:** confirms persistent repressive structures despite prisoner releases. Mission estimated **800–1,000 people released September 2025–August 2026**, but did not state that all releases occurred after January. https://www.ohchr.org/ or report https://www.ecoi.net/en/file/local/2145085/g2612099.pdf
7. **January 29 hydrocarbon reform:** confirmed and substantive. UNCTAD summarizes changes to private-sector participation; reform is not equivalent to independently realized oil output/investment. https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/investment-policy-monitor/measures/5584/venezuela-bolivarian-republic-of-allows-private-participation-in-primary-hydrocarbons-activities

9. **UNHCR:** nearly **7.9 million** displaced Venezuelan refugees/migrants abroad, not 2026 departures, according to UNHCR's country overview. https://www.unhcr.org/emergencies/venezuela-situation
10. **UN Security Council discussion:** actual 5 January 2026 meeting and Secretary-General statement. https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/statements/2026-01-05/secretary-generals-remarks-the-security-council-venezuela

12. **October return of Machado:** Reuters and other reporting confirm October 25, 2026. Her status as 2025 Nobel Peace Prize laureate is independently confirmed. https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/venezuela-opposition-leader-maria-corina-machado-returns-to-venezuela-4971229 and https://www.nobelpeaceprize.org/laureates/2025
