## D. Consolidated historical corrections

### 1. El Mozote and forensic numerical categories

§91.5 now distinguishes **117 individually distinguishable skeletons in the 1992 field excavation** at the El Mozote convent site from **143 individuals identified after laboratory analysis of mixed remains**, comprising **136 children and adolescents and 7 adults**. These are numbers from a particular excavation site, **not** the total deaths in El Mozote and nearby communities. The second source window adds this precision. Grounding: Inter-American Court, *Massacres of El Mozote and Nearby Places v. El Salvador* (2012), §231, and EAAF studies. https://corteidh.scjn.gob.mx/buscador/doc?doc=casos_sentencias%2FCasoMasacresMozoteVsElSalvador_FondoReparacionesCostas.htm

### 2. El Calabozo

§91.5 uses the Salvadoran Truth Commission's conclusion that **more than 200 civilians** were deliberately killed by troops of the Atlacatl Battalion near El Calabozo on August 22, 1982. It does not present that assessment as a complete forensic census. Source: *From Madness to Hope* (1993), case discussion, https://www.trialinternational.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Truth-Commission-Report.pdf.

### 3. Plan de Sánchez

§91.14 attributes **approximately 268 victims** to the Inter-American case record, distinguishing this figure from a mathematically exact and exhaustive casualty census. Grounding: Inter-American Court, *Plan de Sánchez Massacre v. Guatemala* (2004), original merits findings. https://corteidh.or.cr/ver_ficha_tecnica.cfm?lang=es&nId_Ficha=202

### 4. Dos Erres, Río Negro and Guatemala's CEH

The chapter preserves the distinction between **at least 162 recovered human remains** in Dos Erres investigations and the broader number killed; keeps the Río Negro sequence of massacres separate from the dam/resettlement history; and maintains the CEH's **626 documented massacres**, **42,275 documented victims**, estimated **more than 200,000 killed or disappeared**, **83% Maya** among recorded victims and **93% attribution** to state forces or their associates. Those are not interchangeable denominators or a fully enumerated list of every person killed. Sources: National Security Archive Dos Erres cables, CEH, and Notre Dame Peace Accords Matrix, https://peaceaccords.nd.edu/implementation/truth-or-reconciliation-mechanism-1999-9.

### 5. U.S. military assistance to Guatemala

§91.17 now distinguishes 1977 restrictions on direct aid, authorized/commercial sales, limited military training restarted in 1983, intelligence cooperation, and the **December 21, 1990 suspension of materiel assistance including cash sales** amid unresolved investigations of the killing of Michael DeVine and other abuses. The December 1990 measure did **not** immediately terminate every military education arrangement. Sources: U.S. State Department historical summary, 1995 Senate Intelligence Committee hearings, and December 21, 1990 State Department statement. https://1997-2001.state.gov/background_notes/guatemala_0398_bgn.html and https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/sites-default-files-hearings-hearingonguatema00unit.pdf.

### 6. Plan Victoria 82

The final chapter correctly dates the plan to **June 1982** and explicitly avoids reproducing the National Security Archive's anomalous '1962' metadata in one listing. Source: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/33855-document-9-campaign-plan-victoria-82-june-16-1982.

### 7. Reports, agreements and legal findings

- The 1978 Salvadoran IACHR report documents violations involving the security services and ORDEN; original conclusions: https://hrlibrary.umn.edu/iachr/country-reports/elsalvador1978-concl.html.
- The Salvadoran Truth Commission is **not** treated as a court; similarly, the Guatemalan CEH did not issue individual criminal convictions.
- The 2013 Ríos Montt verdict is identified as **subsequently annulled**, without representing it as an unqualified final conviction.
- The Chapultepec agreement is correctly dated **January 16, 1992**, and Guatemala's final peace settlement **December 29, 1996**.
- The source windows remain **editorial paraphrases, not verbatim collations** of original archival pages.
