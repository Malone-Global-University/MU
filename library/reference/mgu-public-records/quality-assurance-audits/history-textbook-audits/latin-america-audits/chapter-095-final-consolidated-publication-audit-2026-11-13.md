## 3. Specific historical corrections performed

**Argentina, 1991–1992 convertibility.** Corrected §95.8 and chronology: original Law 23.928 effective April 1, 1991 fixed **10,000 australes per U.S. dollar**; the peso was introduced January 1, 1992 at **one peso = 10,000 australes = US$1** under Decree 2128/1991. Both original laws are linked and analyzed in their own source window or supporting record.

**Mexico’s 1992 land reform.** Corrected §95.11 and the documentary study: original Agrarian Law **Article 79** covers use/usufruct and leasing; **Article 80** covers permitted transfers of parcelary rights; **Articles 81–83** cover the procedure for adoption of full private title; **Article 46** addresses usufruct as security under legal conditions. No claim of automatic ejido privatization or unrestricted ordinary mortgage of all parcels remains.

**Mexico’s 1982 debt emergency.** Avoids presenting the episode as an indiscriminate repudiation of every obligation. Explicitly identifies inability to meet scheduled principal payments and requests for temporary suspension/restructuring.

**Mexican trade chronology.** WTO official register confirms GATT membership from **24 August 1986**, distinguished from the July 1986 accession decision; both predate NAFTA.

**NAFTA chronology.** Distinguishes October **7, 1992 initialing**, December **17, 1992 formal signing**, 1993 supplemental labor/environment arrangements, and **January 1, 1994 entry into force**.

**Telmex.** Separates the approximately US$1.76 billion December 1990 **controlling share block** from broader later share offerings, explains differential equity/voting rights, and uses the original December 1990 government concession as the Primary Source Window. Later financial retrospectives are identified as retrospective or interested commentary.

**Statistical attribution.** Replaced unsourced aggregate assertions with source-specific numerical tables. Results are measured as published; no figure is represented as proof of a single reform’s causal effect.

## 6. Production limits / exclusions

1. **Chapter 31 parity:** Original Chapter 31 model not supplied; available Chapter 94 reference used. Do not claim byte-exact template identity.
2. **Archival collation:** Nine document windows are explicitly labeled source-studies/paraphrases, not facsimile-collated quotations. Legal texts and statistical catalog records were verified selectively, not every printed edition in every archive.
3. **Cartography:** Three map studies are historically guided investigations requiring an atlas; no authenticated map plates are embedded.
4. **Photography:** One specifically identified archival photograph is linked with source metadata rather than reproduced in the HTML.
5. **Production hosting:** File rendering was tested locally; site upload, server routing, actual external link availability in a deployed environment, and live website integration remain unverified.
6. **PDF visual review:** Web extraction supplied published statistical tables. Remote screenshot services returned errors for two large World Bank PDF downloads; figures were checked against extracted tabular text but no comprehensive facsimile comparison is claimed.
7. **Scope:** The chapter covers historical development and published historical data, not contemporary economic-policy advice; results should not be read as claims that a single reform caused every measured change.
