## 6. Disclosed scholarly limitations

The 2026 cutoff is an evidence boundary rather than a claim that ongoing conflicts or legal cases were resolved. Organizational membership figures, government ties, trafficking-market estimates and causal claims about the effect of emergency powers remain contested. The editorial review checked decisive high-risk records, not every historical statement against an independent archive or the future availability of every external URL. Source Window 2 explicitly identifies secondary reporting where a complete original Venezuelan operation communiqué was not available, and source windows retain labeled paraphrases rather than fabricated quotations. The prison and homicide series have different legal definitions and reference dates, and their qualifications must remain visible.
