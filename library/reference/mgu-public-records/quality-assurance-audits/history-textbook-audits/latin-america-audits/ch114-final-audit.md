## 4. Scholarly limits and responsible disclosure

1. Links to authentic external institutional records were checked by discovery and source inspection during the revision; this was **not a complete automated HTTP availability check of every external bibliography link**, and those destinations may change.
2. The ten source windows mostly contain **clearly identified paraphrases**, not verbatim diplomatic transcriptions or document facsimiles. Source documents remain separately linked for students.
3. The 2026 PRODES result is a **preliminary** August 2025–July 2026 rate, not a finalized calendar-year total. This distinction remains in the chapter.
4. USGS 2025 lithium production figures are **estimates** and may be revised in later annual reports.
5. Some 2026 trade, mineral-policy and port-regulation proceedings were still developing at the chapter's November 16 historical cutoff. Subsequent outcomes are not retroactively inserted.
6. The chapter was tested for technical structure and spot-checked against high-authority dated institutional sources. It has **not** undergone independent external academic peer review or comprehensive line-by-line verification of every interpretive assertion.
7. Identical CSS lineage and general shell were retained from the uploaded HTML; independent source-code parity against an unavailable separate Chapter 31 reference is not asserted.
