## 4. Historical and documentary verification

1. **Deng's offices:** Replaced the ambiguous suggestion that November 1989 was the date he relinquished every military commission post. He retired as chairman of the Party's Central Military Commission in **November 1989**, and of the separate PRC state Central Military Commission in **March 1990**. His lack of formal senior office at the time of the 1992 tour remains correct. Verified using 1989 contemporary reporting and the PRC's official Jiang Zemin career history.
2. **Biographical updates:** Jiang Zemin **(1926–2022)** and Zhu Rongji **(1928–2026)**. Zhu died August 12, 2026, after the chapter's historical endpoint; the update belongs in the encyclopedia/reference entry rather than narrating post-2012 history.
3. **WTO Protocol:** Corrected the overbroad description of the **15-year sunset**. Section 15(d) refers specifically to Section 15(a)(ii)'s special price-comparability methodology; it does not mean that every anti-dumping or safeguard rule expired on the same schedule. China's accession was approved November 10, 2001; Protocol WT/L/432 is dated November 23; membership commenced December 11.
4. **Fiscal series:** Central-government share of *budgetary revenue*: **22.0% in 1993; 55.7% in 1994; 52.2% in 2000; 51.1% in 2010**, checked against China's National Bureau of Statistics. Ratios of budget revenue to nominal GDP depend on historical GDP revision vintages and should not be combined uncritically with IMF's differently defined fiscal revenue measures.
5. **SOE employment:** The tabulated approximate urban state-owned-unit employment figures align with historical NBS series: 1995 **112.61 million**, 2000 **81.02 million**, 2005 **64.88 million**; urban collective employment 1995 **31.47 million**, 2000 **14.99 million**, 2005 **8.10 million**. They refer to *urban employment in ownership-defined units*, not a count of all people working for firms with any state ownership.
6. **2012 external sector:** NBS reports **US$2,048.9bn merchandise exports**, **US$1,817.8bn merchandise imports**, **US$111.7bn actually utilized nonfinancial FDI**, **US$3,311.6bn foreign reserves**, and **163.36 million migrant workers leaving their home townships**; Claude's rounded entries are supportable.
7. **Extreme poverty:** Replaced vague claims of roughly 60%, one-third, and under one-tenth with an internally consistent older World Bank series for **$1.90/day at 2011 PPP**: **57.0% (1993), 31.9% (2002), 6.5% (2012)**. Noted that the first poverty datum is **1993** within an otherwise approximately 1992 comparison column. These are *historical-methodology* figures republished by NWCCW/NBS/UNICEF (2018), not the latest international poverty line or China's national poverty series.
8. **Primary excerpts:** Verified the short Hong Kong Basic Law Article 5 phrase against the official Hong Kong SAR Basic Law and Deng's phrase concerning planning and market forces against a posted English version of his 1992 talks; others remain clearly labeled paraphrases. Source appendix added with traceable document titles/identifiers and data definitions; no unverified page-specific citations were invented.
9. **Housing policy:** Guofa [1998] No. 23 was adopted in July 1998 and directed ending in-kind housing allocation from the second half of that year, subject to local implementation. The manuscript's interpretation is accurate when the policy is not described as instantaneous nationwide home ownership.
10. **2008 stimulus:** The government announced a planned **RMB 4 trillion** multiyear investment envelope on **November 9, 2008**, which included local/credit financing and was not solely central-budget spending. The manuscript appropriately distinguishes the total package from central appropriations.
