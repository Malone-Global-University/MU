## 3. Factual checks and consolidated publication corrections

1. **2020 floating population:** Revised §75.7 to distinguish **375.82 million** in the defined floating population from the additional **116.94 million** with registration/residence separation within the same municipal district; **492.76 million** is the larger separation total. The previous description inaccurately treated the narrower floating-population statistic as including intra-district movement. Primary source: 2020 Seventh Census main results (published 2021), `https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202105/t20210510_1817185.html`.
2. **Birth-series peak:** Revised §75.15 to specify **25.29 million in 1987 as the high point within the UNICEF/NBS 1980–2017 series**, not as an unqualified all-time peak. Source: UNICEF China, *Atlas of Social Indicators* (2018), figure 1.8, `https://www.unicef.org/china/sites/unicef.org.china/files/2019-02/EN%20Atlas%202018-FINAL.pdf`.
3. **New-forms employment:** Revised §75.29 to attribute the **84-million** estimate to an All-China Federation of Trade Unions report publicized in **July 2023**, not 2021. Definition differs from platform workers alone. `https://english.www.gov.cn/policies/policywatch/202307/20/content_WS64b8ce9ec6d0868f4e8ddf7f.html`.
4. **2025 new-energy vehicles:** Revised §75.37 to specify **53.9% of domestic passenger-car retail sales** (CPCA; includes battery-electric and plug-in hybrids), distinct from **47.9% of all vehicle sales** in broader CAAM statistics. `https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/chinas-pv-retail-sales-exceed-2374-million-units-in-2025-is-2026-under-heavy-pressure-2010717117689475073`.
5. **Ne Zha 2:** Replaced §75.46's “needs verification” wording with the supportable 2025 distinction of highest-grossing animated film at the worldwide box office; no unsupported exact lifetime gross was inserted. `https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/highest-box-office-film-gross-for-an-animation`.
6. **2020 urban percentage:** Corrected the urbanization evidence table in §75.1 from a loose “roughly 63 percent” to **63.89%** (2020 census). `https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202105/t20210510_1817185.html`.

## 5. Editorial and evidentiary limitations

The six source windows are paraphrases tied to identifiable documentary originals, and the appendix links to public records. Exact edition-specific quotation and page-number verification was not claimed; no purported verbatim excerpts were introduced. Composite scenes should remain explicitly illustrative, and the chapter's treatment of SARS, COVID-19, demographic policy, surveillance, and environmental impacts must preserve the distinction among government claims, contemporary documentation, and later scholarly analysis. Later statistics are dated to their proper reporting year and their publication year is identified in the source register. Given the rapid pace of contemporary social change, update such data during any future new edition rather than silently changing a locked historical edition.
