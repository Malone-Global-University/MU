## Independent historical verification — findings

**Confirmed:**

- The 20th Central Committee's Fifth Plenum met October 26–29, 2026. Its official communiqué gives 162 full and 117 alternate attendees, names the confirmed expulsions, identifies ten alternate-member promotions, and records Zhang Shengmin's elevation to the Politburo. The source text is at https://en.spp.gov.cn/2026-10/29/c_1223798.htm. The original 2022 composition was 205 full members and 171 alternates. The drop from 376 originally selected members to 279 attending does **not** prove 97 expulsions or vacancies; attendance and membership are not identical categories.
- Official defense announcements confirm investigation of Zhang Youxia and Liu Zhenli in January 2026, expulsions through the Politburo's September 21 decision, and confirmation at the October plenum: https://eng.mod.gov.cn/2025xb/N/T/16439139.html and https://eng.mod.gov.cn/2025xb/N/T/16488081.html. Associated Press reports that Xi Jinping and Zhang Shengmin were the two remaining CMC members as of the October meeting: https://apnews.com/article/afc961e9c125a2e42732f9914e67e70f. The **communiqué does not itself establish the two-person composition**, so this is separately attributed.
- Jimmy Lai's conviction on December 15, 2025 and 20-year sentence on February 9, 2026 are supported by RTHK reporting and the UK government's January–June 2026 Hong Kong report: https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/en/component/k2/1843173-20260209.htm ; https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/six-monthly-report-on-hong-kong-january-to-june-2026/six-monthly-report-on-hong-kong-1-january-to-30-june-2026. The Hong Kong 47 sentences (45 defendants, 4–10 years) are supported by AP's November 2024 coverage.
- Italy left the Belt and Road framework in December 2023 and Panama followed in February 2025, according to a named BRI participation registry: https://greenfdc.org/countries-of-the-belt-and-road-initiative-bri/.
- International Campaign for Tibet's July 3, 2026 fact sheet records 159 known self-immolations inside China since 2009 and 11 in exile. The book's broader description of more than 150 is consistent; the figures are advocacy-compiled and not an official census: https://savetibet.org/tibetan-self-immolations/.
- World Bank *China Economic Update* (December 2024), PDF executive summary page 1, confirms a RMB 10 trillion off-budget/local-government hidden-debt swap over five years: https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/f66f7093d7be0141fe43156e5968c466-0070012024/original/CEU-December-2024-EN-Final.pdf.
- The September 2025 announced 2035 climate goal is a **7–10% reduction in economy-wide net greenhouse-gas emissions from peak levels**, not an emissions reduction already achieved: https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202509/25/content_WS68d47dcac6d00ca5f9a066a5.html.
- The Fifteenth Five-Year Plan was adopted March 12, 2026; the government's 2026 growth goal was 4.5–5%; the National Development and Reform Commission describes 109 major projects: https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202603/13/content_WS69b4b144c6d00ca5f9a09dfd.html and https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/wsdwhfz/202606/t20260601_1405598.html.
- Trump met Xi in Beijing May 14, 2026 (official Chinese and White House records) and Xi visited Washington September 24–25, 2026 (White House). The commercial truce extension through January 10, 2027 was confirmed by China's commerce ministry: https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202609/28/content_WS6aba53c5c6d00ca5f9a0d803.html.
