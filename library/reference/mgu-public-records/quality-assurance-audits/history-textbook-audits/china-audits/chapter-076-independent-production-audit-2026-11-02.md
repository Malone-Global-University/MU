## Historically critical corrections and updates

1. **§76.2 — Central Committee head count is wrong/misleading.** The 20th CCP Central Committee elected in 2022 consisted of **205 full members PLUS 171 alternates, a total of 376**, not “about two hundred full members and alternates.” Present the 2022 organization as the starting cohort and explain subsequent vacancies/expulsions rather than reporting 2022 composition as static in 2026. Sources: [Chinese government full members](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/topnews/202210/22/content_WS6353c5d2c6d0a757729e18a2.html), [Chinese official account](https://en.moj.gov.cn/2022-10/25/c_823959.htm).

2. **§§76.2, 76.4, 76.8, 76.11–76.13 — Critical 2026 omission.** The Fifth Plenum of the 20th Central Committee occurred **October 26–29, 2026**, well inside the cutoff; it confirmed **33 expulsions**, including senior military and civilian figures (among them Zhang Youxia, Liu Zhenli, and Ma Xingrui), recorded **162 full-member and 117 alternate-member attendees (279 total)**, and elevated **Zhang Shengmin** to the Politburo. The Central Military Commission had been reduced to **Xi Jinping and Zhang Shengmin** as its remaining members as reported through the cutoff. Discuss official rationale and analytical implications separately, without extrapolating confidently about combat readiness. Update the 2026 status of the Politburo and CMC instead of presenting their originally elected 2022 composition as the current complement. Sources: [October 29 plenum communique (English and Chinese)](https://centerforchinaanalysis.asiasociety.org/p/communique-of-the-fifth-plenum-of), [Reuters on the expulsions](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-communist-party-confirms-expulsion-33-officials-2026-10-29/), [AP coverage](https://apnews.com/article/afc961e9c125a2e42732f9914e67e70f).

3. **§76.3 — NPC appointment terminology.** The NPC **decides on the choice of the State Council premier on the president's nomination**; don't say it *elects the premier*. The legislature elects some offices, but the constitutional formulation for the premier differs. Source: [PRC Constitution](https://en.moj.gov.cn/2021-06/22/c_634901.htm).

4. **§§76.22–76.23 — Major 2025–2026 Hong Kong omission.** Include the December 15, 2025 conviction and February 9, 2026 **20-year sentence of Jimmy Lai**, with careful distinction among charges, court findings, official government position and civil-liberties critiques; include the legal and civic consequences as of the cutoff. Source: [RTHK's contemporary reporting](https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/en/component/k2/1843173-20260209.htm), [Hong Kong government response](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202602/09/P2026020900687.htm), [UK government semiannual Hong Kong report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/six-monthly-report-on-hong-kong-january-to-june-2026/six-monthly-report-on-hong-kong-1-january-to-30-june-2026).

5. **§§76.44 and its source windows — Arbitral award legal status.** The 2016 Annex VII UNCLOS tribunal decided maritime entitlements and certain conduct, **not sovereignty over land or maritime boundary delimitation**. The award is **final and binding on the parties under UNCLOS**, notwithstanding China's rejection; avoid saying it is binding merely “in the tribunal's view.” Explain the separation between binding status and compliance/enforcement. Source: [Tribunal press release](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/report/2016/ph-cn-20160712-press-release-no-11-english.htm), [Australian DFAT note](https://www.dfat.gov.au/news/marking-8th-anniversary-south-china-sea-arbitral-award).

6. **§76.46 — Specify the 2026 U.S.–China truce extension.** The reported new date was **January 10, 2027**, rather than vague “early 2027.” May 2026 Trump's China visit and September 24, 2026 Xi's Washington state visit are independently supported by contemporaneous official U.S. records. As of cutoff, report negotiation developments as developments, not lasting settlement. Sources: [White House September 25](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/09/president-trump-first-lady-conclude-historic-china-state-visit/), [Reuters September 23](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/us-treasurys-bessent-chinas-he-meet-unfinished-business-before-trump-xi-summit-2026-09-23/), [ICIS report](https://www.icis.com/explore/resources/news/2026/09/24/11234650/us/).

7. **§76.52 — The 15th Five-Year Plan is verified, but improve documentary specificity.** The NPC approved its outline March 12, 2026; 2026 growth target was **4.5–5%** and **109 major projects** appeared in six areas. Distinguish a medium-term plan from the annual government work report. Sources: [State Council report](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202603/13/content_WS69b4b144c6d00ca5f9a09dfd.html), [legislative adoption report](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202603/13/content_WS69b36c16c6d00ca5f9a09d9a.html), [SCIO briefing](https://english.scio.gov.cn/pressroom/2026-03/27/content_118409291_2.html).

8. **§76.51 — Clarify 2025 climate target.** The 2035 pledge was a targeted reduction in **economy-wide net greenhouse-gas emissions of 7–10% from peak levels**, not a guarantee of achieved outcomes. Source: [Chinese MFA September 2025](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/xw/zyxw/202509/t20250925_11716503.html).
