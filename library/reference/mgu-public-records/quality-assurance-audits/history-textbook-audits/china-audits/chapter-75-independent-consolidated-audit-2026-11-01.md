## Historical and statistical verification

1. **2025 demographic baseline verified:** NBS 2025 communiqué (published February 28, 2026), https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202602/t20260228_1962661.html — urban residents 67.89%, total population 1,404.89 million, aged 60+ 323.38 million (23.0%), migrant workers 301.15 million (outside hometown 180.06m; local 121.09m). Original draft's rounded figures are sound; categories must remain explicit.
2. **2025 internet users verified:** CNNIC, released February 5, 2026; government summary https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202602/05/content_WS698442cac6d00ca5f9a08edc.html — 1.125 billion, penetration 80.1%.
3. **2014 soil survey verified and clarified:** April 17 report, https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fzggw/jgsj/njs/sjdt/201404/t20140418_1194725.html — 16.1% of test points, 19.4% cultivated-land test points exceeded relevant standards. NOT the proportion of all land area known to be polluted. Added precise qualifications.
4. **Retirement reform verified:** NPC Standing Committee September 13, 2024; effective 2025 over 15 years (not immediately 2024): https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2024-10-17/china-national-legislature-adopts-decision-to-gradually-raise-retirement-ages/. Added the phased age changes.
5. **2025 updated nationally determined contribution verified:** September 2025 2035 pledge is **7–10% economy-wide net GHG emissions below peak levels**, subject to effort; not an unconditional achieved reduction. https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/mfa_eng/xw/zyxw/202509/t20250925_11716513.html ; https://www.iea.org/policies/29812-second-ndc-of-peoples-republic-of-china. Added precise wording.
6. **Vehicle market:** NEV includes battery EVs and plug-in hybrids; CPCA retail share in 2025 ≈54%; do not confuse NEV with BEVs, or retail with wholesale: https://cnevpost.com/2026/01/07/china-nev-retail-1-387-million-dec-2025-preliminary-cpca/.
7. **SARS removals verified:** Health Minister Zhang Wenkang and Beijing Mayor Meng Xuenong were removed from their Party posts **April 20, 2003**, subsequently replaced in government capacities; avoid collapsing the personnel process into a single governmental dismissal: https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/RL32227.html.
8. **Youth-unemployment measurement:** NBS new 16–24-year-old rate excludes current students; report announced Jan. 17, 2024: https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202401/t20240117_1946641.html. Former and revised series not directly comparable.
9. **Births chronology correction:** Original §75.15 states annual births peaked *in the late 2010s*, historically incorrect. A much higher peak occurred in the 1980s; there was a smaller 2016 post-2000 high (17.86 million) followed by decline, per NBS 2016 https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/201702/t20170228_1467503.html and UNICEF historical series.
10. **COVID restrictions:** State Council joint mechanism's ten measures issued December 7, 2022; statement and provisions independently traceable: https://english.www.gov.cn/statecouncil/ministries/202212/08/content_WS63913c92c6d0a757729e4135.html.
11. **Urbanization definitions:** 2020 census urban resident share 63.89% vs registered urban share 45.4%; 1990 share 26.44%. https://www.stats.gov.cn/WZWSREL2VuZ2xpc2gvUHJlc3NSZWxlYXNlLzIwMjEwNS90MjAyMTA1MTBfMTgxNzE5Mi5odG1s — original's rough numbers are acceptable; use explicit documented values where statistical precision adds instructional value.
