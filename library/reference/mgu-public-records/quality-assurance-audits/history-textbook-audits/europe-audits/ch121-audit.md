## 4. Principal historical verification and corrections

1. **Six million Jewish victims:** Explicitly identified as a reconstructed estimate supported by perpetrator documents and demographic evidence; not claimed as one surviving list. The five killing centers' estimated **2.7 million** Jewish victims are a subset, not an additive extra number. Source: USHMM, *Documenting Numbers of Victims* and *Nazi Killing Centers*.
2. **Nuremberg Laws:** Correctly identifies both laws of **September 15, 1935**, the later implementing regulations and the pseudo-racial system of classification. Primary translation: USHMM, *Nuremberg Laws*.
3. **November Pogrom:** Accurately records **November 9–10, 1938**, and Heydrich's early-November-10 police telegram, with direct link to a published translation (Facing History & Ourselves). Recognizes state direction, not spontaneous disorder.
4. **Disability murder program:** Distinguishes **70,273** deaths in the central T4 gassing phase (January 1940–August 1941) from **250,000–300,000** across the wider Nazi disability murder program and associated actions; no summing of overlapping figures. Primary source: GHDI's **630-PS** translation; historical synthesis: USHMM, *Euthanasia Program*.
5. **Roma and Sinti:** Distinct genocide covered in full §121.8; at least **250,000**, possibly as many as **500,000**, killed per USHMM, with source uncertainty acknowledged. August **2–3, 1944** Auschwitz family-camp liquidation documented.
6. **Babyn Yar:** German perpetrators' report of **33,771 Jews murdered September 29–30, 1941** distinguished from all subsequent killings at the site. Context: USHMM, *Mass Shootings at Babyn Yar*.
7. **Jäger Report:** Dated **December 1, 1941** and described as a perpetrator tally rather than a neutral or complete record. USHMM study of Eyshishok identifies its claim of **137,346** Jewish victims.
8. **Wannsee:** Correctly dated **January 20, 1942** and described as coordinating genocide already under way, not deciding whether it would occur. Section §121.11 links the authenticated original and translated protocol at the House of the Wannsee Conference, German Foreign Office Archive **R 100857, fols. 166–188**. The study window is conspicuously an **editorial paraphrase, not a quotation**.
9. **Killing centers and camps:** Correctly distinguishes Chełmno, Bełżec, Sobibór, Treblinka and Auschwitz-Birkenau; modern USHMM work generally categorizes Majdanek as a concentration camp where mass murder also occurred, rather than an unqualified sixth principal killing center.
10. **Auschwitz:** Museum figures of approximately **1.1 million victims**, including around **one million Jews**, explicitly distinguished from deportations and registrations. Source: Auschwitz-Birkenau State Museum, *The Number of Victims*.
11. **Hungary:** **Approximately 437,000 Jews deported in 147 trains, May 15–July 9, 1944**; defined as deportees in a specific period, with Hungarian gendarmerie participation and Auschwitz destination explained. Source: USHMM, *The Holocaust in Hungary*.
12. **Resistance and 1945:** Warsaw Ghetto Uprising **April 19–May 16, 1943**, not conflated with the citywide Warsaw Uprising of 1944. Auschwitz evacuation January **17–18, 1945**, liberation January **27**, and approximately **7,000** prisoners found in the core complex supported by Auschwitz-Birkenau State Museum.
13. **Source types:** All memorial, demographic, German administrative, and victim-produced sources are clearly distinguished; no witness quotation was invented. The chapter explains when a link provides an institutional source study rather than a digitized facsimile.
