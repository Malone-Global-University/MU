## 1. Authority and remediation summary

The prior first-build audit of `Pasted text(20261114-033301).txt` rejected a nine-section, approximately 5,939-word direct narrative and identified omitted scholarship apparatus, chronology and glossary mismatches, and mobile overflow. The new HTML is a direct holistic expansion of that draft. Historical paragraphs were retained where defensible and redistributed to the twenty approved sections rather than replaced by a generic summary. New material substantially develops postwar reconstruction, Austria, Italian constitutional breakdown, comparative eastern European political systems, labor, welfare, agrarian society, women, banking, national policy alternatives, and international cooperation.

The principal historical safeguards were addressed: (a) the **Creditanstalt crisis of May 1931** is described as a loss announcement, urgent rescue/recapitalization and continuing withdrawals, not simple instantaneous liquidation; (b) **Poland's April 1936 exchange controls** are distinguished from a legal devaluation of the złoty; (c) German **passive resistance ended on 26 September 1923** while the **Ruhr occupation continued until 1925**; (d) Greek refugee figures are characterized as estimates with broader geographical origins; (e) the Red Front Fighters' League's 1929 ban and subsequent illegal activity are distinguished; (f) the 1933 London conference is clearly labeled a short forward-looking epilogue, not part of the chapter's January 1933 endpoint.

## 4. Historical/source checks and documentary limitations

* **Constitutional law:** Weimar's Article 48, including intervention against Länder, temporary suspension of specified rights and parliamentary revocation, is distinguished from Article 54's parliamentary-confidence rule. Direct edited text and English translation: https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/weimar-germany-1918-1933/the-weimar-constitution-august-11-1919
* **Banking history:** Creditanstalt's May 1931 losses, recapitalization, withdrawal pressure and eventual state intervention are represented in line with the IMF historical analysis: https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/001/1998/113/article-A001-en.xml ; contemporaneous official memorandum: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1931v01/d1
* **Poland monetary exception:** April 1936 foreign-exchange restrictions are documented in an American diplomatic telegram; Poland did not simply join the group of countries that devalued formal gold parities: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1936v02/d331
* **British monetary decision:** the September 1931 suspension is supported by the Bank of England's official institutional history: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/about/history
* **International coordination:** Hoover's June 20, 1931 proposal is grounded in his contemporaneous statement: https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/the-presidents-news-conference-671 . The unsuccessful 1933 London conference is treated as an epilogue and has contemporary FRUS records: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1933v01/comp7
* **Social/industrial history:** the 1926 General Strike is connected to contemporary Commons debate: https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/1926-05-12 ; the ILO's constitutional founding is recorded here: https://www.ilo.org/publications/ilo-constitution
* **Elections:** Interactive district-level Reichstag data and their provenance can be checked through GHDI: https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/weimar-germany-1918-1933/interactive-map-reichstag-elections-in-the-weimar-republic-1919-1933

The documentary guide comprises 26 specifically identifiable items, including original constitutional and parliamentary records, FRUS documents, monetary reports, edited data, institutional reference materials, and historiography. It **does not** present editorial paraphrases as verbatim original quotations; a reader doing manuscript-level research should return to original editions or facsimiles. Statistical figures are qualified by year, population, and registration conventions where necessary. Historical geography is text-guided and needs an appropriate period atlas for full map exercises.
