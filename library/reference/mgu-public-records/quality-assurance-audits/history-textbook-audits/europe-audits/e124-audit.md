## Specific publication corrections completed

1. **1949 alliance chronology:** Corrected opening from the erroneous implication of two military alliances in 1949. NATO was established in 1949; Warsaw Pact was not formed until 1955.
2. **German state status:** Clarified that Allied occupation suspended independent exercise of supreme German governmental powers without legally annexing Germany or demonstrating that the German state had ceased to exist. Document: [June 5 Berlin Declaration, FRUS](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1945v03/d165).
3. **Berlin's geography and occupation rights:** Distinguished Berlin's location surrounded by the Soviet occupation zone from its separate four-power administration.
4. **Berlin Airlift:** Distinguished **~2,325,510 U.S. short tons**, approximately **2.11 million metric tonnes**, and **278,228 incoming flights** (the frequently cited US Air Force count) across the full airlift, including operations after the blockade was lifted May 12, 1949. End of airlift September 30, 1949. [US Air Force institutional report](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/3439311/remembering-the-epic-triumph-of-the-berlin-airlift-secretary-kendall-airlift-pa/); the documentary guide additionally links a detailed US Air Forces Europe study. Another counting convention can produce different totals.
5. **May 1949 East German People's Congress voting:** Replaced “roughly 60 percent” with **officially reported 66.1 percent** in the May 15–16 single-list process, explicitly noting disputed counting and voting freedom. [Bundeszentrale für politische Bildung](https://www.bpb.de/themen/nachkriegszeit/grundgesetz-und-parlamentarischer-rat/39040/vom-deutschen-volkskongress-zur-ddr/).
6. **Bonn's federal seat:** Specified the Parliamentary Council's May 10, 1949 vote (**33–29**) and the Bundestag's November 3 confirmation, while preserving the seat's provisional character. [German Bundestag documentary history](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/1949-11-03-regierungssitz-bonn-337836).
7. **Soviet special camps:** Attributed around **43,000 deaths other than executions during 1945–1950** to Soviet camp records rather than giving an undefined or uncontextualized victim total. [German Federal Agency for Civic Education study](https://www.bpb.de/themen/deutschlandarchiv/339546/ein-dunkles-kapitel-sowjetische-sonderhaftanstalten-in-ostdeutschland/).
8. **Documentary mismatch:** The June 5, 1947 Marshall address now links to the [FRUS contemporary speech transcription](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1947v03/d140); the [European Recovery Act, April 3, 1948](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/marshall-plan) is separately identified as implementing legislation, not the speech.
9. **Improved documentary guide:** Retained all twenty-four original entries and added direct links for the Potsdam Protocol, Berlin Declaration, corridor archival report, Churchill speech, currency reform law, airlift historical report, NATO treaty, Basic Law provenance, GDR constitution, and relevant FRUS documentary edition. Translations, historical institutional accounts, archival records, and later scholarship are distinguished.
10. **Glossary taxonomy:** Fixed the incorrect classification of Berlin Blockade/Airlift and Vittles/Plainfare as diplomatic documents.
11. **Mobile design and navigation:** Added a responsive native `<details>` contents menu with all 51 existing navigation destinations, no dependency on new JavaScript, and hid the decorative chapter numeral below 500px to prevent overlap.
