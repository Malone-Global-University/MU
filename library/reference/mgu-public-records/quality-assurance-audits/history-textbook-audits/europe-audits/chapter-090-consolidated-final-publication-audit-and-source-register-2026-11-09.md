## 4. Consolidated historical correction register

The following changes have been implemented directly in the HTML and checked against the linked sources, with emphasis on the facts flagged by the user and the independent prepublication audit:

1. **1807 Duchy of Warsaw agrarian decree (§90.16):** Corrected 1808 to **21 December 1807**; distinguished the decree from the Civil Code becoming effective in 1808; explained personal mobility, landlord property and transition protections; removed unsupported blanket assertion that landlords retained seigneurial judicial courts.
2. **May 20, 1802 slavery law (§90.8, Window 5):** Restored the explicit wording limiting Article II to **other French colonies beyond the Cape of Good Hope**; clarified Article III (trade in covered colonies) and Article IV (ten-year regulatory power); distinguished statutory nonapplication from subsequent coercive restoration elsewhere.
3. **Guadeloupe and French Guiana (§90.8):** Distinguished French military repression, Bonaparte’s **16 July 1802 Guadeloupe consular order**, Guiana’s reenslavement starting in **November 1802**, the **7 December** consular act, and implementation through 1804.
4. **Louisiana Purchase (§90.8):** Stated the treaty signature **30 April 1803** preceded the final defeat at Vertières, **18 November 1803**; removed the misleading claim that Saint-Domingue was already conclusively lost before the Louisiana sale.
5. **Plebiscites (§§90.2, 90.9):** Distinguished official Year VIII count **3,011,007 yes/1,562 no** from Claude Langlois’s finding of roughly **1.55 million actual affirmative returns**; specified 1802 *Bulletin des lois* counts **3,653,600/8,272** and 1804 **3,521,675/2,579**; did not assert identical fraud at all three votes.
6. **Joséphine and Marie-Louise (§90.19):** Separated **15–16 December 1809 civil dissolution**, **January 1810 ecclesiastical annulment**, **11 March Vienna proxy marriage**, **1 April Saint-Cloud civil ceremony**, and **2 April Paris religious ceremony**.
7. **Britain and the Peninsular War (§90.19):** Corrected the false statement that Britain lacked continental allies; British forces fought with Portuguese and Spanish allies in Iberia.
8. **Imperial departmental geography and population (opening, §§90.13, 90.19, quantitative study):** Distinguished **130 directly administered French departments** from separately governed **Illyrian Provinces**, and replaced unsourced **44 million** with the explicitly dated **1812 administrative tally of 42,390,718 for 130 departments**. This count does not include dependent client states.
