3. **UN General Assembly (2022):** ES-11/1 and its 141–5 vote with 35 abstentions, distinguished from a Security Council enforcement measure.
4. **ICC warrants (2023):** the judicial threshold and limited procedural effect distinguished from a conviction.
5. **NATO (2025–2026):** Hague GDP-spending commitments and Ankara's €70 billion Ukraine-assistance pledge distinguished from completed deliveries.
