## 2. Consolidated correction log

1. **France, 1958 referendum (127.7):** corrected the imprecise ~82% figure. Official tabulations show **79.25% metropolitan France** and **85.14% for the larger official French Republic/overseas total excluding Guinea in the official consolidation**. Stated the separate geographic denominators instead of blending them.
2. **Italy, constitutional design and elections (127.9):** distinguished the Constitution's requirement for two chambers and presidential election with regional delegates from the **1948 electoral statutes**. The Senate's single-member districts required more than **65%** for direct election; most seats were allocated by proportional regional methods when no candidate reached the threshold. Clarified why regional delegates did not attend Einaudi's 1948 presidential election (ordinary regional councils had not yet been established).
3. **West German expellees (127.14):** replaced a misleading twelve-million figure for West Germany alone with approximately **7.9–8.0 million in the Federal Republic by 1950**, while approximately **3.6–4.0 million** entered the Soviet occupation zone / East Germany. Noted that population categories and dates must be distinguished from later East-to-West migration.
4. **ECSC chronology (127.14):** separated **signature, April 18, 1951**, from **entry into force, July 23, 1952**.
5. **Italian constitution quotation (source window):** harmonized Article 1's brief English phrase with the official Quirinale translation, **“founded on work.”**
6. **British postwar consensus historiography (127.3):** identified **Ben Pimlott** and his 1988 essay as a scholarly challenge to an overly strong consensus thesis, without eliminating Paul Addison's interpretation.
7. **Italian 1948 campaign documentation:** replaced a generic description of posters with an identifiable **Franco Codognato-attributed Christian Democratic poster**, held at the Museo Nazionale Collezione Salce (catalogue 0500673580, inventory 20549). The original is incomplete; the window notes that physical limitation rather than inventing its missing text.
8. **Eight source windows:** added specific verifiable documentary links and editorial edition/translation qualifications; added a source register at the end of the chapter.
9. **Responsive HTML:** added a native collapsible mobile table of contents and horizontally scrolling wrappers on all historical tables.
