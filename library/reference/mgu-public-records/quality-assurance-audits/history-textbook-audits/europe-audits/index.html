European History Audits

Chapter-level assessment records for the Malone Global University European History textbook series.

Collection Overview

This index lists only the European History chapter audit and verification records documented in the supplied institutional directory inventory, organized by chapter. Chapters without an identified audit are omitted. Where a chapter has multiple reviews, each existing audit or validation record is preserved.

Inventory scope: 57 chapters (67–127); 62 audit and verification records. Links point to the recorded .md files rather than PDF editions. Three separate revision instruction or rebuild directive files are not included because they are not audit reports. A record designated “final” does not by itself establish independent scholarly peer review.
