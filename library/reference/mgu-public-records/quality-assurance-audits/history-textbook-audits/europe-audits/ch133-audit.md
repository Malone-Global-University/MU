## Historical and documentary corrections

1. Added staged market implementation of the ECSC (coal/iron ore/scrap, 10 February 1953; steel, 1 May 1953) and qualified the immediate effects of market opening. Source: [EUR-Lex ECSC Treaty summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/treaty-establishing-the-european-coal-and-steel-community-ecsc-treaty.html).
2. Expanded institutional accountability, the High Authority's revenue and social policies, ministerial and parliamentary relations, the preliminary reference system, and national constitutional responses to Court of Justice doctrine.
3. Expanded regional policy, the budgetary and distributive consequences of agriculture, parliamentary elections and limited legislative authority, and southern enlargement's transitional arrangements.
4. Added direct documentary access links to all eight Primary Source Windows; the EDC study additionally links a UN documentary text and an archival French parliamentary record.
5. Clarified **the original 1957 EEC Treaty had authenticated texts in Dutch, French, German and Italian**; its English-language wording in later official editions is not a fifth 1957 authenticated text. Source: [UN Treaty Collection](https://treaties.un.org/Pages/showDetails.aspx?objid=08000002801413d5).
6. Replaced the vague trade-share statistic with **38% in 1960 and 50% in 1970**, explicitly defined as intra-Community exports as a share of total Community exports. Source: [IMF 1992 study](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/001/1992/094/article-A001-en.xml).
7. Improved population comparison with date-specific demographic observations (founding Six ~167 million in 1957; the Six ~192 million by 1973; Nine ~256 million on 1973 enlargement; Greece ~10 million upon joining, Spain and Portugal ~49 million upon joining). These are not constant-population comparative rates. Source: [INED](https://www.ined.fr/en/all-about-population/data-graphs/population-european-union-1957-2007).
8. Removed the unverified forward navigation URL to still-forthcoming Chapter 134; retained a non-clickable forthcoming label. The Chapter 132 predecessor link remains based on the established exact filename.
