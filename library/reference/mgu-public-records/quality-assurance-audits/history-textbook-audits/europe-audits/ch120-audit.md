## Consolidated historical repairs

1. **1907 Hague Regulations (§120.1):** Articles 42–43 and 46–47 distinguished from Article 48 (ordinary taxes), Article 49 (extra contributions for army or territorial administration), Article 50 (collective penalties), Article 51 (collection procedures and receipts), Article 52 (military requisitions), and Article 55 (usufruct). Primary source: [Yale Avalon, 1907 Hague IV](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/19th_century/hague04.asp).
2. **Vichy and Vel d'Hiv (§120.6):** Vichy's autonomous anti-Jewish legislation retained and the July 16–17, 1942 roundup anchored to **13,152 arrests**, including more than 4,000 children, as a cited police tally. The 1940 and 1941 statutes are linked to original [Légifrance 1940](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000000869361/) and [Légifrance 1941](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000000512616) records.
3. **Polish underground (§120.9):** Membership, political pluralism, Soviet diplomatic conflict, and the 1943 Operation Tempest order remain differentiated from armed strength and political legitimacy. The November 20 order is identified in [Poland's Institute of National Remembrance](https://ipn.gov.pl/pl/historia-z-ipn/236862%2CMarek-Jedynak-Burza-w-Okregu-Radomsko-Kieleckim-Armii-Krajowej.html).
4. **Dutch Hunger Winter (§120.13):** Replaced an unexplained single 20,000-death estimate with NIOD's contrasting excess-mortality definitions and observation of **more than 22,000 direct and indirect victims** in one established interpretation, versus approximately 16,000 excess deaths in another geographical/time-window measure. [NIOD research note](https://www.niod.nl/en/questions/victims-of-the-hunger-winter-hongerwinter-in-numbers/).
5. **Night and Fog (§120.14):** Preserved the explicitly editorial source paraphrase; added direct link to [Nuremberg evidence L-90 at Yale](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/imt/l-90.asp), which includes the December 7, 1941 directive and implementing documents, distinguished in the feature metadata.
6. **French liberation purge (§120.19):** Replaced the conflation of roughly 10,000 summary killings with approximately **8,000–9,000 extrajudicial executions** as differentiated from broader purge deaths including legal executions. The estimate is source-sensitive, not a fixed count.
7. **Warsaw uprising chronology:** Reclassified October 2, 1944 as *uprising/capitulation*, and separately attributed subsequent German destruction of Warsaw to the occupiers' actions. Preserved the distinction from the 1943 Warsaw Ghetto Uprising and the civilian-versus-combatant casualty ranges.
8. **CNR documentary material:** Added an identified [February 21, 1943 instruction to Jean Moulin](https://www.museedelaresistanceenligne.org/media3365-Les-Nouvelles-Instructions-21-fvrier-1943) (Archives nationales 3AG2/181), identified his May 29, 1943 telegram (Archives nationales AG/3(2)/400), and linked the actual [March 15, 1944 CNR program](https://mvr.asso.fr/programme-du-conseil-national-de-la-resistance/).
9. **Labor documentation:** Replaced a vaguely identified German hostage directive in the source list with a specific dated contemporary source, [Sauckel's April 20, 1942 labor-mobilization program](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/nazi-germany-1933-1945/fritz-sauckel-s-labor-mobilization-program-april-20-1942), Nuremberg exhibit 016-PS.
