## Material fact/source repairs applied in the audit-corrected working copy

1. **§71.19:** replaced specific death from cholera with September 1629 death during second Batavia siege, **probably dysentery**; evidence for a modern disease diagnosis is uncertain. Source: https://www.enciklopedija.hr/clanak/coen-jan-pieterszoon
2. **§71.22:** corrected claim that Treaty of Bungaya ended Makassar war in November 1667; fighting resumed, and Somba Opu was taken in June 1669. Source: Leonard Andaya, *The Heritage of Arung Palakka*: https://www.jstor.org/content/oa_book_monograph/10.1163/j.ctvbnm4wp
3. **§71.20:** distinguished English foothold at Run from Dutch seizure of Ai in 1616; English control of both islands in 1621 was incorrect. Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-southeast-asian-studies/article/colonisation-and-cultivation-nutmeg-production-labour-and-ecology-on-the-banda-islands-before-and-after-the-conquest-of-1621/BF86973D1324DB0D0FE680E0BF5291F7
4. **§71.20:** replaced the direct "Run exchanged for Manhattan" trope. The 1667 Treaty of Breda confirmed Dutch retention of Run and English retention of New Netherland in a broader settlement, not a one-to-one island-for-city trade. Source: https://www.cultureelerfgoed.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2017/01/01/shared-cultural-heritage-of-the-united-states-and-the-netherlands/shared_cultural_heritage_of_the_united_stated_and_the_netherlands_1.pdf
5. **Reference people/place entry:** removed suggestion Portugal held territorial settlements in Japan. It had trade, missionary and commercial influence, not sovereignty. Portuguese presence and formal Macau settlement are separately discussed and appropriately qualified. Source: https://asia.si.edu/whats-on/exhibitions/encompassing-the-globe-portugal-and-the-world-in-the-16th-and-17th-centuries/
6. **VOC Quantitative Study:** corrected 4,700 voyages label to specify VOC **and predecessor** outward departures, 1595–1795, matching *Dutch-Asiatic Shipping*. Source: https://www.huygens.knaw.nl/en/resources/dutch-asiatic-shipping-in-the-17th-and-18th-centuries-2/
7. **VOC Quantitative Study:** differentiated aggregate arrivals to VOC settlements (estimated 660,000–1,135,000 people) from much smaller direct VOC company-operated transports and private/staff voyages. Sources: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-global-history/article/towards-a-global-perspective-on-early-modern-slave-trade-prices-of-the-enslaved-in-the-indian-ocean-indonesian-archipelago-and-atlantic-worlds/F3BF7F3CF08AA23010922EB42F3DAAFA ; https://www.ascleiden.nl/content/webdossiers/indian-ocean-slave-trade
8. **Dividend explanatory note:** clarified that 18% of nominal subscribed capital, where cited, is not equivalent to modern annual investor return, especially as share prices varied and payouts could be noncash.
