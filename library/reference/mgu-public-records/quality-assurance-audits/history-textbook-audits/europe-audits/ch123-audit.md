## 5. Historical factual and methodology checks

1. **Allied authority**: June 5, 1945 declaration assumed supreme authority over Germany **without annexation**; separate Germany and Austria arrangements distinguished. Edition: Avalon Project June 5 declaration.
2. **Population transfers**: Potsdam Section XIII both specified orderly/humane transfers and requested temporary suspension of further expulsions pending examination; flight versus expulsion versus organized transfer distinguished.
3. **DP repatriation**: USHMM more than six million repatriated May–December 1945; not stated as the total uprooted population.
4. **Jewish DPs**: USHMM approximately 250,000 at the **end of 1946**, a dated count distinct from all Holocaust survivors; Kielce, survivor agency and community rebuilding addressed.
5. **Nuremberg**: 24 originally indicted, 22 tried (including Bormann in absentia), 19 convicted, three acquitted, twelve death sentences and ten executions. September 30/October 1 judgment distinctions preserved.
6. **Subsequent Nuremberg proceedings**: twelve US proceedings, 185 indicted and 177 tried, distinguished from the four-power IMT and national trials.
7. **International Refugee Organization**: constitution concluded December 15, 1946; entered into force August 20, 1948. UN Treaty Series vol. 18 p. 3 / treaty no. 283.
8. **Reparations and restitution**: state-to-state transfers distinguished from specific property recovery; 1947 American restitution law identified.
9. **Marshall Plan**: June 5, 1947 speech distinguished from April 3, 1948 enabling legislation; other recovery factors acknowledged.
10. **Human rights and international law**: Genocide Convention adopted December 9, 1948 and effective January 12, 1951; Universal Declaration adopted December 10, 1948 and distinguished from a treaty. Nuremberg did not prosecute under the later Convention.
11. **Peace settlements**: five 1947 Paris treaties distinguished from unresolved postwar German and Austrian status.
12. **Chronological boundaries**: no claim that the global war ended in May 1945, and detailed German division/Marshall Plan implementation reserved for Chapters 124–126.
