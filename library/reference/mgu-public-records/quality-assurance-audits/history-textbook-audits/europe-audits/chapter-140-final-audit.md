### Historical corrections made

1. **Granada, 1492 (§140.8):** Replaced erroneous immediate conversion/expulsion claim with initially protective surrender terms, subsequent coercion, Castilian requirements in 1502, Aragonese measures in 1525–1526, and the later Morisco expulsion of 1609–1614.
2. **1054 schism (§140.6 and chronology):** Correctly distinguished the legates' condemnation of Patriarch Michael Keroularios and his supporters from the Constantinopolitan synod's condemnation of the legates; avoided portraying these as excommunication of two whole churches.
3. **Ottoman religious administration (§§140.6 and 140.13):** Removed implications that a fixed, empire-wide `millet` system was fully established in 1453; distinguished local and chronological variation.
4. **Historical map study, c. 1100:** Distinguished the established Christian kingdom of Hungary from still largely non-Christian Baltic territories.
5. **Historical map study, c. 1600:** Corrected anachronistic placement of Bohemia among territories recatholicized only after White Mountain (1620); specified Bohemia's substantial non-Catholic traditions around 1600.
6. **French religious settlement (§140.15 and primary-source feature):** Specified exceptions to the 1905 framework, including chaplaincy expenditure and the distinct legal regime later retained in Alsace-Moselle.
7. **Protestant worship and daily practice:** Distinguished more iconoclastic Reformed traditions from Lutheran retention of images, altarpieces and selected observances.
8. **Church of Ireland chronology:** Distinguished enactment of its disestablishment legislation in 1869 from the effective date in 1871.
9. **Terminology and legibility:** Fixed a grammatically broken definition of radical Reformation and converted 15 literal Markdown-emphasis remnants to valid HTML emphasis.
