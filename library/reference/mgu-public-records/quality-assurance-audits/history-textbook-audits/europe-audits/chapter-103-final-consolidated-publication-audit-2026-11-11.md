## 3. Historical and editorial corrections applied once

1. **§103.2, contemporary reception of racial theory:** Replaced the misleading claim that scientists and most historians uniformly rejected Chamberlain's racial argument. The corrected text now differentiates contemporary supporters and critics from modern scientific invalidity.
2. **§103.11, Jewish emigration 1881–1914:** Separated the more-than-two-million migrants to the **United States** from total Eastern European departures to **all** destinations (more than two and a half million, with acknowledged source and boundary variation). Aligned the Chapter Summary.
3. **Chronology, 1881–1882:** Clarified that pogroms and restrictive rules **accelerated** earlier migration rather than inaugurating it.
4. **Chapter Opening and §§103.15 / source window:** Clarified Émile Zola's July 1898 flight to England to avoid imprisonment after libel proceedings, rather than suggesting an official sentence of exile.
5. **§103.15 Primary Source Window:** Retained the explicitly labeled editorial paraphrase and added direct references to Zola's original *L'Aurore* no. 87, 13 January 1898, pp. 1–2, Gallica ARK `ark:/12148/bpt6k701453s`, and the Shelley Temchin / Jean-Max Guieu English translation (Georgetown University, 2001). Neither text was reproduced in full.
6. **Documentary register:** Replaced the generic institutions-only list with **19 individually identifiable records** grouped into 8 primary/critical editions, 8 institutional references, and 3 interpretive works. Separated actually identifiable texts from recommended scholarship.
7. **Responsive presentation:** Wrapped the two chronology-style tables in horizontal-scrolling containers and added narrowly scoped responsive CSS. Reduced overlapping decorative chapter numbering at mobile sizes. Retained the existing sidebar, Chapter 2 design language, and unchanged navigation script.

Only **3 direct historical section paragraphs** were changed across the 20 numbered sections. No section was truncated, reorganized, or subjected to wholesale stylistic rewriting. The other affected text is the introduction, chronology row, summary, source window, references, and CSS/table wrappers.
