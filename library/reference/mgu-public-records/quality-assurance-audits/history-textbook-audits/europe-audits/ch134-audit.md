## Historical and documentary findings

- Verified Maastricht treaty signature (February 7, 1992) against entry into force (November 1, 1993).
- Retained the three original EMU phases and distinct euro milestones: 1999 initial eleven participants; Greece in 2001; 2002 cash changeover in twelve states.
- Retained historical differentiation of European Union, euro area and Schengen participation, with corrected four-country nonparticipant list for 1999 (Greece, Denmark, Sweden and the United Kingdom).
- Confirmed Lisbon signature (December 13, 2007), Irish referendums (June 12, 2008; October 2, 2009), and entry into force (December 1, 2009).
- Distinguished the never-effective 2004 Constitutional Treaty from the 2007 Lisbon reform and the Charter's changed legal status from December 2009.
- Distinguished central banking authority from fiscal policy largely retained by member states; reserved the detailed euro sovereign-debt crisis for Chapter 138.
- The source windows retain explicit editorial paraphrases, not unverified verbatim text, and now link to identified historical records from EUR-Lex, the ECB historical archive and the Archive of European Integration. Not all external URLs could be tested as freely retrievable in an automated browser because some EU websites gate automated access; their referenced documentary identities were checked against source listings.
