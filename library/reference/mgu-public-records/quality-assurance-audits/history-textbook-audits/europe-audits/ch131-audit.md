- **Spain:** 1976 reform, 1977 elected parliament and Moncloa Pacts, 1978 Constitution, February 1981 coup and 1982 alternation treated as distinct stages; social and regional organizations included.

- **Security:** SALT II signing vs non-ratification; NATO decision identifies both deployment and negotiation tracks, 108 Pershing II launchers and 464 GLCMs; no retrospective inevitability about 1989.
