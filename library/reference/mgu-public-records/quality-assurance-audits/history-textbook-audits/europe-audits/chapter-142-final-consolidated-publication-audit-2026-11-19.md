## Consolidated revision record

1. **Opening — *Empire Windrush* and imperial nationality.** Corrected the implication that 1948 travelers were already part of a postcolonial nationality after British rule had ended. British colonial rule remained in place in much of the Caribbean, and travelers held British-subject status under pre-existing arrangements. Noted the 1948 Act's subsequent passage and 1949 commencement.
2. **Section 142.2 — Roman colonial foundation chronology.** Corrected the grouping of late-republican Lugdunum (43 BCE), Augustan Augusta Emerita (25 BCE), and Claudian Colonia Claudia Ara Agrippinensium (50 CE) under one reign.
3. **Chronology Preview and Major People/Places — colonial periodization.** Replaced the erroneous assertion that Portugal and Spain established the first sustained European overseas empires with a qualified statement referring to enduring *transatlantic* colonial empires, recognizing earlier Venetian and other Mediterranean overseas possessions.
4. **Section 142.8 — VOC migration numbers.** Reframed the approximately 972,000 figure explicitly as **outward passages**, not unique individuals, in company voyages from Europe to Asia between 1602 and 1795.
5. **Section 142.19 — Ghana.** Specified Ghana's independence date, British colonial connection, and post-1945 wave; mentioned Liberia to avoid implying that no African state had been independent earlier.
6. **Section 142.19 — *Windrush* count and British Nationality Act.** Separated the full ship manifest of more than 1,000 people from the contemporaneously counted group of 492 Caribbean migrants. Identified June 1948 arrival; July 30, 1948 royal assent; and January 1, 1949 commencement of the Act.
7. **Section 142.20 — restitution and cutoff.** Added the June 2025 Dutch physical return of 119 Benin objects and the February 2026 Cambridge legal-ownership transfer of 116 objects, explicitly distinguishing ownership from physical repatriation. Established the **November 19, 2026 historical cutoff**.
8. **Research apparatus.** Preserved the existing research project and added a concise curated list of five verified archival or institutional online resources for checking immigration and restitution evidence.
