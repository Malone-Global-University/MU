## 3. Consolidated historical revisions

1. **§130.5, Nanterre:** Identified *Pourquoi des sociologues?* by Daniel (Dany) Cohn-Bendit, Jean-Pierre Duteuil, Bertrand Gérard, and Bernard Granautier. Distinguished distribution at Nanterre in early April 1968 from its publication in *Esprit*, no. 371 (May 1968), pp. 877–882, indexed at JSTOR. The claim that the March 22 Movement was sole corporate author was removed. Source: <https://www.jstor.org/stable/24260496>; contemporary reference: <https://www.persee.fr/doc/comm_0588-8018_1968_num_12_1_1168>.
2. **§130.10, Valle Giulia:** Replaced the reduction of Pasolini's poem to simply siding with police with his actual class argument, sympathetic to the origins of some rank-and-file officers while expressly opposing the police institution and acknowledging student grievances. Cited bibliographic notice for *Il PCI ai giovani!!*, *L’Espresso*, June 16, 1968: <https://storicamente.org/sessantotto-guerrieri_link8>.
3. **§130.1, educational expansion:** Replaced rounded numerical approximations with documented series: France, 203,375 (1960–61) to about 502,000 (1967–68); Italy, 258,181 (1960–61) to 616,898 (1969–70); West Germany, 246,939 (1960) to 421,976 (1970); UK, Robbins Report baseline of 216,000 full-time students (1962–63). The French Senate report itself differs slightly, printing 501,398 in a table and 501,938 in explanatory text, so the final chapter accurately says *about 502,000*. National definitions differ and the data are not a directly comparable four-country panel. Source links are in the chapter.
4. **§130.6, French strike estimates:** Removed the implication that seven to ten million was a documented simultaneous peak. Distinguished participant estimates from working days lost. Explicitly attributed the *rough* 150-million-working-days figure to the INSEE approximation described by V. Scardigli in the 1973 CRÉDOC report; added Gérard Adam's 1970 methodological study. Primary references: <https://www.persee.fr/doc/rfsp_0035-2950_1970_num_20_1_393216> and <https://www.credoc.fr/download/pdf/Sou/Sou1973-2474.pdf>.
5. **§130.9, Dutschke:** Identified photographer Klaus Lehnartz, February 17, 1968, and the bpk-Bildagentur photo rights reference. Clarified that a photograph establishes presence, not a verbatim speech. Kept the image external: <https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/two-germanies-1961-1989/rudi-dutschke-at-a-vietnam-congress-february-17-1968>.
6. **§130.16, British statute:** Added guidance to use the 1967 **as-enacted** version of the Sexual Offences Act, because modern consolidated pages incorporate subsequent repeals and amendments.
