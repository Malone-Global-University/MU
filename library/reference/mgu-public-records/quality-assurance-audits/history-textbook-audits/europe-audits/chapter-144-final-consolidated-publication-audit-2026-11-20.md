## Historical corrections and qualifications

1. **§144.1 / Historical Vocabulary (Civilization):** Replaced the inaccurate description of civilization as a nineteenth-century concept with the documented eighteenth-century emergence of its modern usage and later nineteenth-century hierarchical deployments. The introductory glossary agrees with this correction.
2. **§144.6 / Transatlantic slave trade:** Clarified the standard SlaveVoyages estimate as approximately **12.5 million Africans embarked** and **10.7 million disembarked**, distinct from deaths before embarkation and from total arrivals.
3. **§144.12 / Two world wars:** Replaced an exclusively European-origin narrative with the distinct Asian antecedents to World War II, including Japan's seizure of Manchuria in 1931 and full-scale conflict with China from July 1937, alongside war beginning in Europe in September 1939.
4. **§144.12 / Chinese wartime workers:** Clarified approximately **140,000 Chinese laborers recruited under British and French arrangements**, including approximately **100,000 in the British Chinese Labour Corps** and **40,000 under French arrangements**, serving mainly in **France and Belgium**, not all in a single British corps.
5. **§144.13 / Primary Source Window:** Corrected the misleading suggestion that the Schuman Declaration said little about overseas colonial relationships. The original 1950 declaration explicitly proposed the development of Africa as a European undertaking. Its source-window paraphrase and analysis now reflect this, without claiming a perspective of colonized peoples that the declaration did not express.
6. **§144.15 / European integration:** Added a concise account of the colonial context of early integration and overseas association arrangements in the 1957 Treaty of Rome.
7. **§144.16 / November 2026 cutoff:** Distinguished EU candidate status (June 2022), formal accession negotiations (June 2024), and the opening of negotiations on the fundamentals (June 2026), external relations (July 2026), internal market and competitiveness clusters (October 2026). The chapter explicitly does not imply accession was concluded or the ongoing war resolved.
