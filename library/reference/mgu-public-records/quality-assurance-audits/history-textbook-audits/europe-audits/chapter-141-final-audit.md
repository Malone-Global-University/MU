### Historical and legal corrections applied

1. **§141.8:** Distinguished the Krewo agreement/dynastic union of 1385 from the **1569 Union of Lublin**, which established the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth.
2. **§141.20:** Corrected the 1995 Dayton Peace Agreement description. It confirmed the continuing international legal personality and recognized borders of Bosnia and Herzegovina while establishing a new constitutional distribution of authority between two entities; it did not create the sovereign state from nothing.
3. **§141.20:** Identified the **Conference on Security and Co-operation in Europe (CSCE)** as the body responsible for the 1990 Charter of Paris, and noted its renaming as **OSCE**, effective January 1, 1995.
4. **§141.20:** Corrected EU **Article 7**: European Commission initiated the Poland procedure in 2017, which ended in May 2024; European Parliament initiated the Hungary procedure in 2018, which was still active at this edition's November 2026 cutoff. Distinguished the separate 2020 budget conditionality regulation.
5. **§141.18 and the Primary Source Window:** Distinguished **1950 signature**, **1953 entry into force**, **1959 opening of the Strasbourg Court**, and **1998 Protocol No. 11**, which created the permanent Court with direct individual access, subject to admissibility rules.
6. **§141.19:** Clarified that enforcement of EU law is shared among national authorities and courts, the European Commission, and the CJEU within conferred competences, rather than being under exclusive member-state control.
7. **§141.20:** Added a clear contemporary-history cutoff (November 19, 2026). The text makes no prediction regarding Russia's war against Ukraine.
