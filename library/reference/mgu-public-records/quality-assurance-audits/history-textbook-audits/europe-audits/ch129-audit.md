## Historically significant corrections and qualifications

1. **October 30–31, 1956:** Revised the Soviet-decision narrative and Historical Debate to distinguish the Soviet Presidium's deliberation over withdrawal/negotiation on October 30 from its October 31 reversal toward armed intervention. Nagy's November 1 neutrality/Warsaw Pact announcement could not cause an October 31 decision. The debate now tests causes against the dated evidence rather than inviting an impossible causal ordering. The National Security Archive presents the Soviet working notes: https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB14/doc9.htm.
2. **June 1953 uprising:** Defined the Federal Foundation's 55 documented deaths as a category encompassing several types of death connected with the uprising, not merely demonstrators shot on June 17. Clarified approximately 15,000 arrests and 1,800 East German court judgments by 1955, and the distinction between internal mobilization and official allegations of outside orchestration. https://www.bundesstiftung-aufarbeitung.de/de/recherche/dossiers/volksaufstand-vom-17-juni-1953-der-ddr.
3. **Poznań, June 1956:** Distinguished the 57 deaths in recent historical accounts from the 58 deaths identified in the Polish Institute of National Remembrance's investigation. The reference is explicitly bounded and describes victims from several categories. https://eng.ipn.gov.pl/en/news/12532%2CCommemoration-of-the-70th-anniversary-of-the-Poznan-June-1956-protests.html.
4. **Hungarian reprisals:** Distinguished the commonly cited figure of about 200,000 refugees and an identified historical accounting of 229 executions connected to the revolution in 1957–1961, without presenting all different-era estimates as identical. See Tibor Valuch, *Hungarian Cultural Studies* (2016), https://ahea.pitt.edu/ojs/ahea/article/view/253.
5. **GDR migration before the Wall:** Replaced the unsupported presentation of 2.7–3.5 million as a range for the same 1949–1961 interval with the at-least-2.7-million estimate for that interval, explaining that higher totals can employ different starting dates and categories. https://www.bpb.de/themen/migration-integration/regionalprofile/english-version-country-profiles/262758/historical-and-current-development-of-migration-to-and-from-germany/.
6. **Berlin Wall fatalities:** Defined the Berlin Wall Foundation's minimum of 140 deaths for 1961–1989, comprising 101 attempted escapees, 30 others not attempting escape, one Soviet soldier, and eight border guards. Separately identified at least 251 deaths during/after passage through checkpoints; these distinct categories must not be merged casually. https://www.stiftung-berliner-mauer.de/en/topics/victims-berlin-wall.
7. **August 1968 invasion force:** Clarified that approximately 400,000 personnel describes the larger August occupation operation, not a single initial column entering Prague. https://www.bpb.de/themen/kalter-krieg/prag-1968/274360/der-einmarsch-des-warschauer-pakts-im-ueberblick/.
8. **GDR role in 1968:** Distinguished East German endorsement, logistical assistance, and small liaison/signals elements from the combat divisions held in reserve outside Czechoslovakia. This also explicitly qualifies the contemporary Czechoslovak Presidium statement's claim about troops of five states. https://www.bundesarchiv.de/themen-entdecken/online-entdecken/geschichtsgalerien/21-august-1968-einmarsch-kein-einmarsch/.
9. **1968 deaths:** Identified a Czech institutional count of 108 occupation-related deaths through the end of 1968, noting that other scholarly estimates may differ by definitions and timeframe. https://www.ustrcr.cz/en/project-1968-1969/august-1968-victims-of-the-occupation/.

2. Source windows use identified editorial paraphrases rather than newly introduced, edition-verified verbatim quotations. This follows the series instruction against fabricated quotations.
3. Exact pixel-for-pixel equality with the separate Chapter 2 source model was not re-certified, though the existing shell and core styles were retained.
4. Publication to the live MGU website, external link testing from the deployed domain, and institutional review by a historian are not represented as completed actions.
5. Historical figures depending on varying classifications are accompanied by source-defined limitations rather than misleading claims of absolute consensus.
