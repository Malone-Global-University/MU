## Consolidated historical and editorial corrections

1. **Polish June 1989 election count:** Clearly separates June 4 first round (**160 of 161 open Sejm seats, 92 of 100 Senate seats** won by Solidarity) from final figures after June 18 (**161 of 161**, **99 of 100**). Identifies 299 reserved Sejm seats, 161 freely contested seats, failed party-backed national list, and limitations of 65%/35% allocation.
2. **Soviet referendum of March 17, 1991:** Reports official approximately **80.0% turnout** and **76.4% in favor** for those eligible/participating in the administered vote, with the six-republic boycott and limitations. These percentages are not misrepresented as a mandate of every eligible resident across fifteen republics.
3. **Baltic incorporation:** Distinguishes the German–Soviet secret protocols of 1939 from subsequent Soviet military occupation and annexation in 1940.
4. **German monetary conversion:** Explains 1:1 conversion for defined categories of wages, pensions and savings within age-dependent ceilings, and 2:1 for other categories, with methodological qualifiers for productivity estimates and contemporary monetary source references.
5. **Stasi staffing:** Keeps approximate personnel numbers explicitly rounded and distinguishes full-time staff from unofficial collaborators.
6. **The fall of the Berlin Wall vs. reunification:** Maintains November 9, 1989, as border opening, and October 3, 1990, as German reunification. Maintains September 12, 1990, Two Plus Four signature separately from its March 15, 1991, entry into force.
7. **Soviet dissolution:** Maintains separate accounts of December 8 Belavezha, December 21 Alma-Ata, December 25 presidential resignation, and December 26 Soviet legislative declaration.
8. **Textual tightening:** Compressed repeated transitional material in §§132.9 and 132.17 without dropping the approved historical argument, ensuring the narrative remains below the upper word limit.

## Limitations and editorial disposition

This is approval for **scholarly textual HTML publication**, not an assertion that deployment to the live MGU website has been tested. Full external link integrity across every target, formal W3C validation, historical image reuse-rights clearance, and independently rendered historical map illustrations are beyond the completed audit. Some official archives may limit automated retrieval even where their indexed records verify the documents.

**Decision:** The chapter is suitable for first-edition scholarly textual HTML publication. No additional general expansion is warranted. The editorial lock may be reopened for genuinely material archival, factual, or publication-critical corrections.
