## 5. Limits of this publication approval

1. **Archival collation:** This audit verifies selected published documentary editions and source descriptions; it does not certify exhaustive original-language archival manuscript, printed-page, or diplomatic-facsimile collation across Italy, Germany, France, Denmark, and Austria-Hungary.
2. **Ems source window:** Students must open the linked original and edited texts for verbatim comparison; the chapter itself supplies an interpretative paraphrase, not a reproduced copyrighted translation.
3. **Historical geography:** The geography exercise is text-guided and requires a separate historical atlas or authorized maps. No atlas image plates are embedded.
4. **Artwork:** Werner's retrospective paintings are discussed as historical representations; no licensed artwork was added.
5. **Template and deployment:** The original source's CSS, sidebar, chapter header and footer conventions, and script were preserved. **Exact byte-for-byte parity with the Chapter 2 master was not tested because that master was not present as an independent comparison asset for this audit.** Live-domain deployment, CDN behavior, and publishing system paths were not tested.
6. **External links:** The referenced public pages were verified through external research at audit time, but future link availability and external website behavior are outside the standalone HTML test.
