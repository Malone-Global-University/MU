## 3. Verified historical accuracy and factual edits
The manuscript's controlling interpretation (Dutch provincial/urban oligarchies, Stuart composite monarchy, conflict among Parliament/king/churches, non-democratic post-1688 settlement, and the central place of Ireland and Scotland) is **broadly well grounded**. The following identifiable accuracy corrections were made to the working copy:

1. **§77.3, original Union of Utrecht signatories:** original wording implied Groningen **city** joined with its surrounding territory in January 1579. The signatory body was the **Ommelanden** (territory surrounding Groningen) and not Groningen city; city status and joining chronology differ. Corrected to “delegates from the Ommelanden, the rural districts around Groningen; Groningen city itself did not join as an original signatory.” Nationaal Archief: `https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/beleven/onderwijs/bronnenbox/de-unie-van-utrecht-1579` (Raad van State 1.01.19 inv. 2623); Kossmann and Mellink, *Texts Concerning the Revolt of the Netherlands*, Cambridge, Treaty 37 `https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/texts-concerning-the-revolt-of-the-netherlands/treaty-of-the-union-eternal-alliance-and-confederation-made-in-the-town-of-utrecht-by-the-countries-and-their-towns-and-members-29-january-1579/13298FD6EBFE71B76516834219406313`.
2. **Source Window 3, Peace of Münster:** “ratified 15 May 1648” confused the public **oath of confirmation** with the earlier separate ratifications. Treaty signed 30 January 1648, Philip IV ratified 1 March and the Dutch 18 April; oath ceremony 15 May. Corrected. National Gallery `https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/gerard-ter-borch-the-ratification-of-the-treaty-of-munster`.
3. **§77.31, Newport negotiations:** grouped the **Newcastle Propositions of 1646** and **Newport negotiations of September–November 1648** under the years 1646–47. Corrected the historical sequence and date. Cambridge, “Observations on a Manuscript Account of the Treaty of Newport, A.D. 1648,” `https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/archaeologia/article/abs/viiobservations-on-a-manuscript-account-of-the-treaty-of-newport-ad-1648-in-the-possession-of-the-right-hon-the-earl-of-verulam/BFD4576C4EA86DEEBD4FA9E142D89A2A`.
4. **Source Window 6, Putney dates:** broadened its named range to the **intermittent 28 October–11 November 1647** sequence, while identifying the core 28 October–1 November debates. C. H. Firth, *The Clarke Papers*, vol. I (1891), pp. 226ff.; `https://www.davidmhart.com/liberty/Levellers/ClarkePapers/ClarkePapers1-1891.html`.
5. **Presentation**, labeled §77.54 as Division XI in sidebar so 11-division plan is explicit.
