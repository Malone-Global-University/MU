## 3. Direct revisions completed

1. **One substantive holistic expansion** added explanation of railway capacity and freight coordination; cables and switching networks; European automobile and bicycle systems; factory and quality-control arrangements; legal ownership and management; industrial banking; cartel enforcement and market pressures; state procurement and regulation; specialist labor and occupational hazards; differentiated electrification; trade dependencies; and pre-1914 industrial synthesis. New analytical narrative was added inside existing numbered sections, without renumbering, replacing the established chapter design, or deleting instructional boxes.
2. **§101.8, Nobel tanker chronology:** ordered in **1877**, entered service **1878**, according to the Branobel historical project; no unsupported absolute 'first' label introduced. https://www.branobelhistory.com/distribution/ludvig-nobel-builds-the-worlds-first-modern-tanker/
3. **§101.5, Perkin:** sold the works in **1874** and continued **independent research at home**, not a new academic appointment. https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/people/ap17271
4. **§101.3, Thomas-Gilchrist:** corrected chemistry to a **basic converter lining and lime-rich slag**, removing oxidized phosphorus into phosphate-bearing slag. Primary patent: https://patents.google.com/patent/US217495A
5. **§101.7 and opening chronology, AEG:** company lineage established as **Deutsche Edison-Gesellschaft in 1883** and renamed/reorganized as **AEG in 1887**, rather than implying no earlier company. Rutgers Edison Papers: https://edison.rutgers.edu/life-of-edison/companies/company-details/electric-light%2C-foreign/deutsche-edison-gesellschaft
6. **§101.2, Swiss chemical patents:** clarified that the **1907** revision extended chemical-process protection while chemical-substance protection remained more restricted. See Nicolas Chachereau, research on Swiss patents: https://graphsearch.epfl.ch/en/publication/294613 and https://www.scienceopen.com/hosted-document?doi=10.1080%2F08109020500085528
7. **Perkin Primary Source Window:** now includes directly linked Science History Institute Archives finding aid, collection **2024-518**, for the published 1857 specification: https://archives.sciencehistory.org/repositories/3/resources/788 . Existing editorial analysis is explicitly not presented as a page-checked verbatim patent quotation.
8. **Documentary Source Guide:** added **11 traceable entries** for archival materials, contemporary technical documentation, institutional histories, and scholarly syntheses, including Landes, Mokyr, Chandler and Peters. Corporate histories are explicitly identified as institutional self-description.

## 5. Limits and publication qualification

- The Perkin source panel presents **disclosed editorial analysis** and links an archival finding aid; it does **not** reproduce independently collated wording of the 1857 specification. Students need a copy of the historical document for verbatim document comparison.
- The Geography Study is text-guided; there are **no embedded historical map plates**. Map-based exercises require access to a period-appropriate historical atlas or sourced maps.
- Major dates, methods, and case claims received **selective** verification against scholarly, archive, company-historical, and institutional evidence, not exhaustive page-by-page verification of every statistic, foreign-language manuscript, or company record.
- Company archives and self-histories are disclosed as institutional perspectives rather than independent proof of all claims.
- **Exact Chapter 2 master CSS/head/template byte parity was not independently checked** because no corresponding canonical reference was furnished for direct comparison. The visual architecture and original script were preserved locally without wholesale redesign.
- **Live website deployment, link availability from an external network, accessibility assistive-technology audits, and production server behavior were not tested.**
