## 5. Material historical corrections and interpretation

1. **Joseph de Maistre, publication year:** Corrected *Considérations sur la France* from **1796** in Claude's chronology to first printed edition **1797**, corroborated by Cambridge University Press and a first-edition record in the Bibliothèque nationale de France: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/maistre-considerations-on-france/D8A6075910D6A33D5407F92F3E7B4CA8 and https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb308632697 . Note: the BnF work-authority record separately assigns the work date 1796 and even labels a first edition 1796, whereas its extant printed-edition catalogue specifies 1797. The chapter uses the identifiable printed edition date; the documentary discrepancy should not be concealed in more specialized bibliographic research.
2. **Herder:** Expanded explanation of cultural diversity together with pluralist, cosmopolitan commitments, avoiding conflation with later exclusionary nationalism. Source: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/herder/ .
3. **Fichte:** Retained **1807–1808** chronology and placed his addresses in the Napoleonic occupation and national education debate without pretending they constituted a specific blueprint for 1871. Source: German History in Documents and Images, https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/the-holy-roman-empire-1648-1815/johann-gottlieb-fichte-quot-addresses-to-the-german-nation-quot-1807-08 .
4. **Constant:** Kept an attributed and edition-linked **editorial paraphrase**, not a fabricated quotation, and clarified his defense of civic vigilance along with individual independence. Text: https://oll.libertyfund.org/titles/constant-the-liberty-of-ancients-compared-with-that-of-moderns-1819 .
5. **Delacroix:** Confirmed *Liberty Leading the People* commemorates **1830**, and distinguished political allegory from eyewitness reportage. Sources: https://smarthistory.org/delacroix-liberty-leading-the-people/ and https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/Delacroix.pdf .
6. **Wollstonecraft and Mill:** Restored 1792 and 1869 milestones and differentiated women's arguments for political equality from the later organized suffrage movement assigned to Chapter 102. Library of Congress 1792 record: https://www.loc.gov/item/34033009/ .
7. **Grimm collection:** The 1812 date is for the **first volume**, not the entire collection; the second volume and later edited editions are distinguished. Source: Cambridge University Press, https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/companion-to-the-fairy-tale/workshop-of-editorial-practice-the-grimms-kinder-und-hausmarchen/76B36EE830B6C04CD33BE9B996796016 .
8. **Analytical limits:** Civic and cultural nationalism appear as tendencies, not absolute national categories; Romanticism is neither uniformly liberal nor conservative; conservatism is not equated with reaction; national identity is not projected backwards from modern territorial states.
