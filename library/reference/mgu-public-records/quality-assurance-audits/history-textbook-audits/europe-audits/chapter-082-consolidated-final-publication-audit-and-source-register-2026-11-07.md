## 4. Scholarly and chronological correction register

The following specific editorial corrections were made in the final HTML:

1. **Du Châtelet (Primary Source Window 9):** The 1740 Paris first edition of *Institutions de physique* was **anonymous**. The substantially revised 1742 Amsterdam edition explicitly named her. The source window now distinguishes them; the main text additionally explains the implications of edition-level recognition. Supported by the Center for the History of Women Philosophers and Scientists and the Mathematical Association of America.
2. **Kant and Mendelssohn (Primary Source Window 10):** Replaced the inaccurate claim that the authors wrote from the same city. Mendelssohn was in **Berlin**, Kant in **Königsberg**; their essays appeared in the same Berlin periodical.
3. **Odessa (82.16):** Removed Odessa from the unqualified pre-1790 merchant-center list and explicitly noted its foundation in **1794** and subsequent Greek commercial prominence. Supported by Harvard Ukrainian Studies.
4. **Swedish press law (82.15):** Clarified the **1766** press-law opening, followed by Gustav III's **1772** coup and the more restrictive **1774** ordinance. Supported by the Swedish Parliament's legal-historical overview.
5. **The *Encyclopédie* chronology:** Retained and strengthened correct separation of **17 text volumes completed in 1765** and **11 plate volumes through 1772**. Later supplements and indexes remain distinguished from the original twenty-eight-volume publication.

Separately checked and retained: Portuguese *Real Mesa Censória* founded 1768; Berlin Wednesday Society founded autumn 1783; Lessing's *Nathan der Weise* published 1779 and first staged Berlin 1783; historical French signature-literacy rates are treated as indicators, not full reading proficiency.

The historical verification is **selective and substantive**. It is not a claim that every primary facsimile and every incidental reference has been line-collated against archival originals.
