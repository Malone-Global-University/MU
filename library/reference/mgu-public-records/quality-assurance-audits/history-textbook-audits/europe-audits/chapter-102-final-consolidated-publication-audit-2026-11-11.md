## 3. Corrections made in one consolidated pass

1. **May Day opening, 1890:** Removed the categorical assertion that the marches were not festive. The revised opening explains the coexistence of protest, celebration, and repression, consistent with the Norwegian Labour Movement Archives.
2. **Opening, circa 1870:** Qualified the unsupported claim that most European workers lacked suffrage, union recognition, and party representation. The revised sentence recognizes national differences and existing broad male voting in some states.
3. **§102.6, German imperial constitution:** Replaced a misleading statement about Reichstag dissolution. Article 24 of the 1871 constitution requires a **Bundesrat resolution with the emperor's assent**; dissolution was not an independent chancellor's prerogative.
4. **§102.19, Norway:** Replaced an argument relying on presumed ethnic homogeneity with an explanation focused on women's organizing, political bargaining, and incremental suffrage reform; identified the Sámi and Kven populations and assimilationist policies.
5. **§102.19, later women's franchise:** Distinguished Denmark's 1915 constitutional grant and first parliamentary vote in **1918**, Netherlands legislation in **1919**, and Sweden's steps in **1919** and completed constitutional reform / first national vote in **1921**.
6. **§102.4, Austria in 1907:** Added evidence that equal male parliamentary voting also removed limited parliamentary voting previously available to certain women landowners.
7. **§102.13, Gotha Program:** Retained the explicitly editorial paraphrase, added a direct link to the paired historical document, identified the **1901** English printed text, **1973** Hamerow republication, and **1875** original German published proceedings; noted translation differences in the socialist party's English name. No uncollated transcription was passed off as original.
8. **Documentary Source Guide:** Replaced generic institutional and author names with **12 linked documentary, institutional, parliamentary or archival records** and **6 specifically identified scholarly / contemporary works**. Corrected the **1911 German original / 1915 English edition** distinction for Robert Michels, documented by the Library of Congress. Berger and Purvis are now named with identifiable works and publication details.
9. **Mobile heading:** Added a scoped media rule suppressing the decorative chapter numeral below 601 pixels, removing the previously noted overlap without changing the core Chapter 2 CSS, main section sequence, or scroll-highlighting JavaScript.
