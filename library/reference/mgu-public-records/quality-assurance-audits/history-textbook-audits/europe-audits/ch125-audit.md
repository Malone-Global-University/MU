## Historically substantive corrections

1. Replaced inaccurate Washington–Warsaw distance claim with nonnumeric wording.
2. Differentiated First World War Russian Empire history from Second World War Soviet territory in §125.1.
3. Corrected 1947 peace-treaty communications exception to Romania and Hungary only, not Bulgaria, in §125.3.
4. Corrected and carefully differentiated 1946 Czechoslovak election shares: KSČ ~40.2% in Czech lands, ~31.2% of nationwide votes, and Slovak communist vote ~6.9% of nationwide votes; combined ~38.1%.
5. Clarified that the Prague Slánský trial of November 1952 preceded the January 1953 public Soviet Doctors' Plot announcement.
6. Distinguished the Lisbon 1952 firm roughly 50-division goal from higher provisional 1954 readiness-category goals, including the larger approximately 90-division projection.
7. Removed false suggestion that NATO Council decisions were taken by majority voting; explained consensus and relative power.
8. Reordered the chronology preview so January 1949 Comecon precedes April 1949 NATO.
9. Clarified that Portugal, an authoritarian founding NATO state, prevents characterization of the initial treaty as an alliance exclusively of democracies.
10. Retained Senate vote **82–13** on July 21, 1949, following direct Congressional Record corroboration, notwithstanding a conflicting 83–13 value found on one NATO historical explainer page. The primary U.S. Senate record controls.
