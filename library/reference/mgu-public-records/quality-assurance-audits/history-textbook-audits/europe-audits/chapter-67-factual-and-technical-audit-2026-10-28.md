## Six narrow changes, each original phrase replaced exactly once

1. **§67.6 — White Mountain:** original implied both armies were about 20,000. Qualified the differing estimates and reflected a well-supported approximate 21,000 Bohemian against 26,000–28,000 imperial/League strength, without falsely treating them as exact. Historical background: Habsburg historical site and Cambridge/Oxford analyses.
2. **§67.12 — Magdeburg and Swedish alliances:** softened the categorical causal claim that the sack alone eliminated any option of neutrality for Saxony and Brandenburg. Military threat and political calculations mattered alongside the atrocity.
3. **§67.25 — Habsburg exceptions and Silesia:** differentiated the broad exclusion of Habsburg hereditary lands from limited provisions for Protestant nobles of Lower Austria and Silesia and the three Silesian Churches of Peace.
4. **§67.25 — Papal protest:** described the breve *Zelo domus Dei* as issued around 1650, backdated to November 1648 and published 1651; the original simply said he declared provisions null in 1650.
5. **§67.26 — France and Sweden's guarantee:** removed the claim that their guarantor status derived from territorial acquisitions within the Empire; the mutual peace-guarantee provisions were the legal basis.
6. **§67.27 — Swedish five-million-thaler contribution:** clarified it was an obligation on designated Imperial Circles to fund Swedish army payments and withdrawal, not merely a Swedish sovereign cash prize.

## Flagged claims confirmed or substantially supported

- Peter Hagendorf's surviving manuscript and edited diary cover approximately **1625–1649**. Berlin State Library / German Digital Library, with German History Intersections documents.
- Silesian Churches of Peace: three exceptional Lutheran places of worship permitted; two remain at Jawor and Świdnica; third at Głogów destroyed. UNESCO World Heritage Centre.
- Palatinate 1618 exception to the 1624 confessional normative year, and substantial Habsburg hereditary-land exceptions: Historisches Lexikon Bayerns; Westphalian Peace research archive.
- December 1641 Hamburg preliminary agreement for the congress: original Franco-Imperial instrument, Yale Avalon Project.
- Nuremberg implementation/execution congress ran approximately **May 1649–July 1650**: City of Nuremberg.
- Maximilian von Trauttmansdorff served as chief imperial plenipotentiary approximately **1645–1647**; Adriaen Pauw participated **1646–1648**: Westphalian History Portal.
- Five million rixdollars/thalers for withdrawal of Swedish soldiers: Treaty of Osnabrück, Article XVI, summarized by German History in Documents and Images.
- Alsace settlement's complex cession of Habsburg imperial rights, with other local privileges retained: Treaty of Münster, preserved by Yale Avalon Project.
- Broad critique of '1648 invented fully sovereign nation-states' is compatible with modern historiography and the surviving imperial institutions.
- Dutch treaty signed **30 January 1648**, ceremonially ratified **15 May 1648**: Dutch National Archives and Rijksmuseum.
