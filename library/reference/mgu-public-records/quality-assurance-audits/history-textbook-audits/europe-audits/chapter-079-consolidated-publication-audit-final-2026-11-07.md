## 3. Historical accuracy — targeted corrections directly implemented

1. **The Danish King's Law (`source-kings-law`)**: Replaced the misleading statement that the text was kept secret until 1709 with its restricted early circulation and the fact that full printing came in 1709. The text was known in official circles earlier. Official Danish-law document: https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/retsinfo/1665/20001 ; contextual scholarship: https://cas.au.dk/en/danish-history/modules/module-5-absolute-monarchy-1660-1814/3-absolute-rule-and-the-administration .
2. **Andrusovo (§79.6 and Primary Source Window 4)**: Used “early 1667” because treaty dates differ across languages, documentary styles, and Julian/Gregorian calendars. Confirmed the intended temporary Moscow possession of Kyiv and the 1686 recognition: https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/andrusovo-truce-1667 .
3. **The Silent Sejm (§79.8)**: Distinguished the nominal 1717 establishment of roughly 24,000 *pay portions* from actual soldiers present, clarified permanent revenue arrangements, and removed the assertion that the Sejm formally recognized Russia as the settlement's guarantor. Research: https://www.ldkistorija.lt/the-silent-sejm-the-end-of-the-internal-fights/ and https://publish.iupress.indiana.edu/read/constitution-and-reform-in-eighteenth-century-poland/section/1618b154-0d3f-457a-a0b5-565b8c87d974 .
4. **Kyiv chronology (§79.10)**: Distinguished de facto Muscovite possession before 1686, the temporary 1667 treaty provision, and definitive recognition in the Eternal Peace of 1686, instead of implying Muscovy first took Kyiv in 1686.
5. **Peter's Table of Ranks (Primary Source Window 7)**: Corrected chronology to **24 January 1722 Old Style / 4 February 1722 New Style**, *after*, not near the end of, the Great Northern War (settled 1721). Original printed issue in Russian National Electronic Library: https://kp.rusneb.ru/item/material/tabel-o-rangah-vseh-chinov-voinskih-statskih-i-pridvornyh-1 .
6. **Karlowitz chronology table**: Clarified that Habsburg gains covered most of formerly Ottoman-held Hungary *excluding the Banat*, not undifferentiated “Hungary.” Treaty reference: https://documentsdedroitinternational.fr/ressources/TdP/1699-01-26-TraitedeCarlowitz.pdf .
7. **1774 characterization (chronology table)**: Replaced “first decisive Russian victory over the Ottomans” with a more accurate description of Russian Black Sea strategic gains; earlier Russian-Ottoman victories, including Azov, predated this war.
8. **Quantitative history feature**: Replaced “24,000 men” with “24,000 pay portions,” explained officer costs and lower effective headcounts, and added specific, differentiated historical-source identification for Finnish famine, Swedish estates, the First Partition, Russian law and military ranks, and the Ottoman indemnity.

### Claims confirmed through independent historical source checks

- Danish King's Law signed **14 November 1665**; first complete printing 1709.
- Sweden's constitutional turning point in **1719–1720** and royal strengthening in **1772**.
- The Treaty of Nystad was concluded **10 September 1721 New Style**, and returned most of Finland to Sweden while Russia retained important eastern Baltic possessions.
- Approximately **25–33%** Finnish population mortality in the 1695–97 famine is supported as an estimate, not a census: https://journals.ametsoc.org/doi/10.1175/1520-0477%281979%29060%3C0775%3AGHETWS%3E2.0.CO%3B2 .
- The **1772 First Partition** took approximately **211,000 km²** (near 29% of the Commonwealth), with population loss subject to reconstructed estimates. Historical cartographic reference: https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/the-holy-roman-empire-1648-1815/the-partitions-of-poland-1772-1795 .
- The **4.5 million-ruble** Ottoman indemnity at Küçük Kaynarca is consistent with modern Ottoman documentary study: https://belgeler.gov.tr/eng/full-text/111/tur .
- The treaty's religious articles 7 and 14 did **not unambiguously create a blanket Russian protectorate** over Ottoman Orthodox Christians: Roderic H. Davison, *Slavic Review*, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/slavic-review/article/abs/russian-skill-and-turkish-imbecility-the-treaty-of-kuchuk-kainardji-reconsidered/8BB994FBDE3F721E6A852B073D7F0998 .

The final audit was a substantial, targeted historical verification rather than line-by-line collation of every factual assertion against all relevant archival records.
