## 3. Document-specific historical corrections

### A. Russia–DPRK treaty articles — publication-critical correction

Claude attributed the 2024 treaty's immediate military-assistance clause to **Article 3**, adopting the article number used in a September 18, 2026 Congressional Research Service summary (IF12760). This is a demonstrable documentary misnumbering:

- **Article 3** addresses urgent consultation in response to an immediate threat of armed aggression.
- **Article 4** provides for military and other assistance if armed attack puts a party in a state of war, with express reference to Article 51 of the UN Charter and domestic law.

Corrected both historical prose in §94.17 and the treaty Source Window; also alerted readers to the CRS misnumbering in the source guide. Checked against a publicly available English treaty translation and June 20, 2024 Korean reporting of the released Korean-language text. Translation and original-language editions have not been exhaustively collated.

### B. 1953 Mutual Defense Treaty — textual precision

Removed **“territorial integrity”** from two paraphrases of **Article II**, which specifically refers to threats to **political independence or security by external armed attack**. The text retains Article III's constitutional-process qualification and Article IV's basing provision. The treaty was signed October 1, 1953 and entered into force November 17, 1954.

Reference: https://avalon.law.yale.edu/20th_century/kor001.asp
