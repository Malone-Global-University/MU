## 4. Historical corrections and documentary findings
1. **April 30, 1948 joint statement.** Corrected source-window attribution to distinguish the joint organizational statement from the four-leader communiqué and the leaders’ negotiations. Contextual source: National Institute of Korean History; UNTCOK reporting; FRUS April 30, 1948.
2. **Northern governing offices, 1948.** Clarified Kim Tu-bong as chairman of the Workers’ Party of North Korea, Kim Il Sung as party vice-chairman and dominant governmental leader, later DPRK premier. The distinction between party and government office is maintained.
3. **Northern assembly figures.** Retained 572 reported seats (212 attributed to north, 360 to clandestine southern selection) and clearly labeled the latter a claim lacking independent observation.
4. **Jeju military proclamation.** Corrected Song Yo-chan’s rank from colonel to major in the October 17, 1948 notice, and restated its five-kilometer restriction with attention to the source wording.
5. **Jeju topography and Bukchon.** Corrected Bukchon to a north-coast village in Jocheon-eup, distinct from the destroyed mid-mountain villages; developed January 17, 1949 massacre and estimated several hundred victims.
6. **Jeju armed leadership.** Named Yi Deok-gu as guerrilla commander killed in June 1949, rather than leaving an ambiguous reference to the commander of armed forces.
7. **Jeju loss estimates.** Maintained official investigation estimate of roughly 25,000–30,000 deaths across 1947–1954; separated estimates from recognized victim records and individual adjudication.
8. **14th Regiment founding.** Corrected date to May 4, 1948 at Sinwol-ri, Yeosu, not 1946; distinguished military organization from armed rebels and local civilian participation.
9. **Yeosu–Suncheon military timeline.** Retained Suncheon recapture October 23 and Yeosu recapture October 27, 1948; expanded urban retreat, rural aftermath, screening, irregular proceedings, and distinctions between rebel and state violence.
10. **National Security Law.** Clarified original Law No. 10 of December 1, 1948 had six substantive articles plus supplementary provisions; distinguished statutory wording from policing and prosecutorial application.
11. **Special Investigation Committee.** Distinguished effective paralysis following June 6, 1949 police raid and summer obstruction from formal October 4, 1949 dissolution.
12. **Scholarship.** Corrected Hun Joon Kim book title to The Massacres at Mt. Halla; added directly linked publisher and LTI Korea literature records.
13. **Technical.** Wrapped chronology and evidence tables in independent horizontally scrollable regions; preserved exact original sidebar JavaScript. No page-level overflow at 1365, 900, 390 or 320 px.

## 6. Edition limitations / release control
1. The four primary-source feature passages are **explicit editorial paraphrases**, not facsimile-collated verbatim translations; the joint statement in particular requires attention to the distinction between organizational signatories and the related four-leader communiqué.
2. Conflict deaths, arrests and formal victim recognition categories depend on date, geographical scope and documentary source; no estimates were asserted as exact individual tallies.
3. Map studies remain **text-only analytical exercises**; independently licensed historical atlas plates are not embedded in the HTML.
4. The original Chapter 1 model was not supplied for bytewise styling comparisons; preservation verified relative to the actual uploaded Chapter 66 HTML.
5. Source links and accessible scholarly collection records improve document traceability, but exhaustive original-language archival collation, interviews and signed facsimiles require a separate specialist editorial edition, not a precondition represented as completed here.
