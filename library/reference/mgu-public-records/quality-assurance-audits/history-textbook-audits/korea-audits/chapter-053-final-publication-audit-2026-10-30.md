## Historical corrections made

1. Gojong's accession corrected to **December 1863** in the opening and people preview, while distinguishing the regency from personal rule.
2. Gwangmu era name corrected to **August 16, 1897** adoption/use, preceding October 12 enthronement and October 13 proclamation. Clarified the later proclamation retained the existing era name.
3. Gwanmin Gongdonghoe now begins **October 28, 1898**; Six Articles identified with **October 29**, followed by November 4 Jungchuwon regulations and the aborted November 5 elections.
4. November 1898 initial Independence Club arrests/dissolution differentiated from the movement's **final December 25** suppression.
5. Yangji Amun dated to **July 6, 1898**, rather than September, and the initial survey geographical sequence updated.
6. Corrected conflation of **124 counties surveyed in 1899–1901** with the **cumulative 218 counties surveyed by about 1903** (roughly two-thirds of more than 330 counties). Explicitly distinguished county-count figures from area accurately mapped.
7. Pagoda Park described as planned in the late 1890s and developed beginning around 1899, not definitively opened in 1897; completion/public-use dates remain disputed in scholarship.
8. Mobile chronology table given narrow-screen horizontal scrolling so that the wider three-column content does not overflow the page.
