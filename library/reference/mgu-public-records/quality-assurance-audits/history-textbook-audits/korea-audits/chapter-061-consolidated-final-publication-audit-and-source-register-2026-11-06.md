## 4. Publication-critical corrections applied

1. **§61.1 — Twenty-One Demands (1915):** China accepted the relevant agreements under coercive pressure; the original statement suggesting the concessions were never accepted was too categorical.
2. **§61.2 — Wanbaoshan (1931):** identify the *Chosun Ilbo* extra editions on the evening of July 2 and early July 3; correct the false reporting of alleged Korean deaths. The National Institute of Korean History and Academy of Korean Studies corroborate the chronology. Avoid converting highly contested riot casualty figures into unsupported certainties.
3. **§61.4 — Hayashi:** retain the well-attested unauthorized crossing of September 21 but remove the unverified English rendering of his nickname, which is not necessary to the narrative.
4. **§61.9 — Najin and SMR:** distinguish South Manchuria Railway operation of northern Korean rail lines through a dedicated management office from unspecified ownership/management of port construction; do not collapse them into the same legal role.
5. **§61.13 — July 1937 North China:** specify the 20th Division, with approximately two-thirds reported deployed by July 22 in contemporary American military attaché documents.
6. **§61.16 — Pacific War opening:** Japanese attacks on U.S. and British targets began in December 1941; the Japanese offensive into the Dutch East Indies commenced January 1942, not December 1941.
7. **§61.17 — Korean defense commands:** name the February 6, 1945 conversion of the Korea Army to the Seventeenth Area Army and a parallel administrative defense command, with Kwantung Army responsibility for certain northern preparations.
8. **§61.8, chapter summary, and timeline:** distinguish Japan's March 27, 1933 **notice of withdrawal** from the League of Nations and its March 27, 1935 **effective departure**.
9. **Documentary transparency:** four window-specific provenance notes now identify whether a precise original document was read in full versus reconstructed from other contemporaneous or later sources.
10. **Bibliography:** added 13 independently identifiable source entries: published historical documents, government repositories and three verified scholarly monographs/edited works. Added a sidebar jump link for bibliography.

## 5. Primary-source and bibliography register: provenance

### Documentary windows

- **Window I (§61.3), Mukden:** contrast Japanese military claims with the 1932 Lytton Report and postwar investigation. The original September 1931 Japanese communiqué has **not** been independently authenticated folio-by-folio. Analytical paraphrase only, not a direct historical quotation.
- **Window II (§61.8), League:** the Lytton Report (1932) is available in digitized complete form via the Library of Congress; the 1931 League resolution is cited via historical diplomatic documentation. No original-language passage is falsely passed off as a verified translation.
- **Window III (§61.6), Manchukuo declaration:** March 1, 1932 is well documented, but the purported founding document's exact original folio has **not** been independently collated; reported claims are attributed as a historical paraphrase, not a document quotation.
- **Window IV (§61.14), Mobilization:** Japan Center for Asian Historical Records, law No. 55 (April 1, 1938), ref. `C12121926100`; National Institute of Korean History publishes the May 1938 ordinances extending the law to Korea. Effective May 5, 1938.
