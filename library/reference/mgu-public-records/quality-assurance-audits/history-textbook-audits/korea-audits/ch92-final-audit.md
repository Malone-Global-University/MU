| Year | Estimated DPRK real GDP growth |
|---|---:|
| 2022 | −0.2% |

These are *Bank of Korea estimates* based on indirect evidence and South Korean prices/value-added ratios, not audited DPRK national accounts or household welfare statistics.

### B. Ninth Congress and Workers' Party charter, §92.19

Replaced the unsupported formulation “a reported September 2026 press analysis; not examined” with attributed evidence that the **revised charter, adopted at the February 2026 Ninth Congress, was publicly disclosed by the South Korean Institute for National Security Strategy on September 3**. The narrative identifies formal powers concerning party meetings, appointments and organizational dissolution, and incorporates **October 15** scholarly analysis suggesting that the provisions codified authority already being exercised. It distinguishes textual evidence from uncertain behind-the-scenes deliberations; the unification-policy discussion remains assigned to Chapter 93.

- Contemporary disclosure report: https://m-en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20260903006000315
- Dated research: https://www.38north.org/2026/10/north-koreas-new-party-rules-and-the-limits-of-delegated-authority/
