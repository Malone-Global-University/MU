## C. Historical accuracy corrections integrated in the HTML

1. **§76.6, Hyundai–Ford:** assembly relationship beginning 1968 preserved, but the false statement that the partnership ended in 1970 was replaced. A proposed deeper joint venture was considered from 1970, negotiations over export/production control persisted, and government approval was withdrawn in **1973**. Source: [2014 Korea Development Experience, *Korea's Automotive Industry*](https://www.ksp.go.kr/api/file/download/11484?downloadFilename=2014_Korea%E2%80%99s+Automotive+Industry.pdf). Avoid claiming the 1970 proposed venture was fully operational.
2. **§76.13, color TV:** domestic color-TV manufacturing **for export began in 1974**; broad domestic retail was authorized in **August 1980** and regular domestic color broadcasting began **December 1, 1980**. Source: [National Archives of Korea, color-TV policy history](https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/govpo/detail.do?policySeq=208). Distinguishes manufactured goods from domestic consumer/broadcast adoption.
3. **§76.8 and preliminary chronology, Sunkyong origins:** Chey Jong-gun **acquired and reconstructed the existing war-damaged Suwon textile works in 1953**, rather than building the entire predecessor factory from nothing. Sources: [SK company history](https://www.sk.com/en/about/history.jsp); [SK Heritage Museum](https://www.skheritagemuseum.com/tour/history?lang=en). These are interested corporate-origin narratives, identified as such.
4. **§76.8 and chronology, petroleum expansion:** Sunkyong acquired Korea Oil Corporation in **December 1980**; the **Yukong name was adopted July 1982**. These are not a single 1980 event. Source: SK corporate-history records cited in the register.
5. **§76.3, §76.18 and window 2, 1972 debt decree:** the presidential emergency decree was **proclaimed August 2, 1972**, taking effect **August 3**. Its popular name '8·3 Measures' does not change the proclamation date. Source: [Academy of Korean Studies, 8·3 Measures](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0075707).
6. **§76.3 and §76.18, high leverage:** quantified and bounded the **487.9% manufacturing-sector debt-to-equity ratio in 1980**, compared with 377.1% in 1979, from the historical World Bank comparative table. Explicitly distinguishes debt/equity from debt/assets and a sector aggregate from each individual chaebol's financial statement. Source: [World Bank, DRD 166, table 11](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/350311468774300119/pdf/DRD166.pdf). A former generic 'near 500%' figure was retained only when a series and accounting definition are identified.
7. **§76.15, labor disputes:** the total of **3,749 recorded disputes in the calendar year 1987**, versus **276 in 1986**, is now explicit. It is a **national count**, not all chaebol strikes or a second-half-only number. Source: [Brookings, labor-relations historical table](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/the-economic-debacle-in-northeast-asia-economic-political-and-social-legacies/).
8. **Name consistency:** uses **Jeon Tae-il** consistently, matching Chapter 75. The workers' source window clarifies that his workplace was the **Pyeonghwa Market's small shops**, not a Hyundai or other chaebol factory.
9. **Samsung semiconductor stages:** retained 1974 partial investment and 1977 acquisition completion; independently checked 1983 64Kb DRAM effort and 1984 Giheung production milestone. Sources: [Samsung company chronology](https://www.samsung.com/sg/about-us/company-info/), [Samsung Semiconductor history](https://news.samsungsemiconductor.com/global/about-us/). These corporate chronologies are retrospectively compiled, not unmediated contemporary documents.
10. **Shipbuilding origin:** retained historical core—1971 Livanos two-tanker order, British technical/finance assistance, 1972 Ulsan construction, 1974 delivery—while supplementing with independently documented **Scott Lithgow training in Scotland in 1972**, distinct from founder mythology. Source: Stig Tenold, J. Y. Kang, Song Kim, and Hugh Murphy, “International Transfer of Tacit Knowledge,” *Enterprise & Society* 22, no. 2 (2021): 335–367, [DOI:10.1017/eso.2019.68](https://doi.org/10.1017/eso.2019.68). Bank and shipowner source survival is incomplete; do not assert that all original contracts were page-collated here.
11. **Competition-law chronology:** retained passage of the Monopoly Regulation and Fair Trade Act in 1980 and distinguished **1986 amendments operative April 1987**, including cross-shareholding and holding-company restrictions. The 1999 relaxation is contextual only. Source: [OECD, *Economic Surveys: Korea 2018*, table 1.6](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-economic-surveys-korea-2018_eco_surveys-kor-2018-en/full-report/component-8.html). Statutory window remains a clearly identified paraphrase, not a fictional quotation.
12. **Independent scholarship correctly scoped:** Hwasook Nam's *Building Ships, Building a Nation* (Seattle: University of Washington Press, 2009) studies **Korea Shipbuilding and Engineering Corporation (KSEC)** labor, not Hyundai's Ulsan yard. Source: [University of Washington bibliographic entry](https://history.washington.edu/research/books/building-ships-building-nation-koreas-democratic-unionism-under-park-chung-hee). Edward M. Graham's *Reforming Korea's Industrial Conglomerates* (Peterson Institute for International Economics, 2003) now identifies the former generic Peterson/IIE reading-list entry; [publisher catalog](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/reforming-koreas-industrial-conglomerates/9780881323375/).
