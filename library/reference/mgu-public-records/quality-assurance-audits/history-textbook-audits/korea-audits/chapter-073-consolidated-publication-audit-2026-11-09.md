| Issue | Determination and change |

Military aid is a separate category and was not added to the figure. |

Different concepts/revisions cannot be silently interchanged. |
| Seoul population | Specified approximately **1.57 million in 1955** and **2.44 million in 1960**, with Seoul Museum of History attribution. |
| South Korean primary enrollment | Added historical educational series: **72.9% in 1953** to **96.4% in 1959**; these are administrative historical estimates, not a contemporary universal census. |
| North Korean schooling | Retained 1956 primary-compulsory date and specified **1958 compulsory secondary**, attributed to subsequent DPRK reporting to the UN, not independently measured implementation. |
| Northern aid | Distinguished **1953 Soviet one-billion-ruble grant commitment** from disbursement and **23 November 1953 Chinese eight-trillion-old-yuan grant commitment for 1954–1957**, with the prior wartime aid-cost write-off. This is old, pre-1955 Chinese currency; not modern RMB. |

| Narrative chronology | Replaced the claim that the Korean systems *first* became materially distinct only after 1953. Division and divergent institutions began in the 1945–1948 occupations; postwar reconstruction further entrenched them. |
| Primary source window 1 | Replaced a vaguely attributed compilation with the specifically identifiable **Henry J. Tasca report dated 15 June 1953**, *FRUS* 1952–1954, XV/2, document 632; corrected its **pre-armistice date**, paraphrase and source limitations. |
