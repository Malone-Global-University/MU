## 4. Historical corrections integrated into the manuscript

**A. Moscow negotiations (1945):** Added the U.S. Byrnes proposal presented December 17, the Soviet counterproposal of December 20, the U.S. revision request of December 21, the final agreement's emphasis on a Korean provisional democratic government and Joint Commission, and the difference between an American proposal allowing possible extension of trusteeship and the final up-to-five-year arrangement. Contemporary news accounts are not substituted for the actual negotiating record. Documentary check: FRUS 1945, vol. II, documents 233, 240 and 246.

**B. Left–Right Coalition Seven Principles (October 7, 1946):** Replaced the broad, incomplete précis with an account of all seven provisions. Specifically included different treatments of confiscation and compensated purchase before free transfer to cultivators, important industry nationalization, labor and local-government provisions, collaborators, political detainees, terror, the interim legislature, and civil freedoms. Source: National Institute of Korean History, transcription of the October 7 original.

**C. New Korea Company (1946):** Distinguished Ordinance No. 52 **issued February 21, 1946** from its **March 3 effective date**. The corporation's asset-management role is not mislabeled immediate land redistribution. Source: Korean History Database, 1946 military-government gazette.

**D. Ahn Jae-hong (1947):** Specified his appointment under **Appointment No. 116 dated February 4, effective February 5, 1947, subject to confirmation**; separated him from the Korean Democratic Party in the front reference register. Source: contemporary official gazette, Korean History Database.

**E. UN Resolution 112 (II) (1947):** Named the commission's original nine appointed member states—Australia, Canada, China, El Salvador, France, India, the Philippines, Syria, and Ukrainian SSR—and distinguished Ukrainian SSR's subsequent refusal to serve from its inclusion in the resolution. Added the March 31, 1948 election recommendation and the UN recorded 43–0 vote with six abstentions and eight members not voting. No 1948 election or state-founding narrative was inserted.

**F. North Korea's 1946 committee elections:** Explained united-front nomination limits and black/white ballot-box practices undermining secrecy while acknowledging the organizational role of the elections. Source: Jeon Hyun Soo, *Daegu Sahak* (2014).

**G. Northern land reform:** Differentiated a government-reported confiscated total (c. 1,000,325 *chŏngbo*) from a smaller reported area transferred to recipient households (c. 981,390 *chŏngbo*; 724,522 recipients in that series). Both figures remain expressly attributed to historical official statistical series, whose categories and political origins must be checked before research-level reuse. The chapter does not present these as independently observed exact counts.

**H. September–October 1946 unrest:** Added the 23–24 September railway/general-strike sequence, Daegu food demonstrations, escalation and local variation, and the methodological differences between military government narratives and contemporary Korean press. No invented definitive casualty total was introduced.

## 7. Limitations and publication scope

1. **Historical textual edition, not critical documentary facsimile edition.** The four primary-source windows are identified pedagogical paraphrases. Exhaustive line-by-line collation against every original Korean, English and Russian primary document was not conducted; some records are linked to editions rather than page-image originals.
2. **Map studies are text-only assignments** requiring a historical atlas; the HTML does not embed independently drawn cartographic plates.
3. **Statistics are source-dependent.** Northern reform figures, migration estimates and casualty claims should be rechecked against the exact table, unit, and category for quantitative research. The chapter deliberately avoids treating disputed numbers as precise facts.
4. **Design fidelity:** Existing chapter CSS and JavaScript were preserved and extended only for the responsive chronology table. The original Chapter 1 template was not supplied separately for a bytewise comparison.
5. **External reference links:** Document links were authored and selected against identifiable records; a complete automated availability check of every external site from every user network was not part of this HTML audit.
