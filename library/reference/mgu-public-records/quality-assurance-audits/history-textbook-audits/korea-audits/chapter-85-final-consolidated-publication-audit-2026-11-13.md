## Historical verification and corrected arguments

**First summit / June 15 declaration:** The 2000 meeting and five provisions were checked against the Republic of Korea government publication and KBS archive. §85.8 and its primary source window now specifically explain the provision concerning former long-term prisoners who refused to renounce communism, along with the family-reunion clause. The accompanying invitation to Kim Jong Il to visit Seoul is included; it is not incorrectly portrayed as a completed visit.

**Separated-family reunions / Red Cross:** The 2000 humanitarian source window now analyzes a June 30, 2000 Associated Press dispatch reporting the agreement between the two Red Cross groups. It identifies itself as **contemporaneous press reportage** about an agreement, **not** as the original signed Red Cross protocol. The August 15–18, 2000 first reunion dates are clarified against KBS's historical chronology. Subsequent program developments are not attributed as if they were known by the June 2000 report.

**Summit-related payments:** §85.17 distinguishes (a) the approximately $500 million in transfers documented by investigation and contemporary Congressional Research Service reporting; (b) the involvement of Hyundai affiliates, officials and financial institutions; (c) competing commercial and diplomatic interpretations; and (d) political evaluations about secrecy and accountability. Its itemization is attributed to the 2003 CRS investigative summary and not represented as independent archival bank-record collation.

**Security incidents:** The 1999 confrontation and June 29, 2002 Yeonpyeong/West Sea clash are distinguished; the death of six South Korean sailors in 2002 is retained. Nuclear diplomacy stays background context and is assigned principally to Chapter 86.

**Kaesong:** Construction in 2003, first production in December 2004 and retrospective end-2007 employment/firms figures are distinguished from planned build-out. The chapter discusses state-mediated wages, monitored movement, and the limits of independent worker testimony.

**Roh crossing and 2007 declaration:** Roh Moo-hyun's October 2, 2007 crossing of the Military Demarcation Line on foot and the October 4 declaration are grounded in official and contemporary sources. The text separates completed accomplishments from plans (West Sea zone, freight railway, industrial expansion). It no longer asserts categorically that a successor had no obligation to implement the declaration; the force and implementation of the commitments are described as contested.

**Mount Kumgang shooting:** Park Wang-ja's fatal shooting on July 11, 2008 and subsequent suspension of tourism are distinguished from the **North Korean account** that she entered a restricted area; that claim is not presented as independently settled.

**Editorial balance:** The chapter avoids unqualified declarations that engagement either automatically produced peace or was wholly futile. It discusses North Korean agency, two governments' unequal constraints, concrete humanitarian and economic results, military conflict, political opposition and institutional reversibility.

## Sources and original-evidence limitations

The standalone HTML includes an eleven-entry source register with publicly accessible source locators. The most consequential reference types are the ROK government's publication of the 2000 joint declaration; the ROK Ministry of Foreign Affairs' 2007 declaration account; a contemporary Associated Press report of the Red Cross agreement; archived KBS summit records; contemporary Congressional Research Service analysis of the payment controversy; CRS reporting on Kaesong employment; and Yonhap's July 2008 contemporary reporting.

The three primary-source windows contain **explicitly marked editorial paraphrases and source analyses, not invented quotations**. The 2000 source is based on an official English publication; the 2007 window uses an official South Korean explanation of the joint declaration, not an original-language collation; the Red Cross window treats contemporaneous reportage as a primary record of public reporting, not the original signed protocol. No original Korean manuscripts, closed North Korean administrative records or worker interview recordings were collated. Documentary link presence should not be conflated with exhaustive source criticism or independent onsite verification.

The Geography Study is text-guided and presupposes a historical atlas; no original map plates are embedded. Exact, byte-for-byte Chapter 1 HTML-model fidelity remains **unverified** because the original Chapter 1 model was not supplied in this task. Live website deployment and integration into the complete MGU library were not tested.
