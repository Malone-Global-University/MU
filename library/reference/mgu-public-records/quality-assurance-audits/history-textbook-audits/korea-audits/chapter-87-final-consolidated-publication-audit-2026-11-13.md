## Consolidated historical/source corrections

1. **Semiconductor archival record:** Replaced the unspecific description of what records “typically” say with a documentary analysis referencing the National Archives of Korea *Semiconductor Technology* thematic essay, the listed 1981 industrial promotion document, and the identifiable 1983 64K DRAM audiovisual catalogue item. Clearly distinguishes original records from the retrospective curatorial interpretation; does **not** claim to have fully transcribed the 1983 film.
2. **Broadband and PC bangs:** Uses Tim Kelly, Vanessa Gray and Michael Minges, ITU, *Broadband Korea: Internet Case Study* (March 2003). Figure 1.1 records **21.3 broadband subscriptions per 100 inhabitants** in December 2002. Box 2.3 records **43% of households** with broadband in March 2002, and Box 4.2 refers to **some 25,000 PC bangs**. Dates and denominators kept distinct; no unverified exact count assigned to 2000–2001.
3. **Galaxy S (2010):** Uses Samsung's March 23, 2010 contemporary press release (GT-I9000; Android; 4-inch Super AMOLED; 1 GHz application processor; Social Hub). Separates verifiable specifications from promotional expectations and does not infer sales outcomes from an announcement.
4. **5G rollout:** April 3, 2019 commercial rollout retained, with qualification that national service availability did not imply complete geographic or indoor coverage at launch.
5. **SK Telecom breach:** July 10, 2025 final investigation cited; approximately **26.96 million IMSI/USIM records** compromised, not presumed distinct individual victims.
6. **Coupang breach:** February 10, 2026 MSIT investigation cited; approximately **33.67 million account-information records** on a specified page, with further extensive access activity. Company's narrower claim about retained records is distinguished from official findings about unauthorized access and exposure. Final legal determinations are not invented.
7. **National data center:** September 26, 2025 fire at NIRS Daejeon; dated October 9 government tally of **709** affected digital services (reported by Yonhap); not conflated with permanently lost records.
8. **China fab controls:** U.S. rule published September 2, 2025, loss of VEU status effective December 31, 2025, and the subsequent annual fab-licensing route in 2026 accurately sequenced. A licensing system is not described as an unlimited exemption.
9. **HBM rankings:** Counterpoint's **Q2 2026 HBM revenue** estimates distinguish SK hynix **50%**, Samsung **33%**, Micron **18%** from overall DRAM revenue where Samsung **38%** and SK hynix **25%**; period, segment and rounding stated.
10. **AI Basic Act:** Corrected the previously erroneous future tense: the law **entered into force January 22, 2026**. The build distinguishes enactment and entry into force from actual compliance, effectiveness, procurement or deployment.
11. **Source transparency:** Added a linked, labeled documentary register with 17 references including original reports, company materials, government investigation summaries, contemporary journalism and a market-research tracker. Each new inline reference points to the register.
12. **Narrative depth:** Added historically focused paragraphs throughout every section, especially the policy-to-market transition, platform network effects, labor and online social life, digital government resilience, supply chains, and AI-memory market differences. The approved twenty-section topic order remains unchanged.
