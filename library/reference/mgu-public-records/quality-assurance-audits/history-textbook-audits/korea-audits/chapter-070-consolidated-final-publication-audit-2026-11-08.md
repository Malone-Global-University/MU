## 3. Substantive corrections and additions

1. **§70.2, Allan D. MacLean and Task Force Faith:** Distinguished the November 29 consolidation north/south of the P'ungnyuri inlet from the main attempted breakthrough toward Hagaru-ri **on December 1**; surviving soldiers reached Marine positions on December 1–2. MacLean was wounded/separated in the November 29 action, captured, and is not assigned a fictitious verified death date. Don C. Faith succeeded him and was mortally wounded in the December 1 breakout. Bases: U.S. Army CMH *The Chinese Intervention* and Defense POW/MIA Accounting Agency [MacLean profile](https://dpaa-mil.sites.crmforce.mil/dpaaProfile?id=a0Jt00000001VHiEAM), plus CMH *American Military History*.
2. **§70.1, MacArthur's November 28 cable:** Resolved the draft placeholder. **FRUS 1950, Volume VII, Document 888**, telegram C 69953, dated November 28, 1950, 4:45 p.m. Tokyo, appears at pp. 1237–1238: [official transcription](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1950v07/d888). Retained its accurately labeled editorial paraphrase instead of presenting non-collated text as direct quotation.
3. **§70.9, Omar Bradley:** Contextualized his critique of extending the war into China in testimony to Senate Armed Services and Foreign Relations committees in May 1951 (May 15 prepared statement); differentiated this from Truman's April 11 removal of MacArthur.
4. **§70.11–12, May–June 1951:** Added ROK III Corps' operational breakdown and subsequent dissolution/reallocation of divisions to other commands, the improvement of coordinated support, defensive Line Kansas/Line Wyoming, Operation Piledriver, and why possession of towns in the Iron Triangle did not equate to possession of controlling heights. Source: [Miller, Carroll and Tackley, *Korea, 1951–1953*](https://history.army.mil/Publications/Publications-Catalog/Korea/) and [Army campaign study](https://history.army.mil/Research/Reference-Topics/Army-Campaigns/Brief-Summaries/Korean-War/).
5. **§70.15, 1952 outpost battles:** Expanded ROK 9th Division's defense of White Horse Hill, evidence of advance warning, October 6–15 changing control of key knobs, and the U.S./ROK/Chinese contest at Triangle Hill–Shangganling and Sniper Ridge. Clearly distinguished tactical positions and incompatible postwar casualty claims. Sources: [IX Corps White Horse report](https://cgsc.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/collection/p4013coll11/id/536/) and U.S. Army official accounts.
6. **§70.17–18, air war and bombing:** Added geographic qualifications to air superiority and Soviet–Chinese MiG involvement. Corrected the initial *including* formulation for ~635,000 tons of bombs and ~32,557 tons of napalm, identifying these as widely reported, not universally reconciled, **whole-war/theater aggregate estimates**, not certain bomb tonnage exclusively over northern territory. Compared a different statistical category: SIPRI's 1973 table records **386,037 tons of bombs** and **32,357 tons of napalm** for FEAF alone, based on Futrell; weapons, services and categories are not interchangeable. [SIPRI Yearbook 1973](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/SIPRI%20Yearbook%201973.pdf), p. 147. Document IV correctly identifies the June 25, 1952 meeting **two days after** the June 23 power-plant strikes, with [FRUS Doc. 191](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p1/d191) and [editorial note Doc. 188](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v15p1/d188).
7. **§70.19, maritime war and stalemate:** Added the Wonsan blockade's **861-day duration** as attributed to the Naval History and Heritage Command and explained why sea superiority did not sever land-borne resupply. Expanded the distinction between strategic stalemate and the asymmetrical ability of the combatants to inflict costs, including ROK military growth and Chinese/ North Korean defensive adaptation. [Naval historical account](https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-058/h-058-2.html).
8. **Source register and primary windows:** Preserved the **22-entry** scholarly register; corrected the anonymous *Korea, 1951–1953* reference to **John Miller Jr., Owen J. Carroll, Margaret E. Tackley (1956; CMH Publication 21-2)**, with Army catalog corroboration; identified exact FRUS and Truman/UN URLs; added supplementary documentary locators after the register, not as replacements for scholarship.
