## 4. Verified corrections already applied to the working HTML

1. **§57.6 — Gwanghwamun relocation:** began in 1926 and was completed in September **1927**, not already fully moved in 1926; a demolition proposal had been modified after opposition. Independent architectural history: https://kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002854403
2. **§57.6 — Urban planning ordinance:** the Chōsen Urban District Planning Ordinance was promulgated **June 20, 1934**, effective **June 28, 1934**, No. 18. Korean government law text: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=65264
3. **§57.26 — Motion Picture Film Censorship Rules:** national regime effective **August 1, 1926**, but earlier local/administrative censorship already existed; avoid claiming cinema was unrestricted beforehand. The rules also imposed costly examination fees. Sources: https://oak.go.kr/central/journallist/journaldetail.do?article_seq=19622 ; https://www.koreafilm.or.kr/kofa/publication/books/PB_0000000263
4. **§57.22 — Korean language society:** revived as *Joseoneo Yeonguhoe* **December 3, 1921** and renamed *Joseoneo Hakhoe* **January 10, 1931**; earlier associations existed. The society's own history: https://eng.hangeul.or.kr/homepage/custom/Foundation
5. **§57.29 and review entry — Hwashin:** Park Heung-sik **acquired** Shin Tae-hwa's earlier retail enterprise in 1931 and developed the department store; he did not found the business from nothing. AKS: https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0064744
6. **§57.37 — Kunuhoe:** founded May 27, 1927; its national structure effectively ceased to function in 1931, but there was **no unambiguous formal dissolution declaration** and some local branches continued. AKS and NIKH: https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0007442 ; https://contents.history.go.kr/mobile/kc/view.do?levelId=kc_o400420
7. **§57.18 source box — Sohn Kee-chung photograph:** identifies the *Dong-A Ilbo* publication as **August 25, 1936**; the newspaper received indefinite suspension on **August 29, 1936**, resuming **June 3, 1937**, roughly nine months later. Another Korean paper had published a partly obscured emblem on August 13. AKS: https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0047311 ; Yon Mongyang archive: https://mongyang-archives.org/items/show/2185
8. **Responsive design:** mobile chronology-table containment, no change to desktop visual shell.

### Other dates verified, no correction needed

- Gyeongseong Broadcasting Station JODK began regular broadcasts **February 16, 1927**; separate Korean and Japanese channels commenced **April 26, 1933**. https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0021719

## 5. Primary Source Windows — publication-blocking provenance deficit

There are nine elements visually labeled **Primary Source Window**, but several are not anchored to identifiable original records. **Labelling text a paraphrase does not turn a description of a source type into a paraphrase of a document.** The source window must identify an actual issue, article, legal provision, advertisement, program listing, dated letter, or other traceable archival item, then accurately paraphrase that item and explain its limitations.

| Existing window | Audit | Required remedy |
|---|---|---|
| 1922 education ordinance | Specific legal text identifiable, paraphrase largely plausible | Name the law/edition/articles and archival source; verify provisions |
| 1922–1923 private university petition | A general class of appeals, rather than a definite item | Use the **March 29, 1923 private-university founding statement** or another identified appeal, with edition/date |
| April 1920 *Dong-A Ilbo* opening statement | Identifiable actual opening editorial | Cite its **April 1, 1920 first-page statement** and three articulated commitments, not generic commentary |
| August 1936 erased-flag photograph | Specific newspaper issue identifiable | Cite **August 25, 1936 page/issue**, contemporary censor response and later publication history |
| *Gaebyeok* editorial 1920–1926 | Generic aggregation of magazine contents | Identify one precise issue/article/editorial (or convert to source comparison of named issues) |
| *Arirang* contemporary review | Describes what 'reviews typically said' | Use an identifiable **October 7, 1926 Dong-A Ilbo review** or a verified dated advertisement |
| 1927 JODK schedule | Explicit placeholder requesting a future archive item | Replace with a dated 1927 program schedule from a particular newspaper/station publication; otherwise relabel a documentary-method exercise, not a source window |
| 1930s department-store advertisement | Explicit placeholder requesting a future archive item | Replace with a real dated advertisement, including its named store, displayed goods, terms and limits |
| *Sinyeoja*/*Sinyeoseong* women's writing | Undifferentiated description of themes | Identify the author, article, magazine, issue/month/year, then paraphrase that text |

Do not fabricate translated words, quotes, prices, program times, facsimiles or citations. Replace the missing documentary evidence in the cumulative manuscript. When a verified exhibit cannot be found, choose another identifiable primary source for the same historical question, or change the feature type transparently.
