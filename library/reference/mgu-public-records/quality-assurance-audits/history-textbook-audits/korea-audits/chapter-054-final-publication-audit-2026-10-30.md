## III. Historical verification and corrections

1. **1904 military occupation / Korea Garrison Army:** Japanese archival records date the formation of its headquarters to March 1904 and its arrival in Seoul to April, with further organization in August. The chapter now distinguishes Japanese February troop landings from the development of a more extensive military-police regime later in 1904. Sources: [JACAR](https://www.jacar.go.jp/english/exhibition/glossary_en/term4/0010-0050-0050.html); [National Institute of Korean History](https://db.history.go.kr/id/nh_043_0020_0020_0010).
2. **August 22, 1904 adviser agreement:** The Korea-side signatory was **Yun Chi-ho**, the acting foreign minister. A contemporary U.S. diplomatic note states that Articles I–II were concluded August 19 and Article III on August 22. Added the staging distinction. Sources: [NIHK primary-source transcription](https://contents.history.go.kr/front/hm/view.do?levelId=hm_121_0150); [U.S. Office of the Historian](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1904/d432).
3. **Casualties at Port Arthur and Mukden:** Retained approximate, clearly qualified numbers rather than imposing a single casualty count across incompatible definitions. Combined military-loss categories and later estimates vary; some accounts place Mukden casualties at approximately 90,000 Russian and 75,000 Japanese. Sources: [RAND historical operational study](https://www.rand.org/content/dam/rand/pubs/research_reports/RRA500/RRA591-2/RAND_RRA591-2.pdf) and contemporary [Naval Institute material](https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/1907/january/professional-notes).
4. **July–August 1907 accession:** Correctly distinguishes the executive-power delegation (July 19), the proxy ceremony (July 20), Gojong's formal removal and Sunjong's new designation, and his **August 27, 1907** enthronement. The date of ceremonial accession must not be substituted for the July political crisis. Sources: [Korean studies reference](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0015187); [Archontology's official-gazette chronology](https://www.archontology.org/nations/korea/00_1897_1910_s.php).
5. **Righteous-army regional geography:** Corrected the misleading listing of **Yi Gang-nyeon as a Honam leader**. His principal campaigns were in Gangwon, Chungcheong and northern Gyeongsang. **An Gyu-hong** was a Boseong/Jeolla commander; Jeon Hae-san and Sim Nam-il also belong in the southwestern resistance narrative. Sources: [Yi Gang-nyeon, AKS](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0043493); [An Gyu-hong, AKS](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0034547).
6. **Righteous-army statistics:** The commonly cited Japanese retrospective compilation of **2,852 engagements** and **approximately 140,000 reported participant-instances** covers **August 1907–June 1911**, not simply 1907–1910. These counts do **not** constitute a census of unique participants, and categories such as killed, wounded, captured, and civilian supporter must remain separate. Sources: [NIHK original synthesis](https://db.history.go.kr/modern/level.do?levelId=hdsr_005_0020_0020_0010); [NIHK quantitative study](https://db.history.go.kr/id/kn_023_0060_0050_0020).
7. **1909 southern suppression:** Distinguished a specific South Jeolla report's approximate **420 deaths and 1,687 arrests/surrenders** from larger campaign aggregates; the records are not additive. Source: [AKS southern campaign study](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0066314).
8. **1910 royal seals:** Replaced an unverifiable tale of Sunjong's consort hiding a royal seal with a cautious discussion of the surviving Korean edict's seals and disputed procedural implications. Source: [Yonhap report on the original promulgation documents](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20100811001000315). The source reflects a disputed legal interpretation, not an uncontested conclusion.
9. **1910 chapter boundary:** Removed a forward reference that treated September 1910 association bans as part of the pre-annexation period. Retained pre-annexation restrictions as the relevant subject; colonial rule in September/October 1910 belongs to Chapter 55.
10. **Dokdo/Takeshima:** Retained a restrained reference to Japan's 1905 wartime incorporation notice, without presenting its legality or motivation as established; the later territorial dispute remains reserved to Chapter 88.
11. **Chronology preview:** Split the composite Yalu/Port Arthur row into correct 1904 and 1905 entries and reordered the August 1904 adviser agreement accordingly.
