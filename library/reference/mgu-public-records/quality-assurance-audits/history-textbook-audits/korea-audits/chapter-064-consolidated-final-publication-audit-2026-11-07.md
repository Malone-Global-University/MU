## 4. Direct historical corrections and documentary verification

1. **Yeo–Endō meeting (§64.5 and chronology):** Replaced the vague dating with the **8 a.m., August 15** meeting reported by Yeo in his August 16 speech, published in *Maeil Sinbo* August 17. Qualified the detail explicitly as Yeo’s own contemporaneously reported account; did not claim an independent verbatim transcript of the private meeting. His five demands were restated, including food provision for August–October, political and economic prisoners, organization and noninterference. [NIKH original report](https://db.history.go.kr/id/dh_001_1945_08_16_0050).
2. **CPKI founding (§64.8 and chronology):** Distinguished preparatory activity on the afternoon of August 15, public formation/announcement on August 16, and consolidation of chairman/vice chairman organization by August 17. Dates refer to stages, not contradictory “foundings.” [NIKH historical synthesis](https://contents.history.go.kr/mobile/nh/view.do?levelId=nh_052_0030_0010_0010_0020).
3. **CPKI declaration (§64.8 and Source Window III):** Retained the documentary **August 28** date while noting a separate August 26 chronology in secondary synthesis. Added explicit attention to progressive democratic coalition, opposition to imperial-aligned antidemocratic forces, democratic government, livelihoods, order and independence. Kept the window a labeled editorial paraphrase. [NIKH primary document and commentary](https://contents.history.go.kr/front/hm/view.do?levelId=hm_144_0040).
4. **Soviet operations (§§64.13–64.14):** Distinguished air/naval actions beginning August 9; Unggi August 11, Rajin August 12–13 and Chongjin August 13–16; clarified 25th Army command and order to occupy the northern zone. The narrative cites a Soviet-record-based study placing Pyongyang’s capture August 24 while recording other accounts of a column/headquarters there August 26; these accounts are not collapsed into a false single-day certainty. [Peer-reviewed research](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/international-journal-of-asian-studies/article/war-and-peace-in-liberated-north-korea-soviet-military-administration-and-the-creation-of-north-korean-police-force-in-1945/B26380E11146A0FDF8766960B2947ED3).
5. **Potsdam (§64.2):** Clarified that the surrender demand concerned **Japanese armed forces**, without asserting the declaration itself decreed abolition of all Japanese civil institutions. [U.S. National Archives surrender instrument](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/surrender-of-japan).
6. **The *Ukishima Maru* (§64.17):** Dated sinking to **August 24, 1945, near Maizuru**; identified the **official reported toll of 549 (524 Koreans, 25 Japanese military personnel)** and clearly distinguished this official count from contested larger estimates. No claim that deliberate destruction has been established. [Documentary study](https://apjjf.org/2021/19/bull-ivings).
7. **Occupation preparedness (§64.15):** Added the contemporary August 26 U.S. diplomatic record concerning preliminary plans to use Japanese administrative personnel under military supervision. The text does not treat this plan as unalterable or a public promise. [NIKH/FRUS](https://db.history.go.kr/contemp/level.do?levelId=frus_001r_0010_0190&types=r).
8. **The 38th parallel:** Preserved the essential distinction between a line for accepting Japanese surrender and subsequent political division. The narrative does not imply that a Korean representative approved the August 10–11 selection or that two states already existed in 1945. [Dean Rusk’s later recollection, FRUS](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1945v06/d771); [General Order No. 1, FRUS](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1945v06/d801).
9. **Overseas leadership (§64.18):** Maintained October return of Syngman Rhee and **November 23** return of Kim Ku and the first Provisional Government group, with the condition of personal/private status and no formal Allied recognition. [NIKH documentary record](https://contents.history.go.kr/front/hm/view.do?levelId=hm_144_0090).

## 6. Edition-specific limitations and final judgment

- The four primary-source windows are **editorial paraphrases**, identified as such; original-language and translation records are cited, but an exhaustive line-by-line multilingual diplomatic edition was **not** conducted.
- The two map studies are historically grounded **text-based map exercises** that require external maps; no archival cartographic facsimiles or original plates are embedded.
- Some August 1945 timelines are subject to differences in what a source means by organization, announcement, entry, occupation headquarters or later retrospective memory. The manuscript documents the most consequential differences rather than forcing unjustified precision.
- Structural integrity was checked via DOM parsing, unique ID audit, internal-link audit, Node syntax validation and Chromium rendering; it was not certified under a formal HTML conformance or WCAG testing suite.
