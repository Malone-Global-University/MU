## 3. Historical corrections made directly in the HTML

1. **Four phases of cultural opening, §88.3:** Distinguishes stage announcements on 28 Oct 1998, 10 Sep 1999, 27 Jun 2000 and effective fourth phase on 1 Jan 2004. Separates smaller concerts, expanded live performances, limited instrumental recordings, later Japanese-language recorded vocals, and terrestrial versus cable/satellite broadcasts. Based on research citing Korean cultural-policy records; exact subcategory licensing still requires the original implementing regulations for full regulatory collation.
2. **Yasukuni Shrine, §88.15:** Corrected the erroneous claim that all fourteen enshrined Class-A indictees had been convicted. Twelve were convicted; two died before verdict. Cross-checked against Akiko Takenaka's historical study.
3. **2018 court judgments, §88.14:** Now identifies the 30 October Nippon Steel damages as ₩100 million to each of four plaintiffs and avoids generalizing that sum to the separate 29 November Mitsubishi judgments. Identifies case number 2013Da61381 and explains the distinction between a domestic judgment and an intergovernmental settlement.
4. **Japan–North Korea repatriation, §88.9:** Gives approximately 93,340 persons between 1959 and 1984, **including Japanese spouses and other Japanese family members**, avoiding the false inference that all were ethnic Korean repatriates.
5. **2024 Sado memorial, §88.15:** Gives Seoul's 23 November announcement of nonattendance at the 24 November ceremony, notes unresolved arrangements and selection of Japan's representative, and distinguishes alternative remembrance from lack of commemoration.
6. **2023 export-control reversals, §88.19:** Distinguishes Japanese materials-specific licensing change in March, South Korea's preferential list restoration in April and Japan's Group A restoration effective July 21. The original shorthand implied a simultaneous reciprocal change.
7. **2025 diplomatic meeting, §88.20:** Adds Lee Jae-myung's first summit with Shigeru Ishiba on 17 June 2025 during the G7 in Kananaskis, Canada, verified by Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs records.
8. **2026 diplomacy, §88.20:** Maintains the verified 13 January Nara Lee–Takaichi summit and 15 October Bangkok extension decision for the $10 billion currency-swap arrangement. Clarifies an available swap as a policy instrument, not evidence of actual drawing on it.
9. **2002 World Cup organizational history, §88.6:** Clarifies the separate Korean (KOWOC) and Japanese (JAWOC) committees and FIFA coordination, verified against Korean National Archives official reports, instead of implying one unified national organizing committee.
10. **Analytical and educational development:** All 20 sections strengthened with distinct interpretive content, emphasizing legal categories, document provenance, movement of culture through licensing and rights, marginalized voices, and implementation over announcement.

## 6. Disclosed limitations

1. Source validation was selective and targeted to disputed historical claims and the four primary-source windows; it was **not** a complete original-language archival/facsimile collation of every document and statement.
2. The documentary source studies are labeled editorial paraphrases, not diplomatic verbatim transcriptions, authenticated translations prepared by MGU, survivor oral histories, or page-collated primary quotations.
3. The geography exercise is text-directed and requires a historical atlas or external map; it does not embed maps or claim cartographic plates.
4. Exact byte-for-byte parity with the original MGU Chapter 1 HTML/CSS was not independently established; the editor preserved the supplied chapter's MGU textbook design and made minimal necessary responsive/accessibility additions.
5. External citation link presence was verified in HTML and their research targets checked selectively against retrieved public records; every link's live accessibility under the user's deployment environment was **not** tested.
6. No live MGU website deployment or server-side rendering has been performed. Publication approval here is for the **self-contained scholarly textual HTML edition** only.
