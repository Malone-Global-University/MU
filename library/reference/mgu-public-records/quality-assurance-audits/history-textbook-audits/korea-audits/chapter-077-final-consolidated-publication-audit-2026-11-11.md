## C. Material publication corrections performed

### C1. Remove compromised historical title from scholarly support

Charles K. Armstrong's *Tyranny of the Weak* (Cornell University Press, 2013) was removed from the numbered evidentiary references; the register now explicitly notes why it is excluded: Columbia University found research misconduct associated with that book and its publisher withdrew it. Armstrong's **distinct 2003 title** is separately listed without claiming the withdrawn 2013 book is reliable. This is an evidentiary correction, not a blanket judgment about every other work by the author.

Independent public documentation: Inside Higher Ed, September 13, 2019: https://www.insidehighered.com/news/2019/09/13/columbia-says-historians-acclaimed-book-north-korea-was-plagiarized-publisher-says ; Retraction Watch, September 17, 2019: https://retractionwatch.com/2019/09/17/columbia-historian-stepping-down-after-plagiarism-finding/ .

### C2. Five dated, identifiable documentary anchors inserted

All original feature windows are preserved as **editorial paraphrases/syntheses**, clearly distinct from genuine translated quotations and the historian's interpretations. Added a document-specific anchor and discoverable URL(s) inside each window:

1. **1956 factional crisis** — *Record of a Meeting between the Sino-Soviet Delegation and the Korean Workers' Party Presidium*, **September 20, 1956**; GARF fond 5446, opis 98c, delo 718, folios 18–34, a precisely identifiable contemporary record as cited by James F. Person. Also identified a separate **September 23** draft-resolution meeting record. The general crisis narrative is labeled composite editorial synthesis, not a claim that the single September 20 record contains the entire sequence. References: https://doi.org/10.1080/14682745.2018.1509849 ; https://policycommons.net/artifacts/39378316/minutes-of-the-meeting-concerning-the-discussion-of-a-draft-resolution-of-the-kwp-cc-plenum/40277415/ .
2. **1957–61 planning** — Soviet ambassador A. M. Puzanov, **journal entry of June 9, 1958**, USSR Embassy no. 113 (filed June 12), observing the DPRK Supreme People's Assembly discussion and the omission/minimization of Soviet aid in Ri Jong-ok's presentation. The window now makes clear that this source **cannot itself prove** a later claim of early completion of the plan. https://digitalarchive.umd.edu/document/journal-soviet-ambassador-dprk-m-puzanov-9-june-1958 . Analytical companion: Hyungsub Choi's documented 2004 study.
3. **1955 Juche speech** — Kim Il Sung's text *conventionally dated* **December 28, 1955** and its edited English translation excerpt in *Sources of Korean Tradition*, vol. 2 (2000), **pp. 420–424**. The edition and provenance limits are explicitly disclosed. https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/main_pop/ps/ps_korea-juche.htm .
4. **1972 Socialist Constitution** — explicitly the constitution adopted **December 27, 1972**, identifying original Articles **4, 73, 89–93, 100–101, 107**. Linked a publicly available 1972-version English transcription and a 1986 Library of Congress legal analysis; warned explicitly against treating the 1992/1998/2009 amended text as 1972 wording. https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Socialist_Constitution_of_the_Democratic_People%27s_Republic_of_Korea_(1972) ; https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/llglrdppub/2019668792/2019668792.pdf . The 1972-version transcription was not independently matched against a contemporary Korean facsimile.
5. **1980 party congress** — Kim Il Sung, *Report to the Sixth Congress of the Workers' Party of Korea on the Work of the Central Committee*, **October 10, 1980**, issued by Foreign Languages Publishing House, Pyongyang (1980); and the **October 15, 1980** *Rodong Sinmun* party-leadership roster as cited by subsequent research. The window now distinguishes Kim Il Sung's report from the later leadership elections and makes clear that the original newspaper page has not been facsimile-collated. Bibliographic catalog: https://search.worldcat.org/title/13794830 ; historical roster https://nkleadershipwatch.wordpress.com/the-party/6th-party-central-committee-plenums/ .

This improves **document identification and attribution**, not the evidentiary status of the original editorial paraphrases. The manuscript does not contain five independently collated primary-source translations. Students seeking exact original wording should consult the named editions and archives.

### C4. Targeted chronology and factual precision

- **Chinese aid (§77.5):** distinguishes the November 1953 pledge of **8 trillion old yuan** for 1954–57 from **800 million new yuan** after the 1955 currency reform at **10,000 old:1 new**, distinguishing commitments from actual disbursements. Avoid conflating figures from different monetary denominations or later accounting series. See the contemporaneous Chinese reporting and historical aid surveys identified in the initial audit.
- **Juche (§77.9 and chronology):** date is now conventionally attributed to **December 28, 1955**, without claiming independently verified contemporary speech notes; continued care over retrospective textual revision.
- **Chollima (§77.7):** clarifies that late-1956/early-1957 *production mobilization* developed into the named campaign, while the more specific Chollima Work Team Movement dates to **1959**. No assumption that every form emerged fully formed in December 1956.
- No change to the appropriately nuanced treatment of 1956 critics, Pak Hon-yong's uncertain execution date, 1957–60s songbun formation, the 1967 Kapsan purge, 1972 constitution, or 1974/1980 succession distinction.
