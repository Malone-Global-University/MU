## 3. Historical accuracy corrections — directly applied

1. **Family law, §81.15:** corrected 1977 amendment to **promulgated December 31, 1977, effective January 1, 1979**; next major amendment **promulgated January 13, 1990, effective January 1, 1991**, not “passed in 1989.” Family-head system persisted. Anchors: National Institute of Korean History, https://contents.history.go.kr/front/hm/view.do?levelId=hm_162_0060 and KLRI Act No. 4199, https://elaw.klri.re.kr/eng_service/lawViewContent.do?hseq=15836 . The 1990 amendment is expressly described as a subsequent event outside the main period.
2. **Hyundai Pony, §81.18:** replaced misleading “Korean-designed” characterization. Hyundai assembled and developed the domestic mass-production model with **Giorgetto Giugiaro / Italdesign styling and Japanese-derived mechanical technology**; 1974 presentation, late 1975 market introduction and 1976 broader sales/export. Sources: https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/footer/corporate/vehicle-history/1970/pony and https://www.giorgettofabriziogiugiaro.it/projects/vehicles/pony .
3. **Women's earnings, §81.6:** replaced unsourced unqualified wage-gap assertion with identified **aggregate survey earnings ratios** of ~42% in 1975 and ~43% in 1980 from World Bank's contemporary labor-force study, and explicitly distinguished earnings ratios from equal-pay comparisons. Source: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/398531468915040832/pdf/The-labor-force-participation-of-women-in-the-republic-of-Korea-evolution-and-policy-issues.pdf .
4. **Wawoo apartment, §81.10:** inserted exact April 8, 1970 **33 deaths and 39 serious injuries**, referring to the Academy of Korean Studies account: https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0068304 .
5. **Redevelopment / Olympic displacement, §81.19:** attributed approximately **720,000 affected persons (1983–1988)** to the Asian Coalition for Housing Rights' advocacy-based reporting and later scholarship; explicitly *not* a census-confirmed Olympic-construction-only count. Anchor: contemporary 1989 investigation, https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/095624788900100112 ; subsequent critical historical synthesis https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-2427.2010.00970.x .
6. **Education reform, §§81.13–81.14:** distinguished progressive middle-school entrance changes from 1969, announcement of **1973** high-school equalization policy, initial **1974** Seoul and Busan implementation, and continued tertiary competition. Anchor: https://www.archives.go.kr/next/newsearch/listSubjectDescription.do?id=003164&sitePage= .
7. **Demography and social welfare, §81.16:** documented fertility rate approximate six around 1960 and 1.7 by 1985, staged national health insurance from 1977 to population-wide coverage by 1989, and avoided equating coverage with every service being free.
