## 3. Applied factual corrections

### C1 — 1963 presidential electoral geography, §75.4

Replaced the misleading phrasing that Park was relatively weak in “the northwest.” The manuscript now specifies the strong 1963 performance in both southeastern and southwestern provinces and Jeju, and weaker performance in Seoul and several northern/central regions **of the Republic of Korea**. It explicitly warns against projecting the pronounced 1971 Yeongnam–Honam divide backward onto 1963.

**Evidence:** National Election Commission historical voting tables (2010 research report), source-register item 10. The 1963 national vote margin of 156,026 votes is consistent with those tables.

### C2 — Vietnam deployment stages, §75.11

Corrected the assertion that both the first medical personnel and military engineers arrived in 1965. The initial deployment consisted of **140 medical and Taekwondo personnel dispatched September 11, 1964**. A larger **noncombat engineering/logistics force of approximately 2,000 followed in March 1965**; combat troops arrived later in 1965, followed by the Ninth Division in 1966.

**Evidence:** National Archives of Korea governmental deployment history, source-register item 6, https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/govpo/detail.do?policySeq=503 .

### C3 — Brown Memorandum date, §75.11, source window 3, chronology

Distinguished the **March 4, 1966 date on the document** from its **March 7, 1966 delivery** to the South Korean government by U.S. Ambassador Winthrop G. Brown. The distinction is consistent across the narrative, source-window title, source metadata, contextual discussion, and chronology preview.

**Evidence:** National Institute of Korean History documentary presentation with memorandum text, source-register item 3, https://contents.history.go.kr/front/hm/view.do?levelId=hm_150_0070&treeId=020108 .

### C4 — External-debt figure and definition, §75.16

Replaced the unqualified “around twenty billion dollars by the end of the 1970s” with a source-identified **IMF historical series reporting $20.41 billion total gross external debt at end-1979, including short-term liabilities**. Explicitly distinguished gross external debt from net debt or debt-service payments and explained that alternative institutional/reporting coverage can yield higher totals.

**Evidence:** IMF historical gross external debt table, source-register item 9, https://www.elibrary.imf.org/display/book/9780939934980/ch004.xml . No exact equivalence is asserted with other historical series.

### C5 — Identified KDI HCI scholarship, §75.15

Replaced vague references to unspecified KDI research with exact works:

- Joseph J. Stern, Jihong Kim, Dwight H. Perkins, and Jung-Ho Yoo, *Industrialization and the State: The Korean Heavy and Chemical Industry Drive* (Korea Development Institute, 1995).
- Jihong Kim, *Korean Industrial Policy in the 1970s: The Heavy and Chemical Industry Drive*, KDI Working Paper 9015 (1990).

Both institutional publication records were independently checked. These are later scholarly assessments, not period primary sources. The analysis retains legitimate disagreement over HCI benefits, costs, timing and financing rather than declaring one interpretation settled.

## 4. Historical checks retained without revision

These claims were checked in the initial independent audit and left in appropriately qualified form:

- **May 1964 exchange-rate reform:** official rate approximately 130 to 255–256 won per U.S. dollar, with system consolidation in 1965.
- **1971 presidential voting:** Park's 6,342,828 votes and Kim Dae-jung's 5,395,900 votes, with the denominator of voting percentages to be handled explicitly.
- **1972 Yushin delegate election:** 2,359 delegates, 2,357 Park votes, two invalid.
- **1978 Yushin delegate result:** 2,577 Park votes; do not conflate votes cast for Park with the total membership.
- **1978 National Assembly:** NDP 32.8% / 61 direct seats, DRP 31.7% / 68 direct seats; 77 indirectly selected members.
- **Early Saemaul cement allocations:** often reported as 335 bags for each of approximately 33,267 villages; do not imply that a uniform material allocation proves identical outcomes.
- **Vietnam service:** cumulative rotated deployment above 300,000 personnel and roughly 5,000 South Korean dead; aggregate totals differ by definitions and must not erase documented civilian abuses in Vietnam.
- **1965 bilateral claims agreement:** separate the specific intergovernmental wording from subsequently disputed individual claims.

No wholesale rewrite or arbitrary shortening was necessary, and the core thesis remains that industrial success depended on institutions, foreign relations, firms and labor while authoritarian practices shaped the period without making repression an inevitable prerequisite of development.
