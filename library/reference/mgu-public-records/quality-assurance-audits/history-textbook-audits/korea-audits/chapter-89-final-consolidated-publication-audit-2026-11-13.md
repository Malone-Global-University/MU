## 3. Source-grounded historical accuracy checks

The following targeted verification was performed before incorporating source-specific claims. Citation links below give the directly consulted source or an expressly described secondary presentation. This is an independent editorial source check, **not** an exhaustive audit of every original-language archive, contract or foreign broadcasting record.

| Issue | Verified basis and final treatment |
|---|---|
| **1997 television breakthrough in China** | Government cultural-history reporting identifies the June 1997 CCTV transmission of *What Is Love?* as a significant early national broadcast. The revised text distinguishes earlier Korean production from Chinese airing. See [Korean Culture and Information Service](https://www.korean-culture.org/ebook/access/ecatalogt.jsp?Dir=198&callmode=normal&catimage=&eclang=ko&start=12&um=s) and [Korea.net](https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/Culture/view?articleId=189370). |
| **Meaning and earliest usage of Hallyu** | The revised chapter does **not** claim unanimous agreement on a single 1999 origin. A [KCI-indexed archival article](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002396719) identifies a Taiwanese use connected with Korean popular culture dated December 17, 1998; other conventional accounts describe broader circulation around 1999. |
| **Industry and corporate chronology** | [SM's company chronology](https://cdn2.smentertainment.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/2024-SM-Entertainment-Sustainability-Report_EN-1.pdf) distinguishes 1995 establishment, 1996 H.O.T., 1997 S.E.S., 2000 Beijing concert, and BoA's debut. Historical treatment avoids collapsing SM, YG, and JYP predecessor ventures into one timeline. |
| **1999 Korean cinema** | [1999 Korean film data credited to KOFIC](https://stats.koreanfilm.org/kfilm99.html) reports domestic films' 39.7% nationwide admissions share, up from 25.1% in 1998; 49 Korean film releases; 297 imports; approximately 54.7 million total admissions. The chapter distinguishes Seoul *Shiri* admissions from the nationwide estimate. An independent institutional comparator is available from [KIEP](https://www.kiep.go.kr/galleryDownload.es?bid=0001&list_no=502&seq=1). |
| **1999 Cultural Industries Act** | [WIPO Lex](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/13812) establishes the February 1999 originating act. The linked consolidated English text contains later amendments; the source window **explicitly warns** against reading amended provisions as if present in 1999. The law proves stated policy purpose, not causal effectiveness by itself. |
| **Japanese reception of Winter Sonata** | [Academy of Korean Studies](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0073572) distinguishes the 2002 Korean original from Japanese NHK broadcast beginning in 2003. The chapter does not equate broadcast, fandom, and nationwide social reconciliation. |
| **Jewel in the Palace** | [MBC Global](https://content.mbc.co.kr/english/drama/03/1670401_56047.html) identifies the series and production; historical explanation recognizes localization by foreign broadcasters without ungrounded worldwide viewer totals. |
| **BoA Oricon result** | [KBS World](https://world.kbs.co.kr/service/contents_view.htm?board_seq=166272&menu_cate=enternews&page=396) identifies *Listen to My Heart* (2002) as the artist's first Japanese album to top the Oricon chart. The revised prose specifies the chart rather than unsupported global-sales figures. |
| **Early fan culture: Soompi** | [Soompi's dated April 25, 2006 self-description](https://www.soompi.com/article/357858wpp/about-soompi) supports a March 1998 founding claim and identifies worldwide fan participation. The website's reported one million monthly unique visitors is explicitly attributed as **self-reported**, not independently audited. |
| **2009 contracts and TVXQ chronology** | [Yonhap, July 7, 2009](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20090707005400320) documents the Fair Trade Commission standard-contract announcement. [Yonhap, November 28, 2012](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20121128008300315) retrospectively confirms that the three TVXQ members filed on **July 31, 2009**. The chapter preserves this order and does not recast the seven-year model as a universal statute. |

### Four primary-source windows

1. **1999 film-market statistical records:** a KOFIC-attributed compilation and corroborating institutional study. Correctly labeled as numerical evidence mediated through a later publication, **not** a facsimile-collated film-yearbook original.
2. **1999 Framework Act on the Promotion of Cultural Industries:** specific source provenance and later-amendment disclosure. Paraphrases are the editor's summaries, not purported verbatim quotations.
3. **July 7, 2009 contract reform:** contemporary Yonhap reportage identifying FTC actions. Labeled as a contemporaneous news report rather than falsely identified as a certified contract document.
4. **April 25, 2006 Soompi description:** a genuine dated contemporaneous fan-community statement with self-reported data visibly qualified.

Each window includes creator, date or period, source type, historical context, document-specific editorial paraphrase, analysis, limitations, an actual clickable locator, and at least five student questions. No fabricated primary quotations were inserted.
