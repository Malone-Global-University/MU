## Historical verification and required accuracy refinements

1. **2026 initiatives verified (retain).** The Ministry of Unification issued *Policy for Peaceful Coexistence on the Korean Peninsula* **February 3, 2026**, followed by its work plan **August 5, 2026**. These are unilateral South Korean policy documents, not an inter-Korean agreement. Source: https://www.unikorea.go.kr/web/eng_unikorea/bbs/bbs_0000000000000034/54823 and https://www.unikorea.go.kr/web/eng_unikorea/bbs/bbs_0000000000000034/54911
2. **September 21, 2026 DMZ incident verified as a reported three-injured event (retain with attribution).** Contemporaneous Yonhap accounts report **three South Korean soldiers injured**, a South Korean finding attributing the mines to North Korea, and North Korea's denial. The two governments' opposed claims must remain explicitly attributed. Source: https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20261023005751315
3. **October 23, 2026 talks proposal verified; clarify that it was renewed.** South Korea's October 23 formal offer to clarify the Military Demarcation Line and restore military lines repeated a similar proposal made **in November 2025**; it was **not a completed negotiation**. Revise §§93.19, 93.20, the chronology, and Source Window 8 to identify it as a *renewed* offer. Sources: https://world.kbs.co.kr/service/news_view.htm?Seq_Code=205073&lang=e and https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20261023005751315. Reporting through October 25 found no North Korean response; avoid unsupported certainty about all later private contacts.
4. **Panmunjom Declaration peace-process participation requires precision.** The April 27, 2018 declaration proposed **trilateral meetings (two Koreas plus the United States) or quadrilateral meetings (adding China)** to pursue an end-of-war declaration/peace treaty. The current prose/source paraphrase sometimes says 'with the United States and China' as though both were invariably required. Correct this detail. Primary source: https://www.mofa.go.kr/eng/brd/m_5478/view.do?seq=319130
5. **IOC's January 20, 2018 document naming.** The Olympic Korean Peninsula Declaration is the named arrangement, while the IOC and contemporaneous releases also describe an agreement on joint participation. The claim of twelve North Korean women's hockey players is supportable. Use a precise document title and distinguish named declaration from surrounding IOC news release. Contemporaneous Korean government account: https://www.korea.net/Government/Current-Affairs/International-Events/view?affairId=592&articleId=153160&subId=609
6. **Pyongyang declaration conditional Yongbyon offer (accurate).** September 19, 2018 language conditioned further Yongbyon steps on corresponding U.S. measures. Keep this distinction and cite the original: https://www.mofa.go.kr/eng/brd/m_5478/view.do?seq=319132
7. **Hanoi competing statements (accurate and appropriately attributed).** Trump's February 28 press conference claimed a demand for complete sanctions relief; North Korean foreign minister Ri Yong Ho stated on March 1 that Pyongyang sought partial relief covering five 2016–2017 UN resolutions. The text rightly does not decide what was said privately. Original U.S. transcript: https://2017-2021.state.gov/press-conference-with-president-trump-in-hanoi-vietnam/ and contemporaneous report: https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20190227014060315
8. **North Korea's hostile-state constitutional position (carefully qualified).** KCNA's October 17, 2024 report said the South was a constitutionally designated hostile state; do not claim independently to have collated the full amended constitution. Source: https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20241017000453315
9. **June 11, 2025 loudspeaker suspension (accurate).** Source: https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20250611009800315
10. **October 2026 endpoint.** The chapter fixes November 2026 and does not count the October 23 offer as talks held. Retain this caution. Minor copyedit: distinguish what is documented as of the actual audit cutoff of November 14, 2026 from assertions about events later in November.

Other major dates and chronology—PyeongChang, Panmunjom April 27, Singapore June 12, September 19 declaration, February 2019 Hanoi breakdown, June 16, 2020 liaison-office destruction, 2023–2024 military agreement suspension—conform to the established documentary record in the audit sample. The wider manuscript has not been subject to exhaustive original-language document-by-document collation.
