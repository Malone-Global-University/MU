## 4. Historically material revisions and verified findings

1. **Park Jong-cheol:** January 14, 1987 torture death and the distinction between early official statements and the May 18 priests’ disclosure. The latter is independently catalogued as Korea Democracy Foundation record **00135591** (four pages): https://archives.kdemo.or.kr/isad/view/00135591 . This catalog record is not assumed to be a transcript of every statement made in May.
2. **National coalition and June 10:** Gukbon formed May 27; June 5 organizing instructions and the subsequent mobilization are described by the Korea Democracy Foundation: https://archives.kdemo.or.kr/contents/view/22 and https://archives.kdemo.or.kr/collections/view/10000140 .
3. **Lee Han-yeol:** injured June 9 and died July 5, explicitly distinguished in every relevant passage; see the U.S. Library of Congress research guide and Korean National Archives: https://guides.loc.gov/south-korean-democratization-movement/june-uprising .
4. **June 18 and June 26:** differentiated the anti–tear gas mobilization and nationwide June 26 action; did not conflate varying participant totals derived from organizers, police and historians. The Korea Democracy Foundation independently documents nationwide spread: https://archives.kdemo.or.kr/display/en/move610.html .
5. **June 29 Declaration:** connected mass pressure with possible ruling-elite electoral calculations without inventing evidence that U.S. officials commanded the movement. The original declaration is catalogued as National Archives of Korea **BA0884064**, an eighteen-page record: https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/chronology/archiveDetail.do?evntId=0049319819&flag=2&page=23&sort= . National Institute of Korean History commentary: https://contents.history.go.kr/front/hm/view.do?levelId=hm_151_0050 .
6. **Constitutional chronology:** referendum **October 27, 1987**, promulgation **October 29, 1987**, enforcement **February 25, 1988**. Rights, election rules and constitutional adjudication are tied to the original **Constitution No. 10**, not a paraphrased modern web overview: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=61603&urlMode=engLsInfo&viewCls=engLsInfoR .
7. **1987 presidential election:** approximate vote percentages Roh **36.6**, Kim Young-sam **28.0**, Kim Dae-jung **27.1**, Kim Jong-pil **8.1**. Clarified that Roh won a plurality, not a majority; avoided assuming mechanically that an opposition unity counterfactual would have transferred all votes.
8. **1987 labor disputes:** **3,749** applies to the whole **calendar year** and broadly recorded disputes, not exclusively to July–September strikes. Document **00366613**, an eighteen-page Hyundai labor chronology, is specifically catalogued for 1987: https://archives.kdemo.or.kr/isad/view/00366613 .
9. **April 1988 Assembly election:** Democratic Justice Party **125/299 seats** and **33.96%** votes. The original Inter-Parliamentary Union parliamentary results support this: https://archive.ipu.org/parline-e/reports/arc/REPUBLIC_OF_KOREA_1988_E.PDF .
10. **Seoul Olympics:** September 17–October 2, 1988, **159** participating national Olympic committees; treatment focuses on international legitimacy and security, not duplicating Chapter 81 urban redevelopment: https://olympics.com/en/olympic-games/seoul-1988 .
11. **Accountability:** 1988–1989 parliamentary inquiries and Chun’s December 1989 Assembly testimony are kept distinct from criminal prosecutions and judgments in 1996–1997.
12. **Local democracy:** municipal/county/district councils elected **March 26, 1991**, provincial/metropolitan councils **June 20, 1991**, local executives directly elected **1995**: https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/localSelf/history/timeline/timeline.do .
13. **1990 merger:** distinguished competing claims of stable government, weakened opposition and continuity of existing networks; did not present the merger as either constitutional abolition or unquestioned democratic progress.
14. **1992 election:** Kim Young-sam **42.0%**, Kim Dae-jung **33.8%**, Chung Ju-yung **16.3%** approximate valid-vote shares. His February 25, 1993 inauguration is described as a landmark of civilian leadership within the new direct-election constitutional system, acknowledging the brief civilian presidency of Choi Kyu-hah in 1979–1980 and his election through the governing coalition.

## 7. Publication limits, compatibility, and editorial decision

- **No original-document facsimile collation** was performed for all five window texts. Their quoted labels refer to catalogued records or published editions, and the explanatory paragraphs are clearly editorial studies rather than historical quotations.
- **No exhaustive multilingual archival research** or scholarly page-by-page concordance of every historical proposition was performed. Targeted primary/institutional verification addressed dates, legal instruments, official election returns, participation data, and critical interpretations. The Source Register is a documented research base rather than a complete evidentiary apparatus for every sentence.
- **No embedded atlas plates or photographic facsimiles.** Geography is text guided and students require suitable maps. Three everyday-life positions are expressly illustrative analytic composites, not fabricated interviews.
- **Exact Chapter 1 template byte-for-byte parity is not independently verified.** The publication shell uses the accepted Chapter 81 model styling and conventions; the navigation script is deliberately updated for complete 20-section coverage and functioning scrollspy.
- **No live-site deployment test** was performed. Integrate production breadcrumbs, header/footer components or routes according to the live MGU application when deployed.
