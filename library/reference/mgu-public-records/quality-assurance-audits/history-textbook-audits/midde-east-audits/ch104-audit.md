## Publication-critical corrections made in the HTML

1. **Mount Lebanon Organic Regulation:** Corrected 1861 Article 2 from the mistakenly cited 1864 pattern to **two representatives each** of the six recognized communities (12 total). The **1864 revision** redistributed seats **4 Maronite / 3 Druze / 2 Greek Orthodox / 1 Greek Catholic / 1 Sunni / 1 Shia**. Added a comparative table and corrected the documentary source window and final summary. Source: [1861/1864 French text](https://mjp.univ-perp.fr/constit/lb1861.htm); [Akarlı, *The Long Peace*](https://publishing.cdlib.org/ucpressebooks/public/book/the-long-peace-ottoman-lebanon-1861-1920.html).
2. **Kisrawan revolt:** Removed a spurious January 1858 opening and a false 1859 settlement conclusion. Distinguished **autumn 1858 mobilization**, **early 1859 peasant commonwealth** and **March 1861 defeat** of Shahin's government by Yusuf Karam; corrected the chronology, label, summary, and source-window context. Source: [Makdisi, *The Culture of Sectarianism*](https://publishing.cdlib.org/ucpressebooks/public/book/the-culture-of-sectarianism-community-history-and-violence-in-nineteenth-century-ottoman-lebanon.html).
3. **1860 casualty estimates:** Removed the assertion that approximately ten thousand deaths represented Mount Lebanon **and** Damascus combined. Distinguished scholarly estimates for the **mountain** and **Damascus** separately; maintained uncertainty about their counting basis and total. Source: [Najwa al-Qattan on Damascus](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/comparative-studies-in-society-and-history/article/abs/litigants-and-neighbors-the-communal-topography-of-ottoman-damascus/9AB936A08703A563CD83C19D4B63D03B).
4. **Circassian/Chechen settlements:** Changed Jerash's foundation from 1878 to **1884**, retained **Amman 1878**, and specified **Wadi al-Sir 1880, Naʿur 1901, al-Rusayfa 1904**, with distinct Chechen settlement dates. Source: [Hamed-Troyansky, *IJMES* 49 (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0020743817000617).
5. **Ottoman immigration/refugee law:** Distinguished the **May 1856 Silistra instructions**, **1857 immigration framework**, and **1860 Refugee Commission** from one another. The source window now cautions against treating the exemptions as uniform for every time and place or asserting a generic 1866 law without identifying a document. Source: [Hamed-Troyansky (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0020743817000617).
6. **Amman property case:** Identified **Gülʿazar bin Hamid and eight relatives**, the buyer **Yusuf al-Sukkar**, **a sixteen-room house and six shops**, **98,340 kuruş**, and the archived 1912 land-register folios cited in the published study. The window explicitly states that original registry folios were **not independently collated**. Source: [Hamed-Troyansky (2017), pp. 605, 616–17 and n. 1](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0020743817000617).
7. **Historical geography:** Qualified the Jerusalem sanjak reorganization as developing **1872–1874**, avoiding an over-precise interpretation of a complex sequence.
