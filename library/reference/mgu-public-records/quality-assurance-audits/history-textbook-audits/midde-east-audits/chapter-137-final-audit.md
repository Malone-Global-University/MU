## Historical corrections implemented

1. **Sèvres and Mosul:** Articles 62–64 now distinguish the core proposed autonomy area from Article 64's conditional provision allowing Kurds in the Mosul vilayet to accede voluntarily to a future independent Kurdish state. The treaty was never implemented.
2. **1958 versus 1970:** The 1970 autonomy manifesto is no longer incorrectly called Iraq's first recognition of Arab–Kurdish national partnership. Article 3 of the July 1958 provisional constitution had already recognized the partnership. The 1970 agreement broadened that principle into specified institutional promises.
3. **Mahabad precedence:** The 1946 republic is presented as a landmark rather than unqualifiedly the first Kurdish experiment in modern government.
4. **Kirkuk demographics:** The revised text distinguishes Kirkuk city from the surrounding rural area and province and gives 1957 mother-tongue city figures with their limits, without equating language with political allegiance.
5. **1974 autonomy law:** The law's unilateral character, constrained institutional powers, and Kirkuk territorial exclusion are distinguished from the 1970 negotiated program.
6. **March 1975 refugee estimates:** The categorical “well over 100,000” claim is replaced by an explicitly attributed historical estimate of 90,000–250,000 people entering Iran, while acknowledging uncertainty and subsequent returns.
7. **Iranian Kurdish activism:** The Revolutionary Committee's 1966 formation and 1967–1968 campaign are distinguished; accounts extending consequences into 1969 are noted without falsely shifting the core events.
8. **Pike Committee:** The approximately $16 million account remains attributed to the inquiry and contemporary reporting, rather than presented as an audited universal total for all external assistance.
9. **Bibliographic improvement:** The Hassanpour volume's publisher is corrected to Mellen Research University Press.

## Expansion and scholarly method

All twenty sections gained direct explanatory prose, with priority given to the previously thin 1958–1970 Iraqi political crisis, internal KDP disputes, the 1970–1975 autonomy collapse, Iranian party survival and rural social history, the 1967–1968 Iranian armed movement, and Kurdish organizing in 1960s–1970s Turkey. The expansion strengthens political institutions, social differentiation, minority populations, language policy, land tenure, civilian displacement, and how to interpret partisan and governmental sources. The 1975 endpoint is retained; later Kurdish wars are not imported into this chapter.

A third Historical Vocabulary feature examines **census territory, city boundaries, and governorates**. The initial 24-term preview and 30-term glossary have been reconciled to **20 preview terms, all defined in a 35-entry glossary**. The Chronology Preview is reduced from 18 to 15 entries, combining earlier settlement chronology and preserving the main comparative turning points.

A native `<details>` mobile table of contents was added. It has no JavaScript dependency and complements rather than modifies the original sidebar. Test rendering at desktop (1,400 px) and mobile (390 px) widths found no JavaScript exceptions and no horizontal overflow; the mobile Section 137.10 link navigated to the correct anchor.
