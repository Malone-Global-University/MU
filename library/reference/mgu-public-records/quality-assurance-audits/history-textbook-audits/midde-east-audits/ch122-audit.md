## 3. Factual verification and editorial refinements

1. **Demographic series:** Official full-enumeration census counts of **83,794** Jewish residents in 1922 and **174,610** in 1931 are separated from **December 31 estimates** of **355,157** in 1935 and **445,457** in 1939. Alternative civilian-only census series differ slightly; none are silently combined. Percentages and growth calculations were recomputed in the quantitative study. Related documents: [historical population series](https://www.cjpme.org/fs_007); [official census table reproductions](https://www.jewishdatabank.org/api/download/?mediaId=bjdb%5C1946_Statistics_of_Jews_World_and_US_Jewish_Population_%28AJYB%2C_1946-1947%29.pdf&studyId=910).
2. **Immigration:** British [1936 parliamentary reporting](https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/1936-07-02/debates/cc5629c9-f202-41e4-a080-ae53a014e8ec/Palestine) confirms **61,854 registered Jewish arrivals in 1935**, not all in the labor category. The 1938 parliamentary answer further distinguishes categories. This is not equated with net population increase.
3. **Haavara:** The 1933 agreement is described as a controversial mechanism for emigrant asset transfer through trade in German goods, not as a general political alliance. [USHMM](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/refugees) corroborates its refugee function and wider immigration context.
4. **Jewish Agency and communal organs:** The 1929 enlarged Jewish Agency is distinguished from the Zionist Organization, the Vaad Leumi, Jewish communal regulations of 1928, and the executive powers of the British High Commissioner. Source studies distinguish the contemporary [1929 mandatory annual report](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-206576/) and later official retrospective.
5. **Revisionist chronology:** Betar's founding in Riga in **1923** is distinguished from the Revisionist political organization formed in **1925**.
6. **Higher education:** Technion's first teaching in the winter of **1924–1925** is distinguished from the Hebrew University's **1 April 1925** opening. The contemporary [Nature report](https://www.nature.com/articles/115681a0) distinguishes its ceremonial launch from later operational growth.
7. **Labor and land:** Histadrut's foundation in December 1920 is separated from Bank Hapoalim's foundation in 1921; ownership, cultivated acreage and annual land purchases are differentiated. The [1936 annual report](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-208860/) documents 18,146 dunams purchased by Jews from non-Jews in 1936 versus 72,903 in 1935; these are annual flows, not total holdings.
8. **Haganah and Irgun:** Declared Haganah restraint, later mobile operations, British-authorized auxiliary policing, Special Night Squads and Irgun attacks on civilians are differentiated without conflating legal authority or all organizational conduct.
9. **Partition and White Paper:** Zionist negotiation on the 1937 Peel proposal is distinguished from approval of the proposed borders; 1938 Woodhead developments are kept separate. The [1939 White Paper](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/20th_century/brwh1939.asp) specifies 50,000 ordinary places over five years plus 25,000 refugee places with conditions, and subsequent Arab acquiescence. The 1940 land regulations are not backdated to 1939.
