## Historical verification and targeted corrections
1. **§43.14 — ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn Jaḥdam:** the identification is sound. The governor is ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿUtba al-Fihrī (Ibn Jaḥdam), Zubayrid appointee in Egypt in 684; see Hugh Kennedy, “Egypt as a province in the Islamic caliphate, 641–868,” in *Cambridge History of Egypt* vol. 1 (1998), p. 70. Current naming is defensible; optionally give complete patronymic.
2. **§43.15 — ʿAmr ibn Saʿīd al-Ashdaq:** revolt and death under ʿAbd al-Malik are confirmed. G. R. Hawting, *The First Dynasty of Islam*, 2nd ed., printed p. 59, dates the revolt **689–690**, rather than precisely 689, and notes the killing after reportedly promised safe conduct. Improve the year to **689–690 (69–70 AH)**.
3. **§43.19 — ʿUrwa ibn al-Zubayr:** death “about 712” is acceptable; scholarly works also give c. 712/713 (94 AH). See Andreas Görke and Gregor Schoeler, *The Earliest Writings on the Life of Muḥammad* (2024).
4. **§43.43 — Damascus:** the person described anonymously as “a governor of Damascus, an Umayyad family member” is **al-Walīd ibn Muʿāwiya ibn Marwān, Marwān II’s son-in-law**. Al-Ṭabarī, *History*, vol. XXVII, trans. John Alden Williams (1985), printed pp. **169–172**, reports he was left to hold Damascus, was killed amid internal factional fighting, and the Abbasids breached the city on **2 May 750** (20 Ramaḍān 132). Replace “probably April” with a qualified “late April or early May; al-Ṭabarī dates its capture to 2 May 750”, or cite the transmitted date directly. This distinction is important; do not imply he necessarily died fighting the Abbasid attackers.
5. **§43.23 — Amazigh revolt:** prior errors have been fixed correctly: the Battle of the Nobles in 740, Bagdoura in 741 (Kulthūm killed), and Ḥanẓala’s defense of Qayrawān in 742.
6. **§43.32 — al-Kirmānī:** the previous imprecise “746 or 747” has been corrected to **747**.
7. **§43.17 — the Khāzir force:** revised wording correctly distinguishes the Syrian expedition initiated under Marwān I and maintained under ʿAbd al-Malik.
8. **§43.13 — al-Mukhtār during the first siege of Mecca:** reported participation is documented in al-Ṭabarī, *History* vol. XX, translated G. R. Hawting; preserve “according to the reports” framing.
