### Documentary cross-checks performed for this audit

- The Iran Data Portal reproduces the Majlis decision of June 21, 1981 and Khomeini's dismissal decree of June 22, 1981. This establishes that the removal followed the release of 52 hostages on January 20. See [Dismissal of Bani Sadr as President](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/dismissal-of-bani-sadr-as-president).
- The Iran Data Portal's [1982 Assembly of Experts Election](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/1982-assembly-of-experts-election/) gives December 10, 1982 as the election date. The exact dating of the body's inaugural activities is less important than the corrected distinction between election, first meetings in 1983, and the 1989 amendments.
- Saïd Amir Arjomand's [Constitution of the Islamic Republic](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/constitution-of-the-islamic-republic/) and [Dual Leadership and Constitutional Developments after Khomeini](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780195391794.003.0003) support the separation of June 1989 leadership selection from the July constitutional referendum and the changed constitutional qualifications and powers. The constitution article contains an apparent year error for Bani-Sadr's dismissal; the dated dismissal decree is preferable on that point.
- The financial and diplomatic sequence is consistent in its essentials with [Encyclopaedia Iranica, Hostage Crisis](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/hostage-crisis/), which reports the transfer of approximately $7.98 billion into escrow and approximately $3.67 billion toward bank liabilities. The [Tribunal founding documents](https://iusct.com/foundingdocuments-2/) are the appropriate primary source for the security-account requirement. The security account and broader claims-related transfers should not be represented as identical accounting categories.
- [Human Rights Watch, Iran: 1988 Mass Executions Evident Crimes Against Humanity (2022)](https://www.hrw.org/news/2022/06/08/iran-1988-mass-executions-evident-crimes-against-humanity) documents estimates ranging from 2,800 to 5,000 and the extensive evidence for the killings. [Amnesty International, The 20th Anniversary of 1988 'Prison Massacre' (2008)](https://www.amnesty.org.uk/knowledge-hub/all-resources/iran-20th-anniversary-1988-prison-massacre/) reports an estimated 4,500–5,000. Neither estimate is a definitive victim register; the chapter's qualification is appropriate.
- The earlier audit accepted approximate deaths around 72 in the June 28, 1981 bombing, recognizing differing counts. The chapter need not force a single exact total where sources vary. The earlier audit also accepted the qualitative referendum descriptions as broadly correct. Official margins should not be used as evidence of unrestricted choice or unanimous assent.

## 5. Newly identified correction — constitutional drafting process

**Location:** Section 145.2, second narrative paragraph.

**Current problematic sentence:**

> The Revolutionary Council reviewed it and, rather than putting it directly to a referendum as Bazargan wished, called for the election of an assembly to examine it.

Arjomand's constitutional history attributes the direct-referendum proposal to Khomeini, while Bazargan and Bani-Sadr insisted on elected assembly consideration. This is not merely a stylistic distinction. The issue concerns competing conceptions of constitutional legitimacy and belongs to the primary analytical argument of the section. Arjomand also explains that the proposed large constituent assembly ultimately gave way to the smaller Assembly of Experts for the Constitution. The final manuscript's subsequent analysis of the assembly's political composition can remain intact.

**Recommended replacement:**

> The Revolutionary Council reviewed the draft. Khomeini favored submitting it directly to a referendum, while Bazargan and Bani-Sadr insisted on consideration by an elected constituent assembly. The resulting compromise was the election of a smaller Assembly of Experts for the Constitution, a decision that altered the political setting in which the draft would be revised.

**Evidence:** [Arjomand, Constitution of the Islamic Republic, section on struggles over the draft constitution](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/constitution-of-the-islamic-republic/). The Arjomand account is sufficient for the correction; no precise page quotation is asserted here.

**Disposition:** Single targeted factual correction; no structural change or further general expansion is warranted on this finding. The current chapter file was not modified during preparation of this audit.

The debate uses these works as overlapping scholarly approaches rather than invented direct quotations. The earlier editorial review checked the broad attributions; this audit does not claim to have read each monograph in full or verified every passage against a page-specific citation.

The primary source window explicitly labels its constitutional text as paraphrase. Its interpretation should be read as the chapter's explanatory synthesis rather than a quotation from the constitution. The study appropriately distinguishes the original 1979 constitution from the 1989 amended text. The reading list supplies a starting point, not an exhaustive critical bibliography or a claim that every narrative sentence is supported by an individual citation.

Casualty estimates, referendum conditions, responsibility for bombings, and political-prisoner testimony require careful treatment. The manuscript's attribution of estimates and acknowledgment of evidence limits are appropriate. No assertion of exact totals or complete archival coverage is made by this audit.
