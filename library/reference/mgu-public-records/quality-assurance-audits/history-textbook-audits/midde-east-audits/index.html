Middle Eastern History Audits

Chapter-level assessment and publication review records for Malone Global University's Middle Eastern History textbook series.

Collection Overview

This register links only to Middle Eastern History audit records identified in the supplied institutional directory inventory. Records are arranged by chapter; chapters with no recorded audit are omitted. Where multiple audits exist for a chapter, each is listed.

Inventory scope: 77 chapters (Chapters 40–116); 82 audit records listed in the supplied directory inventory. The links retain the existing .md filenames; they are not links to converted PDF editions. Final audit labels reproduce document filename descriptions and do not independently establish expert peer review.
