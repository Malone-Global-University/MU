## 4. Factual cross-check and targeted decisions

The principal dynastic sequence was checked against *Encyclopaedia Iranica* and UNESCO's Central Asia volume. Tahirid hereditary authority starts in 821; Saffarid seizure of Nishapur is 873; Ya'qub's defeat near Iraq is 876; Isma'il's principal reign is 892–907; his defeat of Amr ibn al-Layth in 900 shifted Khurasan; the Samanids lost Bukhara in 999. Alptegin's Ghazna authority and death are placed in 962 and 963, respectively; Sebuktegin's rule runs 977–997. *Mafātīh al-ʿulum* is dated cautiously **c. 976–991**, not dogmatically 976. The late *Tarikh-i Sistan* is described as retrospective; Narshakhi's Arabic original is distinguished from later Persian transmission. Rudaki's Mulyan anecdote and Ya'qub's vernacular poetry episode are both expressly marked as later traditions, not authenticated eyewitness conversations. Bulliet's name-based conversion model is described as inferential, **not** a direct demographic census. Qarakhanid conversion is not assigned a single incontrovertible date, and the Talas paper-transfer anecdote is not repeated as historical fact.
