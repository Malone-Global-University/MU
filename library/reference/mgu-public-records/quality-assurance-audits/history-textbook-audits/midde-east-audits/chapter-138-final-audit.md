## Historical corrections and substantive expansion

1. **1926 constitutional structure:** The original Constitution established a Senate and Chamber of Deputies; the Senate was abolished in 1927, preceding the unicameral parliament of independence. The 1943 revision and 6:5 Christian–Muslim parliamentary allocation are distinguished from the earlier constitutional and mandate decisions.
2. **UNOGIL's 1958 findings:** The account accurately distinguishes the observation group's failure to substantiate major infiltration, its limited frontier access, and competing government and intelligence assessments.
3. **Palestinian legal status:** Refugee employment, movement and property ownership are described within the 1948–1975 legal context; the special 2001 property restrictions are not projected backward.
4. **UNRWA definition:** Eligibility criteria based on pre-1948 residence and loss of home and livelihood are distinguished from registration and the later administration of services; the changing nature of the agency's early definitions is acknowledged.
5. **Musa al-Sadr:** Corrected the false claim that he became vice president of the Higher Islamic Shia Council in 1969; he was elected council president.
6. **Ain al-Rummaneh geography:** Furn al-Shubbak is correctly identified as a predominantly Christian area rather than a Shia or Palestinian neighborhood; nearby Chiyah and Ghobeiry are geographically distinguished.
7. **April 13, 1975:** Competing casualty totals remain attributed to the source that reported them; U.S. Embassy cables **1975BEIRUT04749** and **1975BEIRUT04794** supplement press accounts. Unconfirmed attributions, participant identity and memoir claims remain marked as uncertain.
8. **Expansion:** Sections on Lebanon's unequal geography, Chehabist development, Palestinian refugees, southern displacement, armed sovereignty, the Lebanese National Movement, Shia representation, workers and women's activism, April 1975, and the first year of fighting have all been expanded with direct explanatory prose. The 1975 chronological boundary remains intact.
9. **Bibliography:** The ten existing scholarly monographs are preserved; eight documentary verification references have been added as direct web links. The main Primary Source Windows include explicit archival or source-identity notes and do not fabricate quotations.
