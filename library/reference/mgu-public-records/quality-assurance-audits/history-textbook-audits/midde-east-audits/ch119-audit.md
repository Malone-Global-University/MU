## 3. Historical corrections and substantive refinements

1. **1921 coup:** Clarifies roles of Reza Khan and Sayyed Zia, particular British facilitators Ironside, Smyth and Smart, Norman's later support, and the lack of evidence that the Foreign Office directed the operation. Sources: Stephanie Cronin and Niloofar Shambayati, Encyclopaedia Iranica.
2. **Dynastic chronology:** Preserves October 31, 1925 Majles deposition, December Constituent Assembly amendments, and April 1926 coronation as distinct steps.
3. **Military and provincial reform:** Explains June 1925 conscription, 1937–1938 adoption of ten ostans, the effects of campaigns in Khuzestan and other regions, and the unequal consequences of forced settlement on pastoral households.
4. **Judicial reform:** Differentiates Davar's 1927 judicial reorganization and staged Civil Code enactment from 1928; explicitly explains the continuing Islamic legal influences and legal accountability problem.
5. **Banking:** Separates Bank Melli's 1927 authorization, 1928 opening, acquisition of note issue, and 15-to-89 branch expansion through 1940. Uses Encyclopaedia Iranica banking history.
6. **Railway:** Uses UNESCO's 1,394-km length and 1927–1938 dates; examines consumer taxation, route, labor, imported technical expertise, and strategic functions.
7. **Petroleum:** Distinguishes November 27, 1932 cancellation, April 1933 agreement, May 1933 ratification, concession-area reduction, per-ton payments and designated distribution provisions, and extension of the concession to 1993. Does not use Anglo-Iranian Oil Company for the pre-1935 company name.
8. **Education:** Distinguishes University of Tehran's 1934 legislation from February 1935 inauguration. The quantitative study identifies two school-enrollment series, one bank series, and the railway measure with limitations.
9. **Naming and language:** Corrects simplistic references to 'renaming Persia': Iran had long been a domestic name; 1935 changed official foreign usage. Explains multilingual experiences rather than assuming Persian and Iranian identities are coextensive.
10. **Social policy:** Distinguishes July 1935 Goharshad attacks from compulsory unveiling publicly inaugurated in January 1936, with attributed uncertainty about casualties and variation in women's experiences.
11. **Political repression:** Develops the personal rule of Reza Shah, Teymourtash, the Fifty-Three, the narrowing of independent Majles authority, and the concentration of property.
12. **Foreign policy and abdication:** Correctly dates Saadabad Pact July 8, 1937, invasion August 25, 1941, and abdication September 16, 1941. The Anglo-Soviet military campaign remains a Chapter 123 topic.

Historical checking was grounded in the scholarly and documentary links listed inside the final HTML, including the *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, UNESCO and U.S. Office of the Historian. Link targets represent source-finding or context evidence, not a claim to independently collated original-language treaty texts.

## 5. Explicit publication limitations

- All twelve primary-source feature boxes are **editorial source studies/paraphrases**, not certified original-language or facsimile-collated primary quotations. The chapter consistently labels them as such. Linked verification references frequently point to scholarly context rather than full primary document texts; future documentary facsimile editions would need original document collation.
- The three Map Studies are **textual atlas-guided analyses**, not embedded original cartographic plates. The Quantitative Study uses attributed published series and approximate endpoints; its percentage changes inherit the rounding of those inputs.
- Exact byte-for-byte equivalence to the independent Chapter 2 model HTML/CSS was not checked because the model file was not independently supplied for comparison; styling and core components were retained from the uploaded Chapter 119 shell.
- Browser testing was performed on local HTML content rather than the live site. The Chapter 118 previous-navigation filename was confirmed locally; Chapter 120 is explicitly marked pending and is not a speculative active link. External URL availability at deployment and server-side wrapper integration have not been certified.
- No human specialist peer review, archival transcription verification, or live deployment was conducted. The approval is for **first-edition scholarly textual HTML**, within these disclosed limitations, not an assertion of final source-critical completeness.
