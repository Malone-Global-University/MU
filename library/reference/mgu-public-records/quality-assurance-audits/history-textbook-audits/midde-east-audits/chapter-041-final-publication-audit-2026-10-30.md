## Seven targeted final textual corrections

1. **§41.15 — Iraqi dīwān and the secretaries:** Replaced wording suggesting that Ṣāliḥ ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān simply displaced Zādān Farrukh with the transmitted account that Zādān Farrukh was killed amid Ibn al-Ashʿath's revolt, after which Ṣāliḥ was entrusted with conversion of the register. The narrative is explicitly attributed to al-Balādhurī rather than treated as independently documented personnel history. Source: al-Balādhurī, *Futūḥ al-buldān*, section 'Naql Dīwān al-Fārisiyya'; accessible Arabic text https://books.islam-db.com/book/فتوح_البلدان/291.
2. **§41.23 — Egyptian fiscal records:** Clarified that reported censuses and surveys occurred from the late seventh into the early eighth century, and dated fiscal director Usāma ibn Zayd al-Tanūkhī's tenure cautiously to c.714–717. Sources: Wadād al-Qāḍī, 'Population census and land surveys under the Umayyads (41–132/661–750),' *Der Islam* 83 (2008), 341–416; Marie Legendre, 'Aspects of Umayyad Administration,' in *The Umayyad World* (2020).
3. **§41.26 — gold and silver coin weights:** Distinguished reformed dinar c.4.25 g from heavier Byzantine solidus c.4.38–4.5 g, and observed that Umayyad dirhams commonly weigh c.2.8–2.85 g, with variation and later normative weight standards not automatically governing every early issue. Sources: Luke Treadwell, 'ʿAbd al-Malik's Coinage Reforms: the Role of the Damascus Mint,' *Revue numismatique* (2009), https://www.persee.fr/doc/numi_0484-8942_2009_num_6_165_2879; 'Coins and Coinage' and 'Dirham ii' in *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/coins-and-coinage/ and https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/dirham/dirham-ii-in-the-islamic-period/.
4. **Primary Source Window I — papyrus description:** Corrected over-general wording that might imply all selected Aphrodito letters are Greek with Arabic protocols; specified that the letters are principally Greek within a multilingual archive. Source: British Library collections and research on Qurra ibn Sharīk's documents; https://searcharchives.bl.uk/. The cited examples P.Lond. IV 1380, 1391, 1401 and 1464 were retained as distinct documents, not a composite quotation.
5. **Primary Source Window IV — report locator:** Added the *Muwaṭṭaʾ* Book of Zakāt, jizya section, and an English numbering example 17.24.49, noting differences across editions. Alternate published numbering includes report 760 or 692. Source: https://hadithunlocked.com/sharh/malik-zarqani/malik%3A17-49 and https://en.tohed.com/hadith/muwatta-yahya/692/.
6. **Primary Source Window IV — transmission status:** Corrected wording implying a documented chain through jurists: Mālik states that the news reached him (*balāgh*) without an uninterrupted chain to ʿUmar II. Kept distinction between retrospective legal evidence and evidence of actually implemented policy.
7. **Primary Source Window II — numismatic specimen:** Added one verified, exact museum specimen, Khalili Collections **AV 1071**, reformed gold dinar AH 77, 4.24 g, unmarked mint probably Damascus, and expanded Walker's bibliographic title. Source: https://khalilicollections.org/collections/islamic-art/khalili-collection-islamic-art-gold-dinar-av1071/.

- The John-of-Damascus/Sarjūn family association remains cautious; no invented genealogy.
