## 3. Direct revisions made (19 surgical changes)

### Historical corrections

**H1 — §91.3:** `Khusraw Pasha (1801–1803)` → **(1802–1803)**. His participation in the 1801 campaign is not the same as the formal governorship beginning in 1802.

**H2 — §91.3:** Replaced the erroneous death-by-plague claim for **Ali Pasha al-Jazairli** with an account of his being killed in **January 1804** in a confrontation involving Mamluk escorts. The text does not falsely claim certainty about the precise motive or instigator.

**H3 — §91.3:** Replaced “murdered by rival Albanians” for **Tahir Pasha** with his killing by **two Janissary officers in May 1803** and the subsequent advancement of Muhammad Ali among the Albanian troops.

**H4 — Persons/Places:** Clarified the three successive sultans' distinct roles: **Selim III** confirmed Muhammad Ali's appointment, **Mahmud II** fought the Syrian expansion, and **Abdülmecid** issued the 1841 settlement.

**H5 — §91.19 and 1841 source study:** Clarified that the final firman restricted succession to **Muhammad Ali's direct male line**, with preference to the **senior eligible male**, and required renewed Ottoman appointment. This is not the same as an automatically passing father-to-eldest-son primogeniture rule. The distinction matters because the succession rule was subsequently changed in 1866.
