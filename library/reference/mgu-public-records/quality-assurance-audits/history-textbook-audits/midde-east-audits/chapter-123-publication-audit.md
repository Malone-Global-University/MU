## 3. Revisions actually made

1. **Corrected the February 4, 1942 Abdeen opening.** Removed the false impression that Axis forces were within a few days' march of Suez at the moment the British compelled King Farouk to appoint a Wafd ministry. Distinguished the early-February political emergency from the later summer 1942 Axis thrust toward El Alamein.
2. **Reconstructed the Persian Corridor quantitative study.** Added annual totals verified against Motter, Appendix A, Table 4:

   | Period | USSR deliveries (long tons) |
   |---|---:|
   | 1942 | 353,114 |
   | 1943 | 1,793,932 |
   | 1944 | 2,555,172 |
   | January–May 1945 | 447,158 |
   | **Total** | **5,149,376** |

   Exact high-volume month is **July 1944: 282,097 long tons**. Annual figures sum precisely to **5,149,376**.
3. **Corrected the distinct 4,159,117-ton metric.** Motter's Appendix A, Tables 1–2 classify this as **Western Hemisphere-origin Russian-aid cargo shipped toward Persian Gulf ports from November 1941 through May 1945**—not exclusively U.S. Lend-Lease deliveries. Table 2 divides it into **4,073,978 long tons designated U.S. shipments** and **85,139 long tons British/Canadian shipments**. Kept it distinct from USSR receiving-point deliveries and the approximately 7.9 million long tons of wider Gulf cargo unloaded.
4. **Expanded historical narrative in §§123.1, 123.9, 123.15 and 123.20.** Strengthened distinctions among mandates, independent treaty states, occupation, transport, wartime civilian supply, women and labor, Palestinian economic experiences, Iran's food problem, postwar sovereignty, and divergent regional outcomes. Preserved regional agency and the neighboring Chapter 124 boundary.
5. **Added per-window documentary access.** All 13 source windows contain labeled source links and notes that distinguish exact item texts from corroborating records, record collections, and syntheses. Added a separate documentary methods/source guide.
6. **Repaired direct chapter navigation on mobile.** Contents menu has links to all 20 historical sections as well as reference, studies and review anchors; link targets have been verified.
7. **Added adjacent-chapter navigation with the authoritative Chapter 122 and Chapter 124 titles.** Destination URLs could not be established with confidence from the available materials. The labels are therefore intentionally non-clickable pending publication assignment rather than fabricated or dead links.
8. **Added accessibility and mobile guards.** Source links are labeled; table wrappers have focus handling and horizontal overflow; navigation uses explicit accessible labels.
