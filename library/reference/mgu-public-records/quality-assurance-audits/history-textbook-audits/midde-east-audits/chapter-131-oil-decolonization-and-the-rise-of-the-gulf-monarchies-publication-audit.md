## Historical corrections made to the supplied initial draft

1. **Qatar's Dukhan petroleum chronology.** Distinguished 1939 exploratory drilling from the successful strike recorded by QatarEnergy in 1940, and both from initial 1949 exports. The source window now points to the original May–June 1935 Qatar concession documents, and the narrative notes why different sources date the discovery differently.
2. **The 1971 islands dispute.** Distinguishes the negotiated Sharjah–Iran memorandum concerning Abu Musa from Iran's unilateral seizure of Greater and Lesser Tunb and the accompanying armed confrontation. The U.S. diplomatic memorandum of December 1, 1971 and the UN-documented island arrangements provide comparison sources.
3. **British protection and local sovereignty.** Differentiates Kuwait's 1899 arrangements, the Trucial Coast's exclusive treaties, Bahrain, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and independent Oman; avoids treating all polities as colonies with identical legal status.
4. **Aramco 1950 arrangements.** Explains how tax/profit-sharing mechanisms increased Saudi revenue without transferring half the company's equity.
5. **Bahrain's 1970 political settlement.** Correctly identifies a United Nations good-offices consultation, **not** a popular referendum; retains the full 1971 independence chronology.
6. **Omani oil and political chronology.** Distinguishes exploratory discoveries and the first 1967 oil shipments from the 1970 succession, Jebel Akhdar's earlier history, and the contemporaneous Dhofar rebellion.
7. **UAE federation dates.** Correctly identifies six founding emirates on December 2, 1971 and Ras Al Khaimah joining February 10, 1972. Original federal constitutional material is now included in a source study.
8. **Anachronism controls.** Does not project the 1973 embargo or the later fully developed welfare and sponsorship systems backward onto the 1930s–1960s.
