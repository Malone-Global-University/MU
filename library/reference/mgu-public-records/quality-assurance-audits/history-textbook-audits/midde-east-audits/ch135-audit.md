## 3. Historical accuracy and documentation

- **May 1967:** Distinguishes the May 16 military communication, May 18 formal governmental demand, May 19 commencement of UNEF's operational withdrawal, and the later physical departure of personnel. Source: UN Secretary-General's UNEF withdrawal report and documentation (A/6730 series).
- **June 5:** States directly that Israel launched the opening attacks; separates the established operational sequence from disputed interpretations of leaders' intentions. Primary-source window uses **FRUS 1964–1968, Volume XIX, Document 158**, the June 5 Tel Aviv diplomatic telegram conveying Prime Minister Eshkol's account and its U.S. annotations.
- **June 8 USS Liberty:** Records **34 Americans killed**, and explicitly distinguishes official U.S. Navy sources reporting approximately **170–171 wounded**; discusses the identification-error explanation and evidence/interpretation dispute without falsely declaring the matter settled.
- **Displacement:** Distinguishes 1948 refugees from those displaced in 1967 and avoids presenting UNRWA's contemporaneous **more than 300,000** report (including approximately **120,000 registered refugees**) as a retrospective final census or an explanation of all individual movements.
- **Golan:** Documents wartime flight and postwar displacement and the survival of specific Druze communities, without uncritically treating all departures as voluntary.
- **East Jerusalem:** Separates capture June 7, legal and municipal measures June 27–28, and UN General Assembly Resolution 2253 (ES-V) July 4. Distinguishes Israeli terminology from the international legal position.
- **Occupation:** Differentiates effective military control, annexation claims, and recognized sovereignty, and cites the 1907 Hague Regulations, 1949 Fourth Geneva Convention, and ICRC interpretation.
- **Resolution 242:** Uses the official UN text, explicitly mentions multilingual wording and negotiation history, and rejects any claim that a single English definite-article distinction resolves the legal debate.
- **Settlements:** Describes the September 1967 Kfar Etzion reestablishment and the Allon Plan as an influential proposal, not a formally adopted comprehensive policy.
- **Boundary discipline:** Limits later Palestinian organization developments to Chapter 136 and the War of Attrition / 1973 war to Chapters 139–140.

**Primary-source locators** are embedded as live external links inside each of the four source windows and bibliography; all quotations in those windows are explicitly paraphrases rather than invented verbatim extracts.
