## Changes made

1. **Hebron after 1929:** corrected the false implication that Jewish residence ceased permanently after the massacre. The text now distinguishes the evacuation of survivors, the return of some Jewish families in 1931, and evacuation in 1936; the latter is treated briefly to preserve the principal chronological cutoff of 1935.
2. **Landlessness inquiry:** replaced a vague description of the Lewis French inquiry with the precise 1932 administrative definition and the recorded application outcomes (3,188 received, 542 accepted, 2,441 rejected, 205 pending), distinguished from the 1933 update. The chapter explicitly explains why accepted claims do not count every landless Palestinian Arab family.
3. **Landownership:** replaced the undated early-1930s approximation with a clearly identified 1932 ownership snapshot (~1,058,500 dunams) and a separate April 1935 measure (1,238,896 dunams), distinguishing ownership from annual purchases or cultivated area.
4. **Quantitative interpretation:** strengthened the discussion of full 1922/1931 census enumerations, Bedouin enumeration, military-personnel adjustments, year-end 1935 estimates, midyear 1935 estimates, and registered immigration flows. The original census arithmetic is retained.
5. **Narrative expansion:** selectively enriched §§120.8, 120.11–120.14, 120.16 and 120.20, adding historical and methodological substance without changing the approved chronology, without rewriting complete sections, and without padding.
6. **Source traceability:** connected all twelve primary-source study windows with titled and dated documentary references and added a separate Documentary Source Register; all study paraphrases remain explicitly identified, not presented as invented verbatim quotations.
7. **Technical navigation:** provided a functional keyboard-accessible native HTML `<details>` mobile chapter contents control, tested link-follow and auto-close; retained the desktop sidebar; maintained scrollable narrow-screen tables; added a previous-chapter link and a deliberately inactive next-chapter label to avoid manufacturing a live URL for unpublished Chapter 121.

## Disclosed limitations and follow-up conditions

- Source Windows 2–12 are labeled editorial paraphrases, descriptions, or multi-record syntheses; they are not certified verbatim archival transcriptions. The linked materials supply traceable evidence, but thematic windows 4–6 and 9–10 are not claimed to derive from one document each.
- The three historical map studies remain atlas-based exercises without embedded cartographic images. This is an acceptable textual-publication choice.
- Historical population figures may vary depending on whether the source gives full census, midyear, year-end, or adjusted estimates; labels must not be dropped during future edits.
- The external Chapter 121 navigation is intentionally inactive until its exact deployed filename and URL are verified.
- Source websites and the live MGU deployment were not exhaustively crawled. A production-domain smoke test is recommended on publication.
- Exact Chapter 2 CSS byte-for-byte parity was not separately asserted; the original supplied Chapter 120 styling was retained.
