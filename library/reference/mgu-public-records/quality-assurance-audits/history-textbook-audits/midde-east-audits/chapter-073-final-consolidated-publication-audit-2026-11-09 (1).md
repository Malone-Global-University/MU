## 3. Historical corrections verified and incorporated

### 3.1 Ottoman *akçe* debasement

- **Original:** the major silver-coin debasement stated categorically as **1584** in the chronology and §73.19, with associated review and timeline text.
- **Final:** **1585–1586** in all affected locations. The inflation discussion now recognizes wider fiscal, monetary, demographic, and market pressures rather than equating debasement itself with the entire price change.
- **Verification:** Şevket Pamuk, *A Monetary History of the Ottoman Empire* (Cambridge, 2000), especially chapter 8, 'The debasement of 1585–86: a turning point?'; contemporary specialist analysis also identifies 1585.
- Sources: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/financial-history-review/article/currency-transition-the-use-of-foreign-coins-and-dollarization-in-seventeenthcentury-ottoman-istanbul/138E92B63BACFAD6A80FD1BE87AE62CE ; https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/monetary-history-of-the-ottoman-empire/ (publisher catalog root; chapter title in print book).

### 3.2 *Avarız* periodization

- **Original:** states that extraordinary *avarız* levies became regular cash taxes as a direct late-sixteenth-century consequence of firearms expansion.
- **Final:** records intermittent sixteenth-century cash collections, with much more systematic regularization particularly in the **seventeenth century** and variation among provinces.
- Preview and final Key Terms definitions now preserve this chronology.
- Source: *Collection of Avâriz and Nüzul Levies in the Ottoman Empire: A Case Study of the Province of Karaman, 1620–1700*, https://www.belleten.gov.tr/eng/full-text/3488/eng .

### 3.3 The Süleymaniye mosque and its wider complex

- **Original:** several references implied the whole Süleymaniye *külliye* was finished in 1557.
- **Final:** clearly distinguishes the **mosque completed in 1557** from associated works continuing in **1559 and beyond**; the separate original-language publication title of Barkan's construction accounts (1550–1557) is preserved without changing its bibliographic identity.
- Source: https://www.islam.museum/en/building/suleymaniye-mosque/ ; specialized construction chronology published by Istanbul University/İSAM.

### 3.4 Chronology scope

- One Chapter 72 campaign-overlap entry (Belgrade, 1521) was removed from the **preview table** to produce 20 rows; the geographic argument and its narrative references were retained. This does not delete Belgrade from the chapter's regional history.

**Important disclosed limitation:** These four windows are rigorous teaching guides anchored to real primary-source editions, not actual manuscript excerpt studies.
