## 3. Substantive factual corrections and sourcing

**Elections and vote-to-seat disproportionality.** Quantitative Study 1, the relevant narrative sections and the 1950 primary-source window use the *same* national YSK/TÜİK series: 1946 DP 61 seats and CHP 397 (unreliable popular-vote counts); 1950 DP 55.2% and 416 of 487 seats, CHP 39.6% and 69 seats; 1954 DP 58.4% and 503 of 541 seats; 1957 DP 48.6% and 424 of 610 seats. The original draft's 408-seat figure for 1950 and its imprecise 1946 and 1957 descriptions were replaced. The YSK/TÜİK historical notes distinguish candidate-average vote counts in the 1950–1957 list-majority system from later vote series. Official source: https://www.ysk.gov.tr/doc/dosyalar/1923-2011-MVSecimleri-Tuik.pdf

**Agriculture and modernization.** Quantitative Study 2 distinguishes tractor numbers (about 1,756 in 1948 and about 44,144 in 1957) from growth in cereals acreage (roughly 7.6 million hectares in 1947 to 13 million in 1955), while labeling the dates and definitions. These are historical series, not a universal index of rural living standards. Supporting research includes https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/record/197289/ and https://atam.gov.tr/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Turkiyenin-Nufus-Politikasi-1923-1980.pdf.

**August 1958 stabilization.** The differentiated effective rates were 9.00 lira per US dollar (selling rate) and 9.00/5.60/4.90 (buying categories), while the 2.80 parity remained a reference until exchange-rate unification. A World Bank account lists $357 million of extraordinary assistance ($215 million new resources) and approximately $424 million of rescheduled commercial arrears; a contemporary U.S. assessment describes about $359 million promised. The categories must not be added indiscriminately. World Bank: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/483311468118438166/pdf/multi0page.pdf and FRUS: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1958-60v10p2/d322.

**Korea and NATO.** Turkey's entry into NATO remains dated February 18, 1952. The U.N. Command historical account distinguishes the more than 4,500 troops in the initial brigade from cumulative wartime casualties: 741 killed in action, 2,068 wounded and 163 missing. Source: https://www.unc.mil/Organization/Contributors/Turkey/.

**The 1959 49'lar affair.** The text now explains that some reconstructions initially count fifty people arrested before the group became conventionally known as the Forty-Niners. Source comparison: https://www.kurdolojiakademi.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Turkiyede-Kurt-Etno-Bolgesel-Hareketi.pdf.

**The April–May 1960 crisis.** The political crisis is dated through April 18 Investigation Commission, April 28 student unrest, May 3 Cemal Gürsel letter, May 21 cadet demonstration and May 27 coup. The 1960 letter is treated with source criticism because later published versions were censored. See https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/cttad/article/771886.

**1961 constitutional referendum.** The text adopts the Turkish Parliament's general historical summary of 61.17% approval on July 9, 1961; some institutional pages cite alternative rounded values. Source: https://tbmm.gov.tr/history. The referendum is identified as occurring under the military-supervised transition.

**Istanbul, September 6–7, 1955.** The text differentiates documented organization, official accountability, survivor experience and longer-term migration effects, without presenting every allegation about individual planners as proven. Scholarly reference: https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/inciss/article/813416.
