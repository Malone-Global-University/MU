## Historical corrections implemented

1. **Mossadegh premiership:** April 28, 1951 parliamentary nomination versus April 29 royal appointment, with chronology and summary synchronized. Reference: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Coup d'État of 1953 (`https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/coup-detat-1953/`).
2. **Kashani:** August 1952 speakership, deteriorating National Front alignment, and the election of Abdollah Moazzami on July 1, 1953. Refer to Iranica's Kashani entry and related declassified accounts.
3. **1954 agreement:** August 5 agreement in principle; September 19 signed principal agreement; October 28 Iranian Senate approval; October 29 royal assent; distinguish an October 30 U.S. intelligence appendix from the principal signature. Refer to *Iranica*, Oil Agreements in Iran, and FRUS document 375.
4. **Compensation versus participation:** UK Parliament documented £25 million to AIOC from Iran over ten years starting in 1957 and £214 million from other companies for participation interests. Different legal transactions, not one treasury payment. Source: *Hansard*, 1–2 November 1954.
5. **IRICON:** In April 1955 five U.S. majors surrendered one point each from their initial 40% aggregate share, producing 35% for those majors plus 5% for independent U.S. participants. Consortium percentages are not Iranian state ownership. Source: *Iranica* and FRUS 1955–1957 document 309.
6. **Coup casualties:** Replaced an unsupported unqualified range with attributed British contemporary estimate (over 50 dead, approximately 300 wounded), noting differing scope and no verified comprehensive count. Source: National Security Archive, *The Battle for Iran*.
7. **Economy:** Rebuilt study 2 using dated figures and explicit disagreements between a 1953 U.S. State Department fiscal-risk account and a 1953 Federal Reserve study showing external-account resilience. Retained uncertainty of different populations, national accounts, periods, and estimation methods. The Federal Reserve study is an internal historical research source and not an independent modern estimate.
8. **Third schematic map:** Added the missing SVG arrow marker definition and removed a potentially misleading unsupported precise northern-border-length label.
