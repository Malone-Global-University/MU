## Integrated changes

1. **Historical corrections integrated:** the 4/8/15 February 1971 sequence of Sadat's canal proposal, Jarring's aide-mémoire and Egypt's response is corrected in §139.15 and the chronology; §139.18 distinguishes Jordan's absence from the initial October attack from its later armored brigade deployment to Syria; §139.10 dates the public destruction of surveillance tapes to late May 1971. No unsupported precise Soviet withdrawal total has been imposed.
2. **Narrative expansion:** added 5,394 words of targeted new prose before net corrections; direct narrative now totals 14,296 words by the specified paragraph-only method. Expansion addresses post-1967 institutional reconstruction, wartime labor and displacement, international diplomacy, 1971 succession and repression, student and worker politics, Soviet personnel categories, the February/May 1973 Ismail–Kissinger meetings, limited-war planning and the 1974 economic opening.
3. **Source tracing:** four retained Primary Source Windows link directly to specific UN records, treaty text or FRUS documents. The bibliography includes a separate ten-entry documentary source register with stable document identifiers. Contemporary diplomatic records are described as sources reflecting the perceptions and interests of their authors, not unmediated access to Egyptian decision-making.
4. **Instructional apparatus:** all nine approved feature classes remain in place. The glossary now has 33 definitions; the chronology has 15 entries. The approved chapter 140/141/143 narrative boundaries have been retained.
5. **Technical fixes:** accessible HTML `<details>` mobile contents menu after the chapter header; keyboard-focus styling; responsive table overflow; duplicate doctype removed; no dependency on JavaScript for mobile contents. Existing desktop sidebar and active-link script are preserved.

## Disclosed limitations

- The supplied Chapter 2 reference HTML itself was not attached. The original manuscript's shell was retained, but exact byte-for-byte template conformity cannot be certified.
- The four primary-source feature boxes contain one short verified quotation from Resolution 242 and otherwise labeled paraphrases; a verbatim transcript of Sadat's May 1971 political declaration was not verified, and the material is not presented as a verbatim quotation.
- Historical memoirs may differ over strategic intentions, Soviet personnel totals and the internal decision-making of Egyptian and Israeli officials. The revised chapter distinguishes retrospective interpretation from the verified primary-document chronology. A full archival/page-specific scholarly edition would require further source collation; such an edition is beyond the current first-edition textual HTML production scope.
- Browser checks were completed by rendering HTML content directly in headless Chromium at desktop and mobile dimensions. Live web server deployment, external URL reachability from the published host, and cross-browser visual comparison have not been tested.

## Publication decision

No further padding is needed. The chapter meets its 13,000–15,000 direct-narrative target, preserves all approved pedagogical components and passes the final local structural and browser validations. The **HTML textual edition may be locked for first-edition publication**. Its deployment and the disclosed scholarly limitations above are separate operational considerations.
