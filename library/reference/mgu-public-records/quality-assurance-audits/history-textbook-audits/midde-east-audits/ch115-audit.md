## Historical corrections and final editorial decisions

1. **Census literacy rates:** Replaced incompatible rounded estimates with consistently calculated **all-age, read-and-write** proportions. In 1927, 1,111,496 / 13,629,488 = **8.16%**; in 1935, 2,475,748 / 16,157,450 = **15.32%**. Male percentages: **12.99% → 22.97%**; female: **3.67% → 7.94%**. The quantitative feature distinguishes the separate population totals, literacy-tabulation denominators and 1935 read-only count (42,130). Source: Nursal Kumaş, *9th International Atatürk Congress*, vol. II, pp. 1653–1656, reproducing the 1927 and 1935 census tables. https://atam.gov.tr/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/9.ULUSLARARASI-ATATURK-KONGRESI-CILT-II.pdf
2. **Constitutional sequence:** Corrected Primary Source Window 8: original 1924 Article 2 included Islam as the state religion; **10 April 1928** removed that provision; **5 February 1937** separately incorporated the Six Arrows, including secularism. Source: Turkish Constitutional Court, https://anayasa.gov.tr/tr/mevzuat/onceki-anayasalar/1924-anayasasi
3. **Lausanne minority guarantees:** Clarified the treaty's general protection of **non-Muslim minorities** (Articles 37–45) versus the Turkish government's narrower administration of recognized communal institutions. Source: Turkish Ministry of Foreign Affairs, https://www.mfa.gov.tr/lausanne-peace-treaty-part-i_-political-clauses.en.mfa
4. **Thrace, 1934:** Incorporated Hatice Bayraktar's findings on the role of government representative İbrahim Tali Öngören; did not falsely infer a single documented order for all attacks. Source: Bayraktar, *Patterns of Prejudice* 40, no. 2 (2006): 95–111, https://doi.org/10.1080/00313220600634238
5. **Dersim, 1937–1938:** Added attributed discussion of the inspectorate-report figures **13,160 killed** and **11,818 deported**, without presenting these figures as an unquestioned exhaustive death toll or collapsing disputed interpretations into one mandated label. Correctly attributed the Sciences Po research article to **Hans-Lukas Kieser**. Source: https://www.sciencespo.fr/mass-violence-war-massacre-resistance/en/document/dersim-massacre-1937-1938.html
6. **Women's representation:** Preserved the sourced distinction of **17 women elected in February 1935** and **18 serving during the parliamentary term** after Hatice Özgener's January 1936 by-election. Source: https://ataturkansiklopedisi.gov.tr/detay/519/Kad%C4%B1n-Temsiliyeti-ve-1935-Se%C3%A7imleri
7. **Student oath:** Corrected an implication that the student oath began only in later decades: the first form was introduced by Education Minister Reşit Galip in **1933**; subsequent forms and uses are distinguished.
8. **Montreux:** Added the distinction between **20 July 1936** signature, **15 August** provisional application, and **9 November** definitive entry into force, together with the commercial-vessel / warship and Black Sea littoral distinctions. Source: United Nations Treaty Collection registration no. 4015, https://treaties.un.org/pages/showDetails.aspx?objid=0800000280166981
9. **Selective depth improvements:** Reworked §§115.6 (courts, civil registry, limits of equality), 115.9 (implementation and school policy), 115.14 (finance and distribution of development), 115.15 (rural households, cities and public health), and 115.18 (Straits and Hatay), together with source-focused additions to §§115.7, 115.12 and 115.16. The approved 20-section structure was maintained.
