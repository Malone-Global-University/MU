## 2. Editorial corrections applied

1. **Turkmenchay date:** Corrected *21 February 1828* to **22 February 1828 Gregorian / 10 February 1828 Julian** in the opening, §94.8, and the Turkmenchay source metadata. The 12-day calendar conversion is supported by contemporaneous dating conventions and a retrospective U.S. diplomatic document.
2. **Treaty indemnity:** Replaced the incorrect *ten million silver rubles* with **ten kurur of tumans (five million tumans), commonly rendered as twenty million Russian silver rubles**, clearly distinguishing the original unit from the converted denomination. Article VI identifies the requirement; subsequent payment adjustments are a separate historical question.
3. **Anglo-Persian War and Indian Rebellion:** Corrected §94.13 to establish that the **Treaty of Paris was signed on 4 March 1857**, **before** the Indian Rebellion began in May 1857. The later rebellion accelerated redeployment of the main British expeditionary force after the peace; it did not cause the peace treaty.
4. **Qajar royal reliefs:** Refined §94.2 to distinguish Rayy from Taq-e Bostan; clarified Mohammad Ali Mirza's role and avoided treating every Qajar relief as identically situated beside Sasanian monuments.
5. **Dar al-Fonun chronology:** Replaced an indefinite December 1851 reference with the supported **29 December 1851** opening date, following Amir Kabir's dismissal.
6. **Chronology ordering:** Moved the Second Treaty of Erzurum (1847) ahead of the 1848–1852 reform entries.
7. **Source transparency:** Made all four primary-source windows more traceable through named published documentary editions, source genres, and archive catalogues. Retained each editorial paraphrase label and did not introduce uncollated direct quotations.
8. **Added a selective documentary source register** with actual retrievable source records for Gulistan (1813), Turkmenchay (1828), Paris (1857), and the Tobacco Protest (1890–1892). Linked this register from the existing sidebar.
9. **Vocabulary completeness:** Preserved the existing 25 term pills and added **25 substantive, concise preview definitions**, adapted from the chapter's own concluding glossary.
10. **Responsive chronology:** Wrapped the two existing historical chronology tables in keyboard-focusable, horizontally scrollable regions, leaving the main document free of horizontal overflow at narrow viewport widths.
11. **Preservation:** All twenty main historical sections, their order, and five thematic divisions remain intact. Added only targeted CSS for definition cards and scroll wrappers; the inherited CSS rules and original script were retained.
