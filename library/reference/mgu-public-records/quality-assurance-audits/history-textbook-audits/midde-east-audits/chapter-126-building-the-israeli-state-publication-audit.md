## Factual and historical revisions

1. Replaced the incorrect claim that Israel had no functioning courts or tax services at independence with an account of inherited Mandate and Yishuv structures; distinguished provisional use and October 1948 formal flag adoption.
2. Corrected the January 1949 elected body to the **Constituent Assembly**, renamed the **First Knesset** on 16 February 1949.
3. Used the official immigration total **687,624 for 15 May 1948–1951** with a defined time window, distinct from net population growth.
4. Distinguished the Law of Return (1950) from the Nationality Law (1952) and the actual eligibility difficulties for some Palestinian residents.
5. Defined ma’abarot occupancy as a **dated early-1952 snapshot** (about 194 camps, over 270,000 residents), not a cumulative decade total.
6. Distinguished the **3 billion DM Israel commitment** and **450 million DM Claims Conference protocol** in the 1952 reparations agreement.
7. Distinguished the **1950 Absentees’ Property Law** and the **1953 Land Acquisition Law** and their separate legal mechanisms.
8. In the Kafr Qasim case, explained the Akevot archival convention of **47 killed in Kafr Qasim plus two in nearby villages**, while noting higher commemorative counts.
9. Distinguished formal termination of most military government on **1 December 1966** from continuing emergency powers or inequalities.
10. Addressed uncertainty around the Baghdad bombings, differentiated migration experiences across the region, and prevented the 1956 and 1967 war chapters from being preemptively retold.
