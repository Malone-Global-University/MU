## 3. Mandatory corrections completed

1. **§79.3: Distinct corps, not a single ghulam organization.** Corrected the misclassification of the *qurchi* royal guard as a development within the *ghulam* corps. The narrative now explicitly differentiates Qizilbash tribal military formations, the qurchis recruited principally from Qizilbash backgrounds and paid by the court, Caucasian-origin ghulam officers and cavalry, musketeers (*tufangchi*), and artillerymen (*tupchi*). Recruitment, pay, command, dependence and tactical functions are explained. Evidence: Masashi Haneda, “Army iii. Safavid Period,” *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/army-iii/.
2. **§79.7: Hormuz and Albuquerque, 1507/1515.** Corrected Claude's inaccurate attribution of the 1515 fortification to Albuquerque's successors. Now distinguishes Albuquerque's first assault and fortification attempt in 1507, his withdrawal in 1508, and his return and consolidated Portuguese occupation in 1515. Describes the later fortress development and Safavid-English cooperation in 1622. Evidence: “Portugal i. Relations with Persia in the Early Modern Age,” *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/portugal-i/.
3. **§79.16: VOC entered during Abbas's reign.** Corrected the misleading statement that the Dutch East India Company established itself only later in the century. Now dates Huybert Visnich's arrival in Isfahan and the Dutch commercial treaty to **1623** (the published study specifies 17 November 1623), distinguishing initial entry from later expansion. Evidence: “Dutch-Persian Relations,” *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/dutch-persian-relations/.
4. **Arakel bibliography:** Corrected Bournoutian's translated two-volume edition date to **2005–2006**; no invented page citation was added.
5. **British Library collections:** Distinguished **IOR/G/29/1/1**, whose catalog describes East India Company correspondence from **19 March 1621–25 November 1625**, from **IOR/G/29/1/2**, dated **2 November 1625–3 August 1697**, whose catalog explicitly includes translated royal farmans and trading privileges. The source-window description does not conflate the two collections. Catalogue https://searcharchives.bl.uk/?f%5Bparent_reference_ssi%5D%5B%5D=IOR%2FG%2F29%2F1&sort=date.

### Other checked chronology

- **Abbas's accession 1587/1588:** Original qualified treatment retained; proclamations, enthronement and effective consolidation need not be assigned an invented single day.
- **Bahrain approximately 1602:** Retained as qualified; published Iranica chronology gives 1601–1603.
- **Isfahan capital transfer 1597–1598:** Retained as the beginning of a process of relocation and building.
- **Si-o-se-pol bridge, early seventeenth century:** Retained, with period context; does not mistake the later Khaju Bridge under Abbas II for Abbas I's construction.
- **Royal silk-export monopoly:** Correctly identifies **1619** as the major proclamation after earlier Crown involvement, without equating an export monopoly with total control of all production.
- **1604 Armenian deportations:** Coercion, mortality and forced resettlement distinguished from the later commercial successes of New Julfa; total population estimates not falsely treated as exact counts.
- **Shah Mosque:** Incomplete at Abbas's death. The chapter's principal chronology ends with the succession of 1629; later decline belongs to Chapter 80.
