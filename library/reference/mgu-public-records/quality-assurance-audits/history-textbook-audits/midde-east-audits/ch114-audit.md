## Historical and documentary corrections completed

1. Separated the 16 March 1920 Allied takeover of Constantinople and later formal dissolution of the imperial parliament.
2. Distinguished the signing of Alexandropol from later operative frontier settlements through Moscow and Kars in 1921; the Alexandropol treaty was not ratified and did not enter into force.
3. Reworked the Smyrna account to distinguish documented civilian violence, the fire's chronology, eyewitness claims about ignition, later scholarly interpretation, and the lack of a reliably established single aggregate death count.
4. Corrected Lausanne's Aegean island arrangements: Greek sovereignty over named islands, Turkish sovereignty with special administration for Imbros and Tenedos, and Italian possession of the treaty-designated Dodecanese in 1923.
5. Clarified the Karaağaç exception to the Maritsa/Evros frontier and the Mosul question's deferral beyond 1923.
6. Separated the principal Treaty of Lausanne from the contemporaneous Straits Convention, commercial agreements, and the separate compulsory population-exchange convention.
7. Distinguished treaty signature (24 July 1923) from the treaty's entry into force (August 1924).
8. Explained the exchange convention's religious/legal definitions, its formal exemptions, the different Article 14 protections affecting Imbros and Tenedos, and its inclusion of some persons displaced before the January 1923 convention.
9. Integrated documented violence against Muslim communities from the 1921 inter-Allied inquiry as well as documented abuses affecting Greek Orthodox, Armenian and other civilians; avoided false equivalence.
10. Clarified the November 1922 abolition of the sultanate versus the March 1924 abolition of the caliphate.
