## 4. Historical corrections and source verification

1. **Esther Handali / Esperanza Malchi corrected.** §§84.9 and 84.19 now clearly distinguish Esther Handali, associated with Nurbanu Sultan and dead around 1588–1590, from Esperanza Malchi, associated with Safiye Sultan and killed amid an Ottoman military uprising in 1600. Scholarly support: Ottoman court-history dissertation, pp. 48 ff., https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file/send?accession=osu1278971259&disposition=inline .
2. **Armenian patriarchate foundation qualified.** §84.6 treats the traditional 1461 Hovakim foundation as a later institutional claim rather than a certainly documented founding charter. The title and scope of patriarchal authority developed gradually. Bibliographic discussion of the traditional account and criticism: https://history.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/dispersion_history_1-26.pdf .
3. **Sulaqa chronology clarified.** The preview now specifies the 1552 election, 1553 Roman recognition, and death in 1555; the prose distinguishes subsequent Syriac and Catholic-affiliated institutional lines. Murre-van den Berg's publisher catalog verifies the 2015 monograph: https://www.peeters-leuven.be/detail.php?id=4065 .
4. **Sabbatean survival clarified.** The 1666 conversion is described as disrupting and transforming the messianic movement, not annihilating every continuing Sabbatean community.
5. **Safavid Jewish persecution substantiated and qualified.** The 1656–1662 measures and Moḥammad Beg connection are documented in *Encyclopaedia Iranica* entries on Kashan and Bābāʾī ben Loṭf: https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/kashan-viii-religious-communities/ and https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/babai-ben-lotf/. The revised text distinguishes community testimony from independently collated individual proclamations.
6. **New Julfa forced migration and differentiation** are strengthened: deportation cannot be reinterpreted as voluntary merchant migration, and non-elite Armenian households must not be reduced to the privileged merchant elite. Arakʿel's *Book of History*, trans. Bournoutian, 2010, is catalogued by the publisher: https://www.mazdapublishers.com/book/arak-el-of-tabriz-book-of-history .
7. **Comparative depth improved:** §§84.9–84.10 now explain internal Jewish differences, taxes, learning, print, responsa, and court intermediaries; §§84.13–84.16 add regional distinctions among Muslim communities and processes of conversion; §§84.17–84.20 expand social encounter, mobility, religious boundaries, crisis, privilege, and comparison.

No invented eyewitness quotations, Ottoman decrees, manuscript shelfmarks, or primary-source translations have been added.
