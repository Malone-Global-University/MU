## Substantive revisions

1. Corrected Qasim–Nasser opposition over Kuwait in 1961 and clarified the Arab League replacement of British military forces.
2. Distinguished the Lebanese political crisis of 1958 from an inaccurately phrased “revolution in Lebanon.”
3. Corrected the 1959–1961 chronology grouping; clarified the difference between party formation in 1947 and its 1953 merger/name.
4. Strengthened discussion of U.S. contacts with Iraqi Baathists while not elevating disputed direct coup-direction allegations into established fact.
5. Clarified the September 1961 Syrian secession communiqués and the evolution from demands for change to separation.
6. Qualified disputed accounts of Assad's air-force decisions during the Jordanian crisis and separated November 1970 actions, public announcements, and later appointments.
7. Expanded social, constitutional, military, administrative, and comparative analysis throughout the chapter.
8. Added specific documentary locators to four source windows and expanded the verified bibliographic apparatus.
9. Repaired 320–390 px mobile horizontal overflow and supplied a mobile chapter-contents control while preserving the existing desktop sidebar.

## Limitations

The four source windows use explicitly labeled paraphrases rather than unverified purported quotations. Their documentary locators make verification possible, but not every disputed event has a definitive archival transcript. Bibliographic hyperlinks are contextual links, not assertions of free full-text availability. Geography Study activities use textual map prompts; the original template did not include standalone embedded maps. There was no independent side-by-side Chapter 2 template comparison.
