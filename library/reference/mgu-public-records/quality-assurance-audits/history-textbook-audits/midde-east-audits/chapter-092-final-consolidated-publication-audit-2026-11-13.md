## Integrated revision work

**Historical and interpretive precision**

1. Corrected the Bosnian autonomy uprising: began in 1831, continued into 1832; Ottoman victory near Sarajevo in June 1832. The old wording falsely implied complete suppression in 1831.
2. Clarified that Mahmud II died July 1, 1839, after the army's defeat at Nizip; the imperial fleet under Ahmed Fevzi Pasha defected **after** his death, during Abdülmecid's succession crisis.
3. Replaced categorical uncertainty about formal endorsement of the 1808 Sened-i İttifak with a more accurate distinction: the full archival text published by Ali Akyıldız in 1998 supports formal governmental endorsement; personal commitment, binding force, actual implementation and later constitutional significance remain questions for historical interpretation.
4. Clarified that the Translation Office emerged in stages beginning in 1821; the replacement of Greek translators continued into 1822.
5. Specified Midhat Pasha's Baghdad governorship as **1869–1872** in §§92.14 and 92.18.
6. Corrected imprecise characterization of Nizamiye courts as wholly secular and of the Council of State as a court supervisor. Explained overlapping court jurisdictions and the administrative/legislative role of the Council of State.
7. Clarified the role of local councils in early muhassıl tax collection.

## Disclosed limitations and editorial controls

1. The four source-study windows contain **editorial paraphrases / teaching analyses**, not verbatim manuscript-collated translations. Their bibliographical identifications were strengthened, but no claim of complete original-language facsimile comparison is made.
2. The two geography exercises are **text-guided**, not embedded historical atlases or map plates. Students need a suitable historical atlas for plotted exercises.
3. The draft/manuscript CSS and active-scroll script were preserved *exactly*, and this page was actually browser tested. However, separate independent byte-level comparison to the earliest original Chapter 1 design template was **not performed**.
4. No live-site deployment, production hosting, link monitoring, or accessibility screen-reader session was conducted. Structural keyboard-focusable table wrappers and internal-link validation passed, but do not substitute for a full WCAG audit.
5. Bibliographic verification was targeted and selective, not an exhaustive catalog or publication-history audit of every referenced work; historically disputed matters are qualified in text.
