## Accuracy corrections

1. **Syrian vs Lebanese refugee status:** Corrected source text: Syria’s Law No. 260 of July 1956 gave qualifying Palestinian residents broad employment and public-service rights, unlike more restrictive Lebanese arrangements. Reference: [EUAA legal-status research](https://www.euaa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/publications/2022-09/2022_09_COI_Report_Syria_%20Targeting%20of%20Individuals_EN.pdf) and [text of Syrian Law 260](https://syria-law.com/splaws/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-260-%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AE-10-7-1956/).
2. **Chronology of hijackings:** Corrected the claim that international hijackings began after 1970–1971. The PFLP had hijacked an El Al airliner in July 1968; its later 1970 aircraft seizures contributed to the Jordanian crisis. Reference: [study of PFLP external operations](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14747731.2026.2677986) and [U.S. court record on the 1970 hijackings](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp/368/1098/1803062/).
3. **Rejection Front:** Separated the PFLP’s September 26, 1974 withdrawal from the Executive Committee from participation in establishing the Rejection Front in October 1974. Reference: [PalQuest chronology](https://www.palquest.org/en/overallchronology-grid?nid=23332).
4. **Charters:** Compared 1964 Article 24 and the 1968 revised charter, rather than describing the earlier text only as rhetorically cautious. References: [1964 charter](https://www.mei.org.in/constitutions/plo-charter), [1968 charter](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-203580/).
5. **Black September:** Distinguished the 1970 fighting, the 1971 suppression of remaining fedayeen positions, and the separate later Black September Organization. Source windows identify [FRUS Document 248](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v24/d248) and [Document 275](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v24/d275).
6. **1974 international status:** Distinguished the [Rabat decision](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-194621/), [UN GA Resolution 3236](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/651005), and [Resolution 3237](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-180059/) from sovereign statehood or recognition of Israel.

- Four primary-source windows use explicitly labeled **paraphrases**, not fabricated direct quotations. The linked official documents allow verification of attribution and article references.
