## Consolidated factual improvements

- Corrected Operation Hiram chronology from misleading **24–31 October** to **28/29–31 October 1948**; separated Operation Yoav's initial **15–22 October** phase from later related operations.
- Clarified that the Arab interstate intervention involved several states with different military roles, including Iraq, which did not border Palestine, and more restricted Lebanese participation.
- Replaced the vague Altalena casualties with **16 Irgun and 3 IDF deaths**.
- Revised the 7 January 1949 RAF incident to refer to **five aircraft shot down**, without inferring five pilot deaths; added a source window citing the British parliamentary record.
- Qualified estimates of Palestinian refugees by **date, eligibility, geographic scope and registration method**.
- Treated the postwar territorial share as **approximately 77–78%**, explicitly referring to the territory west of the Jordan and explaining differences in rounding and denominators.
- Preserved uncertainty regarding the scale and available evidence for killings at **Deir Yassin, al-Dawayima, Safsaf, Saliha and Tantura**, distinguishing documented violence from debates over totals and interpretations.
- Added direct UN treaty and resolution links, contemporary correspondence, published Plan Dalet material, and peer-reviewed or scholarly interpretive evidence. Documentary windows do not invent quotations.
