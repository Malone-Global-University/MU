## 3. Documented substantive corrections in the final pass

**Mexico — Hñähñu women (§130.17).** The original manuscript mistakenly described the three women's release as a single 2009 event. The revised paragraph now distinguishes **Jacinta Francisco Marcial's September 15, 2009 release following prosecutorial withdrawal** from **Alberta Alcántara Juan and Teresa González Cornelio's release on April 28, 2010 after Mexico's Supreme Court intervened**, and separately records the **2017 federal acknowledgment and apology**. It does not imply identical legal dispositions. A paragraph of documentary references now accompanies the treatment.

**Canada — *R. v. Ipeelee* (§130.11).** The judgment is now described as including **Rothstein J.'s dissent in part**, with the more precise distinction between the outcome for Ipeelee and that for Ladue. The source window now links directly to the **published Supreme Court of Canada decision**, rather than merely to a docket page.

**Ontario — Ipperwash (§130.10).** The stable provincial overview is retained, and the inaccessible archival pointer in the submission is replaced with the Ontario government's contemporaneous **May 31, 2007 report-announcement** page. This corroborates the inquiry's institutional provenance without asserting that a potentially restricted archival URL is available to every browser.

**Chapter navigation.** The previous submission hid the desktop sidebar below 900 pixels without a replacement. A **keyboard-operable, native HTML mobile contents panel** now includes all twenty historical sections, front matter, sources, and review, closes when a section is selected, and maintains appropriate anchor visibility. Original desktop branding, CSS tokens, body typography, and features are retained.

These are **targeted corrections**, not a rewriting of the author's historical argument or a move of content to other chapters.

## 4. Principal historical and legal claims checked

### United States — federal, state and tribal criminal jurisdiction

- **1883 *Ex parte Crow Dog* and 1885 Major Crimes Act:** The chapter appropriately distinguishes the original intra-Indigenous jurisdictional dispute and the later Congressional assertion of serious-crimes jurisdiction.
- **Public Law 280 (1953), *Oliphant* (1978), *Duro* (1990), the 1991 federal statutory response, and the Tribal Law and Order Act (2010):** The discussion explains changing authority without claiming that every tribe faces identical jurisdictional arrangements.
- **VAWA reforms (2013 and 2022):** The chapter identifies limited special Tribal criminal jurisdiction and notes procedural rights and capacity requirements. Official background: [U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/tribal/2013-and-2022-reauthorizations-violence-against-women-act-vawa).
- ***McGirt v. Oklahoma* (2020):** The historical outcome is the Creek Reservation's continuing status for the relevant federal criminal-law purposes, **not** unrestricted tribal authority. Primary source: [U.S. Supreme Court opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/19pdf/18-9526_9okb.pdf).
- ***Oklahoma v. Castro-Huerta* (2022):** The chapter correctly distinguishes concurrent state prosecution of non-Indians committing crimes against Indians in Indian country from the *McGirt* holding.

### Canada — policing, sentencing, corrections

- **Ipperwash and Marshall inquiries:** The chapter correctly uses both to examine documented investigative, prosecutorial and police accountability failures, without treating inquiries as proof of successful implementation. Official [Ipperwash Inquiry report landing page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ipperwash-inquiry-report); [Ontario announcement, May 31, 2007](https://news.ontario.ca/en/release/85268/ontario-government-receives-final-report-of-ipperwash-inquiry).
- **Criminal Code §718.2(e), *R. v. Gladue* (1999) and *R. v. Ipeelee* (2012):** The distinction between mandatory consideration of systemic factors and an automatic sentencing discount remains accurate; the partial dissent is now explicit. Direct published [*Ipeelee* judgment, 2012 SCC 13](https://scc-csc.lexum.com/scc-csc/scc-csc/en/8000/1/document.do).
- **Corrections:** The manuscript differentiates under-federal-correctional-jurisdiction figures, prison populations, sentencing populations and different national denominators. The reported **28.6%** pertains to people under the Correctional Service of Canada's jurisdiction at the specified reporting period, not solely people in institutions. Source: [CSC 2026 briefing](https://www.canada.ca/en/correctional-service/corporate/transparency/proactive-disclosure/briefing-materials/secu-2026-05-28.html).
- **2025 Indigenous Justice Strategy:** Date, distinctions-based approach and institutional status are represented as policy, not proven outcomes. Source: [Department of Justice Canada, March 10, 2025](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-justice/news/2025/03/canadas-first-federal-indigenous-justice-strategy-to-address-systemic-discrimination-and-overrepresentation-in-the-canadian-justice-system.html).

### Mexico — constitutional structure and wrongful prosecution

- **Mexican constitutional Article 2 reform:** The chapter distinguishes Indigenous normative systems and their constitutional limits from U.S. tribal criminal jurisdiction and Canadian sentencing doctrine. Primary text: [September 30, 2024 decree](https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/5739986).
- **Jacinta, Alberta and Teresa:** Chronology and procedural distinctions corroborated by [Centro Prodh's detailed case history](https://centroprodh.org.mx/2013/11/20/tribunal-administrativo-discutira-reparacion-del-dano-en-favor-de-las-mujeres-indigenas-alberta-alcantara-y-teresa-gonzalez/), [Mexican Supreme Court April 28, 2010 communiqué](https://www.internet2.scjn.gob.mx/red2/comunicados/comunicado.asp?id=1825), and [OHCHR Mexico's contemporaneous statement](https://hchr.org.mx/comunicados/la-oacnudh-reconoce-la-importancia-del-fallo-de-la-suprema-corte-que-ordeno-la-inmediata-liberacion-de-las-indigenas-alberta-alcantara-y-teresa-gonzalez/). Mexican federal sources also recorded the 2017 apology.
