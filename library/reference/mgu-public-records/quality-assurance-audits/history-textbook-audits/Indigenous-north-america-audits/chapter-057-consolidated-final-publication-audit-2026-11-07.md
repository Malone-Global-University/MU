## 3. Historical and documentary corrections executed

1. **§57.18, Kahnawake (September 1760):** Removed the false assertion that written minutes of the September 14–15 Kahnawake meetings survive. Their proceedings are reconstructed through surviving related colonial evidence, later references, and Indigenous oral history. The late-August Swegatchy conversations, post-capitulation Kahnawake meetings, and the separate September 5 Wendat–Murray instrument are explicitly differentiated. Checked against [Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs Canada](https://rcaanc.gc.ca/eng/1360866174787/1544619566736).
2. **§57.18, Wendat–Murray document:** Included the instrument’s actual British **submission** terminology alongside protections for return to Lorette, religion, customs, and trade. The distinctions between source wording, Indigenous understandings, and subsequent treaty interpretation are maintained. See [official treaty transcription](https://www.cirnac.gc.ca/eng/1361456241366/1581293495454).
3. **§57.12 and reference preview, Cherokee settlement regions:** Replaced the three-region description with **Lower, Middle, Valley, and Overhill** regions, noting regional classification variation. Added a substantive geographic explanation of different town groups and decision-making.
4. **§§57.12 and 57.19, Cherokee peace:** Distinguished **September 1761 preliminary agreement** and **December ratification** rather than presenting December as the only negotiation date. Checked against [South Carolina Encyclopedia](https://www.scencyclopedia.org/sce/entries/cherokee-war-1759-1761/) and [Tennessee Division of Archaeology historical report](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/environment/archaeology/documents/researchseries/arch_rs17_fort_loudoun_2010.pdf).
5. **§57.5, Braddock:** Replaced the oversimplification of absent Indigenous recruitment with a distinction between actual attempts at recruitment and failures of alliance management. Cross-checked against David L. Preston, *Braddock's Defeat* (Oxford University Press, 2015), bibliographically identified at [Smithsonian Libraries](https://www.si.edu/object/siris_sil_1097049).
6. **§§57.10 and 57.18, Seven Nations of Canada:** Explained the variable historical enumeration and the differentiated agency of mission communities. Avoided treating the name as a permanent centralized polity.
7. **§57.15, Easton:** Distinguished Pennsylvania proprietary undertaking, colonial treaty negotiations, and British military assurances; improved treatment of representation, territorial description, and the later disputes. The [1758 published proceedings](https://treaties.okstate.edu/treaties/treaty-of-easton-1758) provide the principal documentary check.
8. **§57.19, regional peacemaking:** Expanded the interpretive difference between Cherokee peace and the Atlantic Northeast treaties; avoided presenting imperial victory as extinguishing Indigenous authority.
9. **§57.1 and §57.8:** Expanded indigenous council decision-making, military and household-scale history, food security, prisoner return, and the limitations of captivity narratives, without romanticizing captivity or flattening regional variations.
10. **Source windows and bibliography:** Revised the 1760 source window to distinguish surviving written instruments from inferred and retrospectively reported understandings; replaced an explicit prepublication placeholder with a register of 18 identified documentary and scholarly references.

**Historical judgment:** Major campaign chronologies and the Indigenous-centered analytic framework remain substantially intact. Source windows contain clearly labeled paraphrase and interpretation, **not** new fabricated verbatim quotes.

## 5. Editorial limits and scholarly disclosure

- **Archival collation:** The identified primary-source windows are historical explanations and editorial paraphrases; they are *not* presented as newly verified verbatim transcriptions from original manuscript pages. Particular French, British, and Indigenous accounts can preserve competing versions of events, such as Jumonville Glen or Fort William Henry.
- **Kahnawake documentation:** No surviving complete September 1760 council minutes have been claimed. Later interpretations are not substituted for contemporary documentary evidence without labeling.
- **Maps:** Geography and campaign map studies require an external historical atlas or teacher-supplied map; this first-edition HTML has text-based map activities, not embedded original cartographic plates.
- **Model shell:** The appearance and interaction match the established chapter-family design, but exact reuse of a separate Chapter 1 master cannot be certified without that file for comparison.
- **Bibliographic links:** The source register includes source pages, academic catalogs, and public documentary texts. Some academic publications are paywalled. This audit verifies principal factual issues and publication metadata, not exhaustive line-by-line archival inspection of every factual claim.
