## 3. Historical and source corrections implemented

1. **Timucua printing:** Replaced “printed in Spain” and “Mexico and Spain” with **Mexico City**, and specified catechisms (1612) and confession manual (1613). The Smithsonian National Anthropological Archives' MS 2401 and the Library of Congress inventory support the chronology and location: https://sova.si.edu/record/naa.ms2401 . Removed the unsupported approximate birth year for Francisco Pareja; retained his documented death in 1628.
2. **Source attribution:** Removed the false attribution of *Tears of Repentance* to David J. Silverman. Included Julius H. Rubin, *Tears of Repentance* (University of Nebraska Press, 2013), and distinguished David J. Silverman's *Faith and Boundaries* (Cambridge University Press, 2005). Source records: https://www.nebraskapress.unl.edu/nebraska/9780803243552/tears-of-repentance/ ; https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/faith-and-boundaries/D9FA451AE43CB2978B59E21B5AB0B775 .
3. **Guale uprising:** Retained the verified 1597 attacks and deaths of five Franciscans, but explicitly cautioned against overstating the cause, leadership, or exact reconstruction of the uprising. J. Michael Francis and Kathleen M. Kole's document study (American Museum of Natural History, 2011): https://www.si.edu/object/siris_sil_977516 .
4. **Gnadenhutten:** Retained qualified estimates of **more than ninety** murdered Christian Lenape in 1782; no unsupported exact harmonization imposed. National Park Service: https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/timeline-moravian-lenape-at-pilgerruh.htm .
5. **Arctic dates:** Verified Lutheran Greenland 1721, Moravian Greenland 1733 and Labrador stations Nain 1771, Okak 1776, Hopedale 1782. Clarified Poul Egede's Greenlandic dictionary (1750) and complete New Testament (1766) and acknowledged the documented Greenlandic translator **Arnarsaq**, correcting a missionary-only attribution. Supporting records: https://lex.dk/Poul_Egede ; https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/polar-record/article/blubber-for-bibles-translating-colonialism-in-inuit-missions-c-17501850/DD91D5233C80CAE41EBDD39458292EDE ; https://journals.lib.unb.ca/index.php/NFLDS/article/download/26116/30357?inline=1 .
6. **Kahnawake:** Replaced an unspecified picture request with a named, dated archival object: Louis Franquet, *Plan du Fort du Sault de St. Louis et du village des sauvages Iroquois* (1752), Newberry Library, Ayer Manuscript Map Collection: https://www.argomaps.org/maps/commonwealth%3Az603vn728/ . Explicitly explained that the plan was drawn decades after Kateri Tekakwitha's lifetime and is not direct evidence of the 1670s village. Note: this is a **linked documentary map**, not an embedded plate.
7. **Alaska:** Bounded the Kodiak mission to 1794–1821, distinguished fur-trade coercion from clergy initiatives, treated the 1821 Russian-American Company charter as an administrative development, and kept the later Veniaminov period appropriately foreshadowed. Library of Congress: https://www.loc.gov/collections/meeting-of-frontiers/articles-and-essays/alaska/russian-orthodox-church-in-alaska/ .
8. **Research register:** Rebuilt with full research book/article details and stable publisher or institutional links. Included Burkhart, Christensen, Farriss, Dierksmeier, Francis/Kole, Greer, Silverman, Rubin, Fisher, Hackel, Rollmann, Carey, and the principal digital documentary repositories. Removed the earlier draft's “editors should verify...” placeholder. Any remaining primary-source limits are transparently explained.
