## Material historical corrections

1. **Marlon Brando and Northwest fish-in:** Corrected from autumn/October 1964 to **March 2, 1964** on the Puyallup River near Tacoma, with a cataloged University of Washington/MOHAI photographic source and further contextualization from UW's Seattle Civil Rights and Labor History Project.
2. **Warrior hearing:** Corrected the venue from Washington, D.C., to **Memphis, Tennessee** at hearings held **February 2–3, 1967** before the National Advisory Commission on Rural Poverty. The individual presentation date is reported differently in some scholarship; the archival finding aid locates the Memphis transcript but does **not** reproduce its full text. All source interpretations are labeled paraphrases.
3. **Warrior essay:** Standardized the title to *Which One Are You?: The Five Types of Young Indians* and supplied an identifiable bibliographic reference to its publication in Stan Steiner's *The New Indians* (1968), without inventing an original manuscript date.
4. **Red Power parade photograph:** Corrected the holding institution to **Stanford University Libraries, Stan Steiner Papers M0700, Series 3, Box 15, Folder 19**; distinguished the Albuquerque Museum's exhibition and interpretive notes from the photograph itself.
5. **AIM beginnings:** Distinguished the central July 1968 meeting organizers from other early members; acknowledged documented women participants instead of treating a four-person list as exclusive.
6. **Alaska Native federation:** Distinguished the October 1966 organizing from more formal federation establishment in 1967.
7. **Indigenous education:** Kept Rough Rock's 1966 opening distinct from Navajo Community College's 1968 founding and 1969 teaching start.
8. **Indian Civil Rights Act:** Added direct access to original **Public Law 90-284, 82 Stat. 73, title II**, distinguishing enacted law from amended current code.
9. **Source study strengthening:** Six integrated primary-source windows now link to specific authentic documents or repository records. The dated fish-in photograph replaces unidentifiable generic newspaper paraphrase; the Poor People's Campaign source points to NPS's reproduced excerpts and document citation; the SFSU window points to the contemporary published demands.
