## 4. Documentary windows and source methodology

All four primary-source study windows remain **clearly identified editorial paraphrases / source studies**, not verbatim excerpts established by collating original manuscripts. This qualification is **material to the approved edition**.

1. **Calhoun, Jackson and the First War.** The December 26, 1817 Calhoun letter has been verified in the Library of Congress Andrew Jackson Papers: https://www.loc.gov/resource/maj.01046_0210_0213/ . Other Jackson/Monroe or congressional texts discussed are not represented as individually collated quotations.
2. **Payne's Landing / Fort Gibson.** The 1832 treaty is directly verifiable through Oklahoma State University's treaties portal: https://treaties.okstate.edu/treaties/treaty-with-the-seminole-1832-0344 ; the March 28, 1833 instrument is in the National Archives-backed Treaties Explorer: https://digitreaties.org/treaties/treaty/146928287/ . The interpretation of consent and the pressure on signatories is distinguished from the documents' literal wording.
3. **Jesup and Black Seminole participation.** The draft source window synthesizes related War Department themes across 1837–1838, not one fully located and page-collated Jesup letter. The revised edition explicitly says so and directs students toward the documentary scholarship, including Porter's work. No fabricated first-person Jesup quotation is introduced.
4. **Worth's 1842 announcement and contemporary Seminole historical perspectives.** The 1842 announcement is a dated and attributed editorial summary, not a facsimile-based transcription. The distinct retrospective interpretation is grounded to the Seminole Tribal Historic Preservation Office timeline at https://www.stofthpo.com/seminole-war-timeline-06052026/ . The two kinds of evidence are not passed off as equivalent primary documents.

These constraints are appropriate for **first-edition historical-text instruction**, but original facsimiles or collated transcriptions would be needed for a documentary critical edition. The user has not required a critical documentary edition at this stage.
