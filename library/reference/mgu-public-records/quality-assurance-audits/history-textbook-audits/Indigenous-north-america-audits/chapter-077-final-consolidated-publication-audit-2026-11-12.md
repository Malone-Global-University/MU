### IV. Treaty and chapter-boundary review

- **Chapter 74** supplies the detailed Sand Creek history; Chapter 77 uses the event only as necessary precursor to Cheyenne political mobilization.
- **Chapter 75** supplies Red Cloud's War and the Bozeman Trail withdrawal; these are summarized briefly.
- **Chapter 76** owns Article XII's land-cession rule, the contested 1876 agreement, February 28, 1877 congressional legislation, and the 1980 compensation ruling; Chapter 77 explains their coercive military setting without conducting a second treaty-law analysis.
- **Chapter 78** owns the Nez Perce War. The final edition retains and clarifies cross-references to **Chapters 82 and 83** for the Canadian numbered treaties and Red River/North-West resistances while not merging those distinct Canadian histories into Chapter 77.
- The chapter does **not** imply that Sitting Bull alone commanded the battlefield or that Crow, Arikara, and Shoshone allies of the United States had no independent political motives.

### VIII. Editorial limitations, unresolved points, and locking policy

1. **Primary sources:** Five source studies are attributed paraphrases/syntheses; full facsimile- or interview-tape-level collation was not performed. For publication, they must remain labeled as such. Do not convert these into claimed original quotations without verification.
2. **Maps:** The historical geography exercises are text-guided and require an external historic atlas or supplied plates; the chapter does not contain a verified embedded map.
3. **Exact CSS parity:** The original Chapter 1 model file was not independently present for a byte-by-byte template comparison. The author's statement of unchanged base CSS and script cannot be independently guaranteed by this audit, though the final chapter uses the same general shell and passes Chromium testing.
4. **Live deployment:** No MGU web-server upload, cross-site link resolution, or production deployment test was performed.
5. **Numbers:** Indigenous casualty totals, village population, and some troop counts are necessarily approximate or reflect inconsistent conventions. The final chapter distinguishes these where material, rather than falsely resolving them.
6. **Significant unresolved testimony:** Crazy Horse's guardhouse struggle, competing battlefield reconstructions, details of Sitting Bull's Sun Dance, and individuals' tactical roles remain evidence-qualified and should not be silently converted to categorical statements.

**Determination:** Publication-quality first-edition *textual HTML* is achieved under the existing MGU standard and these express limitations. No broad additional rewrite is warranted. **LOCK Chapter 77.** The publication-final HTML named above is the canonical text. New edits should be limited to independently established, essential historical, legal, documentary, or production defects, not routine micro-passes.
