## 4. Instructional and source apparatus

Both Primary Source Windows remain attributed paraphrases. The ITK strategy has a direct document landing page; the Vuntut Gwitchin window is explicitly based on corroborating General Assembly records and does not imply that the resolutions are a verbatim reproduction of the entire declaration. No fabricated quotations were introduced. The chapter includes selected source references in each historical section, inline references in expanded passages and direct links to the two source-window records. Source-register IDs are navigable from the text. The chronology and summary were reconciled with major changes. The two supplementary vocabulary features are deliberately retained because they aid interpretation.

The 47 linked records include scientific reports, official legislative and administrative records, Indigenous organizational publications, judicial records, community educational and energy records, and individually identified reporting. The number exceeds the advisory range by two because direct source records were preferred over generic institutional homepages. The register is selective: not every inherited factual sentence has its own reference and individual external links were not all independently tested in a browser.

## 6. Remaining limitations and publication controls

1. This review concentrates on consequential claims, the identified audit issues, and the newly expanded passages. It is not an exhaustive sentence-by-sentence verification of the inherited manuscript. Some general and smaller quantitative statements remain supported only at section level or by the original draft's reporting.
2. The date of physical completion and opening of Mertarvik's permanent school, full relocation expenditures and long-term housing/service completion remain unresolved. The chapter records the status as such.
3. Community decisions following Halong, regional policy implementation and Arctic diplomatic cooperation are ongoing. The chapter does not turn plans, funding commitments or forecasts into completed outcomes.
4. The complete original Vuntut Gwitchin declaration was not reproduced; the primary-source exercise uses an identified paraphrase corroborated by the First Nation's resolutions.
5. The source register contains 47 linked records, but a full external link-health scan and live-site rendering check were not performed. The supplied HTML design was retained rather than redesigned.
