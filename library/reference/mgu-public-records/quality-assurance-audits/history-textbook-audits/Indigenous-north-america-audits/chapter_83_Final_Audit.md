### Mandatory corrections verified and implemented

1. **Louis Riel legislation:** The original claim that federal legislation in **2010** recognized Riel as a founder is false. A 2010 federal bill did not become law. Manitoba's *Louis Riel Act*, C.C.S.M. c. L229, was enacted **December 7, 2023** and bestowed the honorary title *First Premier of Manitoba*. The chapter now distinguishes a provincial honour, an unenacted federal proposal, and the original 1885 conviction. Official statute: <https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/l229.php>.
2. **Manitoba scrip:** The 1874 federal provision for Métis heads of families precedes the 1885 North-West commissions. Distinguish the constitutional **Section 31** children's 1.4-million-acre grant, **Section 32** confirmation of existing holdings, **1874 head-of-family scrip**, later remedial scrip for some children, and subsequent territorial scrip commissions. Supporting records: <https://mhs.mb.ca/docs/mb_history/05/metislands.shtml>, <https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2018/aanc-inac/R5-548-1989-eng.pdf> and <https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2014/csc-scc/JU1-1-2013-1-3.pdf>.
3. **Gabriel Dumont:** Amnesty was in **1886**, not 1888. He joined Buffalo Bill's show in Philadelphia in **July 1886** and returned to Canada later in the decade. Dictionary of Canadian Biography: <https://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/6692?revision_id=30975>.
4. **Manitoba Act documentary window:** Added **Section 22** on denominational schools; kept it separate from **Section 23** (English/French in legislature and courts) and **Sections 31–32** (different land provisions). Official Act and the 2013 Supreme Court analysis are named separately; the study remains editorial paraphrase, not an authenticated direct quotation.
5. **Lists of Rights:** The four-iteration framework is documented as an **editorial classification** (Lawrence Barkwell, Gabriel Dumont Institute). Distinguishes the December 1869 bilingual convention, the January–February 1870 Convention of Forty, and the final March 1870 negotiating version; does not claim all copies or meeting records are identical. Sources: <https://www.metismuseum.ca/resource.php/14525>, <https://www.biographi.ca/en/topics/topic-match-list.php?id=1476>.
6. **1884 petition and 1885 reply:** Separates the December 1884 petition, internal **January 28, 1885** Cabinet authorization of an inquiry, and its communication to petitioners in **February 1885**. Source: <https://gladue.drc.usask.ca/node/2512>.
7. **Fish Creek:** Parks Canada documents roughly **150 Métis, Dakota and Cree defenders**, approximately **400 in Middleton's directly engaged right-hand column** out of about 800 moving along both banks, and **55 Canadian versus six defender casualties**. Combined casualties are not labeled as numbers killed. <https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=739>.
8. **Batoche:** Parks Canada supports **May 9–12, 1885**, fewer than 300 defenders and approximately 800 Canadian attackers. Distinguishes the four days, the Northcote ferry-cable episode, infantry attack, ammunition, civilian consequences and uncertain casualty counts. <https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/sk/batoche/culture/histoire-history>.
9. **Riel trial:** Source Window 4 identifies the 1886 official compilation by its bibliographic title, publisher, year and Canadiana identifier; its passages remain identified as editorial paraphrase. <https://www.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.30472>.
10. **1886 claims:** William Pearce's 1886 report on land and scrip claims must not be mistaken for a blanket record of compensation for wartime household losses. Distinct source records and their different evidentiary purposes are specified. <https://iportal.usask.ca/record/30682>.
11. **Later recognition:** Confirmed 2019 governmental **posthumous exoneration and apology** for Poundmaker (not a normal judicial appeal), and the **July 6, 2021** Canada–Manitoba Métis Federation self-government agreement. <https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2019/05/23/prime-minister-delivers-statement-exoneration-chief-poundmaker>; <https://www.rcaanc-cirnac.gc.ca/eng/1641476532215/1641476589226>.
