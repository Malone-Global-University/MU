## 6. Remaining limitations and editorial decisions

1. The complete original 1874 statute remains unverified. The chapter explicitly distinguishes it from the authentic 1887 amendment and avoids presenting inferred provisions as original quotations. This is an acknowledged source limitation, not a remaining primary-source blocker.
2. The chapter discusses SB 310 as enacted and the Karuk agreement as officially announced; it is not a current legal compliance manual. The full executed agreement and subsequent amendments were not comprehensively reviewed.
3. Not every retained narrative claim was independently verified against a primary source. The selected bibliography improves traceability and the targeted verification resolves the specific material concerns identified in the earlier audit.
4. The original Chapter 1 HTML model was not separately supplied for direct comparison. The revision preserves the supplied Chapter 134 shell and its structure; exact conformity to a separate template cannot be certified. Live MGU deployment and external-link behavior on the production site were not tested.
5. The editor chose the authentic 1887 amendment rather than substituting a wholly contemporary legal document for the nineteenth-century source exercise. The mobile navigation, glossary definitions and source section are limited editorial enhancements to the existing design.

## 7. Final disposition

The chapter satisfies the approved twenty-section plan and retains the full historical and instructional treatment. The legal-source deficiency has been resolved through an authentic primary document, the identified factual errors have been corrected, and the targeted publication checks pass. The remaining limitations are documented and do not warrant another discretionary expansion or revision cycle.
