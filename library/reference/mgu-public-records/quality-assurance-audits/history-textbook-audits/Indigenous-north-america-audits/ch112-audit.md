## Documentary limitations retained and disclosed

- The source window on Oglala impeachment proceedings cites a named documentary-reader excerpt; the original full transcript was not directly inspected. The window asks learners to consult that transcript and does **not** fabricate direct quotations.
- The Smithsonian collection's **catalog description** was checked, but individual negatives were not all inspected; the window does not pretend otherwise.
- The Michigan State scanned activist compilation was not read page by page; relevant May 5 settlement language and its schedule were cross-checked against the independent Marshals Museum reproduction. Its image-rendering screenshot was requested through the web tool but failed to load, so detailed paleographic or marginalia claims are avoided.
- Pacifica transcription can contain automatic transcription errors; the March 11 date is corroborated by other documentary witnesses. The full audio program was not independently listened to.
- Exact totals of later political deaths remain attributed and disputed; injury and military equipment figures are explicitly contextual and not treated as precision measurements.
- The web and local browser tools do not guarantee all external institutional links remain accessible to every reader; an offline URL probe is not represented as live production-site verification.
