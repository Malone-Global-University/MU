## 4. Historical legal corrections — verified

1. **Original IRA §17: 10 years, not 25**. In the 1934 statute, **48 Stat. 988**, the charter clause prevents authorizing sale, mortgage, or a lease exceeding ten years of reservation land. The 25-year language appears after a **1990** amendment; the historical narrative, primary source window, and source register explicitly distinguish these versions. Original: <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-48/pdf/STATUTE-48-Pg984.pdf> (last printed page, p. 988). Compare the amendment notes in <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2020-title25/html/USCODE-2020-title25-chap45.htm>.
2. **Hopi**: The **June 15, 1935 applicability referendum** (Haas: 519 for, 299 against) is a distinct election from the **October 24, 1936 constitution ratification** (651 for, 104 against). Secretary **Harold L. Ickes approved the constitution December 19, 1936**. Chronology and §97.15 updated; abstention and divided village representation are not misrepresented as unanimity. <https://thorpe.law.ou.edu/IRA/hopicons.html>.
3. **Papago / Tohono O'odham**: Constitution **ratified December 12, 1936** and **approved January 6, 1937**, which corrects the earlier conflation with 1937 adoption. <https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/llscd/37026340/37026340.pdf>.
4. **Crow superintendent**: Robert Yellowtail's appointment dated **August 1, 1934** rather than an imprecise mid-1930s reference. Smithsonian National Anthropological Archives institutional record.
5. **Haas referendum comparative totals**: Section 97.16 now gives identified **Table A** voting counts for Crow (112/689), Klamath (56/408), Blackfeet (823/171), Mescalero Apache (273/11), and Santa Clara Pueblo (134/34), and retains the distinction between **258 applicability elections (181/77)** and subsequent constitutional/charter ratifications. <https://thorpe.law.ou.edu/IRA/IRAbook/tribalgovpt1tblA.htm>.
6. **Alaska 1936**: The primary-source window verifies **49 Stat. 1250, §1**, including the condition that organizational eligibility based on a common bond applied to Alaska groups **not previously recognized as bands or tribes**; the cited phrase is no longer presented as unchecked. <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-49/pdf/STATUTE-49-Pg1250.pdf>.
7. **1935 IRA election amendment**: Majority of those actually voting subject to a **30% eligible-voter participation floor**, including exclusion, constitutional, and charter elections. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/25/5127> (June 15, 1935, 49 Stat. 378).
8. **CSKT case**: The constitution was ratified October 4, 1935 and approved October 28; the corporate charter was independently ratified April 25, 1936. Primary institutional record: <https://www.loc.gov/item/36026096/> and <https://www.loc.gov/item/36026827/>.

No newly discovered publication-critical factual contradictions remained within the scope of this consolidated targeted audit. This was not a line-by-line independent peer review of every sentence in the complete historical narrative.
