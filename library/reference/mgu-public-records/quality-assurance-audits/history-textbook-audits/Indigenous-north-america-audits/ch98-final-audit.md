## 4. Historical corrections and verified evidence

1. **Frederick Ogilvie Loft:** The Dictionary of Canadian Biography documents establishment of the League at Ohsweken in December 1918, distinguishing organization from its first Sault Ste. Marie convention in September 1919, and identifies the circular of November 26, 1919 in Library and Archives Canada **RG 10, volume 3211, file 527787, part 1**. [Dictionary of Canadian Biography](https://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/loft_frederick_ogilvie_16E.html).
2. **John Tootoosis and Prairie organization:** University of Saskatchewan research places him among the organizers of the League of Indians of Western Canada at Thunderchild in **1921**, correcting the implied 1930s-only beginnings. [Our Legacy, University of Saskatchewan](https://library.usask.ca/ourlegacy/exhibits/nehiyawak_leadership.php).
3. **Allied Tribes and Parliament:** The 1926 petition and 1927 joint committee are distinguished from any court judgment; the amendment of March 31, 1927 occurred during the inquiry, prior to its final conclusions. [1927 parliamentary proceeding/committee compilation](https://ellingtree.com/files/sources/other-sjc-1927-allied-tribes/); [UBCIC chronology](https://www.ubcic.bc.ca/timeline).
4. **Section 149A / section 141:** The original numbering and later consolidation, claims-related fundraising scope, written-consent mechanism, and repeal in 1951 are distinguished from the false proposition that all Indigenous meetings and legal representation were banned. [Government of Canada, *Historical Development of the Indian Act*](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2017/aanc-inac/R5-295-1984-eng.pdf).
5. **Deskaheh:** The George P. Decker Papers identify *The Redman’s Appeal for Justice* dated **August 6, 1923**, **Series IIID, Box 7, Folder 12**. René Claparède is described as a Swiss writer and advocate, not as a lawyer or verified League official. [Decker Papers](https://fisherpub.sjf.edu/georgepdecker_leagueofnations/); [Dictionary of Canadian Biography](https://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/deskaheh_15E.html).
6. **Six Nations council, 1923–1924:** The Thompson inquiry was commissioned in 1923, completed late that year and released in 1924. The September 17, 1924 order and October 21 election are separated; Mounted Police intervention is placed in the enforcement context. [Thompson inquiry, Government of Canada](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.699960/publication.html).
7. **Treaty 11:** Henry Anthony Conroy handled principal negotiations in 1921; after his death, Thomas William Harris completed the **July 17, 1922 Fort Liard adhesion**. [Crown-Indigenous Relations official treaty text](https://rcaanc.gc.ca/eng/1100100028916/1581294101357).
8. **1930 Alberta NRTA paragraph 12:** The official schedule distinguishes general provincial game-law application from hunting, trapping and fishing **for food** on unoccupied Crown land and other accessible land; the provision was not a new treaty and not an unqualified right to harvest everywhere. [Justice Canada](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/A-10.6/FullText.html).
9. **1939 Indian Association of Alberta:** John Callihoo and Métis advocate Malcolm Norris are identified as collaborating in the founding, without conflating Métis and First Nations legal histories. [Athabasca University Press](https://read.aupress.ca/read/bucking-conservatism/section/c6dd279a-a351-484a-9cf4-5b2391422dcf).
10. **1921 Village Island potlatch prosecution:** U'mista identifies **45 charged**, **22 suspended sentences**, **three held for appeal**, and **20 imprisoned**; the revised narrative differentiates legal repression from cultural and political continuity. [U'mista Cultural Centre](https://www.umista.ca/pages/collection-history).
11. **1920 Canadian schooling measures:** Correctly described as compulsory school-attendance powers, not a universal statutory requirement that all children must attend residential schools specifically.

These are not a claim that each manuscript, parliamentary page, or historical statute was collated line-by-line. **Loft's circular, the entire 1927 committee testimony, the 1927 amendment's complete original wording, and Deskaheh's publication were not transcribed or authenticated line by line for this edition.** The source windows use labeled editorial paraphrases and do not invent quotation marks or precise unverified quotations.
