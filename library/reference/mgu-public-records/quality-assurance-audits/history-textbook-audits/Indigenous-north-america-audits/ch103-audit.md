## Consolidated manuscript corrections

1. **§103.12 and major-people preview.** Replaced Alfonso Villa Rojas as alleged inaugural director of the 1951 Chiapas Tzeltal–Tzotzil regional coordinating center with **Gonzalo Aguirre Beltrán** (1951–1952). Villa Rojas remains identified for earlier regional ethnographic work. Grounding: Universidad Veracruzana's scholarly biography, https://diccionariover.uv.mx/egvadmin/bin/view/biografia/Aguirre%20Beltr%C3%A1n%2C%20Gonzalo .
2. **§103.5, Canadian census.** Recast **125,521 (1941)** and **165,607 (1951)** as Statistics Canada's historical **“Native Indian and Eskimo” ethnic-origin census classification**, not legal enrollment in the Indian Act register. Kept separate discussion of Indian registration after the 1951 Act. Grounding: Statistics Canada historical yearbook, https://www65.statcan.gc.ca/acyb02/1967/acyb02_19670197014-eng.htm .
3. **§103.5, Mexican census.** Corrected the mistaken implication that bilingual Indigenous-language speakers were omitted. Both monolingual and bilingual Indigenous-language speakers aged five and older were enumerated; specified ~2.49 million in 1940 and ~2.45 million in 1950, with the appropriate denominator and distinction from Indigenous identity. Grounding: UNAM multilingual-demography materials, https://www.nacionmulticultural.unam.mx/100preguntas/pregunta-tema.php?c_pre=11&tema=11 and INEGI historical series.
4. **All six Primary Source Windows and Mexican INI exercise.** Added **14 external document or official historical-source links**, each clearly distinguishing an original statute, a later compilation, a signing statement, and historical institutional commentary. Retained explicitly labeled paraphrases instead of inventing quotation text.
5. **Mobile Contents navigation.** Preserved the responsive `<details>` menu and made it close after selecting an anchor, verified in Chromium.
