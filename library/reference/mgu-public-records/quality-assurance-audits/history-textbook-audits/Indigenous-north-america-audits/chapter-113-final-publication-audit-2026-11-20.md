## Substantive revision record

1. Corrected the Article 3 Medicine Creek treaty extract to mark the omission of its proviso and linked the complete official text.
2. Replaced the incorrectly attributed Billy Frank Jr. memoir source window with his authored **2009 “Being Frank”** column, explicitly presenting it as a verified first-person source, paraphrased rather than fabricated as a quotation.
3. Corrected the geographic setting of Hank Adams's **January 1971** shooting to the Puyallup River near Tacoma.
4. Reconstructed **Puyallup I–III** with the 1977 sovereign-immunity holding, the distinction between the tribal government and individual fishermen, on-reservation catch limits, and the case-specific rather than universal apportionment method.
5. Clarified Judge Belloni's **1969 Sohappy** ruling without attributing to it a universal fixed fifty-percent allocation.
6. Clarified the **1970** initial federal case as seven trustee-represented tribes, expanded to fourteen participating tribal parties by the 1974 decision; distinguished this from the NWIFC's 19 original members.
7. Deepened the legal discussion in §§113.11, 113.13–113.14, 113.16, 113.18–113.19, including historical evidence, run accounting, legal remedy, and co-management institutions.
8. Corrected the **1979 Fishing Vessel** treatment: initial equal division, moderate-living ceiling, on-reservation and ceremonial/subsistence catches counted toward treaty share, and scarcity-priority issue expressly unresolved.
9. Clarified that **1980 Phase II** district court treatment of hatchery fish and environmental claims was modified in **1985**, when the Ninth Circuit affirmed hatchery inclusion but vacated generalized habitat declaratory relief as too abstract.
10. Corrected Billy Frank Jr.'s first NWIFC chair term to **May 1977**, with service again from **1981 onward**.
11. Corrected the overly specific claim about Sampson Tulee's gear: the Supreme Court opinion identifies a net, without specifying a dip net.
12. Updated summary and chronological apparatus to reflect substantive legal corrections.
