Indigenous North American History Audits

Chapter-level assessment and verification records for Malone Global University's Indigenous North American History textbook series.

Collection Overview

This register links only to Indigenous North American History audit and verification files identified in the supplied institutional directory inventory. Records are arranged by chapter, and chapters with no recorded audit are omitted. Where multiple audits or verification documents exist for a chapter, each is listed.

Inventory scope: 48 chapters (Chapters 43–95); 59 existing records. The linked source records currently carry .md extensions, so these are links to the inventory's recorded files, not to converted PDF editions. Final audit labels reflect file titles and do not independently certify external peer review.
