## Direct historical/legal changes made

1. **§93.11 — Canadian 1917 military vote:** Corrected the erroneous male-only formulation; the *Military Voters Act* of 1917 included qualifying British subjects of both sexes serving or formerly serving in the armed forces, including First Nations people with Indian status and some under age twenty-one. Clarified the separate *Wartime Elections Act* and kept 1920 veteran exceptions distinct. Verification: Elections Canada, [Milestones in the Evolution of the Federal Electoral System](https://www.elections.ca/content.aspx?dir=bkg&document=mils&lang=e&section=vot) and [*A History of the Vote in Canada*, ch. 2](https://elections.ca/content.aspx?dir=his%2Fchap2&document=index&lang=e&section=res).
2. **§93.16 — William Paul Sr.:** Replaced unsourced degree assertion with Whitworth study, law by correspondence through LaSalle, and **Alaska Bar admission recorded by the Alaska Historical Society as 1920** (some historical reminiscences give 1921). Described his Tongass-region upbringing without asserting a single birthplace where biographies differ. Preserved the 1924 territorial legislative election and organization history. Verification: [Alaska Historical Society, *Guide to ANCSA Sources*, vol. 1](https://alaskahistoricalsociety.org/wp-content/uploads/Guide-to-ANCSA_VOL1.pdf); [State of Alaska chronology](https://alaska.gov/kids/learn/chronology.htm).
3. **§93.17 and Chronology Preview — Grand River intervention in 1924:** Separated the **September 17** Canadian order in council imposing the elective band council framework, the **October 21** election, and the police exclusion of hereditary chiefs from the council house. Acknowledged intra-community political disagreement. Verification: [John Leonard Taylor, 1984](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2017/aanc-inac/R5-295-1984-eng.pdf), p. 162 in the original printed pagination. Another historical analysis dates a council-house takeover to October 7; exact sequence is not collapsed into one event.
4. **§93.10 — Status and burial:** Qualified an overgeneralized burial consequence; a potential dispute over burial is not presented as a uniform provision of the 1876 Indian Act.
5. **§93.13 — 1926 Alaska congressional debate:** Added the recorded exchange among Reps. **Charles F. Curry (California)** and **Charles D. Carter (Oklahoma)**, and Alaska delegate **Dan Sutherland**, distinguishing stated reasons from likely unequal effects. The House bill H.R. 9211 was not finally passed at the particular sitting referenced. Source: [*Congressional Record*, vol. 67 (1926), House p. 7785, “Literacy Test for Voters in the Territory of Alaska”](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-CRECB-1926-pt7-v67/pdf/GPO-CRECB-1926-pt7-v67-13.pdf).
6. **Primary Source Window 5:** Replaced a generic reading-guide placeholder with page-specific documented editorial paraphrase of the named lawmakers' arguments, analysis, limitations, perspective, and five source questions. No fictional quotation or unverified census number was introduced.
7. **Source Windows 1–4:** Updated exact verified short quotations for *Elk v. Wilkins* and the 1924 citizenship statute and attached document-level links to the *Elk* opinion, the National Archives statute, the 1876 Canadian Indian Act, the 1885 legislative compilation, and an Elections Canada explanatory account. Primary-source window interpretive analysis remains explicitly labeled as such.
8. **Scholarly register:** Replaced “John L. Tobias and related scholarship” with a fully identified **John Leonard Taylor, *Canadian Indian Policy during the Inter-war Years, 1918–1939* (Indian and Northern Affairs Canada, 1984)**, and separately identified **Robert G. Moore, *The Historical Development of the Indian Act*, 2nd ed., edited by John Leslie and Ron Maguire (1978)**. Source records: [Government of Canada Taylor catalogue](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.838945/publication.html) and [Government of Canada Moore/Leslie/Maguire catalogue](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.840238/publication.html).

The 1926 debate is tied to named speakers and a specific page, but the 39-MB GovInfo period PDF was not independently collated page-by-page as a facsimile; its official searchable transcription was used and a visual screenshot attempt was blocked by size. - Desktop and mobile behavior was tested locally using Chromium. Deployment to the live MGU site and parity against a separately hosted Chapter 1 model remain unverified.
- The historical date cutoff for the chapter's main narrative is c. 1930, with identified later events used only as interpretive context. No routine second rewrite is recommended.
