Quality Assurance and Audits

Formal assessments of MGU educational publications, departmental content, institutional reports, technical materials, and digital systems.

Collection Overview

Quality Assurance and Audits is the assessment and verification collection within MGU Public Records. It preserves documented evaluations of Malone Global University's scholarly content, instructional programs, institutional publications, website materials, and supporting information systems.

This collection answers what was examined, against which criteria, what was found, and what corrective action or disposition followed. It is distinct from the production history of a work, the plan that authorized its development, and the editorial rules against which it was assessed.

Formal audit reports are published as PDF documents only. This HTML index serves as a directory and does not reproduce the reports in webpage form. Audit conclusions must be read in light of the scope, methods, and limitations disclosed in each document.

Assessment Areas

  • Academic and instructional quality: departmental coverage, lesson sequencing, prerequisite alignment, learning objectives, instructional completeness, and suitability for the intended learner.
  • Scholarly and factual review: accuracy, chronology, evidence quality, attribution, citation support, interpretive balance, and treatment of uncertainty in textbooks, encyclopedia articles, and research publications.
  • Editorial and structural compliance: adherence to an approved outline, section completeness, document organization, publication apparatus, formatting, and internal consistency.
  • Digital and technical verification: HTML structure, accessible navigation, broken links, duplicate identifiers, document rendering, PDF usability, and reproducible technical checks.
  • Institutional document review: consistency of planning reports, stated assumptions, scope, proposed policies, cross-document dependencies, and implementation readiness.
  • Catalog and record integrity: completeness of catalog entries, metadata accuracy, duplicated records, indexing quality, and cross-reference integrity.

Minimum Requirements for Audit PDFs

To support traceability and meaningful comparison, each audit should identify:

  • Subject and version: the exact department, page, chapter, document, or data collection examined, including its relevant date or revision.
  • Review scope and criteria: the questions investigated, applicable standards or approved plans, and any explicitly excluded subjects.
  • Review methods and evidence: whether checks were textual, source-based, programmatic, rendered, sampled, or otherwise performed, with references to supporting materials where feasible.
  • Findings and significance: confirmed defects, potential concerns, compliant elements, outstanding uncertainties, and prioritization of corrective actions.
  • Disposition and limitations: required revisions, follow-up review, acceptance recommendation, unresolved issues, and the limits of what the audit establishes.
  • Review attribution: preparation date, responsible reviewing function, use of AI assistance, and whether any independent subject-matter or human expert review actually took place.

AI-Assisted Review and Scholarly Assurance

MGU may publish AI-assisted audits of departments, reports, textbook chapters, reference collections, and digital materials. Such documents should describe the assistance used and distinguish automated checks, editorial judgment, source verification, and independently conducted expert review.

An AI-assisted evaluation does not, by itself, constitute independent scholarly peer review or prove that every factual statement has been verified. A publication-ready recommendation should therefore identify the testing actually completed and any substantive limitations remaining.

Where an audit leads to revisions, the report should make clear whether it evaluates the original draft or a corrected edition. Any subsequent acceptance decision or follow-up audit should be separately identified.

Filing Boundaries

Quality Assurance and Audits contains evaluative findings. Requirements for scholarly evidence, citations, and publication acceptance belong in Editorial and Publication Standards. Reports of what was produced belong in Production Records; proposals for future institutional or departmental development belong in Institutional Planning or Department Planning.

Document identifiers, revision histories, supersession controls, and master registers are maintained under Document Control and Versioning. The Library Catalog Codex defines cataloging rules; audits of compliance with those rules may be filed here.

Quality findings should not be presented as binding policies merely because they appear in a published report. Adoption or approval of a revised standard remains a separate institutional decision.
