Development, Architecture, and Operation of backup-mgu.ps1

Documents the creation and operation of MGU's PowerShell and Robocopy backup utility, developed to preserve a dated pre-modernization working-file baseline of the university's website project. Covers recursive file copying, exclusion of the C:\MGU\desk directory, non-destructive preview mode, CSV inventory reporting, logging, and file-preservation safeguards.

Records the successful November 20, 2026 initial backup reported by the operator, while distinguishing that execution milestone from independent verification of copied files and restoration readiness.
