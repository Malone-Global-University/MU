Department Planning

Department Planning Overview

The Department Planning collection preserves planning reports, organizational frameworks, instructional development strategies, and operational standards for Malone Global University departments. It provides a durable reference point for how departmental programs are structured, expanded, reviewed, and maintained.

These materials are institutional planning records, not student lessons. Each document is maintained in one canonical Library location and may be linked from the relevant department, curriculum pages, and other MGU indexes.

Collection Scope and Filing Standards

Department Planning is the authoritative location for department-level strategy, content architecture, instructional sequencing frameworks, departmental organizational plans, and related development policies.

Documents should clearly identify their issuing department, subject, publication or revision date, and approval status. A proposed plan must remain identified as proposed until the institution formally adopts it. Superseded editions should be distinguishable from current documents rather than silently replacing the historical record.

Individual course lessons, encyclopedia explanations, quick-reference charts and tables, and single-purpose tutorials belong in their designated instructional or Library collections. Department Planning links to those resources where needed but does not duplicate them.
