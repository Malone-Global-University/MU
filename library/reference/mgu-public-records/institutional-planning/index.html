Institutional Planning

Institutional Planning Overview

Institutional Planning is the public document collection for Malone Global University's university-wide development strategy. Its reports examine the establishment, organization, expansion, and long-term capacity of MGU as an institution. Topics include institutional offerings, governance and board development, physical facilities, faculty and staffing, capital allocation, and educational media investment.

This collection publishes formal institutional reports and whitepapers as PDF documents. HTML pages in MGU Public Records serve as navigational indexes only; they do not replace, reproduce, or simplify the executive text of the underlying publications.

Institutional Planning Documents

MGU National Branch Identification and Numbering Standard

Establishes a proposed national identification and numbering framework for Malone Global University's planned network of state headquarters, regional campuses, and satellite branches. Defines the MGU- prefix and geographic telephone area-code identification system, with sequential alphabetical suffixes assigned according to formal branch foundation order. Establishes permanent identifier rules, geographic service-territory designation, centralized national branch registration, administrative assignment authority, historical record preservation, and duplicate-prevention controls. Includes illustrative branch identifiers, a six-stage implementation program, and provisions for future domestic and international expansion.

MGU Academic Portfolio — Foundational Schools of Discipline and Occupational Study

Establishes the proposed foundational academic architecture of Malone Global University, comprising six vocational and professional schools and one principal School of Classical Liberal Arts and Sciences. Defines the university's community-building educational mission, the constituent-school organizational model, and the role of the liberal arts school as both a permanent academic foundation and an incubator for emerging disciplines. Establishes preliminary criteria for elevating mature academic programs into independent schools based on instructional development, institutional investment, administrative capacity, and formal authorization. Preserves future expansion opportunities while deferring detailed departmental structures, course development, credentials, and operational commitments.

Collection Scope and Filing Boundaries

Institutional Planning addresses what MGU should establish, offer, acquire, finance, staff, or expand at the university level. Department Planning addresses how already established departments implement and develop their own programs. Administrative Procedures documents recurring operating processes, while Institutional Decisions and Directives preserves adopted institutional decisions.

Future institutional planning documents may address board formation, facilities acquisition, institutional academic offerings, faculty development, and investment in educational video programming. These subjects are areas of scope, not links to documents that have already been published.

Documents in this collection are publicly readable institutional records. Their stated status distinguishes proposals from adopted plans; publication alone does not constitute formal approval.
