Institutional Planning

Institutional Planning Overview

Institutional Planning is the public document collection for Malone Global University's university-wide development strategy. Its reports examine the establishment, organization, expansion, and long-term capacity of MGU as an institution. Topics include institutional offerings, governance and board development, physical facilities, faculty and staffing, capital allocation, and educational media investment.

This collection publishes formal institutional reports and whitepapers as PDF documents. HTML pages in MGU Public Records serve as navigational indexes only; they do not replace, reproduce, or simplify the executive text of the underlying publications.

Collection Scope and Filing Boundaries

Institutional Planning addresses what MGU should establish, offer, acquire, finance, staff, or expand at the university level. Department Planning addresses how already established departments implement and develop their own programs. Administrative Procedures documents recurring operating processes, while Institutional Decisions and Directives preserves adopted institutional decisions.

Future institutional planning documents may address board formation, facilities acquisition, institutional academic offerings, faculty development, and investment in educational video programming. These subjects are areas of scope, not links to documents that have already been published.

Documents in this collection are publicly readable institutional records. Their stated status distinguishes proposals from adopted plans; publication alone does not constitute formal approval.
